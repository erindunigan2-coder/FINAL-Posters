## Phase 4 -- Passivation Chemistry (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5".

Section label: `PASSIVATION CHEMISTRY -- CITRIC VS. NITRIC`

**Left panel -- CITRIC ACID PASSIVATION (Emerald accent):**
Subtitle: `ASTM A967 -- Practice C`

```
Chemistry:  Citric acid 4--10% (w/v)
Temperature: Ambient to 60 C (140 F)
Time:       20--60 min
pH:         ~2 (as-mixed)
```

Advantages:
```
+ Environmentally preferred -- biodegradable
+ No NOx fumes generated
+ Safer to handle than HNO3
+ Chelates free iron from surface
+ Lower wastewater treatment cost
+ Gaining industry acceptance rapidly
```

Limitation: `Citric may not meet legacy specs requiring nitric acid`

**Right panel -- NITRIC ACID PASSIVATION (Amber accent):**
Subtitle: `ASTM A967 -- Practice A/B`

```
Chemistry:  HNO3 20--50% (v/v)
  Optional: + Na2Cr2O7 2--6 oz/gal
Temperature: Ambient to 60 C (140 F)
Time:       20--60 min
pH:         Strongly acid (< 1)
```

Advantages:
```
+ Long-established industry standard
+ Strong oxidizer -- effective oxide builder
+ Well-documented in legacy specifications
+ Effective for all stainless grades
```

Limitations:
```
NOx fume generation -- ventilation required
Higher waste disposal cost
Dichromate addition adds Cr(VI) -- avoid
```

**Process parameters table:**

| Method | Chemistry | Temp | Time | Rinse After | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Citric A | 4--10% citric | 49--60 C | 20--30 min | DI cascade | ASTM A967 Practice C |
| Citric B | 4--10% citric | Ambient | 30--60 min | DI cascade | ASTM A967 Practice C |
| Nitric A | 20--25% HNO3 | Ambient | 30--60 min | DI cascade | ASTM A967 Practice A |
| Nitric B | 20--50% HNO3 | 49--60 C | 20--30 min | DI cascade | ASTM A967 Practice B |

## Phase 5 -- Standards + Industry Requirements

Y: 14.5" to 22.0".

Section label: `APPLICABLE STANDARDS`

| Standard | Scope | Key Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| ASTM A967/A967M | Chemical passivation for SS parts | Citric and nitric practices; test methods |
| ASTM A380 | Cleaning, descaling, passivation of SS | Broader scope; references A967 |
| ASME BPE | Bioprocessing equipment | EP + passivation for product-contact; SF4; DI rinse |
| SEMI F19 | Semiconductor gas/fluid systems | Ultra-clean; passivation required after EP |
| FDA 21 CFR | Medical devices, food contact | Passivation is standard practice |

**When required vs. optional cards:**

| ALWAYS PASSIVATE WHEN: | PASSIVATION MAY BE OPTIONAL WHEN: |
|---|---|
| Pharma/biotech per ASME BPE | General industrial/decorative EP only |
| Medical devices per FDA | No customer spec requires it |
| Semiconductor per SEMI | EP itself provides adequate passive layer |
| Any spec calling out ASTM A967 | Even optional -- 20 min soak is cheap insurance |
| Customer PO requires passivation | |

## Phase 6 -- Verification Testing + Cr:Fe Enrichment

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Three verification test cards:**

| Test | Procedure | Pass | Fail | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FERROXYL | Apply K3Fe(CN)6 + HNO3 in DI water | No blue spots in 15 min | Blue spots = free iron | ASTM A380 |
| COPPER SULFATE | Swab CuSO4, wait 6 min | No copper (pink) deposit | Copper deposit = active iron | ASTM A967/A380 |
| WATER-BREAK | Spray DI water over surface | Water sheets uniformly | Beading = organic contamination | Visual |

**Cr:Fe enrichment strip (3 progressive cards):**

| Stage | Cr:Fe Ratio | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical polish | ~1.5:1 | Native passive oxide |
| After EP | 3:1 to 5:1 | EP enriches Cr by dissolving Fe |
| After EP + Passivation | 5:1+ | Passivation further dissolves Fe, thickens Cr oxide |
