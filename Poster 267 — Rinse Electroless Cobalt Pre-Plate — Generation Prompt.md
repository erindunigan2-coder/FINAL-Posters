## Phase 5 -- Contamination Drag-In Table

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHAT DRAG-IN DOES TO YOUR COBALT BATH`.

| Contaminant Source | Drag-In Chemistry | Effect on Cobalt Bath | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| HCl activation | Chloride (Cl-) | Accelerates bath aging; can cause pitting | Moderate |
| HF activation (Si wafers) | Fluoride (F-) | Attacks glass tank lining; destabilizes complexant | High |
| Sn/Pd colloidal | Excess Sn2+ | Can reduce bath stability; acts as nucleation site | High |
| Chromic acid etch residue | Cr6+ | Poisons stabilizer system -- even trace amounts | Critical |
| Pd activation residue | Pd2+ ions | Can trigger uncontrolled deposition at ppm levels | Moderate |

Header `#3A4055`. Alternating rows. Severity: Moderate `#E8A020`, High/Critical `#E05C5C`.

## Phase 7 -- Rinse Parameters + Tips

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `RINSE SPECIFICATIONS + BEST PRACTICES`.

**Left -- Parameters (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Water quality | DI preferred (especially after HF or Sn/Pd activation) |
| Temperature | Ambient |
| Time | 30--60 seconds (balance cleanliness vs. transfer speed) |
| Conductivity target | <20 uS/cm for critical work |
| Agitation | Gentle overflow; avoid vigorous agitation |

**Right -- Best Practices (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

| Practice | Why |
|---|---|
| Dedicated rinse tank (not shared) | Prevents cross-contamination from other process lines |
| Frequent water changes | Rinse tank accumulates activation chemistry over a shift |
| Position tank adjacent to Co bath | Physical proximity minimizes transfer time |
| Consider spray rinse for rack work | Faster than immersion; reduces total transfer time |
