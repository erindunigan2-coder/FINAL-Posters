# Poster #169 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Manganese Phosphate -- Pre-Condition

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of 8. This is the hot water rinse between the alkaline clean and the surface conditioning / acid pickle stage. The key difference from other phosphate rinsing: this rinse runs HOT (120--160 F) because the aggressive alkaline cleaners used for MnPhos parts leave stubborn residues that cold water does not fully remove. Alkaline carryover into the acid conditioning stage neutralizes the acid and wastes chemistry. Multiple rinse stages are common for heavily soiled parts.

Hero visual: rinse tank schematic showing overflow configuration, temperature control, and conductivity monitoring.

| Consequence | Effect |
|---|---|
| Alkaline into acid pickle | Neutralizes acid, wastes chemistry, reduces etch rate |
| Alkaline into phosphate bath | Raises pH above operating range, kills coating formation |
| Surfactant residue carried forward | Creates foam in phosphate bath, causes skip areas |
| Silicate residue on surface | Passivates steel, prevents phosphate crystal nucleation |

## Design Notes

The hot rinse distinction is the key takeaway for this poster. Most rinse posters in the series show ambient temperature -- this one must visually emphasize the elevated temperature with the amber temperature callout. The conductivity/pH/overflow trifecta gives the operator three concrete things to measure, which is exactly the kind of actionable content that makes these posters worth hanging on a wall.
