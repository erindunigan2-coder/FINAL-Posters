# Poster #542 -- Construction Workup
## Surface Prep -- Suspension Plasma Spray (SPS)

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Surface prep for SPS is identical to conventional APS -- the substrate sees grit blasting to create an anchor profile, and the APS bond coat is sprayed first. The SPS topcoat goes directly over the bond coat with no additional blasting. Hero visual: grit blast parameter table with profile verification methods.

### ZONE 2 -- Grit Blast Specification Table

**Section label:** `GRIT BLAST SPECIFICATION FOR SPS (= APS STANDARD)` -- Y: 3.1".

**BLOCK B -- Primary Parameter Table**

Y: 3.8" to 10.0".

| Parameter | Typical Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Media | White or brown alumina (Al2O3) | White preferred for aerospace |
| Grit size | 24--36 mesh (coarse) for metals; 60 mesh for thin substrates | Finer for delicate parts |
| Blast pressure | 40--80 PSI (275--550 kPa) | Adjust for substrate hardness |
| Nozzle distance | 100--200 mm (4--8 inches) | Consistent distance critical |
| Blast angle | 60--90 degrees to surface | 90 deg for maximum depth |
| Anchor profile (Ra) | 3--8 um (125--325 uin) | Spec-dependent |
| Surface cleanliness | SSPC-SP 5 / NACE No.1 (White Metal) or SA 3 (ISO 8501) | Premium standard |

**Substrate temperature limits callout (below table):**

| Substrate | Max Temp During Spray | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Low-carbon steel | 150--200 degC | Higher OK if no dimensional concern |
| Tool steel (heat-treated) | 150 degC | Avoid tempering |
| Aluminum alloys | 120--150 degC | Risk of annealing |
| Titanium alloys | 150--200 degC | Oxidation above 400 degC |

### ZONE 3 -- Media Selection + Why Alumina

**Section label:** `MEDIA SELECTION` -- Y: 15.7".

**Left -- BLOCK C: Media Comparison (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Media | Grit Size | Hardness | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| White alumina | 24--60 mesh | Mohs 9 | Aerospace preferred |
| Brown alumina | 24--60 mesh | Mohs 9 | General; lower cost |
| Silicon carbide | 24--60 mesh | Mohs 9.5 | Hard substrates; Ti prep |
| Garnet | 36--80 mesh | Mohs 7--8 | Non-ferrous; less aggressive |

**Right -- BLOCK D: Why Alumina for Aerospace (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Callout box with `#2EC4B6` accent:
- `No ferrous contamination risk on nickel or titanium substrates`
- `Alumina fractures to expose fresh cutting edges (self-sharpening)`
- `Steel grit can embed and cause galvanic corrosion sites`
- `99%+ purity alumina required for critical aerospace applications`

### ZONE 4 -- Profile Verification

**Section label:** `PROFILE VERIFICATION -- THREE METHODS` -- Y: 22.2".

**BLOCK E -- Three Method Cards**

| Method | Description | Precision |
|---|---|---|
| Replica tape (Testex) | Press-o-film tape + micrometer. Field-friendly. | +/- 0.5 um |
| Surface profilometer | Ra, Rz measurement. Lab or portable instrument. | +/- 0.1 um |
| Visual comparison | SSPC-VIS 1 comparator standards. Quick screening. | Qualitative |

**BLOCK F -- Cleanliness Standards**

| Standard | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| SSPC-SP 5 | White Metal Blast | 100% bare metal; no stains, oxides, or mill scale |
| SSPC-SP 10 | Near-White Blast | 95% bare metal; light staining OK |
| ISO 8501 SA 3 | Blast-Cleaning to Visually Clean Steel | Equivalent to SP 5 |

### ZONE 5 -- SPS-Specific Notes

**Section label:** `SPS-SPECIFIC SURFACE PREP NOTES` -- Y: 28.7".

| Card | Note |
|---|---|
| 1 | Grit blast is for the substrate -- the APS bond coat is sprayed onto this profile |
| 2 | No additional blasting between APS bond coat and SPS topcoat |
| 3 | APS bond coat surface is as-sprayed -- its natural roughness provides adhesion for SPS |
| 4 | Profile verification is done BEFORE bond coat application, not before SPS topcoat |
