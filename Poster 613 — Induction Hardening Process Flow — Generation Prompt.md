## Phase 6 -- Induction vs. Flame + Troubleshooting

### Comparison (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `INDUCTION VS. FLAME -- WHICH SURFACE HARDENING?`.

**Left -- Induction (Amber):** Electromagnetic (eddy currents). Excellent precision (+/- 0.005--0.010"). Heating rate 100--1000 F/sec. Seconds per part. No atmosphere required. High-volume, precision. High capital cost. CNC-controlled. Highlight: `Fastest surface hardening method -- seconds per part.`

**Right -- Flame (Teal):** Oxy-fuel flame. Moderate precision (+/- 0.020--0.030"). 50--200 F/sec. Minutes per part. No atmosphere. Large parts, low volume, field repairs. Low capital. Manual or semi-auto. Highlight: `No custom coil needed -- adapts to any geometry.`

### Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| QUENCH CRACKING | Excessive power, sharp corners, severe quench | Control power/time; generous radii; proper quenchant |
| SOFT SPOTS | Non-uniform coupling, no rotation | Verify coil gap; check rotation; verify surface carbon |
| SHALLOW CASE | Frequency too high, power too low, short time | Adjust parameters; verify frequency selection |
| THROUGH-HARDENED | Frequency too low or time too long | Reduce time; increase frequency; redesign coil |

Coral left accent. Fix text in `#27AE60`.
