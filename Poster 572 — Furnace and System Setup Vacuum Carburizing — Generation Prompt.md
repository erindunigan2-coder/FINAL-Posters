## Phase 5 -- Vacuum System

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `THE VACUUM SYSTEM -- PUMPS, PRESSURE, AND LEAK INTEGRITY`.

**Left -- Pump Chain (Teal accent):** Roughing pump (rotary vane, atm to ~1 mbar) -> Roots blower (0.1--10 mbar range, volume for C2H2 evacuation) -> Diffusion/turbo pump (optional, <0.01 mbar). Base pressure: <0.1 mbar before heating.

**Right -- Performance Specs (Amber accent):** Leak rate <5 microns/hr. Pump-down: 15--45 min. O-ring/seal maintenance: inspect before every run, replace on schedule. Vacuum gauges: Pirani (rough), capacitance manometer (process), ionization (high vacuum).

## Phase 6 -- Temperature Capability + Spec Strip

**Temperature (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `TEMPERATURE ADVANTAGE -- WHY LPC RUNS HOTTER`.

Comparison table:

| Parameter | Gas Carburizing | Vacuum (LPC) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard range | 1650--1750 F | 1700--1850 F |
| Maximum practical | 1750 F | 1900 F |
| Upper limit | Grain growth + severe IGO | Grain growth (no IGO) |
| Cycle time for 0.040" ECD | 4--5 hr at 1700 F | 1.5--2.5 hr at 1800 F |
| Time savings | -- | 30--50% shorter |

Why: no O2 = no IGO; IGO is the temperature limiter in gas; without it, push to 1850--1900 F; +100 F = ~2x diffusion. Use fine-grain steels above 1850 F.

Callout bar: Teal tint. `Higher temperature = faster diffusion = shorter cycles = more parts per day. That is the business case for LPC in three equations.`

**Spec Strip (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `AMS 2750 REQUIREMENTS FOR VACUUM FURNACES`. Four cards, Teal accent:
1. FURNACE CLASS -- Class 2 (+/-10 F) aerospace, Class 3 (+/-15 F) general; TUS quarterly

3. THERMOCOUPLES -- Types K or N; sheathed; optical pyrometry as supplement
