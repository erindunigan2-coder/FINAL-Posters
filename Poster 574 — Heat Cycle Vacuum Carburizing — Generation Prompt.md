## Phase 6 -- Distortion Comparison + Grain Growth

**Distortion (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `DISTORTION: LPC WITH HPGQ vs. GAS WITH OIL QUENCH`.

*HPGQ Advantage (Emerald):* No film boiling (oil has vapor blanket = non-uniform cooling). Gas quench: one mechanism (forced convection) = uniform. Controllable: adjust pressure and velocity. Typical: 30--50% less distortion vs. oil.

*The Trade-Off (Amber):* HPGQ H-factor 0.10--0.40 vs. oil 0.25--0.80 -- still less severe. Thick sections >1.5" in lean alloys may not harden. Helium expensive (5--10x N2). Solution: transfer to integrated oil quench.

**Grain Growth Warning (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label in Coral: `GRAIN GROWTH -- THE HIGH-TEMPERATURE TRADE-OFF`. Full-width card, Coral accent, two columns:

*Problem:* Higher temps accelerate grain growth. Large grains reduce fatigue life and toughness. Above 1900 F, grain growth is the limiter.
*Solution:* Al-killed fine-grain steels (ASTM 5+). Microalloy additions (Nb, Ti, V). Verify per ASTM E112 on every qualification load. Reduce temperature, not time.
