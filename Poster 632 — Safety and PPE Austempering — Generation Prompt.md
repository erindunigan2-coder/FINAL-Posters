| Hazard | Source | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Molten salt burns | Salt bath 400--700 F | SEVERE | Full PPE; controlled immersion |

| Radiant heat | Furnace door at 1500+ F | MODERATE | Face shield; minimize door-open time |

## Phase 6 -- Emergency Response + Top 5 Rules

**Emergency cards** (2x2 grid):
1. MOLTEN SALT BURN: Do NOT apply water; dry sterile dressing; call EMS.
2. SALT BATH FIRE: Evacuate; Class D or dry sand; NEVER water.
3. CO / GAS EXPOSURE: Fresh air; SCBA if unconscious; call EMS.
4. MOISTURE EXPLOSION: Evacuate splash zone; wait for cool-down.

**5 Non-Negotiable Rules** (Coral border):
1. PRE-HEAT everything entering molten salt.
2. NEVER introduce moisture.
3. KEEP organics away from nitrate/nitrite salt.
4. WEAR full PPE for every salt bath operation.
5. VERIFY atmosphere purge before endo gas.
