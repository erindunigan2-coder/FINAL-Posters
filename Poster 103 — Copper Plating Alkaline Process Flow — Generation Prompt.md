- **Box 3:** `Steel: 10--30% HCl, 15--30 sec` / `Zinc DC: 1--3% HCl, 5--10 sec` / `Brass: 5--10% H2SO4`. Check: `CRITICAL: Do NOT over-etch zinc die castings` (`#E05C5C`).

- **Box 5:** `Cu: 3--8 g/L` / `Complexant: per TDS` / `pH 8--11` / `110--145 F` / `10--30 ASF`. Purpose: Adherent copper strike (0.1--0.3 mil). Check: `Complexant concentration controls deposit quality`.
- **Box 6:** `Ambient` / `Double overflow or cascade`. Check: `Cu is a contaminant in downstream baths`.
- **Box 7:** `To acid copper: mild H2SO4 dip -> plate` / `To nickel: direct or mild activation` / `Standalone: anti-tarnish dip`. Check: `Strike is rarely the final deposit`.

## Phase 6 -- Non-Cyanide vs. Cyanide Comparison

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `ALKALINE NON-CYANIDE VS. CYANIDE COPPER -- WHY THE SWITCH?`.

**Left -- Alkaline Non-Cyanide Copper:**
Accent `#27AE60`. Subtitle: `The Modern Strike`.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Chemistry | Complexant-based (HEDP, citrate, tartrate) |
| pH | 8--11 |
| Temperature | 110--145 F |
| Cathode efficiency | 40--70% |
| Deposit quality | Matte to semi-bright |
| Throwing power | Good |
| Substrates | Steel, zinc DC, ABS plastic |
| Safety | No cyanide -- standard alkaline handling |
| Waste treatment | Standard hydroxide precipitation |

Bottom: `No cyanide = no HCN risk, no cyanide destruction in waste treatment, no specialized ventilation.` -- `#27AE60`

**Right -- Cyanide Copper (Legacy):**
Accent `#E05C5C`. Subtitle: `Still Used -- For Good Reason`.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Chemistry | CuCN + free NaCN |
| pH | > 12 |
| Cathode efficiency | 30--60% (strike); 60--90% (plate) |
| Deposit quality | Better adhesion on difficult substrates |
| Throwing power | Excellent |
| Safety | ACUTELY LETHAL -- cyanide |
| Waste treatment | Two-stage alkaline chlorination |

Bottom: `Cyanide copper gives superior adhesion on zinc die castings. But the safety and environmental cost is enormous.` -- `#E05C5C`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| POOR ADHESION ON ZINC DC | Over-etching; complexant too low | Reduce acid time (5--10 sec max); check complexant |
| DARK / DISCOLORED | Organic contamination or low complexant | Carbon treat; replenish complexant |
| THIN / SPOTTY COVERAGE | Low CD or low Cu metal | Increase CD; add copper |
| pH DRIFT (FALLING) | Acid drag-in from activation | Improve pre-plate rinse; adjust pH with NaOH |
