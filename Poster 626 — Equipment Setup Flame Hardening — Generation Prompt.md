## Phase 5 -- Flame Type Guide

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `FLAME ADJUSTMENT -- THREE TYPES AND WHEN TO USE EACH`. Three side-by-side panels.

| Flame Type | Accent | Use for Flame Hardening? | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEUTRAL | `#27AE60` | YES -- PREFERRED | Equal O2 and fuel; sharp inner cone; neither adds nor removes carbon |
| REDUCING (CARBURIZING) | `#E8A020` | ACCEPTABLE | Slight excess fuel; helps prevent surface oxidation |
| OXIDIZING | `#E05C5C` | NO -- AVOID | Excess oxygen; decarburizes the steel surface |
