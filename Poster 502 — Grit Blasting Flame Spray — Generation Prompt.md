## Phase 4 -- Blast Parameters + Why Rougher (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.0". Section label: `GRIT BLAST SPECIFICATION FOR FLAME SPRAY`.

**Parameter Table (Y: 5.0"-10.5"):** 7-row table, full width.

| Parameter | Typical Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Media | Angular alumina (Al2O3) or steel grit | Alumina preferred for non-ferrous substrates |
| Grit size | 16-36 mesh | Coarser than HVOF/APS due to lower particle energy |
| Blast pressure | 60-100 PSI (415-690 kPa) | More aggressive than APS or HVOF |
| Nozzle distance | 150-250 mm (6-10 in) | Closer = deeper profile |
| Blast angle | 60-90 degrees | 90 deg for max depth; 60-75 deg for cleaning |
| Anchor profile (Ra) | 4-12 microns (175-500 uin) | Rougher profile needed vs. higher-velocity processes |
| Surface cleanliness | SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal) | Per AWS C2.18 for corrosion protection |

Header fill `#3A4055`. Alternating rows.

**"Why Rougher?" Callout (Y: 11.0"-13.5"):** Full-width, top accent `#E8A020`.

Left: `LOW VELOCITY = LOW IMPACT ENERGY` -- Flame: 40-200 m/s; HVOF: 600-1000 m/s; Plasma: 200-600 m/s. JetBrains Mono `#F0EDE8`.
Right: `ROUGHER PROFILE COMPENSATES` -- More surface area, deeper valleys, Ra 4-12 um vs. 3-8 um for APS. Inter Medium `#27AE60`.

## Phase 5 -- Media Selection + Profile Verification

### Media Selection (Y: 14.0"-20.5")

Section label: `BLAST MEDIA SELECTION`. Three side-by-side cards.

| Media | Accent | Hardness | Best For | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WHITE ALUMINA | `#2EC4B6` | Mohs 9 | Aerospace; non-ferrous; no Fe contamination | Higher |
| ANGULAR STEEL GRIT | `#E8A020` | Mohs 7-8 | Infrastructure; bridge work; structural steel | Lower (recyclable) |
| GARNET | `#27AE60` | Mohs 7-8 | Non-ferrous; less aggressive; field work | Moderate |

Bottom warning: `Steel grit on titanium or nickel alloys = galvanic corrosion risk. Use alumina for non-ferrous work.` `#E05C5C`.

### Profile Verification (Y: 20.5"-27.0")

Section label: `VERIFY YOUR PROFILE -- THREE METHODS`. Three cards.

| Method | How | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| TESTEX REPLICA TAPE | Press tape into surface; measure with micrometer | Field-friendly; +/- 0.5 mil |
| SURFACE PROFILOMETER | Stylus traces surface; measures Ra, Rz directly | Lab-grade; +/- 0.1 um |
| VISUAL COMPARATOR | Compare to SSPC-VIS 1 reference photos | Quick pass/fail; subjective |

Standards strip: `SSPC-SP 5 = White Metal | SSPC-SP 10 = Near-White | ISO 8501 Sa 3 = White Metal equiv`.

## Phase 6 -- Blast-Related Failures

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Section label: `WHEN BLASTING GOES WRONG`. Four cards, accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| POOR BOND STRENGTH | Insufficient profile depth (Ra too low) | Re-blast with coarser media or higher pressure |
| EMBEDDED MEDIA | Blast angle too steep or media too soft | Use angular media at 60-75 deg |
| SUBSTRATE DAMAGE | Excessive pressure on thin material | Reduce pressure; increase standoff; finer grit |
| FLASH RUST | Blast-to-spray time exceeded in humid conditions | Spray within 2-4 hours; monitor humidity |
