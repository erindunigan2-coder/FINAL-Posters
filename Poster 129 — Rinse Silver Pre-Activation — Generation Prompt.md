## Phase 6 -- Rinse Efficiency

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `SINGLE VS. CASCADE RINSE`.

**Left -- Single Rinse (accent `#E8A020`):** Water usage HIGH / Removal 95--97% / Risk: marginal for sensitive processes.

**Right -- Cascade (accent `#27AE60`):** Water usage LOW (50--90% less) / Removal >99.5% / Two or three tanks in series.

Banner: `For silver plating: cascade rinse is strongly recommended.`

## Phase 7 -- When Rinse Fails

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHEN THE RINSE FAILS`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ALKALINE CARRY-OVER | Insufficient rinse time | Neutralizes acid activation |
| 2 | pH CREEP IN ACID | Repeated alkaline drag-in | Acid becomes ineffective |
| 3 | CONTAMINATION TO STRIKE | Alkaline/particulate reaches strike | Immersion deposit forms; peels |
| 4 | WATER SPOTS | Hard water minerals | Adhesion defects under silver |
