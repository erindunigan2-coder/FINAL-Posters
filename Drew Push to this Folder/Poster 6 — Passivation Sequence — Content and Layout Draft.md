## Section 1 — Watson Flag Status and Design Decisions

**Status: THREE FLAGS — all Drew confirmation, non-blocking.**

**Flag 1 (Drew):** Confirm salt spray hour ranges — industry-typical values used; Drew may narrow to A Brite NZP P1/P2-specific data.

**Flag 2 (Drew):** Confirm drying temperature limits — trivalent: 150 deg F (65 deg C) used here; some sources allow up to 180 deg F.

**Flag 3 (Drew):** Confirm whether hex self-healing property should be featured (it is a genuine technical advantage of hex, but may raise questions about why the industry is switching to trivalent).

**Design decisions:**

- **HERO visual: the Color Spectrum Strip.** A horizontal strip showing passivation colors in order of increasing protection — Clear/Blue, Yellow, Black, Olive Drab — with salt spray hours below each. This is the most visually striking and immediately useful element. Nobody has published this as a clean graphic.

- **Process flow as secondary visual.** A horizontal flow showing the full post-plating sequence (Zinc Plate -> Rinse -> Bright Dip -> Rinse -> Passivate -> Rinse -> Dry). This anchors the poster in the production process.

- **Trivalent vs. hexavalent presented honestly.** The poster covers both systems with RoHS compliance clearly flagged. The self-healing property of hex is included because it is technically important and well-documented — omitting it would compromise accuracy.

- **Contamination is the practical takeaway.** Watson's data on iron contamination killing passivation baths is the kind of actionable knowledge that saves operators money. A contamination warning callout is essential.

### BLOCK E — Operating Parameters (Zone 4)

**Two-column layout: Trivalent (left) | Hexavalent (right)**

**Left — Trivalent Parameters:**

Title (Barlow SemiBold, 20 pt, `#27AE60`):
> TRIVALENT OPERATING PARAMETERS

Table (3 columns: Parameter | Range | Notes):

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Chemistry | Cr³⁺ sulfate or chloride based |
| pH | 1.5-2.5 (up to 3.0 for thicker films) |
| Temperature | 70-140 deg F (21-60 deg C) |
| Immersion | 30-90 sec (clear); 60-180 sec (thick) |
| Agitation | Gentle — avoid turbulence |
| Max dry temp | < 150 deg F (65 deg C) |

Drew's note callout (Inter Regular, 13 pt, `#E8A020`):
> Raising pH to 2.5 allows thicker film build — part stays longer without zinc degradation.

**Right — Hexavalent Parameters:**

Title (Barlow SemiBold, 20 pt, `#E05C5C`):
> HEXAVALENT OPERATING PARAMETERS

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Chemistry | CrO₃-based (dichromate, chromic acid) |
| pH | 1.0-2.0 (clear); 0.5-1.5 (yellow) |
| Temperature | 70-100 deg F (21-38 deg C) |
| Immersion | 5-30 sec (clear); 15-60 sec (yellow); 30-120 sec (OD) |
| Agitation | Gentle rack movement |
| Max dry temp | < 300 deg F (150 deg C) |

### BLOCK F — Contamination Thresholds Table (Zone 5 — Left)

**Section label (Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 22 pt, `#F0EDE8`):**

> WHAT KILLS THE PASSIVATION BATH

Table (4 columns):

| Contaminant | Source | Threshold | Effect |
|-------------|--------|-----------|--------|
| Iron (Fe³⁺) | Steel racks, parts | >100-150 ppm | Yellow/brown discoloration |
| Zinc (Zn²⁺) | Drag-in from plating | High levels | Film discoloration |
| Copper (Cu²⁺) | Drag-in, rack corrosion | >30 ppm | Darkens film |
| Organics | Brightener drag-in | Variable | Spotty passivation |
| Chloride | Acid zinc KCl drag-in | Variable | Accelerates zinc attack |

**Prevention callout (Inter Medium, 15 pt, `#E8A020`):**

> Drag-in is the #1 source of passivation bath contamination. Rinse thoroughly between plating and passivation.

### BLOCK G — Post-Passivation Care (Zone 5 — Right)

**Two stacked callout boxes:**

**Rinse Warning (Teal `#2EC4B6` accent):**

Callout box: fill `#1E2435`, border 1.5 pt `#2EC4B6`, corner radius 6 pt

Title (Barlow SemiBold, 16 pt, `#2EC4B6`):
> RINSE AFTER PASSIVATION

Body (Inter Regular, 14 pt, `#F0EDE8`):
> Use cold to warm water — NOT hot. Minimal agitation. Brief immersion. Aggressive rinsing or hot water damages the freshly formed film.

**Drying Warning (Amber `#E8A020` accent):**

Callout box: fill `#1E2435`, border 1.5 pt `#E8A020`, corner radius 6 pt

Title (Barlow SemiBold, 16 pt, `#E8A020`):
> DRYING TEMPERATURE

Body (Inter Regular, 14 pt, `#F0EDE8`):
> Trivalent: below 150 deg F (65 deg C). Hexavalent: below 300 deg F (150 deg C). Excessive heat destroys corrosion resistance.
