## Phase 5 -- Blind Hole Advantage

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `THE BLIND HOLE ADVANTAGE -- WHY LPC EXCELS AT COMPLEX GEOMETRY`.

**Left -- The Physics (Teal accent):** `MEAN FREE PATH EFFECT`. At atmospheric pressure: mean free path ~0.0003" -- gas can't penetrate deep holes. At 5--15 mbar: mean free path ~0.1--0.3" -- acetylene travels farther, penetrates blind holes, bores, internal passages.

**Right -- Practical Impact (Emerald accent):** Parts that benefit: internal splines, cross-drilled shafts, keyways, fuel injectors, any L/D > 3:1. Gas carburizing workaround: aggressive agitation, fan speed, orientation -- still may fail. LPC: low pressure naturally penetrates; multiple pulses ensure saturation; uniformity verified by simulation.

Callout bar: Teal tint. `If your part has a blind hole deeper than 3x its diameter, vacuum carburizing will produce a more uniform case than gas carburizing. That is physics, not marketing.`

## Phase 6 -- Temperature Measurement + Loading Rules

**Temperature (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `TEMPERATURE MEASUREMENT IN VACUUM`.

*Thermocouples (Amber):* Type K or N, MUST be sheathed (bare wire contaminates vacuum). Load TC in heaviest section. Per AMS 2750. Limitation: thermal mass lag.

*Optical Pyrometry (Teal):* Non-contact through viewport. Real-time surface temp. No contamination risk. Limitations: clean viewport required, emissivity must be known. Trend: increasingly used alongside TCs.

**Loading Rules (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `LOADING RULES FOR VACUUM CARBURIZING`. Four cards, Teal accent:
1. SPACING -- min 0.25--0.5"; dense loads need more cycles; verified by simulation
2. CFC TRAYS -- handle carefully (brittle at edges); inspect for cracks every load
3. ORIENTATION -- bores open-end-up for C2H2 penetration; drain holes clear
4. LOAD WEIGHT -- CFC lighter = faster heat-up; max still limited by furnace + HPGQ capacity
