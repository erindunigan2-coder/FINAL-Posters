## Phase 4 -- Process Flow Diagram (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section label: `THE COMPLETE PROCESS -- STAGE BY STAGE`.

Eight-stage U-flow diagram. Top row 4 boxes L-to-R, bottom row 4 boxes R-to-L. Each box: W: 5.0", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, radius 8, top accent 4 pt.

**Top Row (Y: 3.8"-8.3"):**

| Stage | X | Accent | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Solvent Clean | 0.5" | `#2EC4B6` | SSPC-SP1, wipe/vapor degrease. Remove oils, greases. Check: No visible oil residue before blast. |
| 2. Surface Prep (Blast) | 6.0" | `#2EC4B6` | SSPC-SP10 min (IOZ: SP5). Profile 1.5-3.0 mils. CRITICAL: Blast within 4-8 hr of priming (`#E05C5C`). |
| 3. Blow-Down | 11.5" | `#2EC4B6` | Oil-free compressed air. ASTM D4285 blotter test. Check: Verify air is oil-free. |
| 4. Pretreatment | 17.0" | `#E8A020` | IOZ: NONE (blast IS prep). Epoxy: optional iron phos. CAUTION: Phosphate under IOZ defeats galvanic (`#E05C5C`). |

**Bottom Row (Y: 9.5"-14.0"):**

| Stage | X | Accent | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5. Primer Application | 17.0" | `#E8A020` | IOZ: 2.5-4.0 mils. OZ: 2.0-3.5 mils. Epoxy: 1.0-3.0 mils. Continuous agitation -- zinc settles rapidly. |
| 6. Flash / Set | 11.5" | `#27AE60` | IOZ: 30-60 min (50% RH). Stripe coat edges first. CRITICAL: Too thick IOZ = mud cracking (`#E05C5C`). |
| 7. Cure | 6.0" | `#27AE60` | IOZ: 24-72 hr (moisture). OZ: 7-14 days. IOZ requires > 40% RH to cure. |
| 8. Inspection & Recoat | 0.5" | `#C8D0D8` | DFT: ASTM D7091 / SSPC-PA 2. Adhesion: D4541. Mist coat required over IOZ before full topcoat. |

**Legend strip** (Y: 14.3"): `#2EC4B6` Cleaning & Prep | `#E8A020` Pretreatment & Application | `#27AE60` Flash & Cure | `#C8D0D8` Inspection.

| Stage | Method | Profile/DFT | Time | Type | Key Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Solvent Clean | SSPC-SP1 | -- | Per soil | All | No residue |
| 2. Blast | SP10/SP5 | 1.5-3.0 mils | Per spec | IOZ: SP5 | Profile + cleanliness |
| 3. Blow-Down | Oil-free air/vacuum | -- | Immediate | All | D4285 blotter |
| 4. Pretreatment | None (IOZ) / optional (epoxy) | -- | -- | Varies | No phosphate under IOZ |

| 6. Flash | Solvent evaporation | -- | 30-60 min | IOZ critical | No mud cracking |
| 7. Cure | Hydrolysis/amine | -- | 24 hr-14 days | Varies | RH > 40% for IOZ |
| 8. Inspection | DFT + adhesion | Per PA 2 | -- | All | Mist coat before topcoat |

### IOZ vs OZ (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `ZINC-RICH PRIMER -- INORGANIC VS. ORGANIC`. Two side-by-side panels.

**Left -- IOZ (accent `#27AE60`):** Ethyl silicate binder. 75-85% zinc. 2.5-4.0 mils. SP5. Hydrolysis cure 24-72 hr. B117: 1,500-3,000+ hr. Mud cracking > 5 mils. Mist coat required. `Best long-term galvanic protection -- bridges, ships, offshore.`

**Right -- OZ (accent `#E8A020`):** Epoxy/PU binder. 65-80% zinc. 2.0-3.5 mils. SP10. Binder cure 7-14 days. B117: 500-1,500 hr. Brush/roll for repair. No mist coat needed. `Easier to apply and repair -- preferred for field maintenance.`

## Phase 6 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| MUD CRACKING | IOZ > 5 mils DFT | Reduce to 2.5-4.0 mils; thinner coats |
| POOR ADHESION | Insufficient profile / contamination | Re-blast to SP10/SP5; check salts |
| FLASH RUST | Blast-to-prime delay too long | Prime within 4 hr in humid conditions |
| TOPCOAT BUBBLING | Solvent trapped in porous IOZ | Apply mist coat first; allow full cure |
