## Phase 6 -- Double Rinse + Pitting Risk

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Why Double Rinse:** Single = 90% removal. Double = 99%. AMS 2473 / MIL-DTL-5541F require double rinse. Contaminated deox = uneven smut = reject.

**Right -- Pitting:** Chloride ions penetrate Al oxide and initiate localized corrosion. Pitting is IRREVERSIBLE. Prevention: DI/RO water.
