## Phase 6 -- Emergency Procedures

Y: 21.0" to 28.5". Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE -- ACT FAST, ACT RIGHT`.

2x2 grid of glass cards (W ~11.0", H ~3.0"), each with 4 pt coral top accent.

**Skin Contact:**
1. Flush immediately with large volumes of water
2. Continue flushing minimum 15 minutes
3. Do NOT attempt to neutralize with acid (exothermic reaction)
4. Remove contaminated clothing while flushing
5. Seek medical attention

**Eye Contact:**
1. Flush with eyewash station for minimum 15 minutes
2. Hold eyelids open during flush
3. Seek immediate medical attention
4. Do not rub eyes

**Ingestion:**
1. Do NOT induce vomiting
2. Drink water or milk to dilute
3. Seek immediate medical attention
4. Bring SDS to medical provider

**Chemical Spill:**
1. Contain with absorbent material
2. Neutralize residual with dilute acid (vinegar or citric)
3. Wear full PPE during cleanup
4. Dispose per local regulations

Titles: Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E05C5C`. Steps: Inter 12 pt numbered.
