# Poster #47 -- Construction Workup
## Zinc-Nickel Plating -- Process Flow

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This is the cluster overview poster for EP-03: Zinc-Nickel Plating. Zn-Ni is the high-performance corrosion-resistant alloy that replaced cadmium plating in automotive and is rapidly growing in aerospace. The headline number: 12--16% nickel in the deposit delivers 5--10x the salt spray resistance of plain zinc with proper passivation (1,000+ hours to red rust). This poster shows the complete 8-stage process and positions Zn-Ni as the performance leader.

Design philosophy: U-flow diagram as the hero (same as Posters #31 and #39), but the comparison callout is "Why Zinc-Nickel?" -- comparing Zn-Ni against plain zinc (not acid vs. alkaline). The performance story is the centerpiece: Zn-Ni is the answer when plain zinc can't meet the salt spray spec.

- Badge: `STAGE 1`, fill `#2EC4B6`

- Parameters: `140--180 F` / `4--8 oz/gal` / `EC: 20--75 ASF`

*Box 2 -- Rinse (Pre-Activation):*
- Badge: `STAGE 2`, fill `#2EC4B6`
- Name: `Rinse` / Subtitle: `Pre-Activation`
- Parameters: `Ambient` / `Double overflow preferred`
- Purpose: `Remove all alkaline cleaner residues`
- Check: `< 500 uS/cm conductivity`

*Box 3 -- Acid Activation:*
- Badge: `STAGE 3`, fill `#E8A020`
- Name: `Acid Activation`
- Parameters: `HCl 10--30% v/v (alk bath)` / `HCl or H2SO4 5--15% (acid bath)` / `15--60 sec`
- Purpose: `Remove surface oxides for alloy adhesion`
- Check: `CAUTION: H-embrittlement -- Zn-Ni parts are often high-strength steel` (Coral)

*Box 4 -- Rinse (Pre-Plate):*
- Badge: `STAGE 4`, fill `#2EC4B6`
- Name: `Rinse` / Subtitle: `Pre-Plate`
- Parameters: `Ambient` / `Single overflow minimum`
- Purpose: `Prevent acid drag-in to Zn-Ni bath`
- Check: `Acid into alkaline Zn-Ni bath crashes pH`

*Box 5 -- Zn-Ni Plate (Main Tank):*
- Badge: `STAGE 5`, fill `#27AE60`
- Name: `Zinc-Nickel Plate`
- Parameters: `Alkaline: Zn 8--10, Ni 1.0--1.5 g/L` / `NaOH 120--140 g/L` / `75--85 F` / `Alloy: 12--16% Ni`
- Purpose: `Co-deposit Zn-Ni alloy onto substrate`
- Check: `Ni% is THE critical parameter -- XRF every shift`

*Box 6 -- Rinse (Post-Plate):*
- Badge: `STAGE 6`, fill `#2EC4B6`
- Name: `Rinse` / Subtitle: `Post-Plate`
- Parameters: `Ambient` / `Double counterflow`
- Purpose: `Remove alkaline drag-out before passivate`
- Check: `CRITICAL: NaOH drag-in destroys passivate` (Coral)

*Box 7 -- Passivate (Trivalent):*
- Badge: `STAGE 7`, fill `#E8A020`
- Name: `Passivate` / Subtitle: `Trivalent Chromate`
- Parameters: `pH 1.5--3.0` / `30--90 sec` / `70--110 F`
- Purpose: `Corrosion-resistant conversion coating`
- Check: `Hex chromate essentially never used on Zn-Ni`

*Box 8 -- Seal + Dry/Cure:*
- Badge: `STAGE 8`, fill `#E8A020`
- Name: `Seal + Dry / Cure`
- Parameters: `Sealer: 150--180 F, 30--60 sec` / `Cure: 150--170 F, 15--20 min`
- Purpose: `Seal passivate + cure for maximum salt spray`
- Check: `Sealer is almost always required for Zn-Ni`

## Design Notes

Zinc-nickel is the prestige process in this series -- the poster should feel like a step up from the plain zinc posters in authority and ambition. The 1,000+ hour salt spray number is the hook. The comparison against plain zinc makes the value proposition visceral: 5--10x the corrosion resistance at 2--3x the cost.

The parameter table defaults to alkaline Zn-Ni because it's the industry workhorse for automotive barrel work. The acid variant is footnoted for completeness. If space permits, the acid variant deserves more than a footnote -- but for a process flow overview poster, the alkaline numbers are correct as the primary reference.

Key specs to call out: ASTM B841 (general), AMS 2417 (aerospace), GM 6046M / GMW 3044 (GM), VW TL 244 (VW), Ford WSS-M21P30-A4 (Ford). These should appear in the footer or as a sidebar spec strip if the generation prompt architect (Elara) has room.
