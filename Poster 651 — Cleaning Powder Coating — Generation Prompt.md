3. Conversion Coating (Amber): iron phos or zirconium, 100--140 F, 25--75 mg/ft2.
4. Fresh Water Rinse (Silver): DI/RO, < 50 uS/cm.

## Phase 5 -- Cleaner Chemistry + Critical Controls

**Left -- Cleaner Components**: NaOH, metasilicate, surfactants, EDTA/GLDA/citrate, phosphate builders. Warning: `Silicate residues cause adhesion failure.`

**Right -- Critical Controls**: Oil breakthrough (water-break test ASTM F22), silicate residues (switch to silicate-free), conductivity monitoring (rinse < 200 uS/cm between stages, final DI < 50 uS/cm).

---

## Phase 6 -- Rinse Quality + Defect Grid

**Conductivity Table**: after cleaner < 500 uS/cm, after conversion < 50 uS/cm, final < 50 uS/cm. **Water-Break Test** (ASTM F22): spray, observe 30 sec -- continuous sheet = PASS, beading = FAIL. `The single most reliable go/no-go test.`

**Defect Grid** (3x2): oil fish-eye, silicate haze, osmotic blistering, poor conversion coating, adhesion loss, flash rust.
