## Phase 5 -- Parameters + HCl vs H2SO4

Y: 14.0" to 22.0". Section: `ACTIVATION PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameter Table (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Acid type | HCl (hydrochloric) -- strongly preferred |
| HCl concentration | 10--50% v/v (typically 25--33% for steel) |
| H2SO4 alternative | 5--15% v/v (less common for acid zinc) |
| Temperature | Ambient (65--85 F / 18--29 C) |
| Time (light oxide) | 15--60 sec |
| Time (heavy scale) | 1--5 min |
| Agitation | None to mild -- part movement only |
| Rinse after | Immediate -- do not let parts dry |

**Right -- HCl vs H2SO4 Comparison (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

**Top -- HCl (accent `#E8A020`):**
- Standard for acid zinc lines -- used in >90% of installations
- Dissolves iron oxide faster than H2SO4
- Forms soluble FeCl3 -- no surface residues
- Less hydrogen absorption (lower H-embrittlement risk)
- Fumes -- ventilation required (OSHA PEL 5 ppm ceiling)
- Attacks copper and brass -- avoid on mixed-metal racks

**Bottom -- H2SO4 (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
- Lower fuming than HCl -- better for enclosed areas
- Slower oxide removal -- longer immersion needed
- Forms insoluble FeSO4 on surface -- can cause adhesion issues
- Higher risk of hydrogen absorption on steel
- More common in alkaline zinc pre-treatment than acid zinc
- More economical for high-volume (lower acid cost)

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 22.0" to 28.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT ACTIVATION`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under-activation | Zinc peels, poor adhesion | Time too short or acid too weak | Extend time; check acid concentration |
| Over-activation | Rough zinc, pitting | Time too long or acid too strong | Reduce time; dilute acid |
| H-embrittlement | Delayed fracture (high-strength parts) | Hydrogen absorbed during acid contact | Limit to 15--30 sec; bake at 375 F within 4 hr |
| Smut on surface | Dark residue after activation | Carbon smut from high-C steel or cast iron | Add proprietary de-smutter; light mechanical action |
| Flash rust | Orange tint before reaching zinc tank | Parts dried between activation and rinse | Immediate transfer; never let parts air-dry |

## Phase 7 -- H-Embrittlement Warning + Safety

Y: 28.0" to 32.5". Section: `CRITICAL SAFETY -- HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT` `#E05C5C`.

**Left -- H-Embrittlement Panel (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**
FULL border 2pt `#E05C5C`. Title: `HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT WARNING` `#E05C5C`.
- High-strength steel (>= 145 ksi / >= 31 HRC) absorbs hydrogen during acid contact
- Atomic hydrogen diffuses into the steel lattice and causes delayed brittle fracture
- REQUIREMENTS: Limit to 15--30 sec max; bake 375 F (191 C) within 4 hours of plating; hold 4--24 hours; never skip the bake
- Spec: `ASTM B850, AMS 2759/9` `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Acid Safety (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):**
Title: `ACID HANDLING SAFETY` `#E8A020`.
- HCl fumes: corrosive -- hood ventilation required
- OSHA PEL: HCl 5 ppm ceiling
- H2SO4: exothermic on dilution -- add acid to water, NEVER reverse
- Chemical splash goggles + face shield
- Acid-resistant gloves and apron
- Eyewash + safety shower within 10 sec
- Neutralize spills with soda ash (Na2CO3)
