## Phase 4 -- "Why E-Coat Is Different" Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `FLASH & LEVELING -- WHY E-COAT BREAKS THE RULES`. Three columns.

**Liquid Spray (`#C8D0D8`):** Flash 5--15 min, solvent evaporates, leveling via tail solvents. Orange peel if solvents too fast. TE 25--70%.

**Powder Coating (`#C8D0D8`):** No solvent. Gel-flow-cure in oven. Orange peel if flow too short. TE 60--70% (95--98% with reclaim).

**E-Coat (`#E8A020`):** Film deposited by electric field -- already in final position. No solvent in deposited film. No leveling needed. Orange peel NOT an issue. Flash zone = drain + blow-off. TE >99%. Highlight: `The electric field does the leveling. Physics, not chemistry, controls uniformity.`

## Phase 6 -- Material Efficiency + Environmental

Y: 21.8" to 26.8". Section label: `MATERIAL EFFICIENCY -- THE E-COAT ADVANTAGE`.

**Left -- `PAINT UTILIZATION BREAKDOWN` (`#27AE60`):**
First-pass: 70--85% / UF recovery: +10--25% / Total: >99% / Waste: <1%. Large stat: `>99% PAINT UTILIZATION` 28pt `#27AE60`. `No other coating method comes close.`

**Right -- `ENVIRONMENTAL PROFILE` (`#2EC4B6`):**
VOC from application: Near zero. VOC from cure: Moderate (blocking agent, captured by afterburner). Wastewater: Minimal (closed-loop). Overspray waste: Zero. `Only significant emission is blocking agent VOC during bake.`

## Phase 6B -- Troubleshooting Strip

Y: 27.8" to 32.3". 4 cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| DRIP MARKS AFTER BAKE | Pooled coating in recesses | Optimize body angle; verify drain holes |
| WATER SPOTS | DI contaminated or conductivity high | Verify DI < 20 uS/cm |
| POOR PAINT RECOVERY | UF membranes fouled | Clean/replace; check trans-membrane pressure |
| CONTAMINATION IN BAKE | Oil/silicone carry-over | Audit upstream cleaning; check for leaks |
