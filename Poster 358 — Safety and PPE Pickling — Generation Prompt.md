## Phase 6 -- Hydrogen Gas Explosion Warning

Y: 20.5" to 24.5". Full-width coral-tinted glass banner with warning triangle icon.

Title: `HYDROGEN GAS -- EXPLOSION HAZARD` (32 pt `#E05C5C`)

`Acid + iron = hydrogen gas. H2 is explosive at 4-75% in air -- the widest flammable range of any common gas. A single spark near an unventilated pickle tank can cause a catastrophic explosion.`

`VENTILATION IS NOT OPTIONAL. Local exhaust ventilation at the tank rim is required for ALL acid pickling operations.` (`#E8A020`)

## Phase 7 -- Emergency Procedures

Y: 24.5" to 32.5". Section label: `EMERGENCY FIRST AID`.

2x2 grid of glass cards (coral accent):

**Skin Contact:** Flush 15+ min. Do NOT neutralize acid on skin. Remove clothing. Seek medical attention.

**Eye Contact:** Eyewash 15 min. Hold eyelids open. Seek IMMEDIATE attention.

**HCl Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. Administer O2 if available. Do NOT re-enter without respiratory protection.

**Acid Spill:** Contain with absorbent. Neutralize with soda ash or lime. Ventilate. Do NOT use water on large H2SO4 spills (exothermic).
