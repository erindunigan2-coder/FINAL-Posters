## Phase 4 -- Three Pillars (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `THE THREE PILLARS OF PART PREPARATION -- VACUUM EDITION`.

Three boxes, H: 8.8":

**Cleaning (Teal):** Clean AND Dry -- Both Non-Negotiable. Standard: oil-free, grease-free, scale-free. Vacuum-specific: COMPLETELY DRY (moisture contaminates pump oil, degrades vacuum, causes outgassing). FORBIDDEN: chlorinated solvents (degrades pump oil, toxic byproducts), silicone lubricants. Recommended: hydrocarbon solvent, alkaline wash + thorough rinse, forced-air or vacuum dry, water-break test then verify DRY.

**Masking (Amber):** Copper Plate + Mechanical Options. Cu electroplate 0.001--0.002" (same as gas, still the standard). Mechanical masking: tight-fitting caps/plugs MORE EFFECTIVE in vacuum (low pressure = no gas penetration past tight seals). Stop-off paint: verify vacuum-compatible (no volatile binders).

**Surface Condition (Emerald):** Same Standards, Better Outcome. Acceptable: machined, ground, scale removed. Not acceptable: rust, heavy scale, EDM recast, decarburized layer. THE PAYOFF: no IGO = grinding stock REDUCED by 0.001--0.002" per surface = less total machining = cost savings.

## Phase 5 -- Vacuum-Specific Requirements

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `WHAT'S DIFFERENT ABOUT PREP FOR VACUUM?`

**Left -- Critical Differences (Coral accent):** `VACUUM-CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS`. 1. Absolute dryness (degrades pump oil, increases pump-down, causes outgassing). 2. No chlorinated solvents (HCl, toxic chlorine, pump contamination). 3. No silicone (SiO2 on hot surfaces, cross-contaminates loads). 4. Outgassing awareness (volatiles evaporate, contaminate chamber/viewport).

**Right -- Best Practices (Emerald accent):** Cleaning sequence: alkaline soak -> hot rinse (2 stages) -> forced-air dry (150+ F) -> vacuum dry (best) -> visual inspection. Storage: process within 4 hours; re-clean if delayed; clean kraft paper cover. Handling: clean gloves only; no bare-hand contact; fingerprints = non-uniform carburizing.

## Phase 6 -- Grinding Stock Comparison + Checklist

**IGO Advantage (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `THE IGO ADVANTAGE -- LESS GRINDING, LESS MACHINING, LESS COST`.

*Gas Carburizing (Amber):* IGO layer 0.0005--0.001". Grinding stock 0.005--0.015" per surface. AGMA requires min 0.002" removal. More machining before and after.

*Vacuum Carburizing (Emerald):* IGO layer ZERO. Grinding stock 0.003--0.010" per surface (reduced by 0.001--0.002"). Some specs allow as-carburized surface. Savings compound on high-volume parts.

Callout bar: Emerald tint. `Zero IGO is not just a metallurgical advantage -- it's a manufacturing cost advantage. Less grinding stock = less total machining on every part.`

**Checklist (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `PRE-LOAD INSPECTION CHECKLIST -- VACUUM CARBURIZING`. Eight items, checkbox border `#2EC4B6`.

Left: Parts clean, parts COMPLETELY DRY, masked areas verified, no chlorinated solvent residue.
Right: Surface condition per drawing, clean gloves only, parts identified/tracked, time since cleaning < 4 hours.
