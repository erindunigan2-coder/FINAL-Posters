**Box 2 -- Rinse (Post-Clean):** Badge `STAGE 2` `#2EC4B6`. Params: `Ambient` / `Overflow or spray`. Purpose: `Remove cleaner and surfactant residues`. Check: `Surfactant carryover contaminates pickle and oxide bath`

**Box 3 -- Acid Pickle:** Badge `STAGE 3` `#E8A020`. Params: `HCl 20--50% v/v, ambient` / `or H2SO4 10--25%, 120--160 F` / `2--15 min`. Purpose: `Remove rust, scale, passivation film`. Check: `Do not over-pickle -- roughens surface` `#E05C5C`

**Box 4 -- Rinse (Pre-Oxide):** Badge `STAGE 4` `#2EC4B6`. Params: `Ambient` / `Thorough immersion`. Purpose: `Remove all acid before hot caustic bath`. Check: `SAFETY: Water into 285 F caustic = violent spattering` `#E05C5C`

**Box 5 -- Black Oxide Bath:** Badge `STAGE 5` `#27AE60`. Params: `NaOH: 600--900 g/L` / `NaNO2: 30--75 g/L` / `280--295 F (138--146 C)` / `15--30 min`. Purpose: `Convert surface iron to magnetite (Fe3O4)`. Check: `< 275 F = RED ROUGE (not black)` `#E05C5C`

**Box 6 -- Cold Rinse:** Badge `STAGE 6` `#2EC4B6`. Params: `Ambient` / `1--2 min`. Purpose: `Remove bulk caustic and dissolved salts`. Check: `Parts at ~285 F -- steam and thermal shock expected`

**Box 7 -- Hot Rinse:** Badge `STAGE 7` `#2EC4B6`. Params: `180--200 F (82--93 C)` / `1--2 min`. Purpose: `Further removes caustic; heats part for oil dip`. Check: `Optional: chromate dip here for extra protection`

**Box 8 -- Oil / Wax Seal:** Badge `STAGE 8` `#E8A020`. Params: `150--180 F (66--82 C)` / `2--5 min immersion`. Purpose: `Fill magnetite pores -- corrosion protection`. Check: `MANDATORY: bare black oxide = < 1 hr salt spray` `#E05C5C`

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `AT-A-GLANCE PARAMETERS`.

| Stage | Chemistry | Temp | Time | Key Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Alkaline Clean | Alk cleaner 4--8 oz/gal | 160--190 F | 5--15 min | Water-break-free |
| 2. Rinse | Fresh water | Ambient | 1--2 min | No surfactant carryover |

| 8. Oil/Wax Seal | Water-displacing oil or wax | 150--180 F | 2--5 min | MANDATORY for protection |

## Phase 6 -- Hot Alkaline vs. Room Temp

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHY HOT ALKALINE? -- CLASS 1 VS. CLASS 2`.

Two panels:

**Left -- HOT ALKALINE (CLASS 1)** (accent `#27AE60`): Subtitle: `The Industry Standard`. Operating temp 280--295 F / NaOH + NaNO2 + NaNO3 / True magnetite (Fe3O4) / Excellent adhesion / 0.5--2.5 um / < 0.05 mil dimensional change / 24--100+ hr SST (oiled) / Deep lustrous blue-black / MIL-DTL-13924 Class 1. Highlight: `True conversion coating -- magnetite is part of the metal surface`

**Right -- ROOM TEMP (CLASS 2)** (accent `#E05C5C`): Subtitle: `Not Recommended`. Ambient / Selenium dioxide or copper selenite / Deposited copper selenide (NOT magnetite) / Poor adhesion / Surface deposit only / 2--8 hr SST (oiled) / Black but non-lustrous / MIL-DTL-13924 Class 2. Highlight: `Not a true conversion coating -- essentially a deposited stain. Inferior in every way.`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | RED / BROWN OXIDE | Bath temp < 275 F; depleted NaNO2 | Increase temp; add NaNO2; check SG |
| 2 | 6.33" | SMUTTY / RUBS OFF | Bath too hot; over-immersion; depleted NaOH | Cool bath; reduce time; add NaOH |
| 3 | 12.16" | NON-UNIFORM / SPOTS | Oil contamination; poor cleaning; parts touching | Improve cleaning; rearrange load |
| 4 | 18.0" | RUSTING AFTER BLACK | No oil seal; caustic residue attracting moisture | Oil immediately after rinse; improve rinse |
