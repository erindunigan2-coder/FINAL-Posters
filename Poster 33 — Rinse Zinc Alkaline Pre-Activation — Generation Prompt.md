## Phase 6 -- Zone 5: Common Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0".

### Step 11 -- Section label
`WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-ACTIVATION RINSE` Y: **21.2"**.

### Step 12 -- Problem table (4 rows)

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alkaline carry-over | Activation neutralized | Insufficient rinse | Add cascade stage; increase flow |
| High conductivity | Parts still alkaline | Rinse water exhausted | Monitor; increase water flow |
| Spotting/staining | White residue | Hard water minerals | Switch to DI; soften water |
| Foam in rinse | Surfactant drag-out | High-foam cleaner | Low-foam cleaner; defoamer |

Standard styling: Problem `#E05C5C`, Symptom `#E8A020`, Cause `#F0EDE8`, Fix `#27AE60`.
