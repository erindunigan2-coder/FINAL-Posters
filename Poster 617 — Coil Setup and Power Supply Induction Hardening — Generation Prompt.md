## Phase 6 -- Flux Concentrators + Frequency Guide

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Flux Concentrators (Amber):**

What they do: magnetic laminations or ferrite behind/around coil concentrate field toward workpiece. When: one-side heating, reducing stray heat, complex geometries, gear contour (critical). Materials: Fluxtrol (iron powder composite -- most common), Ferrotron (ferrite), silicon steel laminations (legacy). Effect: 20--40% efficiency improvement in targeted zone.

**Right -- Frequency vs. Case Depth Quick Reference:**

| Frequency | Case Depth | Application |
|---|---|---|
| 1--3 kHz (low) | 0.120--0.300 in | Large shafts, heavy sections |
| 3--10 kHz (medium) | 0.060--0.150 in | Axle shafts, spindles |
| 10--50 kHz (medium-high) | 0.030--0.080 in | Gears, cams, smaller shafts |
| 100--500 kHz (high) | 0.010--0.040 in | Small parts, gear teeth, valve seats |

Note: `Skin depth = 503 x sqrt(rho/(mu x f)). Doubling frequency halves reference depth. Choose frequency FIRST, then adjust power and time.` Amber.
