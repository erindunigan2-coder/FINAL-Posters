## Phases 5-7 -- Content Zones

Bath: H3PO4 75--85% + HNO3 3--5% + Cu(NO3)2 0.5--1% (catalyst). Temp: 190--220 F (88--105 C). Time: 30--120 sec.

Chemical polish mechanism: preferential dissolution of surface peaks.

Alloy compatibility: 5657, 1100, 6463 (best). 6061 (acceptable). 2024, 7075 (poor -- Cu causes selective attack).

Fume management: NOx fumes are toxic -- local exhaust mandatory.

4 defect cards: haze, pitting, orange peel, streaking.
