## Phase 4 -- Blast Specification Table + Media Guide (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section label: `GRIT BLAST SPECIFICATION -- WIRE COMBUSTION SPRAY`.

**Parameter Table (Y: 3.8"-10.5"):**

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Media | Angular alumina (Al2O3) or angular steel grit | Alumina: no ferrous contamination; steel grit: lower cost, recyclable |
| Grit size | 16-36 mesh | Coarser than APS/HVOF (24-60 mesh) |
| Blast pressure | 60-100 PSI (415-690 kPa) | Higher than aerospace (40-80 PSI) |
| Nozzle distance | 150-300 mm (6-12 inches) | Manual; maintain consistent |
| Blast angle | 60-90 degrees | Perpendicular preferred |
| Anchor profile (Ra) | 4-12 um (160-475 microinches) | Deeper than APS; coarser droplets need more grip |
| Surface cleanliness | SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal Blast) | Per AWS C2.18; ISO 8501-1 Sa 3 |
| Alternative cleanliness | SSPC-SP 10 (Near-White Blast) | Acceptable for some specs; verify |

Header fill `#3A4055`. Data: JetBrains Mono 12pt.

**Media Selection Guide (Y: 11.0"-15.3"):** Two side-by-side panels.

*Left -- Angular Alumina* (accent `#2EC4B6`): Hardness 9 Mohs; self-sharpening; no ferrous contamination; higher cost; limited recyclability. Best for non-ferrous substrates and galvanic-sensitive applications.

*Right -- Angular Steel Grit* (accent `#E8A020`): Hardness 7-8 Mohs; rounds with use; ferrous contamination (no stainless/aluminum/titanium); lower cost; excellent recyclability (50-200 cycles). Best for carbon steel infrastructure.

## Phase 6 -- Field vs. Shop + Common Problems

### Field vs. Shop Blasting (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `FIELD BLASTING vs. SHOP BLASTING`.

**Left -- Field Blasting** (accent `#E8A020`):
- Portable blast pots (100-600 lb)
- Expendable media (not recyclable in field)
- Dust control: vacuum shroud, wet blasting, tarps
- Environmental permits may be required
- Humidity/dew point monitoring critical
- Old lead paint: lead-safe work practices
- Containment on bridges (blast curtains, vacuum)

**Right -- Shop Blasting** (accent `#27AE60`):
- Blast room with recyclable media (steel grit preferred)
- Integrated dust collection
- Controlled humidity and temperature
- Consistent parameters
- Media recycling via magnetic separator
- Shorter blast-to-spray window
- Not always practical -- many structures cannot be moved

### Common Problems (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `BLAST PROBLEMS -- 4 COMMON ISSUES`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| INSUFFICIENT PROFILE | Grit too fine; pressure too low | Use 16-24 mesh; increase to 80-100 PSI |
| FLASH RUST AFTER BLAST | High humidity; exceeded time window | Monitor dew point; spray within 2 hr humid |
| EMBEDDED CONTAMINANTS | Blasting over oil without degreasing | Always degrease BEFORE blasting |
| MEDIA CONTAMINATION | Recycled grit carrying oil | Test media cleanliness; replace batches |
