## Phase 6 -- Common Problems & Fixes

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE CLEANING STAGE`.

5-row table. Columns: Problem (3.5") | Symptom (5.0") | Cause (7.0") | Fix (7.5%).

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skip plating | Bare spots on zinc deposit | Silicate residue from cleaner | Switch to non-silicated cleaner immediately |
| Water break | Fish-eyes or uneven zinc deposit | Residual oil -- cleaner too weak or too cold | Increase concentration or temp; extend time |
| Poor adhesion | Zinc peels on bending or forming | Oxide layer not removed (under-cleaning) | Add electroclean step; anodic preferred |
| Pitting in zinc | Small pits scattered across deposit | Oil micro-droplets carried into plate tank | Carbon treat zinc bath; improve agitation |
| Smut / dark residue | Grey film before activation | High-carbon steel or cast iron releasing smut | Add de-smutting step; light anodic electroclean |

## Phase 7 -- Safety

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `SAFETY -- ALKALINE CLEANING & ELECTROCLEANING` `#E8A020`.

**Left -- Chemical Hazards (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Title: `CHEMICAL HAZARDS` `#E05C5C`.
- NaOH/KOH: severe burns on contact -- pH > 12
- Hot solution (140--180 F): thermal burn risk
- Mist/vapor: respiratory irritant -- ensure ventilation
- Never add water to concentrated cleaner -- add cleaner to water
- Electrocleaner generates gas (H2 or O2) -- avoid ignition sources near cathodic tanks

**Right -- PPE (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
Title: `REQUIRED PPE` `#E8A020`.
- Chemical splash goggles (minimum) or face shield
- Chemical-resistant gloves (nitrile or neoprene)
- Chemical-resistant apron
- Eyewash station within 10 seconds travel
- SDS posted and accessible at all times
