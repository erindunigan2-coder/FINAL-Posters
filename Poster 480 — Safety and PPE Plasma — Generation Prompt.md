Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section label: `8 HAZARDS YOU MUST CONTROL`.

4x2 grid. Each card: 5.5" x 5.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Pos | Hazard | Details | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | NOISE (100-130 dB) | Exceeds 140 dB at source; permanent hearing damage in minutes | Double hearing protection; NRR 30+ |
| R1C2 | UV/IR RADIATION | Intense UV comparable to welding arc; skin and eye burns | Shade 10-14 welding helmet; no exposed skin |
| R1C3 | METAL & CERAMIC FUMES | NiCr, Co, Cr2O3, ZrO2; some carcinogenic | LEV; HEPA; P100 RPE minimum |
| R1C4 | DUST EXPLOSION | Fine Al, Ti, Mg powders explosive when airborne | Inert atmosphere handling; grounding; no open flames |
| R2C1 | THERMAL BURNS | Hot substrate, fixtures, overspray; plasma jet lethal | Heat-resistant gloves; leather apron; face shield |
| R2C2 | ELECTRICAL (40-80 kW) | DC power; HF arc start; lethal shock potential | Lockout/tagout; insulated gloves; proper grounding |
| R2C3 | COMPRESSED GAS | High-pressure Ar, H2 (flammable), N2; asphyxiation risk | Secured cylinders; H2 leak detection; O2 monitoring |
| R2C4 | OVERSPRAY DUST | Respirable particles; long-term lung damage | Enclosed booth; downdraft ventilation; HEPA filtration |

Hazard name: 18pt `#E05C5C`. Controls label: `CONTROL:` 11pt `#27AE60`.
