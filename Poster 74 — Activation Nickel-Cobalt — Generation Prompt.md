## Phase 5 -- Activation Methods Detail

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `ACTIVATION METHODS -- DETAIL`.

Three callout boxes:

**ACID DIP** (accent `#2EC4B6`): HCl 20--50% v/v | Ambient | 15--60 sec | Carbon/alloy/low-alloy steel | Removes native Fe oxide.

**WOOD'S STRIKE** (accent `#E8A020`): NiCl2 240 g/L + HCl 125 mL/L | Ambient--80 F | 20--60 ASF, 2--5 min | Inconel, Waspaloy, stainless, titanium (after etch) | Deposits thin adherent Ni through oxide | MANDATORY for passive alloys.

**TITANIUM ETCH** (accent `#E05C5C`): HF 2--5% + HNO3 20--35% v/v | Ambient, 30--120 sec | Removes TiO2 passive film | IMMEDIATELY rinse and Wood's strike -- Ti re-passivates in seconds | `Calcium gluconate gel MUST be on-site` `#E05C5C`.

## Phase 6 -- Wood's Strike Parameters

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WOOD'S NICKEL STRIKE -- FULL PARAMETERS`.

| Parameter | Range | Optimal | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| NiCl2 * 6H2O | 200--280 g/L | 240 g/L | Sole nickel source |
| HCl (conc.) | 80--160 mL/L | 125 mL/L | Keeps pH very low |
| pH | < 1.0 | < 0.5 | Do not adjust upward |
| Temperature | Ambient--80 F | Ambient | Do not heat |
| Current density | 15--60 ASF | 20--40 ASF | Lower for complex parts |
| Time | 2--5 min | 3 min | Thin strike (~0.1 mil) |
| Anodes | Ni S-rounds in Ti baskets | -- | Dissolve to maintain Ni |
| Agitation | Cathode rod or air | -- | Gentle only |

Below: `Transfer parts LIVE (current on) from Wood's strike directly to the NiCo plating bath. Breaking current allows re-passivation.` `#E8A020`

## Phase 7 -- Common Activation Failures

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG`. Four cards:

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DEPOSIT PEELING | Passive oxide not removed; current broken during transfer | Verify Wood's strike; maintain live transfer |
| 2 | BLISTERING | H2 trapped under deposit; insufficient bake | Bake 375 F / 4+ hr for high-strength steel |
| 3 | DARK STRIKE | HCl depleted in Wood's bath; NiCl2 too low | Replenish HCl; analyze NiCl2 |
| 4 | TI RE-PASSIVATION | Delay between HF etch and Wood's strike | Reduce transfer time to under 30 sec |
