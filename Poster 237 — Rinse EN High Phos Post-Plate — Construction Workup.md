# Poster #237 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- EN (High Phos) -- Post-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 6 of 8. The post-plate rinse serves three purposes: stop the autocatalytic reaction, remove EN drag-out chemistry, and prepare the surface for post-treatment. Cold water is preferred because it quenches the reaction faster. Parts must not air-dry between the EN bath and rinse -- dried EN solution causes permanent staining on the fresh deposit.

## Design Notes

The post-plate rinse poster is more about handling discipline than rinse chemistry. The DO/DON'T format is the most operator-friendly layout for this content -- it is scannable, memorable, and actionable. The drag-out recovery section adds economic value: EN chemistry is expensive, and a simple stagnant recovery rinse can save 15-30% on chemical costs. The visual inspection guide teaches operators what "good" looks like -- critical for a process where the deposit appearance is less obviously bright than electrolytic nickel.
