## Phase 6 -- Sulfamate vs. Watts Comparison

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `SULFAMATE VS. WATTS -- CHOOSING YOUR NICKEL BATH`.

**Left -- Sulfamate Nickel (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E8A020`. Title: `SULFAMATE NICKEL` `#E8A020`. Subtitle: `The Engineering Standard`.
- Primary salt: Ni(SO3NH2)2 + H3BO3 | 130--145 F | 20--75 ASF (up to 200+) | 96--100% cathode eff | Matte (no brighteners) | Near-zero to compressive stress | Electroforming, aerospace, buildup/salvage | Chloride optional (0--30 g/L); can run chloride-free | Higher cost
- Bottom bar `#E8A020`: `The only nickel process that can achieve near-zero internal stress. The standard for electroforming, waveguides, molds, and fatigue-critical components.`

**Right -- Watts Nickel (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#27AE60`. Title: `WATTS NICKEL` `#27AE60`. Subtitle: `The Decorative & Functional Standard`.
- Primary salt: NiSO4 + NiCl2 + H3BO3 | 130--150 F | 30--50 ASF | 92--98% cathode eff | Semi-bright to bright | Tensile stress (moderate) | Decorative, undercoat for chrome | Class I+II brighteners | Required chloride (37--55 g/L NiCl2) | Lower cost
- Bottom bar `#27AE60`: `Most versatile nickel process -- bright appearance, good leveling, broad application range. The workhorse of decorative plating.`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting Quick Hits

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON SULFAMATE NICKEL PROBLEMS`.

Four cards, gap 0.33". Each: W: 5.5", H: 2.7", fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E05C5C`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH TENSILE STRESS | Organic contamination, sulfamate hydrolysis, excess chloride | Carbon treat; check bath temp (>160 F hydrolyzes); reduce chloride |
| 2 | BRITTLE DEPOSIT | Excessive saccharin, metallic contamination | Reduce saccharin; dummy plate |
| 3 | PITTING | Low wetting agent, particulates, poor agitation | Add anti-pit; improve filtration; increase agitation |
| 4 | ROUGH DEPOSIT | Anode sludge, particulate, insufficient filtration | Inspect anode bags; increase filtration turnover |
