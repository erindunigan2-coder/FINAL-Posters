## Phase 5 -- Dragout Reduction + Water Quality

Y: 14.5" to 20.5".

**Left -- Dragout Reduction:** Dwell and drain (10--15 sec, 50--80% reduction), slow withdrawal, spray rinse, air knife.

**Right -- Water Quality:** DI (<10 uS/cm ideal) | RO (10--50 uS/cm excellent) | Softened city (100--300 uS/cm, early rinses only) | Raw city (first rinse only -- chloride risk).

Note: `Chloride in rinse water eventually reaches the CrO3 bath. DI water is the only way to eliminate this risk entirely.` `#E05C5C`

---

## Phase 6 -- Failure Modes

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG`.

| Failure | Cause | Effect on Type I |
|---|---|---|
| Alkaline dragover to etch | Incomplete rinsing | Uneven etch on thin-coat parts |
| Surfactant in CrO3 bath | Cleaner carryover through etch/desmut | Foaming; disrupts voltage control |
| Chloride in CrO3 bath | City water in rinse | Pitting -- catastrophic on thin coating |
| Accelerated etch rate | Alkaline drag into etch bath | Over-etch on fatigue-critical parts |

---

## Phase 7 -- Type I-Specific Rinse Rules

Y: 26.5" to 32.5".

- DI water mandatory for final rinse stage
- Cascade preferred (minimum 2 stages; aerospace: 3)
- Conductivity monitoring at every rinse stage
- Chloride and sulfate are the critical contaminants for CrO3 bath
