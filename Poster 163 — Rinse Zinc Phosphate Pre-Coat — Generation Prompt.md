## Phase 6 -- What Happens When You Rinse

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `THE CONSEQUENCE`.

**Left -- After Conditioning (No Rinse):** Emerald accent. Ti particles intact. Nucleation sites: millions/cm2. Fine crystals 2--10 um. Coating weight 150--350 mg/ft2.

**Right -- After Conditioning + Rinse (Wrong):** Coral accent. Ti colloid washed away. Nucleation: scattered (grain boundaries only). Coarse crystals 50--100+ um. Porous, weak, failed adhesion.

---

## Phase 7 -- Where the Actual Rinse Belongs

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `RINSE PLACEMENT CLARIFICATION`. Teal accent.

Correct flow: CLEAN -> RINSE (PRE-CONDITION) -> RINSE (PRE-COAT) -> CONDITION -> PHOSPHATE.
Both rinses serve the conditioner. Neither belongs AFTER the conditioner.
`If your line has a rinse tank between the conditioner and the phosphate bath, either bypass it or repurpose it as a pre-conditioner rinse.`
