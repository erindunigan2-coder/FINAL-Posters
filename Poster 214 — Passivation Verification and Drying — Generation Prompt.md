## Phase 4 -- Verification Test Matrix Hero

Y: 2.9" to 16.0". Section: `VERIFICATION TESTING -- PER ASTM A967`.

### Step 5 -- Five verification test cards (Y: 3.8" to 12.5")

Five stacked horizontal cards:

1. **PRACTICE A -- COPPER SULFATE TEST** (`#E8A020`): CuSO4/H2SO4 immersion 6 min. Pass: no copper (pink/red). Fail: pink/red = free iron. Note: Does NOT work on free-machining grades (303, 416).

2. **PRACTICE B -- SALT SPRAY (ASTM B117)** (`#2EC4B6`): Salt spray chamber, 2--24 hr. Pass: no rust. Aerospace commonly specifies 24 hr.

3. **PRACTICE C -- HIGH HUMIDITY** (`#27AE60`): 24 hr at 95% RH, 95 F. Pass: no rust. Most stringent short-term test. Highly reliable for all alloys.

4. **PRACTICE D -- WATER IMMERSION** (`#2EC4B6`): Immerse in water 24 hr. Pass: no rust. Use DI water to avoid mineral deposits masking results.

5. **FERROXYL TEST** (`#E8A020`): K3Fe(CN)6 + HNO3 solution. Pass: no blue. Fail: blue (Turnbull's blue) = free iron. Most sensitive test.

### Step 6 -- Copper sulfate test expanded (Y: 12.5" to 15.8")

**Left -- How It Works:** Fe0 + Cu2+ -> Fe2+ + Cu0 (copper deposits on iron). If passivated: Cr2O3 blocks iron exposure, no reaction = PASS.

**Right -- LIMITATIONS** (`#E05C5C`): Does NOT work on free-machining grades (303, 416, 420F) -- sulfur/selenium inclusions give false positives. Use ferroxyl or salt spray instead. False negatives if solution exhausted, surface acid-etched, or test time < 6 min.

## Phase 5 -- Drying Requirements

Y: 16.0" to 22.5". Section: `DRYING -- SIMPLER THAN YOU THINK, HARDER THAN YOU'D EXPECT`.

Three panels:

**Acceptable Drying** (`#27AE60`): Air dry ambient (simplest), warm forced air 120--150 F (accelerated), compressed air blow-off (complex geometries, oil-free filtered air).

**DO NOT** (`#E05C5C`, coral-tinted glass): HIGH-TEMPERATURE OVEN DRYING. > 300 F causes thermal oxidation (heat tint) -- literally undoing the passivation. Also: do NOT wipe with shop rags (carbon steel particles contaminate).

**Trapped Moisture** (`#E8A020`): Parts MUST be completely dry before packaging. Trapped moisture causes crevice corrosion (stagnant water depletes O2, passive film breaks down), water staining, microbial growth. Rotate parts, air blow blind holes, extended warm air for assemblies.

## Phase 6 -- Standards & Post-Treatment

Y: 22.5" to 28.5". Section: `APPLICABLE STANDARDS & POST-TREATMENT`.

**Left -- Standards Table:**

| Standard | Scope |
|---|---|
| ASTM A967/A967M | Chemical passivation of SS -- PRIMARY STANDARD |
| AMS 2700 | Passivation of corrosion resistant steels (aerospace) |
| ASTM A380 | Cleaning, descaling, and passivation |
| QQ-P-35 (Canceled) | Legacy federal spec; superseded by ASTM A967 |
| ASTM F86 | Surface prep of metallic surgical implants |
| SEMI F42 | SS evaluation for semiconductor |

**Right -- POST-TREATMENT** (`#27AE60`): Normally NONE required. Passive film IS the final surface. Enhanced: electropolish BEFORE passivation (standard aerospace/medical protocol). VCI paper/bags for long-term storage.

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | CuSO4 FALSE POSITIVE (303/416) | Free-machining; S inclusions react | Use ferroxyl or salt spray instead |
| 2 | 6.33" | RUST AFTER VERIFICATION PASS | Trapped moisture; inadequate drying | Improve drying; air blow; extend warm air |
| 3 | 12.16" | HEAT TINT FROM DRYING | Oven > 300 F | Use ambient or warm air only (< 150 F) |
| 4 | 18.0" | FAILED SALT SPRAY | Insufficient passivation; wrong bath for alloy | Re-passivate; check bath; consult alloy guide |
