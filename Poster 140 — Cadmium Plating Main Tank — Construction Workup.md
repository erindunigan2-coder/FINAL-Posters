# Poster #140 -- Construction Workup
## Cadmium Plating (Cyanide) -- Main Tank

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. The cyanide cadmium plating tank. This poster covers the aerospace-standard cyanide cadmium bath (the dominant bath type) plus a reference panel for the acid chloride alternative. Hero visual: tank cross-section with cadmium anodes, cathode, current flow. Key control: free NaCN and cadmium metal concentration. The poster must clearly state that this tank contains BOTH cyanide and cadmium -- two of the most hazardous substances in the plating industry.

## Design Notes

The densest poster in EP-14. The dual-hazard nature of the cadmium bath (cyanide + carcinogen) makes the hazard callout at the bottom of the tank diagram essential. The acid chloride alternative panel is included as a reference because some specifications are moving away from cyanide where possible while retaining cadmium. The defect grid leads with HE failure because that is the #1 failure mode in aerospace cadmium plating.
