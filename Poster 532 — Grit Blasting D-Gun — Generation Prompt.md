## Phase 5 -- Media Selection Guide

Y: 14.0" to 22.0". Section label: `BLAST MEDIA SELECTION -- WHY WHITE ALUMINA`.

6-row table. Columns: Media | Hardness (Mohs) | Grit Size | D-Gun Use? | Reason.

| Media | Hardness | Size | Use? | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White alumina 99.5%+ | 9 | 36--60 mesh | YES -- STANDARD (`#27AE60`) | No ferrous contamination; aerospace approved |
| Brown alumina | 9 | 36--60 mesh | ACCEPTABLE (`#E8A020`) | Lower purity; OK non-aerospace |
| Silicon carbide | 9.5 | 36--60 mesh | SPECIALTY (`#2EC4B6`) | Harder substrates (Ti); more aggressive |
| Angular steel grit | 7--8 | G25--G40 | NO (`#E05C5C`) | Ferrous contamination; galvanic corrosion |
| Garnet | 7--8 | 36--80 mesh | NO (`#E05C5C`) | Insufficient hardness; inconsistent |
| Glass bead | 5--6 | Various | NO (`#E05C5C`) | Peened surface, not angular profile |

Purity callout (coral): `99.5%+ PURITY: Brown alumina contains Fe2O3 impurities. On Ni-alloy and Ti turbine components, iron contamination creates galvanic corrosion sites. White alumina is calcined to remove iron -- corrosion prevention, not cost preference.`

| Defect | Color | Cause | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| OVER-BLASTING (Ra too high) | `#E05C5C` | Excessive pressure/time/coarse media | Stress at peaks; embedded grit; cracks |

| SURFACE OXIDATION | `#E05C5C` | Excessive time to spray; high humidity | Oxide film; reduced bond |
