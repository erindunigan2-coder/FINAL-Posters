# Poster #171 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Manganese Phosphate -- Pre-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 4 of 8. This is the critical rinse between acid pickle/conditioning and the manganese phosphate bath. Any acid carryover -- HCl, H2SO4, dissolved iron -- upsets the free acid balance in the phosphate bath and creates excessive sludge. Multiple rinse stages may be needed. This rinse runs at ambient temperature (unlike the post-clean rinse which runs hot). The poster must make the acid carryover danger unmistakable.

Hero visual: rinse tank with emphasis on the transition from acid environment to the near-boiling phosphate bath.

### ZONE 4 -- Acid Carryover Danger

**Section label:** `WHAT ACID CARRYOVER DOES TO YOUR PHOSPHATE BATH` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Consequence Panel (Y: 15.3" to 20.3")**

Full-width rounded rect, W: 23.0", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

Four consequence rows:

| Carryover | What Happens | Bath Impact |
|---|---|---|
| HCl residue | Lowers pH, increases free acid | Etches too aggressively, thin/powdery coating |
| H2SO4 residue | Sulfate contamination builds | Sludge generation, poor crystal structure |
| Dissolved iron (Fe2+/Fe3+) | Excess iron in bath beyond normal | Accelerates sludge, darkens coating to brown/red |
| Dissolved copper/chrome | Metallic contamination | Immersion deposits, coating defects, skip areas |

Each row: alternating `#1E2435` / `#252B3D`, H: 0.9".
Carryover column: Inter Medium 13 pt `#E05C5C`.
What Happens: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`.
Bath Impact: JetBrains Mono 12 pt `#F0EDE8`.

- Text: `The MnPhos bath operates at 190--210 F. Hot baths amplify every contamination problem. What you can tolerate at ambient, you cannot tolerate at 200 F.` -- Inter Medium, 13 pt, `#E8A020`

### ZONE 5 -- Multi-Stage Rinse + Water Quality

**Left -- Multi-Stage Rinsing (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Section label: `WHEN TO ADD RINSE STAGES` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

- Rounded rect, H: 5.0", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`

*Single Rinse Adequate:*
- `Light pickle (short time, dilute acid)`
- `Low-alloy steel with minimal scale`
- `Good overflow rate maintained`

*Two or Three Rinses Needed:*
- `Heavy pickle (15+ min in concentrated acid)`
- `High-alloy or cast parts dissolving significant metal`
- `High-production line with large dragout volume`
- `Any time phosphate bath free acid keeps drifting high`

*Counter-Flow Cascade:*
- `Most water-efficient multi-stage approach`
- `Fresh water enters last rinse, overflows backward`
- `Each rinse stage is cleaner than the last`

**Right -- Water Quality (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Section label: `WATER QUALITY MATTERS` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

- Rounded rect, H: 5.0", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`

| Factor | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Conductivity | < 500 uS/cm | Confirms low dissolved solids |
| pH | 6.0--8.0 (neutral) | Confirms acid is rinsed away |
| Hardness | < 200 ppm as CaCO3 | Hard water deposits interfere with coating |
| Chloride | < 100 ppm | Excess chloride accelerates corrosion |
| Temperature | Ambient | No heating needed for this rinse |

JetBrains Mono 12 pt for values, Inter Regular 12 pt for descriptions.

### ZONE 6 -- Key Rules + Dragout Reduction

**Left -- Key Rules (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Section label: `NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

5 rule rows, each with teal left border:
1. `ZERO acid into the phosphate bath -- the goal is zero, not "a little"`
2. `Multiple rinse stages after heavy pickle`
3. `Monitor rinse pH -- must be neutral (6.0--8.0)`
4. `Do not let parts dry between rinse and phosphate`

## Design Notes

The "near-boiling bath" transition is the unique angle here. The visual showing parts going from ambient rinse into a 200 F phosphate bath should create a sense of urgency -- anything you carry in gets amplified by heat. The acid carryover consequence table is the centerpiece of the educational content. The dragout reduction tips give operators something actionable they can implement the same day they read the poster.
