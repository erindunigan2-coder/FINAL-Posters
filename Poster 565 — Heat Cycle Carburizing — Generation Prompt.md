## Phase 4 -- Case Depth vs. Time (HERO)

**Left -- ECD Table (W: 14.0", Emerald accent):** `EFFECTIVE CASE DEPTH (ECD) AT 1700 F`. Measured to 50 HRC (513 HV, ~0.40% C) per SAE J423.

| Target ECD (in.) | ECD (mm) | Time at Temp | TCD (approx.) | Application |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Right -- Sqrt Law (W: 8.5", Amber accent):** ECD = K x sqrt(t). K ~0.018--0.025 in./sqrt(hr) at 1700 F. Double ECD = 4x time. +100 F = ~2x diffusion coefficient.

## Phase 5 -- Temperature Decisions

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `CHOOSING YOUR CARBURIZING TEMPERATURE`.

Three cards:

| Card | Temp | Accent | Stat | Key Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1650 F (899 C) | `#2EC4B6` | CONSERVATIVE | Fine-pitch gears; thin cases; less distortion; finer grain (ASTM 6-8); ~30% longer than 1700 F |
| 2 | 1700 F (927 C) | `#27AE60` | INDUSTRY STANDARD | General production; balanced speed vs. grain; all tables reference this; THIS IS YOUR DEFAULT |
| 3 | 1750 F (954 C) | `#E8A020` | HIGH PRODUCTIVITY | Deep cases >0.060"; ~25% faster; grain growth (ASTM 4-5); higher IGO; requires Al-killed steel |

## Phase 6 -- Worked Example + Key Relationships

**Worked Example (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `WORKED EXAMPLE -- 0.040" ECD AT 1700 F`.

Horizontal timeline: HEAT (1--2 hr) -> BOOST (2.5 hr, Cp 1.0%) -> DIFFUSE (1.5 hr, Cp 0.80%) -> TO QUENCH. Total: ~5--6.5 hr. Result: 0.040" ECD, surface C 0.80--0.85%, 60--62 HRC.

**Key Relationships (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `KEY METALLURGICAL RELATIONSHIPS`. Three cards, Emerald accent:
1. ECD vs. TCD -- TCD = 1.5--2.0x ECD; NOT interchangeable
2. SQRT(TIME) RULE -- most important number in carburizing economics
3. TEMPERATURE EFFECT -- +100 F = ~2x diffusion; why vacuum carburizing at 1800+ F is gaining ground
