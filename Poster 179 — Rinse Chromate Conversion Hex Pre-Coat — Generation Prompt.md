## Phase 5 -- Three Contaminants

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THREE CONTAMINANTS -- ALL MUST GO`. Three cards:

**Acid Residue:** Amber. HNO3/HF from deox. Accelerates bath consumption, disrupts pH, shortens bath life.
**Dissolved Metals:** Coral. Cu, Fe, Si from alloy dissolution. Copper causes dark/sooty coatings. Iron contaminates bath.
**Fluoride:** Teal. From HF deoxidizer. Excess over-activates chromate reaction, causes powdery coating.

---

## Phase 6 -- Time-Critical Transfer

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Reoxidation Timeline:** 0--30 sec = minimal (ideal) | 1--5 min = acceptable | 5--15 min = significant regrowth | > 15 min = may need re-deox.

**Right -- Best Practices:** Position rinse tanks close to chromate | Small lots | Don't air-dry between | Keep parts wet until ready | If > 15 min: re-deoxidize.

---

## Phase 7 -- Monitoring

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". pH every 4 hr (6.0--8.0) | Conductivity every 4 hr (< 200 uS/cm) | Dissolved metals weekly (Cu < 5 ppm, Fe < 10 ppm) | Fluoride daily if HF deox (< 10 ppm) | Transfer time every load (< 5 min). Three problem cards: dark/sooty chromate, thin/patchy, bath pH rising.
