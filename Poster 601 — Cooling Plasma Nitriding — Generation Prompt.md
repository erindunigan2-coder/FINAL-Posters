## Phase 5 -- No Quench Comparison

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `WHY NO QUENCH? -- THE FUNDAMENTAL DIFFERENCE`.

**Left -- PLASMA NITRIDING (`#27AE60`):** Nitride PRECIPITATION in ferrite; ferrite throughout; slow cool (hours); no quench; near zero distortion; ZERO cracking risk; 0.0001--0.0005 in/surface growth. Badge: `PRECIPITATION HARDENING`.

**Right -- CARBURIZING (`#E8A020`):** MARTENSITE formation (phase transformation); austenite -> martensite on quench; rapid quench (seconds); REQUIRED; significant distortion; real cracking risk; variable growth + post-grind. Badge: `TRANSFORMATION HARDENING`.

## Phase 6 -- Dimensional Change + Shutdown

### Dimensional Change (Y: 22.0"--32.5", left)

Section label: `DIMENSIONAL CHANGE`. Growth 0.0001--0.0005 in/surface; from nitrogen lattice expansion; predictable and consistent; parts CAN be finish-machined before nitriding. Compare: carburizing requires 0.005--0.015 in grinding stock.

### Shutdown Procedure (right)

Section label: `SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE`. 8 steps:

| Step | Action | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | De-energize DC | Plasma extinguished; parts still hot |
| 2 | Maintain N2/N2H2 atmosphere | Prevents oxidation |
| 3 | Monitor TC temp | Record cool-down rate |
| 4 | Below 300 F: backfill | Do NOT open at higher temp |
| 5 | Backfill with N2 | Slow bleed; equalize |
| 6 | Open door | Parts may be warm |
| 7 | Unload parts | Inspect for anomalies |
| 8 | Transfer to inspection | Microhardness, WL check |
