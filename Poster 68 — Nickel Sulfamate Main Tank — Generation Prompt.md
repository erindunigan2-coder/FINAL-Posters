| Component | Electroforming | General Engineering | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nickel sulfamate | 300--450 g/L | 450--650 g/L | Primary Ni source (high solubility) |

### Step 7 -- Hydrolysis warning (Y: 12.3" to 13.8")

Full-width callout, fill `#E05C5C` at 15%, border 2pt `#E05C5C`.
- Title: `CRITICAL: SULFAMATE HYDROLYSIS` `#E05C5C`
- `Nickel sulfamate hydrolyzes to nickel ammonium sulfate IRREVERSIBLY at temperatures > 160 F (71 C) or pH < 3.0. Hydrolysis increases stress permanently. The bath cannot be recovered -- partial or full replacement required. NEVER allow temperature or pH to exceed limits.`

### Step 9 -- Stress control factors (Y: 17.8" to 19.8")

Title: `WHAT CONTROLS DEPOSIT STRESS` `#E8A020`.

| Factor | Effect on Stress |
|---|---|
| Higher chloride (NiCl2) | Increases tensile stress |
| Higher temperature (>140 F) | Hydrolysis risk; stress erratic |
| Lower pH (<3.5) | Hydrolysis risk; tensile increases |
| Organic contamination | Increases tensile stress dramatically |
| Saccharin (stress reducer) | Shifts tensile to compressive; 50--200 mg/L |
| Higher current density | Slightly increases tensile |
| Metallic impurities (Cu, Fe, Zn) | Increase stress and embrittlement |

## Phase 6 -- Defects + Anode Management

Y: 20.0" to 27.0". Section: `COMMON DEFECTS AND ANODE MANAGEMENT`.

### Step 10 -- Defects table (Y: 20.8" to 24.5")

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| High tensile stress | Chloride too high, organics, hydrolysis | Reduce NiCl2; carbon treat; check for hydrolysis |
| Cracking | Extreme tensile + low ductility | Add saccharin; purify bath |
| Pitting | Low wetting agent, particles, poor filtration | Add anti-pit (sparingly -- organics affect stress) |
| Rough deposits | Particulates, anode dissolution products | Filter; anode bags; maintain Ti baskets |
| Peeling | Inadequate activation or strike | Review Wood's strike procedure |
| Sulfamate hydrolysis | Temp >160 F or pH <3.0 | IRREVERSIBLE -- partial or full bath replacement |

### Step 11 -- Anode management (Y: 24.8" to 26.8")

Callout, fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E8A020`. Title: `ANODE MANAGEMENT`.
- S-Rounds (sulfur-depolarized): Standard. Dissolve evenly. Preferred.
- R-Rounds (electrolytic): Used when zero-sulfur deposit specified.
- Anode bags: MANDATORY (PP, 1--5 micron).
- Ti baskets: clean regularly, inspect for damage.
- A:C ratio 1:1 to 2:1.
- Air agitation AVOIDED near anodes: introduces CO2 and organics.

## Phase 7 -- Analytical Methods + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Two-column layout.

**Left -- Analytical Methods (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#2EC4B6`.

| Analysis | Method |
|---|---|
| Nickel metal | EDTA titration @ pH 10, murexide |
| Chloride | Mohr titration (AgNO3 / K2CrO4) |
| Boric acid | Mannitol + NaOH titration |
| pH | Calibrated meter (check daily) |
| Deposit stress | Contractometer or stress strip |
| Thickness | XRF, beta backscatter, or micrometer |
| Hull cell | 267 mL, 2A, 10 min at bath temp |

**Right -- Safety (X: 15.5", W: 8.0"):**
Coral-tinted glass.
- Nickel: #1 industrial allergen (dermal). IARC Group 1 (inhalation).
- Sulfamate solutions: moderately acidic (pH 3.5--4.5). Skin/eye irritant.
- PPE: nitrile gloves minimum, safety goggles.
- Ventilation mandatory. Fume suppression or local exhaust.
- Wastewater: Ni precipitates at pH 9.5--10.5. Discharge limits 0.5--3.4 mg/L.
