# Poster #232 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- EN (High Phos)

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of 8. Skip plating is the number-one defect in electroless nickel operations -- and it starts here. If the surface is not perfectly clean, the autocatalytic reaction will not initiate. No amount of bath chemistry adjustment can compensate for a dirty part. This poster covers alkaline soak cleaning and optional electrocleaning, with substrate-specific callouts for steel, aluminum, and copper alloys.

### ZONE 4 -- Substrate-Specific Cleaning

**Section label:** `CLEANING BY SUBSTRATE` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- 4-Row Substrate Matrix**

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Column widths (23.0" total):
- Substrate (4.5") | Cleaner Type (5.5") | Special Notes (7.0") | Caution (6.0")

Header row: Rectangle fill `#3A4055`, H: 0.5". Barlow SemiBold, 14 pt, `#F0EDE8`.

| Substrate | Cleaner Type | Special Notes | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon & alloy steel | Standard alkaline soak | Most common EN substrate; highly catalytic | No special precautions |
| Aluminum alloys | Non-etch alkaline (pH <10.5) | Dedicated aluminum cleaner; avoid surface attack | Excessive etch = rough surface |
| Copper & brass | Standard alkaline soak | Copper is catalytic; no Pd activation needed | Tarnish: bright dip after clean |
| Stainless steel | Standard alkaline + electroclean | Passive oxide requires aggressive activation after | Wood's strike recommended |

Data: JetBrains Mono Regular, 12 pt, `#F0EDE8`. Caution column: `#E05C5C`.
