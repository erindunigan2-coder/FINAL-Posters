## Phase 6 -- Mineral Contamination

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT TAP WATER MINERALS DO TO A GOLD BATH`.

| Mineral | Typical in Tap | Gold Bath Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calcium | 10--200 ppm | Problem at any level | Insoluble salts, roughens deposit |
| Iron | 0.1--5 ppm | > 10 ppm | Dark deposit, roughness |
| Copper | 0.01--1 ppm | > 5 ppm | Dark, discolored |
| Zinc | 0.01--5 ppm | > 1 ppm | Pitting, discoloration |
| Chloride | 10--250 ppm | > 1 ppm | Attacks gold complex -- catastrophic |
| Silica | 5--50 ppm | Problem at any level | Hazy, poor adhesion |

Thresholds in `#E05C5C`. Bottom note: `A single batch rinsed in tap water can introduce enough chloride to damage a gold bath permanently.`
