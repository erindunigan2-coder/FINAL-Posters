## Phase 5 -- Why Cleaning Matters More for Sulfamate

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHY SULFAMATE DEMANDS BETTER CLEANING`.

### Step 7 -- Organic-stress connection (Y: 15.3" to 18.5")

Full-width callout, fill `#E05C5C` at 10%, border 2pt `#E05C5C`.
- Title: `THE ORGANIC-STRESS CONNECTION` `#E05C5C`
- In Watts: organic contamination causes pitting and haze -- cosmetic problems. In sulfamate: organic contamination spikes tensile stress immediately and unpredictably, destroying the primary advantage.
- Sources: Oil not removed by soak clean | Surfactant carry-over | Decomposed electrocleaner additives | Masking tape adhesive | Fingerprints
- `Every organic molecule that reaches the sulfamate bath shifts the deposit toward higher tensile stress. For electroforming where stress must be near-zero, cleaning is not "good enough" until it is perfect.`

## Phase 6 -- Anodic vs. Cathodic + Electroforming Mandrels

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column layout.

**Left -- Anodic vs. Cathodic (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

*Anodic (Reverse):* accent `#27AE60`. O2 scrubbing, removes smut, no metal embedding. `RECOMMENDED FOR NICKEL`.

*Cathodic (Direct):* accent `#E8A020`. H2 scrubbing, heavy soil removal. CAN embed metals, CAUSE HE on high-strength steel. `USE FIRST FOR HEAVY SOIL ONLY`.

**Right -- Electroforming Mandrel Cleaning (X: 12.5", W: 11.0"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E8A020`. Title: `CLEANING ELECTROFORMING MANDRELS`.
- Aluminum mandrels: alkaline etch, then anodize or chromate release layer. Clean to water-break-free before chromate.
- Steel mandrels: standard clean + light chrome flash or proprietary release agent.
- Wax/epoxy mandrels: solvent clean only -- no alkaline or acid immersion.
- Any contamination on the mandrel transfers to the electroform surface.

## Phase 7 -- Common Failures + Safety

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column layout.

**Left -- 4 Cleaning Failures (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

| Failure | Root Cause | Result in Sulfamate Bath |
|---|---|---|
| Organic carry-over | Cleaner too dilute or time too short | Stress spike -- defeats purpose of sulfamate |
| Surfactant drag-in | Cleaner not fully rinsed | Organic contamination of bath |
| Metal embedding (cathodic) | Excessive cathodic cleaning | Dark spots, inclusions |
| Mandrel contamination | Release agent contaminated | Electroform adhesion failure or surface defects |

**Right -- Safety (X: 15.5", W: 8.0"):**
Coral-tinted glass callout.
- Alkaline cleaners: caustic burn (pH 12--14). Gloves, goggles, apron.
- Electrocleaner gas: ventilation required.
- Hot solutions (140--190 F): splash burn risk.
- Nickel salts: IARC Group 1 carcinogen (inhalable). Mist extraction near heated solutions.
