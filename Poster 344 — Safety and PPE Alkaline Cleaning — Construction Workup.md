# Poster #344 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Alkaline Cleaning

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Poster 2 of 7 in the CT-01 cluster. This is the safety poster -- it hangs next to the soak tank and reminds operators what chemicals they are working with, what PPE is required, and what to do when something goes wrong. The hero visual is a PPE equipment diagram. The chemical hazard table is the reference backbone. Emergency procedures must be readable from 6 feet at speed -- someone splashed with caustic does not have time to squint.

---

## Part 1 -- Workflow Orientation

### Limitations to Flag
1. **PPE diagram (Block B -- HERO):** Silhouette figure with labeled callouts pointing to each PPE item. Built with rectangles and text callouts -- no complex illustration.
2. **Chemical hazard table (Block D):** Four-row table with GHS pictogram placeholders (text labels, not images).
3. **Emergency procedure cards (Block E):** Four cards (skin, eye, ingestion, spill) with step-by-step instructions.
4. **Temperature hazard callout (Block F):** Single coral-tinted warning panel.

## Design Notes

This is the safety poster. It must be the most readable poster in the cluster at distance. The emergency procedure cards must work when someone is panicking -- large text, numbered steps, no ambiguity. The PPE diagram is the hero because operators need a visual reminder of what "fully suited up" looks like. The coral accent dominates this poster more than any other in the cluster -- intentional.
