|---|---|---|---|

| R2C3 | THERMAL BURNS | Parts/fixtures at 1400--1600 F; radiant heat | Full face shield + aluminized components; 14 in gauntlet gloves |

## Phase 6 -- Emergency Response

Y: 26.0"--32.5". Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE` in `#E05C5C`.

**Left -- AMMONIA EXPOSURE (`#E05C5C`):** Move to fresh air; administer O2 if breathing difficulty; flush eyes 15+ min; remove contaminated clothing; call emergency services. Note: `NH3 attacks copper/brass -- use ONLY steel or stainless fittings`.
