## Phase 6 -- Paint Compatibility + Warnings

### Paint System Compatibility (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `PAINT SYSTEMS OVER ARC SPRAY COATINGS`. Three cards:

**EPOXY SYSTEMS (`#27AE60`):** Most common topcoat. Epoxy primer + epoxy intermediate + polyurethane topcoat. Excellent adhesion to sealed TSC. UV-stable PU topcoat for exterior. Total DFT 150--300 um.

**ZINC-RICH PRIMER (`#E8A020`):** Inorganic or organic zinc-rich over TSC zinc. Additional cathodic protection at damage. Common on bridge/marine. Follow with epoxy + PU topcoat.

**SILICONE SYSTEMS (`#2EC4B6`):** High-temp service (stacks, exhaust, hot structures). Silicone aluminum paint over sealed Al coating. Service to 540 degC (1000 degF). Limited UV resistance.

### Time-Critical Warnings (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `TIME-CRITICAL POST-TREATMENT WARNINGS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Warning | Detail |
|---|---|
| SEAL WITHIN 4 HOURS | Porous coatings absorb moisture within hours. In humid conditions, seal within 2 hours. |
| DO NOT SEAL WET COATINGS | If moisture entered (rain, delay), may need re-blast. Sealing traps water at interface. |
| CURE BEFORE TOPCOAT | Uncured sealer causes intercoat adhesion failure with paint system. |
| INSPECT BEFORE SEAL | Final visual and thickness BEFORE sealing. Once sealed, coating cannot be inspected directly. |
