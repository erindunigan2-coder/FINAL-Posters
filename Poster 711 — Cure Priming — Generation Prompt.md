## Phase 4 -- Three Cure Mechanisms (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section label: `THREE CURE CHEMISTRIES -- THREE DIFFERENT RULES`. Three panels.

**Panel 1 -- IOZ Hydrolysis (accent `#27AE60`):** Ethyl silicate + H2O -> hydrolysis -> Si-OH condenses to Si-O-Si network -> ceramic-like matrix. RH > 40% required. Full cure 24-72 hr. In dry air mist with water. Badge: `Needs moisture, not heat`.

**Panel 2 -- Epoxy Cross-Linking (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Resin A + amine B -> ring-opening -> 3D thermoset. Irreversible. Ambient 50-100 F. Force cure 140-180 F. Full cure 7-14 days. Badge: `Time + temperature dependent`.

**Panel 3 -- Moisture-Cure PU (accent `#E8A020`):** -NCO + moisture -> carbamic acid -> amine + CO2 -> urea linkages. Single component. RH > 30%. Full cure 24-72 hr. Badge: `One component, moisture does the work`.

## Phase 5 -- Cure Parameters + Topcoating

### Parameters Table (Y: 15.0"-21.5")

Section label: `CURE PARAMETERS -- ALL PRIMER TYPES`. 6-row table.

| Type | Mechanism | Conditions | Full Cure | Recoat Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IOZ (ethyl silicate) | Hydrolysis + condensation | Ambient, 40-80% RH | 24-72 hr | 24 hr-30 days |
| IOZ (alkali silicate) | Water evap + silicate hardening | Ambient | 24-48 hr | 24 hr-30 days |
| OZ (2K epoxy-zinc) | Amine-epoxy cross-linking | Ambient to 120 F | 7-14 days | 4-24 hr; 3-7 days max |
| OZ (moisture-cure PU) | Isocyanate + moisture | Ambient, > 30% RH | 24-72 hr | Per TDS |
| Epoxy (2K) | Amine cross-linking | Ambient to 120 F | 7-14 days | 4-24 hr; 3-7 days max |
| Chromated epoxy (aero) | Amine | 77 F or 250 F force | 7 days or 1 hr force | Per spec |

### Topcoating Over IOZ (Y: 21.5"-27.5")

Section label: `TOPCOATING OVER ZINC-RICH -- THE RULES`. Three parts.

Left -- Mist Coat: IOZ is porous (75-85% zinc + air voids). Full topcoat = solvent penetration = bubbling. Apply 0.5-1.0 mil mist coat to seal, flash 1-4 hr, then full coat. `Skip mist coat = bubbles. Every time.`

Center -- 30-Day Rule: Big stat `30` 72pt `#E05C5C`. Max days before topcoating. After 30 days: white zinc corrosion products. Sweep blast SP7 to remove, then topcoat immediately.

Right -- OZ Exception: Not porous like IOZ. No mist coat. Standard recoat. Scuff sand if max exceeded. `OZ is more forgiving for multi-coat systems.`
