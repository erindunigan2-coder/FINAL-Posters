### 4-Step Flow (Left, W: 14.5")

| Step | Accent | Title | Parameters | Critical Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#2EC4B6` | AQUEOUS ALKALINE CLEAN | 50--70 C; pH 10--12; 5--15 min immersion/spray | Remove all oils, greases, fluids, fingerprints |

## Phase 5 -- Contamination Types Table

Y: 14.0" to 22.0". Section label: `CONTAMINATION IDENTIFICATION AND REMOVAL`.

6-row table. Columns: Contaminant | Source | Removal Method | If Not Removed.

| Contaminant | Source | Removal | If Not Removed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Machining oils/coolants | CNC machining, grinding | Alkaline clean (Step 1) | Adhesion failure; delamination |
| Fingerprints | Bare-hand contact | Alkaline clean + glove protocol | Localized adhesion failure (pattern visible) |
| Shop dust/particulates | Ambient contamination | Alkaline clean + DI rinse | Inclusions at interface; porosity |
| Mineral deposits | Tap water rinse | DI water final rinse (Step 2) | White residue; adhesion reduction |
| Rust/oxide scale | Storage corrosion | Grit blast removes | Must be removed by blasting |
| Residual cleaning agent | Incomplete rinsing | Extended DI rinse; verify quality | Chemical contamination at interface |

"If Not Removed" column in `#E05C5C`. Contaminant names in `#E8A020`. Header fill `#3A4055`.

## Phase 6 -- Time Windows + Common Failures

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

### Left -- Critical Time Windows (W: 11.5")

Section label: `CRITICAL TIME WINDOWS`. Three stacked cards:

| Window | Accent | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLEAN TO BLAST | `#E8A020` | < 4 hours (same shift preferred) | Surface re-contaminates with dust and humidity |
| BLAST TO SPRAY | `#E05C5C` | < 4 hours (some specs <2 hrs) | Blasted surface oxidizes; humidity accelerates |
| RINSE TO DRY | `#2EC4B6` | Immediately | Standing water leaves deposits; promotes flash rust |

Humidity note: `In >60% RH, reduce all windows by 50%. Monitor with hygrometer.` Amber callout.

### Right -- Common Cleaning Failures (W: 11.0")

Section label: `COMMON CLEANING FAILURES`. Five stacked cards:

| Failure | Color | Cause | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| WATER BREAK ON SURFACE | `#E05C5C` | Incomplete oil removal | Adhesion failure at spots |
| FINGERPRINT PATTERN IN COATING | `#E05C5C` | Bare-hand contact after cleaning | Localized delamination matching finger |
| WHITE RESIDUE AT INTERFACE | `#E8A020` | Tap water instead of DI | Mineral inclusions; reduced bond |
| FLASH RUST BEFORE BLAST | `#E8A020` | Excessive time; high humidity | Oxide layer reduces adhesion |
| CLEANING AGENT RESIDUE | `#2EC4B6` | Insufficient rinsing | Chemical contamination; gas evolution |
