# Poster #100 -- Construction Workup
## Copper Plating (Acid) -- Main Tank

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. The acid copper sulfate plating tank. This is the densest poster in the EP-09 cluster -- comparable to Poster #36 (Zinc Alkaline Main Tank) and Poster #23 (Watts Nickel). The bath is deceptively simple: copper sulfate, sulfuric acid, chloride ion, and three organic additives (carrier, brightener, leveler). The devil is in the chloride and additive balance. Near 100% cathode efficiency. Phosphorized copper anodes are mandatory.

Hero visual: plating tank cross-section with phosphorized copper anodes, workpiece, current flow lines, and the carrier-brightener-Cl- interaction diagram.

### ZONE 5 -- Defect Diagnosis Grid

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK F -- 3x2 Grid**

Y: 21.3" to 26.3".

| Position | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | HAZY / MILKY | `#2EC4B6` | Low Cl-, organic contamination, low brightener | Check Cl- by AgNO3 titration; carbon treat |
| R1C2 | DULL DEPOSIT | `#E8A020` | Excess Cl- (>100 ppm), organic byproducts | Dilute bath or treat with H2O2; reduce HCl addition |
| R1C3 | ROUGHNESS | `#E05C5C` | Anode sludge (wrong anode, no bags), particulate | Install PP anode bags; increase filtration |
| R2C1 | PITTING | `#E05C5C` | Oil contamination, low carrier, poor agitation | Carbon treat; add carrier; increase air agitation |
| R2C2 | BURNING (HCD) | `#E8A020` | CD too high, low Cu metal, high acid | Reduce CD; add CuSO4; check acid:Cu ratio |
| R2C3 | SKIP PLATING | `#E05C5C` | No strike on steel, immersion deposit, silicate film | Apply copper strike; check cleaner for silicates |

## Design Notes

This is the most information-dense poster in EP-09 and poster number 100 in the series -- a milestone. The Cl- control gauge is the visual equivalent of the NaOH:Zn ratio gauge in the zinc main tank poster. In acid copper, chloride is THE single most important additive control. The four-component breakdown (instead of three for zinc) reflects the additional complexity of the brightener-carrier-Cl- interaction.

The phosphorized copper anode callout earns its space because wrong anodes in acid copper are a shop-killing mistake. Pure copper anodes produce Cu+ and particle contamination that no amount of filtration can fully solve.

Note: pH is NOT measured in acid copper. The bath contains 45--100 g/L concentrated sulfuric acid. Always show pH as "--" or "N/A (strongly acidic)" in all tables and callouts.
