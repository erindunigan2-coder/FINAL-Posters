## Phases 5-7 -- Content Zones

Bath options: Oxalic acid 30--50 g/L (+ H2SO4 optional), or Sulfophthalic acid blend (proprietary). Temp: 50--75 F (10--24 C) -- LOWER than Type II. Voltage: 50--80 V (higher than Type II). Time: 30--60 min. Film: 15--25 um.

Color control: shade depends on alloy + voltage + time + temperature. Color matching requires tight process control. Bronze to black range only -- no bright colors.

Alloy effects: 6063 produces truest colors. 6061 acceptable. 2024/7075 produce uneven colors.

UV stability: EXCELLENT -- integral color is part of the oxide, not a dye trapped in pores.

4 defect cards: wrong shade, uneven color, streaking, soft coating.
