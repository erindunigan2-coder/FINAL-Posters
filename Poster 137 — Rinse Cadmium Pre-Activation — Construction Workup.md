# Poster #137 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Cadmium -- Pre-Activation

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of 8. Standard pre-activation rinse. Removes alkaline cleaner drag-out before acid activation. For cadmium plating, the rinse itself is standard, but the context is not -- every minute counts because the next step (acid activation) introduces hydrogen embrittlement risk to high-strength steel.

### ZONE 6 -- Contamination Consequences

**Section label:** `WHEN THE RINSE FAILS` -- Y: 26.7".

**BLOCK F -- Four Cards (Y: 27.3" to 32.3")**

| Card | Problem | Cause | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ALKALINE IN ACID BATH | Insufficient rinse | Acid neutralized; activation fails; poor adhesion |
| 2 | EXTENDED ACID TIME | Weak acid requires longer dip | More hydrogen absorbed into high-strength steel |
| 3 | PARTICULATE CARRY-OVER | Dirty rinse water | Rough cadmium deposit |
| 4 | INCONSISTENT ACTIVATION | Variable rinse quality | Variable cadmium adhesion lot to lot |
