## Phase 5 -- Preheat Decision + Cleaning

**Preheat Flowchart**: Section < 2" --> no preheat needed | Section > 2" --> preheat to 800--1000 F, hold 30--60 min. Why: heavy sections develop thermal gradients that cause cracking.

**Cleaning Protocol** (6 items): solvent degrease, remove scale, verify completely dry, inspect for defects, no salt contamination (if oil quench), no oil contamination (if salt quench). Contaminant risks: moisture = explosion (salt)/boil-over (oil), oil = fire in salt, scale = non-uniform quench.

---

## Phase 6 -- Hardenability Requirement + Footer

**Hardenability Panel**: During 5--15 min hold, steel must remain 100% austenite. Low-hardenability steels transform during hold --> mixed microstructure. Quick reference: 4340 EXCELLENT, 4140 GOOD, 52100 GOOD, H13 EXCELLENT, 1045 POOR (< 0.5" only), 1020 NOT SUITABLE.
