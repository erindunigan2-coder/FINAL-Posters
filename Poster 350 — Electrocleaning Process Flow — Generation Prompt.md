### Step 5 -- Mode selection decision diamond

Diamond below Box 3: `SELECT MODE` (`#E8A020`). Three branches:

**Anodic (+)** (emerald-accented glass card):
- `Work = Anode (+)` / `O2 at surface` / `No H embrittlement` / `Required before Ni`
- Tag: `PREFERRED FOR MOST PLATING`

**Cathodic (-)** (coral-accented glass card):
- `Work = Cathode (-)` / `H2 at surface` / `Aggressive scrub` / `Risk: H embrittlement + smut`
- Tag: `HEAVY SOIL ONLY -- NEVER FINAL STEP BEFORE Ni`

**Periodic Reverse** (teal-accented glass card):
- `Alternating Polarity` / `Cathodic scrub + anodic finish` / `Best compromise`
- Tag: `FINAL PHASE MUST BE ANODIC`

All three converge to: `RINSE --> ACID ACTIVATE --> PLATE` strip.

## Phase 5 -- Mode Comparison Table

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `ANODIC vs. CATHODIC vs. PERIODIC REVERSE -- THE FULL PICTURE`.

Glass table. Columns: Property | Anodic | Cathodic | Periodic Reverse.

| Property | Anodic | Cathodic | Periodic Reverse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work Polarity | Anode (+) | Cathode (-) | Alternates |
| Gas at Work | O2 (oxygen) | H2 (hydrogen) | Both alternately |
| Gas Volume | Lower (O2) | Higher (H2) -- more scrubbing | Both |
| H Embrittlement Risk | NONE (`#27AE60`) | HIGH (`#E05C5C`) on >40 HRC steel | Reduced (anodic final phase) |
| Cathodic Smut Risk | NONE (`#27AE60`) | YES (`#E05C5C`) -- dissolved metals plate onto work | Stripped by anodic phase |
| Cleaning Aggressiveness | Moderate | Aggressive | Best overall |
| Pre-Nickel | REQUIRED (`#27AE60`) | NEVER (`#E05C5C`) as final step | OK if final phase is anodic |
| Typical Cycle | 60-120 sec | 60-120 sec | 10s cathodic / 10s anodic (or 3:1 ratio) |

## Phase 6 -- Why Electroclean?

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section label: `WHY ELECTROCLEAN? -- WHAT SOAK CLEANING CANNOT DO`.

Two glass cards.

**Left -- Soak Clean Alone** (slate-accented):
- Removes: Bulk oils, drawing compounds, fingerprints, most visible contamination
- Cannot remove: Monolayer organic films, embedded smut, oxide films in recesses, contamination in surface roughness
- `GOOD -- but not good enough for critical plating` (`#E8A020`)

**Right -- Soak Clean + Electroclean** (emerald-accented):
- Gas bubbles physically scrub the surface / Anodic dissolution removes thin metal layer / Exposes fresh, active, oxide-free metal / Final monolayer contamination eliminated
- `THE INDUSTRY STANDARD for decorative Ni/Cr, gold, silver, and all critical deposits` (`#27AE60`)

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

Four coral-accented glass cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| ADHESION FAILURE | Cathodic smut; cathodic before nickel | Switch to anodic; carbon treat; check metal contamination |
| H EMBRITTLEMENT | Cathodic on high-strength steel (>40 HRC) | ALWAYS anodic for HRC >40; bake 375-410 F within 4 hrs per ASTM B849 |
| UNEVEN GAS | Poor electrical contact; rack tip corrosion | Clean rack tips; check bus bar connections |
| EXCESSIVE FOAM | Surfactant overdose; oil drag-in | Reduce surfactant; improve soak cleaner upstream |
