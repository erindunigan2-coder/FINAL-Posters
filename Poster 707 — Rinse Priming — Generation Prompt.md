## Phase 5 -- No Rinse Rule + Humidity + Dew Point

### No Rinse + Humidity (Y: 14.0"-20.5")

Two columns.

Left -- Why No Rinse: Water = immediate flash rust. Flash rust = weak oxide. IOZ galvanic circuit broken. Blast profile IS the prep. Rule: `Blast -> Blow-Down -> Prime. No water. No exceptions (on steel).`

Right -- Humidity Table: < 40% = 8-12+ hr. 40-60% = 4-8 hr. 60-80% = 2-4 hr. > 80% = 30 min-2 hr. Rain/dew = zero (do not blast).

### Dew Point (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `DEW POINT -- THE 5 DEG F RULE`. Big stat: `5 deg F` 72pt `#E8A020`. Surface temp must exceed dew point by 5 F minimum. Condensation causes amine blush (epoxy) or cure inhibition (IOZ). Measure: sling psychrometer + surface thermometer per ASTM E337, or digital dew point meter. Check every shift.

---

## Phase 6 -- Aerospace Exception + Flash Rust Grading

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two columns.

Left -- Aerospace Exception: Al DOES include rinse/dry steps after conversion coating. Alkaline clean -> rinse -> deoxidize -> rinse -> conversion coating -> rinse -> dry -> prime within 24 hr. DI/RO water < 50 uS/cm.

Right -- Flash Rust Grading: None (bright, prime immediately `#27AE60`). Light (faint tint, most primers accept `#E8A020`). Medium (visible staining, re-blast recommended `#E05C5C`). Heavy (dense rust, MUST re-blast `#E05C5C`). Ref: ASTM D7087, SSPC-VIS 4/5.
