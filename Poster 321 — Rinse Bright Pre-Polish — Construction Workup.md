# Poster #321 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse (Pre-Polish) -- Bright Anodizing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of 8. This rinse removes all alkaline cleaner residue before the bright dip. Cleaner drag-in to the bright dip raises the pH of the phosphoric/nitric acid mix, reduces polishing efficiency, and generates foam that traps parts in a corrosive vapor zone. On bright work, inadequate rinsing shows as haze, streaks, or uneven polishing in the bright dip.

## Design Notes

This rinse poster has more urgency than typical rinse posters because the downstream process (bright dip) operates at near-boiling temperature -- any contamination is amplified dramatically. The foam generation hazard is unique to bright dip contamination and should be visually prominent.
