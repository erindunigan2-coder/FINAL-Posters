## Phase 6 -- Cathodic vs. Anodic Comparison

Y: 23.3" to 28.3". Section label: `CATHODIC VS. ANODIC -- WHICH E-COAT?`.

**Left -- `CATHODIC EPOXY E-COAT` (accent `#E8A020`):** >95% automotive worldwide. Cathode = no metal dissolution. 200--400 V. 18--22% solids. DFT 0.6--1.2 mils. Throwing power excellent. 500--1,000+ hr B117. 316 SS anodes.

**Right -- `ANODIC ACRYLIC E-COAT` (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Simpler chemistry. Anode = metal dissolves. 50--250 V. 8--14% solids. DFT 0.4--0.8 mils. Moderate throwing power. Carbon/graphite anodes. Small appliances.

## Phase 6B -- Troubleshooting Strip

Y: 29.4" to 32.3". 4 cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CRATERS / PINHOLES | Phosphate porosity or bath contamination | Improve seal rinse; filter bath |
| THIN FILM IN CAVITIES | Voltage too low or conductivity off | Increase voltage; check 1,000--1,800 uS/cm |
| POOR CURE (MEK FAIL) | Low oven temp or insufficient time | Verify metal temp 340--360 F for 20--30 min |
| ORANGE PEEL / ROUGHNESS | Solids too high or P/B ratio drift | Check 18--22%; verify P/B 0.15--0.25 |
