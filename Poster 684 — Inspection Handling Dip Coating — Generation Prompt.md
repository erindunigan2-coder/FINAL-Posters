## Phase 4 -- Five-Test Battery Hero

Y: 5.0" to 15.0". Section label: `THE FIVE-TEST BATTERY -- ADAPTED FOR THICK COATINGS`. Top row 3, bottom row 2.

**1. DFT (`#27AE60`):** Thin < 10 mils: D7091. Thick > 10 mils: micrometer or ultrasonic. Range 5--40+ mils. `Specify MINIMUM DFT, not target.`

**2. Adhesion (`#2EC4B6`):** Thin < 5 mils: D3359 cross-hatch. Thick > 5 mils: knife adhesion test -- score and peel by hand. Pass: cannot peel. `Cross-hatch does not work on 20-mil films.`

**3. Hardness (`#E8A020`):** D2240. Shore A: soft/flexible (plastisol, rubber). Shore D: hard thermoplastics (nylon). `Shore A for PVC/rubber. Shore D for nylon. Never mix scales.`

**4. Flexibility (`#27AE60`):** Thin: D522 mandrel. Thick: cold-temp bend at -20 F. PVC must stay flexible at service temp. `Cold bend is the real-world test.`

**5. Visual (`#E8A020`):** Drip marks, bare spots, bridging, pinholes, color, texture. Bright, even lighting.

## Phase 6 -- Cold-Temp Flexibility + Chemical Resistance

Y: 22.3" to 26.3". Two-column.

**Left -- `COLD-TEMPERATURE BEND TEST` (border `#27AE60`):** -20 F outdoor / 0 F freezer / 32 F mild. Pass: no cracking. For dishwasher racks, outdoor furniture, automotive underbody.

**Right -- `CHEMICAL SPOT TEST` (`#E8A020`):** Reagent drops 1--24 hr. 10% NaOH, 10% H2SO4, detergent, mineral oil, gasoline. PVC: good chemical, poor solvent. Nylon: resists solvents, absorbs moisture.

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| DFT BELOW MINIMUM | `#E05C5C` | Insufficient dip time or low viscosity | Increase time; raise viscosity |
| ADHESION FAILURE (PEEL) | `#E05C5C` | Missing/wrong primer | Verify per matrix (Poster #680) |
| DRIP MARKS AT BOTTOM | `#E8A020` | Gravity drainage | Rotate; extend drain |
| PINHOLES / BLISTERS | `#E8A020` | Outgassing or trapped moisture | Pre-bake; complete drying |
| BRIDGING | `#2EC4B6` | High viscosity spanning openings | Lower viscosity; increase drain |
| COLD BEND FAILURE | `#2EC4B6` | Wrong plasticizer or coating for temp | Increase plasticizer; select nylon |
