Nine flow boxes in U-flow (top row 5 L-to-R, vertical connector, bottom row 4 R-to-L). Each box: W 4.2", H 4.3", fill `#1E2435`, radius 8, top accent 4 pt. Arrows: 3 pt `#3A4055`.

**Top Row:**

| # | Stage | Accent | Key Params | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-Clean | `#2EC4B6` | Alkaline wash, rinse, dry | Remove oils, chips, ALL moisture -- moisture + molten salt = EXPLOSION |
| 2 | Preheat | `#E8A020` | 600--700 F (316--371 C), air | Mandatory moisture removal before molten salt -- safety-critical step |
| 3 | Nitrocarburize | `#27AE60` | Salt bath 1050--1075 F, NaCNO/KCNO, 60--120 min | N + C diffusion into ferrite; epsilon Fe2-3N compound zone |
| 4 | Oxidizing Quench Q1 | `#E8A020` | NaNO3/NaNO2 salt 700--800 F, 15--30 min | Creates magnetite (Fe3O4) layer sealing compound zone pores |
| 5 | Rinse | `#2EC4B6` | Hot water rinse | Remove residual salt before polish |

**Bottom Row (R-to-L):**

| # | Stage | Accent | Key Params | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Polish (P) | `#2EC4B6` | Buffing/lapping, Ra 8--16 micro-inch | Smooth surface, expose fresh compound zone for Q2 |
| 7 | Second Oxidizing Quench Q2 | `#E8A020` | Same bath 700--800 F, 15--30 min | Second oxide layer on polished surface -- completes corrosion system |
| 8 | Final Rinse | `#2EC4B6` | Hot water + rust preventative | Remove all salt; dry thoroughly |
| 9 | Inspection & QA | `#27AE60` | Zone 10--25 um, 600--1000 HV, 200--500 hr salt spray | Verify compound zone, hardness, corrosion, appearance |

## Phase 6 -- Process Variants + Troubleshooting

### Process Variants (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `FNC PROCESS VARIANTS -- SAME METALLURGY, DIFFERENT METHODS`.

Four variant cards in a row:

| Card | Name | Accent | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SALT BATH FNC | `#E8A020` | Original method. Molten cyanate salt at 1050--1075 F. Uniform case. Requires cyanide waste management. |
| 2 | GAS FNC | `#27AE60` | NH3 + CO2 atmosphere. No molten salt. Lower corrosion resistance unless supplemented. |
| 3 | Q-P-Q | `#2EC4B6` | Full oxidizing quench + polish + second quench. Maximum corrosion: 200--500 hr salt spray. Gold standard. |
| 4 | PLASMA FNC | `#C8D0D8` | Ion bombardment in vacuum. Precise case control. Selective treatment. Most expensive. |

Pill callout below: `Different methods, same metallurgy. Every FNC variant produces an epsilon iron nitride compound zone. Q-P-Q adds the oxidizing quench and polish that makes corrosion resistance exceptional.` Teal.

### Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON FNC/Q-P-Q FAILURES`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| POOR CORROSION | Insufficient quench time; poor polish | Extend Q1/Q2; improve polish to spec Ra |
| THIN COMPOUND ZONE | Low bath temp; depleted cyanate | Verify temp; replenish to 35--40% CNO |
| STAINING/DISCOLORATION | Salt residue; contaminated bath | Improve rinse; maintain bath purity |
| PITTING | Moisture on parts before immersion | Improve preheat; extend preheat time |

Coral left accent. Fix text in `#27AE60`.
