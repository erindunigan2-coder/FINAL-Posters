## Phase 4 -- Case Depth Control (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `THREE VARIABLES CONTROL CASE DEPTH`. Three side-by-side variable panels:

| Variable | Accent | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| FLAME INTENSITY | `#E8A020` | Controlled by tip size and gas pressure. Higher = faster surface heating = shallower case. |
| DISTANCE | `#2EC4B6` | 0.25--0.75 in gap. Closer = more intense, narrower. Farther = gentler, wider. |
| SPEED | `#27AE60` | Traverse: 2--12 in/min. Rotation: 30--120 RPM. Slower = deeper case. |

Key insight: `Unlike induction (where frequency is the primary depth control), flame hardening has NO frequency variable.`

Parameter table (7 rows): surface temp 1500--1650 F, flame distance 0.25--0.75 in, traverse 2--12 in/min, rotation 30--120 RPM, spot heat 10--60 sec, typical case 0.050--0.250 in, tolerance +/- 0.030 in.

## Phase 5 -- Four Heating Methods

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `FOUR HEATING METHODS`. 2x2 grid.

| Method | Accent | Parameters | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPOT (STATIONARY) | `#E8A020` | Both stationary; 10--60 sec | Localized hardening, repair |
| PROGRESSIVE (SCANNING) | `#2EC4B6` | Traverse 2--12 in/min; spray follows | Ways, rails, shafts; most repeatable |
| SPIN | `#27AE60` | 30--120 RPM; stationary flame head | Journals, bushings, rolls |
| COMBINATION | `#E8A020` | Rotates AND traverses | Long cylindrical parts |
