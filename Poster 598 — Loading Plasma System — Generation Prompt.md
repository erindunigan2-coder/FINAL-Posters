## Phase 5 -- Hollow Cathode Effect

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `THE HOLLOW CATHODE EFFECT -- KNOW THE ENEMY` in `#E05C5C`.

**Left -- PROBLEM (`#E05C5C`):** Parts too close = plasma concentrates in gap = local temp spikes, surface damage, melting. Also inside blind holes and small bores. Rules: min hole dia = 3x depth; min spacing = 10 mm.

**Right -- SOLUTION (`#27AE60`):** Maintain 10 mm; plug violating holes; pulsed DC duty cycle; Active Screen (ASPN) eliminates entirely -- screen is cathode, parts at floating potential, no arcing.

## Phase 6 -- Loading Rules + Active Screen

### Loading Rules (Y: 22.0"--32.5", left)

7-row checklist:

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Every part must contact cathode | No contact = no plasma = no nitriding |
| Min 10 mm spacing | Prevent hollow cathode |
| Blind holes: dia = 3x depth | Concentrated plasma |
| Non-magnetic fixtures | Magnetic distorts plasma field |
| TC on representative parts | Pulsed DC adjusts based on TC |
| Parts must not shadow each other | Shadowed areas = less bombardment |
| Flat parts: vertical or on pins | Contact surfaces will not nitride |

### Active Screen vs. Conventional (right)

**CONVENTIONAL DC:** Parts = cathode; plasma directly on parts. PRO: sputters passive films. CON: arcing, edge effect, hollow cathode.

**ASPN (`#27AE60`):** Metal screen = cathode; parts at floating potential; plasma on screen not parts; N species diffuse from screen. PRO: no arcing, uniform, handles complex geometry. CON: higher cost. Badge: `INCREASINGLY COMMON IN PRODUCTION`.
