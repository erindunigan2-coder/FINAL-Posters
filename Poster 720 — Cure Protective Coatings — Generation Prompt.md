## Phase 5 -- Temperature Effects + Immersion

### Temperature Cards (Y: 14.5"-20.5")

Section label: `TEMPERATURE -- THE MASTER VARIABLE`. Three cards.

**Cold < 50 F (`#2EC4B6`, badge CAUTION):** Amine epoxies cure extremely slowly or stop. Blush risk increases. Fix: cycloaliphatic amine, heated enclosure.

**Ideal 50-90 F (`#27AE60`, badge OPTIMAL):** Standard range. Normal pot life. Watch overnight drops. 65-80 F sweet spot.

**Hot > 90 F (`#E05C5C`, badge DANGER):** Pot life collapses. Material may gel. Fix: reduce batch, shade materials, apply cooler hours, extended-pot-life hardener.

### Immersion Service (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `IMMERSION SERVICE -- THE FULL-CURE REQUIREMENT`. Two columns.

Left -- Why: Tank linings require FULL cure before chemical contact. Min 7 days at 77 F (amine). Force cure 150-200 F. Potable water: NSF/ANSI 61. Filling undercured = softening + blistering within weeks.

Right -- Verification Before Filling: MEK D4752 100+ rubs. Shore D D2240 > 75. Holiday wet sponge D5162 at 67.5 V. Holiday spark NACE SP0188 at 100 V/mil. DFT PA 2. `DO NOT fill based on time alone. VERIFY cure. Filling undercured = five-to-six-figure rework.`

## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| INCOMPLETE CURE (SOFT) | Temp too low / wrong mix ratio | Verify ratio; increase temp; low-temp hardener |
| AMINE BLUSH | Cool/humid cure; dew overnight | Wash + scrub; sand; prevent with 5 F rule |
| POT LIFE EXPIRED (GELLED) | Past pot life; high temp | Discard; reduce batch; track time |
| TANK LINING FAILURE | Filled before full cure / moisture | Verify cure (MEK 100+, Shore D 75+); verify moisture |

Key insight: `"Dry to touch" may be only 20-30% cured. The only reliable verification is the solvent rub test.`
