## Phase 4 -- Single vs. Two-Stage (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section label: `SINGLE-STAGE VS. TWO-STAGE (FLOE) PROCESS`.

**Left -- SINGLE-STAGE (`#E8A020`):** 925--975 F; 15--30% dissociation; 24--90 hr; WL 0.0005--0.001 in. Simple to operate; produces relatively thick WL; limited compound zone control. Use when WL acceptable or desired; Class 2; non-aerospace.

**Right -- TWO-STAGE FLOE (`#27AE60`):** Stage 1: 925--975 F, 15--30% diss, 15--40 hr (case building). Stage 2: 1000--1050 F, 75--85% diss, 10--30 hr (WL control). Stage 2 raises temp + dissociation = lower nitrogen activity at surface = WL stops growing while diffusion zone deepens. Use for aerospace (AMS 2759/6), Class 0 or 1. Highlight: `Stage 2 decouples case depth from white layer thickness`.

## Phase 5 -- KN + White Layer Classes

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `NITRIDING POTENTIAL (KN) & WHITE LAYER CLASSES -- AMS 2759/10`.

**Left -- KN Definition:** Formula `KN = P(NH3) / P(H2)^(3/2)` in `#E8A020` 20 pt. Higher KN = more N at surface = faster compound zone growth. Lower KN = less N = reduced/absent compound zone. Automated KN control uses H2 + NH3 sensors for real-time regulation.

**Right -- White Layer Classes:**

| Class | WL Maximum | Stage 1 KN | Stage 2 KN |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | NO white layer | 4--15 | 0.2--0.8 |
| 1 | 0.0005 in (12.7 um) | 4--15 | 0.4--2.6 |
| 2 | 0.001 in (25 um) | 4--15 | 1.2--5.5 |

Note: `Stage 1 KN high (4--15) for all classes. Stage 2 KN varies dramatically -- Class 0 drops to 0.2--0.8.`

## Phase 6 -- Monitoring + Case Depth

### Monitoring Methods (Y: 22.0"--27.5")

Section label: `MONITORING & CONTROL METHODS`. 3 cards:

**BURETTE METHOD (`#2EC4B6`):** Sample exhaust over KOH; vol reduction = % undissociated; every 1--4 hr; +/-2--3%; standard per AMS 2759/6D.

**HYDROGEN SENSOR (`#E8A020`):** Thermal conductivity; continuous; +/-1% H2; required for AMS 2759/10A KN control.

**NH3 ANALYZER (`#E8A020`):** IR or electrochemical; paired with H2 for KN calculation; continuous; PLC closed-loop.
