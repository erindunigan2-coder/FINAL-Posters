Three water quality cards:

## Phase 6 -- Contamination Transfer

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GETS DRAGGED INTO THE PHOSPHATE BATH`.

| Contaminant | Source | Impact on Phosphate |
|---|---|---|
| Alkaline residue | Poor upstream rinsing | Raises bath pH; reduces coating weight |
| Calcium / Magnesium | Hard rinse water | Interferes with Fe phosphate deposition; spotty coating |
| Chlorides | City water or acid carry-over | Accelerates flash rust between stages |
| Silicates | Cleaner drag-through | Inhibits phosphate nucleation; bare spots |
| Oil / organics | Incomplete cleaning | Skip areas; poor adhesion |

Bottom: `In 3-stage systems, this rinse does not exist. The phosphate bath must tolerate all upstream contamination -- which is why 5-stage lines produce better coatings.` `#E8A020`

## Phase 7 -- Parameters & Monitoring

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

| Parameter | Target | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | Ambient to 80 F | Thermometer |
| Conductivity | < 500 uS/cm (< 200 ideal) | Conductivity meter |
| pH | 6.0--8.0 (neutral) | pH meter |
| Overflow rate | 1--3 gal/min | Flow meter or visual |
| Hardness | < 150 ppm (as CaCO3) | Test kit or water report |

Three key rules: `KEEP IT FLOWING` / `DUMP WHEN DIRTY` / `MATCH TO SPEC`.
