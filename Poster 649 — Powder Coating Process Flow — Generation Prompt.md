## Phase 6 -- Zero VOC Comparison + Chemistry Families

**Left -- Powder Coating** (Emerald): zero VOC, 60--70% first-pass transfer, 95--98% total utilization, 2--4 mils single coat, 350--400 F cure. Highlight: `No solvent. No overspray waste. No VOC permits.`

**Right -- Liquid Spray Paint** (Amber): 2.0--4.2 lb/gal VOC, 25--45% transfer, 50--75% utilization, 1--3 mils multi-coat, air dry to 350 F.

**Chemistry Families** (6 cards): Epoxy (Coral, poor UV, interior) | Hybrid (Amber, moderate UV) | Polyester TGIC (Emerald, excellent UV, architectural) | Polyester HAA (Emerald, TGIC-free) | Urethane (Teal, auto wheels) | Acrylic (Teal, auto clearcoat).
