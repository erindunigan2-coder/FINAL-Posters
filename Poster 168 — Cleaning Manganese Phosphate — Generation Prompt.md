Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `CLEANING APPROACH -- SINGLE VS. TWO-STAGE`.

**Left -- Soak Clean:** Teal accent. 4--10 oz/gal | 150--190 F | 5--15 min | Adequate for lightly soiled parts.

**Right -- Two-Stage (Soak + Electroclean):** Amber accent. Soak first for bulk contamination, electroclean second for final prep. Cathodic or anodic, 3--8 V, 50--100 ASF. Required for heavily contaminated parts.
