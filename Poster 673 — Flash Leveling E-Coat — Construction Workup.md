# Poster #673 -- Construction Workup
## Flash / Leveling -- E-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 7 of 9. E-coat breaks every rule of conventional painting. There is no leveling in the traditional sense -- the electric field deposits the film exactly where it belongs. No flash time for solvent evaporation -- e-coat is aqueous with no volatile solvents in the deposited film. What this stage DOES cover is the post-e-coat drain zone, air blow-off, and the UF permeate recovery system that makes e-coat the most material-efficient coating process on the planet.

## Design Notes

This poster flips the "flash/leveling" concept on its head. By comparing e-coat to spray and powder side by side, we show why e-coat is fundamentally different -- and fundamentally better for material efficiency. The >99% paint utilization stat is the hero number. The drain zone sequence grounds the concept in practical operations: what actually happens between the e-coat tank and the bake oven. The environmental comparison reinforces that e-coat is the cleanest coating process in existence from an application standpoint.
