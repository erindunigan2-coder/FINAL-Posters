## Phase 5 -- Drag-Out Chemistry Downstream

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHAT CLEANER DRAG-OVER DOES DOWNSTREAM`.

Four connected callout boxes:
1. ETCH TANK: Alkaline drag-over raises pH, depletes free NaOH, causes uneven etch rate. Hardcoat etch is SHORT (30--90 sec) -- any dilution proportionally larger.
2. DESMUT TANK: Alkaline contamination neutralizes acid. Smut residue remains -- trapped under hard coat = delamination.
3. ANODIZE TANK: Hardcoat runs at 10--12% H2SO4. Lower concentration = less tolerance. Cleaner surfactants cause foaming.
4. FINAL COATING: Soft spots. Burning. Non-uniform thickness. Delamination. All traceable to a 30-second rinse that was too short.

## Phase 7 -- Hardcoat-Specific Rinse Rules

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `TYPE III RINSE -- KEY RULES`.

1. Cascade rinse preferred -- single immersion NOT adequate
2. Dwell 10--15 sec above cleaner tank to drain
3. Agitate parts in rinse -- move rack 3--5 times
4. Conductivity: <200 uS/cm minimum, <50 uS/cm aerospace
5. If etch is SKIPPED, this rinse feeds directly into desmut -- extra thoroughness required
6. Ambient temperature -- no heating required
