# Poster #553 -- Construction Workup
## Masking & Fixturing -- Wire Combustion Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Wire combustion spray masking is the simplest in the thermal spray family. No robots, no complex fixtures, no precision jigging. Tape, aluminum foil, metal shields, and common sense. The process is hand-held and manual -- the operator IS the manipulator. Shop work may use a turntable for cylindrical parts; field work uses whatever gets the job done within spec. This poster keeps it practical and field-oriented.

### ZONE 4 -- Masking Best Practices

**Section label:** `MASKING RULES & EDGE QUALITY` -- Y: 22.2".

**Left -- BLOCK F: Application Rules (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6` 0.06".

Title: `MASKING APPLICATION RULES` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#2EC4B6`.

1. `Apply masking AFTER grit blasting, not before`
2. `Press tape edges firmly -- lifted edges cause feathered overspray`
3. `Overlap tape strips by minimum 6 mm (1/4")`
4. `Metal shields should overhang spray edge by minimum 25 mm (1")`
5. `Masking must withstand atomizing air blast (40--80 PSI)`
6. `Check masking integrity after each spray pass`
7. `Remove masking while coating is still warm (easier removal)`

**Right -- BLOCK G: Edge Quality (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020` 0.06".

Title: `OVERSPRAY & EDGE CONTROL` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`.

- `Wire spray produces more overspray than plasma or HVOF (lower velocity)`
- `Overspray dust settles on masked areas -- shake off between passes`
- `Tape edge produces sharper line than metal shield (shield creates a gap)`
- `For critical edges: tape the edge line, then shield the bulk area`
- `Overspray beyond masking is normal -- plan clean-up`
- `Specification may define acceptable overspray zone width`

**BLOCK H -- Four Mistake Cards**

| Card | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | MASKING BLOWS OFF | Tape not pressed firmly; wind lifts edges | Press tape edges hard; use metal shields in wind; add tape anchors |
| 2 | 6.33" | OVERSPRAY UNDER MASK | Gap between mask and surface; lifted tape edge | Press edges; use tape-then-shield combo for critical edges |
| 3 | 12.16" | CHARRED CARDBOARD | Flame too close to masked area; cardboard not rated for heat | Use metal shields near flame path; replace cardboard with foil tape |
| 4 | 18.0" | COATING PULLS WITH MASK | Masking removed too late; coating bonded to tape edge | Remove masking while still warm; score edge with knife before pulling |
