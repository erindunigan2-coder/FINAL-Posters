## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting + Footer

### Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four coral-accented cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| POROSITY IN COATING | Insufficient velocity or poor gas ratios | Verify O2/C2H2; check barrel; increase frequency |
| POOR BOND STRENGTH | Surface contamination or stale blast | Re-blast; reduce time; verify cleanliness |
| UNEVEN THICKNESS | Robot path error or inconsistent powder charge | Reprogram traverse; verify metering calibration |
| EXCESSIVE NOISE/VIBRATION | Barrel wear or misalignment; fixture resonance | Inspect barrel bore; check cooling; isolate fixture |
