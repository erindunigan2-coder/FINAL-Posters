## Phase 4 -- 4-Step Cleaning Sequence (HERO)

Four vertical step cards with downward arrows between them. Each card: X: 1.0", W: 22.0", H: 2.2", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6.

| Step | Y | Accent | Name | Method | Pass/Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5.0" | `#2EC4B6` | SOLVENT DEGREASE | Aqueous alkaline clean (preferred) or vapor degrease (legacy). Remove all oils, greases, machining fluids, fingerprints. | Visible contamination = fail |
| 2 | 7.8" | `#2EC4B6` | ALKALINE WASH | Immersion or spray wash. 50-70 degC, pH 10-12, 5-15 min. Rinse thoroughly. DI water final rinse for aerospace. | Residual alkaline = fail |
| 3 | 10.6" | `#27AE60` | WATER-BREAK-FREE TEST | ASTM F22 equivalent. Surface must sheet water uniformly with no beading. Any beading = residual contamination. | Any water break = FAIL -- repeat Steps 1-2 |
| 4 | 13.4" | `#E8A020` | DRY | Forced air or oven dry. No moisture at time of grit blast. Moisture causes flash rust on steel substrates. | Surface must be bone dry |

## Phase 5 -- Chrome-Strip Handling + Time Windows

### Chrome-Strip Handling (Y: 15.5"-22.0")

Section label: `CHROME REPLACEMENT JOBS -- INCOMING PART HANDLING` centered, 28 pt.

**Left -- The Problem (W: 11.0", accent `#E05C5C`):**
Title: `PARTS ARRIVING FROM CHROME STRIP`. Common incoming conditions: residual chrome not fully removed; etch products from acid strip (pitting); hydrogen embrittlement from stripping acid; dimensional undersize from strip + grinding. Every one affects bond strength.

**Right -- The Solution (W: 11.5", accent `#27AE60`):**
Title: `VERIFICATION STEPS`. Six numbered items: visual inspect for residual chrome (shiny patches); spot-check with dilute copper sulfate solution (copper deposits on steel, not on chrome); verify dimensions before cleaning; if acid-stripped: bake 375 degF / 4 hrs per ASTM B849; full alkaline clean sequence AFTER bake; proceed to grit blast only when clean and verified.

Bottom note full width: `If residual chrome is found, DO NOT proceed to grit blast -- return to stripping. Blasting over chrome creates a weak interface that will delaminate in service.` 14pt `#E05C5C`.

### Time-Between-Steps (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `CRITICAL TIME WINDOWS -- DO NOT EXCEED` centered, 28 pt.

Three horizontal time-window bars, full width, H: 1.2", fill accent at 15%, border 1pt accent:

| Window | From | To | Max Time | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cleaning complete | Grit blast start | Same shift (ideally < 4 hrs) | `#2EC4B6` |
| 2 | Grit blast complete | Spray start | < 4 hours (some specs: < 2 hrs) | `#E8A020` |
| 3 | Spray start | Part handling | Allow full cool-down | `#27AE60` |

Center: big time value in Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 36pt, accent color.

Below bars: `Humidity matters: in high-humidity environments (>60% RH), reduce all time windows by half. Freshly blasted steel rusts FAST.` 14pt `#E05C5C`.
