## Phase 4 -- Conversion Coating Types (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `FOUR CONVERSION COATING CHEMISTRIES`. Four panels (W: 5.5", H: 8.5", fill `#1E2435`).

**Panel 1 -- Chromate (accent `#E05C5C`):** Badge `LEGACY`. Hex chromate (CrVI). Chromic acid + silica sol. Roll/spray. 10-30 mg/ft2. Best adhesion/corrosion. Declining -- RoHS/REACH restricted.

**Panel 2 -- Ti/Zr Chrome-Free (accent `#27AE60`):** Badge `CURRENT STANDARD`. Fluorotitanic/fluorozirconic acid. Roll/spray. 5-15 mg/ft2. Comparable performance. REACH compliant. Multi-metal compatible.

**Panel 3 -- Dry-in-Place (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Badge `EMERGING`. Proprietary chrome-free polymers. Roll-apply only. < 5 mg/ft2. Adequate for light-duty. Eliminates post-treatment rinse.

**Panel 4 -- Phosphate (accent `#E8A020`):** Badge `NICHE`. Iron or zinc phosphate. Spray. 25-75 mg/ft2. Good for heavy-gauge. Less common for high-speed coil. More stages and sludge.

## Phase 5 -- Application Methods + Chrome-Free Transition

### Application Methods (Y: 14.5"-20.5")

Section label: `HOW CONVERSION COATINGS ARE APPLIED AT LINE SPEED`. Two columns.

Left -- Roll Apply: Rubber roll applies directly. Precise, uniform. Preferred for Ti/Zr and dry-in-place. No overspray waste. Roll condition determines uniformity.

Right -- Spray Apply: Nozzle headers. Excess recirculates. Traditional for chromate/phosphate. Easier maintenance. Requires post-treatment rinse.

Callout: `Roll apply is gaining ground -- eliminates rinse step, reduces water and line footprint.`

### Chrome-Free Transition (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `THE CHROME-FREE TRANSITION`. Three columns.

Col 1 -- Why: EU REACH, RoHS, customer specs, CrVI = IARC Group 1 carcinogen.

Col 2 -- Performance comparison table: Adhesion (dry/wet), salt spray, multi-metal, compliance.

Col 3 -- Bottom Line: Chrome-free meets/approaches chromate for most applications. Transition driven by regulation, not performance failure.

## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| LOW COATING WEIGHT | Depleted chemistry | Titrate and adjust; check roll uniformity |
| UNEVEN TREATMENT (STREAKING) | Water droplets or uneven roll | Verify dryer; check roll condition |
| ADHESION FAILURE AFTER FORMING | Conversion coating missing one side | Check both sides; verify roll coverage |
| CHEMISTRY CONTAMINATION | Oil carryover from cleaning | Improve upstream cleaning; oil < 5 mg/m2 |

Key insight: `Coating weight is invisible. The only way to verify is measurement -- XRF, strips, or supplier methods.`
