## Phase 5 -- Quench Media + Nitrogen Advantage

### Quench Media (Y: 13.5"--19.5")

Section label: `QUENCH MEDIA -- SELECTING SEVERITY`. 3-row table:

| Medium | H-Value | Temp | Best For | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fast oil (agitated) | 0.50--0.70 | 120--180 F | Standard production | Most common for CN |
| Fast oil (still) | 0.25--0.35 | 120--180 F | Distortion-sensitive | Lower severity |
| Polymer (PAG) | 0.20--0.60 | 75--120 F | Thin sections; clean process | Adjustable 5--25% PAG |

Bottom: `Because nitrogen lowers the critical cooling rate, even H = 0.35 fully hardens 1018. NOT possible with carburizing alone.`

### Nitrogen Advantage (Y: 19.5"--26.0")

Section label: `THE NITROGEN ADVANTAGE -- WHY CARBONITRIDING WORKS`.

**Left -- CARBURIZING 1018 (`#E8A020`):** Surface 0.80% C; oil quench H=0.50; result: pearlite + bainite (SOFT); 1018 lacks Cr/Mo/Ni; case 30--40 HRC (FAIL).

**Right -- CARBONITRIDING 1018 (`#27AE60`):** Surface 0.70% C + 0.30% N; same quench H=0.50; result: MARTENSITE (HARD); N lowers critical cooling rate; case 58--63 HRC (PASS).

Center divider with arrow: `+ NITROGEN` in `#2EC4B6`.

## Phase 6 -- Oil Maintenance

Y: 26.0"--32.5". Section label: `QUENCH OIL MAINTENANCE`. 5-row table:

| Test | Frequency | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flash point | Weekly | > 300 F | Below flash = fire risk |
| Viscosity | Monthly | Per spec | Degraded oil = inconsistent quench |
| Water content | Weekly | < 0.1% | Water = steam explosion |
| Cooling curve | Quarterly | Per ASTM D6200 | Verifies actual quench severity |
| Particulate/sludge | Monthly | Clear, no sediment | Sludge insulates; uneven quench |
