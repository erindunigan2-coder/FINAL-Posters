## Phase 5 -- Rinse Water Quality + Drying Detail

### Quality Table (Y: 14.5"-20.5")

Section label: `RINSE WATER TARGETS`. 6-row table. Columns: Parameter | Stage 1 | Stage 2 (Final) | Why.

| Parameter | Stage 1 | Stage 2 (Final) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water source | Overflow from Stage 2 | Fresh DI | Counterflow conserves water |
| Conductivity | < 200 uS/cm | < 30 uS/cm | High TDS = salt deposits = adhesion failure |
| pH | 7-9 (carryover) | 6.5-7.5 (neutral) | Alkaline carryover interferes with conversion |
| Flow rate | Overflow | Per line speed/width | Must maintain target |
| Monitoring | Continuous conductivity | Continuous conductivity | Alarm if exceeded |
| Temperature | Ambient | Ambient | No heating needed |

Footnote: `Counterflow reduces DI consumption by 40-60% vs. single-pass rinse.`

### Drying Detail (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `DRYING -- WHY BONE-DRY MATTERS`. Two columns.

Left -- Dryer Types: IR pre-heater (fast, compact), Convection (even, heavier gauge), Combination (best of both, most modern).

Right -- Why Preheat 100-120 F: Removes moisture, improves conversion reactivity, Ti/Zr works better warm, prevents condensation, strip MUST be dry before conversion contact.

## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section label: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 RINSE / DRY DEFECTS`. Four cards.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| UNEVEN CONVERSION COATING | Water droplets entering treatment | Verify dryer temp; check IR elements |
| SALT DEPOSITS | High-TDS rinse water | Check DI system; replace resin |
| ALKALINE CARRYOVER | Insufficient rinse | Increase flow rate; verify nozzle coverage |
| STRIP TOO HOT (> 150 F) | Dryer overheating at reduced speed | Interlock dryer to line speed |

Key insight: `At 400 ft/min, a surviving water droplet becomes a 400-foot streak of uneven conversion coating.`
