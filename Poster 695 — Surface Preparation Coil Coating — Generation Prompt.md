## Phase 4 -- Substrate Types (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `FOUR SUBSTRATE TYPES -- WHAT YOU ARE COATING`.

Four equal panels (W: 5.5", H: 8.5", fill `#1E2435`, top accent 4 pt).

**Panel 1 -- HDG (accent `#E8A020`):** Badge `HDG`. Hot-Dip Galvanized Steel. Cross-section: steel substrate (0.015-0.060") + zinc coating (G60-G90) + mill oil. Zinc weight 0.60-0.90 oz/ft2. Spangle: regular/minimized/zero. Mill oil 50-200 mg/m2. Most common for building panels.

**Panel 2 -- Galvalume (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Badge `GL`. 55% Al / 43.5% Zn / 1.5% Si. AZ50-AZ55. Superior corrosion to HDG. Premium roofing and wall panels.

**Panel 3 -- CRS (accent `#E05C5C`):** Badge `CRS`. Cold-Rolled Steel (bare). No metallic coating. Must coat immediately. Flash rust risk. Appliance interiors, hidden structural.

**Panel 4 -- Aluminum (accent `#27AE60`):** Badge `AL`. 3003, 3105, 5005, 5052 alloys. Native oxide layer. Lighter gauge 0.015-0.040". Gutters, downspouts, composite panels, cans.

## Phase 5 -- Incoming Inspection Table

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section label: `WHAT THE COIL COATER CHECKS ON ARRIVAL`.

7-row table. Columns: Parameter | Specification | Method | Why It Matters.

| Parameter | Spec | Method | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gauge | 0.015-0.060" per order | Micrometer | Wrong gauge = forming failure |
| Width | Per order +/- 0.010" | Width gauge | Out of spec = trim waste |
| Coil weight | 5,000-25,000 lb | Scale | Affects accumulator run time |
| Surface condition | No rust, scale, damage | Visual | Defects telegraph through coating |
| Oil weight | 50-200 mg/m2 | Gravimetric | Excess overloads cleaner |
| Zinc weight (HDG/GL) | G60, G90, AZ50 per spec | XRF | Determines corrosion performance |
| Spangle (HDG) | Regular/minimized/zero | Visual | Large spangle telegraphs through thin coatings |

## Phase 6 -- Accumulator + Defects

### Accumulator (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `THE ACCUMULATOR -- KEEPING THE LINE RUNNING`. Full-width panel, two columns.

Left -- How It Works: Stores 200-600 ft of strip. Pays out during coil changes (2-5 min). Line runs full speed from accumulator. Without it, line stops for every coil change.

Right -- Key Parameters table: Storage 200-600 ft | Change time 2-5 min | Stitcher: resistance weld or rivet | Tension: dancer roll / load cell.

### Incoming Material Defects (Y: 26.5"-32.5")

Section label: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 INCOMING MATERIAL DEFECTS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| SPANGLE SHOW-THROUGH | Large zinc crystals through thin topcoat | Specify minimized or zero-spangle HDG |
| EXCESSIVE MILL OIL | Oil > 200 mg/m2 overloads cleaner | Reject coil or increase cleaner concentration |
| EDGE DAMAGE / BURRS | Shipping/handling damage | Trim edges in-line; reject if beyond trim zone |
| RUST / STAINING | Moisture during storage | Store indoors, dry, temp-controlled |

Key insight: `In coil coating, surface preparation is not something you do -- it is something you specify and verify.`
