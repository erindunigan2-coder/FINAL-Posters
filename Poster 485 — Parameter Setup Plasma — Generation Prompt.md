14-row table, full width. Columns: Parameter (4.5") | Range (5.0") | Units (2.5") | Notes (11.0").

| Parameter | Typical Range | Units | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arc current | 400-800 | A | Higher current = higher enthalpy; heavier electrode wear |
| Arc voltage | 50-80 | V | Determined by gas composition and flow rates |
| Power | 25-60 (up to 80) | kW | Power = V x A; total energy input |
| Primary gas (Ar) | 35-60 | SLPM | Stabilizes arc; carries plasma |
| Secondary gas (H2) | 5-15 | SLPM | Increases enthalpy dramatically |

| Standoff distance | 75-150 | mm | Closer = denser; farther = more porous |
| Spray angle | 75-90 | degrees | Below 45 deg causes shadowing and porosity |
| Traverse speed | 200-1000 | mm/s | Robot-controlled for uniformity |

## Phase 6 -- Parameter Map + Deposition Efficiency

### Parameter-to-Property Map (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `WHICH KNOB CONTROLS WHICH OUTCOME`. Two-column layout, accent `#E8A020`:

| To Increase... | Adjust... |
|---|---|
| Coating density | Decrease standoff; increase power; use H2 secondary |
| Bond strength | Preheat substrate; optimize Ra; increase particle velocity |
| Deposition rate | Increase powder feed rate; increase power |
| Surface smoothness | Finer powder; increase velocity; closer standoff |
| Porosity (intentional) | Increase standoff; reduce power; coarser powder |
| Coating hardness | Optimize melting (fully molten splats); minimize oxide content |

### Deposition Efficiency (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Full-width callout, border `#E8A020`. Left: Big number `40-70%` 56pt `#E8A020`. Label: `Deposition Efficiency (DE)`. Right: `Only 40-70% of powder becomes coating. The rest is overspray. DE depends on standoff, power, powder size, spray angle. Higher DE = lower cost per part.` Final line `#27AE60`: `Optimize before scaling production.`
