## Phase 6 -- Zinc DC Danger Zone + Problems

Y: 20.5" to 27.0".

### Danger Zone (coral border, full-width)

Title: `ZINC DIE CAST -- THE ACTIVATION DANGER ZONE` -- 24 pt `#E05C5C`.

**Left -- What Happens If Over-Etched:** HCl attacks zinc metal | Surface becomes porous/spongy | Cu deposits INTO pores, not ON surface | Weak mechanical bond | Deposit peels under tape, bend, or thermal cycling.

**Right -- How to Get It Right:** 1--3% HCl only | 5--10 sec max -- use a timer | No agitation | Rinse immediately | Enter strike within 30 sec | If in doubt, 5 sec is better than 15.

Bottom: `Adhesion testing (ASTM B571 bend test) on every new setup and after any process change.` -- `#2EC4B6`

### Problem Table

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cu peeling on zinc DC | Over-etching | Reduce HCl to 1%; reduce to 5 sec |
| Poor adhesion on steel | Under-activation | Increase HCl or time |
| Staining on brass | HCl dezincification | Switch to H2SO4 for brass |
| Re-oxidation before strike | Parts drying | Minimize transfer; keep wet |
| Pitting on zinc DC | Acid attack on porosity | Reduce concentration; improve cleaning |

## Phase 7 -- Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Acid Handling:** HCl corrosive, fumes, ventilation | H2SO4 corrosive, add to water | Dilute acids still burn | Zinc + acid = H2 gas -- ventilate.

**Right -- PPE:** Goggles, acid-resistant gloves, apron, local exhaust over HCl, eyewash + shower within 10 sec.
