## Phase 5 -- Efflorescence Warning

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHITE SALT EFFLORESCENCE -- THE SILENT KILLER`.

**Left -- Prompt Transfer:** Emerald accent. Uniform gray coating | No white deposits | Seal adhesion GOOD | Paint adhesion GOOD.

**Right -- Delayed Transfer:** Coral accent. White salt crystals forming | Soluble salts precipitate as water evaporates | Seal adhesion COMPROMISED | Paint adhesion RISK OF DELAMINATION.

Bottom: `Efflorescence is not always visible to the naked eye but always affects performance.`

## Phase 6 -- Rinse Quality & Counter-Flow
