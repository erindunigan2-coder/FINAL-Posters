# Poster #125 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Gold -- Post-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 6 of 8. The post-plate rinse for gold is the single most economically important rinse in any plating shop. Gold drag-out from the plating bath is pure money leaving the tank on every rack and barrel. The first rinse must be a stagnant DI recovery rinse that captures this gold for return to the bath. A good gold shop recovers 95%+ of all gold used. A bad gold shop watches it go down the drain.

## Design Notes

This is the most economically important poster in the gold cluster. The three-tank recovery hero immediately shows operators the system they should be running. The economics panel puts dollar values on drag-out loss -- $2,000--10,000/year on a busy line is a number that gets management attention. The gold waste collection section is often overlooked in plating shops -- spent filters and carbon sludge contain recoverable gold that pays for the refining service. The "rinse as profit center" message inverts the typical perception of rinsing as a cost.
