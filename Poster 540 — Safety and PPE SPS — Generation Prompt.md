## Phase 4 -- Hazard Matrix (HERO)

Y: 3.8" to 15.3". Section label: `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION -- INHERITED APS + SPS-SPECIFIC`.

11-row table. Columns: Hazard (4.0") | Details (8.5") | Controls (7.0") | Severity (3.5").

| Hazard | Details | Controls | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noise | 100-130 dB at operator | Double hearing protection; NRR 30+ | `#E05C5C` HIGH |
| UV/IR Radiation | Intense UV from plasma arc | Shade 10-14 welding helmet; no exposed skin | `#E05C5C` HIGH |
| Metal/Ceramic Fumes | NiCr, Co, ZrO2 vary by feedstock | LEV with HEPA; P100 RPE minimum | `#E05C5C` HIGH |
| Dust Explosion | Fine metal powders explosive when airborne | Inert handling; grounding; no open flames | `#E05C5C` HIGH |
| Thermal Burns | Substrate/fixture temps; hot overspray | Heat-resistant gloves; leather apron | `#E8A020` MED |
| Electrical | DC 40-80 kW; HF arc start | LOTO; insulated gloves; grounding | `#E8A020` MED |
| Compressed Gas | High-pressure Ar, H2 (flammable), N2 | Secured cylinders; H2 leak detection | `#E8A020` MED |
| Overspray Dust | Respirable particles in booth | Enclosed booth with downdraft ventilation | `#E8A020` MED |
| **ETHANOL VAPOR** | Flash point 13 degC / 55 degF | **Explosion-proof booth; vapor monitoring; grounding** | `#E05C5C` **HIGH** |
| **NANO-PARTICLE EXPOSURE** | Submicron/nano ceramics; enhanced lung penetration | **HEPA 99.97%; nano-rated P100 RPE** | `#E05C5C` **HIGH** |
| **PRESSURIZED LIQUID** | Suspension feed at 2-10 bar | **Rated hoses; relief valves; spill containment** | `#E8A020` MED |

## Phase 5 -- SPS-Specific Hazard Callouts

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `SPS-SPECIFIC HAZARDS -- BEYOND STANDARD APS`.

Three side-by-side callout boxes, fill `#1E2435`:

**Ethanol Vapor (accent `#E05C5C`):** Flash point 13 degC (55 degF). Explosion-proof booth MANDATORY. Continuous vapor monitoring. Ground all containers/lines. NFPA 30 storage.

**Nano-Particles (accent `#E05C5C`):** Particle size 50 nm-5 um. TLVs NOT established -- precautionary principle. HEPA 99.97%. P100 nano-rated RPE. Biological monitoring recommended.

**Pressurized Liquid (accent `#E8A020`):** Feed pressure 2-10 bar. Rated hoses. Relief valves. Spill containment for ethanol suspension.

| Substance | OSHA PEL | ACGIH TLV |
|---|---|---|
| Chromium (metal) | 1.0 mg/m3 | 0.5 mg/m3 |

### Emergency Response (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE -- 4 SCENARIOS`. Four cards:

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| ETHANOL FIRE | CO2 or dry chemical; evacuate; do NOT use water |
| FUME EXPOSURE | Fresh air; O2 if available; medical attention |
| ARC FLASH BURN | Cool with water; do not remove stuck clothing; EMS |
| NANO-DUST RELEASE | Evacuate; wet-wipe only (no dry sweep); HEPA vacuum |
