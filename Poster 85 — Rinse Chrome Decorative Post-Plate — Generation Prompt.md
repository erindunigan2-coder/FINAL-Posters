## Phase 5 -- Drag-Out Recovery Economics

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `DRAG-OUT RECOVERY -- WHY IT PAYS`.

Three benefit columns (each W: 7.33", accent on top):

| Benefit | What | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| CHEMICAL SAVINGS (`#E8A020`) | Recovered Cr returns to bath | 30--60% reduction in chemical additions |
| WASTE REDUCTION (`#2EC4B6`) | Less Cr in wastewater | Lower treatment chemical costs |
| COMPLIANCE (`#27AE60`) | Less Cr(VI) to reduce/discharge | Easier permit compliance |

Bottom: `A simple static recovery rinse before the cascade can return 50--70% of drag-out to the chrome bath. The cheapest improvement most chrome shops can make.` -- `#E8A020`

## Phase 6 -- Environmental Compliance

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE -- CHROME WASTEWATER`.

Coral-bordered panel:

**Hexavalent chrome wastewater rules:**

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cr(VI) reduction | MUST reduce Cr(VI) to Cr(III) -- sodium metabisulfite or ferrous sulfate |
| pH adjustment | After reduction: precipitate Cr(OH)3 at pH 7.5--8.5 |
| Discharge limits | Total Cr: varies by jurisdiction (typically 0.5--2.0 mg/L) |
| Record keeping | Monitor and log all chrome discharges |
| Segregation | Chrome wastewater must be segregated from cyanide wastewater |

**Trivalent chrome wastewater:** `Cr(III) wastewater is simpler -- no reduction step needed. Precipitate directly at pH 7.5--8.5.` -- `#27AE60`. `But still a regulated metal -- discharge limits apply.`

## Phase 7 -- Common Issues

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `COMMON RINSE PROBLEMS`.

2x2 grid:

| Problem | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHROME STAINING | `#E8A020` | Residual drag-out drying on parts | Improve rinse; do not let parts dry |
| HAZY FINISH | `#E8A020` | Hard water minerals on chrome surface | Use DI water for final rinse |
| COMPLIANCE VIOLATION | `#E05C5C` | Cr(VI) in discharge exceeding limits | Install/upgrade drag-out recovery; verify reduction |
| RECOVERY TANK OVERFLOW | `#E05C5C` | Drag-out too dilute; not returned | Monitor concentration; return when TDS target met |
