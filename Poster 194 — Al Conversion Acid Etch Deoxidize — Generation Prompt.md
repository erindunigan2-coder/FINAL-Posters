## Phase 4 -- Deoxidize Stage Detail Hero

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `ACID ETCH / DEOXIDIZE -- SURFACE ACTIVATION`.

### Step 5 -- Stage detail panel (Y: 3.8" to 8.5")

Full-width rounded rect (23.0" x 4.5"), fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`.

**Left -- What this stage does** (Inter 14 pt, numbered):
1. Dissolves the natural aluminum oxide (Al2O3) layer -- reforms in seconds on bare Al
2. Removes smut (dark residue from Cu, Si, Fe, Mn alloying elements)
3. Exposes fresh, active aluminum for Zr deposition -- oxide or smut = no coating

**Right -- Key control callout** (accent `#E05C5C`):
`TIME-CRITICAL: Aluminum re-oxidizes almost instantly in water. Minimize transit time from deoxidize rinse to conversion coating bath -- ideally < 5 minutes. If parts sit in air or water between deox and coating, the fresh oxide layer reforms and coating adhesion suffers.`

### Step 6 -- Deoxidizer chemistry options (Y: 9.0" to 15.0")

Four chemistry panels (H: 5.5", fill `#1E2435`, top accent 4 pt):

| Chemistry | X | W | Accent | Params | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nitric Acid (HNO3) | 0.5" | 5.5" | `#E8A020` | 30--50% by vol, ambient, 1--5 min | 6xxx alloys; does not remove Si/Cu smut alone |
| Nitric + HF | 6.25" | 5.5" | `#E05C5C` | 30% HNO3 + 1--3% HF, ambient, 30 sec--3 min | 2xxx/7xxx (high-Cu) and cast Al. Safety tag: `HAZARDOUS -- HF` |
| Non-Chrome (Ferric Sulfate) | 12.0" | 5.5" | `#27AE60` | Per supplier, ambient--120 F, 1--5 min | Cr-free compliance; good for 6xxx; growing market |
| Acidic Fluoride (ZrF/TiF) | 17.75" | 5.75" | `#2EC4B6` | pH 2.5--4.5, 0.1--0.5 g/L, ambient--100 F, 30 sec--2 min | Multi-metal automotive activation |

## Phase 5 -- Alloy Selection Guide

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `ALLOY FAMILY -- DEOXIDIZER SELECTION`.

5-row table. Columns: Alloy Family (3.5") | Examples (3.5") | Smut Character (4.5") | Recommended Deox (5.0") | Notes (6.5").

| Alloy Family | Examples | Smut Character | Recommended Deox | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6xxx (low alloy) | 6061, 6063 | Light gray; easy to remove | HNO3 or non-chrome (ferric sulfate) | Most common automotive alloy |
| 2xxx (high copper) | 2024, 2014 | Dark, Cu-rich; tenacious | HNO3/HF required | Aerospace -- Cu smut resists mild acids |
| 7xxx (zinc-copper) | 7075, 7050 | Dark, Cu-rich; tenacious | HNO3/HF required | Same as 2xxx -- copper drives chemistry |
| Cast Al (high Si) | 356, A356 | Gray; silicon particles | HNO3/HF (HF dissolves SiO2) | Cast parts need HF for Si inclusions |
| Multi-metal (auto) | Mixed in one line | Varies by substrate | Acidic fluoride (ZrF/TiF) | One activation bath for all substrates |

## Phase 6 -- Multi-Metal Activation

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `MULTI-METAL ACTIVATION -- ONE BATH, EVERY SUBSTRATE`.

Two panels:

**Left -- How It Works** (accent `#2EC4B6`):
Acidic fluoride rinse (H2TiF6 or H2ZrF6 at 0.1--0.5 g/L) activates ALL substrates simultaneously. Params: pH 2.5--4.5, ambient--100 F, 30 sec--2 min, spray or immersion.

**Right -- What Happens on Each Metal** (accent `#E8A020`):
Three mini callouts:
- `ALUMINUM` (Amber): Fluoride dissolves Al2O3; acid removes smut. Fresh Al exposed for Zr deposition.
- `STEEL` (Teal): Acid activates iron surface; removes light oxide. Prepares for Zr deposition.
- `GALVANIZED` (Emerald): Acid etches zinc oxide; fluoride activates surface. Zr deposits uniformly.

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | DARK SMUT REMAINS | Wrong deoxidizer for alloy; HF needed for Cu/Si | Match deoxidizer to alloy family; add HF if needed |
| 2 | 6.33" | OVER-ETCHED SURFACE | Acid too concentrated; time too long; temp too high | Reduce concentration/time/temp; check solution age |
| 3 | 12.16" | COATING ADHESION FAILURE | Re-oxidation between deox and coating (> 5 min) | Minimize transit time; keep parts wet |
| 4 | 18.0" | PITTING ON ALUMINUM | Chloride contamination in deoxidizer | Use chloride-free reagents; check water quality |
