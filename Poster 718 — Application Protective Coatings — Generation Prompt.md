## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| POT LIFE EXCEEDED (GELLED) | Mixed too long ago; temp too high | Reduce batch; track timer; keep cool |
| DFT TOO THICK (SAGS) | Too close, slow, or overlap | Increase distance; speed; reduce overlap |
| DFT TOO THIN (HOLIDAYS) | Insufficient overlap, pressure | Stripe coat first; verify tip/pressure |
| WRONG MIX RATIO | Proportioner uncalibrated / manual error | Verify ratio before each batch; calibrate |

Key insight: `Every defect is too much or too little. The DFT gauge is the most important tool -- not the spray gun.`
