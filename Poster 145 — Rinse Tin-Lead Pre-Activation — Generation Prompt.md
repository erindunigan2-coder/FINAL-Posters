## Phase 6 -- Drag-In Consequences

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT HAPPENS WHEN ALKALINE REACHES THE ACID`.

Full-width panel, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`. Three columns:

| Column | Header | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Left (`#E8A020`) | THE DRAG-IN | Alkaline cleaner carried on parts and rack. Typical: 0.1--0.5 mL per sq ft. Electronics substrates drag less due to smooth surfaces. |
| Center (`#E05C5C`) | THE REACTION | NaOH + acid --> salt + H2O. Neutralizes activation acid. Raises pH. Reduces effectiveness. Alkaline film buffers the surface -- oxide removal fails. |
| Right (`#E05C5C`) | THE RESULT | Oxide film remains on copper surface. Tin-lead adhesion fails. Blistering after reflow. Solder joint failure in field. |

Bottom: `One good rinse prevents one bad plating lot. For solder plate on electronics, one bad lot means field returns.` `#27AE60`
