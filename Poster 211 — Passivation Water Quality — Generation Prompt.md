## Phase 4 -- Water Quality by Stage Hero

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `WATER QUALITY REQUIREMENTS -- BY PROCESS STAGE`.

### Step 5 -- Stage-by-stage water quality table (Y: 3.8" to 9.5")

| Stage | Water Type | Conductivity | Cl- Max | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Post-Clean Rinse | DI/RO (aero); low-Cl tap (general) | < 500 uS/cm | < 25 ppm | Remove cleaner; no Cl carryover |
| Post-Pickle Rinse | DI/RO preferred | < 200 uS/cm | < 10 ppm | Remove HF/HNO3 residues completely |
| Pre-Passivation Rinse | DI or RO | < 50 uS/cm | < 5 ppm | Last wet stage before acid; Cl-free critical |
| Passivation Bath Makeup | DI water | < 10 uS/cm | < 1 ppm | Chloride in acid bath causes pitting |
| Post-Acid Rinse | DI/RO (aero/med); low-Cl (general) | < 200 uS/cm | < 10 ppm | Remove all acid; prevent staining |
| Final Rinse (if used) | DI water | < 10 uS/cm | < 1 ppm | Spot-free drying for appearance parts |

### Step 6 -- Chloride pitting mechanism (Y: 10.0" to 15.0")

**Left -- WHY CHLORIDE PITS STAINLESS** (`#E05C5C`): Cl- penetrates Cr2O3 at defect sites. Creates autocatalytic pit: metal dissolves -> MCl2 + H2O -> M(OH)2 + 2HCl -> pH drops -> MORE dissolution. Pit feeds itself. During passivation, surface actively dissolving -- chloride accelerates uncontrolled localized attack.

**Right -- CHLORIDE LIMITS** (`#27AE60`): General industrial < 25 ppm / Food/pharma < 10 ppm / Aerospace (AMS 2700) < 5 ppm / Medical/implant < 1 ppm / Semiconductor (SEMI F42) < 0.1 ppm. Note: Lower-Cr grades (410, 420) MORE susceptible -- use tightest limit achievable.

## Phase 5 -- DI / RO System Basics

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `DI AND RO WATER SYSTEMS -- THE ESSENTIALS`.

**Left -- DI (Deionized) Water** (`#27AE60`): Ion exchange resins remove all dissolved ions. Output: < 10 uS/cm (often < 1), Cl- < 1 ppm, TDS < 5 ppm. Maintenance: monitor conductivity continuously, regenerate/replace resin beds, alarm at 20 uS/cm.

**Right -- RO (Reverse Osmosis) Water** (`#2EC4B6`): Membrane rejects 95--99% dissolved ions. Output: 10--50 uS/cm, Cl- typically < 5 ppm. Maintenance: replace pre-filters, monitor rejection %, replace membranes every 2--5 years. RO + DI polisher = best of both.

## Phase 6 -- Monitoring Schedule

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WATER QUALITY MONITORING SCHEDULE`.

| Parameter | Method | Frequency | Target | Action if Out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conductivity | Inline meter | Continuous | < 50 uS/cm (DI rinses) | Regenerate/replace DI resin |
| Chloride | AgNO3 spot test or strip | Daily | < 25 ppm general; < 5 aero | Switch to DI; investigate source |
| pH | pH meter or strip | Daily | 5.5--7.5 | Investigate contamination |
| TDS | TDS meter | Weekly | < 25 ppm | DI/RO system degradation |
| Iron (Fe) | Colorimetric kit | Weekly | < 0.5 ppm | Upstream corrosion/contamination |

Below: `Continuous conductivity monitoring with an alarm is the single most cost-effective water quality investment.` (`#27AE60`)
