## Phase 4 -- Cascade Rinse Hero + Contamination Callout

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE POST-PLATE RINSE -- REMOVING COPPER DRAG-OUT`.

### 2--3 Tank Cascade (Y: 5.0" to 10.0")

Tank 1 (amber tint): captures alkaline copper drag-out. Tank 2 (neutral). Tank 3 (teal tint): clean, parts exit. Exit: `TO NEXT STEP (acid dip, acid Cu, Ni, or anti-tarnish)`.

Target: < 200 uS/cm at final stage.

### Downstream Contamination Callout (Y: 11.0" to 14.0")

Full-width amber panel. Title: `WHERE COPPER DRAG-OUT CAUSES PROBLEMS DOWNSTREAM`.

Four risk columns:
- **Acid Copper Bath:** Alkaline carry-over raises pH locally. Risk: LOW (`#27AE60`).
- **Nickel Bath:** Cu > 50 ppm: dark deposits, pitting. Risk: HIGH (`#E05C5C`).
- **Chrome Bath:** Cu > 200 ppm: discoloration, dark spots. Risk: HIGH (`#E05C5C`).
- **Waste Treatment:** Chelant carry-over (HEDP, pyro) keeps Cu in solution through hydroxide precip. Risk: MODERATE (`#E8A020`).

## Phase 5 -- Rinse Parameters + Drag-Out Recovery

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameters:** DI preferred (especially before Ni/Cr) | Ambient | 2--3 cascade | 30--60 sec | < 200 uS/cm | No visible blue-green in final tank.

**Right -- Drag-Out Recovery (accent `#E8A020`):** Static tank before cascade captures concentrated chemistry for return. Alkaline Cu baths are expensive -- complexant costs more than simple salts. Recovery reduces chemical consumption and waste treatment load.

---

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cu carry-over to nickel | Dark Ni deposits, pitting | Insufficient rinsing | Add stage; check flow |
| Chelant carry-over to waste | Cu not precipitating | HEDP/pyro complexes Cu | Segregate Cu rinse; sulfide precip |
| Alkaline carry-over to acid | Localized pH spike at Cu surface | Insufficient rinsing | Extend time or add stage |
| Cu tarnishing in rinse | Orange/brown discoloration | Hot water + air | Ambient water; minimize time |
| Blue-green rinse tank | Heavy Cu drag-out | High bath viscosity; poor drain | Increase drain time |
