## Phase 5 -- Rinse Parameters + Drag-Out Recovery

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameters:** DI preferred | Ambient | 2--3 cascade + optional drag-out | 30--60 sec | < 100 uS/cm | Drain 15--20 sec | No blue-green in final tank.

**Right -- Drag-Out Recovery (accent `#27AE60`):**
Static tank captures concentrated drag-out for return to plating bath. Saves CuSO4. Reduces Cu load on waste treatment. Extends downstream rinse life.

## Phase 6 -- Cross-Contamination Warning + Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0".

### Cross-Contamination Warning (coral border, full-width)

Title: `COPPER IS A CONTAMINANT DOWNSTREAM`.

**In Nickel Baths:** > 5 ppm Cu -> dark deposits at LCD, haze. Fix: dummy plate 2--5 ASF.
**In Zinc Baths:** > 5 ppm Cu -> black spots, dark LCD. Fix: dummy plate, zinc purification.

Bottom: `Any time copper-plated parts share a line with nickel or zinc, post-copper rinsing is the single most important contamination control.` -- `#E05C5C`

### Problem Table

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue-green rinse | Cu building | Insufficient flow | Add cascade; drag-out recovery |
| Dark LCD in nickel | Cu contamination | Poor rinsing | Improve rinse; dummy plate Ni |
| Tarnish on copper | Oxidation | Parts drying | Minimize transfer; keep wet |
| Staining after dry | Water spots | Hard water | DI in final rinse |
