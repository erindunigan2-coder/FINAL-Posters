## Phase 4 -- Anti-Tarnish Comparison Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `ANTI-TARNISH TREATMENT OPTIONS`.

2x2 grid of treatment panels:

**Chromate Dip (accent `#E8A020`):** Dilute chromate, 5--15 sec, ambient. Good traditional standard. Hex chromium -- RoHS RESTRICTED badge (Coral). Legacy aerospace/military specs.

**BTA Dip (accent `#2EC4B6`):** 0.1--1% BTA, 30--60 sec, ambient. Moderate -- invisible organic barrier. RoHS-compliant. Common for electronics.

**Proprietary Protector (accent `#27AE60`):** Organic polymer, immersion or spray, per TDS. Good -- most common modern approach. RoHS-compliant. Recommended default.

**Clear Lacquer (accent `#C8D0D8`):** Clear coat spray or dip, 1--5 um thick. Excellent barrier. Increases contact resistance significantly -- NOT for electrical contacts. Decorative tableware.

## Phase 5 -- Tarnish Mechanism

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `HOW SILVER TARNISHES`.

**Left -- The Reaction (accent `#E05C5C`):** `2 Ag + H2S --> Ag2S + H2`. Silver sulfide is BLACK. H2S sources: industrial atmosphere, rubber, eggs, skin oils, sulfur packaging. SO2 also attacks in humidity. Accelerated by humidity, heat, UV.

**Right -- Prevention Strategy (accent `#27AE60`):** 1. Anti-tarnish chemical treatment (always) / 2. Sulfur-free packaging / 3. Avoid rubber bands, sulfur cardboard / 4. Desiccant packs / 5. Anti-tarnish paper wraps / 6. Controlled atmosphere for long-term.

## Phase 6 -- Application Routing

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `CHOOSE BY APPLICATION`.

| Application | Treatment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical Contacts / RF | BTA or thin proprietary | Contact resistance must stay low |
| Connectors / Terminals | BTA or proprietary | Balance protection and function |
| Decorative (Tableware) | Lacquer | Maximum protection; appearance primary |
| Aerospace Fasteners | Per spec (often chromate) | Anti-galling primary purpose |
| Waveguides / Microwave | Matte silver + thin protector | Maximum conductivity |
| General Industrial | Proprietary protector | Best balance cost/protection/compliance |

Note: `Anti-tarnish must NOT significantly increase contact resistance. Verify with milliohm meter.`

## Phase 7 -- Storage + Handling

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `STORAGE AND HANDLING`.

Four cards (Amber accent -- informational):

| # | Topic | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PACKAGING | Sulfur-free bags or anti-tarnish paper. NEVER rubber bands. Avoid uncoated cardboard. |
| 2 | ENVIRONMENT | <60% RH ideal. Avoid rubber, eggs, sulfur chemicals. |
| 3 | HANDLING | Cotton or nitrile gloves. Skin oils accelerate tarnish. Fingerprints are permanent. |
| 4 | INSPECTION | Visual before shipment. Yellow/brown haze = tarnish beginning. Re-treat or reject. |
