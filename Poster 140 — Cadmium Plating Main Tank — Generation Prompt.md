## Phase 4 -- Plating Tank Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE CYANIDE CADMIUM PLATING TANK`.

Tank cross-section (W: 21.0", H: 7.5", fill `#252B3D`, border 3pt `#C8D0D8`). Pure cadmium anodes (99.95%+), bars or balls in baskets with bags. Cathode: `HIGH-STRENGTH STEEL`. Current flow: `Cd(CN)4 2- migration`. Rectifier above.

**Params (right):** `Cd metal: 18--35 g/L` / `Total NaCN: 90--150 g/L` / `Free NaCN: 15--45 g/L` / `NaOH: 15--30 g/L` / `Temp: 75--90 F` / `CD: 10--25 ASF rack / 3--8 barrel` / `Cathode eff: 85--95%`.

**Hazard callout (bottom):** Fill `#E05C5C` at 15%, border 1pt `#E05C5C`: `ENCLOSED TANK with local exhaust ventilation, HEPA filtration. Cadmium mist is carcinogenic.`

## Phase 5 -- Bath Composition

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY -- CYANIDE CADMIUM (AEROSPACE STANDARD)`.

Four component boxes:

**CADMIUM METAL (`#27AE60`):** CdO 20--40 g/L / Cd metal 18--35 (target 22--27) / Low: thin spots, poor LCD / High: slightly rough.

**SODIUM CYANIDE (`#2EC4B6`):** Total NaCN 90--150 g/L / Free NaCN 15--45 (target 20--30) / Complexes Cd / Low: immersion deposit / High: reduced efficiency.

**CAUSTIC (`#E8A020`):** NaOH 15--30 g/L (target 20) / Conductivity and stability.

**BRIGHTENER (`#C8D0D8`):** Per supplier TDS / Typically nickel-based or organic / Semi-bright to bright / Monitor by Hull cell.

## Phase 6 -- Defect Grid

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS`.

| Pos | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | HE FAILURE (POST-PLATE) | `#E05C5C` | Delayed/insufficient bake | Bake per AMS 2759/9 within 1 hr |
| R1C2 | DARK DEPOSIT | `#E8A020` | Organic, metallic impurities | Carbon treat; dummy plate |
| R1C3 | POOR ADHESION | `#E05C5C` | Surface contamination, poor activation | Improve cleaning; verify acid time |
| R2C1 | ROUGH DEPOSIT | `#E8A020` | Particulate, anode sludge | Check bags; filter |
| R2C2 | THIN LCD COVERAGE | `#2EC4B6` | Low Cd, poor throwing power | Add CdO; check free NaCN |
| R2C3 | CARBONATE BUILDUP | `#2EC4B6` | CO2 absorption from air | Freeze-out at <35 F; barium treatment |

## Phase 7 -- Anodes + Alt Bath + Contamination

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Anodes + Acid Chloride Alternative:**
Anode panel: Pure cadmium 99.95%+ / Bars or balls / A:C 1:1--2:1 / Bags required.
Alt bath panel: `ALTERNATIVE: ACID CHLORIDE Cd BATH` / CdCl2 25--40 g/L / NH4Cl 150--225 g/L / pH 3.5--5.5 / CD 10--40 ASF. `Non-cyanide option. Less common.`

**Right -- Contamination Thresholds:**

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | > 5 ppm | Dark LCD |
| Lead | > 2 ppm | Brittle deposit |
| Iron | > 50 ppm | Rough deposit |
| Chromium | > 1 ppm | Poor coverage, dull |
| Organic | visible | Dark, streaky |
| Carbonate | > 30 g/L | Reduced conductivity, rough |
