# Poster #157 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Iron Phosphate -- Post-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 4 of 6. The post-coat rinse removes unreacted phosphate acid and soluble salts from the freshly formed iron phosphate coating. Two critical rules dominate this poster: NO hot water (thermal shock damages the thin amorphous film) and NO delay (parts must move promptly to prevent flash rust). This poster is less about water chemistry and more about timing and temperature discipline.

### ZONE 3 -- Timing & Temperature Hero

**Section label:** `TWO RULES -- NO EXCEPTIONS` -- Y: 4.4".

**BLOCK B -- Two-Rule Visual (Y: 5.0" to 14.0")**

Two large panels side by side:

**Left -- Rule 1: No Hot Water (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Large rounded rect, H: 8.5", fill `#1E2435`, top accent 4 pt `#E05C5C`
- Big icon area: thermometer symbol with X mark
- Title: `NO HOT WATER` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 36 pt `#E05C5C`
- Subtitle: `Ambient to 80 F (27 C) ONLY` JetBrains Mono 18 pt `#F0EDE8`

Explanation (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 160%):
- `The iron phosphate film is thin (0.25--1.0 um) and amorphous.`
- `Hot water causes thermal shock to the freshly formed film.`
- `Thermal shock = micro-cracking, delamination, poor seal adhesion.`
- `Cold to ambient rinse water preserves the coating intact.`

Bottom callout:
- `NEVER rinse freshly phosphated parts with water above 80 F.` Inter Medium 16 pt `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Rule 2: No Delay (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Large rounded rect, H: 8.5", fill `#1E2435`, top accent 4 pt `#E8A020`
- Big icon area: clock symbol with exclamation mark
- Title: `NO DELAY` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 36 pt `#E8A020`
- Subtitle: `Phosphate to rinse: IMMEDIATELY` JetBrains Mono 18 pt `#F0EDE8`

Explanation:
- `The wet phosphate film begins oxidizing the moment it leaves the bath.`
- `Extended dwell in humid air = flash rust (orange-brown staining).`
- `Flash rust ruins the coating and forces rework.`
- `Automated lines: conveyor speed must not allow dwell > 30 seconds.`

Bottom callout:
- `Parts should move from phosphate to rinse without pause. Every second counts.` Inter Medium 16 pt `#E8A020`

## Design Notes

The two-rule hero is the core visual concept. "No hot water" and "No delay" are the two rules that matter at this stage. Everything else is standard rinse practice. The flash rust section gives this poster real diagnostic value -- every phosphate line operator has dealt with flash rust, and most have struggled to eliminate it.
