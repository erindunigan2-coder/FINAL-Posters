## Phase 5 -- Chrome vs. Non-Chrome

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THE CHROME TRANSITION`.

**Left -- Chrome Seal (Legacy):** Coral accent. Badge: `BEING PHASED OUT`. Hexavalent chromic acid (CrO3). Restricted (REACH, RoHS, OSHA). Cr(VI) is a known human carcinogen. Replacement is mandatory for most industries.

**Right -- Non-Chrome Seal (Current):** Emerald accent. Badge: `INDUSTRY STANDARD`. Zr-based, silane/siloxane, organic polymer. Good to excellent performance approaching chrome. Fully compliant with REACH, RoHS.

Verdict banner: `If your spec does not explicitly require chrome seal, use non-chrome. The performance gap has closed.` `#27AE60`

## Phase 7 -- Defects + Final Checklist

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Post-Treatment Defects:**

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Water spots after dry | Contaminated rinse; hard water; dirty seal | Use DI water; clean seal bath |
| Paint blistering | Moisture not fully removed; oven temp too low | Increase oven time/temp; verify dryness |
| Seal not adhering | Contaminated post-coat rinse; wrong pH | Clean rinse tank; check seal pH |
| Coating degraded | Oven > 400 F; excessive time | Reduce oven temp; check thermocouple |

**Right -- Pre-Paint Checklist:** Emerald accent. Six items: Coating weight 40--60 mg/ft2, uniform iridescent blue/gold, smooth non-powdery feel, completely dry, no flash rust, no water spots.
