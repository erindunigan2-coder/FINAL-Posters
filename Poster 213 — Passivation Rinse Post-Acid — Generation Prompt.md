## Phase 4 -- Rinse Stage Detail Hero

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `RINSE -- POST-ACID`.

### Step 5 -- Stage detail panel (Y: 3.8" to 8.5")

Parameters: Water: DI for aerospace/medical / Temp: Ambient to warm / Method: Overflow, multiple stages for critical / Time: 1--3 min per stage / Chloride: < 25 ppm general, < 5 ppm aerospace.

Purpose: Remove ALL acid residues. Nitric = staining/continued etching. Citric = microbial growth. Both must be removed completely.

`#1 FAILURE MODE` callout (`#E05C5C`): `Trapped acid in crevices, blind holes, threads, and lap joints. The acid continues to etch in concentrated, localized area -- causing brown staining hours or days after passivation.`

### Step 6 -- Where acid hides (Y: 9.0" to 15.0")

Six location cards (3x2 grid, accent `#E05C5C`):
1. BLIND HOLES -- Rotate parts 180 degrees during rinse. Air blow if needed.
2. THREADED HOLES -- Capillary action wicks acid in. Extended soak + air blow.
3. LAP JOINTS / SEAMS -- Acid won't drain without mechanical assist. Separate if possible.
4. INSIDE TUBES -- Horizontal tubes trap acid at low points. Flush with rinse water.
5. UNDER RUBBER FIXTURES -- Masking and plugs trap acid. Remove before rinsing.
6. WELD ROOTS / CREVICES -- Trap acid indefinitely. Multi-stage rinse + extended soak.

## Phase 5 -- Rinse Protocol by Application

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `RINSE PROTOCOL -- BY APPLICATION`.

Three panels:

**General Industrial** (`#2EC4B6`): 1--2 stages, low-Cl tap, < 500 uS/cm, 1--2 min, rotate to drain, pH strip verify.

**Aerospace (AMS 2700)** (`#E8A020`): 2--3 stages counter-flow + DI final, < 50 uS/cm, 2--3 min, air blow blind holes, ultrasonic for complex geometry.

**Medical / Pharma** (`#27AE60`): 3+ stages DI all, < 10 uS/cm, Cl- < 1 ppm, 2--5 min, mandatory rotation + air blow, residual ion test, ASTM F86 reference.

---

## Phase 6 -- Nitric vs. Citric Rinse Considerations

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `RINSE DIFFERENCES -- NITRIC vs. CITRIC RESIDUES`.

**Left -- AFTER NITRIC** (`#E8A020`): Brown/orange staining risk. HIGH rinse urgency -- acid continues etching. Dichromate (Nitric 1): Cr6+ residue = hazardous waste rinse. Smell test: if you can smell HNO3, not rinsed enough.

**Right -- AFTER CITRIC** (`#27AE60`): Microbial growth risk -- bacteria thrive in citric residue. MODERATE rinse urgency -- milder but still needs removal. Biofilm risk for medical/pharma parts -- multi-stage DI mandatory.

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | BROWN STAINING (DELAYED) | Trapped acid in crevices; appears hours/days later | Improve rinse; rotate parts; air blow |
| 2 | 6.33" | CHLORIDE PITTING | Chloride in rinse water on fresh passive film | Switch to DI/RO; test water for chloride |
| 3 | 12.16" | STREAKING / TIDE MARKS | Uneven rinse; slow withdrawal | Full immersion; smooth withdrawal |
| 4 | 18.0" | ACID SMELL ON PARTS | Insufficient rinsing | Add second rinse; increase time |
