# Poster #461 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Electropolishing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Cleaning for electropolishing is uniquely unforgiving. Unlike plating (where the deposit can sometimes mask minor surface defects), EP exposes everything. Deep scratches, weld defects, heat tint, and inclusions are all revealed and amplified. This poster drives home the mantra: EP does not create a finish -- it reveals the substrate. Pre-mechanical preparation and pre-cleaning are the entire ballgame.

### ZONE 3 -- Pre-Finish & Cleaning Hero

**Section label:** `SURFACE PREPARATION + CLEANING SEQUENCE` -- Y: 4.4".

**BLOCK B -- Pre-Finish Decision Guide (Y: 5.0" to 9.5")**

Rounded rect, full width, H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`.

Title: `BEFORE YOU CLEAN -- IS THE SURFACE READY FOR EP?` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E8A020`

Decision checklist (Inter Medium 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 170%):

```
[ ] Machining marks: Must be 120--240 grit or finer. Heavy marks will NOT be removed by EP.
[ ] Welds: Must be ground smooth and blended BEFORE EP. EP highlights weld defects -- it does not hide them.
[ ] Heat tint: Remove mechanically or by pickling BEFORE EP. Discoloration will remain.
[ ] Scratches: Deep scratches (> 25 um) will survive EP. Mechanical polish first.
[ ] Alloy grade: Avoid 303 SS (free-machining) -- MnS inclusions cause pitting during EP.
[ ] Casting porosity: EP opens pores. If surface has porosity, consider mechanical polish only.
```

Bottom note: `Rule of thumb: EP improves Ra by 50--75%. If starting at Ra 0.8 um, expect 0.2--0.4 um. If starting at Ra 3.2 um, EP alone will NOT reach 0.5 um.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#27AE60`

**BLOCK C -- Four-Step Cleaning Sequence (Y: 10.0" to 14.0")**

Four horizontal step cards:

| Step | X | W | Accent | Title | Parameters | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | 5.5" | `#2EC4B6` | ALK SOAK CLEAN | 50--70 C, 5--15 min, soak or ultrasonic | Remove oils, grease, shop soils |

### ZONE 5 -- Substrate Caution Cards

**Section label:** `MATERIAL-SPECIFIC CAUTIONS` -- Y: 22.2".

**BLOCK F -- Four Caution Cards (Y: 22.9" to 28.0")**

2x2 grid:

| Position | Substrate | Accent | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | 303 SS (FREE-MACHINING) | `#E05C5C` | Contains MnS inclusions that dissolve during EP, leaving pits. AVOID for EP applications. Specify 304 or 316 instead. |
| R1C2 | WELDED ASSEMBLIES | `#E8A020` | Grind welds smooth and blend before EP. Use matching filler metal. Heat-affected zone etches differently -- some visibility unavoidable. |

| R2C2 | CAST PARTS | `#E05C5C` | Porosity and inclusions are common in castings. EP opens pores and highlights shrinkage. Mechanical polish may be the better choice. |

## Design Notes

The hero concept here is unusual for a cleaning poster -- the real story is what happens BEFORE cleaning. The pre-finish decision guide (Block B) is the most valuable section. A shop that skips mechanical prep and goes straight to EP will get disappointing results every time. The 303 SS warning is a specific, actionable callout that many shops learn the hard way.
