## Phase 5 -- CrO3:SO4 Ratio + Cr3+ Control

Y: 14.0" to 20.5". Section: `THE MASTER CONTROL PARAMETERS`.

### CrO3:SO4 Ratio Panel

Amber-bordered rect. Title: `CrO3:SO4 RATIO -- THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT PARAMETER`.

Three horizontal zones:
- `> 125:1 (sulfate too low)` -- coral tint: `Milky deposits, poor coverage, poor hardness`
- `80:1 to 100:1` -- emerald tint: `OPTIMAL -- good coverage, full hardness, best efficiency`
- `< 75:1 (sulfate too high)` -- coral tint: `Poor throwing power, burning, pitting`

Adjustments: `Add sulfate: dilute H2SO4 | Remove sulfate: BaCO3 precipitates BaSO4`.

### Cr3+ Control Panel

Accent `#2EC4B6`. Title: `TRIVALENT CHROMIUM (Cr3+) CONTROL`.
- Must be 1--3% of total CrO3 (2--5 g/L)
- Too low: poor throwing power, burning at HCD
- Too high (> 5%): severe efficiency loss, dull deposits
- Reduce Cr3+: dilution or electrolytic oxidation (high anode area)
- Increase Cr3+: add sugar (sucrose) or proprietary reducer

## Phase 6 -- Defects + Contamination

Y: 20.5" to 27.0". Section: `COMMON DEFECTS AND CONTAMINATION`.

### Defect Table (Y: 21.3" to 24.5")

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Burning (brown/black at HCD) | CD too high, temp low, Cr3+ high, sulfate high | Reduce CD; raise temp; check ratio |
| Milky/hazy | CrO3:SO4 too high (low sulfate), low temp | Add H2SO4; raise temp |
| Poor LCD coverage | Low CrO3, poor ratio, high Cr3+ | Increase CrO3; correct ratio |
| Pitting | Contamination (Fe, Cu), gas pitting | Remove contaminants; filtration |
| Roughness | Lead particles, bath contamination | Filter; maintain anodes |
| Poor adhesion | Inadequate reverse etch | Extend anodic etch time |
| Non-uniform thickness | Anode geometry mismatch | Reshape anodes; add shields |

### Contamination Thresholds (Y: 24.8" to 26.8")

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect | Removal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron | > 5--10 g/L | Reduced efficiency, dull | Porous pot or partial dump |
| Copper | > 200 ppm | Dark spots | Dummy plate; porous pot |
| Cr3+ (excess) | > 5% of CrO3 | Severe efficiency loss | Dilute or electrolyze |
| Chloride | > 200 ppm | Pitting, anode attack | Silver precip or dilute |
| Organic | Any significant | Cr3+ buildup | Run bath hot, high anode area |

## Phase 7 -- Anode Management + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Conforming Anodes (W: 14.0"):**
- Material: Lead alloy (Pb-6%Sn). Or platinized Ti for special apps.
- Shape: CONFORMING -- mirrors workpiece geometry for uniform current.
- A:C ratio: 2:1 to 3:1 (higher than most baths).
- Maintenance: Inspect for lead sludge. Clean or replace periodically.
- Filtration must handle lead particles (10--25 um).
- Shields and thieves: used extensively.

**Right -- Safety & Regulatory (W: 8.0"):**
Coral panel:
- Cr(VI): IARC Group 1 carcinogen (lung cancer)
- OSHA PEL: 5 ug/m3
- EPA: hazardous waste D007
- Full enclosure or lip exhaust with mist eliminators
- Fume suppressants mandatory (PFAS-free)
- P100 respirator or supplied air for maintenance
- Dedicated chrome room with contained drainage
- Biological monitoring: urinary chromium
- Wastewater: Cr6+ reduced to Cr3+ then precipitated at pH 8--9
- EPA limit: 0.5 mg/L total Cr daily max
