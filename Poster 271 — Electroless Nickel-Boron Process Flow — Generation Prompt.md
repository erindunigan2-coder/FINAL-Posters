## Phase 6 -- Zone 5: Troubleshooting Quick Hits

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | SKIP PLATING | Poor activation or stabilizer poisoning | Verify activation; check stabilizer; try Pd flash |
