# Poster #625 -- Construction Workup
## Fixturing -- Flame Hardening

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Flame hardening fixturing ranges from precision CNC-controlled traverse systems to a skilled operator holding a torch freehand. That range is the defining feature of this process. Unlike induction (where the coil is a precision-engineered tool), flame hardening can be performed with standard welding equipment and improvised fixtures. This poster covers the full spectrum from manual to automated.

## Design Notes

The "freehand is legitimate" message is the unique content on this poster. No other hardening process can be performed with such minimal equipment. The manual vs. automated comparison honestly addresses the tradeoff: flexibility vs. repeatability. For the heavy equipment, mining, and maintenance shops that are the core audience for flame hardening, the freehand capability is the reason they choose this process.
