# Poster #168 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Manganese Phosphate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of 8. Manganese phosphate parts are typically the dirtiest things in the shop -- gears caked in machining oil, weapon components covered in grinding compound, bearings dripping with coolant. This is not a gentle wipe-down. Two-stage cleaning (soak clean followed by electrocleaner) is common. Abrasive blasting may precede chemical cleaning for castings with foundry scale. The poster must convey that cleaning for MnPhos is aggressive and thorough -- the coating will not tolerate shortcuts.

Hero visual: a cleaning tank cross-section showing immersion soak cleaning with heavy industrial parts (gears, shafts) on a rack.

## Design Notes

This poster needs to convey that manganese phosphate cleaning is NOT your typical spray-washer situation. These are heavy, oily, industrial parts. The visual language should feel heavier and more industrial than the iron phosphate or zinc plating cleaning posters. The two-stage cleaning comparison is the key decision point for the shop -- when to add an electroclean step.
