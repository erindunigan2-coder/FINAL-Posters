## Phase 5 -- Metal Temp vs. Oven Temp + Oven Types

**METAL TEMPERATURE -- NOT OVEN AIR TEMPERATURE** (Coral border): `A large steel part may require 30+ min of oven time to reach 375 F metal temp.` Use thermocouple data loggers (Datapaq, ECD). `Running by oven air temp alone guarantees undercure on heavy parts and overcure on thin parts simultaneously.`

**Oven Types**: Convection (most common, even heat, mixed parts) | IR (rapid heat-up, thin/uniform parts, line-of-sight only) | IR + Convection combo (best of both: speed + uniformity).

---

## Phase 6 -- MEK Rub Test + Undercure/Overcure + Defect Grid

**MEK Double Rub Test (ASTM D4752)**: MEK-soaked cloth, 2 lb pressure, 50 double rubs. `50+ with no softening = FULLY CURED. < 50 = UNDERCURED.`

**Undercure** (Coral): soft film, poor chemical resistance, poor adhesion, MEK dissolves. Fix: increase metal temp or time.
**Overcure** (Amber): yellowing, brittle, loses flexibility, wastes energy, mandrel bend fails. Fix: reduce temp or time.

**Defect Grid** (3x2): soft film/MEK fail, yellowing, poor adhesion, brittleness/cracking, late outgassing, uneven gloss.
