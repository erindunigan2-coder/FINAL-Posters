# Poster #127 -- Construction Workup
## Silver Plating (Cyanide) -- Process Flow

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Cluster overview poster for EP-13: Silver Plating (Cyanide). Shows the complete 8-stage process at a glance -- every stage visible in one U-flow diagram. Silver is the most electrically conductive metal on earth. There is no substitute for high-frequency electrical contacts, RF components, and anti-galling threaded fasteners. The bath is cyanide-based -- safety messaging is non-negotiable on every poster in this cluster.

Key distinction from other plating clusters: silver requires a mandatory strike bath before the plate bath. The strike prevents immersion deposition on copper substrates (which peels). This two-tank main plating approach is called out prominently in the flow diagram.

### Limitations to Flag

1. **Process flow diagram (Block B -- HERO):** Eight rounded rectangles in a U-flow: top row L-to-R (stages 1--4), vertical connector, bottom row R-to-L (stages 5--8). Stage 5 (Silver Strike) and Stage 6 (Silver Plate) are both Emerald-accented but Stage 5 gets a special dashed-border treatment to distinguish strike from plate. Arrows are simple right-pointing (top row) and left-pointing (bottom row) connectors with a vertical link between rows.

2. **Parameter summary table (Block D):** A compact 8-row table (one row per stage) with key parameters.

3. **"Why Cyanide Silver?" callout (Block E):** Single callout box explaining why cyanide is used (no viable acid alternative for most applications). Includes conductivity comparison vs. other metals.

4. **Troubleshooting quick-hit strip (Block F):** A horizontal strip of 4 common problems with one-line fixes.

5. **CYANIDE SAFETY badge:** Coral-tinted glass panel at top-right of header zone. Standardized "CYANIDE PROCESS -- SAFETY CRITICAL" badge per Watson collaboration flag.

6. **4 pt left-border accents on callout boxes.**

7. **Global Colors / swatch remap for Light edition:** Manual recolor per remap table.

8. **Print size -- 24x36".**

## Design Notes

This is the "map" poster for the entire Silver Plating cluster. The critical distinction from zinc or nickel: silver requires TWO plating tanks (strike + plate). The flow diagram must make this visually obvious -- Stage 5 (strike) uses a dashed border while Stage 6 (plate) uses a solid border, both in Emerald. The cyanide safety badge in the header is mandatory on all 8 posters in this cluster. Silver tarnishing is the #1 post-plating failure mode -- the anti-tarnish stage is not optional for most applications.
