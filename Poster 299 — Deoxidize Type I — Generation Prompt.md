## Phase 4 -- Desmut Chemistry Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THREE DESMUT APPROACHES`.

Three panels:
- **Standard HNO3** (`#27AE60`): HNO3 25--50% / Ambient / 30--120 sec. For 6xxx, 5xxx, 1xxx. No HF needed.
- **HNO3 + HF** (`#E8A020`): HNO3 25--50% + HF 1--3% / Ambient / 60--180 sec. For 2024, 7075 (copper alloys). HF safety callout.
- **Chromic-Sulfuric Deox** (`#E05C5C`): Na2Cr2O7 + H2SO4 (legacy). `Contains Cr6+ but permitted since the line already uses CrO3`. Excellent on all alloys. Being phased out industry-wide.

---

## Phase 5 -- Alloy Routing Table

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHICH ALLOY GETS WHICH DESMUT`.

| Alloy | Smut Character | Recommended Desmut | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6061 / 6063 | Light gray | Straight HNO3 | Standard; easy removal |
| 5052 | Light | Straight HNO3 | Good response |
| 2024 | Heavy dark copper | HNO3 + HF or chromic-sulfuric | Cu residue requires aggressive attack |
| 7075 | Moderate Cu/Zn | HNO3 + HF preferred | Faster desmut than 2024 |
| Cast (high Si) | Very heavy, gritty | Not recommended for Type I | Si disrupts thin oxide |

## Phase 6 -- HF Safety + Defects

Y: 20.5" to 26.5".

**Left -- HF Safety:**
`HF SAFETY -- NON-NEGOTIABLE` `#E05C5C`. Calcium gluconate, training, buddy system.

**Right -- Failure Modes:**

| Failure | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Visible smut through thin coating | Incomplete desmut | Extend time; use HF for Cu alloys |
| Pitting | Over-aggressive HF or too long | Reduce HF concentration or time |
| Discoloration at seams | Trapped smut in crevices | Agitate; consider ultrasonic desmut |
| Chloride contamination of CrO3 bath | HCl residue from HF desmut | Thorough rinse after desmut |

## Phase 7 -- Cr(VI) Context

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `THE Cr(VI) CONTEXT`.

`This desmut step may use chromic-sulfuric acid deox -- which contains hexavalent chromium. This is still permitted on Type I lines because the anodize bath itself is CrO3. However, the industry trend is to replace chromic-sulfuric deox with HNO3/HF or ferric sulfate alternatives even on Type I lines, as part of the broader Cr(VI) phase-out.` `#E8A020`

Callout: `When the line converts from Type I to BSAA, the chromic-sulfuric deox must also be replaced.`
