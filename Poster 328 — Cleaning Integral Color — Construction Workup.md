# Poster #328 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Integral Color Anodizing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of 8. The cleaning stage is identical in chemistry to standard Type II cleaning -- non-silicated alkaline soak clean. The critical emphasis for integral color: any residual organic contamination causes skip anodizing, which on an integral color part manifests as visible bare spots against the colored oxide. No second chance -- integral color shows everything.

### ZONE 4 -- Cleaner Chemistry + Process Tips

**Section label:** `CLEANER SELECTION -- WHAT MATTERS` -- Y: 14.7".

**Two-column layout:**

**Left (X: 0.5", W: 11.0") -- Cleaner Types:**

| Cleaner Type | Suitability | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Non-silicated alkaline (inhibited) | PREFERRED | Standard for all anodizing pre-treatment |
| Silicated alkaline | NEVER | Silicate blocks oxide growth |
| Solvent / emulsion | PRE-CLEAN ONLY | For heavy oils, buffing compound removal |
| Acid cleaner | NOT TYPICAL | May be used for light oxide removal only |

## Design Notes

The key message of this poster: "Integral color shows everything." Unlike thick dyed Type II where minor cleaning lapses may be hidden, integral color amplifies every defect. The silicate warning is the most critical technical point -- it deserves the prominent coral-accented callout. The water-break test is the operator's daily quality check.
