## Phase 5 -- Soak Clean vs. Electroclean

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `TWO CLEANING METHODS`.

**Left -- SOAK CLEAN (IMMERSION)** (accent `#2EC4B6`, subtitle `Standard Method`): 4--8 oz/gal, 160--190 F, 5--15 min, mechanical or air agitation. Best for general production. Limitation: may not remove embedded shop soils. Add solvent degrease for heavily oiled parts.

**Right -- ELECTROCLEAN (ANODIC)** (accent `#E8A020`, subtitle `Critical Parts Method`): 30--60 ASF (anodic), 140--180 F, 1--3 min. Gas evolution scrubs microscopic soils. Best for precision parts, firearms, optics. Anodic = oxygen evolution = aggressive cleaning without metal removal.

## Phase 6 -- Contamination Consequence Grid

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT POOR CLEANING COSTS YOU`.

3x2 grid:

| Contaminant | Defect After Blackening | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| FINGERPRINTS | Red/brown fingerprint pattern visible through black oxide | Wear clean gloves; no bare-hand contact |
| CUTTING OIL | Skip areas -- bare metal or red spots where oil blocked oxide | Degrease before soak clean |
| RUST / MILL SCALE | Non-uniform black; rough, pitted appearance | Acid pickle after clean (Stage 3) |
| SILICATE RESIDUE | Thin or absent oxide -- silicate blocks Fe3O4 nucleation | Use silicate-free cleaners |
| SHOP GRIT / DUST | Trapped particles under oxide -- rough, non-lustrous | Filter cleaner; agitate parts |
| SURFACTANT CARRYOVER | Foaming in acid pickle; spotty activation | Thorough rinse after clean (Stage 2) |

## Phase 7 -- Parameters + Key Controls

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `KEY CLEANING CONTROLS`.

**Left -- Parameter Table:**

| Parameter | Target | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Free alkalinity | 4--10 pts | Titrate 10 mL with 0.1N H2SO4 to phenolphthalein |
| Total alkalinity | 8--20 pts | Titrate to methyl orange |
| Temperature | 160--190 F | Thermocouple or IR gun |
| Oil loading | Monitor | Skim surface regularly |
| Water break test | Pass | Post-rinse: water sheets, no beading |

**Right -- Five Black Oxide Cleaning Rules:**
1. Clean like it's surgery -- black oxide reveals every flaw
2. Silicate-free cleaners only -- silicates poison oxide formation
3. Water-break-free or send it back through
4. Solvent degrease first for heavy oils
5. Electroclean for anything going on a customer's desk
