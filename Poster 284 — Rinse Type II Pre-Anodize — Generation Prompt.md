## Phase 5 -- Contamination Thresholds

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHAT HAPPENS WHEN CONTAMINANTS REACH THE ANODIZE TANK`.

| Contaminant | Source | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chloride (Cl-) | Rinse water; city water | >25 ppm | PITTING -- most damaging |
| Fluoride (F-) | HF desmut dragover | Trace amounts | Attacks growing oxide |
| Copper (Cu) | Dissolved from 2024/7075 | >10 ppm | Mottling and discoloration |
| Iron (Fe) | Water; tank; racking | >100 ppm | Brownish discoloration |
| Acid residue | Desmut dragover | pH shift | Localized concentration changes |

Bottom: `Chloride is the #1 rinse-water contaminant. DI water eliminates the risk entirely.` `#E05C5C`

## Phase 7 -- Failure Modes + Aerospace Requirements

Y: 26.5" to 32.5".

**Left -- Failures:**

| Failure | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Pitting in anodize | Chloride dragover >25 ppm | Switch to DI water; add cascade stage |
| Soft/thin coating | Fluoride from HF desmut | Improve rinsing; extend time |
| Mottled coating | Dissolved Cu/Fe carryover | Monitor metal levels |
| Bath chemistry drift | Acid dragover shifts pH | Thorough rinsing; dwell and drain |

**Right -- Aerospace Note:**
Triple cascade, DI water at all stages, continuous conductivity monitoring, fluoride monitoring mandatory after HF desmut.
