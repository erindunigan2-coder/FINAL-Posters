## Phase 4 -- Standard Dry vs. Preheat Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.5". Section label: `TWO DRYING PATHS -- STANDARD AND PREHEAT`.

**Left -- `STANDARD OVEN DRY` (border `#E8A020`):** For plastisol and solution dip. 4-step flow: DI rinse -> Air blow-off -> Oven dry 200--250 F, 10--15 min -> Cool to ambient. Warning `#E05C5C`: `Plastisol cure at 350--400 F will violently blister any trapped moisture.`

**Right -- `HOT-DIP PREHEAT PATH` (border `#27AE60`):** For nylon, PE, PVC hot-dip. 4-step flow: Rinse -> Preheat 400--600 F -> Inherently eliminates ALL moisture -> Transfer to dip. Note: `Preheat does double duty: dries AND sets dip temperature.`

---

## Phase 5 -- Rinse Quality + Water Management

Y: 15.8" to 20.8". Two-column.

**Left -- Rinse Quality:** After clean: city water < 500 uS/cm. Final: DI < 50 uS/cm. Salt deposits cause osmotic blistering.

**Right -- `COUNTERFLOW RINSE`:** Fresh DI enters final stage, overflows backward. Monitor conductivity.

---

## Phase 6 -- Moisture Verification + Blow-Off

Y: 21.8" to 26.3". Two-column.

**Left -- `HOW TO VERIFY DRY`:** Visual, touch, compressed air probe, IR thermometer > 150 F. Warning: Recesses trap invisible water.

**Right -- `COMPRESSED AIR BLOW-OFF`:** Before oven entry. Focus recesses. Oil-free, filtered. Moisture separator mandatory.

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| BLISTERING (CURE) | `#E05C5C` | Trapped moisture boiling at 350--400 F | Complete drying; blow-off recesses |
| OSMOTIC BLISTERING (SERVICE) | `#E8A020` | Salt deposits from hard water | DI final rinse < 50 uS/cm |
| FISH-EYE (AIR LINE) | `#E05C5C` | Oil from compressed air | Oil/water separator |
| WATER SPOTS | `#E8A020` | Hard water drying on surface | DI rinse; blow-off standing water |
| ADHESION LOSS (PREHEAT) | `#2EC4B6` | Oxidation from excessive preheat | Control preheat per spec |
| UNEVEN PREHEAT | `#2EC4B6` | Thick/thin sections at different temps | Extend preheat soak |
