## Liquid Spray Painting -- Process Flow

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Cluster overview poster for Liquid Spray Painting. The U-flow shows the complete sequence -- 9 stages from racking through inspection. Liquid paint is the oldest and most versatile industrial coating method: from air-dry alkyds to baked automotive clearcoats, from 25% transfer efficiency conventional guns to 95% rotary bells. The film-forming mechanisms panel shows seven different ways a liquid coating becomes a solid film -- no other coating method offers this range.

### ZONE 4 -- Film-Forming Mechanisms + Spray Methods

**Two-column layout (Y: 22.2" to 27.3"):**

**Left -- Film-Forming Mechanisms (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Title: `SEVEN FILM-FORMING MECHANISMS` -- Barlow SemiBold, 18 pt, `#F0EDE8`

| Mechanism | How It Works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Lacquer | Solvent evaporation only; no reaction | Nitrocellulose, acrylic lacquer |
| Oxidative dry | Solvent evap + O2 cross-linking | Alkyd enamels |
| Moisture cure | Isocyanate + atmospheric moisture | Moisture-cure PU |
| 2K chemical cure | Part A + Part B react when mixed | 2K urethane, 2K epoxy |
| Waterborne coalescence | Water evap; particles deform + fuse | Acrylic latex |
| High-solids | Solvent evap + cross-linking (less solvent) | High-solids epoxy |
| UV cure | Photoinitiator + UV light = polymerization | UV acrylate |

Data: JetBrains Mono 11 pt. Mechanism names: Inter Medium 12 pt in `#E8A020`.

**Right -- Spray Methods (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Title: `SPRAY METHOD COMPARISON` -- Barlow SemiBold, 18 pt, `#F0EDE8`

| Method | Transfer Eff. | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Conventional Air Spray | 25--45% | High-quality topcoats, small parts |
| HVLP | 65%+ | General industrial, EPA compliance |
| Airless | 50--70% | Large surfaces, high production |
| Air-Assisted Airless | 60--75% | Heavy industrial, structural steel |
| Electrostatic Rotary Bell | 85--95% | Automotive OEM, highest efficiency |

Bottom note: `HVLP is the regulatory minimum in most jurisdictions. Electrostatic rotary bell is the gold standard for automotive OEM.` -- Inter Medium 12 pt `#27AE60`

### ZONE 5 -- Troubleshooting Strip

**Section label:** `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS` -- Y: 27.7".

**BLOCK G -- Four Cards (Y: 30.7" to 32.3")**

| Card | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RUNS / SAGS | Too heavy a coat or too slow gun speed | Reduce fluid flow; increase gun speed; check viscosity |
| 2 | ORANGE PEEL | Too-fast solvent evap, low atomization, gun too far | Slow thinner; increase atomizing pressure; reduce distance |
| 3 | FISH-EYE / CRATERING | Silicone or oil contamination | Identify source; use fish-eye eliminator additive |
| 4 | POOR ADHESION | Contamination or exceeded recoat window | Clean surface; scuff sand if recoat window exceeded |

Each card: Rounded rect W: 5.5", H: 1.4", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6, left accent 0.06" `#E05C5C`.
