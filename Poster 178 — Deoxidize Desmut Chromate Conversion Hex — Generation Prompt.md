## Phase 5 -- Deoxidizer Chemistry Options

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `DEOXIDIZER FORMULAS -- CHOOSE BY ALLOY`.

| Type | Concentration | Temp | Time | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nitric acid | 30--50% | Ambient | 1--5 min | General Al; 6xxx |
| Nitric + HF | 30% HNO3 + 1--3% HF | Ambient | 30 sec--3 min | 2xxx, 7xxx, cast Al |
| Chromic-sulfuric (legacy) | CrO3 + H2SO4 | 140--160 F | 5--10 min | Heavy oxide removal |
| Non-chrome | Ferric sulfate, persulfate | Ambient--120 F | 1--5 min | RoHS/REACH lines |
| Ammonium bifluoride | 4--8 oz/gal | Ambient | 1--3 min | Cast alloys |

Note: `Chromic-sulfuric deox adds ANOTHER Cr(VI) source. Non-chrome alternatives are available.` coral.

## Phase 6 -- Alloy-Specific Guide

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `ALLOY-SPECIFIC DEOXIDIZE GUIDE`. Four cards (2x2):

**2xxx (High Cu):** Amber. HNO3+HF required. Copper smut is tenacious.
**7xxx (Zn-Cu):** Amber. Same as 2xxx. Copper smut.
**6xxx (Low Alloy):** Emerald. Mild HNO3 sufficient. Easiest.
**Cast (High Si):** Coral. MUST use HF or ammonium bifluoride. Longer soak.

## Phase 7 -- Defects & Monitoring

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Monitoring: acid concentration, fluoride, dissolved metals, temp, visual. Defects: residual smut, over-etched, pitting (excess HF), uneven deox, bare spots downstream.
