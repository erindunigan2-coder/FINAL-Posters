3x2 grid of hazard cards. Each: W: 7.33", H: 5.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

**Row 1 (Y: 3.8"):**

| Card | Hazard | Stat | Key Details | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (X: 0.5") | CARBON MONOXIDE (CO) | `20% of endo gas` 28pt Coral | Colorless/odorless; IDLH: 1,200 ppm; OSHA PEL: 50 ppm TWA; NIOSH REL: 35 ppm; Lethal at 12,800 ppm in minutes | CO monitors at every door. Flame curtains. Never stand in front of open furnace door. |
| 2 (X: 8.17") | HYDROGEN (H2) | `40% of endo gas` 28pt Coral | Explosive range: 4--75% in air; Burns with invisible flame; Lighter than air -- accumulates at ceiling | Burn-off pilots at doors. Adequate ventilation above furnace. Never extinguish pilot while endo flowing. |
| 3 (X: 15.83") | ATMOSPHERE EXPLOSION | `PURGE FAILURE = FIREBALL` 20pt Coral | Endo gas explosive mixed with air; Most dangerous moment: furnace startup; N2 purge min 5 vol changes; Burn-off pilot MUST be lit first | Follow lockout purge procedure exactly. Verify O2 < 1% before introducing endo gas. |

**Row 2 (Y: 9.7"):**

| Card | Hazard | Stat | Key Details | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 (X: 0.5") | QUENCH OIL FIRE | `1500 F parts + oil` 28pt Coral | Flash point must be monitored; Loss of agitation = localized overheating; Degraded oil = lower flash point | Maintain oil below flash point. CO2 or foam suppression. Regular oil analysis. |
| 5 (X: 8.17") | BURNS & RADIANT HEAT | `1700 F+` 28pt Coral | Radiant heat from open doors; Contact burns from hot fixtures; Heat stress in furnace area | Full face shield. Aluminized heat suit. Heat-resistant gloves. Hydration and rest breaks. |
| 6 (X: 15.83") | CONFINED SPACE | `PIT FURNACES + QUENCH TANKS` 18pt Coral | Pit furnaces are confined spaces; Residual CO/H2 after shutdown; O2 displacement by nitrogen | Lockout/tagout. Confined space entry permit. Continuous atmospheric monitoring. Buddy system. |
