## Phase 4 -- Five Spray Methods Hero

Y: 5.0" to 15.0". Section label: `FIVE SPRAY METHODS -- MATCHED TO YOUR APPLICATION`. Top row 3, bottom row 2.

**Conventional Air Spray (`#E8A020`):** 30--60 psi / 25--45% TE / Excellent finish. Best for: High-quality topcoats, small parts. Trade-off: Best finish, worst efficiency.

**HVLP (`#27AE60`):** Max 10 psi at cap / 65%+ TE / Very good finish. Best for: General industrial, regulatory compliance. Note: EPA minimum TE.

**Airless (`#2EC4B6`):** 1,500--3,000 psi fluid / 50--70% TE / Good finish. Best for: Large surfaces, high production.

**Air-Assisted Airless (`#E8A020`):** 5--30 psi air + 500--1,500 psi fluid / 60--75% TE. Best for: Heavy industrial, structural steel.

**Electrostatic Rotary Bell (`#27AE60`):** 20,000--60,000 RPM / 85--95% TE / Excellent finish. Best for: Automotive OEM. Note: The gold standard.

## Phase 6 -- Viscosity + Wet Film Tools

Y: 22.3" to 26.3". Section label: `TWO ESSENTIAL SHOP-FLOOR TOOLS`.

**Left -- `VISCOSITY CONTROL` (border `#E8A020`):**
Zahn Cup (D4212): #2 cup 12--20 sec, #4 cup 15--25 sec. Ford Cup (D1200): #4 15--30 sec. Calibrate to 77 F.
Warning `#E05C5C`: `Cold paint sprays thick and sags. Hot paint sprays thin and runs. Temperature matters.`

**Right -- `WET FILM GAUGE (ASTM D4414)` (accent `#27AE60`):**
Notched gauge pressed into wet paint. DFT = WFT x (% Volume Solids / 100). Example: 4 mil WFT x 60% = 2.4 mil DFT.

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| RUNS / SAGS | `#E05C5C` | Too heavy, too close, too slow | Reduce fluid; increase speed; check viscosity |
| ORANGE PEEL | `#E8A020` | Fast solvent, low pressure, too far | Slow thinner; more pressure; closer |
| DRY SPRAY | `#E05C5C` | Too far, low pressure, too viscous | Closer; more pressure; thin paint |
| OVERSPRAY TEXTURE | `#E8A020` | Adjacent pass overspray on wet film | Adjust overlap; consistent distance |
| FISH-EYE / CRATERING | `#E05C5C` | Silicone or oil contamination | Identify source; fish-eye additive |
| SOLVENT POP | `#2EC4B6` | Solvent trapped under fast-drying topcoat | Flash between coats; slow thinner |
