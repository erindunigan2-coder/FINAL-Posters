## Phase 4 -- Dual Mechanism Hero

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `TWO PATHS TO PASSIVATION`.

### Step 5 -- Dual mechanism panels (Y: 3.8" to 10.0")

**Left -- NITRIC ACID (HNO3)** (accent `#E8A020`): `Oxidizing Acid -- Dissolves AND Oxidizes`.
1. IRON DISSOLUTION: Fe + dilute HNO3 -> Fe(NO3)2 + H2. Cr and Ni resist dissolution.
2. OXIDIZING ENVIRONMENT: HNO3 actively promotes Cr2O3 formation while dissolving iron.
3. PASSIVE FILM: As free iron removed, Cr-enriched surface + oxidizing power -> Cr2O3.
RESULT: Simultaneous iron removal + passive film formation.

**Right -- CITRIC ACID (C6H8O7)** (accent `#27AE60`): `Chelating Acid -- Sequesters Iron`.
1. IRON CHELATION: Fe3+ + citrate -> Fe(citrate) complex. Chelates free iron into solution.
2. NOT AN OXIDIZER: Passive film relies on DISSOLVED OXYGEN in solution and air.
3. PASSIVE FILM: With iron removed, exposed Cr reacts with O2 -> Cr2O3.
RESULT: Iron removed by chelation; passive film via dissolved oxygen.

## Phase 5 -- Alloy Selection Guide

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `ALLOY FAMILY -- BATH SELECTION GUIDE`.

| Alloy Family | Grades | Preferred Bath | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Austenitic (300) | 304, 316, 321, 347 | Citric 1--3 or Nitric 2/3 | Easy; high Cr + Ni |
| Ferritic (400) | 430, 434, 444 | Nitric 3 or 4 | Moderate; lower Ni |
| Martensitic (400) | 410, 420, 440C | Nitric 1 or 2 (dichromate) or Citric 4 | Harder; low Cr (~12%) |
| Precipitation Hardening | 17-4 PH, 15-5 PH | Citric or Nitric 2/3 | Similar to austenitic |
| Duplex | 2205, 2507 | Citric or Nitric 3 | Straightforward |
| Free-Machining | 303, 416, 420F | Nitric 1 or 2 (dichromate) | S/Se inclusions; most difficult |

Note: `Free-machining grades are the most difficult. Sulfur/selenium inclusions create preferential attack sites. Nitric 1 often the only reliable option.` `#E8A020`

## Phase 6 -- Film Characteristics + Industry Trend

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHAT PASSIVATION PRODUCES`.

**Left -- THE PASSIVE FILM** (`#27AE60`): Cr2O3-rich amorphous oxide / 1--5 nm / Invisible (no color change) / No dimensional change / Self-healing. Key: `Passivation does NOT add anything. It REMOVES contamination and allows natural passive film to form.`

**Right -- THE CITRIC ACID TREND** (`#2EC4B6`): Safety (mild vs. fuming HNO3), waste (non-hazardous, biodegradable), regulatory (minimal), spec coverage (ASTM A967 Types 5--8). Gaining share rapidly. Now an EQUAL option under ASTM A967. Industry moving toward citric for new installations, medical, food. Nitric remains dominant in legacy aerospace and free-machining grades.
