## Phase 5 -- Blast Profile + SSPC Grades

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". **Blast Media Table**: aluminum oxide 1.5--3.0 mil, steel grit 1.0--2.5, garnet 1.0--2.0, glass bead 0.5--1.0. **SSPC Grades**: SP-1 (solvent clean), SP-6 (commercial blast, standard for powder), SP-10 (near-white), SP-5 (white metal).

Target profile: 1.0--2.5 mils per ASTM D4417. Blast-to-coat window: `4 hours maximum in humid environments. Flash rust kills adhesion.`

---

## Phase 6 -- Outgassing + Multi-Substrate + Defect Grid

**Outgassing Warning** (Coral): Cast aluminum, hot-rolled steel, galvanized trap gases. During cure, gases escape through molten film = pinholes. Fix: pre-bake at cure temp 10--20 min.

**Substrate-Specific Prep**: Steel (blast SP-6, iron/zinc phos) | Aluminum (alkaline etch, chromate/non-chrome conversion, outgassing risk on castings) | Galvanized (sweep blast, adhesion promoter, SEVERE outgassing -- always pre-bake).

**Defect Grid** (3x2): adhesion loss, fish-eye/cratering, outgassing pinholes, flash rust blisters, poor profile adhesion, mill scale lifting.
