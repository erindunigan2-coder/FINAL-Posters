## Phase 5 -- HE Bake + Inspection

Y: 13.0" to 19.5". Section: `HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT BAKE AND INSPECTION`.

### Step 8 -- HE bake (Y: 13.8" to 16.5")

Fill `#1E2435`, border 2pt `#E05C5C`. Title: `HE BAKE -- SAME REQUIREMENTS AS WATTS NICKEL` `#E05C5C`.
- Required for steel >=31 HRC per ASTM B850.
- 375 +/- 25 F (190 +/- 14 C)
- Within 4 hours general; within 1 hour some aerospace specs
- Duration: 8--24 hours by hardness class
- Sulfamate generates LESS hydrogen (95--100% cathode eff) but bake requirement is the same
- Electroforming on mandrels: HE bake NOT applicable (mandrels are not structural)

## Phase 6 -- Common Failures

Y: 19.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT POST-TREATMENT`.

| Failure | Root Cause | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Cracking during grinding | Excessive tensile stress | Scrap; review stress control |
| Electroform distortion | Stress too high for separation | Shell warps on release; stress reducer needed |
| HE failure in service | Bake skipped or delayed | Delayed brittle fracture; catastrophic |
| Under-thickness | CD too low; poor distribution | Out of spec; re-plate or scrap |
| Over-thickness | Plating time too long | Excessive grinding; dimensional risk |
| Adhesion failure | Inadequate activation or Wood's strike | Deposit lifts during grinding or in service |
| Surface roughness | Particulates; poor filtration | Extra grinding; reduced yield |

**Right -- Safety (X: 15.5", W: 8.0"):**
Coral-tinted glass.
- Grinding nickel: generates Ni dust (respiratory, IARC Group 1). Local exhaust + P100 respirator.
- HE bake ovens: burn hazard at 375 F. Lockout/tagout.
- Mandrel dissolution (NaOH for Al): caustic burn hazard.
- Nickel dust: combustible in fine powder. Manage accumulation.
- PPE: respirator for grinding; heat-resistant gloves for oven.
