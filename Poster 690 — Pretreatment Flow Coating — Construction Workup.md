# Poster #690 -- Construction Workup
## Pretreatment -- Flow Coating

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 4 of 7. Pretreatment bridges the gap between a clean, profiled surface and a coating that stays on it. For most flow coating applications on steel, that means iron phosphate -- simple, effective, and cheap. Zinc phosphate is the upgrade path when salt spray hours matter. Same specifications as liquid spray painting -- the chemistry does not care how the paint arrives, only that the surface is ready for it.

Hero visual: side-by-side comparison of iron phosphate vs. zinc phosphate showing coating weight, crystal structure, and salt spray performance.

## Design Notes

This poster bridges chemistry and practicality. The iron vs. zinc phosphate comparison is the centerpiece -- most shops run iron phosphate and wonder if they should upgrade. The answer is usually no, unless the specification says otherwise. The selection guide table gives them a decision framework. The coating weight measurement methods panel gives QC something actionable.
