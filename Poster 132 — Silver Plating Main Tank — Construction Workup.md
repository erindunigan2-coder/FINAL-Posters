# Poster #132 -- Construction Workup
## Silver Plating (Cyanide) -- Main Tank

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stages 5 and 6 of 8. This poster covers BOTH the silver strike and the silver plate bath -- they are two separate tanks but functionally linked. The strike prevents immersion deposition on copper; the plate bath builds thickness to spec. This is the densest poster in the EP-13 cluster.

Hero visual: a plating tank cross-section with silver anodes, cathode (workpiece), current flow lines, and silver ion migration. The strike bath gets a smaller inset diagram above the main plate bath diagram.

### ZONE 5 -- Defect Diagnosis Grid

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK F -- 3x2 Grid (Y: 21.3" to 26.3")**

| Position | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | TARNISHING (POST-PLATE) | `#E8A020` | Sulfide in atmosphere/rinse, no anti-tarnish | Anti-tarnish treatment (Stage 8) |
| R1C2 | DARK DEPOSIT | `#E05C5C` | Organic contamination, carbonate >75 g/L, sulfide, low free CN | Carbon treat; freeze-out carbonate |
| R1C3 | PEELING FROM COPPER | `#E05C5C` | No strike or inadequate strike | Verify strike bath; extend strike time |
| R2C1 | ROUGH / NODULAR | `#E8A020` | Anode sludge, particulate, high local CD | Check anode bags; increase filtration |
| R2C2 | BURNING AT HCD | `#2EC4B6` | CD too high, low silver metal | Reduce CD; add silver cyanide |
| R2C3 | DULL DEPOSIT | `#2EC4B6` | Low brightener, organic contamination | Hull cell check; carbon treat |

Each card: W: 7.33", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`, radius 4, left accent in defect color.

## Design Notes

This is the densest poster in EP-13 -- two separate baths on one poster. The dual-tank hero is unusual for the series but necessary because the strike and plate are functionally inseparable. The free cyanide gauge is the equivalent of the NaOH:Zn ratio gauge in Poster #36 -- the single most important control parameter. Sulfide sensitivity is unique to silver and must be prominently called out.
