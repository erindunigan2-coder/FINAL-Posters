**Right -- two callout cards:**

1. `PURPOSE` (accent `#2EC4B6`): `Remove ALL alkaline cleaner residues before the acid deoxidize stage. Alkaline carryover neutralizes the deoxidizer, wastes chemistry, and produces incomplete deoxidation that leads to coating failures.`

2. `CRITICAL CHECK` (accent `#E05C5C`): `If rinse pH stays above 9.0, cleaner drag-over is excessive. Increase overflow rate, add a second rinse stage, or reduce cleaner concentration.`

### Step 6 -- Rinse method comparison (Y: 9.5" to 15.0")

Three side-by-side panels:

**Overflow Immersion** (accent `#2EC4B6`): `1--3 gal/min overflow` / `Parts fully submerged` / Best for: rack lines, small batch. Limitation: higher water consumption.

**Spray Rinse** (accent `#E8A020`): `15--25 psi, ambient` / `Fan nozzles for coverage` / Best for: conveyor lines, high-throughput. Limitation: recesses may not rinse completely.

**Counter-Flow (2-Stage)** (accent `#27AE60`): `Fresh DI enters Stage 2, overflows to Stage 1` / `Parts travel Stage 1 -> Stage 2` / Best for: automotive OEM, high-volume. Limitation: more tankage, higher capital.

## Phase 6 -- Drag-Over Impact

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `ALKALINE DRAG-OVER -- THE INVISIBLE KILLER`.

Two panels:

**Left -- The Problem** (accent `#E05C5C`):
1. Alkaline cleaner clings to parts and rack
2. Drag-over enters the deox tank
3. Alkaline chemistry raises deox pH
4. Acid is neutralized -- less effective deox
5. Oxide and smut remain on aluminum surface
6. Ti/Zr coating deposits unevenly or not at all
7. Paint adhesion fails in service

**Right -- The Prevention** (accent `#27AE60`):
- Adequate drain time over cleaner (10--15 sec)
- Fresh water overflow at 1--3 gal/min
- Counter-flow rinse for high-volume lines
- Monitor rinse conductivity daily
- Monitor rinse pH -- must drop below 9.0
- Double rinse stages for aerospace processing
- Periodic dump-and-refill if conductivity climbs
