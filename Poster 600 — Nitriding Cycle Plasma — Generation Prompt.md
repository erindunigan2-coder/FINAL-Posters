## Phase 5 -- Gas Ratio Control

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `GAS RATIO = COMPOUND LAYER CONTROL`.

Horizontal bar gauge showing N2% left to right:

| N2% | Result |
|---|---|
| 5% | Diffusion zone ONLY -- NO compound layer (`THIS IS UNIQUE TO PLASMA`) |
| 15% | Thin gamma-prime layer |
| 25% | STANDARD -- mixed compound layer |
| 50% | Thicker compound layer |
| 80% | Thick epsilon layer |

3 callout boxes below:

**GAMMA-PRIME (`#27AE60`):** More ductile; better fatigue; preferred; lower N2 favors.
**EPSILON (`#2EC4B6`):** Harder; better wear; higher N2 + lower temp favors.
**NO COMPOUND LAYER (`#E8A020`):** Diffusion zone only; max fatigue life; only in plasma; requires < 10% N2.

## Phase 6 -- Case Depth + Mechanism

Section label: `CASE DEPTH VS. TIME (at 970 F / 520 C)`. 4-row table:

| 20 hr | 0.010--0.016 in | 0.0003--0.0006 in |
| 40 hr | 0.016--0.024 in | 0.0004--0.0008 in |

Note: `Plasma is 20--30% faster than gas for equivalent case depth.`

### Mechanism (right)

Section label: `HOW IT WORKS -- THE PHYSICS`. 9 numbered steps:

1. DC ionizes N2/H2 at 1--5 mbar
2. N+ ions accelerate toward cathode (workpiece)
3. Ion bombardment SPUTTERS surface -- removes oxides/passive films
4. This is why plasma works on stainless (breaks Cr2O3)
5. Atomic N implants and diffuses inward
6. N combines with Cr/Al/Mo/V to form nitride precipitates
7. Coherent precipitates strain lattice = extreme hardness
8. NO PHASE TRANSFORMATION -- steel stays ferritic
9. NO QUENCH -- hardness from precipitation

Bottom: `Dimensional change: 0.0001--0.0005 in/surface. Parts CAN be finish-machined BEFORE nitriding.`
