## Phase 5 -- Waste Segregation

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `CYANIDE WASTE -- SEGREGATION IS MANDATORY`.

Three columns:

**CN Waste Stream (`#E05C5C`):** Source: Rinse 1 + bath dumps. Two-stage alkaline chlorination: `CN- --> CNO-` (pH >10, ORP +350 mV) then `CNO- --> CO2 + N2` (pH 8--9, ORP +600 mV). NEVER mix CN with acid waste.

**Silver Recovery (`#E8A020`):** Electrolytic or IX. Feed: Rinse 1 water. Recovery 90--95%. Silver is precious metal -- recovery pays for equipment.

**Final Discharge (`#27AE60`):** After CN destruction + Ag removal. Test: residual CN <0.1 mg/L, Ag <0.05 mg/L, pH 6--9. Discharge per POTW permit.
