| Tank 1 -- Drag-Out (Still) | `#E8A020` | `DRAG-OUT RINSE (Still -- No Overflow)` |
| Tank 2 -- Running Rinse 1 | `#2EC4B6` | `RUNNING RINSE 1 (Counterflow)` |
| Tank 3 -- Final Rinse | `#27AE60` | `FINAL RINSE (Fresh Water In)` |

**Part movement arrows** (above tanks, pointing RIGHT): `PARTS MOVE THIS WAY -->` (3 pt `#3A4055`)
**Water flow arrows** (below tanks, pointing LEFT): `<-- CLEAN WATER FLOWS THIS WAY` (3 pt `#2EC4B6`)

Tank contents:

*Tank 1:* `HIGH cleaner concentration` / `No overflow -- captures 50-70% of cleaner drag-out` / `Return to cleaner tank periodically`

*Tank 2:* `MODERATE dilution` / `Overflow feeds backward (counterflow)` / `pH < 10`

*Tank 3:* `LOW -- near clean water` / `Fresh water inlet here` / `pH < 9.0 (target)` / `Conductivity < 200 uS/cm`

**Bottom callout** (teal glass pill):
Title: `THE COUNTERFLOW PRINCIPLE`
`Parts move from dirty to clean. Water moves from clean to dirty. Each stage dilutes further. The final rinse sees the least contamination and uses the least water. This is why counterflow rinsing uses 5-10x less water than a single overflow rinse at the same cleanliness level.`

## Phase 6 -- Drag-Out Recovery

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section label: `DRAG-OUT RECOVERY -- SAVE CHEMISTRY, SAVE MONEY`.

Full-width glass card with three-column interior:

**What is Drag-Out?** (`#E8A020`):
`Drag-out is the cleaner solution clinging to parts and racks as they leave the tank. Typical: 50-200 mL per m2 of surface area. This is your chemistry leaving the building.`

**The Still Rinse Trick** (`#2EC4B6`):
`A still (non-flowing) rinse tank immediately after the soak clean captures 50-70% of drag-out. Return this concentrated rinse to the cleaner tank periodically. Cost: one extra tank. Savings: 50-70% chemistry reduction.`

**Drag-Out Math** (`#27AE60`):
JetBrains Mono: `At 100 mL/m2 drag-out:` / `1000 m2/day production =` / `100 L/day cleaner lost` / `x 250 days/year =` / `25,000 L/year` / `Still rinse recovers ~15,000 L`
