## Phase 4 -- Pore Structure Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE ANODIC OXIDE -- PORE STRUCTURE`.

Three panels across the width showing the pore structure cross-section in three states:

**Panel 1 -- As-Anodized:** Hexagonal cells with empty central pores, barrier layer at bottom, aluminum substrate below. Labels: `Pore mouth (open)` / `Cell wall (Al2O3)` / `Pore (~10--25 nm diameter)` / `Barrier layer (~1 nm/V)`. Note: `Pores are OPEN -- coating absorbs anything it contacts`

**Panel 2 -- After Dye:** Same structure with colored dots inside pores (dye molecules). Labels: `Organic dye molecules` / `Physically trapped in pores`. Note: `Dye is NOT permanent until sealed -- it will bleed out in hot water` `#E05C5C`

**Panel 3 -- After Seal:** Pore mouths closed (swelled shut by hydration). Labels: `Hydrated pore mouth (sealed)` / `Dye locked inside` / `Boehmite (AlOOH) forms`. Note: `Seal = hydration of Al2O3 to AlOOH` `#27AE60`

Bottom callout: `The pore structure is the key to understanding dyeing AND sealing. The oxide is not a solid wall -- it is a forest of nano-scale tubes. Dye fills the tubes. Seal closes them.`

## Phase 5 -- Dye Parameters + Color Mechanisms

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `DYEING -- COLOR INSIDE THE OXIDE`.

**Left -- Dye Parameters:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Chemistry | Organic acid dyes (azo, anthraquinone, phthalocyanine) |
| Concentration | 0.5--5 g/L (typical 1--3 g/L) |
| Temperature | 50--65 C (120--150 F) |
| pH | 5.0--6.5 |
| Time | 5--30 minutes (more = deeper color) |
| Min coating | 8 um (0.3 mil) |
| Agitation | Mild |

**Right -- Dye Color Types:**

| Type | UV Stability | Palette |
|---|---|---|
| Organic dyes | Moderate | Full spectrum -- any color |
| Inorganic (metal salt) | Excellent | Limited -- gold, bronze, black |
| Two-step electrolytic | Excellent | Bronze to black |

## Phase 6 -- Seal Method Comparison

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `SEAL OPTIONS -- CHOOSE BY APPLICATION`.

| Seal Method | Temp | Chemistry | Time | Corrosion (B117) | Dye Retention | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hot DI water | 205--212 F | DI water <50 uS/cm; pH 5.5--7.5 | 1--3 min/um; min 15 min | 336+ hrs | Moderate | General purpose |
| Nickel acetate | 158--185 F | 5--8 g/L Ni(OAc)2; pH 5.5--7.0 | 15--25 min | 500+ hrs | Excellent | Dyed parts; aerospace |
| Sodium dichromate | 194--205 F | 50--80 g/L; pH 5.0--6.5 | 15--30 min | 1000+ hrs | Good | Max corrosion (legacy -- Cr6+) |
| Cold seal | 77--86 F | Nickel fluoride-based; pH 5.5--6.5 | 15--20 min | Good | Good | High volume; energy savings |

Dichromate row: coral accent + `Contains Cr6+ -- regulatory phase-out`.
Bottom note: `Nickel acetate seal is the modern standard for dyed work.`

## Phase 7 -- Seal Testing + Failure Modes

Y: 26.5" to 32.5".

**Left -- Seal Quality Testing:**

| Test | Standard | Pass Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Dye spot test | ASTM B680 | Sealed surface resists dye |
| Admittance (STEP) | ISO 2931 | Lower admittance = better seal |
| Acid dissolution | ASTM B680 | <30 mg/dm2 loss |

Note: `Dye spot test is the fastest field check.`

**Right -- Failure Modes:**

| Failure | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Seal bloom (white haze) | Tap water minerals; pH too high | Use DI water; check pH |
| Dye bleed during seal | Hot water seal temp too high | Switch to nickel acetate |
| Crazing (fine cracks) | Thermal shock -- cold part into boiling seal | Pre-warm parts |
| Incomplete seal | Time too short or temp too low | Verify time and temp; re-seal |
| Color shift after seal | Normal -- slight lightening | Expected 1--3% reflectance change |
