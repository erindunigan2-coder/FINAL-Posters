## Phase 4 -- Gel-Flow-Cure Timeline Hero

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section label: `TEMPERATURE vs. VISCOSITY -- THE THREE PHASES`. Horizontal timeline with three colored zones:

Phase 1 -- MELT/GEL (Amber, 0--5 min): temp rising, viscosity drops sharply, particles soften and merge.
Phase 2 -- FLOW/LEVEL (Emerald, 5--13 min): temp at 350--400 F, viscosity at MINIMUM, `THIS is when finish quality is determined`.
Phase 3 -- CROSSLINK/CURE (Coral, 13--20 min): viscosity rises steeply, film hardens irreversibly, point of no return.

Key: `The flow/level phase is the ONLY window where surface finish can improve. Once cross-linking starts, the surface is locked in.`

## Phase 5 -- Three-Phase Breakdown

Three cards: Melt/Gel (first 2--5 min, particles soften, benzoin degassing) | Flow/Level (next 3--8 min, minimum viscosity, surface tension drives leveling, THIS PHASE DETERMINES ORANGE PEEL) | Crosslink/Cure (remaining time, viscosity rises, irreversible 3D polymer network).

---

## Phase 6 -- Factors + Orange Peel + Defect Grid

**Factors Affecting Leveling** (5-row table): particle size (finer = smoother), cure schedule (slower ramp = more flow), flow additives (reduce viscosity), film thickness (thicker flows better), powder formulation (varies).

**Smooth vs. Orange Peel**: Smooth (Emerald) = adequate flow time, correct particle size, proper thickness. Orange peel (Coral) = insufficient flow, coarse particles, thin film, back-ionization. `Not repairable without strip and recoat.`

**Defect Grid** (3x2): orange peel, yellowing (overbake), embrittlement, pinholes, surface cratering, thin spots with texture.
