### ZONE 3 -- Application Technique (HERO)

**Section label:** `SPRAY APPLICATION PROCEDURE` -- Y: 4.4".

**BLOCK B -- 6-Step Application Technique**

Y: 5.0" to 11.5". Six cards in a 2x3 grid (3 columns, 2 rows). Each card: W: 7.3", H: 3.0", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6, top accent 4 pt `#27AE60`.

| Step | Title | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PREHEAT SUBSTRATE | Use flame gun (no feedstock) to heat substrate to 80--120 degC (176--248 degF). Improves adhesion by reducing thermal shock on first pass. Verify with contact pyrometer or IR gun. |
| 2 | FIRST PASS -- BOND LAYER | Spray first pass at close standoff for maximum particle impact. This layer anchors everything above it. Keep traverse speed steady -- no stopping on the surface. |
| 3 | BUILD PASSES | Add 25--75 microns per pass depending on material. Overlap each pass 25--50% of spray footprint width. Maintain consistent standoff and traverse speed. |
| 4 | MONITOR TEMPERATURE | Check substrate temperature between passes. Keep below 150 degC for steel, 120 degC for aluminum. Use compressed air cooling between passes if needed. |
| 5 | IN-PROCESS THICKNESS CHECK | Measure thickness on sacrificial tabs or non-critical areas using mag gauge or eddy current. Compare to target. Total range: 100--2000 microns depending on application. |
| 6 | FINAL VISUAL CHECK | Uniform coverage, no bare spots, no blistering, no excessive orange peel. Coating should have consistent texture and color across the entire spray area. |

Step numbers: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 28 pt `#27AE60`. Title: Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#F0EDE8`. Details: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8` at 80%.

### ZONE 4 -- Wire vs. Powder Application

**Section label:** `WIRE VS. POWDER -- APPLICATION DIFFERENCES` -- Y: 15.7".

**BLOCK D -- Side-by-Side Application Notes**

Y: 16.3" to 21.5".

**Left -- Wire Application (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#27AE60`
- Title: `WIRE APPLICATION` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#27AE60`

Content (Inter Regular 14 pt, line height 160%):
- `Wire feed rate controls deposition: 1--8 m/min`
- `Watch for "spitting" -- unmelted wire fragments in coating`
- `Spitting fix: reduce wire speed or increase flame heat`
- `Compressed air atomizes molten wire tip into fine droplets`
- `Higher particle velocity (80--200 m/s) than powder variant`
- `Best for: zinc/aluminum corrosion coatings, dimensional repair with ductile metals`

**Right -- Powder Application (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `POWDER APPLICATION` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`

Content:
- `Powder feed rate: 20--60 g/min via gravity hopper or carrier gas`
- `Lower particle velocity (40--100 m/s) -- more porous deposits`
- `Adjust carrier gas flow for consistent powder plume`
- `Self-fluxing alloys (NiCrBSi): spray first, fuse later`
- `Fusing remelts coating at 1000--1100 degC -- eliminates porosity`
- `Best for: wear coatings, self-fluxing alloys, exotic material selection`

### ZONE 6 -- Defect Watch

**Section label:** `DEFECT WATCH -- WHAT TO LOOK FOR DURING SPRAY` -- Y: 28.7".

Four cards, W: 5.5", H: 2.5", left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Card | X | Defect | Visual Cue | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | SPITTING (WIRE) | Visible sparks or chunks in spray pattern; rough, bumpy deposit | Reduce wire feed rate; verify flame is neutral |
| 2 | 6.33" | SUBSTRATE OVERHEATING | Part discoloration; coating lifting at edges; substrate warping | Stop spraying; cool with compressed air; increase traverse speed |
| 3 | 12.16" | UNEVEN THICKNESS | Visible ridges or valleys in coating; inconsistent color | Check traverse speed consistency; verify overlap is 25--50% |
| 4 | 18.0" | DELAMINATION DURING SPRAY | Coating flaking off as new passes are applied | Stop immediately; strip and re-blast; check for contamination |
