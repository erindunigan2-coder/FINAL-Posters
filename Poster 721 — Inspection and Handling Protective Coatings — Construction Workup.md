This poster does not just close the Protective Coatings cluster. It closes the entire Plating Posters library. 721 posters, covering every major metal finishing and coating process used in industry. From zinc plating to zinc-rich primers. From anodizing to urethane topcoats. From the plating tank to the paint booth. If it goes on metal, it is in this library. And this poster is the last inspection before sign-off.

### ZONE 5 -- Safety for 2K Systems

**Section label:** `SAFETY -- THE CHEMISTRY THAT PROTECTS CAN ALSO HARM` -- Y: 21.2". Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 28 pt, `#F0EDE8`.

**BLOCK E -- Safety Table (Y: 21.8" to 26.8")**

Full-width rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C` 0.06".

| Hazard | Details | Protection |
|---|---|---|
| Isocyanate exposure (urethane) | TLV-TWA: 0.005 ppm (HDI monomer); respiratory sensitizer; causes occupational asthma | Supplied-air respirator (SAR) for spray; full protective clothing; chemical-resistant gloves |
| Epoxy resin (BPA diglycidyl ether) | Skin sensitizer; contact dermatitis; once sensitized, cannot work with epoxy again | Nitrile gloves (no latex); long sleeves; barrier cream; avoid skin contact |
| Amine hardeners | Skin/eye corrosive; vapor irritant; strong sensitizers | Splash goggles; chemical gloves; local exhaust ventilation |
| Solvent exposure (MEK, xylene, MAK) | CNS effects; flammable; monitor LEL in spray areas | Organic vapor cartridge respirator (short exposure); SAR (extended); LEL monitoring |
| Confined space (tank lining) | OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146; oxygen-deficient, explosive, or toxic atmosphere | Continuous air monitoring; supplied air; attendant required; permit system |

## Design Notes

This is it. Poster 721. The last poster in the entire Plating Posters Inc library.

The five-category inspection flowchart converging to a single ACCEPT/REJECT decision diamond is not just a diagram -- it is a statement about what quality means in protective coatings. Five independent categories of evidence, all required, all flowing to one binary decision. That decision diamond is the quality gate that every coated structure must pass through before it goes into service.

Holiday detection gets a full zone because it is unique to protective coatings and the stakes are existential: a single pinhole in a tank lining can perforate the steel. The safety zone exists because these 2K chemistries are among the most hazardous materials applied in any painting process -- isocyanate at 0.005 ppm TLV is one of the lowest exposure limits in the entire field of industrial hygiene.

The library close-out callout at the bottom is not vanity -- it is a bookend. Poster 1 was the beginning. Poster 721 is the final inspection, the sign-off, the moment where the last coating is verified and accepted. From zinc plating to zinc-rich primers. From the first rinse to the last holiday test.
