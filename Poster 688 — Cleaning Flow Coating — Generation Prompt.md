## Phase 4 -- Water Break Test Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `THE WATER BREAK TEST -- YOUR GO / NO-GO CHECK`.

Two large panels side by side.

**Left -- PASS (`#27AE60`):** `Water-Break-Free Surface`. Continuous unbroken water sheet on metal. ASTM F22. Surface tension holds water film.

**Right -- FAIL (`#E05C5C`):** `Water Breaks on Surface`. Scattered droplets with gaps. Oil contamination present. `DO NOT PROCEED -- re-clean and retest.` Even fingerprint-level oil (< 1 mg/ft2) causes breaks.

## Phase 5 -- Cleaning Methods Table

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `CLEANING METHODS FOR FLOW COATING`.

| Method | Chemistry | Temp | Time | Best For | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solvent Wipe | Acetone, MEK, IPA | Ambient | 2-rag | Job shop / small batch | Water break |
| Alkaline Wash | pH 10-12, 2-5% | 130-160 F | 2-5 min | Batch immersion | Water break |
| Multi-Stage Spray | Alkaline + rinse | 120-150 F | 60-120 sec/stage | Continuous lines | Conductivity < 200 uS/cm |
| Vapor Degrease | TCE or nPB vapor | Boiling point | Until condensation stops | Heavy oil loads | Visual + water break |

Footnote `#E8A020`: `Two-rag method: wet rag dissolves, dry rag removes before solvent evaporates and redeposits.`

## Phase 6 -- Recirculation Contamination Warning

Y: 21.3" to 26.3". Section label: `WHY CLEANING MATTERS MORE FOR FLOW COATING`.

Full-width callout `#E05C5C` accent: `THE RECIRCULATION PROBLEM`. Three-step contamination chain:

1. `Oil on One Part` -- as little as 1 mg/ft2.
2. `Oil Enters Reservoir` -- excess drains back carrying oil.
3. `Every Part Contaminated` -- recirculated coating carries oil to every subsequent part.

Bottom strip `#E05C5C`: `In spray painting, one dirty part affects one part. In flow coating, one dirty part contaminates the entire reservoir.`

## Phase 6B -- Cleaning Failures + Insight

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 4 cards + insight.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| FISH-EYES IN COATING | Silicone/oil contamination | Strain reservoir; re-clean; check air |
| ADHESION LOSS (FIELD) | Incomplete oil removal | Water break test every batch |
| COATING REJECTION | Solvent residue from single-rag wipe | Use two-rag method |
| SILICATE RESIDUE | High-silicate cleaner | Use silicate-free formulation |

Insight `#E8A020`: `The water break test (ASTM F22) is the single most important in-process check for flow coating cleaning.`
