## Phase 5 -- Alloy Guide + "Tri Is Less Forgiving"

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Alloy-Specific Deoxidizer (wider):**

| Alloy | Examples | Deoxidizer | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2xxx (high Cu) | 2024, 2014 | HNO3/HF | Cu smut is tenacious |
| 6xxx (low alloy) | 6061, 6063 | Mild HNO3 or non-chrome | Easiest, most forgiving |
| 7xxx (Zn-Cu) | 7075, 7050 | HNO3/HF | Same as 2xxx |
| Cast (high Si) | 356, A356 | HNO3/HF (extra HF) | Silicon requires fluoride |

**Right -- "Tri Is Less Forgiving":** Coral accent. Hex Cr6+ can "burn through" minor contamination during coating. Tri Cr3+ CANNOT. Incomplete deox = patchy or absent tri coating. `This is the #1 reason tri processes fail when converting from hex.` Rule: `If you could get away with a lazy deox on hex, you cannot on tri.`
