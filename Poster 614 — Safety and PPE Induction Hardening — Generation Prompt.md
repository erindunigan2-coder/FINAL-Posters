### Safety Advantage + Prohibited Items (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

**Left -- No Atmosphere Hazards (Emerald):** Unlike furnace: no CO, no H2, no NH3, no flammable atmosphere, no nitrogen purge, no burn-off pilots, no explosion risk. `One of the safest heat treatments from an atmosphere standpoint.`

**Right -- Prohibited Near Energized Coil (Coral):** Cardiac pacemakers/defibrillators. Steel watches, rings, belt buckles. Steel tools. Credit cards. Cell phones. Loose clothing near rotation. `EMF can heat ferrous objects on your body without warning -- burns can occur inside gloves.`
