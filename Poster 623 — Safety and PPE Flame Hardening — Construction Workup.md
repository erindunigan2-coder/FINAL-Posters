# Poster #623 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Flame Hardening

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Flame hardening safety is welding safety -- open flame, compressed gases, UV/IR radiation, and the ever-present risk of flashback. Unlike induction (where the hazards are electromagnetic), flame hardening hazards are the kind every shop knows: fire, burns, and explosive gas mixtures. The acetylene pressure limit (never exceed 15 psig) is the single most critical safety rule on this poster.

| Pos | Hazard | Accent | Details | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | ACETYLENE EXPLOSION | `#E05C5C` | Acetylene is unstable above 15 psig (1 bar gauge); wide flammability range (2.5--100% in air); cylinders contain acetone solvent | NEVER exceed 15 psig; store upright; chain to wall; OSHA 29 CFR 1910.253 |
| R1C2 | OXYGEN ENRICHMENT | `#E05C5C` | Oxygen is an accelerant, not a fuel. Enriched O2 causes spontaneous ignition of oil, grease, clothing. Even 23.5% O2 (vs. 20.9% normal) is hazardous | No oil or grease on O2 fittings EVER; ventilate work area; no smoking |

| R2C3 | COMPRESSED GAS CYLINDERS | `#2EC4B6` | Heavy cylinders (130--300 lb); high pressure (2,200 psi O2); falling cylinder becomes projectile; valve damage | Chain all cylinders; protective caps when moving; store O2 and acetylene 20 ft apart or with firewall |

### ZONE 4 -- Acetylene Safety

**Section label:** `ACETYLENE -- THE 15 PSIG RULE` -- Y: 22.2".

**BLOCK E -- Two-panel layout**

**Left -- The 15 PSIG Rule (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Rounded rect H: 5.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent 0.06" `#E05C5C`
- Title: `NEVER EXCEED 15 PSIG` -- Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E05C5C`
- Body (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 160%):
```
Acetylene becomes UNSTABLE above 15 psig
(1 bar gauge / 29.4 psia). It can decompose
explosively without any external ignition source.

CYLINDER FACTS:
- Cylinder pressure: ~250 psi (full, 70 F)
- Acetylene dissolved in acetone inside
  porous filler material
- Regulator reduces to working pressure
- WORKING PRESSURE: 5--15 psig maximum

IF YOU SUSPECT A LEAK:
- Shut off at cylinder immediately
- Move cylinder outdoors if safe to do so
- Never use a flame to check for leaks
- Use soapy water only
```

Data: JetBrains Mono Regular 14 pt `#E05C5C`. Body: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`.

**Right -- Oxygen Enrichment Warning (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Rounded rect H: 5.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent 0.06" `#E8A020`
- Title: `OXYGEN -- THE INVISIBLE ACCELERANT` -- Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E8A020`
- Body (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 160%):
```
Oxygen is NOT a fuel -- it is an accelerant.
Materials that do not burn in air WILL burn
violently in oxygen-enriched atmospheres.

CRITICAL RULES:
- NEVER use oil or grease on O2 fittings,
  regulators, or hoses (spontaneous ignition)
- O2 cylinders stored 20 ft from fuel gas
  cylinders (or 5 ft firewall between them)
- Never use oxygen to blow off clothing
  (oxygen-saturated fabric ignites easily)
- Crack cylinder valve before attaching
  regulator (clears debris)

PROPANE ALTERNATIVE:
Oxy-propane: lower flame temp (~4,530 F)
but less explosion risk than acetylene.
Used for deep-case flame hardening.
```

## Design Notes

The 15 psig acetylene rule is the most critical safety message on this poster and must be visually dominant. Flame hardening shares hazards with welding/cutting operations, and many shops already have oxy-fuel safety training in place -- this poster reinforces that training specifically in the flame hardening context. The oxygen enrichment panel is often overlooked but extremely important: the "invisible accelerant" framing makes the danger concrete.
