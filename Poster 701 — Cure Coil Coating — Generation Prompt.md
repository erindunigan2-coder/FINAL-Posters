## Phase 5 -- Oven Parameters + PMT/VOC

### Parameters Table (Y: 14.5"-20.5")

Section label: `PRIMER OVEN vs. FINISH OVEN`. Comparison table.

| Parameter | Primer Oven (Stage 7) | Finish Oven (Stage 9) |
|---|---|---|
| PMT | 400-450 F (204-232 C) | 430-480 F (221-249 C) |
| Oven length | 80-150 ft | 100-200 ft |
| Residence time | 15-40 sec | 20-60 sec |
| Oven type | Gas-fired convection | Gas-fired convection |
| Afterburner | Thermal or catalytic | Thermal or catalytic |
| Cure verification | MEK 100+ double rubs | MEK 100+ double rubs |
| PMT measurement | In-line IR pyrometer | In-line IR pyrometer |

Critical callout: `PMT = PEAK METAL TEMPERATURE, not oven air temperature. Oven air may be 50-100 F hotter. Measure by IR pyrometer.` `#E05C5C`.

### PMT + VOC (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Two columns.

Left -- PMT Measurement: In-line IR pyrometer (continuous), thermocouple data logger (periodic profiling), contact thermocouple (spot checks). `Profile whenever speed, gauge, or coating changes. 50 ft/min reduction = 20-40 F PMT increase.`

Right -- VOC Emissions: 50-70% volume solids = significant solvent. Thermal oxidizer 1,400-1,600 F > 95% destruction. Catalytic 600-800 F. `EPA 40 CFR Part 63, Subpart SSSS: Surface Coating of Metal Coil.`

## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| UNDERCURE (MEK FAILS) | PMT too low / time too short | Increase oven temps; reduce speed; check pyrometer |
| OVERCURE (BRITTLE/YELLOWING) | PMT too high from speed reduction | Interlock oven to speed; profile after changes |
| FORMING CRACK AFTER CURE | Over-crosslinked film (overcure) | Verify T-bend; check PMT history; higher-flex chemistry |
| BLISTERING AFTER QUENCH | Moisture under coating / solvent trap | Verify pre-dry stage; check upstream rinse carryover |

Key insight: `The most dangerous moment is a speed reduction. Strip spends more time in oven, PMT rises. Without interlock, overcure and embrittlement follow.`
