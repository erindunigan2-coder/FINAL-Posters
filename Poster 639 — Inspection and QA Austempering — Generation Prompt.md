## Phase 5 -- Hardness Targets + Metallographic Criteria

**Hardness Table** (Y: 13.5" to 20.0"): 7-row table mapping material, salt temp, bainite type, HRC, HB, tensile. Note: `HRC for steel; HB for ADI per ASTM A897.`

**Accept vs. Reject** (Y: 20.0" to 27.0"):
ACCEPT (Emerald): 100% bainite, < 10% RA for steel, zero pearlite, zero martensite. Etch: 2% nital, 5--10 sec.
REJECT (Coral): pearlite (transfer too slow), untempered martensite (pulled early), excessive RA, mixed bainite + pearlite.
