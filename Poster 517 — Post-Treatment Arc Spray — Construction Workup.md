# Poster #517 -- Construction Workup
## Post-Treatment -- Arc Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Post-treatment for arc spray is dominated by sealing. Unlike flame spray (which has a fusing option), arc spray coatings are almost never heat-treated -- they are sealed and painted. The hero content is the AWS C2.18 seal coat systems and the service life data that makes arc spray the gold standard for structural steel protection. The numbers are staggering: 20--40+ years in urban/industrial environments, dramatically exceeding hot-dip galvanizing for many applications.

### ZONE 4 -- Seal Application Procedure

**Section label:** `SEAL APPLICATION PROCEDURE` -- Y: 15.7".

**BLOCK D -- Step-by-Step Sealing**

Y: 16.3" to 21.5". Six steps in a 2x3 grid.

| Step | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify coating thickness meets specification | Seal does not add corrosion protection if coating is too thin |
| 2 | Apply seal within 4 hours of spray completion | Before moisture enters interconnected porosity |
| 3 | Apply sealer by brush, spray, or roller | Brush preferred for small areas; spray for large areas |
| 4 | Work sealer into coating surface | The goal is penetration into pores, not just surface coverage |
| 5 | Allow seal coat to cure per manufacturer's instructions | Typically 4--24 hours depending on product and temperature |
| 6 | Apply topcoat paint system if specified | Primer + intermediate + topcoat per paint specification |

Step numbers: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#27AE60`. Action: Inter Regular 14 pt. Notes: Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8` at 70%.

### ZONE 6 -- Time-Critical Warnings

**Section label:** `TIME-CRITICAL POST-TREATMENT WARNINGS` -- Y: 28.7".

Four cards, W: 5.5", H: 2.5", left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Card | X | Warning | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | SEAL WITHIN 4 HOURS | Porous coatings absorb moisture within hours. Trapped moisture under seal accelerates substrate corrosion. In humid environments, seal within 2 hours. |
| 2 | 6.33" | DO NOT SEAL WET COATINGS | If moisture has entered the coating (rain, condensation, delay), the coating may need to be re-blasted. Sealing over wet porosity traps water at the interface. |
| 3 | 12.16" | CURE BEFORE TOPCOAT | Allow seal coat to cure fully before applying topcoat paint. Uncured sealer causes intercoat adhesion failure with paint system. |
| 4 | 18.0" | INSPECT BEFORE SEAL | Final visual and thickness inspection BEFORE sealing. Once sealed, the coating cannot be inspected directly. Document all results. |
