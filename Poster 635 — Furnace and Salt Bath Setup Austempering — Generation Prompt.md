## Phase 5 -- Salt Bath Specification

Y: 14.5" to 21.5". Section label: `AUSTEMPERING SALT BATH -- DETAILED SPECIFICATION`. 9-row table: composition 50/50 NaNO2/KNO3, melting ~290 F, operating 300--1100 F (austempering uses 400--750 F), uniformity +/-5 F, agitation required, H-factor 0.25--0.45, maintenance (water/organic monitoring).

Warning: `SALT IS AN OXIDIZER. Nitrate/nitrite reacts violently with oil, grease, or organic contamination.`

## Phase 6 -- Furnace Options + Agitation

**Left -- Atmosphere Furnace** (most common for steel): endo gas or N2, 1500--1650 F, batch IQ/pit/continuous.
**Right -- Salt Bath Austenitize** (direct salt-to-salt): BaCl2-based, fastest transfer, caution: BaCl2 is toxic.

**Agitation panel** (3 columns): No agitation (+/-15--25 F, mixed structures) | Proper agitation (+/-5 F, uniform bainite) | Methods: propeller (most common), pump (best uniformity), gas bubbling (rare), manual (inadequate).
