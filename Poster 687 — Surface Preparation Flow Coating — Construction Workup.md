# Poster #687 -- Construction Workup
## Surface Preparation -- Flow Coating

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of 7. Surface prep for flow coating is almost always on steel -- blast cleaning to SSPC standards. Flow coating is forgiving on appearance but unforgiving on adhesion. A bad blast is a bad coating.

Hero visual: cross-section diagram showing substrate profile after blast, with anchor pattern labeled.

### ZONE 4 -- SSPC Standards Table

**Section label:** `SSPC SURFACE PREPARATION STANDARDS` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- 4-Row Comparison Table**

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Column widths (23.0" total):
- Standard (3.5") | Name (5.0") | Description (8.0") | Typical Use (6.5")

| Standard | Name | Description | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| SSPC-SP3 | Power Tool Cleaning | Power wire brush, grind, sand to remove loose mill scale and rust | Maintenance / touch-up only |
| SSPC-SP6 | Commercial Blast | Remove all visible oil, grease, dirt, mill scale, rust, paint -- at least 2/3 of surface area free of staining | General industrial primers; MINIMUM for flow coat |
| SSPC-SP7 | Brush-Off Blast | Light blast to remove loose material; tight mill scale may remain | Not recommended for flow coat |
| SSPC-SP10 | Near-White Blast | At least 95% of surface free of all visible residue | High-performance primers, zinc-rich |

Data: Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`. Standards: JetBrains Mono 13 pt `#2EC4B6`. Row for SP6 highlighted with left accent `#27AE60` (recommended minimum).

### ZONE 6 -- Common Prep Failures

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 PREP FAILURES` -- Y: 26.7".

**BLOCK F -- Four Problem Cards (Y: 27.3" to 30.3")**

| Card | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ADHESION LOSS | Insufficient profile or contamination | Re-blast; verify SSPC grade |
| 2 | FLASH RUST | Humidity or delay between blast and prime | Prime within 4 hours; control humidity < 85% RH |
| 3 | THIN SPOTS ON PEAKS | Profile too deep for coating thickness | Reduce blast intensity; increase DFT |
| 4 | MILL SCALE REMAINING | Incomplete blast -- brush-off instead of commercial | Re-blast to SP6 minimum; inspect under raking light |

**Key insight callout (Y: 30.6" to 32.3"):**
- Full-width rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020` 0.06"
- Text: `The most common surface prep failure on flow coat lines is not the blast -- it is the time delay between blasting and coating. Flash rust forms in as little as 30 minutes at > 85% RH. Blast and prime the same shift whenever possible.` Inter Medium 14 pt `#E8A020`

## Design Notes

Surface prep is the foundation poster for the flow coating cluster. The blast profile cross-section is the hero -- it must communicate the mechanical interlock concept visually. The SSPC table is the reference that a quality engineer or inspector will use daily. Flash rust is the #1 real-world failure mode on flow coat lines because of the time delay between blasting and the relatively slow flow coat application.
