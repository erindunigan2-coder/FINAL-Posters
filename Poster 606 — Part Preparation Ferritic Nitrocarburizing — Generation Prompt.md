## Phase 4 -- Three Pillars (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `THREE REQUIREMENTS: CLEAN, DRY, AND DRY AGAIN`.

Three tall cards side by side:

**Cleaning (Teal):** Alkaline wash, remove all oils/cutting fluids/chips. Dry completely -- forced air or oven. Check blind holes, threads, cavities for trapped water. Contaminants to remove: cutting fluid, rust preventative, fingerprints, paint, soap residue. Why: residue contaminates salt bath, moisture causes explosion.

**Preheat (Amber):** 600--700 F (316--371 C). Mandatory safety step. Drives off ALL residual moisture. Hold 15--30 min until parts reach temperature throughout. Verify no visible moisture. THIS IS NOT OPTIONAL. Transfer directly to salt bath -- minimize delay.

**Masking (Silver):** Mechanical masking ONLY. Tight-fitting metal caps/plugs, threaded plugs. Copper plate NOT effective (nitrogen diffuses through). Standard stop-off paints dissolve in molten salt. Most FNC parts are fully treated -- masking uncommon.

## Phase 5 -- No Pre-Q&T + Preheat Detail

### No Pre-Q&T Callout (Y: 14.5"--18.5")

Full-width, left accent `#27AE60`. Title: `FNC DOES NOT REQUIRE PRE-QUENCH-AND-TEMPER`.

Two-column comparison: Gas Nitriding (must be Q&T before, adds full heat treat cycle) vs. FNC/Q-P-Q (as-machined, normalized, or annealed OK; operates below Ac1; saves entire heat treat cycle).

### Preheat Detail (Y: 18.5"--25.5")

Section label: `PREHEAT: THE SAFETY-CRITICAL STEP` in `#E8A020`.

**Left -- If Preheat Is Skipped (Coral accent):** Moisture contacts 1050 F salt -> steam (1,600x expansion) -> explosive salt ejection -> 6+ ft splash -> severe burns -> equipment damage -> regulatory investigation. THIS HAS HAPPENED.

**Right -- Correct Procedure (Emerald accent):** 1. Clean/dry. 2. Load on fixtures. 3. Preheat oven 600--700 F. 4. Hold 15--30 min. 5. Inspect blind holes/threads. 6. Transfer directly -- minimize condensation. 7. Immerse slowly.

## Phase 6 -- Pre-Immersion Checklist

Y: 25.5" to 32.5". Section label: `PRE-IMMERSION CHECKLIST`.

Six checklist cards (3x2), left accent `#2EC4B6`:

| Check | Content |
|---|---|
| CLEANED? | Alkaline wash complete. No visible oil, chips, or residue. |
| DRIED? | Force air or oven dried. No visible moisture. Blind holes inspected. |
| PREHEATED? | Parts at 600--700 F throughout. Held minimum 15 min. Transfer without delay. |
| MASKED? | If selective: mechanical masks tight-fitting. No copper plate. |
| FIXTURED? | Parts oriented for salt drainage. No cupping. Drain holes clear. |
| SALT BATH READY? | Bath at 1050--1075 F. Cyanate 35--40% CNO. Sludge cleared. |
