## Phase 4 -- Two-Stage Cleaning Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `TWO-STAGE ALKALINE CLEAN`. Two large panels connected by arrow.

**Left -- `STAGE 1A: SPRAY PRE-CLEAN` (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
Spray, pH 10--12, 1--3%, 120--140 F, 60--90 sec, 15--25 psi. Gross soil removal. Controls: Nozzle alignment / Low-foam surfactant.

**Right -- `STAGE 1B: IMMERSION MAIN CLEAN` (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
Immersion, pH 10--12, 2--5%, 130--150 F, 120--180 sec, mechanical agitation. Deep cleaning of enclosed cavities. Controls: Oil loading dump > 5--8 g/L / Free alkalinity 3--8 pts.

## Phase 5 -- Cleaner Chemistry + Controls

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `CLEANER CHEMISTRY -- WHAT IS IN THE TANK`.

Three callout boxes: **Alkaline Builder** (`#2EC4B6`): NaOH, sodium metasilicate. CAUTION: Silicate residues cause phosphate failure. **Surfactant System** (`#E8A020`): Non-ionic/anionic blend, low-foam for spray. **Chelating Agents** (`#27AE60`): EDTA, GLDA, citrate. Sequester hard water.

Critical controls strip: Free Alk (3--8 pts) | Total Alk (15--25 pts) | Water-Break Test (Pass/Fail).

## Phase 6 -- Defect Grid + Body Drainage

**Defect Grid (Y: 21.3" to 26.3"):** 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| PHOSPHATE FAILURE | `#E05C5C` | Residual oil preventing crystal growth | Increase clean time/temp |
| WATER BREAKS | `#E8A020` | Insufficient cleaning | Verify water-break-free |
| SILICATE RESIDUE | `#E05C5C` | High-silicate cleaner | Switch to low-silicate |
| FLASH RUST | `#E8A020` | Excessive dwell between clean and phosphate | Reduce transfer time |
| FOAM OVERFLOW | `#2EC4B6` | Wrong surfactant for spray | Low-foam surfactant |
| POOR CAVITY CLEAN | `#E05C5C` | Inadequate drain hole design | Extend immersion; verify drains |

**Body Drainage + Water-Break (Y: 26.7" to 32.3"):**

Left -- `DIP-DRAIN-DIP DESIGN`: Bodies tilt 3--5 deg. Cavities must flood AND drain completely.

Right -- `WATER-BREAK TEST (ASTM F22)`: PASS: unbroken sheet 30 sec. FAIL: water beads. `If the water breaks, stop. Nothing downstream will save a dirty surface.` (`#27AE60`)
