## Phase 5 -- CrO3 Bath Contamination Limits

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `WHAT CONTAMINANTS DO TO THE CrO3 BATH`.

| Contaminant | Source | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sulfate (SO4) | Acid dragover, water | >0.5 g/L | Blistering; disrupts oxide growth |
| Chloride (Cl-) | Water; HCl residue | >25 ppm | Pitting -- catastrophic on thin film |
| Fluoride (F-) | HF desmut dragover | Trace | Attacks barrier layer |
| Dissolved metals (Cu, Fe) | Prior tank contamination | >10 ppm | Discoloration |
| Organic contamination | Cleaner carryover | Any | Foaming; voltage instability |

## Phase 6 -- Conductivity Targets + Fluoride Warning

Y: 22.0" to 28.5".

**Left -- Conductivity by Application:**

| Application | Target | Cascade Stages |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | <100 uS/cm | Double cascade |
| Aerospace | <50 uS/cm | Triple cascade |
| Critical/optical | <20 uS/cm | Triple + spray |

**Right -- Fluoride Dragover Warning:**
`If HF desmut was used upstream, add an extra rinse stage or extended immersion time. Fluoride at ppm levels degrades the thin Type I oxide. Monitor final rinse for fluoride.` `#E05C5C`

## Phase 7 -- Failure Modes

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blistering in anodize | Sulfate dragover >0.5 g/L | Improve rinse; analyze CrO3 bath |
| 2 | Pitting | Chloride or fluoride contamination | Switch to DI water; add rinse stage |
| 3 | Voltage instability | Organic contamination (surfactant) | Thorough rinsing; replace CrO3 bath |
| 4 | Thin/uneven coating | General contamination | Improve rinse discipline |
