## Phase 4 -- Cure Timeline Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.0". Section: `THE CURE TIMELINE -- FROM WET TO DONE`.

Horizontal timeline bar spanning full width:
- 0 hr: `IMMERSION COMPLETE` (amber) -- wet coating, begin air dry
- 1 hr: `SURFACE DRY` (amber) -- dry to touch, still soft
- 4 hr: `HANDLE WITH CARE` (amber) -- can rack/transfer
- 24 hr: `FULL CURE` (emerald) -- full hardness, sealer may be applied
- 72 hr: `PAINT DEADLINE` (coral) -- most aerospace specs
- > 7 days: `AGING -- REDUCED ADHESION` (coral)

Three drying method boxes:
- Ambient Air Dry (emerald): Preferred. Room temp, clean air, forced air OK.
- Mild Heat -- Tri Only (amber): Some tolerate up to 150 F. Check supplier TDS.
- NEVER: Oven > 160 F (coral): Some tri coatings also degrade. When in doubt, air dry.

Bottom: `Key advantage of tri over hex: inherently more thermally stable because no Cr6+ to decompose. But "more stable" is not "indestructible."` teal.

## Phase 5 -- Sealer Options + No Self-Healing

Y: 14.0" to 19.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Supplementary Sealers (wider):** Three sealer cards:
- Silane/Siloxane (teal): Hydrophobic barrier. Most common. SST 168 -> 500+ hr.
- Zirconium-Based (emerald): Inorganic oxide topcoat. Good chemical resistance. Aerospace-qualified.
- Organic Polymer (amber): Waterborne/solvent-borne. May affect paint adhesion.
Note: `Sealer is OPTIONAL per MIL-DTL-5541 -- but strongly recommended for tri coatings.`

**Right -- No Self-Healing:** Coral accent. Hex self-heals via Cr6+ migration. Tri CANNOT. Sealers fill this gap -- second protective layer. Key stat: `168 hr -> 500+ hr` (36 pt emerald) bare SST improvement with silane sealer.

## Phase 6 -- Specifications + Paint Window

Y: 19.5" to 25.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Standards:** MIL-DTL-5541F Type II | Class 1A (168 hr SST) | Class 3 (low resistance) | AMS 2487 | SAE ARP 6584 | ASTM B921 | NADCAP AC7108 | ASTM B117.

**Right -- Paint Application Window:** Amber accent. Apply within 72 hr (most specs). Tri is excellent paint base. Primer must be qualified over trivalent (not just hex). Some specs allow 7--14 days if stored properly. Paint prep checklist: surface dry (24 hr min), no handling marks, clean low-humidity storage, document interval.
