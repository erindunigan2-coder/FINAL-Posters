## Phase 4 -- Four-Point Inspection (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `FOUR-POINT INSPECTION`. Four cards in a row.

| Point | Title | Method | Standard | What to Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HARDNESS | Rockwell C | ASTM E18 | Same targets as conventional Q&T |
| 2 | DISTORTION | Dimensional (before/after) | Per drawing | THE KEY METRIC: OD change, roundness, flatness |
| 3 | MICROSTRUCTURE | Metallography | ASTM E3/E407 | 100% tempered martensite; no pearlite, no bainite |
| 4 | CRACK DETECTION | MPI or dye penetrant | ASTM E1444/E165 | Lower risk than conv. Q&T but not zero |

**Distortion Comparison Data**: Martempering OD change 0.0001--0.0005 in vs. conventional 0.001--0.005 in. Roundness 0.0002--0.0010 vs. 0.002--0.010. Distortion reduction: 50--80%.

Why less: equalization ensures surface and core transform together = minimal thermal gradient = minimal stress = minimal distortion.

---

## Phase 5 -- Defect Reference Table

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". 5-row table: bainite in micro (salt too hot/hold too long), pearlite (transfer too slow), retained austenite (temper skipped), cracking (stress risers), distortion still excessive (salt temp/agitation).

---

## Phase 6 -- Acceptance Criteria + Footer

**PASS** (Emerald): hardness within spec, distortion within drawing tolerance (< conv. Q&T), 100% tempered martensite, no cracks, dimensions within print, salt removed, surface acceptable.

**REJECT/REWORK** (Coral): hardness below min, pearlite (process failure), bainite (process failure), cracks (zero tolerance), distortion exceeds tolerance, RA > spec limit (rework: additional temper/sub-zero), salt corrosion on surface.
