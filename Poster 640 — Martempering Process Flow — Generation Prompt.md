## Phase 6 -- Three-Way Comparison

Section label: `MARTEMPERING vs. AUSTEMPERING vs. CONVENTIONAL Q&T`. Three columns.

**Martempering** (Emerald): tempered martensite, salt hold = equalization (5--15 min), transformation during air cool, temper YES, 50--80% less distortion, 45--65 HRC, bearings/gears/tools.

**Austempering** (Amber): bainite, salt hold = full transformation (30--120 min), transformation in salt, temper NO, 60--90% less distortion, 35--55 HRC, springs/clips/ADI.

**Conventional Q&T** (Coral): tempered martensite, rapid quench to RT, transformation during quench, temper YES, baseline distortion, 45--65 HRC, general purpose/all sections.
