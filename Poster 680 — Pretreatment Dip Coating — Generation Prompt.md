## Phase 5 -- Iron Phosphate Baseline

Y: 15.8" to 20.8". Section label: `IRON PHOSPHATE -- THE STANDARD CONVERSION COATING`.

**Left -- Iron Phosphate Parameters (`#2EC4B6`):** 25--75 mg/ft2, pH 3.5--5.5, 100--140 F, 60--120 sec. Applied BEFORE adhesion promoter primer.

**Right -- `WHEN CONVERSION COATING ALONE WORKS` (`#27AE60`):** Solution dip (epoxy, rubber) on steel: phosphate + direct dip. Decision rule: DFT > 5 mils AND thermoplastic = need adhesion promoter. DFT < 3 mils AND thermoset = iron phosphate may suffice.

## Phase 6 -- Primer Application Parameters

Y: 21.8" to 26.3". Section label: `ADHESION PROMOTER PRIMER APPLICATION`.

| Primer Type | Method | DFT | Cure | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phenolic (PVC) | Spray/dip | 0.3--1.0 mil | Bake 300--350 F | Must be fully cured |
| Epoxy (nylon) | Spray/dip | 0.5--1.0 mil | Bake 350--400 F | Withstands 400--600 F preheat |
| CPO (PE) | Spray/wipe/dip | 0.3--0.5 mil | Air dry or bake | Thin is better |
| Iron phosphate | Immersion/spray | 25--75 mg/ft2 | Rinse and dry | Before primer |

Bottom note `#E05C5C`: `Always cure primer FULLY before dipping. Uncured primer dissolves into the dip coat = zero adhesion.`
