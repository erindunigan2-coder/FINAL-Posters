## Phase 4 -- Cleaning Mechanism Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE ALKALINE CLEANING STAGE -- FOR ALUMINUM`.

Two side-by-side panels:

**Left -- Non-Etch Cleaning (CORRECT):** Emerald accent. Badge: `CORRECT FOR ALUMINUM`. Bright aluminum surface with mild alkaline cleaner pH 9--11. Silicate inhibitors protect Al. Surface remains bright and smooth.

**Right -- Caustic Etching (WRONG):** Coral accent. Badge: `DANGEROUS FOR ALUMINUM`. Roughened surface + dark smut layer. Chemical equation: `2Al + 2NaOH + 2H2O -> 2NaAlO2 + 3H2`. Alloy smut precipitates. Over-etched = poor, uneven chromate.

## Phase 5 -- Spray vs. Immersion

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Two-column.

**Immersion:** 4--8 oz/gal | 120--160 F | 3--10 min | pH 9--11.
**Spray:** 2--4 oz/gal | 100--140 F | 1--3 min | pH 9--11.

## Phase 6 -- Cleaner Selection + Alloy Notes

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Cleaner Guide:** Non-etch alkaline (pH 9--11) = YES. Silicate-inhibited (pH 10--12) = YES. Fluoride-bearing = YES (controlled). Mild caustic NaOH < 5% = CAUTION. Strong caustic NaOH > 5% = NO.

**Right -- Alloy Sensitivity:** 2024/2014 (high Cu): smuts easily, avoid caustic. 7075/7050: same as 2xxx. 6061/6063: less sensitive, mild alkaline sufficient. 356/A356 cast (high Si): longer soak time needed.

## Phase 7 -- Monitoring & Defects

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column. Monitoring: pH, concentration, temp, oil content, water-break test. Defects: smut on surface, residual oil, uneven chromate downstream, pitting on aluminum.
