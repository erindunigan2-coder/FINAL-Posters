# Poster #561 -- Construction Workup
## Part Preparation -- Carburizing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Part prep for carburizing is the foundation -- skip it and you get soft spots, non-uniform case depth, and rejected parts. This poster covers three pillars: cleaning, masking (copper stop-off), and surface condition. The copper electroplating stop-off is a direct bridge to the electroplating poster series -- this is where heat treatment meets plating.

| Masking / Stop-Off | 8.17" | 7.33" | `#E8A020` | MASKING & STOP-OFF |
| Surface Condition | 15.83" | 7.67" | `#27AE60` | SURFACE CONDITION |

Each box: Rounded rect H: 8.8", fill `#1E2435`, left accent 0.06".

*Cleaning pillar:*
- Title: `CLEANING` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#2EC4B6`
- Subtitle: `Remove Everything That Blocks Carbon` JetBrains Mono 12 pt `#F0EDE8` at 60%

Content (Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 155%):
```
REQUIRED CONDITION:
Oil-free, grease-free, scale-free, rust-free

METHODS:
- Alkaline wash (spray or immersion)
  140--160 F, 5--10 min
- Solvent degreasing (vapor or immersion)
- Mechanical: wire brush for heavy scale

WHY IT MATTERS:
- Oil/grease residue = carbon barrier
  at surface = SOFT SPOTS
- Scale blocks carbon absorption
- Fingerprints leave visible marks
  on finished case

WATER-BREAK TEST:
After final rinse, water should sheet
uniformly. Any beading = contamination.
```

*Masking / Stop-Off pillar:*
- Title: `MASKING & STOP-OFF` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E8A020`
- Subtitle: `Protect Areas That Must NOT Harden` JetBrains Mono 12 pt `#F0EDE8` at 60%

Content:
```
COPPER ELECTROPLATE STOP-OFF:
- 0.001--0.002" Cu minimum thickness
- Cyanide Cu strike + acid Cu plate
- Cu is an effective carbon diffusion barrier
- Per AMS 2759/7 specification
- Most reliable method for production

STOP-OFF PAINT:
- Commercial anti-carburizing paints
- Brush or spray application
- Less reliable than Cu plate for deep cases
- Suitable for shallow cases (<0.030" ECD)

AREAS TYPICALLY MASKED:
- Threads (prevent brittleness)
- Bearing seats (maintain ductility)
- Weld preps (prevent cracking)
- Any area requiring subsequent machining
```

*Surface Condition pillar:*
- Title: `SURFACE CONDITION` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#27AE60`
- Subtitle: `The Starting Point Determines the Finish` JetBrains Mono 12 pt `#F0EDE8` at 60%

Content:
```
ACCEPTABLE:
- Machined surfaces (turned, ground, milled)
- Forged (scale removed)
- Stamped/formed (clean)

NOT ACCEPTABLE:
- Heavy mill scale (blocks carbon)
- Rust or corrosion pitting
- Decarburized layer from prior heat treat
  (must be removed by machining)
- EDM recast layer (hard, non-uniform carbon)

SURFACE FINISH:
- Finer finish = more uniform carburizing
- Rough machining marks can cause
  localized carbon variation
- Critical surfaces should be ground
  or fine-machined before carburizing
```

### ZONE 4 -- Copper Stop-Off Detail

**Section label:** `COPPER ELECTROPLATE STOP-OFF -- THE HEAT TREATER'S BRIDGE TO PLATING` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Two-column layout (Y: 15.3" to 21.8")**

*Left -- Process Steps (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):*

Rounded rect fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`.

Title: `COPPER STOP-OFF PROCESS` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`

Numbered steps (Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`):
```
1. CLEAN -- Alkaline soak clean per plating
   requirements (same as any Cu plating prep)

2. ACTIVATE -- Acid activation to expose
   clean base metal surface

3. CU STRIKE -- Cyanide copper strike bath
   (0.0002--0.0005" flash coat for adhesion)

4. CU PLATE -- Acid copper plate to build
   thickness: 0.001--0.002" minimum

5. RINSE & DRY -- Thorough rinse; air dry
   or forced air

6. INSPECT -- Verify thickness by X-ray
   fluorescence (XRF) or micrometer on
   witness coupon

7. MASK VERIFICATION -- Confirm all
   specified areas are covered; no holidays
```

*Right -- Why Copper Works (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):*

Rounded rect fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#27AE60`.

Title: `WHY COPPER?` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#27AE60`

Content (Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`):
```
CARBON SOLUBILITY IN COPPER: ~0%
Carbon does not dissolve in copper at any
temperature relevant to carburizing. The Cu
layer acts as an absolute barrier to carbon
diffusion.

THICKNESS MATTERS:
- 0.001" Cu = standard protection
- 0.002" Cu = deep case / long cycle
- Thinner than 0.001" risks pinholes
  and localized carbon penetration

AFTER CARBURIZING:
Cu stop-off is typically stripped by:
- Ammonium persulfate solution
- Dilute nitric acid
- Cyanide copper strip (if permitted)

CROSS-REFERENCE:
See Plating Posters series for detailed
copper electroplating process parameters
and bath chemistry.
```

Bottom callout (Y: 21.3"):
- Pill bar, fill `#E8A020` at 15%, border 1 pt `#E8A020`
- `Copper stop-off is where electroplating meets heat treatment. A plater's precision protects a heat treater's case.` Inter Medium 14 pt `#E8A020`, center.
