# Poster #204 -- Construction Workup
## Black Oxide -- Conversion Stage

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. This is the heart of the process -- the hot alkaline bath where iron is converted to magnetite (Fe3O4). This poster is the densest in the CC-07 cluster. It covers bath composition, the temperature-is-everything rule, the chemistry of magnetite formation, film characteristics, and a defect diagnosis grid. The hero number is 285 F -- the temperature that separates black from red.

Hero visual: a blackening tank cross-section showing the concentrated NaOH/NaNO2 bath at operating temperature with parts immersed and steam rising.

### ZONE 5 -- Defect Diagnosis Grid

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 5 COMMON DEFECTS` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK F -- Defect Cards**

Y: 21.3" to 26.3". 3 top + 2 bottom centered.

| Position | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | RED / BROWN OXIDE | `#E05C5C` | Bath temp < 275 F; depleted NaNO2; diluted bath | Increase temp; add NaNO2; check SG |
| R1C2 | SMUTTY / RUBS OFF | `#E8A020` | Bath too hot (> 300 F); over-immersion; depleted NaOH | Cool bath; reduce time; add NaOH |
| R1C3 | NON-UNIFORM / SPOTS | `#E8A020` | Oil contamination; parts touching; poor cleaning | Improve cleaning; rearrange load |
| R2C1 | RUSTING AFTER BLACK | `#E05C5C` | No oil seal; caustic residue; insufficient rinse | Oil immediately; improve rinse |
| R2C2 | RED FINGERPRINTS | `#E05C5C` | Handling before oil seal; localized contamination before bath | Gloves always; no bare-hand contact |

Each card: Rounded rect W: 7.33", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`, radius 4, left accent 0.06".

## Design Notes

This is the densest poster in the CC-07 cluster -- the equivalent of Poster #23 (Watts Nickel) and Poster #36 (Zinc Main Tank). The temperature gauge is the most important single visual element. Every black oxide operator needs to internalize that 275 F is the line between black and red. The chemistry summary (two-reaction sequence) is simplified but accurate -- Watson confirmed the NaOH dissolution / NaNO2 oxidation / magnetite precipitation pathway. The dimensional change callout (< 0.05 mil) is the key selling point for black oxide in precision applications.
