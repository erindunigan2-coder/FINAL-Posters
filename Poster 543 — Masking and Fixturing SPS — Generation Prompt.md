## Phase 4 -- Masking Materials (HERO)

Y: 3.8" to 12.0". Section label: `MASKING MATERIALS FOR SPS`.

**Materials Table (5 rows):**

| Material | Temp Rating | Reusable | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hi-temp tape (silicone adhesive) | 260 degC (500 degF)+ | No | Quick masking; small areas |
| Metal masks (SS, Cu) | >500 degC | Yes | Production runs; repeatable geometry |
| Silicone plugs/caps | 260 degC+ | Yes | Holes, bores, threads |
| Ceramic fiber tape | >1000 degC | No | Extreme temperature zones |
| Liquid maskant (peelable) | Varies | No | Complex geometry; conformal |

**SPS-Specific Masking Callout (coral accent `#E05C5C`):**

- Liquid suspension overspray SPLASHES onto masked areas
- Protective splash shields may be needed beyond standard thermal masking
- Ethanol-based suspensions: all materials must be ethanol-compatible
- Do NOT use materials that dissolve or degrade in ethanol vapor

## Phase 5 -- Fixturing + Cooling

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `FIXTURING FOR SPS`.

**Left -- Fixture Specifications (W: 11.0"):**

| Fixture Type | Application | Key Specs |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation (lathe-type) | Cylindrical parts | 60-200 RPM; adjustable |
| Turntable | Flat/irregular parts | Variable speed; indexing |
| Multi-axis robot mount | Complex geometry | 6-axis; line-of-sight access |
| Custom jig | Part-specific | Uniform standoff design |

Rules: No shadow on spray pattern. Ground fixture to workpiece. Fixture mass affects thermal management.

**Right -- Cooling Considerations (W: 11.5", accent `#E05C5C`):**

- Cooling air nozzles at substrate backside -- CRITICAL for SPS
- SPS standoff 40-80 mm (much closer than APS 75-150 mm) -- more heat
- Substrate temp 200-400 degC during SPS -- monitor continuously
- Compressed air must be dry and oil-free

## Phase 6 -- Best Practices + Mistakes + SPS Notes

### Best Practices (Left, Y: 22.0"-28.5", accent `#2EC4B6`)

- Mask AFTER cleaning, BEFORE grit blast
- Verify adhesion after blast -- blast can lift tape
- Check mask integrity between bond coat and SPS passes
- Remove masks carefully -- avoid chipping coating edges
- Label all masks for re-use tracking

### Common Mistakes (Right)

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Masking over contamination | Trapped contaminants; delamination |
| Tape not temp-rated | Burns, melts, or leaves residue |
| Shadow from fixture | Uncoated areas or thin spots |
| No splash shield (SPS) | Liquid overspray contaminates masked surfaces |

### SPS-Specific Notes (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `SPS-SPECIFIC MASKING NOTES`. Four cards:

1. Liquid overspray: unmolten suspension is liquid ethanol/water -- it splashes
2. Ethanol atmosphere: booth has flammable vapor -- materials must be non-sparking
3. Check masks between APS bond coat and SPS topcoat -- bond coat spray may shift position
4. SPS produces finer overspray -- masking seals need to be tighter
