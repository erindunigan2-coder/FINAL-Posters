## Phase 4 -- Five Pretreatment Options Hero

Y: 5.0" to 15.0". Section label: `FIVE PRETREATMENT OPTIONS -- MATCHED TO YOUR APPLICATION`.

Top row of 3, bottom row of 2. Each rounded rect fill `#1E2435`.

**Iron Phosphate (`#2EC4B6`):** 25--75 mg/ft2 / 250--500 hr B117 (w/ alkyd) / 500--750 hr (w/ epoxy). Best for: Production lines.

**Zinc Phosphate (`#E8A020`):** 150--500 mg/ft2 / 750--1,500 hr B117 (w/ epoxy). Best for: Automotive, extended warranty.

**Wash Primer (`#27AE60`):** DFT 0.3--0.5 mil / PVB resin + H3PO4 / DOD-P-15328 / Etches + converts + primes in ONE coat / Can apply to damp surfaces. Best for: Field touch-up.

**Chromate Conversion (`#E05C5C`):** MIL-DTL-5541 Type I (hex) / Type II (trivalent) / RoHS: hex phasing out. Best for: Aerospace aluminum.

**Self-Etching Primer (`#2EC4B6`):** DFT 0.5--1.0 mil / Acid-functional / Single-component / No mixing. Best for: Auto refinish, aerospace field repair.

## Phase 5 -- Wash Primer Detail + Self-Etch

Y: 16.3" to 21.3". Section label: `WASH PRIMER -- THE FIELD APPLICATION HERO`.

**Left -- `WASH PRIMER -- HOW IT WORKS` (border `#27AE60`):**
Component A: PVB resin in solvent. Component B: H3PO4 catalyst in alcohol. Mix 4:1 A:B. Pot life 8--24 hr.
Three actions: 1. ETCH 2. CONVERT 3. PRIME. Apply at 0.3--0.5 mil. Do NOT exceed 0.5 mil.

**Right -- `SELF-ETCHING PRIMER` (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
Single-component acid-functional. No mixing. DFT 0.5--1.0 mil. Less aggressive etch than wash primer.

**Right -- `CHROMATE CONVERSION -- ALUMINUM` (accent `#E05C5C`):**
Hex chrome (Type I): Maximum protection. Trivalent (Type II): RoHS-compliant. Non-chrome (Ti/Zr): Growing acceptance. RoHS flag: Default to trivalent or non-chrome unless spec mandates hex.
