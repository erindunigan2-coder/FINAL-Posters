## Phase 6 -- Flow vs. Spray Comparison

Y: 22.9" to 28.3". Section label: `FLOW VS. SPRAY -- WHEN DOES EACH WIN?`.

**Left -- `FLOW COATING` (`#E8A020`):** 90-95% TE. Pump + nozzles + pan. +/- 30-50% uniformity. Excellent interior coverage. Low appearance grade. No compressed air. No overspray. Best for: Tanks, enclosures, structural steel.

**Right -- `SPRAY PAINTING` (`#2EC4B6`):** 30-85% TE. Gun + booth + air + exhaust. +/- 5-15% uniformity. Line-of-sight only. High appearance. Significant overspray. Best for: Appearance-critical, tight DFT.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CURTAINING / SAGS | Viscosity low or drain too short | Increase viscosity; orient parts |
| HOLIDAYS / MISSED AREAS | Shielded surfaces | Reposition; add nozzles; touch-up |
| SKINNING IN TANK | Stagnant surface | Agitate or cover; strain continuously |
| VISCOSITY DRIFT | Solvent evaporation | Check hourly; add solvent per TDS |
