## Phase 4 -- Six-Material Cure Table Hero

Y: 5.0" to 15.0". Section label: `CURE PARAMETERS BY COATING TYPE`.

| Coating | Cure Temp | Time | Mechanism | Critical Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PVC Plastisol | 350--400 F | 10--20 min | Fusion (PVC solvates into plasticizer) | Over-fusion > 420 F: HCl gas |
| Nylon 11/12 | Melt on hot part, cool | Cooling varies | Thermoplastic solidification | Melt ~350--365 F |
| Polyethylene | Melt on hot part, cool | Cooling varies | Thermoplastic solidification | Melt ~230--275 F |
| Epoxy (solution) | 300--400 F | 15--30 min | Thermoset cross-linking | Undercure = poor chem resistance |
| Rubber (latex) | 250--350 F | 15--30 min | Vulcanization | Under-vulcanized = soft |
| Silicone | 300--400 F | 10--30 min | Condensation/addition cure | Per manufacturer |

Below `#E8A020`: `Always define cure by METAL TEMPERATURE, not oven air temperature.`

## Phase 5 -- PVC Plastisol Fusion Detail

Y: 16.3" to 21.3". Section label: `PVC PLASTISOL -- THE FUSION WINDOW`. Three-phase horizontal strip.

**Gel Point (`#E8A020`, ~250 F):** PVC begins absorbing plasticizer. Porous, weak. NOT fused.

**Fusion Point (`#27AE60`, ~350 F):** Complete solvation. Continuous, tough film. TARGET. Below = weak.

**Over-Fusion (`#E05C5C`, > 420 F):** Decomposition. TOXIC HCl GAS. Discolors, becomes brittle. `HCl GAS IS CORROSIVE AND TOXIC. Monitor oven temperature closely.`

---

## Phase 6 -- Thermoplastic vs. Thermoset

Y: 22.3" to 26.3". Two-column.

**Left -- `THERMOPLASTIC CURE` (`#2EC4B6`):** No chemical reaction. Melts, solidifies. CAN be remelted. Nylon, PE, PP, PVC fusion.

**Right -- `THERMOSET CURE` (`#E8A020`):** Chemical cross-linking. Irreversible. CANNOT be remelted. Epoxy, rubber, silicone. Undercure/overcure both failures.

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| PVC UNDER-FUSION | `#E05C5C` | Metal temp < 350 F | Thermocouple verify; extend time |
| PVC OVER-FUSION / HCl | `#E05C5C` | Metal temp > 420 F | Reduce oven temp; profile with logger |
| BLISTERING DURING CURE | `#E8A020` | Trapped moisture/air | Pre-dry; pre-bake porous substrates |
| EPOXY UNDERCURE | `#E8A020` | Insufficient time at temp | MEK rub test; extend cure |
| UNEVEN NYLON THICKNESS | `#2EC4B6` | Non-uniform cooling | Control cooling rate |
| RUBBER UNDER-VULCANIZED | `#2EC4B6` | Oven too low or sulfur depleted | Verify profile; check compound |
