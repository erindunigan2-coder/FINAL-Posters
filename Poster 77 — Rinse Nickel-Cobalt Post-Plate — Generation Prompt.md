## Phase 5 -- Drag-Out Recovery Economics

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THE ECONOMICS OF DRAG-OUT RECOVERY`. Two-column layout.

**Left -- WITHOUT RECOVERY (accent `#E05C5C`):**
Drag-out rate 1--4 gal / 1000 ft2 | Each gallon contains: Ni sulfamate ~350 g/L, CoSO4 ~30 g/L, boric acid ~40 g/L | All goes to wastewater | You pay for chemistry AND treatment. `Double cost: buy it, then pay to dispose it.`

**Right -- WITH RECOVERY (accent `#27AE60`):**
Captures 50--70% of drag-out | Return to bath when specific gravity approaches 50% of bath | Reduces chemical consumption 30--50% | Reduces wastewater load. `The recovery tank is the cheapest piece of equipment on the line with the fastest ROI.`

## Phase 6 -- Post-Plate Surface Check

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT TO CHECK BEFORE MOVING FORWARD`.

| Check | Good | Bad | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visual appearance | Uniform semi-bright metallic | Dull, dark, or streaky | Hold -- investigate bath chemistry |
| Adhesion (tape test) | No lifting | Deposit lifts or blisters | Activation/strike failure -- strip and re-plate |
| Thickness (XRF spot) | Within spec | Out of tolerance | Adjust CD or plating time |
| Co% (XRF) | Within spec (18--25%) | Out of range | Adjust bath metals, temp, or CD |
| Surface roughness | Smooth, no nodules | Gritty, rough | Filter bath; inspect anode bags |
| Staining after rinse | No discoloration | Stains or spots | Improve rinse; use DI water |

Good column `#27AE60`. Bad column `#E05C5C`. Action column `#E8A020`.

## Phase 7 -- Common Failures

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG`. Four cards:

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | STAINING ON DEPOSIT | Plating solution drying on surface | Move to rinse immediately; do not air-dry |
| 2 | CHEMISTRY LOSS | No drag-out recovery; high drag-out rate | Install recovery tank; optimize rack drainage |
| 3 | CONTAMINATED RECOVERY TANK | Iron, organics, or particulate buildup | Monitor; filter before return; dump if contaminated |
| 4 | POST-TREATMENT INTERFERENCE | Residual NiCo solution under Cr topcoat | Improve final rinse; add second overflow stage |
