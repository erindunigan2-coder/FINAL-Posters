## Phase 6 -- Comparison + Troubleshooting

### Gas Nitriding vs. Carburizing (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

**Left -- GAS NITRIDING (`#27AE60`):** 925--1050 F (below Ac1) | Nitride precipitates in ferrite | 0.005--0.030 in | 15--90 hr | NONE (furnace cool) | Near zero distortion | 700--1200 HV | Best for Nitralloy, H13, 4140, 4340 | Q&T required BEFORE. Highlight: `Zero distortion + extreme surface hardness`.

**Right -- CARBURIZING (`#E8A020`):** 1650--1750 F | Martensite from quench | 0.020--0.250 in | 2--50 hr | Oil/gas quench required | Moderate-high distortion | 58--63 HRC | Best for 8620, 9310, gears. Highlight: `Deep cases on alloy steels`.

### Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"--32.5"). 4 cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| EXCESSIVE WHITE LAYER | KN too high; single-stage only | Implement 2-stage Floe; reduce KN |
| SOFT SPOTS | Contamination; Cr oxide passive film | Improve cleaning; abrasive activate |
| CORE SOFTENING | Nitride temp exceeded temper temp | Verify temper was nitride + 50 F min |
| SPALLING / FLAKING | Thick brittle epsilon white layer | Control to gamma-prime; reduce WL thickness |
