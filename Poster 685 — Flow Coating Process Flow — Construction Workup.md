## Flow Coating -- Process Flow

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Cluster overview poster for Flow Coating. Pour it on, let gravity do the work, collect the excess, and recirculate. Flow coating is the simplest industrial coating method and achieves 90-95% transfer efficiency -- better than any spray method. The U-flow shows the 7-stage sequence. The three-method comparison (curtain flow, flood/flow, dip-drain hybrid) is the classification framework. The recirculation system management panel is the unique operational content: viscosity creep from solvent evaporation, contamination filtering, and pot life management for 2K systems.

- Title: `CURTAIN FLOW` -- Barlow SemiBold, 20 pt, `#E8A020`
- Description: `Coating poured from a weir or slot die in a continuous curtain. Part passes through on a conveyor.`
- Best for: `Flat panels, uniform surfaces`
- Viscosity: `20--40 sec (Zahn #2)`

- Description: `Nozzles flood coating over the part from above. Coating flows down by gravity over all surfaces.`
- Best for: `Large 3D parts -- tanks, enclosures, frames`
- Viscosity: `15--30 sec (Zahn #2)`
- Advantage: `Coats complex shapes including interiors`
- Limitation: `Less uniform than curtain; prone to runs on vertical`

*Dip-Drain Hybrid (X: 15.83", W: 7.67", H: 4.3"):*

- Advantage: `Complete surface coverage including interiors`

**Right -- Recirculation Management (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Callout box, fill `#1E2435`, border 2 pt `#E8A020`.
Title: `RECIRCULATION -- THREE CRITICAL CONTROLS` -- Barlow SemiBold, 20 pt, `#E8A020`

**1. Viscosity Creep:**
- `Recirculated coating loses solvent to evaporation`
- `Viscosity rises over time`
- `Check viscosity every 1--2 hours (Zahn cup)`
- `Add solvent to maintain target range`

**2. Contamination:**
- `Recirculated coating accumulates dirt, substrate particles, cured skin`
- `Strain/filter continuously: 60--100 mesh screen minimum`
- `Skim surface skin from stagnant areas`

**3. Pot Life (2K Systems):**
- `The entire recirculation volume has a limited working life`
- `2K flow coating requires careful pot life management`
- `Mix only what you can use within pot life`
- `Flush system before material gels`

## Design Notes

Flow coating is the hidden workhorse of industrial coating -- it lacks the glamour of electrostatic spray or e-coat, but its 90-95% transfer efficiency is second only to e-coat, and its simplicity (pump + nozzles + drain pan) makes it accessible to any shop. The three-method comparison gives the reader a clear decision framework: curtain for flat, flood for 3D, dip-drain for small parts. The recirculation management panel is the unique operational content -- viscosity creep from solvent evaporation is the single most common quality problem in flow coating, and it is entirely preventable with a Zahn cup and a solvent can.
