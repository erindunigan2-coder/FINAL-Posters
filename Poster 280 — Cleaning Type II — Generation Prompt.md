## Phase 6 -- Water-Break Test + Failure Modes

Y: 20.5" to 26.5".

**Left -- Water-Break Test:**
Two side-by-side panels:
- PASS (`#27AE60`): `Continuous water film covers entire surface -- no beading for 30+ seconds`
- FAIL (`#E05C5C`): `Water beads, breaks up, or recedes -- organic residue still present`

**Right -- Failure Modes Table:**

| Failure | Cause | Downstream Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Skip anodizing | Organic residue on surface | Bare spots -- no oxide growth |
| Thin/uneven oxide | Partial organic film | Light/discolored patches after dyeing |
| Poor dye uptake | Silicate deposit from wrong cleaner | Invisible blockage -- most frustrating to diagnose |
| Streaking in etch | Cleaner not fully rinsed | Surfactant carryover accelerates etch unevenly |

| 3 | FINGERPRINTS | Bare-hand handling | Standard alkaline soak; prevent with glove handling |
