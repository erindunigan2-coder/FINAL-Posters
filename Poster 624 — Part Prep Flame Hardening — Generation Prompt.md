## Phase 6 -- Stress Relief + Edge Prep

**Left -- Stress Relief Before Flame Hardening** (accent `#E8A020`):
When: large castings, heavy forgings, extensive machining, abrupt section changes. Parameters: 1050--1200 F, 1 hr/inch, furnace cool to 500 F. Skip for simple shapes, small parts.

**Right -- Edges and Corners: The Overheat Zone** (accent `#E05C5C`):
Problem: thin edges heat faster (less mass, greater surface-to-volume). Prevention: radius all corners min R = 0.060 in; deburr edges; direct flame away; reduce distance at edges. Cast iron special: gray iron is notch-sensitive -- radius first. Highlight: `If you cannot radius the edge, you may not be able to flame harden it safely.`
