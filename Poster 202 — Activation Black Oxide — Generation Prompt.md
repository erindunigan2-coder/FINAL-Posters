## Phase 4 -- Acid Pickle Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `ACID PICKLE -- ACTIVATING THE STEEL SURFACE`.

Pickle tank cross-section: acid solution, border `#E8A020`. Parts submerged with H2 bubble evolution (circles `#F0EDE8` at 20%) and rust/scale flakes dissolving (`#E05C5C` at 30%). Reaction: `Fe + 2HCl --> FeCl2 + H2`. Removes: rust (Fe2O3), mill scale (Fe3O4), passive oxide film, heat tint.

Bottom: `HCl is the most common pickle acid for black oxide -- fast, ambient temperature, excellent activation.` `#E8A020`

## Phase 5 -- Three-Acid Comparison

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `CHOOSING YOUR PICKLE ACID`.

**HCl (MURIATIC)** (accent `#27AE60`): 20--50% by vol, ambient, 2--10 min. Fast. First choice for black oxide. Generates HCl fumes -- ventilation required.

**H2SO4** (accent `#E8A020`): 10--25% by vol, 120--160 F, 5--15 min. Moderate speed (needs heat). Better for heavy scale. Less fuming.

**H3PO4** (accent `#2EC4B6`): 10--25% by vol, ambient--140 F, 5--15 min. Slowest. Gentlest. Residual phosphate can aid oil retention.

---

## Phase 6 -- Over-Pickle + Surface Effects

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `THE OVER-PICKLE PROBLEM`.

**Left -- PROPERLY PICKLED** (accent `#27AE60`): Smooth, uniformly active surface. Lustrous deep blue-black oxide result. Just enough time to remove oxide/scale. Minimal dimensional change.

**Right -- OVER-PICKLED** (accent `#E05C5C`): Rough, pitted, etched grain boundaries. Matte, non-lustrous, pitted black result. Too much time or too strong acid. Measurable metal loss.

Bottom: `The goal is ACTIVATION, not excavation. Remove the oxide film; leave the metal intact.` `#E8A020`

---

## Phase 7 -- H-Embrittlement + Safety

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT + SAFETY`.

**Left -- H-EMBRITTLEMENT WARNING** (accent `#E05C5C`): Acid pickling generates hydrogen at steel surface. H atoms diffuse into high-strength steel (> 40 HRC). Bake 375 F for 4+ hr within 4 hr of pickling per ASTM B633 / ASTM F519. Use inhibited acids for hardened steel.

**Right -- ACID PICKLE SAFETY** (accent `#E8A020`): HCl fumes are corrosive -- ventilation mandatory. H2SO4 hot baths generate acid mist. PPE: acid-resistant gloves, face shield, chemical apron, safety glasses. Ventilation: slot exhaust at tank lip. NEVER add water to concentrated acid -- add acid to water.
