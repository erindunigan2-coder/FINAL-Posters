## Phase 5 -- Gas & Air Supply

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `GAS & AIR SUPPLY SPECIFICATIONS`.

**Left -- Fuel & Oxygen (W: 11.0"):** accent `#E05C5C`.

| Gas | Pressure | Flow Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oxygen | 20-40 PSI | 30-60 SCFH | Adjust for neutral or slightly oxidizing flame |
| Acetylene | 10-15 PSI | 15-40 SCFH | NEVER exceed 15 PSI -- decomposition risk |
| Propane (alt) | 10-20 PSI | 20-50 SCFH | Safer; lower flame temp (~2800 degC) |

Safety callout: `ACETYLENE: NEVER EXCEED 15 PSI GAUGE` in JetBrains Mono 16pt `#E05C5C`.

Additional:
- Flashback arrestors on BOTH O2 and fuel lines -- mandatory
- Secure cylinders upright; chain to cart or wall
- Test connections with soapy water -- never open flame
- Purge each line separately before lighting

**Right -- Atomizing Air (W: 11.5"):** accent `#2EC4B6`.

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Pressure | 40-80 PSI |
| Flow rate | 20-40 CFM |
| Quality | Dry, oil-free |
| Source | Portable compressor or plant air |
| Function | Atomizes molten wire tip; propels toward substrate |

Note: `Higher air pressure = finer atomization = denser coating. But also more overspray.`

## Phase 6 -- Gun Anatomy + Wire Feed + OEM Systems

### Gun Components (Y: 22.0"-28.5", left W: 11.0")

Section label: `GUN COMPONENTS & WIRE FEED`.

Title: `GUN ANATOMY` accent `#E8A020`.

| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| Oxy-fuel nozzle | Concentric flame cone; mixes fuel and oxygen |
| Wire guide tube | Centers wire in flame; feeds through nozzle center |
| Compressed air cap | Surrounds flame; atomizes molten wire tip |
| Wire feed drive | Pulls wire from spool through guide tube |
| Trigger / controls | Start/stop wire feed; gas and air valves |

Note: `Self-contained, hand-held. Total weight: 1-3 kg (2-7 lb).`

### Wire Feed Mechanisms (right W: 11.5")

*Air-Turbine Feed* (accent `#27AE60`):
- Air drives turbine powering wire feed rollers
- Self-powered -- no electrical connection
- Speed varies with air pressure (linked)
- Standard on most portable guns

*Electric Motor Feed* (accent `#E8A020`):
- Motor drives wire feed independently of air
- Speed adjustable independently
- Requires electrical power
- More precise; preferred for shop/automated
