## Phase 6 -- Zinc Phosphate vs. Iron Phosphate

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHY ZINC PHOSPHATE? -- ZINC vs. IRON PHOSPHATE`.

**Left -- Zinc Phosphate:** Amber accent. Crystalline (hopeite/phosphophyllite) | 150--1000+ mg/ft2 | 2--10 um crystals | Painted SST 500--1500+ hr | Conditioner REQUIRED | Steel/Al/galv | MIL-DTL-16232, GM 6041M, Ford WSS-M2P188. Bottom: `When the spec says zinc phosphate, there is no substitute.`

**Right -- Iron Phosphate:** Teal accent. Amorphous | 20--80 mg/ft2 | Bare SST 2--24 hr | No conditioner | Steel primarily | TT-C-490. Bottom: `Lower cost, simpler, but less protection. Spec-dependent.`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| COARSE CRYSTALS | Conditioner failure | Check conditioner pH 7.5--9.5; replace |
| LIGHT COATING | Low TA, low temp, short time, high FA | Increase TA; reduce FA; increase time/temp |
| INCOMPLETE COVERAGE | Oil contamination; cleaner failure | Improve cleaning; check substrate |
| EXCESS SLUDGE | High iron carryover; insufficient filtration | Improve filtration; reduce drag-over |
