## Phase 4 -- Cleaning Sequences Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `CLEANING BY MANDREL TYPE`.

Three tall panels:

**Panel 1 -- PERMANENT METAL (`#27AE60`):** SS, Ni, Chrome-Plated.
```
1. Solvent wipe (IPA or acetone) -- fingerprints
2. Alkaline soak clean -- 50-65 C, 5-10 min, ultrasonic if available
3. DI water rinse -- 30-60 sec cascade
4. Apply fresh release agent -- per Poster 471
5. DI rinse (if dip-type release)
6. Transfer to EF tank immediately
```

**Panel 2 -- EXPENDABLE (`#E8A020`):** Al, Zn, Wax, Plastic.
```
1. Solvent wipe if organic residue (skip for fresh-machined)
2. Light alkaline clean -- 30-50 C, 2-5 min, MILD pH only
   CAUTION: Strong alkali dissolves Al
3. DI rinse
4. Acid activation (Al): HNO3 10-25%, ambient, 15-30 sec
5. DI rinse
6. Wax/plastic: verify conductive coating intact
7. Transfer immediately
```

**Panel 3 -- NON-CONDUCTIVE (`#2EC4B6`):** Wax, Glass, Plastic, 3D-Print.
```
1. Verify conductive coating -- check continuity with multimeter
2. Gentle IPA wipe ONLY -- no ultrasonic (may damage coating)
3. No alkaline soak, no acid dip
4. Electroless Ni coating: mild HCl 2-5%, 10 sec, DI rinse
5. Transfer immediately -- clean gloves only
```

**Key Principle Callout (Y: 12.8"):** `Every contaminant on the mandrel surface is trapped at the mandrel-deposit interface. Fingerprints become voids. Dust becomes pits. Oil becomes adhesion failures. The interior surface of the electroform is only as clean as the mandrel.`

## Phase 5 -- Contamination Effects + Activation

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `WHAT CONTAMINATION DOES TO THE ELECTROFORM`.

**Contamination Table:**

| Contaminant | Source | Effect | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fingerprints | Bare-hand handling | Voids; poor interior finish | Lint-free gloves always |
| Dust/particles | Shop air, dirty rags | Embedded particles; pits | Clean environment; package until use |
| Oxide film | Air exposure on Al/steel | Poor nucleation; rough start | Acid activate immediately |
| Old release agent | Buildup from prior cycles | Won't separate or separates unevenly | Fresh release each cycle |
| Organic residue | Machining oil, polish compound | Pitting; gas pocketing | Thorough alkaline clean |
| Water spots | City water minerals | Rough interior spots | DI water final rinse |

**Activation Cards (Y: 19.5"):** Two side-by-side.

Left -- `METAL MANDREL ACTIVATION` (`#27AE60`): SS: no acid after chromate release. Al: HNO3 10-25%, 15-30 sec. DI rinse after all steps.

Right -- `CONDUCTIVE COATING ACTIVATION` (`#E8A020`): Electroless Ni: HCl 2-5%, 10 sec. Silver paint: IPA wipe, avoid acid. Graphite: no activation needed.

## Phase 6 -- Verification + Troubleshooting

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Verification Methods (Y: 22.0" to 26.0"):** Three cards.

| Card | Title | Accent | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water-Break Test | `WATER-BREAK TEST` | `#27AE60` | Spray DI water; should sheet uniformly, no beading. Break = organic contamination. |
| Visual Inspection | `VISUAL INSPECTION` | `#2EC4B6` | Bright light, oblique angle. Fingerprints, dust, scratches, water spots = re-clean. |
| Continuity Check | `CONTINUITY CHECK` | `#E8A020` | Non-conductive mandrels: multimeter across surface. < 10 ohm. High R = patchy coating. |

**Mandrel Cautions (Y: 26.5"):** Two warning cards.

Aluminum: NaOH dissolves Al -- use only mild, low-pH alkaline cleaners.
Wax/Plastic: Avoid acetone on wax/acrylic. No ultrasonic on fragile mandrels.

**Cleaning Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5" to 32.5"):** Four problem cards.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| PITTING ON INTERIOR | Dust/particles on mandrel | Improve cleanliness |
| ROUGH INTERIOR START | Oxide not removed | Acid activate; minimize time to tank |
| ADHESION TO MANDREL | Release agent skipped/degraded | Fresh release every cycle |
| GAS POCKETING | Organic contamination | Thorough alkaline clean + solvent wipe |
