## Phase 6 -- Zinc Fume Fever + Emergency

### Zinc Fume Fever (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `ZINC FUME FEVER -- THE #1 ARC SPRAY HEALTH RISK`. Three columns:

**Col 1 -- SYMPTOMS (`#E05C5C`):** Onset 4--12 hr. Fever, chills, sweating. Muscle aches, headache. Nausea, fatigue, metallic taste. Resembles flu. Resolves 24--48 hr. Repeated exposure does NOT build immunity.

**Col 2 -- PREVENTION (`#27AE60`):** Supplied-air RPE. LEV with adequate CFM. Enclosed booth with downdraft. Monitor: TLV 2 mg/m3 (ACGIH). Rotate operators. Pre-shift hydration.

**Col 3 -- IF SYMPTOMS OCCUR (`#E8A020`):** Remove from exposure. Fresh air. Seek medical attention. Report incident. Do not return until symptom-free. Review RPE and ventilation.

### Emergency Procedures (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `EMERGENCY PROCEDURES`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Emergency | Action |
|---|---|
| ELECTRICAL SHOCK | De-energize immediately; do not touch victim if live; call 911; CPR if needed |
| FIRE | CO2 or dry chemical extinguisher; clear area; call fire dept |
| FUME INHALATION | Fresh air immediately; call 911 if breathing difficulty; medical eval |
| ARC FLASH / BURN | Cool burns with water; eye exposure flush 15 min; seek medical attention |
