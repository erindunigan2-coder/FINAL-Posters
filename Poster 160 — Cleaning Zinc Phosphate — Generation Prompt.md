## Phase 4 -- Cleaning Mechanism Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE ALKALINE CLEANING STAGE`.

Cross-section: steel substrate + heavy soil layers (stamping oil, rust preventative) + alkaline cleaner solution (pH 10--13). Parameters: Free alkalinity Spray 3--8 pts / Immersion 5--12 pts. Total alkalinity Spray 8--20 pts / Immersion 12--30 pts. Two-stage cleaning common on automotive lines.

## Phase 6 -- Two-Stage Cleaning + Warnings

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Two-Stage Cleaning:** Amber accent. Stage A: PRE-CLEAN (gross soils). Stage B: CLEAN (final pass, precise alkalinity). Common on automotive body-in-white lines.

**Right -- Critical Warnings:** Coral accent. Three cards: (1) SILICATE CLEANERS -- poison the Zn phosphate bath. (2) ETCH INHIBITORS -- may leave surface too passive. (3) INCOMPLETE CLEANING -- Zn phosphate amplifies failures.

## Phase 7 -- Monitoring & Defects

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Monitoring:** FA titration every 4 hr | TA titration every 4 hr | Temp continuous | Oil content continuous | Water-break test every load.

**Right -- Defects:**

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Skip areas | Residual oil, wax | Increase conc/temp/time; add pre-clean |
| Phosphate powdery | Over-etched surface | Reduce alkalinity; add etch inhibitor |
| Uneven crystals | Mixed soils, variable cleaning | Improve loading; pre-clean heavy parts |
| Bath contamination | Silicate carry-over | Switch to silicate-free immediately |
