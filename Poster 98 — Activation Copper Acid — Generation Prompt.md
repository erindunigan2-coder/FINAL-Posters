## Phase 5 -- Activation Parameters

Y: 14.0" to 20.5". Section: `ACTIVATION PARAMETERS -- DETAILED`.

| Parameter | H2SO4 Dip | HCl Dip | Microetch (PCB) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concentration | 5--10% v/v | 10--50% v/v (typ. 25--33%) | Na persulfate 100--200 g/L |
| Temperature | Ambient | Ambient | Ambient to 95 F |
| Time | 15--30 sec | 15--60 sec | 30--90 sec |
| Primary substrate | Cu, brass, after-strike steel | Steel (before strike) | PCBs only |
| Purpose | Remove light oxide | Remove heavier oxide/scale | Create micro-roughness |
| Key control | Do not over-etch copper | Minimize time on high-strength steel | Etch depth 0.5--1.0 um |

## Phase 6 -- Immersion Deposit Warning + Problems

Y: 20.5" to 27.0".

### Immersion Deposit Warning (coral border, full-width)

Title: `WARNING: IMMERSION DEPOSITION` -- 24 pt `#E05C5C`.

When a more active metal (steel, zinc) contacts acid copper without current, copper spontaneously deposits by displacement. This "immersion deposit" is red, powdery, and has ZERO adhesion. It peels on the slightest stress.

This is why steel and zinc die castings MUST have a copper strike before acid copper.

Key: `No strike on steel or zinc = guaranteed peel failure. No exceptions.` -- 16 pt `#E05C5C`.

### Problem Table

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Red powdery deposit on steel | No strike -- immersion deposit | Apply strike first |
| Over-etched copper | H2SO4 too strong or too long | Reduce to 5%; limit 15 sec |
| Staining after activation | Acid residue drying | Rinse immediately |
| Pitting in copper plate | Contaminated acid dip | Replace acid; filter |

## Phase 7 -- Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Acid Safety:** H2SO4 corrosive -- always add acid to water | HCl fumes -- ventilation | Persulfate oxidizer | H2O2/H2SO4 exothermic.

**Right -- PPE:** Goggles, acid-resistant gloves (butyl/neoprene), apron, eyewash + shower within 10 sec, local exhaust over HCl tanks.
