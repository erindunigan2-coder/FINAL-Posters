## Phase 5 -- Thickness + Hardness

Y: 15.0" to 20.0".

**Left -- Thickness Measurement:**
Eddy Current (ND, `#27AE60`): Standard production method. Accuracy +/- 5%. Calibrate with foil standards on same alloy.
Cross-Section (destructive, `#E8A020`): Gold standard. Cut, mount, polish, measure under microscope. Used for qualification/dispute.

**Right -- Hardness Testing:**
`MIL-A-8625F MINIMUM HARDNESS`:

| Alloy Series | Minimum | Rockwell C |
|---|---|---|
| 2xxx (Al-Cu) | ~550 HV | Rc 60 min |
| 7xxx (Al-Zn) | ~600 HV | Rc 65 min |
| All other alloys | ~650 HV | Rc 70 min |

Method: Vickers/Knoop, 25--100 gf, measure at mid-thickness.

## Phase 6 -- Seal Quality + Dimensional Verification

Y: 20.0" to 25.0".

**Left -- Dye Spot Test (ASTM B680):**
1. Apply dye solution (5 min). 2. Rinse and blot. 3. Compare stain.
PASS: No stain `#27AE60`. FAIL: Dark stain `#E05C5C`.
Note: PTFE-sealed parts -- dye spot test not applicable.

**Right -- Dimensional Verification:**
```
Hard coat builds ~50% outward, ~50% inward.
2.0 mil coating: +1.0 mil outward per surface.
Shaft 1.000": final 1.002"
Bore 1.000": final 0.998"
```
Always verify with actual measurement.
