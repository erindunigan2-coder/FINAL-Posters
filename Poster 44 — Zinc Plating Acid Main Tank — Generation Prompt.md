## Phase 5 -- Bath Chemistry + pH Control

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY -- THE FOUR ESSENTIALS`.

### Step 6 -- Four-component breakdown (Y: 15.3" to 18.3")

Four side-by-side callout boxes (H: 2.8", fill `#1E2435`):

| Component | X | W | Accent | Title | Concentration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zinc Chloride | 0.5" | 5.5" | `#27AE60` | ZINC METAL | 25--35 g/L |
| Potassium Chloride | 6.25" | 5.5" | `#2EC4B6` | CONDUCTIVITY | 180--200 g/L |
| Boric Acid | 12.0" | 5.5" | `#E8A020` | pH BUFFER | 25--30 g/L |
| Brightener | 17.75" | 5.75" | `#C8D0D8` | BRIGHTENER | Per supplier TDS |

Each box includes: concentration, source, role, low/high effect.

*Zinc Chloride:* Source ZnCl2. Role: Zn2+ ions. Low = dull/poor LCD. High = burning at HCD.
*Potassium Chloride:* Source KCl (replaces NH4Cl). Role: conductivity, anode dissolution. Low = high voltage. High = minimal issue.
*Boric Acid:* Source H3BO3 (dissolved hot). Role: buffers cathode film pH. Low = pH excursion, dark LCD. Precipitated = rough/gritty.
*Brightener:* 3-component system: carrier + brightener + wetter. Carrier = grain refinement. Brightener = reflectivity. Wetter = pit prevention.

### Step 7 -- pH Control Gauge (Y: 18.6" to 20.3")

Full-width rounded rect, H: 1.5", fill `#1E2435`.
Title: `THE CRITICAL PARAMETER: pH` / `pH Controls Everything in Acid Zinc` `#E8A020`.

- Red zone left: `< 4.8` -- boric acid precipitation, dark LCD, rough
- Green zone center: `4.8--5.8` (narrow optimal `5.0--5.4` brighter band)
- Red zone right: `> 5.8` -- zinc hydroxide haze, reduced brightness

## Phase 7 -- Hull Cell + Contamination

Y: 26.5" to 32.5".

**Left -- Hull Cell Strip (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Section: `THE HULL CELL`. Test: `267 mL | 2 A | 10 min | 75 F`.

5-segment panel strip:
- HCD edge: `#E8A020` at 40% -- `Burned = low zinc or high temp`
- Upper mid: `#27AE60` at 50% -- `Bright = good`
- Center: `#27AE60` at 70% -- `OPTIMAL -- bright + level = balanced brightener`
- Lower mid: `#27AE60` at 40% -- `Semi-bright = acceptable`
- LCD edge: `#3A4055` at 60% -- `Dull/dark = iron contamination or low brightener`

Good panel note: bright across 70--80% of width. LCD dullness beyond 5 ASF equivalent is normal.
KCl vs NH4Cl note: NH4Cl brighter at LCD but creates ammonia wastewater (POTW < 10 mg/L).

**Right -- Contamination Thresholds (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
Section: `CONTAMINATION THRESHOLDS`.

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Iron | > 50 ppm | Dull, dark deposit (especially LCD) |
| Copper | > 5 ppm | Dark LCD, black spots, immersion deposit |
| Lead | > 2 ppm | Dark LCD, brittle deposit |
| Chromium | > 1 ppm | Dull, poor coverage at all CDs |
| Organic (oil) | Visible | Pitting, haze, poor ductility |
| Carbonate | N/A (acid bath) | Not a concern -- acid pH dissolves carbonates |

Iron treatment: `Add 0.5--1 mL/L H2O2 (30%), raise pH to 5.5 briefly, filter Fe(OH)3, readjust pH.` `#2EC4B6`
