## Phase 6 -- Emergency Procedures

Y: 23.5" to 32.5". Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE` in `#E05C5C`.

Three tall panels side by side:

**Molten Salt Burn:** 1. REMOVE victim. 2. Do NOT remove adhered salt (tears tissue). 3. Flood with water 20+ min. 4. Call 911 immediately. 5. Remove clothing only if not stuck. 6. Sterile dressing. 7. Treat for shock.

**Steam Explosion:** 1. EVACUATE immediately. 2. Do NOT approach bath. 3. Call 911 if injured. 4. Wait for eruption to subside. 5. Secure area (molten salt on floor/walls). 6. Account for all personnel. 7. Investigate moisture source. 8. NEVER resume until root cause fixed.

**Fire in Salt Bath:** CRITICAL: NEVER USE WATER. Water on salt bath fire = steam explosion + fire spread. CORRECT: 1. Dry chemical (Class D). 2. Dry sand. 3. Salt bath lid/cover. 4. Evacuate if uncontrolled. 5. Inform fire dept of molten salt / cyanate hazards.
