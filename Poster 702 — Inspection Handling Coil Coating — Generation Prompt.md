## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| MEK RUB FAILURE (< 100) | Undercure -- PMT too low / speed too fast | Check PMT; reduce speed or increase oven temp |
| T-BEND CRACKING | Overcure or wrong chemistry | Verify PMT history; higher-flex chemistry |
| COIL SWEATING | Cold coil in warm/humid environment | Acclimate before unwrapping; monitor dew point |
| SCRATCH ON SURFACE | Handling damage | Paper interleave; polished forming tools |

Key insight: `50,000+ sq ft per hour. Every defect that passes inspection multiplies at that rate.`
