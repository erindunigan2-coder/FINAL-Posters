## Phase 4 -- Blast Parameters + HVOF vs. APS Comparison (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.0". Section label: `BLAST SPECIFICATION FOR HVOF` centered, 28 pt.

**Left -- Blast Parameter Table (W: 11.0"):**

| Parameter | Typical Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Media | White alumina (Al2O3); 99%+ purity for aerospace | No ferrous contamination |
| Grit size | 36-60 mesh | Finer than APS (24-36 mesh) |
| Blast pressure | 40-60 PSI (275-415 kPa) | Lower than APS (40-80 PSI) |
| Nozzle distance | 100-150 mm | Controlled distance for uniformity |
| Blast angle | 75-90 degrees to surface | 90 deg for max depth |
| Anchor profile (Ra) | 3-6 microns (125-250 microinches) | Moderate profile sufficient |
| Cleanliness | SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal) or SP 10 (Near-White) | Most HVOF specs require SP-5 |

**Right -- HVOF vs. APS Profile Comparison (W: 11.5", accent `#E8A020`):**

Title: `WHY HVOF NEEDS LESS AGGRESSIVE PREP`.

| Process | Particle Velocity | Profile (Ra) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| APS | 200-600 m/s | 3-8 um | Lower velocity -- needs rougher profile for mechanical interlock |
| HVOF | 600-900 m/s | 3-6 um | Supersonic impact provides excellent bonding even on moderate profile |

Key insight: `HVOF's high kinetic energy means particles "hammer" into the surface. The impact energy alone creates superior mechanical interlocking. Over-blasting can damage thin substrates without improving bond strength.`

Note: `Alumina from blasting gets incorporated into the first coating layer -- this is generally acceptable and does not compromise HVOF coating integrity.`

## Phase 5 -- Profile Verification + Chrome Replacement + Standards

### Profile Verification (Y: 14.0"-20.5")

Section label: `HOW TO VERIFY YOUR PROFILE` centered, 28 pt.

Three side-by-side cards, top accent 4pt `#2EC4B6`:

| Method | Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Testex Replica Tape | Press-o-film tape pressed into surface, measured with micrometer. Reads peak-to-valley directly. Field standard. | Field verification; quick QC checks |
| Surface Profilometer | Stylus traces surface; outputs Ra, Rz values digitally. Most precise method. | Lab verification; specification compliance |
| Visual Comparator | SSPC-VIS 1 standard photos. Compare blasted surface to reference images. Qualitative but fast. | First-pass field assessment |

### Chrome Replacement + Cleanliness Standards (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

**Left -- Chrome Replacement Dimensional Prep (W: 11.0", accent `#E8A020`):**
Title: `CHROME REPLACEMENT: GRIND BACK BEFORE BLAST`. Steps: strip all chrome; grind substrate to dimensional tolerance BEFORE blasting; verify no residual chrome; grit blast to SP-5; HVOF spray 200-400 um over-dimension; final grind to drawing dimension.

**Right -- SSPC Cleanliness Standards (W: 11.5", accent `#2EC4B6`):**
Title: `CLEANLINESS STANDARDS`.

| SSPC | ISO 8501 | NACE | Description |

## Phase 6 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section label: `GRIT BLAST PROBLEMS -- QUICK FIXES` centered, 24 pt.

Four cards in a row, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| EMBEDDED GRIT | Excessive pressure or too close | Reduce pressure to 40-60 PSI; increase distance |
| INSUFFICIENT PROFILE | Worn media or pressure too low | Replace media; verify blast pressure |
| SUBSTRATE DAMAGE | Over-blasting thin walls or edges | Reduce pressure; use finer grit; mask edges |
| RESIDUAL CHROME | Blasting over unstripped chrome | Stop -- return to stripping; never blast over chrome |
