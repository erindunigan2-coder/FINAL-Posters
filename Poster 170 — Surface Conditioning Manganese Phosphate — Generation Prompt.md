## Phase 4 -- Acid Pickle Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE ACID PICKLE`.

Tank cross-section with parts submerged showing rough-to-smooth surface transition. Fume indicators above tank. Parameters: HCl 10--25% or H2SO4 10--20% | Ambient--150 F | 2--15 min | Visual endpoint: clean, uniformly gray metal.

Bottom: `Pickle until the surface is uniformly clean. Under-pickling leaves scale. Over-pickling wastes metal and risks hydrogen embrittlement on high-strength steel.`

---

## Phase 5 -- Acid Selection

Y: 14.5" to 20.0". Section: `ACID SELECTION -- HCl VS. H2SO4`.

**Left -- HCl:** Amber accent. 10--25% | Ambient | Fast, aggressive | Excellent on mill scale | Minimal smut | Strong fumes. Verdict: `Preferred for most MnPhos work.`

**Right -- H2SO4:** Teal accent. 10--20% | 120--150 F for best results | Slower at ambient | Can leave carbon smut on high-C steel | Less fuming. Verdict: `Used when HCl fume control is a problem.`

---

## Phase 6 -- Hardened Steel + Conditioning

Y: 20.0" to 25.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Hardened Steel (>40 HRC):** Coral accent. Resists acid attack | Slower coating, lighter weight | Extended pickle or proprietary activator | Dilute HCl activation dip (5--10%, 1--3 min) | CAUTION: H-embrittlement risk -- bake within 4 hr at 375 F if > 39 HRC.

**Right -- Conditioning Options:** Amber accent. Three approaches: (1) No Conditioner (most common -- acid pickle IS the surface prep). (2) Mn-Based Pre-Dip (5--10% bath concentration, ambient, 1--2 min). (3) Ti/Zr Conditioner (same products as zinc phosphate).

## Phase 7 -- Defects + Key Rules

Y: 25.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- 4 Defects:** Under-pickled, over-pickled, smut on surface, flash rust.

**Right -- 6 Rules:** Pickle ONLY if rust/scale present | Visual endpoint | MUST rinse after pickle | Hardened steel needs activator | H-embrittlement bake for high-strength | Don't let parts dry.
