## Phase 4 -- Six Cure Methods Hero

Y: 5.0" to 15.0". Section label: `SIX CURE METHODS -- THE WIDEST RANGE IN COATING`. 3x2 grid.

**Air Dry (`#27AE60`):** 65--85 F / 1--7 days. Alkyds, latex, moisture-cure PU. No oven required.

**Force Dry (`#E8A020`):** 140--180 F / 30--60 min. Alkyds, modified acrylics. Supplements natural cure.

**Low-Temp Bake (`#2EC4B6`):** 200--250 F / 20--30 min. Modified alkyds, polyesters. True bake cure.

**High-Temp Bake (`#E8A020`):** 250--350 F / 15--30 min. Thermoset acrylics, baking enamels. Hardest films.

**2K Chemical Cure (`#27AE60`):** Ambient--140 F / Pot-life dependent. 2K epoxy, 2K urethane. Warning: `Mixed material must be used within pot life or discarded`

**UV Cure (`#2EC4B6`):** Ambient / 1--10 sec. UV acrylate. Fastest cure. Limitation: Line-of-sight only.

## Phase 5 -- Pot Life Management

Y: 16.3" to 21.3". Section label: `POT LIFE -- THE 2K CLOCK IS TICKING`.

**Left -- Pot Life Table:**

| System | Pot Life at 77 F | Temperature Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2K Epoxy (polyamide) | 2--4 hours | Halves per 18 F rise |
| 2K Epoxy (amine-adduct) | 30 min--2 hours | Very temperature-sensitive |
| 2K Urethane (aliphatic) | 2--8 hours | Slower reaction |
| 2K Urethane (aromatic) | 15 min--2 hours | Fast reaction |

**Right -- `POT LIFE RULES -- NO EXCEPTIONS` (border `#E05C5C`):**
5 rules: Clock starts at mix, not spray. Exceeded pot life = WASTE. Gelled material clogs equipment. Hot weather = smaller batches. Never pour unused back into fresh.
