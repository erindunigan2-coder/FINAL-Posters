## Phase 5 -- Acid/Cyanide Safety

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THE ACID/CYANIDE HAZARD`.

Full-width Coral panel (border 2pt `#E05C5C`, fill `#E05C5C` at 12%).

**Left -- THE REACTION:** `NaCN + HCl --> NaCl + HCN (gas)` / `KCN + H2SO4 --> K2SO4 + HCN (gas)` / `HCN lethal at 300 ppm. Immediate and violent.`

**Right -- YOUR DEFENSE:** 1. Thorough rinse between acid and cyanide / 2. Monitor rinse pH (must be >5) / 3. Never skip or rush / 4. Maintain overflow flow rate / 5. HCN monitors at line (OSHA requirement) / 6. Emergency shower within 10 sec walk.

---

## Phase 6 -- Rinse Verification

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `HOW TO VERIFY THE RINSE`.

**Left -- pH Paper Test (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Touch to part surface. >pH 5: PASS. <pH 5: FAIL, re-rinse. `Cost: pennies. Risk avoided: lethal.`

**Right -- Conductivity Monitoring (accent `#E8A020`):** Install meter on overflow. Rising = acid accumulation. Alarm set point per SOP. Automatic fresh water preferred.

## Phase 7 -- Common Errors

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `TIMING AND ERRORS`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | SURFACE RE-OXIDATION | Long delay | Move within 1--2 min |
| 3 | RINSE WATER CONTAMINATION | No overflow; stagnant | Maintain continuous flow |
