# Poster #326 -- Construction Workup
## Seal / Post Treatment -- Bright Anodizing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stages 7--8 of 8 (Dye + Seal combined into one poster). This poster covers the final steps: optional dyeing and sealing. The unique challenge for bright anodize: hot water seal can slightly cloud the mirror finish because boehmite formation scatters light. Nickel acetate seal at a lower temperature, or cold nickel fluoride seal, may preserve more reflectivity. Dyeing over bright-dipped surfaces produces brilliant jewel-tone colors.

### ZONE 4 -- Seal Methods Comparison

**Section label:** `SEAL METHOD COMPARISON -- IMPACT ON REFLECTIVITY` -- Y: 14.7".

**Full-width comparison table (Y: 15.3" to 20.3"):**

| Seal Method | Temp | Time | Corrosion (B117) | Reflectivity Impact | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nickel acetate | 180--200 F | 20--30 min | 750--1500+ hr | MINIMAL cloudiness | PREFERRED for bright work |
| Cold seal (NiF2) | 75--85 F | 5--15 min | 336--750 hr | MINIMAL cloudiness | High-volume bright lines |
| Hot DI water | 200--212 F | 20--30 min | 336--750 hr | SLIGHT cloudiness | Acceptable for non-premium |
| Dichromate | 190--210 F | 15--20 min | 1000--1500+ hr | YELLOWS clear coat | NOT recommended for bright |

"PREFERRED" in `#27AE60`. "NOT recommended" in `#E05C5C`. "SLIGHT" in `#E8A020`.

| Position | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | HAZE AFTER SEAL | `#E8A020` | Hot water seal on bright finish | Switch to NiAc or cold seal |
