## Phase 5 -- Sealing Guidance

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `SEALING -- WHEN YOU DO AND DON'T NEED IT`.

### Left -- Sealing Decision (W: 11.0")

**Not Required (Standard)** -- emerald accent: Porosity <0.5% (often <0.2%). No interconnected porosity. Continuous barrier as-sprayed. No heat treatment. Standard for D-Gun WC-Co.

**May Be Specified (Rare)** -- amber accent: Salt spray >500 hrs (B117). Immersion in corrosive fluids. Customer spec mandates. Epoxy/phenolic impregnation. Apply AFTER grinding, BEFORE inspection.

### Right -- D-Gun vs. HVOF Post-Treatment (W: 11.5")

Teal-accented glass card. 6-row comparison:

| Step | D-Gun | HVOF |
|---|---|---|
| Grinding | Diamond or CBN | Diamond or CBN |
| Achievable Ra | 0.05--0.4 um | 0.1--0.4 um |
| Sealing? | Rarely | Rarely |
| Heat treatment? | No | No |
| Stock removal | 50--200 um | 50--200 um |
| Superfinishing? | Yes (lapping) | Yes (lapping) |

`Post-treatment workflows are virtually identical. The difference is in as-sprayed quality -- D-Gun starts denser, harder, smoother.`

## Phase 6 -- Grinding Warnings + Procedure

### Grinding Warnings (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `GRINDING WARNINGS -- PROTECT THE COATING`. 2x2 grid, coral accent:

| Warning | Detail |
|---|---|
| NEVER DRY GRIND | Thermal shock = micro-cracking, delamination, carbide decomposition. Always flood coolant. |
| LIGHT PASSES ONLY | >25 um/pass pulls WC grains from binder. Surface pitting and sub-surface damage. Stay 5--15 um. |
| CHECK WHEEL CONDITION | Glazed/loaded wheels = excess heat + poor finish. Dress regularly. Monitor vibration. |
| VERIFY GRINDING ALLOWANCE | Spray thickness must include stock. At-minimum coating grinds below spec. Plan ahead. |

### Post-Treatment Sequence (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `POST-TREATMENT SEQUENCE`. Four cards in a row with arrows:

| Step | Accent | Title | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#E8A020` | VISUAL INSPECT AS-SPRAYED | Verify coverage/thickness before committing to grinding |
| 2 | `#27AE60` | DIAMOND GRIND TO DIMENSION | Flood coolant; 5--15 um/pass; grind to drawing |
| 3 | `#2EC4B6` | SEAL (IF SPECIFIED) | Epoxy/phenolic after grinding; cure before inspection |
| 4 | `#E8A020` | MEASURE AND DOCUMENT | Final thickness, Ra, visual. Record for QA package. |
