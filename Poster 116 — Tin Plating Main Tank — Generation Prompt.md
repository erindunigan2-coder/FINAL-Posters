## Phase 7 -- Hull Cell + Contamination

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column layout.

**Left -- Hull Cell (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):** Test conditions: `267 mL | 1--2 A | 5--10 min | 70 F`. 5-segment strip: HCD burned/rough | Upper mid smooth | Center optimal | Lower mid semi-bright | LCD dull. Note: `Good panel: uniform matte-to-bright across 50--70% width`.

**Right -- Contamination Thresholds (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Iron | > 20 ppm | Dark deposit, catalyzes Sn2+ oxidation |
| Copper | > 5 ppm | Immersion deposit, dark spots |
| Lead | > 2 ppm | Dark LCD, stress |
| Chloride | > 10 ppm (sulfate) | Pitting, anode attack |
| Organic (oil) | visible | Pitting, dull deposit |
| Sn4+ (stannic) | > 10% of total Sn | Sludge, rough deposit |
