## Series Design Framework

**Series 1 (Summary Layer):** Each poster covers a broad concept, process overview, or cross-process reference topic. Audience: any shop, any process. These are the "30,000-foot view" posters.

**Series 2–10 (Granular Layer):** Each process is broken into individual stage-posters (Process Flow, Cleaning, Rinse, Activation, Main Tank, Post Treatment, etc.). Audience: specific shops running that exact process. A zinc plating shop buys the entire Zinc Alkaline cluster (8 posters) for their line walls.

**Cluster convention:** Each process = one "cluster" of 7–10 posters. The Process Flow poster (#X1 of each cluster) is the summary for that specific process — the remaining posters cover individual stages in depth.
