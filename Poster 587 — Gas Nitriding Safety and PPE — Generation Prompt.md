| Standard | Limit Type | Value | Action Required |

| NIOSH REL | TWA (10 hr) | 25 ppm | Recommended (more conservative) |

## Phase 6 -- Emergency Response

Y: 25.5"--32.5". Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROCEDURES` in `#E05C5C`.

**Left -- AMMONIA RELEASE (`#E05C5C`):** 7 numbered steps: evacuate; approach upwind; water FOG (not solid stream); do NOT re-enter without SCBA; NH3 rises; ventilate from TOP; call hazmat. Note: `NH3 at 300 ppm is IDLH -- do NOT attempt rescue without full SCBA and buddy system.`

**Right -- HYDROGEN ACCUMULATION (`#E8A020`):** 7 steps: if pilot fails shut off NH3; purge with N2; ventilate; do NOT relight until < 1% H2; inspect pilot. Note: `H2 flame is nearly INVISIBLE in daylight -- use detector or paper test.`
