**Right -- Why Tighter Specs?** Coral accent. Fe phosphate failure = uneven coating. Zn phosphate failure = dead conditioner + coarse crystals.

## Phase 6 -- Downstream Impact + Counter-Flow

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Failure Cascade:** Coral accent. 5-step chain: Alkaline drag-over -> conditioner pH rises -> Ti colloid dies -> fewer nucleation sites -> COARSE CRYSTALS (50--100+ um).

**Right -- Counter-Flow Design:** Emerald accent. Fresh water enters LAST tank, overflows backward. 50--70% water savings. Standard on automotive OEM lines.
