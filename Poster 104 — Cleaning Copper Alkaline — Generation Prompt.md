## Phase 5 -- Parameter Tables

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `CLEANING PARAMETERS -- DETAILED`.

**Left -- Soak Clean:** 4--8 oz/gal | 140--180 F | 3--10 min (zinc DC may need 5--15) | pH 11--13 | Non-silicated preferred.

**Right -- Electroclean:** Steel: cathodic then anodic | Zinc DC: CATHODIC ONLY (5--10 ASF) | Steel: 20--50 ASF | 140--180 F | 30--60 sec. `Never anodic on zinc die castings -- ever.`

## Phase 6 -- Substrate Notes + Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `SUBSTRATE-SPECIFIC CLEANING CHALLENGES`.

Three cards:

| Substrate | Accent | Key Challenge |
|---|---|---|
| ZINC DIE CAST | `#E8A020` | Porosity traps oil. Ultrasonic recommended. Cathodic EC only. Do NOT over-clean. |
| STEEL | `#2EC4B6` | Mill scale, rust preventatives. Standard soak + anodic EC works. |
| BRASS | `#27AE60` | Light oils and tarnish. Moderate cleaning. Non-silicated cleaner. |

Problem table: Water break -> poor adhesion | Zinc dissolution -> pitting | Embedded metals -> rough Cu | Cleaner film -> skip plating.
