# Poster #267 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Electroless Cobalt -- Pre-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 4 of 8. The pre-plate rinse removes activation chemistry (acid, Pd salts, HF residues) before parts enter the electroless cobalt bath. This is the most critical rinse in the line -- contamination drag-in directly affects bath chemistry and stability. Short transfer time is essential to prevent surface re-oxidation.

Hero visual: rinse tank with timing emphasis -- stopwatch graphic and transfer arrow showing the urgency of minimizing exposure time between activation and cobalt bath.

### ZONE 4 -- Contamination Drag-In Table

**Section label:** `WHAT DRAG-IN DOES TO YOUR COBALT BATH` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Contamination Table (Y: 15.3" to 20.3")**

| Contaminant Source | Drag-In Chemistry | Effect on Cobalt Bath | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| HCl activation | Chloride (Cl-) | Accelerates bath aging; can cause pitting | Moderate |
| HF activation (Si wafers) | Fluoride (F-) | Attacks glass tank lining; destabilizes complexant | High |
| Sn/Pd colloidal | Excess Sn2+ | Can reduce bath stability; acts as nucleation site | High |
| Chromic acid etch residue | Cr6+ | Poisons stabilizer system -- even trace amounts | Critical |
| Pd activation residue | Pd2+ ions | Can trigger uncontrolled deposition at ppm levels | Moderate |

Header row: `#3A4055`, H: 0.5". Data rows: alternating `#1E2435` / `#252B3D`.
Severity column: color-coded -- Moderate `#E8A020`, High `#E05C5C`, Critical `#E05C5C` bold.

### ZONE 6 -- Rinse Parameters + Tips

**Section label:** `RINSE SPECIFICATIONS + BEST PRACTICES` -- Y: 26.7".

**Two-column layout (Y: 27.3" to 32.3"):**

**Left -- Parameters (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Water quality | DI preferred (especially after HF or Sn/Pd activation) |
| Temperature | Ambient |
| Time | 30--60 seconds (balance cleanliness vs. transfer speed) |
| Conductivity target | <20 uS/cm for critical work |
| Agitation | Gentle overflow; avoid vigorous agitation |

**Right -- Best Practices (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

| Practice | Why |
|---|---|
| Dedicated rinse tank (not shared) | Prevents cross-contamination from other process lines |
| Frequent water changes | Rinse tank accumulates activation chemistry over a shift |
| Position tank adjacent to Co bath | Physical proximity minimizes transfer time |
| Consider spray rinse for rack work | Faster than immersion; reduces total transfer time |

## Design Notes

The pre-plate rinse is the most critical rinse in the electroless cobalt line. The timing emphasis (stopwatch graphic, transfer window timeline) is the unique visual hook. Watson notes that cobalt baths are less stable than EN baths, making drag-in contamination even more consequential. The contamination table is specific to cobalt -- HF residues from silicon wafer activation are a concern unique to this process cluster.
