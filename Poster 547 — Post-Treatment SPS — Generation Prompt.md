## Phase 4 -- "DO NOT" Hero for TBCs

Y: 3.8" to 15.3". Section label: `TBC APPLICATIONS -- PRESERVE THE COLUMNAR STRUCTURE`.

**Three large "DO NOT" cards (W: 7.33", H: 7.5", accent `#E05C5C`):**

| Card | Title | Body |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | DO NOT GRIND | Removes column tips; destroys columnar architecture. Strain tolerance comes from independent columns -- flatten them and you have an expensive lamellar coating. |
| 2 | DO NOT SEAL | SPS TBC porosity is FUNCTIONAL. Inter-columnar gaps reduce thermal conductivity (0.7-1.2 W/mK). Sealing increases heat transfer -- defeats the purpose. |
| 3 | DO NOT QUENCH | Rapid cooling induces thermal shock in columnar structure. Allow controlled cool-down in ambient air. |

Title: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 28pt `#E05C5C`.

**As-Sprayed Functional Strip (Y: 12.0"-15.3", fill `#252B3D`):**

Title: `THE AS-SPRAYED STATE IS THE PRODUCT` `#27AE60`.

Three metrics: Porosity 10-25% (reduces conductivity) | Column structure 50-200 um (independent flex) | Surface finish as-sprayed Ra 5-15 um (acceptable for TBC).

`Unlike conventional coatings, SPS TBCs do not need finishing. The microstructure you sprayed is the microstructure you want.` `#27AE60`.

## Phase 5 -- Bond Coat Heat Treatment

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `BOND COAT DIFFUSION HEAT TREATMENT`.

**Left -- Treatment Details (W: 11.0", accent `#E8A020`):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Temperature | 1050-1080 degC |
| Atmosphere | Vacuum or inert (argon) |
| Duration | 2-4 hours |
| Purpose | Interdiffusion; improves adhesion + oxidation resistance |
| Timing | BEFORE SPS topcoat |

Note: `Bond coat treatment, not topcoat. Done after APS bond coat, before SPS topcoat.` `#E8A020`.

**Right -- Process Sequence (W: 11.5"):**

5-step vertical flow with arrows:

1. APS Bond Coat (MCrAlY) -- `#E8A020`
2. Diffusion Heat Treatment (vacuum, 1050-1080 degC, 2-4 hr) -- `#E8A020`
3. Cool to Room Temperature -- `#2EC4B6`
4. SPS Topcoat (YSZ) -- `#27AE60`
5. Controlled Cool-Down -- DONE -- `#2EC4B6`

Critical: `Do NOT apply SPS before heat treatment` `#E05C5C` between steps 2-3.

## Phase 6 -- Non-TBC Exceptions + Decision

### Wear Coatings (Left, Y: 22.0"-28.5", accent `#2EC4B6`)

- Light polishing for dimensional control
- Avoid aggressive grinding -- finer microstructure is sensitive
- Diamond lapping preferred over wheel grinding
- Epoxy sealing acceptable for non-thermal apps
- Less common -- SPS is primarily a TBC process

### Biomedical Coatings (Right, accent `#2EC4B6`)

- Hydroxyapatite (HA) on orthopedic implants
- Biocompatible polymer sealing may be specified
- Surface roughness controlled for cell adhesion
- Porosity may be intentional (bone ingrowth) or minimized
- FDA/ISO 13485 requirements apply

### Decision Flowchart (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `POST-TREATMENT DECISION`.

Three-box horizontal flow:

`IS IT A TBC?` (`#3A4055`) -> YES (`#27AE60`): `NO POST-TREATMENT. Controlled cool-down only. Leave columnar structure intact.` | NO (`#E8A020`): `Light polish or seal per specification. Consult application requirements.`
