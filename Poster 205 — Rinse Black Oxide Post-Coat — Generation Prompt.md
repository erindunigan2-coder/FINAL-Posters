## Phase 4 -- Two-Tank Rinse Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `TWO-STAGE RINSE -- COLD THEN HOT`.

Two tanks side by side with arrow between:

## Phase 5 -- Thermal Shock + Ventilation

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THERMAL SHOCK AND VENTILATION`.

**Left -- Thermal Shock** (accent `#E8A020`): Parts at ~285 F into ambient cold water. Temperature drop ~220 F in seconds. Effects: immediate steam, potential warping on thin-wall parts, cracking risk on brittle/cast iron. For critical parts: gradual cooling or extend hot rinse time.

**Right -- Ventilation** (accent `#E05C5C`): Cold rinse generates visible steam cloud carrying traces of caustic mist. Required: exhaust ventilation at tank lip. PPE: face shield, chemical gloves, chemical apron. Lower parts slowly -- do not drop.

---

## Phase 6 -- Optional Chromate Dip

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `OPTIONAL: CHROMATE DIP IN HOT RINSE`.

**Left -- WHY ADD CHROMATE?** (accent `#27AE60`): 0.5--2 oz/gal CrO3. 2--5x corrosion improvement. Passivates exposed iron in coating imperfections. When it makes sense: military/defense specs, extended storage, humid environments.

**Right -- THE Cr(VI) PROBLEM** (accent `#E05C5C`): Hexavalent chromium is a known human carcinogen. OSHA PEL: 5 ug/m3. EPA reporting. EU REACH authorization. RoHS non-compliance. Hazardous waste disposal. Most modern shops are eliminating Cr(VI). ALTERNATIVE: Oil seal alone provides adequate protection for most applications.

---

## Phase 7 -- Transition to Oil Seal

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `PREPARING FOR THE OIL SEAL`.

Title: `WHY THE HOT RINSE MATTERS FOR OIL PENETRATION`.

**Left:** Magnetite is microporous -- millions of pores must be filled with oil. Hot part (180--200 F) + hot oil (150--180 F) = best penetration. Oil displaces water from pores. This filled-pore structure IS the corrosion barrier.

**Right:** If parts cool before oil dip: water trapped in pores, oil sits on surface without penetrating, corrosion starts from within. RULE: Parts go from hot rinse DIRECTLY into oil with NO delay. Do not air-dry between rinse and oil.
