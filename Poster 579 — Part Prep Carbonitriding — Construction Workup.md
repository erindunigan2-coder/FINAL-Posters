# Poster #579 -- Construction Workup
## Part Prep -- Carbonitriding

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Part preparation is the "foundation" poster for carbonitriding. A contaminated surface will not absorb carbon or nitrogen uniformly -- oil residue creates soft spots, scale blocks diffusion, and inadequate masking leads to unwanted case hardening on threads or bearing seats. This poster covers the full prep sequence: cleaning, surface inspection, masking (copper stop-off or paint), and pre-load verification.

### ZONE 3 -- Masking Methods

**Section label:** `MASKING -- TWO METHODS, ONE GOAL: BLOCK THE CASE` -- Y: 12.2". Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 28 pt, `#F0EDE8`.

**BLOCK D -- Side-by-Side Comparison**

Y: 12.9" to 18.8". Two callout boxes.

**Left -- Copper Electroplate Stop-Off (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 5.7", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#27AE60` 0.06"
- Title: `COPPER ELECTROPLATE STOP-OFF` -- Barlow SemiBold, 18 pt, `#27AE60`
- Content:
  - `Thickness: 0.001--0.002 in minimum`
  - `Method: Cyanide copper strike + acid copper plate`
  - `Effective barrier: carbon and nitrogen cannot penetrate`
  - `Standard per AMS 2759/7`
  - `Requires plating setup -- adds process time`
  - `Strip after heat treat (cyanide strip or mechanical)`
- Highlight: `The gold standard -- most reliable stop-off for production` -- `#27AE60`

**Right -- Commercial Stop-Off Paint (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 5.7", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020` 0.06"
- Title: `COMMERCIAL STOP-OFF PAINT` -- Barlow SemiBold, 18 pt, `#E8A020`
- Content:
  - `Applied by brush, spray, or dip`
  - `Multiple coats may be required`
  - `Faster application than copper plate`
  - `Less reliable on deep cases or long cycles`
  - `Must be fully cured before furnace entry`
  - `Residue removal after heat treat`
- Highlight: `Convenient for short runs -- verify coverage on first article` -- `#E8A020`

### ZONE 4 -- Surface Condition Checklist

**Section label:** `SURFACE CONDITION -- ACCEPT / REJECT` -- Y: 19.2". Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 28 pt, `#F0EDE8`.

**BLOCK E -- Checklist**

Y: 19.8" to 25.8". Two columns: Accept (left, Emerald) and Reject (right, Coral).

**Accept column (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 5.8", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#27AE60` 0.06"
- Title: `ACCEPT` -- Barlow SemiBold, 22 pt, `#27AE60`
- Items (Inter Regular, 14 pt, `#F0EDE8`, line height 170%):
  - `Chemically clean surface (water-break-free)`
  - `No visible rust, scale, or oxide`
  - `Machined or ground finish per drawing`
  - `Masking intact with no holidays`
  - `Material certification verified`
  - `Parts dry and at ambient temperature`
  - `Load map reviewed and approved`

**Reject column (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 5.8", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C` 0.06"
- Title: `REJECT` -- Barlow SemiBold, 22 pt, `#E05C5C`
- Items:
  - `Oil, grease, or fingerprints visible`
  - `Rust or scale present`
  - `Masking cracked, peeling, or incomplete`
  - `Wrong steel grade (no material cert)`
  - `Moisture on surface or in blind holes`
  - `Parts nested or touching in fixture`
  - `Stop-off paint not fully cured`
