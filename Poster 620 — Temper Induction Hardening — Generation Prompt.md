## Phase 4 -- Oven vs. Induction Temper (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `TWO TEMPERING METHODS -- WHEN TO USE EACH`.

Two large side-by-side panels (H 7.0"):

**Left -- Oven Temper (Amber):** THE CONVENTIONAL METHOD. 300--400 F (149--204 C). 1--2 hr at temperature. Standard batch or conveyor oven. Air atmosphere. Excellent uniformity. Required by most specs (AMS 2759/12). Slow throughput. Best for spec compliance, complex geometries, mixed loads. Highlight: `Still the standard for aerospace and specification-controlled work.`

**Right -- Induction Temper (Emerald):** THE PRODUCTION METHOD. 350--500 F (higher to compensate for short time). 5--30 seconds. Second coil at reduced power. No atmosphere needed. Good uniformity on symmetric parts. Not accepted by all specs -- verify. Fast throughput (inline). Best for high-volume, automotive, inline. Highlight: `Seconds instead of hours -- the induction shop's productivity advantage.`

Full-width callout below (Coral): `TEMPER IMMEDIATELY AFTER QUENCH. As-quenched martensite is under extreme stress. Delay = cracking risk. Oven: load within 1 hour. Induction: passes directly from quench to temper coil -- seconds of delay.`

## Phase 6 -- Hardness Chart + Never Skip Warning

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Hardness vs. Temper Temperature (Amber):**

Text-based chart:

| Temper Temp | 1045 HRC | 4140 HRC | 4340 HRC |
|---|---|---|---|
| As-quenched | 60 | 60 | 62 |
| 250 F | 59 | 59 | 61 |
| 300 F | 57 | 58 | 60 |
| 350 F | 55 | 56 | 59 |
| 400 F | 53 | 55 | 57 |
| 450 F | 50 | 53 | 55 |
| 500 F | 47 | 50 | 53 |

Trend: ~2 HRC per 50 F. Alloy steels retain better than plain carbon.

**Right -- Never Skip Temper (Coral):**

`NEVER SKIP TEMPER`. Untempered martensite: extremely hard (58--62 HRC), extremely brittle, severe internal stress, prone to spontaneous cracking.

If skipped: delayed cracking (hours/days/weeks), catastrophic brittle fracture, stress corrosion cracking, dimensional instability.

THE 1-HOUR RULE: Parts must enter temper within 1 hr of quench. Never left as-quenched overnight. Some specs require 30 min.

Bottom: `Temper is not optional. It is mandatory. Untempered parts are nonconforming.`
