## Phase 5 -- Zinc Fume Fever + Acetylene Safety

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `TOP HAZARDS -- ZINC FUMES & ACETYLENE`.

**Left -- Zinc Fume Fever (W: 11.0"):** accent `#E05C5C`.
Title: `ZINC FUME FEVER` -- subtitle `The #1 Occupational Health Risk`.
- Caused by inhaling zinc oxide fumes during spraying
- Symptoms: chills, fever, headache, muscle aches, nausea
- Onset: 4-12 hours after exposure
- Duration: 24-48 hours (self-resolving)
- Repeated exposure does NOT build tolerance
- Prevention: P100 RPE or supplied air -- NO exceptions

Bottom: `OSHA PEL: 5 mg/m3 (TWA) | ACGIH TLV: 2 mg/m3 (TWA); 10 mg/m3 (STEL)` in JetBrains Mono `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- Acetylene Safety (W: 11.5"):** accent `#E05C5C`.
Title: `ACETYLENE SAFETY` -- subtitle `Non-Negotiable Rules`.
- NEVER exceed 15 PSI gauge pressure (`#E05C5C` bold)
- Acetylene is unstable above 15 PSI -- decomposition explosion risk
- Secure cylinders UPRIGHT at all times
- Test connections with soapy water -- never open flame
- Install flashback arrestors on both O2 and C2H2 lines
- Purge lines before lighting; O2 off first when shutting down

Bottom: `Propane is a safer alternative fuel for many wire spray applications. Lower flame temp (~2800 degC) but eliminates acetylene decomposition risk.` in `#E8A020`.

## Phase 6 -- PPE Checklist + Field Hazards + Emergency Response

### PPE Checklist (Y: 22.0"-28.5", left W: 11.0")

Section label: `REQUIRED PPE`.

| Item | Rating | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Respirator | P100 minimum; supplied air for extended work | REQUIRED (`#E05C5C`) |
| Safety glasses | Shade 3-5 lens | REQUIRED |
| Hearing protection | NRR 25+ (above 85 dB) | REQUIRED |
| Heat-resistant gloves | Flame exposure rated | REQUIRED |
| Leather apron | Full torso coverage | REQUIRED |
| Safety boots | Steel toe, EH rated | REQUIRED |
| FR clothing | Flame-resistant coverall or jacket | REQUIRED |
| Hard hat | If overhead/field work | SITUATIONAL (`#E8A020`) |
| Fall protection | Harness if elevated (> 6 ft) | SITUATIONAL (`#E8A020`) |

### Field Work Hazards (right W: 11.5")

Title: `FIELD WORK -- ADDITIONAL HAZARDS` accent `#E8A020`.
- Elevated work (bridges, tanks): fall protection per OSHA 1926
- Confined spaces: entry permit; ventilation; gas monitoring
- Wind: deflects spray pattern; increases fume exposure
- Rain/moisture: stop work -- flash rust and porosity
- Public access: barricade; post warning signs

### Emergency Response Strip (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE -- 4 SCENARIOS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| ZINC FUME FEVER | Fresh air; fluids and rest; self-resolves 24-48 hr; seek medical if severe |
| ACETYLENE LEAK / FIRE | Shut off gas at cylinder; evacuate; DO NOT attempt to extinguish cylinder fire -- call fire dept |
| THERMAL BURN | Cool with water 10+ min; do not remove stuck clothing; seek medical for burns > palm-size |
| FALL FROM HEIGHT | Do not move victim; call EMS immediately; secure scene; first aid for shock |
