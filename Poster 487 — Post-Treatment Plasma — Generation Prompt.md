## Phase 4 -- Three-Path Decision Tree (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `POST-TREATMENT PATHS -- CHOOSE BY APPLICATION`.

Three side-by-side path cards, each 7.33" x 9.0", fill `#1E2435`, top accent 4pt.

**Path 1 -- SEAL (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
- When: Porous coatings needing corrosion/wear protection
- Method: Vacuum impregnation (epoxy, silicone, phenolic, AlPO4)
- Key: Fills interconnected porosity; no dimension change
- Apps: Wear coatings in corrosive environments; hydraulic seals
- Caution: Epoxy max ~200 degC. AlPO4 for high-temp (800+ degC)

**Path 2 -- GRIND (accent `#E8A020`):**
- When: Final dimensions or finish required by drawing
- Method: Diamond grinding (ceramics); CBN or SiC (metals)
- Key: Coolant required -- thermal shock cracks coatings
- Apps: Dimensional parts; bearing/seal surfaces
- Achievable: Ra 0.2-1.6 um; stock removal 50-200 um

**Path 3 -- HEAT TREAT (accent `#E05C5C`):**
- When: MCrAlY bond coats requiring diffusion bond
- Method: Vacuum furnace, 1050-1080 degC, 2-4 hours
- Key: Creates metallurgical bond; not for ceramic topcoats
- Apps: Turbine components with TBC systems
- Caution: High temp exceeds many substrate capabilities

## Phase 5 -- Sealer Selection

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section label: `SEALER SELECTION GUIDE`.

| Sealer Type | Max Temp | Method | Best For | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Epoxy | ~200 degC | Vacuum impregnation | General wear/corrosion | Temperature limited |
| Silicone | ~250 degC | Brush or spray | Higher temp service | Less chemical resistance |
| Phenolic | ~250 degC | Vacuum impregnation | Chemical resistance | Brittle; limited thermal cycling |
| Aluminum phosphate | ~800 degC | Inorganic ceramic sealer | High-temp turbine parts | Expensive; specialized |
| Laser glazing | Surface densification | Laser beam | Research/specialty; max density | Not widely available |

## Phase 6 -- Grinding + Heat Treatment

### Grinding Parameters (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `GRINDING APS COATINGS -- DO NOT THERMAL SHOCK`.

| Coating | Wheel | Speed | Infeed | Coolant | Ra |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Al2O3 | Diamond (resin) | 20-30 m/s | 5-15 um/pass | Required | 0.2-0.8 um |
| Cr2O3 | Diamond (resin) | 20-30 m/s | 5-10 um/pass | Required | 0.2-0.8 um |
| YSZ | Diamond (resin) | 20-30 m/s | 5-15 um/pass | Required | 0.4-1.6 um |
| NiCr/MCrAlY | CBN or SiC | 25-35 m/s | 10-20 um/pass | Required | 0.2-1.0 um |

Callout: `COOLANT IS MANDATORY. Dry grinding generates thermal shock that cracks ceramic coatings and creates subsurface damage invisible to the eye but fatal in service.` `#E05C5C`.

### Heat Treatment (Y: 26.5"-32.5")

Section label: `DIFFUSION HEAT TREATMENT -- MCrAlY BOND COATS`.

**Left -- Spec:** Atmosphere: Vacuum (< 10^-4 torr). Temp: 1050-1080 degC `#E05C5C`. Time: 2-4 hrs. Cooling: Furnace cool or controlled gas quench.

**Right -- Purpose:** Creates interdiffusion zone. Improves oxidation resistance. Not required for single-layer coatings. Verify substrate tolerates 1050+ degC.
