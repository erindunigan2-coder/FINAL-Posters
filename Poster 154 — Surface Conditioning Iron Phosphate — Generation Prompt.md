## Phase 4 -- Comparison Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `IRON PHOSPHATE vs. ZINC PHOSPHATE -- CONDITIONING`.

Two large panels, each ~11" wide, H: 8.5".

**Left -- Iron Phosphate (accent `#E8A020`):** Title: `IRON PHOSPHATE` / Subtitle: `NO SEPARATE CONDITIONER` `#27AE60`. Clean, active iron surface with uniform phosphate deposition directly from cleaned surface. Big stat: `0` 72pt `#27AE60` / `conditioning tanks required`.

Key points: cleaning exposes fresh iron; no nucleation aid needed (amorphous coating by direct acid attack); crystal structure irrelevant; surface prep = oil-free and oxide-free only.

**Right -- Zinc Phosphate (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Title: `ZINC PHOSPHATE` / Subtitle: `Ti COLLOID MANDATORY` `#E05C5C`. Steel surface with Ti nucleation sites and crystal growth. Big stat: `1` 72pt `#E05C5C` / `conditioning tank required -- non-negotiable`.

Key points: without conditioner = coarse crystals (50--100+ um); with conditioner = fine crystals (2--10 um); conditioner is THE most critical step in zinc phosphate.

## Phase 5 -- Why No Conditioner?

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THE CHEMISTRY BEHIND THE DIFFERENCE`.

Full-width panel, fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E8A020`. Two-column interior:

**Left -- Iron Phosphate Mechanism:** `AMORPHOUS COATING` `#E8A020`. Steps: acid attacks steel --> Fe2+ dissolves --> local pH rises --> iron phosphate precipitates as amorphous film. `No crystal nucleation step. No grain size to control.`

**Right -- Zinc Phosphate Mechanism:** `CRYSTALLINE COATING` `#2EC4B6`. Steps: Ti colloid adsorbs to surface --> acid attacks steel --> local pH rise --> Zn3(PO4)2 crystallizes on Ti sites. `Fewer nucleation sites = larger crystals = worse coating.`

---

## Phase 6 -- Exception + Over-Conditioning

Y: 20.5" to 26.5".

**Left -- The Exception (accent `#E8A020`):** Title: `THE ONE EXCEPTION`. Some 5-stage lines use mild acid conditioning rinse: `0.5--1% phosphoric acid` / `Ambient` / `30 sec--1 min`. Purpose: lowers surface pH, promotes uniform initial acid attack. `This is NOT a Ti colloid conditioner. It is a mild acid pre-dip.` `#E8A020`

**Right -- Over-Etched Surfaces (accent `#E05C5C`):** Title: `OVER-ETCHED SURFACES`. Over-pickled/over-etched steel = excessively active surface --> heavy, powdery phosphate > 80 mg/ft2 --> poor adhesion. `The cleaner should clean WITHOUT aggressive etching.` `#E05C5C`

## Phase 7 -- Surface Readiness Checklist

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `IS THE SURFACE READY?`.

Six-item checklist with pass/fail indicators:

| Check | Pass Condition | Fail Indicator |
|---|---|---|
| Water-break-free | Water sheets uniformly | Beading = residual oil |
| No visible soils | Clean and uniform | Staining, fingerprints |
| pH of rinse water | < 9.0 | Above 9.0 = cleaner drag-over |
| No silicate residue | No white haze | White/iridescent film = silicates |
| No flash rust | Surface bright | Orange-brown = too long between stages |
| Consistent appearance | Uniform across part | Patchy = inconsistent cleaning |

Pass: `#27AE60`. Fail: `#E05C5C`.
