## Phase 5 -- Steel Grades + Q&T Rule + Masking

### Steel Grades (Y: 14.5"--22.0")

Section label: `WHICH STEELS RESPOND TO PLASMA NITRIDING?`. 7-row table:

| Category | Grades | HV | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nitralloy | 135M, N | 900--1100 | EXCELLENT |
| Chrome-Moly | 4140, 4340 | 500--700 | GOOD |
| Hot Work Tool | H11, H13 | 900--1100 | EXCELLENT |
| PH Stainless | 17-4PH, 15-5PH | 1000--1200 | EXCELLENT |
| Austenitic SS | 304, 316 | 900--1200 | EXCELLENT (plasma only) -- S-phase |
| Tool Steels | M2, D2, A2 | 1000--1200 | EXCELLENT |
| Plain Carbon | 1018, 1045 | < 400 | DO NOT USE (`#E05C5C`) |

### Q&T Rule + Masking (Y: 22.0"--32.5")

**Left -- THE CRITICAL RULE (`#E8A020`):** Temper >= nitriding temp + 50 F. Violation = core softens. Examples: 975 F -> 1025 F min; 1050 F -> 1100 F min. Stress relieve after rough machining. Growth only 0.0001--0.0005 in/surface -- finish-grind BEFORE nitriding.

**Right -- MASKING:**
*Plasma (`#27AE60`):* MECHANICAL MASKING -- close-fitting steel masks; no electroplating; reusable; UNIQUE TO PLASMA.
*Gas (`#E8A020`):* ELECTROPLATE STOP-OFF -- tin 0.0003--0.0005 in; copper 0.0005--0.001 in; must be stripped.
