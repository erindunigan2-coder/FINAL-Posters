# Poster #154 -- Construction Workup
## Surface Conditioning -- Iron Phosphate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This poster is unique in the CC-01 cluster because iron phosphate does NOT typically use a separate surface conditioner. That is the headline. The cleaning step IS the conditioning step. This poster contrasts iron phosphate (no conditioner) with zinc phosphate (Ti colloid mandatory) and explains why. The exception -- a mild acid conditioning rinse on some 5-stage lines -- gets a focused callout.

This is a "what you DON'T need" poster. It exists to prevent shops from over-engineering their iron phosphate lines.

3. **Exception callout (Block E):** The mild acid conditioning rinse used on some 5-stage lines.

## Design Notes

This poster flips the expected format. Instead of deep-diving into a complex stage, it explains why this stage does NOT exist for iron phosphate -- and why that matters. The big "0 conditioning tanks required" stat in the hero is the anchor. The contrast with zinc phosphate (where conditioning is everything) gives context. The over-etching warning catches the edge case that does cause real problems.
