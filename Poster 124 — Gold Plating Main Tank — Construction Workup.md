# Poster #124 -- Construction Workup
## Gold Plating -- Main Tank

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. The main gold plating tank -- the most expensive bath in any plating shop. This poster is the densest in the EP-12 cluster, covering bath composition, operating parameters, insoluble anode setup, gold replenishment, the hardener balance (cobalt or nickel), and a defect diagnosis grid. Every element reinforces the central message: gold content is money, and bath control is gold accountability.

Hero visual: a plating tank cross-section showing platinized titanium anodes (insoluble), cathode, and gold ion migration -- with emphasis on the chemical replenishment path (no soluble anodes).

## Design Notes

This is the densest and most important poster in the EP-12 cluster. The "GOLD ADDED BY CHEMICAL ADDITION" callout in the hero is critical -- many operators from other plating processes assume anodes dissolve to replenish the bath. In gold, anodes are insoluble and all gold is added manually. The gold tracking gauge and accountability callout are the economic backbone of this poster. The Hull cell note ("per lot, not daily") differentiates gold from other processes where daily Hull cells are standard. The cathode efficiency callout (30--50%) prevents operators from misinterpreting normal gold plating behavior as a problem.
