# Poster #378 -- Construction Workup
## Solvent Cleaning / Degreasing -- Process Flow

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This is the cluster overview poster for CT-06: Solvent Cleaning / Degreasing. Solvent cleaning is the "heavy artillery" of soil removal -- it dissolves organic contaminants directly rather than emulsifying them. Vapor degreasing is the crown jewel: solvent boils, vapor condenses on cold parts, dissolved soil drips away, and the parts get rinsed in continuously distilled solvent. It is self-purifying and elegantly simple. This poster maps the full flow from soil assessment through transition to aqueous cleaning.

Design philosophy: a clear 7-stage flow with a vapor degreasing cross-section as the hero visual, a 4-method comparison panel, a solvent selection decision tree, and a regulatory awareness callout (F-listed waste is no joke). This is the "map" for the entire cluster.

### ZONE 3 -- Four-Method Comparison

**Section label:** `FOUR CLEANING METHODS COMPARED` -- Y: 15.7".

**BLOCK D -- Four-Method Table**

Y: 16.3" to 21.8". Column widths (23.0" total):
- Method (4.0") | Description (6.0") | Effectiveness (4.5") | Contact Time (4.0") | Best For (4.5")

Header row: fill `#3A4055`, H: 0.5".

| Method | Description | Effectiveness | Contact Time | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold Immersion | Parts soaked in liquid solvent at room temp | Moderate -- good for light-medium soil | 3-15 min | Accessible; low equipment cost |
| Vapor Degreasing | Parts in hot vapor zone; condensing solvent dissolves soil | Excellent -- self-purifying; fresh condensate always clean | 1-5 min (until condensation stops) | Heavy soil; precision; best overall |
| Spray / Wipe | Manual solvent application by cloth or spray | Variable -- operator-dependent; uneven | Until visually clean | Spot cleaning; touch-up |
| Ultrasonic + Solvent | Ultrasonic cavitation in solvent bath | Superior -- combines dissolution with mechanical action | 2-10 min | Complex geometries; blind holes |

Effectiveness color coding:
- Moderate: `#E8A020`
- Excellent / Superior: `#27AE60`
- Variable: `#E05C5C`

Data: Inter Regular 13 pt. Method names: Barlow SemiBold 14 pt.

## Design Notes

This is the "map" poster for the Solvent Cleaning cluster. The vapor degreasing cross-section MUST be immediately understandable -- it is the single most elegant cleaning concept in metal finishing (self-distilling, self-rinsing, continuously pure). The regulatory awareness zone carries extra weight here because solvent waste is F-listed -- this is the most heavily regulated waste category in cleaning operations and shops need to know it. The remaining 6 posters (#379--#384) zoom into safety, solvent preparation, the cleaning stage, rinse/transition, secondary treatment, and inspection.
