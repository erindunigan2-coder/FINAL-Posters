## Phase 4 -- Three-Path Decision Tree + Fusing Deep-Dive (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `POST-TREATMENT PATHS -- CHOOSE BASED ON COATING TYPE`.

**Three Decision Cards (Y: 5.0"-10.5"):** Each W: 7.3", H: 5.3".

**SEAL (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
- When: Zn/Al corrosion coatings, general metallic where porosity must be blocked
- Method: Epoxy, phenolic, wax, or silicone by brush, spray, or vacuum impregnation
- Result: Fills porosity; prevents moisture ingress; extends service life
- Key: Seal within 4 hours of spray per AWS C2.18

**FUSE (accent `#E8A020`):**
- When: Self-fluxing alloys only (NiCrBSi, NiBSi, CoCrWC)
- Method: Oxy-acetylene torch or furnace at 1000-1100 degC
- Result: Porosity 5-15% --> <1%; bond >70 MPa (metallurgical); hardness 700-900 HV
- Key: `CRITICAL: Fusing exceeds most substrate temp limits. Verify compatibility BEFORE spraying.` (`#E05C5C`)

**MACHINE (accent `#27AE60`):**
- When: Dimensional repair, bearing surfaces, finished dimension required
- Method: Conventional turning/milling (soft metals) or diamond grinding (hard/fused)
- Result: Achievable finish Ra 0.2-1.6 um depending on material
- Key: Spray 50-200 um oversize to allow for grinding

**Fusing Deep-Dive (Y: 11.0"-15.0"):** Sublabel: `SELF-FLUXING ALLOY FUSING -- FLAME SPRAY'S UNIQUE ADVANTAGE`.

**Left -- TORCH FUSING (accent `#E8A020`):** Operator heats with neutral flame until surface "sweats" (glossy wet appearance = remelted). 1000-1100 degC. Skill-dependent; visual endpoint. Best for field work, small areas. Risk: uneven heating.

**Right -- FURNACE FUSING (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Vacuum or controlled atmosphere at 1000-1100 degC. Uniform heating; more consistent. Best for production, critical components. Higher cost, superior repeatability.

**Property Improvement Strip:** `#252B3D` bar.

| Property | As-Sprayed | After Fusing |
|---|---|---|
| Porosity | 5-15% | <1% |
| Bond strength | 10-30 MPa (mechanical) | >70 MPa (metallurgical) |
| Hardness (NiCrBSi) | 300-500 HV | 700-900 HV |

As-sprayed: `#F0EDE8` at 60%. Fused: `#E8A020`.

## Phase 5 -- Sealing Options + Machining Guide

### Sealing Options (Y: 15.5"-22.0")

Section label: `SEALING OPTIONS FOR POROUS FLAME SPRAY COATINGS`.

| Sealer | Application | Max Temp | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Epoxy | Brush, spray, vacuum impreg | 120 degC (250 degF) | General corrosion; Zn/Al on steel |
| Phenolic | Brush or spray | 150 degC (300 degF) | Chemical resistance |
| Silicone | Brush or spray | 250 degC (480 degF) | Higher-temp; Al on exhaust systems |
| Wax | Dip or brush | 60 degC (140 degF) | Low-cost; temporary protection |
| Aluminum phosphate | Spray or brush | 800 degC (1470 degF) | High-temp inorganic; specialty |

Below: `For Zn/Al per AWS C2.18: apply seal within 4 hours. Do not allow moisture to enter porous coating before sealing.` `#2EC4B6`.

### Machining Guide (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `MACHINING FLAME SPRAY COATINGS`.

**Left -- TURNING & MILLING (accent `#27AE60`):** Soft metallic coatings (Zn, Al, bronze, babbitt, un-fused NiCrBSi). Carbide tooling; light cuts 0.1-0.3 mm. Use coolant. Finish: Ra 1.6-6.3 um.

**Right -- DIAMOND GRINDING (accent `#E8A020`):** Hard coatings (fused NiCrBSi, stellite, WC, ceramics). Diamond or CBN wheels. Wet grinding mandatory. Infeed: 5-15 um/pass. Finish: Ra 0.2-1.6 um. Superfinishing possible <0.1 um.

Below: `Spray 50-200 um oversize for machining allowance. Account for this in your spray thickness target.`

## Phase 6 -- Safety Warnings

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `CRITICAL POST-TREATMENT WARNINGS`. Four cards, accent `#E05C5C`.

| Warning | Detail |
|---|---|
| FUSING EXCEEDS SUBSTRATE LIMITS | Most steels, all aluminum, heat-treated parts cannot survive 1000-1100 degC. Verify BEFORE spraying. |
| TORCH FUSING FIRE HAZARD | Open flame at 1000+ degC. Full fire watch; clear combustibles 10 m; extinguisher at hand. |
| SEAL BEFORE MOISTURE | Porous coatings absorb moisture within hours. Seal Zn/Al within 4 hours or trap moisture at interface. |
| GRINDING DUST HAZARD | NiCrBSi, stellite, WC dust is hazardous. Wet grinding + local exhaust mandatory. RPE if dry unavoidable. |
