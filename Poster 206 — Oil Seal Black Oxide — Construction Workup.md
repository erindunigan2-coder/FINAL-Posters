# Poster #206 -- Construction Workup
## Oil / Seal -- Black Oxide

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 8 of 8. The final and arguably most important step in the black oxide line. Without an oil, wax, or lacquer seal, black oxide provides essentially zero corrosion protection (< 1 hour salt spray). The oil fills the micropores in the magnetite film, creating the actual barrier. This poster covers the three seal options (oil, wax, lacquer), their application parameters, and drives home the message that the seal IS the protection.

**Right -- Best Practices (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Callout box, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`:

```
1. Oil immediately after hot rinse -- no delay
2. Oil temperature: 150--180 F for best penetration
3. Drain excess oil after dipping (2--3 min drain)
4. Centrifuge small parts to remove excess
5. Store oiled parts in VCI paper or clean dry environment
6. Re-oil parts that have been stored > 90 days
7. If customer requires dry finish: wax or lacquer, not "no seal"
8. NEVER ship bare (unsealed) black oxide parts
```

Inter Medium 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 180%.

## Design Notes

The salt spray bar chart is the poster's anchor visual -- it makes the case for sealing more powerfully than any paragraph of text. The contrast between "< 1 hour bare" and "100+ hours with oil" is staggering, and that gap should be visually dramatic. The three seal options give shops a decision framework based on their application requirements. The micropore illustration in the hero zone connects the macroscopic process (oil dip) to the microscopic reality (pore filling) -- that connection is what makes this poster educational rather than just procedural.
