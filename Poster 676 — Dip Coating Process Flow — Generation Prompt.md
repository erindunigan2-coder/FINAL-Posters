## Phase 5 -- Three-Family Comparison

Y: 16.8" to 21.8". Section label: `THREE DIP COATING FAMILIES`. Three columns.

**Plastisol (PVC) (`#E8A020`):** PVC fused in plasticizer at 350--400 F. DFT 5--40 mils. Tool handles, dishwasher racks. Warning: `Over-fusion above 420 F releases toxic HCl gas.`

**Hot-Dip Thermoplastic (`#2EC4B6`):** Preheated part melts powder on contact. DFT 8--25+ mils. Preheat 400--600 F. Nylon, PE, PP. `Part temperature at dip determines film build.`

**Solution/Dispersion Dip (`#27AE60`):** Immersed in liquid coating. DFT 0.5--3 mils. Withdrawal speed controls thickness. Can linings, precision thin coats. `Withdrawal speed is the primary control: slower = thinner.`

## Phase 6B -- Troubleshooting Strip

Y: 29.4" to 32.3". 4 cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| DRIP MARKS / CURTAINING | Fast withdrawal or high viscosity | Reduce speed; lower viscosity; rotate during drain |
| BLISTERING | Trapped moisture or air | Pre-dry; pre-bake porous substrates |
| PINHOLES / OUTGASSING | Gas from substrate during cure | Pre-bake substrate at cure temp before dipping |
| BRIDGING | Coating spans holes/slots | Reduce viscosity; increase drain time |
