## Phase 5 -- Contamination Risk

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHAT ACID DRAG-IN DOES TO YOUR CHROME BATH`.

Full-width panel, accent `#E05C5C`:

| Contaminant | Effect on Chrome Bath | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| HCl drag-in | Introduces chloride -- attacks lead anodes (hex), disrupts bath | HIGH (`#E05C5C`) |
| H2SO4 drag-in | Increases sulfate -- shifts CrO3:SO4 ratio (hex) | MODERATE (`#E8A020`) |
| Nickel salts | Builds up as metallic contamination over time | LOW (`#2EC4B6`) |

Bottom: `Chloride is the biggest risk. Even small amounts attack lead anodes and create bath instability. If you use HCl activation, this rinse is non-negotiable.` -- `#E05C5C`

## Phase 6 -- Operating Parameters

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `OPERATING PARAMETERS (WHEN RINSE IS PRESENT)`.

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Water source | City water or DI | DI reduces mineral contamination |
| Temperature | Ambient | No heating |
| Rinse type | Single overflow | Speed is still the priority |
| Dwell time | 15--30 sec | Minimize to prevent re-passivation |
| Flow rate | Moderate continuous | Enough to dilute acid |
| Agitation | 2--3 dips | Quick immersions |
| pH check | Optional (> 5.0) | Ensures acid is adequately rinsed |

## Phase 7 -- Common Issues

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `COMMON PROBLEMS AT THIS STAGE`.

2x2 grid:

| Problem | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHROME BATH RATIO DRIFT | `#E05C5C` | Acid drag-in shifting sulfate levels | Improve rinse; monitor CrO3:SO4 ratio |
| RE-PASSIVATION | `#E05C5C` | Parts sat too long in rinse after activation | Shorten dwell; move parts immediately |
| CHLORIDE CONTAMINATION | `#E8A020` | HCl activation without adequate rinse | Switch to H2SO4 activation or add rinse |
| WATER SPOTS | `#E8A020` | Hard water deposits on parts | Use DI water for final rinse stage |
