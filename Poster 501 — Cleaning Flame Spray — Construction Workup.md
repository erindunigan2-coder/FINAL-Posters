# Poster #501 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Flame Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Pre-spray cleaning for flame spray. Flame spray is frequently used in field repair where ideal shop cleaning is not available -- this poster covers both shop-standard and field-expedient methods. The core message: clean before you mask, and clean before you blast. Skip the prep, ruin the coating.

### ZONE 3 -- Cleaning Sequence (HERO)

**Section label:** `PRE-SPRAY CLEANING SEQUENCE` -- Y: 4.4".

**BLOCK B -- 4-Step Vertical Flowchart**

Y: 5.0" to 14.5". Four large step boxes stacked vertically with connecting arrows. Each box spans full width within margins.

Each step box: Rounded rect, W: 23.0", H: 2.0", fill `#1E2435`, left accent 0.06" in Teal.

| Step | Title | Method (Shop) | Method (Field) | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SOLVENT DEGREASE | Vapor degrease or aqueous alkaline clean | Solvent wipe (acetone or MEK) | No visible oil residue |
| 2 | ALKALINE WASH | Immersion/spray, 50--70 degC, pH 10--12, 5--15 min | Alkaline wipe or scrub if immersion unavailable | Rinse thoroughly |
| 3 | RINSE & DRY | Clean water rinse; forced air or oven dry | Solvent wipe to remove residue; air dry | No moisture at blast time |
| 4 | INSPECT | Water-break-free test (ASTM F22 equivalent) | Visual inspection -- surface must be dry and free of contamination | Proceed to grit blast immediately |

Step number: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 24 pt `#2EC4B6` (in left accent zone).
Title: Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#F0EDE8`.
Shop method: Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`. Field method: Inter Regular 14 pt `#E8A020`.
Verification: JetBrains Mono 13 pt `#27AE60`.

Arrows between boxes: 3 pt `#3A4055`, downward.

### ZONE 4 -- Contaminant Identification

**Section label:** `WHAT KILLS ADHESION -- 6 CONTAMINANTS` -- Y: 15.2".

**BLOCK D -- 3x2 Contaminant Grid**

Y: 15.8" to 20.8". Cards W: 7.33", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Position | Contaminant | Source | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | OIL / GREASE | Machining fluids, fingerprints, lubricants | Coating delamination; porosity at interface |
| R1C2 | MOISTURE | Humidity, condensation, incomplete drying | Steam blisters during spray; porosity |
| R1C3 | MILL SCALE | Hot-rolled steel surface oxide | Flakes off under coating -- catastrophic adhesion loss |
| R2C1 | RUST | Ambient corrosion on stored steel | Weak oxide layer -- coating bonds to rust, not metal |
| R2C2 | OLD COATINGS | Previous paint, thermal spray, or plating | Must be completely removed -- grit blast alone may not suffice |
| R2C3 | SHOP DUST / GRIT | Airborne particles settling on cleaned surface | Inclusions at coating interface; porosity |

Contaminant: Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#E05C5C`. Source/Consequence: Inter Regular 13 pt.
