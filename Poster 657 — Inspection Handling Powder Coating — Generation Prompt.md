## Phase 4 -- Test Method Matrix Hero

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `THE SEVEN TESTS -- YOUR QUALITY CHECKLIST`. 7-row table:

| Test | ASTM | Method | Pass Criteria | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Film Thickness (DFT) | D7091 | Magnetic (steel) or eddy current (Al) | Per spec +/- 0.5 mil | 5 parts/batch |
| Adhesion (Cross-Cut) | D3359 B | Score lattice, tape pull 180 deg | 4B--5B | Per batch/shift |
| Adhesion (X-Cut) | D3359 A | Score X, tape pull | No removal at intersection | Quick field check |
| Pencil Hardness | D3363 | Push calibrated pencils | Typical F to 3H | Per batch |

| Impact Resistance | D2794 | Drop weighted tup | 80--160 in-lb typical | Per batch |
| Cure (MEK Rub) | D4752 | MEK cloth, 50 double rubs | 50+ no softening = cured | Per oven load |

## Phase 5 -- Salt Spray + DFT Measurement

**Salt Spray Performance** (ASTM B117): iron phos + polyester 500--750 hr, zinc phos + polyester 1,000--1,500 hr, iron phos + epoxy 1,000+ hr, nanoceramic + polyester 500--750 hr.

**DFT Measurement Basics**: steel = magnetic gauge, aluminum = eddy current, 3--5 readings per part, 5 parts/batch, calibrate on uncoated substrate, avoid edges/corners.

---

## Phase 6 -- Handling Rules + Packaging + Defect Grid

**Handling Rules** (Coral accent): Cool to < 120 F before handling, fingerprints embed permanently in warm film, clean cotton/nitrile gloves only, silicone gloves = fish-eye on next batch, inspect before removing from rack.

**Packaging** (Emerald accent): No surface contact between parts, foam dividers, kraft paper interleaving, bubble wrap for complex shapes, cardboard edge protectors.

**Defect Grid** (3x2): low DFT, adhesion fail (0B--3B), MEK fail (< 50 rubs), mandrel bend crack, low impact, handling damage.
