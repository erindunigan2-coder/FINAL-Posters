## Phase 4 -- Decision Tree Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `TO ETCH OR NOT TO ETCH?`.

Hero visual: decision flowchart.
- `Is etch prohibited by specification?` YES --> SKIP ETCH (go to desmut)
- NO --> `Is the part fatigue-critical?` YES --> NO ETCH or VERY LIGHT (NaOH 20--30 g/L, 15--30 sec)
- NO --> `Does cosmetic finish require etch?` YES --> LIGHT ETCH (NaOH 30--45 g/L, 30--90 sec)

Three approach panels:
- **No Etch:** Skip to desmut. Most common for Type I. Preserves dimensions and fatigue life.
- **Light Etch:** NaOH 20--40 g/L, 130--150 F, 15--60 sec. Removes minor oxide, not mill finish.
- **Standard Etch:** NaOH 40--80 g/L, 130--150 F, 1--3 min. Rarely used for Type I. Risk: material removal on thin-wall parts.

## Phase 5 -- Alloy-Specific Etch Behavior

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `ALLOY BEHAVIOR`.

| Alloy | Etch Recommendation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | Very light or skip | Cu smut heavy; fatigue-critical applications |
| 6061 | Light etch acceptable | Standard aerospace alloy for Type I |
| 7075 | Very light or skip | Zn causes rapid etch; fatigue-critical |
| Cast | NOT RECOMMENDED for Type I | Si disrupts thin oxide film |

## Phase 7 -- Failure Modes

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 ETCH FAILURES`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OVER-ETCH | Too long or too hot for thin-coat spec | Reduce time; lower temp; or skip etch entirely |
| 2 | SURFACE ROUGHNESS | Heavy etch on thin-oxide application | Skip etch; mechanical prep instead |
| 3 | FATIGUE REDUCTION | Material removal at critical surfaces | Eliminate etch per specification |
| 4 | HEAVY SMUT | Cu alloy (2024, 7075) etch residue | Expected -- aggressive desmut next step |
