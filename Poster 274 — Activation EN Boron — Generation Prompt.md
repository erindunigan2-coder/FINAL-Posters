## Phase 6 -- EN-B Specific Insights

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `EN-B ACTIVATION -- WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT`.

**Left -- "More Forgiving" (accent `#27AE60`):**
- DMAB's strong reducing power makes EN-B initiate on most clean metallic surfaces
- Less sensitive to marginal activation than EN-P
- Does NOT mean skip activation -- spec-grade adhesion requires proper prep (ASTM B841)

**Right -- HE Risk (accent `#E05C5C`):**
- EN-B wear applications = high-strength steel substrates
- Acid activation introduces hydrogen
- ASTM B849/B850: HE bake 190--210 C, 2--23 hrs, within 4 hrs of plating for >1000 MPa UTS
- Cathodic electroclean also introduces H -- minimize time

## Phase 7 -- Safety + Troubleshooting

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Safety:** HCl/H2SO4 (corrosive), HNO3 (oxidizer), NiCl2 (carcinogen as aerosol), Chromic acid (Cr6+).

**Right -- Troubleshooting:**

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Skip plating | Activation too brief | Extend time; verify coverage |
| Blistering | Over-activation | Reduce time |
| Poor adhesion on Al | Single zincate | Always double zincate |
| Uneven deposition | Non-uniform activation | Improve agitation |
