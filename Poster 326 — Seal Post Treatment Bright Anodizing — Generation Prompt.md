## Phases 5-7 -- Content Zones

Dye parameters: standard organic dyes, 120--150 F, pH 5--6.5. Bright anodizing produces vivid, candy-like colors due to underlying mirror surface.

Seal: Nickel acetate PREFERRED (158--185 F). Hot water seal risks haze. Cold seal acceptable.

Reflectance testing: specular gloss meter (60-degree geometry). ASTM D523.

Failure modes: seal bloom (haze), dye bleed, color shift.
