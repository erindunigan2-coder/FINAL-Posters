# Poster #81 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Chrome (Decorative) -- Pre-Activation

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of 8. This rinse removes nickel drag-out before the activation step (or before direct entry to the chrome bath). The defining constraint: every second spent here is a second the nickel surface is passivating. This is a speed rinse, not a thorough multi-stage cascade.

Hero visual: a rinse tank cross-section with flow indicators and a prominent "TIME IS YOUR ENEMY" callout showing the passivation clock ticking.

## Design Notes

The paradox of this rinse stage: in most plating lines, rinsing is about thoroughness. Here, thoroughness kills you. The passivation clock in Zone 3 is the hook. The trade-off diagram in Zone 5 crystallizes the decision every operator makes at this tank, whether they realize it or not. Keep the parameter panel minimal -- this is not a complex stage, and inflating it would dilute the speed message.
