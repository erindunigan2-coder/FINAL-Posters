# Poster #173 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Manganese Phosphate -- Post-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 6 of 8. This is the warm water rinse between the near-boiling phosphate tank and the oil seal. The key tension: you must rinse thoroughly enough to remove unreacted acid and salts, but you must NOT over-rinse. The porous hureaulite crystal coating absorbs water that is difficult to displace with oil later. Warm water (100--140 F) is specified -- not cold (thermal shock on hot parts) and not hot (continued etching). Brief immersion: 30 seconds to 2 minutes maximum.

Hero visual: rinse tank showing the transition from hot coating bath to oil seal.

### ZONE 5 -- Rinse Timing + Temperature

**Left -- Why Warm? (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Section label: `WHY WARM WATER?` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

Three temperature zones:

| Temperature | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Cold (< 80 F) | Thermal shock on hot parts; slow drying; water stays in pores longer | AVOID |
| Warm (100--140 F) | Gradual cool-down; faster evaporation; residue dissolves easily | CORRECT |
| Hot (> 150 F) | Continued etching of coating; can dissolve thin areas | AVOID |

Warm row highlighted with emerald accent.

**Right -- Timing Window (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Section label: `THE TIMING WINDOW` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

Horizontal timeline bar:

- `2 min` to `5 min`: `Risk zone -- water absorption begins` -- fill `#E8A020` at 30%
- `> 5 min`: `OVER-RINSED -- pores saturated` -- fill `#E05C5C` at 40%

Below bar:

## Design Notes

The over-rinse danger is the hero story. The side-by-side crystal comparison (pores open vs. pores waterlogged) is the most important visual on this poster -- it explains the mechanism behind the "do not over-rinse" rule in a way that text alone cannot. The timing bar gives a concrete, visual reference for operators. The "no dry-off oven" callout answers the question every new operator asks: "where is the dryer?"
