## Phase 4 -- Two-Track Cleaning (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `TWO SUBSTRATES -- TWO CLEANING PATHS`. Two panels.

**Left -- Steel (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Three steps: 1) Solvent Clean SP1 (remove oils, before blast). 2) Salt Removal (pressurized fresh water, coastal/marine, before blast). 3) Post-Blast Blow-Down (oil-free air/vacuum, D4285 blotter test).

**Right -- Concrete (accent `#E8A020`):** Three steps: 1) Alkaline Degrease (oils, tire marks). 2) Power Wash (3,000-5,000 psi, remove laitance). 3) Acid Etch fallback (10-15% muriatic when mechanical unavailable, rinse thoroughly, neutralize).

## Phase 5 -- Salt Removal + Acid Etch/Oil-Free Air

### Salt Removal (Y: 14.5"-21.0")

Section label: `SALT REMOVAL -- CRITICAL FOR MARINE AND COASTAL STEEL`. Two columns.

Left -- Procedure: 1) Pressurized fresh water 3,000-5,000 psi before blast. 2) Dry completely. 3) Blast SP10/SP5. 4) Bresle patch ISO 8502-6. 5) Verify Cl < 3, SO4 < 10. 6) If exceeded: re-wash, re-blast, re-test.

Right -- Osmotic Blistering Mechanism: Salts trapped under coating are hygroscopic. Attract moisture via osmosis. Water accumulates, blister grows, corrosion begins. `"It looks clean" is not a test result.`

### Acid Etch + Oil-Free Air (Y: 21.0"-27.0")

Two columns.

Left -- Acid Etch (Concrete): Last resort. 10-15% muriatic acid. Apply to dry concrete. Vigorous fizzing = etching (no fizz = sealed surface). Rinse THOROUGHLY. Neutralize with baking soda. 24+ hr drying. `Shot blasting is always superior.`

Right -- Oil-Free Air D4285: Blotter test procedure. PASS: clean and dry. FAIL: fix compressor. Cross-ref Poster #706.

## Phase 6 -- Mistakes + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Two columns.

Left -- Mistakes: Skip salt wash (blistering within months). Blast without solvent clean (oil in profile). Acid etch without rinsing (acid residue). Power wash without drying (trapped moisture). Skip blotter test (invisible oil).

Right -- Safety: Muriatic acid (corrosive, HCl fumes, PPE). Solvents (flammable/toxic, ventilate). High-pressure water (injection hazard). Compressed air (> 30 psi injection risk). Alkaline cleaners (skin/eye, gloves/goggles).
