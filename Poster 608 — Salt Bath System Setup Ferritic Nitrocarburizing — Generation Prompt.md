## Phase 4 -- Salt Bath Specifications (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `TWO BATHS, TWO FUNCTIONS -- THE CHEMISTRY OF Q-P-Q`.

Two large side-by-side cards (H: 8.5"):

**Nitrocarburizing Salt Bath (Emerald accent):** THE CORE PROCESS. Composition: NaCNO/KCNO + Na2CO3/K2CO3. Target CNO: 35--40%. Temp: 1050--1125 F (most common 1075 F). Function: cyanate decomposes at steel surface, releasing N + C that diffuse into ferrite. Produces epsilon Fe2-3N compound zone (0.0004--0.001") + diffusion zone (0.005--0.025"). Immersion: 60--120 min standard, up to 240. Heating: external electric or immersed electrodes.

**Oxidizing Salt Quench Bath (Amber accent):** THE CORROSION BARRIER. Composition: NaNO3/NaNO2. Temp: 700--800 F. Function: creates magnetite (Fe3O4) sealing compound zone pores. WITH quench (Q-P-Q): 200--500 hr salt spray. WITHOUT: 50--100 hr estimate. Immersion: 15--30 min per quench. NOTE: "quench" is NOT hardening -- no martensite, microstructure stays ferritic.

## Phase 5 -- Gas FNC Alternative + Bath Maintenance

### Gas FNC (Y: 14.5"--22.0")

Section label: `GAS FNC -- THE SALT-FREE ALTERNATIVE`.

Two-column comparison:

**Gas FNC (Teal):** NH3 + CO2 atmosphere, 1050--1100 F, 60--180 min. Advantages: no molten salt, no cyanate waste, cleaner, lower regulatory burden, easier to automate. Limitations: cannot produce Q-P-Q oxidizing quench; lower corrosion unless supplemented.

**Salt Bath FNC (Amber):** Advantages: full Q-P-Q available, uniform heat transfer, established for firearms/auto/hydraulics. Limitations: safety, cyanate waste, CN monitoring, higher cost, bath maintenance. Bottom line: salt bath for max Q-P-Q corrosion; gas for cleaner operation.

### Bath Maintenance (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `BATH MAINTENANCE -- KEEPING THE CHEMISTRY RIGHT`.

Three cards:

| Card | Accent | Content |
|---|---|---|
| CYANATE ANALYSIS | `#27AE60` | Target 35--40% CNO. Every shift or per load. Titration method. Low CNO = thin zone. Replenish per supplier schedule. |
| CYANIDE MONITORING | `#E05C5C` | CN is a byproduct of bath operation. Monitor per regulatory schedule. Hazardous waste disposal. NEVER dump salt down drain. |
| SLUDGE & DRAGOUT | `#E8A020` | Iron fines/scale at bottom -- periodic removal. Track dragout volume and replenish. Cross-contamination between baths degrades both -- adequate drip time. |
