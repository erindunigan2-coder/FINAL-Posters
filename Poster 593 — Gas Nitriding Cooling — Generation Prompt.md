## Phase 4 -- NO QUENCH Hero + Explanation

Y: 2.9" to 13.5".

**Typographic hero:** `NO QUENCH` -- 140 pt `#E8A020` centered. Below: `Furnace Cool Under Ammonia to 300 F -- Then Air Cool to Ambient` -- 32 pt at 60%.

**Left -- WHY NO QUENCH (`#27AE60`):** Operates at 925--1050 F, below Ac1 (~1333 F). Steel remains FERRITIC. No phase transformation, no austenite. Hardness from nitride PRECIPITATES (Al/Cr/Mo/V + N). Precipitates stable on cooling. This is PRECIPITATION hardening, not TRANSFORMATION hardening.

**Right -- WHAT IF YOU QUENCHED? (`#E05C5C`):** ZERO additional hardness; THERMAL SHOCK; cracking risk at case/core transition; DISTORTION (the thing nitriding avoids); waste of time and money. `If you quench a nitrided part, you achieve nothing good and risk everything nitriding was selected to protect.`

| Step | Accent | Title | Content | Key Data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#E8A020` | MAINTAIN ATMOSPHERE | NH3 flowing during cool; prevents oxidation | NH3 ON until 300 F |

### Distortion Comparison (Y: 20.0"--27.0")

Section label: `DISTORTION COMPARISON -- NITRIDING VS. QUENCH-BASED PROCESSES`. 6-row table:

| Process | Quench | Typical Distortion | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gas Nitriding | NONE | Near zero (< 0.0005 in) | Thermal gradient only |
| Plasma Nitriding | NONE | Near zero | Same as gas |
| Gas Carburizing | Oil (fast) | 0.002--0.010+ in | Phase transformation + shock |
| Vacuum Carburizing | Gas (moderate) | 0.001--0.005 in | Phase transformation |
| Induction | Spray (fast) | 0.001--0.005 in | Localized transformation |
| Carbonitriding | Oil (fast) | 0.001--0.008 in | Phase transformation + shock |

## Phase 6 -- FAQ

Y: 27.0"--32.5". Section label: `FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS`. 3 cards:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the part need tempering after nitriding? | NO. The hold IS a long, low-temp soak. No quench stress. Core retains pre-nitriding Q&T hardness. |
| Can I speed up cooling? | Not recommended. Rapid cooling = thermal shock + surface cracking on brittle case. Patience is the process. |
| Sub-zero treatment after nitriding? | Not applicable. No austenite exists to convert. Sub-zero is a carburizing tool, not a nitriding tool. |
