## Phase 6 -- Common Inspection Findings

Y: 27.0"--32.5". Section label: `COMMON FINDINGS AND ROOT CAUSES`. 4 cards:

| Finding | Root Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|
| SHALLOW CASE | Low temp, short time, or excess NH3 | Increase time/temp; optimize NH3 |
| HIGH RETAINED AUSTENITE | Excess NH3; surface N too high | Reduce NH3; sub-zero treatment |
| SURFACE MICROPOROSITY | Excess NH3; N gas entrapment | Reduce NH3 addition rate |
| LOW SURFACE HARDNESS | Quench too slow; wrong grade; decarb | Verify quench; check cert; check Cp |
