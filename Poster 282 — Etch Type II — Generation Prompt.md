## Phase 6 -- Alloy-Specific Etch Behavior

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `ALLOY BEHAVIOR -- EVERY ALLOY ETCHES DIFFERENTLY`.

| Alloy | Etch Time | Smut Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6061 / 6063 | 2--3 min (standard) | Light, easily removed | Gold standard for anodizing |
| 5052 | 2--3 min | Slight grayish tint | Good etch behavior |
| 1100 | 2--3 min | Minimal smut | Soft; watch for over-etch |
| 2024 | 1--2 min (SHORTER) | HEAVY dark copper smut | Cu residue requires aggressive desmut (HNO3/HF) |
| 7075 | 1--2 min (SHORTER) | Zn + Cu smut | Faster etch rate; reduce time |
| Cast (high Si) | NOT RECOMMENDED | Gritty, rough surface | Si does NOT dissolve in NaOH |

2024/7075 rows: left accent `#E05C5C`. Cast row: `#E05C5C` notes.

## Phase 7 -- Etch Reaction + Failure Modes

Y: 26.5" to 32.5".

**Left -- Etch Reaction:**
`2Al + 2NaOH + 2H2O --> 2NaAlO2 + 3H2` in prominent callout `#E8A020`.
- `Aluminum dissolves in caustic, forming sodium aluminate`
- `Hydrogen gas evolution is vigorous -- ventilation essential` `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Failure Modes:**

| Failure | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Over-etching | Too long, too hot, or too concentrated | Reduce time; check temp and NaOH |
| Under-etching | Too short, dissolved Al too high | Extend time; partial dump |
| Heavy smut | Cu/Zn alloys (2024, 7075) | Expected -- aggressive desmut next step |
| Pitting | Grain boundary attack from over-etch | Reduce time; check alloy compatibility |
| Streaking | Mill finish variation not removed | Extend etch or increase concentration |
