## Phase 4 -- Blast Parameters + Media (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.0". Section label: `BLAST SPECIFICATION FOR APS`.

**Blast Parameter Table (left, W: 11.0"):**

| Parameter | Typical Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Media | White/brown alumina (Al2O3) | Aerospace: white alumina only (99%+ purity) |
| Grit size | 24-36 mesh (coarse); 60 mesh (thin substrates) | Coarser = deeper profile |
| Blast pressure | 40-80 PSI (275-550 kPa) | Higher for harder substrates |
| Nozzle distance | 100-200 mm (4-8 in) | Closer = more aggressive |
| Blast angle | 60-90 deg | 90 deg max depth; 60-75 wider pattern |
| Anchor profile (Ra) | 3-8 um (125-325 uin) | Specification dependent |
| Cleanliness | SSPC-SP 5 / NACE No.1 / SA 3 | Most APS specs require SP-5 |

**Media Selection Guide (right, W: 11.5"):**

| Media | Grit Size | Hardness | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| White alumina | 24-60 mesh | Mohs 9 | Aerospace; no ferrous contamination |
| Brown alumina | 24-60 mesh | Mohs 9 | General purpose; lower cost |
| Angular steel grit | G25-G40 | Mohs 7-8 | Infrastructure; bridge work |
| Silicon carbide | 24-60 mesh | Mohs 9.5 | Hard substrates; titanium prep |
| Garnet | 36-80 mesh | Mohs 7-8 | Non-ferrous; less aggressive |
| Chilled iron grit | G25-G40 | Mohs 7-8 | Heavy structural steel |

## Phase 5 -- Profile Verification + Standards

### Profile Verification (Y: 14.0"-20.5")

Section label: `HOW TO VERIFY YOUR PROFILE`. Three side-by-side cards, top accent `#2EC4B6`:

| Method | Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Testex Replica Tape | Press-o-film pressed into surface, measured with micrometer. Peak-to-valley. Field standard. | Field verification; quick QC |
| Surface Profilometer | Stylus traces surface; outputs Ra, Rz digitally. Most precise. | Lab verification; spec compliance |
| Visual Comparator | SSPC-VIS 1 standard photos. Compare to reference images. Qualitative. | First-pass field assessment |

### Why Alumina + Cleanliness Standards (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

**Left -- Why Alumina:** accent `#E8A020`. Three reasons: (1) No ferrous contamination on Ni/Ti `#27AE60`, (2) Self-sharpening -- fractures to expose fresh edges `#F0EDE8`, (3) Steel grit embeds and causes galvanic corrosion `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- SSPC Cleanliness:** accent `#2EC4B6`.

| SSPC | ISO 8501 | NACE | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| SP 5 | Sa 3 | No. 1 | White Metal -- all contamination removed |
| SP 10 | Sa 2.5 | No. 2 | Near-White -- 95% cleaned |
| SP 6 | Sa 2 | No. 3 | Commercial -- 67% cleaned |

Note: `Most APS specifications require SP-5 (White Metal). Accept nothing less.` `#E8A020`.

## Phase 6 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section label: `GRIT BLAST PROBLEMS -- QUICK FIXES`. Four coral-accented cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| EMBEDDED GRIT | Excessive pressure or too close | Reduce pressure; increase nozzle distance |
| INSUFFICIENT PROFILE | Low pressure or worn media | Increase pressure; replace media |
| NON-UNIFORM PROFILE | Inconsistent angle or distance | Maintain 60-90 deg; steady hand/robot |
| FERROUS CONTAMINATION | Steel grit on Ni/Ti substrate | Switch to alumina; NEVER use steel on aerospace |
