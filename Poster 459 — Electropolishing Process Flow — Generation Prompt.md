| Stage | Name | Accent | Key Parameters | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alkaline Clean | `#2EC4B6` | 50--70 C, soak/ultrasonic, 5--15 min | Water-break-free surface |

## Phase 6 -- How EP Works + Troubleshooting

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left panel -- ANODIC DISSOLUTION (Emerald accent):**

```
1. Part made anodic (+) in acid electrolyte
2. Viscous film forms on surface -- rich in dissolved metal ions
3. Film is THINNER over microscopic peaks
4. Higher current density at peaks = faster dissolution
5. Peaks dissolve preferentially -- surface smooths
6. Result: 50--75% Ra improvement typical
```

Highlight: `EP does not create a finish -- it reveals and improves the substrate's metallurgical surface`

**Right panel -- THE POLISHING PLATEAU (Amber accent):**

| Voltage Region | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Low voltage | Active etching -- NOT polishing |
| Polishing plateau | Current constant as voltage rises -- operate HERE |
| High voltage | O2 evolution -- pitting and gas streaks |

Highlight: `Always verify on polishing plateau before production. Too low = etching. Too high = pitting.`

**Troubleshooting strip -- 4 cards (Coral accent):**

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| PITTING | Above plateau / Cl- contamination | Verify voltage; test for chloride |
| ORANGE PEEL | Below plateau; insufficient time | Increase voltage to plateau; extend time |
| STREAKING | Non-uniform current; gas entrapment | Reposition parts; tilt for gas escape |
| STAINING | Slow removal; poor rinsing | Immediate rinse; quick transfer |
