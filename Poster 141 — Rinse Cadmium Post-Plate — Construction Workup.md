# Poster #141 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Cadmium -- Post-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 6 of 8. The post-plate rinse removes cyanide and cadmium drag-out from freshly plated parts. BOTH waste streams are hazardous: cyanide requires alkaline chlorination destruction, and cadmium is a priority pollutant under the Clean Water Act. All rinse water from this stage must be SEGREGATED and treated as hazardous waste. There is no "drain it and forget it" option.

### ZONE 4 -- Waste Segregation

**Section label:** `DUAL WASTE STREAMS -- CYANIDE AND CADMIUM` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Two-Column Waste Panel (Y: 15.3" to 20.3")**

**Left -- Cyanide Waste (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`
- Title: `CYANIDE DESTRUCTION` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E05C5C`
- `Two-stage alkaline chlorination:`
- `Stage 1: CN- --> CNO- (pH > 10, ORP +350 mV)`
- `Stage 2: CNO- --> CO2 + N2 (pH 8--9, ORP +600 mV)`
- `Cyanide must be destroyed BEFORE cadmium precipitation`
- `Residual CN < 0.1 mg/L after treatment`

**Right -- Cadmium Waste (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `CADMIUM PRECIPITATION` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`
- `After CN destruction, precipitate Cd as:`
- `Cd(OH)2 (hydroxide) at pH 9--11 -- most common`
- `or CdS (sulfide) -- lower solubility, tighter limits`
- `Settle, filter, dewater sludge`
- `Sludge is RCRA hazardous waste (D006) -- manifest for disposal`
- `EPA discharge limit: 0.1 mg/L (monthly avg)`

## Design Notes

The heaviest waste-treatment poster in the series. Cadmium waste is the most regulated plating waste stream. The dual waste panel (CN destruction + Cd precipitation) is the key visual -- operators and waste treatment operators both need this reference. The discharge limits table provides real regulatory numbers that shops can check against their POTW permits. The RCRA hazardous waste designation (D006) for cadmium sludge is a critical detail that must appear prominently.
