## Phase 5 -- Reverse Etch Parameters

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `REVERSE ETCH PARAMETERS`.

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bath | Chrome plating bath (CrO3 + sulfate) | Same bath used for plating |
| Workpiece polarity | ANODIC (+) | Part is the anode |
| Current density | 100--200 ASF (anodic) | Higher CD = more aggressive etch |
| Time | 30 sec to 3 min | Depends on substrate/oxide |
| Temperature | Bath operating temp (120--140 F) | Part heats during etch |
| What it does | Dissolves oxide and thin metal layer | Micro-rough surface for adhesion |
| Iron buildup | Dissolved iron enters bath | Monitor; >8--10 g/L causes rough deposits |
| Transition | Reverse polarity immediately | No delay -- same bath |

Key insight: `The reverse etch serves THREE purposes: (1) removes oxide, (2) roughens surface for adhesion, (3) pre-heats the part to reduce thermal shock. Genius in its simplicity.` -- `#E8A020`

## Phase 6 -- External Acid Etch Alternative

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `ALTERNATIVE: EXTERNAL ACID ETCH`.

Full-width panel, accent `#3A4055`. Title: `WHEN REVERSE ETCH IS NOT ENOUGH`.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Solution | 20--50% v/v HCl or 10--30% v/v H2SO4 |
| Temperature | Ambient to 120 F |
| Time | 1--5 min |
| Use case | Heavy oxide, hardened steel, case-hardened surfaces |
| Rinse after | Single rinse before chrome bath |
| Caution | H-embrittlement risk on high-strength steel (>= 40 HRC) |

When to use: Reverse etch alone leaves visible oxide after 3 min at 200 ASF | Hardened tool steel | Heavy scale from heat treatment | Significant corrosion.

Bottom: `Most hard chrome shops use reverse etch 95%+ of the time. External acid etch is the exception.` -- `#2EC4B6`

## Phase 7 -- Failure Modes

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 ACTIVATION FAILURES`.

2x2 grid:

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHROME PEELING | `#E05C5C` | Reverse etch too short | Extend etch time; increase anodic CD |
| OVER-ETCHED SURFACE | `#E8A020` | Etch too long or CD too high | Reduce time; validate with test panels |
| HIGH IRON IN BATH | `#E05C5C` | Excessive reverse etch dissolves steel | Shorten etch; dummy plate; dilute |
| POOR ADHESION ON HARDENED STEEL | `#E8A020` | Reverse etch insufficient for hardened substrate | Use external acid etch first |
