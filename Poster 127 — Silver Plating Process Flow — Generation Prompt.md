## Phase 5 -- Why Cyanide Silver?

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHY SILVER? -- CONDUCTIVITY IS KING`.

**Left -- Why Cyanide? (accent `#27AE60`):** Only commercially viable method / Cyanide complexes silver for controlled deposition / Prevents immersion on copper / KCN preferred over NaCN / Near-100% cathode efficiency / No viable acid alternative. `Strike is not optional -- skip it and silver peels.`

**Right -- Conductivity Comparison (accent `#E8A020`):**

| Metal | % IACS | Color |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 106% | `#27AE60` |
| Copper | 100% | `#E8A020` |
| Gold | 70% | `#E8A020` |
| Aluminum | 61% | `#F0EDE8` |
| Nickel | 25% | `#F0EDE8` at 60% |
| Tin | 15% | `#F0EDE8` at 60% |

Bottom bar: `Silver exceeds copper. For RF/microwave, matte silver maximizes conductivity.`

## Phase 6 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | TARNISHING | Sulfide in atmosphere/rinse; no anti-tarnish | Apply BTA or proprietary protector |
| 2 | 6.33" | PEELING FROM COPPER | No strike or inadequate strike | Verify strike bath; 15--30 sec minimum |
| 3 | 12.16" | DARK DEPOSIT | Organic, carbonate >75 g/L, sulfide | Carbon treat; freeze-out carbonate |
| 4 | 18.0" | BURNING AT HCD | CD too high; low silver metal | Reduce CD; add silver cyanide |
