# Poster #221 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- EN (Low Phos) -- Post-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 6 of the EN Low-P process. The post-plate rinse stops the EN reaction, removes drag-out chemicals, and prepares the surface for post-treatment (heat treatment or passivation). Unlike electroplating rinses, the EN reaction is still active on the part as it exits the bath -- the cold rinse water quenches this reaction. Parts must not air-dry between the EN bath and rinse or dried EN solution causes permanent staining.

Hero visual: cascade rinse with handling callouts showing the "no touch, no dry" rules.

### ZONE 5 -- Common Problems & Fixes

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE POST-PLATE RINSE` -- Y: 21.2".

| Problem | Symptom | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watermarks / staining | White or iridescent marks on surface | Parts air-dried before reaching rinse | Transfer immediately; keep parts wet |
| Fingerprints etched in | Permanent marks matching finger contact | Handling without gloves | Enforce glove use; rack handling only |
| Galvanic attack | Dark spots at part-to-part contact | Parts stacked wet after plating | Separate parts during rinse and drying |
| Incomplete reaction stop | Deposit continues to build in rinse | Rinse water too warm | Use cold water; increase flow rate |

### ZONE 6 -- Drag-Out Recovery + Safety

**Left -- Drag-Out Recovery (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Title: `DRAG-OUT RECOVERY` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#2EC4B6`
- `EN drag-out contains dissolved nickel and hypophosphite -- valuable chemistry`
- `A static drag-out tank (before the flowing rinse) recovers 30-50% of drag-out`
- `Recovered drag-out can be returned to the EN bath to extend bath life`
- `Reduces waste treatment load and chemical costs`

**Right -- Safety (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Title: `SAFETY NOTES` `#E8A020`
- `EN rinse water contains dissolved nickel -- do not discharge untreated`
- `Nickel is a regulated heavy metal -- waste treatment required`
- `Rinse water may be mildly alkaline (pH 8-9) from drag-out`
- `Wet floors: slip hazard`
- `SDS for EN chemicals must be posted`
