# Poster #40 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Zinc (Acid)

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of the acid chloride zinc process. Cleaning for acid zinc has one additional concern that alkaline zinc doesn't: silicated cleaners. A silicate film on the surface will cause skip plating in an acid zinc bath. Non-silicated cleaners are mandatory, not optional. The hero visual is a tank cross-section with parts immersed, showing the dual-stage approach (soak + electroclean) typical of acid zinc lines.

### ZONE 5 -- Common Problems & Fixes

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE CLEANING STAGE` -- Y: 22.2".

**BLOCK F -- Problem Table (5 rows)**

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skip plating | Bare spots on zinc deposit | Silicate residue from cleaner | Switch to non-silicated cleaner immediately |
| Water break | Fish-eyes or uneven zinc deposit | Residual oil -- cleaner too weak or too cold | Increase concentration or temp; extend time |
| Poor adhesion | Zinc peels on bending or forming | Oxide layer not removed (under-cleaning) | Add electroclean step; anodic preferred |
| Pitting in zinc | Small pits scattered across deposit | Oil micro-droplets carried into plate tank | Carbon treat zinc bath; improve agitation |
| Smut / dark residue | Grey film before activation | High-carbon steel or cast iron releasing smut | Add de-smutting step; light anodic electroclean |

Column widths: Problem (3.5") | Symptom (5.0") | Cause (7.0") | Fix (7.5").

- Problem: Barlow SemiBold, 14 pt, `#E05C5C`
- Symptom: Inter Regular, 13 pt, `#E8A020`
- Cause: Inter Regular, 13 pt, `#F0EDE8`
- Fix: Inter Medium, 13 pt, `#27AE60`

## Design Notes

The silicate warning is the single most important differentiator between this poster and Poster #32 (Cleaning -- Alkaline). In alkaline zinc, silicated cleaners are merely undesirable. In acid zinc, they are catastrophic. The coral-bordered full-width callout must be visible from across the shop floor.

The electrocleaner column is more prominent here than in the alkaline version because acid zinc lines more frequently include a dedicated electroclean step -- the higher contamination sensitivity of the acid bath demands it.
