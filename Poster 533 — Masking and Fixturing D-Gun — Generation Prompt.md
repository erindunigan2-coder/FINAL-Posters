## Phase 4 -- Masking Requirements + Robotic Callout (HERO)

### Masking Comparison Table (Left, W: 14.5")

7-row table. Columns: Masking Type (3.5") | D-Gun Compatible? (3.0") | Reason (8.0").

| Masking Type | Compatible? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Custom-machined stainless steel | YES -- STANDARD (`#27AE60`) | Withstands detonation; reusable; precision-cut |
| Custom-machined Inconel | YES -- PREMIUM (`#27AE60`) | Superior heat/impact resistance |
| Mild steel masks | ACCEPTABLE (`#E8A020`) | Lower cost; shorter life; may deform |
| High-temperature masking tape | NO (`#E05C5C`) | Destroyed by detonation; debris contaminates |
| Silicone plugs/caps | NO (`#E05C5C`) | Shattered/displaced; debris contamination |
| Ceramic fiber tape | NO (`#E05C5C`) | Fragments under impact; fiber contamination |
| Liquid peelable maskant | NO (`#E05C5C`) | Cannot withstand detonation forces |

Header fill `#3A4055`.

Energy rationale callout (amber): `WHY METAL MASKS? D-Gun particles at 750--1000 m/s destroy soft masking. Debris becomes contaminant inclusions. Metal masks absorb impact through elastic deformation -- reusable hundreds of times.`

### "No Manual Spraying" Callout (Right, W: 8.0")

Coral-tinted glass, border 2pt `#E05C5C`. Title: `ROBOTIC OPERATION` 28pt + `MANDATORY` 24pt.

Body: D-Gun NEVER operated manually. Reasons: 130--150 dB noise, blast overpressure, 25 mm spot precision, 1--15 Hz cycle rate. All spraying by 6-axis robot, programmed traverse, remote operator, camera monitoring.

`Manual D-Gun spraying does not exist in any specification.`

### Cooling Note (below, teal accent)

Cooling air between cycles. IR pyrometer monitoring. Cool fixture and backside of part.

Six cards in 2x3 grid. Each W: 11.0", H: 2.0".

| Requirement | Accent | Details |
|---|---|---|
| PRECISION BALANCE | `#E8A020` | Dynamic balance required. 1--15 Hz pulsed loading creates vibration. |
| VIBRATION ISOLATION | `#E05C5C` | Isolating pads/spring mounts. Prevent resonance from detonation pulses. |
| LINE-OF-SIGHT ACCESS | `#27AE60` | No fixture shadowing. 25 mm spot -- even small obstructions block coverage. |
| ROTATION SPEED CONTROL | `#2EC4B6` | Synchronize rotation with frequency and traverse for uniform overlap. |

| ELECTRICAL GROUNDING | `#C8D0D8` | ESD prevention. Metal mask bonded to fixture ground. |
