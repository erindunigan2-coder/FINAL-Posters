## Phase 6 -- Acetylene Safety + Emergency Procedures

**Left -- The 15 PSIG Rule** (accent `#E05C5C`):
Acetylene becomes UNSTABLE above 15 psig. Cylinder pressure ~250 psi. Working pressure 5--15 psig max. If leak suspected: shut off at cylinder, move outdoors, soapy water only.

**Right -- Oxygen: The Invisible Accelerant** (accent `#E8A020`):
NEVER use oil on O2 fittings. Store O2 cylinders 20 ft from fuel gas. Never blow off clothing with oxygen. Propane alternative: lower flame temp (~4,530 F), less explosion risk.

**Emergency strip** (Y: 28.5" to 32.5"). 4 cards:

| Emergency | Action |
|---|---|
| FLASHBACK | Shut off torch oxygen FIRST, then fuel; inspect before relight |
| CLOTHING FIRE | Stop, drop, roll; fire blanket; call 911 |
| CYLINDER FIRE | Evacuate 50+ ft; call fire department; do NOT extinguish |
| BURN INJURY | Cool with running water 10+ min; sterile dressing; seek medical |
