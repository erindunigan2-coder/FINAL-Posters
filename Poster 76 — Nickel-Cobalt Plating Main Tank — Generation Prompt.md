## Phase 5 -- Bath Composition + Alloy Control

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY + ALLOY CONTROL`.

### Step 6 -- Four-component breakdown (Y: 15.3" to 18.0")

Four callout boxes:

**NICKEL SULFAMATE** (accent `#2EC4B6`): 300--400 g/L | Optimal 350 | Primary Ni source; low stress.

**COBALT SULFATE** (accent `#E8A020`): 10--60 g/L (CoSO4*7H2O) | Optimal 15--40 | Sole Co source; added chemically.

**BORIC ACID** (accent `#C8D0D8`): 30--45 g/L | Optimal 37--40 | pH buffer at cathode film.

**NICKEL CHLORIDE** (accent `#3A4055`): 0--15 g/L | Optimal 5--10 | Promotes anode dissolution.

### Step 7 -- Alloy control gauge (Y: 18.3" to 20.3")

Title: `CONTROLLING COBALT % IN THE DEPOSIT`.

Two horizontal bar gauges:

**Current Density:** Low CD (20 ASF) = Lower Co% `#2EC4B6` ... High CD (60 ASF) = Higher Co% `#E8A020`. Label: `Higher current density = more cobalt in deposit`.

**Temperature:** Low T (100 F) = Higher Co% `#E8A020` ... High T (150 F) = Lower Co% `#2EC4B6`. Label: `Higher temperature = less cobalt in deposit`.

Below: `Typical target: 18--25% Co by weight. Verify by XRF or wet chemistry (EDTA total metals minus DMG nickel = cobalt).`

## Phase 6 -- Defect Diagnosis Grid

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS`.

3x2 grid. Each card W: 7.33", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`, accent in defect color.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| CO% OUT OF SPEC | `#E8A020` | Bath ratio drift, temp shift, CD mismatch | Analyze metals; adjust CoSO4; verify CD + temp |
| CRACKING (HEAT TREAT) | `#E05C5C` | Too rapid ramp, excess Co, H2 damage | Slow ramp to 300 C; verify Co% <30%; pre-bake for H2 |
| ROUGH / NODULAR | `#E8A020` | Particulate, anode sludge, torn bags | Inspect/replace bags; continuous filtration |
| LOW HARDNESS | `#2EC4B6` | Co too low; organic contamination | Add CoSO4; carbon treat; XRF |
| PITTING | `#E05C5C` | Wetting agent depleted, H2, low pH | Add wetting agent; check pH; increase agitation |
| POOR THROWING POWER | `#2EC4B6` | Low NiCl2, low boric acid, high CD | Add NiCl2; replenish H3BO3; reduce CD |

## Phase 7 -- Analytical Verification + Safety

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column layout.

**Left -- Analytical Verification (accent `#27AE60`):**

| Method | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| XRF | Co% and Ni% -- fast, non-destructive |
| Wet chemistry (EDTA + DMG) | Total metals; Ni by DMG; Co by difference |
| Hull cell | Visual quality across CD range (120--140 F) |
| Vickers hardness | 400--500 HV as-plated target |

Title: `COBALT COMPOUNDS -- HEALTH HAZARD`.

- Respiratory protection for mist/dust
- Same nickel dermatitis concerns
- Local exhaust mandatory
- ACGIH TLV 0.02 mg/m3 TWA | OSHA PEL 0.1 mg/m3 TWA
