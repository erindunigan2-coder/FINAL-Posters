## Phase 5 -- Pre vs. Post E-Coat Rinse

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `TWO RINSE WORLDS -- BEFORE AND AFTER THE E-COAT TANK`.

**Left -- `PRE-E-COAT RINSE` (`#2EC4B6`):** Standard city water + DI. DI < 20 uS/cm. NO dry-off oven -- body enters e-coat WET. Highlight: `No dry-off oven. The wet surface is electrochemically active.`

**Right -- `POST-E-COAT RINSE (UF RECOVERY)` (`#E8A020`):** UF permeate (not water). 2--3 counterflow + DI final. >95% dragout recovered. >99% total utilization. Highlight: `Closed-loop recovery. Near-zero paint waste.`

## Phase 6 -- UF Parameters + Controls

Y: 21.3" to 26.3". Section label: `UF SYSTEM -- PARAMETERS AND MONITORING`.

UF Parameter Table:

| Parameter | Range | Method | Action Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| MWCO | 50,000--100,000 | Manufacturer spec | -- |
| Permeate flow | 5--20 gal/min/100 ft2 | Flow meter | < 70% baseline = foul |
| Permeate conductivity | 500--2,000 uS/cm | Meter | Rising = membrane damage |
| Trans-membrane pressure | 10--30 psi | Gauge | > 40 psi = fouling |

Three control boxes: Permeate Quality (`#2EC4B6`) | Membrane Cleaning (`#E8A020`) | DI Final Rinse (`#27AE60`).

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| WATER SPOTS ON CURED FILM | `#E05C5C` | High TDS in final rinse | Verify DI < 20 uS/cm |
| PAINT LOSS / LOW RECOVERY | `#E8A020` | UF membranes fouled | Clean or replace membranes |
| CRATERING AFTER BAKE | `#E05C5C` | Contamination in permeate | Filter permeate; find source |
| UNEVEN FILM IN CAVITIES | `#E8A020` | Insufficient rinse coverage | Verify body drainage paths |
| RISING BATH CONDUCTIVITY | `#2EC4B6` | UF not removing enough anolyte | Check anode box bleed rate |
| MEMBRANE FOULING | `#E05C5C` | Biological growth or resin buildup | Cleaning schedule; biocide |
