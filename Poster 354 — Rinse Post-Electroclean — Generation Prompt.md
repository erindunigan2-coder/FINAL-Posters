## Phase 6 -- Drag-Out Recovery

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section label: `DRAG-OUT RECOVERY -- SAME PRINCIPLE, HIGHER STAKES`.

Full-width glass card, three columns:

**Why Recover?** (`#E8A020`): Electrocleaner chemistry is expensive. Every part carries solution out. Still rinse captures 50-70%.

**Slow Withdrawal** (`#2EC4B6`): 5-10 second drain over tank. Reduces consumption 20-30% with zero capital cost.

**Air Blow-Off** (`#27AE60`): Compressed air between electroclean and rinse. Removes clingy drag-out from blind holes. Common on automatic lines.

---

## Phase 7 -- Alkaline-to-Acid Transition Warning

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Section label: `THE ALKALINE-TO-ACID HANDOFF -- DO NOT SKIP THIS`.

Full-width coral-tinted glass panel.

**Left -- When Alkaline Meets Acid:**
- Neutralizes acid (raises pH) / Reduces acid strength / Creates uneven activation / Parts have spotty acid contact / Result: poor adhesion under plate
- `This failure is INVISIBLE at the electrocleaner -- it shows up as blisters, peelers, or skip plate AFTER plating.`

**Right -- Prevention** (`#27AE60`):
1. Double or triple counterflow rinse
2. Monitor final rinse conductivity daily
3. Slow withdrawal from electrocleaner
4. Use drag-out rinse before flowing rinse
5. If conductivity rises, increase water flow before adhesion problems
- `The rinse is cheap. The rejects are not.`
