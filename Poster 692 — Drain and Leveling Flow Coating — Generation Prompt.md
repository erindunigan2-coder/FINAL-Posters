## Phase 4 -- Part Orientation / Drainage Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `PART ORIENTATION CONTROLS FILM DISTRIBUTION`. Three panels.

**Vertical (0 deg, `#E05C5C` POOR):** Graduated color: thin top (0.3 mil), target middle, heavy bottom + drip edge (2.5 mils). Maximum variation.

**Angled (15-30 deg, `#27AE60` BEST):** More uniform. High 1.8 / Low 0.8 mils. Balanced drainage. Minimal drip edge. Orient pocket to drain.

**Rotated (tumble, `#2EC4B6` SPECIALTY):** Even distribution +/- 15-20%. Only practical for small parts on rotating fixture.

## Phase 5 -- Drain Parameters Table

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `DRAIN AND FLASH PARAMETERS`.

| Parameter | Range | Increase Effect | Decrease Effect | Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drain time | 30-120 sec | Thinner, more uniform | Thicker, more runs | Timer/conveyor |
| Flash time | 5-15 min ambient | Smoother, less solvent pop | Faster, risk entrapment | Ambient + conveyor |

| Viscosity | 15-40 sec Zahn #2 | Less drainage, thicker | More drainage, thinner | Solvent per TDS |
| Part tilt angle | 15-30 deg | More directional drainage | Less directional | Fixture design |

## Phase 6 -- Thickness Variation Reality

Y: 21.3" to 26.3". Section label: `WHAT +/- 30-50% VARIATION REALLY MEANS`.

**Left -- Example 2.0 mil target:** Bar gauge: Red < 1.0 (holidays) / Yellow 1.0-1.4 / Green 1.4-2.6 (spec range) / Yellow 2.6-3.0 / Red > 3.0 (runs).

**Right -- Specification Strategy:** Specify MINIMUM DFT, not target. Measure top/mid/bottom. Accept thick bottoms. If tight tolerance needed: switch to spray. D7091: 3+ readings/zone, 3 zones/part.

Bottom `#E8A020`: `Flow coating trades precision for efficiency. 90-95% utilization but +/- 30-50% variation. For industrial primers, that trade-off is almost always worth it.`

## Phase 6B -- Failures + Insight

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 4 cards + insight.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CURTAINING / SAGS | Viscosity low, drain short, poor angle | Increase viscosity; extend drain; tilt |
| DRIP EDGE BUILDUP | Gravity pooling at lowest point | Rotate during drain; design drain points |
| HOLIDAYS ON UPWARD FACES | Horizontal surfaces shed coating | Tilt to eliminate horizontal; touch-up spray |
| SOLVENT POP IN OVEN | Insufficient flash | Extend flash; reduce tunnel temp; reduce build |

Insight `#E8A020`: `The best fixture designer in a flow coat shop is worth their weight in gold. Part orientation is the single most effective control for film distribution.`
