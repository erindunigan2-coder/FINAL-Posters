## Phase 5 -- Suspension Feed System Detail

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `THE SPS DIFFERENCE -- SUSPENSION FEED SYSTEM`.

**Left -- Feed System Types (W: 11.0"):**

*Pressurized Vessel:* Sealed 2-10 bar, consistent flow, requires agitation, preferred for production.

*Peristaltic Pump:* Positive displacement, pulsed flow (may need dampener), easier to vary rate, better for R&D.

**Right -- Suspension Preparation (W: 11.5"):**

Particle size: 50 nm-5 um (`#27AE60`). Solids loading 5-30 wt%. Carrier: ethanol (better atomization) or water (safer). Preparation: ball/bead milling. Shelf life LIMITED -- agitate before use. Ethanol requires explosion-proof handling (`#E05C5C`).
