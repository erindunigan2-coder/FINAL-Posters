## Phase 4 -- Substrate Routing Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `CLEANING SEQUENCE -- SUBSTRATE DETERMINES THE PATH`.

Two parallel paths:

**Left -- Copper/Brass Path (accent `#2EC4B6`):** 1. Alk Soak Clean (4--8 oz/gal, 140--180 F, 3--10 min) | 2. Rinse | 3. Electroclean Anodic (3--5 min, 20--50 ASF) | 4. Rinse | 5. Acid Dip (H2SO4 5--10%, 15--30 sec) | 6. Rinse -> `DIRECTLY TO SILVER STRIKE`.

**Right -- Steel Path (accent `#E8A020`):** 1. Alk Soak Clean (4--8 oz/gal, 140--160 F) | 2. Rinse | 3. Electroclean Anodic (3--5 min, 20--50 ASF) | 4. Rinse | 5. Acid Activation (HCl 10--20%, 15--30 sec) | 6. Rinse -> `MUST COPPER STRIKE FIRST -- silver will not adhere to steel directly` (Coral).

## Phase 5 -- Operating Windows

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `OPERATING WINDOWS`.

**Left -- Soak Clean (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Conc: 4--8 oz/gal (target 6) | Temp: 140--180 F (target 160) | Time: 3--10 min (target 5) | Type: non-silicated preferred.

**Right -- Electroclean (accent `#E8A020`):** Current: 20--50 ASF (target 30) | Time: 3--5 min (target 3) | Polarity: Anodic (reverse) | Temp: 140--160 F.
Note: `Anodic cleaning preferred -- cathodic deposits smut on copper/brass.`

**Water-Break-Free Banner (full width, `#27AE60`):** `THE TEST: Rinse the part. If water sheets off uniformly with no beading, the surface is clean. Any break = re-clean.`

## Phase 6 -- Substrate Compatibility

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `SUBSTRATE COMPATIBILITY -- SILVER ADHESION`.

| Substrate | Direct Silver? | Required Undercoat | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper | YES (with strike) | None | Most common |
| Brass | YES (with strike) | None | Same as copper |
| Nickel-plated | YES (with strike) | None | H2SO4 5%, 10--15 sec |
| Steel / Iron | NO | Copper strike first | Will not adhere |
| Stainless steel | NO | Nickel + copper strike | Very poor without undercoat |
| Aluminum | NO | Zincate + copper strike | Rarely plated |
| Kovar / Alloy 42 | YES (with strike) | None | Electronics |

"NO" in `#E05C5C`. "YES" in `#27AE60`.

## Phase 7 -- Common Mistakes

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `CLEANING MISTAKES THAT RUIN SILVER PLATE`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FINGERPRINTS ON CLEANED PARTS | Handling without gloves | Gloves mandatory clean to plate |
| 2 | WATER BREAKS AFTER RINSE | Silicone contamination | Change cleaner; check water |
| 3 | SMUT ON COPPER AFTER CLEAN | Cathodic electroclean | Switch to anodic |
| 4 | DELAY BETWEEN CLEAN AND PLATE | Re-oxidation | Process within 30 min |
