# Poster #550 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Wire Combustion Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Zinc fume fever is the headline hazard -- spraying zinc wire without proper respiratory protection can cause flu-like symptoms within hours. Oxy-acetylene flame means open-flame fire risk. Acetylene safety is non-negotiable (never exceed 15 PSI). This is a field-heavy process so the poster must also address outdoor/elevated work hazards that shop-based thermal spray processes don't encounter.

### ZONE 3 -- Zinc Fume Fever + Acetylene Safety

**Section label:** `TOP HAZARDS -- ZINC FUMES & ACETYLENE` -- Y: 15.7".

**Left -- BLOCK C: Zinc Fume Fever (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C` 0.06", H: 5.5".

Title: `ZINC FUME FEVER` -- Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 24 pt `#E05C5C`.
Subtitle: `The #1 Occupational Health Risk` -- Inter Medium 14 pt `#E05C5C`.

Body (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 155%):
- `Caused by inhaling zinc oxide fumes during spraying`
- `Symptoms: chills, fever, headache, muscle aches, nausea`
- `Onset: 4--12 hours after exposure`
- `Duration: 24--48 hours (self-resolving)`
- `Repeated exposure does NOT build tolerance`
- `Prevention: P100 RPE or supplied air -- NO exceptions`

Bottom callout (JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#E05C5C`):
`OSHA PEL for zinc oxide fume: 5 mg/m3 (TWA)`
`ACGIH TLV: 2 mg/m3 (TWA); 10 mg/m3 (STEL)`

**Right -- BLOCK D: Acetylene Safety (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C` 0.06", H: 5.5".

Title: `ACETYLENE SAFETY` -- Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 24 pt `#E05C5C`.
Subtitle: `Non-Negotiable Rules` -- Inter Medium 14 pt `#E05C5C`.

Body (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 155%):
- `NEVER exceed 15 PSI gauge pressure` (`#E05C5C` bold)
- `Acetylene is unstable above 15 PSI -- risk of decomposition explosion`
- `Secure cylinders UPRIGHT at all times`
- `Test all connections with soapy water -- never open flame`
- `Install flashback arrestors on both O2 and C2H2 lines`
- `Purge lines before lighting; O2 off first when shutting down`

Bottom callout (Inter Medium 13 pt `#E8A020`):
`Propane is a safer alternative fuel for many wire spray applications. Lower flame temp (~2800 degC) but eliminates acetylene decomposition risk.`

**Right -- BLOCK F: Field Work Hazards (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020` 0.06".

Title: `FIELD WORK -- ADDITIONAL HAZARDS` -- Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`.

| Hazard | Control |
|---|---|
| Elevated work (bridges, tanks) | Fall protection per OSHA 1926; scaffolding or man-lift |
| Confined spaces | Confined space entry permit; ventilation; gas monitoring |
| Wind | Can deflect spray pattern; increase fume exposure; shield work area |
| Rain / moisture | Stop work -- moisture on substrate causes flash rust and porosity |
| Public access | Barricade work area; post warning signs for fumes and noise |

Note: `Wire combustion spray is the most common field thermal spray process. Field conditions introduce hazards not present in shop work.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E8A020`.
