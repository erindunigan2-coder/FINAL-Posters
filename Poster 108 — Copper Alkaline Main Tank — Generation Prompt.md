## Phase 4 -- Dual Bath Comparison + Live Entry

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `TWO BATH TYPES -- ONE PURPOSE`.

### Side-by-Side Bath Comparison (Y: 5.0" to 11.0")

**Left -- Copper Pyrophosphate (accent `#E8A020`):**
Subtitle: `The Proven System`.

| Component | Concentration |
|---|---|
| Cu2P2O7 | 53--84 g/L |
| K4P2O7 | 200--350 g/L |
| NH4OH | 1--5 mL/L |
| KNO3 | 5--15 g/L |
| Cu metal | 22--34 g/L |
| P2O7:Cu ratio | 7:1 to 8:1 (critical) |

Key: `pH 8--9 | 100--140 F | 10--80 ASF | CE 70--90% | OFHC or phosphorized Cu anodes`.

**Right -- Copper HEDP / Chelant (accent `#27AE60`):**
Subtitle: `The Modern Replacement`.

| Component | Concentration |
|---|---|
| Cu metal | 5--30 g/L |
| HEDP chelant | 50--100 g/L |
| Auxiliary chelant | 10--30 g/L |
| NaOH or KOH | 30--80 g/L |
| Conductive salt | 15--30 g/L |

Key: `pH 9--13 (strike at 12--13) | 100--160 F | 5--30 ASF | CE 30--70% | OFHC Cu or insoluble anodes`.

### Live Entry Callout (Y: 11.5" to 14.3")

Full-width coral panel. Title: `LIVE ENTRY -- THE MOST IMPORTANT TECHNIQUE IN THIS BATH`.

**Left -- What It Means:** Current ON before part touches solution. Connect to cathode bar above bath. Apply initial strike CD (20--30 ASF pyro; 15--20 ASF HEDP). Lower part into bath with current flowing. Prevents immersion copper.

**Right -- Why It Matters:** Without current, Cu2+ displaces substrate metal on contact. Immersion copper is thin, powdery, NON-ADHERENT. Everything plated on top peels. At pH > 12 (HEDP strike), risk is lower -- but live entry is still best practice.

## Phase 5 -- Operating Parameters + Defects

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `OPERATING PARAMETERS AND COMMON DEFECTS`.

### Parameter Comparison Table

| Parameter | Pyrophosphate | HEDP / Chelant |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | 100--140 F | 100--160 F |
| pH | 8.0--9.0 | 9.0--13.0 (strike at 12--13) |
| CD | 10--80 ASF | 5--30 ASF |
| CE | 70--90% | 30--70% |
| Deposit | Semi-bright, good leveling | Matte to semi-bright |
| Plating time (strike) | 1--5 min (0.1--0.3 mil) | 1--5 min (0.1--0.3 mil) |

### Common Defects

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No adhesion on steel | pH too low; no live entry | Raise pH to 12+; ensure live entry |
| Immersion Cu (powdery) | Part entered without current at low pH | Use live entry; increase strike CD |
| Dull deposits | Low temp; depleted additives | Raise temp; replenish per TDS |
| Roughness | Particulates; orthophosphate buildup (pyro) | Filter; monitor ortho:pyro ratio |
| Poor TP (pyro) | P2O7:Cu out of range | Correct to 7:1--8:1 |

## Phase 6 -- Analytical Methods + Anode Management

Y: 20.5" to 27.0".

**Left -- Analytical Control (W: 14.0", accent `#2EC4B6`):**

| Analysis | Method |
|---|---|
| Copper metal | Iodometric (Na2S2O3/starch) or EDTA |
| Free pyrophosphate (pyro) | Acid titration |
| pH | Calibrated pH meter |
| P2O7:Cu ratio (pyro) | Calculated from Cu and P2O7 |
| Orthophosphate (pyro) | Spectrophotometric (molybdate blue) |
| Hull cell | 267 mL, 1--2A, 5--10 min |

Note: `P2O7:Cu ratio = primary control for pyro baths. For HEDP, pH and Cu metal are primary.` -- `#E8A020`

**Right -- Anodes (W: 8.0", accent `#E8A020`):**
Pyro: OFHC or phosphorized Cu, PP bags, A:C 1:1 to 2:1.
HEDP: OFHC Cu (soluble) OR insoluble (MMO/platinized Ti) -- insoluble requires Cu replenishment. PP bags on soluble anodes.

## Phase 7 -- Contamination + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Contamination:**

| Contaminant | Threshold | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Orthophosphate (pyro) | > 100 g/L | Partial dump; maintain pH > 8 and temp < 140 F |
| Iron | > 50 ppm | Carbon treatment + filtration |
| Organic | Any significant | Activated carbon 1--3 g/L |
| Chloride | > 50 ppm (pyro) | Dilute or silver chloride precipitation |
| Acid drag-in | pH drop | Improve rinse; readjust pH |

**Right -- Safety (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
NO CYANIDE -- primary safety advantage. Alkaline (pH 8--13): caustic burn at high pH. PPE: goggles, gloves, apron. Cu compounds: toxic by ingestion, aquatic toxicity. Chelants keep Cu in solution through hydroxide precipitation -- may need sulfide precip or IX. Heated bath: burn hazard up to 160 F.
