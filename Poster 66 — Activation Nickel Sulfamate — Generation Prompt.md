**Right -- Mandrel Preparation (X: 12.5", W: 11.0"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E8A020`. Title: `MANDREL PREPARATION -- NOT ACID ACTIVATION`.
- Aluminum: chromate or anodize release layer
- Steel: thin chrome flash (0.1--0.3 microns) or proprietary release agent
- Wax/epoxy: no chemical treatment; surface finish critical
- Glass: conductive coating (sputtered metal or conductive paint)
- Mandrel surface determines electroform surface quality.

## Phase 7 -- Failure Modes + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Two-column layout.

**Left -- 4 Activation Failures (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

| Failure | Root Cause | Effect in Sulfamate |
|---|---|---|
| Under-activation | Time too short, acid too dilute | Adhesion failure -- deposit peels under stress |
| Over-activation | Time too long on aerospace alloys | Intergranular attack, weakened substrate |
| Skipping Wood's strike | Assumed unnecessary on stainless/Inconel | Deposit peels immediately -- catastrophic |
| Mandrel release failure | Improper or absent release layer | Electroform cannot separate from mandrel |

**Right -- Safety (X: 15.5", W: 8.0"):**
Coral-tinted glass.
- HCl fumes: OSHA PEL 5 ppm. Ventilation mandatory.
- HF (titanium activation): extreme burn hazard. Calcium gluconate gel immediately available.
- Acid splashes: full PPE.
- Aerospace shops: follow facility-specific protocols per substrate.
