## Phase 4 -- Separation Pathway Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `SEPARATION BY MANDREL TYPE`.

Three tall panels:

**Panel 1 -- PERMANENT MANDREL (`#27AE60`):** SS, Ni, Chrome-plated.

```
METHOD 1: MECHANICAL
  Pry at edge with plastic/wood tools (never steel).
  Requires draft angle (1-3 deg).

METHOD 2: THERMAL DIFFERENTIAL
  Heat to 150-200 C. Ni CTE 13 um/m/C vs SS 16 um/m/C.
  Gap forms. Cool rapidly. Pry after 2-3 cycles.

METHOD 3: FLEX
  Thin-walled on cylindrical: flex to break release bond, slide off.

MANDREL REUSE: Inspect, re-polish, fresh release agent. Hundreds of cycles.
```

**Panel 2 -- EXPENDABLE MANDREL (`#E8A020`):** Al, Zn, Wax, Plastic.

```
ALUMINUM: 10-20% NaOH, 60-80 C. H2 gas -- ventilate!
  2Al + 2NaOH + 2H2O -> 2NaAlO2 + 3H2
ZINC: 10-20% HCl, ambient. H2 gas.
WAX: Melt or dissolve (trichloroethylene, naphtha).
3D-PRINTED POLYMER: Acetone (ABS), NaOH (PLA), or burn out > 300 C.
```

**Panel 3 -- LOW-MELT ALLOY (`#2EC4B6`):** Cerrolow, Cerrobend, Wood's Metal.

```
Heat in water bath 10-20 C above mp (47-70 C). Alloy melts and drains.
No mechanical force. Complex geometry OK. Alloy re-cast for next mandrel.
CAUTION: Wood's metal contains LEAD and CADMIUM. Cerrolow 117 = Bi-Pb-Sn-In.
Lead-free alternative: Cerrolow 136 (58 C, Bi-Sn-In).
```

**Bottom insight:** `The separation method must be chosen BEFORE mandrel fabrication. A permanent mandrel needs draft angles and release agent designed in. An expendable mandrel needs compatible dissolution chemistry. Plan the separation from the start.`

## Phase 5 -- Mechanical + Chemical Details

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Two-column layout.

**Left -- MECHANICAL SEPARATION (`#27AE60`):**

```
DRAFT ANGLE: 1-3 deg taper. No taper = no separation.

CTE VALUES:
  Nickel:    13.0 um/m/C
  SS:        16.0 um/m/C
  Aluminum:  23.0 um/m/C
  Copper:    16.5 um/m/C

Heat 150-200 C, quench cold water. Repeat 2-3x.
TOOLS: Plastic wedges, wood dowels, rubber mallets -- NEVER metal.
LUBRICATION: DI water or light oil at interface.
```

**Right -- CHEMICAL DISSOLUTION:**

| Mandrel | Chemistry | Temp | Rate | Safety |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminum | 10-20% NaOH | 60-80 C | 0.5-2 mm/hr | H2 gas; alkali burns |
| Zinc | 10-20% HCl | Ambient | Faster than Al | H2; HCl fumes |
| Wax | Heat or solvent | Varies | Min-hours | Fumes; fire risk (naphtha) |
| ABS | Acetone | Ambient | Hours | Flammable; fume hood |
| PLA | 5-10% NaOH | 60 C | Hours | Same as Al |
| Low-melt alloy | Heat > mp (47-70 C) | Water bath | Minutes | Lead/cadmium; gloves |

## Phase 6 -- Post-Separation Handling + Failures

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- RINSING PROTOCOL (`#2EC4B6`):**

After chemical dissolution: thorough DI rinse, check pH neutral. After mechanical: rinse interior gently, IPA wipe for release residue. Dry with filtered air or N2. CLEAN GLOVES ONLY on interior surface.

**Right -- INTERIOR SURFACE PROTECTION (`#E8A020`):**

```
THE INTERIOR SURFACE IS THE PRODUCT.
- Store wrapped in lint-free tissue or clean PE film
- Never stack interior-to-interior or interior-to-hard
- Mark exterior vs. interior clearly
- Reflector/waveguide interior: DO NOT TOUCH. EVER.
- Best time to inspect interior -- photograph under oblique light
```

**Separation Failures (Y: 28.5" to 32.5"):** Four cards.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CANNOT SEPARATE | Release agent failed; bonded | Thermal cycling; may need to scrap |
| INTERIOR SCRATCHED | Metal tools used | Plastic tools only; train operators |
| ELECTROFORM DISTORTED | Forced separation; stress release | Gentler technique; anneal before separation |
| INCOMPLETE DISSOLUTION | Mandrel residue in recesses | Extend time; agitate; increase temp/conc |
