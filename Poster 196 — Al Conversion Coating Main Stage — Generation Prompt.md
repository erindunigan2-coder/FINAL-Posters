## Phase 4 -- Chemical Mechanism Hero

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `DEPOSITION MECHANISM -- HOW THE COATING FORMS`.

### Step 5 -- 4-step mechanism diagram (Y: 3.8" to 12.0")

Four large rounded rects in vertical flow (W: 22.0", H: 1.8", fill `#1E2435`), connected by downward arrows (3 pt `#3A4055`).

| Step | Y | Accent | Title | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.8" | `#E8A020` | ACID ATTACK ON SUBSTRATE | Mildly acidic Zr bath (pH 3.8--5.0) dissolves substrate: Al -> Al3+ + 3e- / Fe -> Fe2+ + 2e- / Zn -> Zn2+ + 2e- |
| 2 | 6.0" | `#2EC4B6` | LOCAL pH RISE | As metal dissolves and H+ consumed (2H+ + 2e- -> H2), interface pH RISES. Bulk 3.8--5.0, interface climbs to 5.5+. |
| 3 | 8.2" | `#27AE60` | ZIRCONIUM HYDROLYSIS | Rising pH drives hydrolysis: ZrF6 2- + 2H2O -> ZrO2 + 6F- + 4H+ (simplified). Amorphous ZrO2 precipitates on surface. |
| 4 | 10.4" | `#27AE60` | FILM FORMATION | Result: thin, adherent, amorphous ZrO2, 20--100 nm. Nearly invisible. Multi-metal. No sludge. No heavy metals in waste. |

Titles: Barlow SemiBold 18 pt accent. Formulas: JetBrains Mono 13 pt. Descriptions: Inter 13 pt.

### Step 6 -- Bath chemistry composition table (Y: 12.5" to 15.3")

| Component | Concentration | Function |
|---|---|---|
| H2ZrF6 (hexafluorozirconic acid) | 50--200 ppm as Zr | Film-forming species -- source of ZrO2 |
| Free fluoride (F-) | 10--50 ppm | Activator -- dissolves native Al2O3 barrier |
| Cu2+ (optional) | 5--30 ppm | Accelerator -- Cu deposits cathodically |
| Organic polymer (some formulations) | 100--500 ppm | Co-deposited organic sealer in hybrid systems |
| pH (controlled by H2ZrF6 + alkali) | 3.8--5.0 | Critical operating range |

## Phase 5 -- Operating Parameters

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `OPERATING PARAMETERS -- SPRAY vs. IMMERSION`.

Two panels:

- Standard for rack lines and aerospace. Better coverage in recesses.

## Phase 6 -- Film Characteristics

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `FILM CHARACTERISTICS -- WHAT YOU GET`.

Two panels:

**Left -- FILM PROPERTIES** (accent `#27AE60`):

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Appearance | Clear to pale iridescent (nearly invisible) |
| Thickness | 20--100 nm (0.02--0.10 um) |
| Coating weight | 5--30 mg/m2 as Zr (XRF) |
| Structure | Amorphous ZrO2 / TiO2 |
| Salt spray -- bare | 24--72 hours |
| Salt spray -- with e-coat | 500--1500+ hours |
| Sludge generation | Negligible |
| Wastewater | No Cr, Ni, Mn -- simple treatment |

**Right -- MULTI-METAL CAPABILITY** (accent `#E8A020`):
One bath, three substrates, same reaction:
- ALUMINUM -> Al3+ -> local pH rise -> ZrO2
- STEEL -> Fe2+ -> local pH rise -> ZrO2
- GALVANIZED -> Zn2+ -> local pH rise -> ZrO2

Compared to zinc phosphate: no sludge, no heavy metals, lower energy (ambient vs. 130+ F), fewer stages (7 vs. 10+).

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | NO VISIBLE COATING | Normal -- the film IS nearly invisible | Verify by XRF (target 5--30 mg/m2 Zr); do NOT judge by eye |
| 2 | 6.33" | LOW XRF READINGS | pH too high (> 5.0); fluoride depleted | Adjust pH to 3.8--5.0; replenish fluoride; check bath |
| 3 | 12.16" | POOR PAINT ADHESION | Coating too thin; surface contamination | Increase time; improve cleaning; verify pH |
| 4 | 18.0" | INCONSISTENT READINGS | Mixed substrates; variable deox quality | Optimize per substrate; standardize deox; monitor bath age |
