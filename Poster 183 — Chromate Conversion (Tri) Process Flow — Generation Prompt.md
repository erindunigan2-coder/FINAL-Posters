## Phase 4 -- Process Flow Hero (U-Flow)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `THE COMPLETE PROCESS -- STAGE BY STAGE`.

Seven-stage U-flow: Top row 4, bottom row 3 + empty spacer note.

**Top Row:** 1. Alkaline Clean (Teal) | 2. Rinse Pre-Deox (Teal) | 3. Deoxidize/Desmut (Amber) | 4. Rinse Pre-Coat (Teal).

**Bottom Row:** 5. Tri Chromate Coat (Emerald) | 6. Rinse Post-Coat (Teal) | 7. Air Dry (Amber) | (empty spacer: `No heated cure required. Full hardness in 24 hours.` emerald).

**Box content:**
1. `Non-etch, pH 9--11 | 120--160 F | 3--10 min` -- Silicate-inhibited preferred
2. `Ambient | Double rinse standard | DI/RO preferred` -- Chloride/sulfate causes pitting
3. `HNO3 30--50% or non-chrome | HNO3/HF for high-Cu` | `Ambient, 1--5 min` -- Tri baths less forgiving than hex `#E05C5C`
4. `Ambient | Double rinse` -- < 5 min to coating bath
5. `pH 3.5--4.2 (narrow!)` | `65--95 F | 2--5 min` -- NO Cr6+ in the film -- no self-healing
6. `Ambient to 100 F | 15--60 sec | DI preferred` -- More robust than fresh hex but still handle carefully
7. `Ambient | Forced air OK | Full cure 24 hr` -- Some tri coatings tolerate up to 150 F -- check TDS

Stage legend strip: Teal, Amber, Emerald, Coral.

## Phase 6 -- Tri vs. Hex Comparison

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `TRI VS. HEX -- THE HONEST TRADE-OFF`.

**Left -- Trivalent (Type II):** Emerald accent. ZERO Cr6+ | NO self-healing | 0.02--0.10 um film | Clear to pale blue | 168 hr SST min | pH 3.5--4.2 (narrow) | More thermally stable | Very low resistance | RoHS+REACH compliant. Bottom: `Full regulatory compliance -- the direction the industry is moving.`

**Right -- Hexavalent (Type I):** Coral accent. 10--30% Cr6+ (carcinogen) | YES self-healing | 0.25--1.0 um gel | Gold to brown | 168--336 hr SST | pH 1.3--1.8 (wider) | Degrades above 140 F | REACH restricted, OSHA PEL 5 ug/m3. Bottom: `Superior self-healing -- but regulatory clock is ticking.`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| NO COATING | pH > 4.5 or fluoride depleted or surface not deoxidized | Adjust pH 3.5--4.2; add fluoride; check deox |
| WHITE HAZE | Over-immersion or pH < 3.0 or excess fluoride | Reduce time; adjust pH; check F- |
| FAILED 168 HR SST | Coating too thin or contamination or no sealer | Increase time; improve cleaning; add sealer |
| BLUE/PURPLE FILM | Zr precipitation or bath contamination | Check chemistry; filter; verify pH |
