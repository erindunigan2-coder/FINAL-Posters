# Poster #511 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Arc Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Pre-spray cleaning for arc spray. The primary arc spray substrate is structural steel -- bridges, marine structures, industrial infrastructure. The cleaning challenge is often field conditions: existing rust, mill scale, old paint (sometimes lead-containing), and weather exposure. This poster covers both shop-standard and field-expedient cleaning with special emphasis on the time window between cleaning, blasting, and spraying (AWS C2.18 specifies limits based on relative humidity).

### ZONE 3 -- Cleaning Sequence (HERO)

**Section label:** `STRUCTURAL STEEL CLEANING SEQUENCE` -- Y: 4.4".

**BLOCK B -- 5-Step Cleaning Flowchart**

Y: 5.0" to 10.5". Five vertical cards connected by arrows.

Each card: W: 23.0", H: 0.9", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6, left accent 4 pt (color varies by step).

| Step | Title | Action | Left Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | REMOVE LOOSE MATERIAL | High-pressure water blast or mechanical scraping to remove loose rust, scale, and debris | `#2EC4B6` |
| 2 | DEGREASE | Solvent wipe (acetone, MEK) or alkaline wash to remove oil, grease, and machining fluids | `#2EC4B6` |
| 3 | REMOVE OLD COATINGS | Grit blast, chemical strip, or mechanical removal of all previous paint, galvanize, or thermal spray | `#E8A020` |
| 4 | FINAL INSPECTION | Verify surface cleanliness to SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal) or SP 10 (Near-White) as specification requires | `#27AE60` |
| 5 | DRY AND PROTECT | Forced air dry; protect from moisture and re-contamination until grit blast begins | `#2EC4B6` |

Step numbers: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 24 pt accent color. Title: Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#F0EDE8`. Action: Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8` at 80%.

Arrows between cards: 2 pt `#3A4055`, filled arrowhead down.

**BLOCK C -- Contaminant Identification**

Y: 11.0" to 14.5".

Section sublabel: `WHAT KILLS ADHESION -- CONTAMINANTS TO REMOVE` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E05C5C`. Y: 11.0".

Six contaminant cards in a 3x2 grid. Each: W: 7.3", H: 1.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Card | Contaminant | Why It Must Go |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oil and grease | Prevents mechanical bond; causes porosity and delamination |
| 2 | Rust (iron oxide) | Weak oxide layer crumbles under coating; adhesion failure guaranteed |
| 3 | Mill scale | Hard oxide from hot rolling; appears intact but delaminates under thermal spray |
| 4 | Old paint / coatings | Thermal spray cannot bond to paint -- must expose bare metal |
| 5 | Moisture / condensation | Causes flash rust; creates porosity in coating; weakens bond |
| 6 | Lead paint (HAZMAT) | Must be identified and handled per EPA RRP rule; containment required during removal |

Contaminant: Barlow SemiBold 14 pt `#E05C5C`. Why: Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`.

### ZONE 5 -- Humidity and Time Windows

**Section label:** `TIME WINDOWS -- CLEAN TO BLAST TO SPRAY` -- Y: 21.2".

**BLOCK E -- AWS C2.18 Time/Humidity Reference**

Y: 21.8" to 27.0".

| Relative Humidity | Max Time: Blast to Spray | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| <40% RH | Up to 8 hours | Low moisture risk; extended window acceptable |
| 40--60% RH | 4 hours maximum | Standard practice; AWS C2.18 baseline |
| 60--80% RH | 2 hours maximum | Flash rust risk increasing; minimize delay |
| >80% RH | 1 hour or less | High flash rust risk; spray as soon as possible after blast |

Table header: fill `#3A4055`. Data rows: alternating `#1E2435` / `#252B3D`.
RH values: JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#E8A020`. Time values: JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#2EC4B6`. Rationale: Inter Regular 13 pt.

>80% row: full row highlighted with `#E05C5C` at 12% fill.

Below table:
- Callout, left accent `#E8A020`: `These are general guidelines per AWS C2.18. Some specifications are more restrictive. When in doubt, spray as soon as possible after blasting. If flash rust appears, re-blast.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E8A020`.

### ZONE 6 -- Critical Rules

**Section label:** `NON-NEGOTIABLE CLEANING RULES` -- Y: 27.7".

Four cards, W: 5.5", H: 3.0", left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Card | X | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | CLEAN BEFORE BLAST | Blasting contaminated surfaces embeds oil and grease into the profile -- you cannot blast your way through contamination |
| 2 | 6.33" | BLAST BEFORE SPRAY | Arc spray bonds by mechanical interlocking. No profile = no bond. Period. |
| 3 | 12.16" | RESPECT THE TIME WINDOW | Flash rust begins within minutes in humid conditions. If rust appears before spray, re-blast. |
| 4 | 18.0" | IDENTIFY LEAD PAINT FIRST | Lead paint removal requires containment, worker monitoring, and proper disposal per EPA. Test before you blast. |

Rule: Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#E05C5C`. Why: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`.
