## Phase 5 -- Rinse Quality + Counterflow Design

**Conductivity Table**: after clean < 500, after conversion < 50, final < 50 uS/cm. Check daily.

**Counterflow Rinse Diagram**: Fresh DI --> Rinse 3 --> overflow --> Rinse 2 --> overflow --> Rinse 1 --> drain. `Saves 50--70% water vs. independent rinses.`

---

## Phase 6 -- Dry-Off Oven + Cool-Down + Defect Grid

**Oven Parameters**: 250--300 F, 10--15 min at metal temp, recirculating fans, air knife pre-oven optional.

**The 90 F Rule** (Coral border): Parts MUST cool to < 90 F before powder booth. Hot parts cause premature melt, uneven DFT, orange peel. Verify with contact thermometer or IR gun. Exception: hot flocking for thermoplastic (400--500 F).

**Defect Grid** (3x2): osmotic blistering, water spots, moisture blistering, uneven DFT (hot parts), flash rust, poor adhesion (rinse contamination).
