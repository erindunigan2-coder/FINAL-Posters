## Phase 6 -- Cleaner Compatibility + Defects

Y: 20.5" to 26.5".

**Left -- What to Avoid (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):** label `WHAT TO AVOID` `#E05C5C`.

Three coral-accented warning cards:
1. `SILICATE CLEANERS` -- interfere with phosphate nucleation; control below 0.5% if used
2. `EXCESS CAUSTIC (NaOH > 5%)` -- passivates steel, inhibits phosphate; keep pH < 11.5
3. `ETCH INHIBITORS` -- leave surface too passive; match cleaner to process

**Right -- Cleaning Defects (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Skip areas on phosphate | Residual oil, fingerprints | Improve cleaner conc/temp/time |
| Phosphate too heavy/powdery | Over-etched surface | Reduce cleaner alkalinity |
| Staining after phosphate | Silicate residue | Switch to silicate-free cleaner |
| Uneven coating | Mixed soils, variable racking | Pre-clean heavily soiled parts |

## Phase 7 -- Monitoring & Control

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `BATH MONITORING`.

| Parameter | Method | Frequency | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free alkalinity | Titration (phenolphthalein) | Every 4 hours | Per supplier TDS |
| Total alkalinity | Titration (methyl orange) | Every 4 hours | Per supplier TDS |
| Temperature | Thermometer/thermocouple | Continuous | 120--160 F |
| pH | pH meter or strip | Daily | 9.5--11.5 |
| Concentration | Titration or refractometer | Every 4 hours | 2--8 oz/gal |
| Water-break test | Visual inspection | Every load | Complete sheeting |

Bottom: `Bath life is extended by oil skimming, overflow, and periodic dump-and-recharge. When cleaning quality drops despite correct chemistry, the bath is loaded with dissolved soils -- time to dump.`
