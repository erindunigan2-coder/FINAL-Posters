# Poster #557 -- Construction Workup
## Post-Treatment -- Wire Combustion Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Unlike SPS where post-treatment is "don't," wire combustion spray post-treatment is "absolutely must." The seal coat is what takes a porous zinc or aluminum coating and turns it into a corrosion protection system rated for decades. Without sealing, interconnected porosity allows moisture to reach the steel substrate directly. The seal coat fills the pores and creates a continuous barrier. Full paint systems can be built on top for aesthetic and additional barrier protection. This poster is the "what to do after you spray" reference.

Title: `SEAL COAT APPLICATION` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#27AE60`.

| Step | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apply while coating is still warm | Better sealer penetration into pores |
| 2 | Brush, roll, or spray application | Brush preferred for best pore penetration |
| 3 | First coat: thin, worked into surface | Force sealer into interconnected porosity |
| 4 | Allow to tack (per sealer spec) | Typically 15--60 min depending on sealer and conditions |
| 5 | Second coat if specified | Some specs require two seal coats |
| 6 | Cure per manufacturer instructions | Full cure before topcoat or service |

Note: `Apply sealer as soon as practical after spray -- while the coating is still warm from the spray process. This maximizes penetration into porosity.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#27AE60`.
