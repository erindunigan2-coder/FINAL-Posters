# Poster #302 -- Construction Workup
## Seal / Post Treatment -- Chromic Acid Anodizing (Type I)

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 8 of 8 (Stage 7 = post-anodize rinse, covered by rinse principles from Poster 300). The seal step for Type I is simpler than Type II because the thin coating has limited pore volume. Dyeing is rarely specified. The unique story here is the self-healing property -- residual Cr(VI) trapped in the sealed coating acts as a built-in corrosion inhibitor. The most common post-treatment is unsealed for paint/primer adhesion base.

### ZONE 4 -- Self-Healing Mechanism

**Section label:** `THE SELF-HEALING PROPERTY -- UNIQUE TO TYPE I` -- Y: 14.7".

Full-width callout panel, fill `#1E2435`, border 1 pt `#E8A020`, Y: 15.3" to 20.3":

**Left side (X: 1.0", W: 12.0") -- Mechanism explanation:**

Three-step sequence:

| Step | Visual Concept | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intact sealed coating | `Cr(VI) ions are trapped within the sealed anodic oxide pores. The coating provides barrier protection.` |
| 2 | Scratch through coating | `A scratch exposes bare aluminum. Corrosion would normally initiate immediately.` |
| 3 | Self-healing | `Cr(VI) leaches from adjacent pores into the scratch. It passivates the exposed aluminum, creating a protective film. No other anodize process does this.` |

Each step: Rounded rect W: 3.5", H: 4.0", fill `#252B3D`, numbered badge `#E8A020`.

**Right side (X: 14.0", W: 9.5") -- Key facts:**

- `No BSAA or Type IC replacement replicates this property.` Inter Medium 14 pt `#E05C5C`
- `The transition to BSAA trades self-healing for Cr(VI) elimination.` Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8` at 80%

## Design Notes

The four seal panels give the operator a clear decision matrix: what seal to use depends on the downstream application. The self-healing mechanism visual is the crown jewel of this poster and unique to the Type I cluster. It explains WHY the industry still tolerates Cr(VI) in specific applications -- and it is a technically beautiful concept that makes excellent poster content. The engineering trade-off (self-healing vs. Cr(VI) elimination) is stated honestly and without advocacy.
