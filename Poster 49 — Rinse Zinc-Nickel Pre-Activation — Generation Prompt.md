## Phase 5 -- Rinse Parameters + Drag-Out

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameter Table (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Water type | DI preferred; city water acceptable |
| Temperature | Ambient |
| Flow rate | 2--5 gal/min per stage |
| Stages | 2--3 stage cascade (double overflow preferred) |
| Immersion time | 30--60 sec per stage |
| Agitation | Air or part movement |
| Conductivity target | < 500 uS/cm at final stage |
| Drain time | 10--15 sec |
| Rinse criterion | No alkaline residue (pH paper check) |

**Right -- Drag-Out Callout (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):**
Title: `WHY THIS RINSE MATTERS FOR Zn-Ni` `#E8A020`.
- For plain zinc, cleaner drag-out is primarily a pH issue
- For Zn-Ni, the problem is ORGANIC drag-out -- surfactants and chelators
- Organic contaminants in the Zn-Ni bath shift alloy composition unpredictably
- A shift from 14% Ni to 10% Ni can cut salt spray performance in half
- Good rinsing removes both alkaline and organic residue

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-ACTIVATION RINSE`.

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alkaline carry-over | Acid activation neutralized | Insufficient rinse | Add cascade stage; increase flow |
| Organic carry-over | Alloy Ni% shifts out of spec | Surfactant/chelator drag-out | Improve rinse; use low-residue cleaner |
| High conductivity | Parts still alkaline | Rinse water exhausted | Monitor conductivity; dump and refill |
| Foam in rinse | Surfactant contamination | High-foam cleaner | Switch to low-foam cleaner; add defoamer |

## Phase 7 -- Water Quality + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Section: `WATER QUALITY & SAFETY`.

**Left -- Water Quality (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Title: `WATER QUALITY MATTERS` `#2EC4B6`.
- DI water: < 10 uS/cm -- best for critical parts
- City water: 200--600 uS/cm -- acceptable for most Zn-Ni work
- Hard water: > 300 ppm CaCO3 -- risk of spotting
- Monitor final rinse conductivity daily
- Dump and refill when conductivity exceeds 1000 uS/cm

**Right -- Safety (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
Title: `SAFETY NOTES` `#E8A020`.
- Rinse water is mildly alkaline from drag-out
- Overflow to waste treatment -- not down the drain
- Wet floors: slip hazard
- pH check rinse water periodically -- near neutral (6--8)
