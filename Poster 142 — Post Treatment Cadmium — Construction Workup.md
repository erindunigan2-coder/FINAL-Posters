# Poster #142 -- Construction Workup
## Post Treatment -- Cadmium

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stages 7 and 8 of 8. This poster covers BOTH chromate conversion coating and hydrogen embrittlement bake -- the two post-treatment steps for cadmium plating. The HE bake is the most life-safety-critical step in the entire cluster. Delayed or omitted baking of cadmium-plated high-strength steel has caused fatal aircraft component failures. This is not hyperbole -- it is documented history.

### ZONE 5 -- AMS 2759/9 + Bake Rules

**Section label:** `BAKE SPECIFICATIONS -- AMS 2759/9 AND ASTM B850` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK E -- Two-Panel (Y: 21.3" to 26.3")**

**Left -- AMS 2759/9 (High-Strength Steel):**
- Rounded rect, W: 11.0", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`
- Title: `AMS 2759/9` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E05C5C`
- `Applies to: Steel >200 ksi (>1380 MPa) tensile strength`
- `Temperature: 375 +/- 25 F`
- `Time: 12--24 hours (strength-dependent)`
- `Start bake: Within 1 hour of plating`
- `Verification: ASTM F519 notch tensile test`

**Right -- ASTM B850 (General):**
- Rounded rect, W: 11.5", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `ASTM B850` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`
- `Applies to: General hydrogen embrittlement relief`
- `Temperature: 375 +/- 25 F`
- `Time: 3 hours minimum (lower-strength steels)`
- `Start bake: Within 4 hours of plating`
- `Less stringent than AMS 2759/9 -- used for non-aerospace`

Bottom note: `When in doubt, use the MORE stringent specification. Over-baking is not harmful. Under-baking can be fatal.` Inter Medium 14 pt `#27AE60`
