# Poster #219 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- EN (Low Phos) -- Pre-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 4 of the EN Low-P process. This is the most critical rinse in the entire EN process line. Chloride drag-in from HCl activation causes pitting in the EN bath. Chromate drag-in from plastic activation etching poisons the bath at ppm levels. For zincated aluminum, the rinse must be fast -- the zinc layer begins to oxidize immediately, and oxidized zinc will not initiate EN deposition.

Hero visual: cascade rinse with contamination callout icons showing the three critical drag-in threats.

**Right -- Contamination Threshold Table (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Title: `WHAT KILLS AN EN BATH -- DRAG-IN THRESHOLDS` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E05C5C`

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect in EN Bath |
|---|---|---|
| Chloride (Cl-) | > 5 ppm | Pitting; accelerated bath aging |
| Chromate (Cr6+) | > 0.5 ppm | Stabilizer poisoning; bath goes inert |
| Zinc (from zincate) | Slight OK | Excessive = zinc contamination |
| Sulfate (from H2SO4) | > 50 ppm | Reduces deposition rate |
| Organic (from cleaner) | Trace | Pitting; bath instability |

### ZONE 5 -- Common Problems & Fixes

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-PLATE RINSE` -- Y: 21.2".

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chloride carry-over | Pitting in EN deposit | HCl activation not rinsed out | Switch to DI rinse; add counterflow stage |
| Chromate poisoning | EN bath stops plating entirely | Trace Cr6+ from plastic etch line | Separate rinse system; DI rinse; test with test strips |
| Skip plating on Al | EN does not initiate on aluminum | Zincate layer oxidized during slow rinse | Minimize rinse time; transfer to EN within 30 sec |
| Watermarks | Stains on deposit surface | Parts air-dried between rinse and EN bath | Keep parts wet at all times during transfer |

### ZONE 6 -- Zincate Timing + Safety

- `RULE: Transfer from pre-plate rinse to EN bath within 30 seconds.`
