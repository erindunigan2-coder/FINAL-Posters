## Phase 5 -- Rinse Parameters + Drag-Out

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameters:** DI preferred | Ambient | 2--5 gal/min | 2--3 cascade | 30--60 sec (steel/brass); 20--30 sec (zinc DC) | < 500 uS/cm | pH 6--8.

**Right -- Why This Rinse Matters (accent `#E8A020`):** Alkaline drag-in neutralizes activation acid. On zinc DC, residual alkaline film creates uneven etching -> adhesion failure. For steel, acid wastes energy on neutralization. For zinc DC: rinse quickly, move to activation fast.

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alkaline carry-over | Acid neutralized | Insufficient stages | Add cascade; increase flow |
| Zinc oxidation in rinse | White haze on zinc DC | Prolonged water contact | Reduce rinse time (20--30 sec) |
| Spotting/staining | Mineral deposits | Hard water | Switch to DI |
| Foam in rinse | Surfactant carry-over | High-foam cleaner | Low-foam cleaner; defoamer |
