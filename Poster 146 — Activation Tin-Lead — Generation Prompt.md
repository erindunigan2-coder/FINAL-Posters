## Phase 5 -- Substrate Activation Table

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `ACTIVATION BY SUBSTRATE`.

| Substrate | Acid | Concentration | Temp | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Copper / Brass | MSA | 5--10% v/v | Ambient | 15--30 sec | Preferred for MSA plating bath |
| Copper / Brass | HBF4 | 5--10% v/v | Ambient | 15--30 sec | Legacy fluoborate bath lines |
| Nickel-plated | MSA or H2SO4 | 5% v/v | Ambient | 10--15 sec | Light touch -- protect Ni layer |
| Steel (with Cu strike) | H2SO4 | 5--15% v/v | Ambient | 15--60 sec | Rare -- steel gets Cu strike first |

Note below: `Match your activation acid to your plating bath acid. MSA bath = MSA activation. This eliminates cross-contamination risk and simplifies waste treatment.` `#E8A020`

## Phase 6 -- Over vs. Under Activation

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `GET IT RIGHT -- OVER VS. UNDER`.

**Left -- Under-Activated (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`
- Title: `UNDER-ACTIVATED` `#E05C5C`
- Symptoms: oxide remains, poor adhesion, blistering at reflow, solderability failure, peel test failure
- Visual cue: `Looks OK wet -- fails at reflow` `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Over-Activated (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `OVER-ACTIVATED` `#E8A020`
- Symptoms: etched copper, grainy deposit, dissolved Cu contaminates bath, immersion deposits, dimensional loss
- Visual cue: `Visible etch on copper = too long or too strong` `#E8A020`

Center verdict: `The window is narrow: 15--30 seconds in 5--10% acid. Set a timer. Every time.` `#27AE60`

## Phase 7 -- Acid Selection

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `CHOOSE YOUR ACTIVATION ACID`.

Three side-by-side boxes:

**Left -- MSA (X: 0.5", W: 7.33"):** accent `#27AE60`. Modern choice, matches MSA bath, biodegradable, zero cross-contamination. Verdict: `USE THIS for MSA solder lines` `#27AE60`.

**Center -- Fluoboric Acid (X: 8.16", W: 7.33"):** accent `#E8A020`. Legacy, matches fluoborate bath, fluoride wastewater, being phased out. Verdict: `Legacy lines only` `#E8A020`.

**Right -- Sulfuric Acid (X: 15.83", W: 7.67"):** accent `#2EC4B6`. Universal, sulfate drag-in risk for MSA bath, OK for Ni-plated, lowest cost. Verdict: `OK for Ni-plated; avoid for Cu into MSA bath` `#2EC4B6`.
