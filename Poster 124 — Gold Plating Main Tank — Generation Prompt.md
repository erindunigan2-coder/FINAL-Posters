## Phase 4 -- Plating Tank Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE ACID HARD GOLD PLATING TANK`.

Tank cross-section (W: 21.0", H: 7.5", fill `#252B3D`, border 3pt `#C8D0D8`).

**Anodes:** Platinized titanium (Pt/Ti), fill `#3A4055`. Label: `INSOLUBLE -- gold is NOT replenished from anodes`.

**Cathode:** Fill `#E8A020` at 30%. Label: `CATHODE (WORKPIECE)` / `Au+ depositing as metallic Au + Co/Ni`.

**"GOLD REPLENISHMENT" callout:** `GOLD ADDED BY CHEMICAL ADDITION` / `KAu(CN)2 or gold sulfite salt -- never from soluble anodes`.

**Bath parameters (right):** `Au: 8--12 g/L (1.1--1.6 oz/gal)` / `Hardener (Co or Ni): 1.0--2.0 g/L` / `Citric acid/citrate: 80--120 g/L` / `pH: 4.0--4.5`.

**Left side:** `Temp: 100--120 F` / `CD: 5--10 ASF (rack) / 1--3 ASF (barrel)` / `Cathode eff: 30--50%` / `A:C ratio: 1:1 to 3:1` / `Agitation: Mild -- no excessive air`.

Bottom callout: `Cathode efficiency 30--50% is normal for acid hard gold. Do not increase CD to compensate.`

## Phase 5 -- Bath Composition + Gold Tracking

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY + GOLD TRACKING`.

**Four component boxes (row):**
GOLD (`#E8A020`): 8--12 g/L / KAu(CN)2 or sulfite / Replenish by amp-hr + analysis / Below 4 g/L: rough, dark.
HARDENER (`#2EC4B6`): Co 1.0--2.0 g/L / Controls hardness 130--200 HK / Low = soft, high = brittle.
BUFFER (`#F0EDE8`): Citric acid 80--120 g/L / Buffers cathode film pH.
pH (`#27AE60`): 4.0--4.5 optimal / Below 3.5: rough / Above 5.0: complex instability / Adjust with citric (down) or KOH (up).

**Gold Tracking Gauge (full width):** Title: `GOLD ACCOUNTABILITY`. `Daily analysis | Track amp-hours | Theoretical: 122.5 g Au / 1000 amp-min | GOLD LEDGER`. Note (Coral): `If you are not analyzing gold daily, you are guessing. In gold plating, guessing is hemorrhaging.`

## Phase 6 -- Defect Diagnosis Grid

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS`.

3x2 grid:

| Pos | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | SOFT DEPOSIT | `#2EC4B6` | Low hardener, low CD | Analyze hardener; adjust CD |
| R1C2 | DARK / DISCOLORED | `#E05C5C` | Metallic contamination, organics | Dummy plate; carbon treat |
| R1C3 | ROUGH / NODULAR | `#E8A020` | Low gold, particulate | Replenish Au; increase filtration |
| R2C1 | POOR ADHESION | `#E05C5C` | Ni oxidized, bad activation | Re-activate; reduce transfer time |
| R2C2 | HIGH CONTACT RESISTANCE | `#E8A020` | Excess hardener, organic | Reduce hardener; carbon treat |
| R2C3 | POROSITY | `#E05C5C` | Thin deposit, low Au | Increase thickness; replenish Au |
