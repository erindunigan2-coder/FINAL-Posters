## Phases 5-7 -- Content Zones

Standard Type II parameters: H2SO4 150--200 g/L, 64--72 F (tight control), 12--18 ASF.

Key: temperature MUST stay at low end (64--68 F) for maximum clarity. Higher temp = haze.

Film thickness: 8--15 um typical. Thinner = clearer. Thicker = more durable but hazier.

Clarity vs protection tradeoff: the fundamental tension of bright anodizing.
