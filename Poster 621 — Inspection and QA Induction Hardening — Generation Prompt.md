| Card | Method | Accent | Standard | Measures | When Required |

## Phase 5 -- Pattern Verification

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `PATTERN VERIFICATION -- THE INDUCTION-SPECIFIC INSPECTION`.

**Left -- Acid Etch Process (Teal):**

1. Section part through hardened zone (destructive -- use coupon/sacrifice part). 2. Mount and polish cross-section. 3. Etch: 10% ammonium persulfate or 5% nital. 4. Hardened zone appears DARK against lighter core. 5. Measure: pattern location, depth, transition zone, symmetry.

**Right -- Pattern Defects (Coral):**

| Defect | What You See | Likely Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Asymmetric pattern | One side deeper | Part off-center; uneven gap |
| Pattern too shallow | Thin dark zone | Freq too high; power too low; time too short |
| Pattern too deep | Exceeds spec | Freq too low; time too long; through-hardened |
| Sharp transition | Abrupt boundary | Quench too severe at boundary; may crack |
| Unintended hardening | Dark zone beyond target | Stray flux; coil design issue |
| No pattern visible | Uniform etch | Through-hardened or not hardened at all |

## Phase 6 -- Defects Table + First-Article

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Common Defects:**

| Defect | Cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Soft spots | Off-center; decarb; scale | Center part; verify stock; remove scale |
| Through-hardening | Freq too low; power too high; time too long | Increase freq; reduce power/time |
| Cracking | Too-rapid quench; deep case; sharp corners | Reduce severity; radius transitions |
| Non-uniform pattern | Coil design; gap variation | Redesign coil; verify fixturing |
| Overheating (grain growth) | Power too high; dwell too long | Reduce power; verify control |
| OD growth out of spec | Excessive martensite volume | Reduce case depth; adjust tolerance |

Defect in `#E05C5C`. Remedy in `#27AE60`.

**Right -- First-Article Inspection (Emerald):**

`FIRST-ARTICLE INSPECTION -- NON-NEGOTIABLE`. Induction is process-dependent -- result depends on exact coil position, gap, power, time, quench. UNLIKE furnace (entire load same conditions), induction parameters drift: coil wear, fixture wear, power drift, quench concentration changes.

Protocol: 1. Run first part with production params. 2. Section + acid etch for pattern. 3. Microhardness traverse for case depth. 4. Surface hardness 3+ locations. 5. MPI for cracks. 6. Dimensional check. 7. APPROVE before production.

Frequency: every new setup, coil change, power supply maintenance, new material lot.
