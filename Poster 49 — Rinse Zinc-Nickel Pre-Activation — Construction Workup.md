# Poster #49 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Zinc-Nickel -- Pre-Activation

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of the zinc-nickel process. Watson's brief says "Double overflow preferred." Same fundamental rinse as Posters #33 and #41 -- strip alkaline cleaner residues before acid activation. The cascade rinse hero and drag-out callout follow the established pattern.

For Zn-Ni specifically: inadequate rinsing here means cleaner residue survives into the activation and ultimately into the Zn-Ni bath, where it disrupts alloy composition. The standard is higher than plain zinc.

### ZONE 4 -- Rinse Parameters + Drag-Out

**Section label:** `RINSE PARAMETERS` -- Y: 14.7".

**Left -- Parameter Table (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Water type | DI preferred; city water acceptable |
| Temperature | Ambient |
| Flow rate | 2--5 gal/min per stage |
| Stages | 2--3 stage cascade (double overflow preferred) |
| Immersion time | 30--60 sec per stage |
| Agitation | Air or part movement |
| Conductivity target | < 500 uS/cm at final stage |
| Drain time | 10--15 sec |
| Rinse criterion | No alkaline residue (pH paper check) |

**Right -- Drag-Out Callout (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):**

Title: `WHY THIS RINSE MATTERS FOR Zn-Ni` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`

Body:
- `For plain zinc, cleaner drag-out is primarily a pH issue.`
- `For Zn-Ni, the problem is ORGANIC drag-out -- surfactants and chelators from the cleaner.`
- `Organic contaminants in the Zn-Ni bath shift the alloy composition unpredictably.`
- `A shift from 14% Ni to 10% Ni can cut salt spray performance in half.`
- `Good rinsing removes both alkaline residue AND organic residue.`

### ZONE 5 -- Common Problems & Fixes

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-ACTIVATION RINSE` -- Y: 21.2".

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alkaline carry-over | Acid activation neutralized | Insufficient rinse | Add cascade stage; increase flow |
| Organic carry-over | Alloy Ni% shifts out of spec | Surfactant/chelator drag-out from cleaner | Improve rinse; use low-residue cleaner |
| High conductivity | Parts still alkaline | Rinse water exhausted | Monitor conductivity; dump and refill |
| Foam in rinse | Surfactant contamination | High-foam cleaner | Switch to low-foam cleaner; add defoamer |

## Design Notes

The differentiation from Posters #33 and #41 is in the copy, not the layout. The rinse stage is physically identical. But the consequences of failure are different for Zn-Ni: organic drag-out matters more than alkaline pH drag-out because it disrupts alloy composition. The tagline and the drag-out callout carry this message.
