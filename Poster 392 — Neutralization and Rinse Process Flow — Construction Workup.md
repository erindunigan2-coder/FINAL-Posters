# Poster #392 -- Construction Workup
## Neutralization & Rinse -- Process Flow

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This is the cluster overview poster for CT-08: Neutralization & Rinse Systems. This cluster covers the waste management and water quality side of metal finishing -- the interface between the plating line and the environment. The hero concept is the counterflow cascade rinse: fresh water enters at the cleanest stage and flows counter to part movement, achieving 90-95% water reduction vs. single running rinse. The rinse ratio math (R_total = r^N) is the most powerful water conservation formula in the industry.

Design philosophy: a clear process flow showing the full waste treatment path, the counterflow principle as a hero diagram, rinse architecture comparison, and discharge compliance awareness. This is the "map" poster for the entire cluster.

### ZONE 4 -- Rinse Ratio Math + Key Principles

**Dimensions:** Y: 22.0" to 28.5" (~6.5" tall).

**Two-column layout:**

**Left -- Rinse Ratio Formula (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Section label: `THE RINSE RATIO` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

Rounded rect, H: 6.0", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`, radius 6.

- Formula: `R_total = r^N` JetBrains Mono 28 pt `#E8A020`
- Where:
  - `R_total = total dilution ratio` JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#F0EDE8`
  - `r = rinse ratio per stage` JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#F0EDE8`
  - `N = number of counterflow stages` JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#F0EDE8`

- Worked example (callout box within, fill `#252B3D`):
  - `Example: 3-stage counterflow, r = 10 per stage`
  - `R_total = 10^3 = 1,000:1 dilution`
  - `Target: 1,000:1 to 10,000:1 for most plating` `#27AE60`

- Key insight: `Adding one more counterflow stage multiplies your dilution by r -- not adds to it. This is exponential, not linear.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E8A020`

**Right -- Key Design Principles (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Section label: `DESIGN PRINCIPLES` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

Five principle cards stacked vertically. Each: Rounded rect, H: 1.1", fill `#1E2435`, left accent 0.06".

| Principle | Accent |
|---|---|
| Counterflow is ALWAYS better than parallel single rinses | `#27AE60` |
| Reduce drag-out FIRST -- slow withdrawal, air knife, drain time 10--15 sec | `#E8A020` |
| Drag-out volume: 50--200 mL per m2 of part surface | `#2EC4B6` |
| Conductivity monitoring is the cheapest quality tool in the shop | `#E8A020` |
| NEVER share drains between cyanide and acid rinse streams | `#E05C5C` |
