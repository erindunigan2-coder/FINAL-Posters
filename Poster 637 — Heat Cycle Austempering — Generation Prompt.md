## Phase 4 -- Austenitizing Parameters Hero

Y: 4.2" to 13.5". Section label: `AUSTENITIZING PARAMETERS -- STEEL vs. ADI`. Side-by-side panels.

**Left -- Steel**: 1525--1600 F, 30--90 min, endo gas or N2, must exceed Ac3 by 50--100 F. Common grades: 1065--1095 at 1475--1550 F, 4150/4340 at 1525--1575 F, 5160/6150 at 1500--1575 F.

**Right -- ADI (Ductile Iron)**: 1550--1650 F, 60--120 min (longer -- carbon dissolves from graphite nodules), min 80% nodularity per ASTM A897. Higher austenitize temp = more retained austenite = higher ductility.

Key: `ADI holds longer because carbon must dissolve from graphite nodules into austenite.`

---

## Phase 5 -- Section Size Limitations

Y: 13.5" to 20.5". Section label: `SECTION SIZE -- THE HARD LIMIT`. 5-row table: plain carbon ~0.200", alloy spring 0.5", medium-carbon alloy 1.0--1.5", high-alloy bearing 0.5--0.75", ductile iron (ADI) 3--4".

Warning: `If the core cannot cool fast enough to avoid the pearlite nose, austempering WILL NOT WORK.`

## Phase 6 -- TTT Concept + Cycle Timeline

**TTT Diagram** (Y: 20.5" to 27.5"): Austenite region, pearlite nose (Coral, DO NOT ENTER), bainite bay (Emerald, 400--750 F), Ms line (Amber). Three cooling curves: thin/high-alloy clears nose (Emerald), borderline grazes (Amber), thick/low-alloy hits nose (Coral).

**Complete Cycle Timeline** (Y: 27.5" to 32.5"): 5-segment horizontal strip: heat-up (Amber, 30--60 min) --> austenitize (Amber, 30--90 min) --> transfer (Coral, < 15 sec) --> salt bath hold (Emerald, 30--120 min) --> air cool/wash (Teal). Total: 2--5 hours. `NO TEMPER STEP -- bainite is the final structure.`
