## Phase 5 -- Quench Media Specifications

Y: 14.0" to 21.5". Side-by-side specification tables.

**Martempering Salt Bath** (Amber): 50/50 NaNO2/KNO3, 350--600 F, H = 0.30--0.50, uniformity +/-5 F.
**Marquench Oil** (Teal): proprietary petroleum, 250--400 F, flash point must exceed operating by 50+ F, H = 0.20--0.35, uniformity +/-10 F.

## Phase 6 -- H-Factor Table + Media Selection

**H-Factor Comparison** (Y: 21.5" to 28.0"): 10-row table from still air (0.02) through agitated water (1.0--1.5) with visual proportional bars. Martempering salt and marquench oil rows highlighted with Emerald accent.

**Media Selection Guide** (Y: 28.0" to 32.5"): Choose SALT when: max uniformity, temp > 400 F, highest H-factor needed. Choose HOT OIL when: no salt infrastructure, lower cost, salt cleanup problematic, lower severity acceptable.
