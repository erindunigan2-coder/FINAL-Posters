## Phase 5 -- Contaminant Impact on Hardcoat Bath

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `WHAT DRAG-IN DOES TO THE HARDCOAT BATH`.

2x2 grid:

| Contaminant | Effect | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| FLUORIDE (F-) `#E05C5C` | Catastrophic pitting at high CD. Attacks barrier layer. | < 5 ppm |
| CHLORIDE (Cl-) `#E05C5C` | Pitting corrosion. Accelerated at near-freezing temp. | < 25 ppm |
| SULFATE / ACID `#E8A020` | Raises acid concentration. Softens coating. | Monitor SG |
| ORGANICS `#E8A020` | Foaming. Disrupts current distribution. Burning at foam. | No visible foam |

## Phase 6 -- Conductivity Targets + DI vs City Water

Y: 22.0" to 28.5".

**Left -- Conductivity Gauge:**
- 0--50 uS/cm: AEROSPACE / CRITICAL `#27AE60`
- 50--100 uS/cm: COMMERCIAL HARDCOAT `#2EC4B6`
- 100--200 uS/cm: CAUTION -- MARGINAL `#E8A020`
- >200 uS/cm: FAIL -- DO NOT PROCEED `#E05C5C`

**Right -- DI vs City Water:**
DI Water (`#27AE60`): `0.1--5 uS/cm` / Zero chlorides/fluorides / MANDATORY for final stage.
City Water (`#E05C5C`): `100--500+ uS/cm` / Contains Cl- (10--50 ppm typical) / Acceptable for Stage 1 ONLY.

## Phase 7 -- Key Rules Strip

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `TYPE III PRE-ANODIZE RINSE -- KEY RULES`.

Four cards:
1. TRIPLE CASCADE -- Three tanks minimum. Two is marginal. One is unacceptable.
2. DI WATER FINAL STAGE -- City water = chloride contamination.
3. CONDUCTIVITY MONITOR -- Check every load. < 50 uS/cm for aerospace.
4. HF RINSE-OUT -- If HF desmut was used, add extra rinse. Fluoride is the #1 pitting contaminant.
