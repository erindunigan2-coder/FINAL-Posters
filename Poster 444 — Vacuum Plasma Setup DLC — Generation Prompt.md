## Phase 5 -- Ion Etch Parameters

Y: 21.8" to 26.8".

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Gas | Ar (argon) |
| Pressure | 1--5 mTorr |
| Substrate bias | -400 to -800 V (PECVD) / -800 to -1200 V (arc) |
| Duration | 5--20 min |
| Mechanism | Ar+ ions bombard substrate, sputtering away surface oxide and contaminants |
| Result | Clean, activated metal surface with micro-roughness for interlayer adhesion |
| Temperature rise | Monitor -- bias heating can raise substrate 50--100 C |

Callout: `The Ar+ ion etch is the single most important adhesion step in the entire DLC process. It removes the invisible native oxide that blocks bonding between the interlayer and substrate. Insufficient etch time or low bias voltage = delamination within days.`
