# Poster #126 -- Construction Workup
## Post Treatment -- Gold

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stages 7--8 of 8. Post-treatment for gold plating is simpler than most processes -- gold does not tarnish, corrode, or grow whiskers. The main steps are a hot DI water rinse (for spot-free drying), forced air or vacuum drying, and final inspection. For functional gold (electronics, connectors), thickness measurement and contact resistance testing are the critical quality gates. For jewelry gold, anti-tarnish or lacquer may be applied. This poster covers all post-treatment paths and the inspection protocols that prove the gold deposit meets spec.

### ZONE 4 -- Thickness Measurement

**Section label:** `GOLD THICKNESS MEASUREMENT -- THREE METHODS` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Three-Method Comparison**

Y: 15.3" to 20.0". Three callout boxes.

**Method 1 -- XRF (X-Ray Fluorescence):**
- Rounded rect, X: 0.5", W: 7.33", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#27AE60`
- Title: `XRF` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#27AE60`

Content:
- `Non-destructive`
- `Fast: 10--30 sec per reading`
- `Measures Au + Ni + substrate simultaneously`
- `Most common method in production`
- `Accuracy: +/- 5--10% for thin films`
- `Requires calibration standards`

**Method 2 -- Beta Backscatter:**
- Rounded rect, X: 8.0", W: 7.33", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `BETA BACKSCATTER` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`

Content:
- `Non-destructive`
- `Uses radioactive isotope source`
- `Good for Au on Ni on Cu (multi-layer)`
- `Slower than XRF`
- `Better accuracy on very thin films`
- `Requires radiation safety program`

**Method 3 -- Gravimetric (Strip and Weigh):**
- Rounded rect, X: 15.5", W: 8.0", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`
- Title: `GRAVIMETRIC` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#2EC4B6`

Content:
- `Destructive -- dissolves the gold`
- `Weigh -> strip Au -> reweigh -> calculate`
- `Most accurate absolute method`
- `Used for calibration of XRF/beta`
- `Slow, labor-intensive`
- `ASTM B488 reference method`

## Design Notes

Gold post-treatment is simpler than most processes -- gold does not need anti-tarnish, reflow, or passivation. The value of this poster is in the inspection and specification content. The thickness measurement comparison gives quality engineers the method selection guidance they need. The specifications table is the most referenced content on the poster -- operators and QE staff will check it daily. The application matrix differentiates soft gold (wire bonding) from hard gold (connectors) -- two completely different products with different post-treatment needs. The "gold does not tarnish" tagline reframes post-treatment from protection to verification.
