## Phase 4 -- Three-Family Parameters Hero

Y: 5.0" to 15.0". Section label: `THREE COATING FAMILIES -- APPLICATION PARAMETERS`. Three columns.

**Plastisol (`#E8A020`):** Bath ambient--120 F. Preheat none or 200--300 F. Immersion 2--30 sec. Withdrawal 2--12 in/sec. DFT 5--40 mils. Primary control: DIP TIME + VISCOSITY.

**Hot-Dip Thermoplastic (`#2EC4B6`):** Fluidized bed ambient. Part preheat 400--600 F. Immersion 2--10 sec. DFT 8--25+ mils. Primary control: PART TEMPERATURE + DIP TIME.

**Solution Dip (`#27AE60`):** Bath ambient--100 F. No preheat. Immersion 1--10 sec. Withdrawal 1--6 in/sec (SLOWER = THINNER). DFT 0.5--3 mils. Multiple dips common. Primary control: WITHDRAWAL SPEED + VISCOSITY. Landau-Levich equation governs.

## Phase 5 -- Thickness Control Mechanisms

Y: 16.3" to 21.3". Section label: `WHAT CONTROLS YOUR FILM THICKNESS`.

**Left -- Control Variable Summary:**

| Family | Primary | Secondary | Measure With |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plastisol | Dip time | Viscosity, preheat | Micrometer/DFT gauge |
| Hot-dip | Part temperature | Dip time | IR thermometer + micrometer |
| Solution | Withdrawal speed | Viscosity, solids | DFT gauge/micrometer |

**Right -- `VISCOSITY -- THE UNIVERSAL SECONDARY CONTROL`:** Plastisol: particle size, plasticizer ratio. Solution: solids content, solvent additions. Zahn cup or Brookfield. Solvent evaporation raises viscosity over time.

## Phase 6 -- Drainage and Drip Control

Y: 22.3" to 26.3". Two-column.

**Left -- `BATCH PARTS -- DRAIN AND ROTATE`:** 10--60 sec drain. Rotate/invert. Part orientation on rack matters.

**Right -- `CONTINUOUS WIRE/CABLE -- AIR KNIVES`:** 100--1,000+ ft/min. Air knives or die strippers. Precise, uniform. Critical for electrical insulation.

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| DRIP MARKS / CURTAINING | `#E05C5C` | Fast withdrawal or high viscosity | Reduce speed; lower viscosity |
| THIN SPOTS / BARE AREAS | `#E8A020` | Air pockets, low viscosity | Slower immersion; raise viscosity |
| BRIDGING | `#E05C5C` | Coating spans openings | Reduce viscosity; increase drain |
| UNEVEN HOT-DIP | `#E8A020` | Non-uniform part temperature | Extend preheat soak |
| PLASTISOL TOO THICK | `#2EC4B6` | Excessive dip time or viscosity | Reduce time; lower viscosity |
| WIRE ECCENTRICITY | `#2EC4B6` | Wire off-center through die | Align guides; center die |
