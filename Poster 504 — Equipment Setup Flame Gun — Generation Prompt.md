## Phase 5 -- Gas Supply + Startup Checklist

### Gas Supply (Y: 15.5"-22.0")

Section label: `GAS SUPPLY -- PRESSURE SETTINGS`.

5-row table:

| Gas | Regulated Pressure | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Oxygen (O2) | 15-40 PSI | Standard welding-grade O2 |
| Acetylene (C2H2) | 10-15 PSI | NEVER exceed 15 PSI -- unstable above this |
| Propane (alternative) | 10-20 PSI | Lower flame temp; wider safety margin |
| Compressed air (atomizing) | 40-80 PSI (wire gun) | Oil-free, dry air required |
| Compressed air (carrier) | 20-40 PSI (powder gun) | Entrains powder into flame |

Acetylene row: highlighted `#E05C5C` at 15% fill. Pressure values: JetBrains Mono `#E8A020`.

Below table -- safety callout (accent `#E05C5C`): `ACETYLENE SAFETY: Flash-back arrestors on BOTH O2 and C2H2 lines. Check with soapy water before every shift. Store acetylene upright. Never use copper fittings (forms explosive copper acetylide).`

### Startup Checklist (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `PRE-SPRAY STARTUP CHECKLIST`. 8 steps in 2 columns.

| Step | Action | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect gun for damage, worn tips, blocked orifices | Replace worn parts before lighting |
| 2 | Connect gas lines; verify flash-back arrestors | Both O2 and fuel lines |
| 3 | Open cylinders slowly; set regulators to spec | O2 first, then fuel gas |
| 4 | Leak-check all fittings with soapy water | Zero bubbles |
| 5 | Load wire spool or fill powder hopper | Correct feedstock material and size |
| 6 | Set atomizing air or carrier gas pressure | Per parameter spec |
| 7 | Light flame per OEM procedure; adjust to neutral | Neutral = equal inner cones |
| 8 | Test fire on scrap coupon | Verify pattern, adhesion, no spitting |

Step numbers: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#2EC4B6`. Verify: JetBrains Mono `#27AE60`.

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS`. Four cards, accent `#E05C5C`.

| Warning | Detail |
|---|---|

| FLASHBACK ARRESTORS | Required on both O2 and fuel lines -- inspect before every shift |
