# Poster #170 -- Construction Workup
## Surface Conditioning -- Manganese Phosphate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 3 of 8. This stage covers acid pickling (rust/scale removal) and optional surface conditioning (crystal refinement). Manganese phosphate is less conditioner-dependent than zinc phosphate -- it does not require a titanium colloid activator in most cases. However, hardened steel parts above 40 HRC resist acid attack and may need a proprietary activator or extended etch to open the surface. The poster must address both the pickle and conditioning functions, and the special case of hardened/tempered parts.

Hero visual: acid pickle tank with parts submerged, showing the surface transformation from scaled/rusted steel to clean, active metal.

### ZONE 4 -- Acid Options Comparison

**Section label:** `ACID SELECTION -- HCl VS. H2SO4` -- Y: 14.7".

**Left -- Hydrochloric Acid (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `HYDROCHLORIC ACID (HCl)` -- Barlow SemiBold, 18 pt, `#E8A020`

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Concentration | 10--25% by volume |
| Temperature | Ambient (preferred) |
| Speed | Fast -- aggressive at room temp |
| Scale removal | Excellent on mill scale |
| Smut | Minimal -- dissolves iron oxides cleanly |
| Fumes | Strong -- requires good ventilation |
| Rinse | Easy to rinse |

*Bottom note:* `Preferred for most MnPhos work. Fast, clean, room temperature.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#27AE60`

**Right -- Sulfuric Acid (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`
- Title: `SULFURIC ACID (H2SO4)` -- Barlow SemiBold, 18 pt, `#2EC4B6`

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Concentration | 10--20% by volume |
| Temperature | 120--150 F (49--66 C) for best results |
| Speed | Slower at ambient; needs heat |
| Scale removal | Good, especially with heat |
| Smut | Can leave carbon smut on high-carbon steel |
| Fumes | Less fuming than HCl |
| Rinse | More difficult -- sulfate residues |

*Bottom note:* `Used when HCl fume control is a problem or for extended soak on heavy scale.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#2EC4B6`

### ZONE 5 -- Hardened Steel + Conditioning

**Left -- Hardened Steel (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Section label: `HARDENED STEEL (>40 HRC)` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#E05C5C`.

- Rounded rect, H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`
- Content (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 155%):
  - `Hardened and tempered parts resist acid attack`
  - `Result: slower coating, lighter weight, thinner film`
  - `Fix: extended pickle time or proprietary activator`
  - `Dilute HCl activation dip (5--10%, 1--3 min) often needed`
  - `Some shops use a dilute Mn phosphate "pre-dip" (5--10% of bath concentration)`
  - `CAUTION: H-embrittlement risk on high-strength steel` (`#E05C5C`)
  - `Bake within 4 hours at 375 F if Rockwell > 39 HRC`

**Right -- Surface Conditioning Options (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Section label: `CONDITIONING OPTIONS` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#E8A020`.

- Rounded rect, H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`

Three conditioning approaches:

*No Conditioner (Most Common):*
- `Mn phosphate is less conditioner-dependent than Zn phosphate`
- `Clean, active steel surface is often sufficient`
- `The acid pickle IS the surface preparation`

*Mn-Based Pre-Dip:*
- `5--10% of normal bath concentration, ambient, 1--2 min`
- `Seeds the surface with Mn ions for finer crystal nucleation`
- `Used for improved coating uniformity`

*Ti/Zr Conditioner:*
- `Titanium or zirconium colloid activator`
- `Same products used for zinc phosphate conditioning`
- `Produces finer crystal structure and more uniform coating weight`

### ZONE 6 -- Defects + Key Rules

**Left -- Defects (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Section label: `CONDITIONING DEFECTS` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

4 defect cards stacked:

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| UNDER-PICKLED | Insufficient time or weak acid | Extend time; increase acid concentration |
| OVER-PICKLED | Excess time or temp; aggressive acid | Reduce time/temp; dilute acid |
| SMUT ON SURFACE | Carbon smut from H2SO4 on high-C steel | Switch to HCl or add smut remover step |
| FLASH RUST | Parts sat in air between pickle and rinse | Move parts immediately; keep wet |

Each card: Rounded rect, W: 11.0", H: 1.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- Key Rules (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Section label: `NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

6 rule rows, each with amber left border:
1. `Pickle ONLY if rust, scale, or oxide is present`
2. `Visual endpoint -- uniform gray metal, no scale`
3. `MUST rinse thoroughly after pickle -- acid carryover creates sludge`
4. `Hardened steel (>40 HRC) needs activator or extended pickle`
5. `H-embrittlement relief bake for high-strength steel`
6. `Do not let parts dry between pickle and rinse`
