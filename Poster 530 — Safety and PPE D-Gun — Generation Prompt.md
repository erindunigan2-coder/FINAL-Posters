## Phase 4 -- Hazard Matrix + Remote Operation Callout (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 14.0". Section label: `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION MATRIX`.

### Hazard Table (Left, W: 15.5")

7-row table. Columns: Hazard (3.0") | Details (5.0") | Controls (4.5") | Severity (3.0").

| Hazard | Details | Controls | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| NOISE | 130--150 dB detonation pulses; loudest thermal spray | Double hearing protection NRR 30+; sound-isolated booth; remote operation | EXTREME |
| BLAST / OVERPRESSURE | Repeated detonation waves; barrel failure risk | Barrel inspection schedule; burst disc; never operate damaged barrel | HIGH |
| COMBUSTIBLE GAS | O2 + C2H2 in detonable mixtures | NFPA/OSHA gas handling; flashback arrestors; leak detection | HIGH |
| METAL FUMES | Cobalt, nickel, chromium from WC-Co and alloy powders | LEV; HEPA filtration; P100 minimum RPE | HIGH |
| THERMAL | Hot barrel and fixtures despite water cooling | Heat-resistant PPE; never touch barrel during/after operation | MODERATE |
| UV/IR RADIATION | Moderate detonation flash | Shade 5--8 eye protection; no exposed skin | MODERATE |
| VIBRATION | Pulsed operation transmits through structure | Vibration-isolating mounts; structural assessment | MODERATE |

Severity badges: EXTREME=`#E05C5C`, HIGH=`#E8A020`, MODERATE=`#C8D0D8`. Header fill `#3A4055`.

### Remote Operation Callout (Right, W: 7.0")

Coral-tinted glass, border 2pt `#E05C5C`. Title: `REMOTE OPERATION` 32pt + `MANDATORY` 28pt in `#E05C5C`.

Body: NO human inside booth during operation. Controls: 6-axis robot, remote console, camera monitoring. Booth provides: acoustic isolation, blast containment, fume extraction, overspray containment.

## Phase 6 -- PPE Checklist + Emergency Procedures

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

### Left -- PPE by Role (W: 12.0")

Section label: `PPE REQUIREMENTS BY ROLE`. Two side-by-side cards:

**Booth Setup / Maintenance** (Amber accent): Double hearing NRR 30+, shade 5--8 glasses, heat-resistant gloves, leather apron/FR coat, steel-toed boots, P100 half-mask, face shield. Note: `Equipment OFF during entry. LOTO required.`

**Remote Operator** (Teal accent): Hearing protection, safety glasses, standard clothing, E-stop access, camera active, comms with booth area. Note: `Operator OUTSIDE booth at all times during firing.`

### Right -- Emergency Procedures (W: 10.5")

Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE`. Four stacked cards:

| Emergency | Color | Response |
|---|---|---|
| GAS LEAK (O2/C2H2) | `#E05C5C` | E-stop. Evacuate. Ventilate. No ignition sources. Verify gas-free before re-entry. |
| BARREL FAILURE | `#E05C5C` | E-stop. Do not enter booth. Allow cooling. Inspect structural damage. |
| FIRE IN BOOTH | `#E8A020` | E-stop. Activate suppression. Do not open doors (O2 feed). Call fire response. |
| FUME OVEREXPOSURE | `#E8A020` | Remove to fresh air. Medical attention. Report cobalt symptoms: cough, wheeze, dyspnea. |
