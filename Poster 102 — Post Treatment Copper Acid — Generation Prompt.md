## Phase 4 -- Three Application Pathways Hero

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section: `THREE PATHS AFTER ACID COPPER`.

Three tall cards (H: 9.0"):

**Card 1 -- Decorative (accent `#27AE60`):**
`Cu + Ni + Cr Stack`. After acid copper: Rinse -> Nickel (5--25 um) -> Rinse -> Chrome (0.25--0.75 um) -> Rinse + Dry. No anti-tarnish needed. Copper provides leveling, ductility, corrosion barrier.
Application: `Automotive trim, plumbing, hardware, electronics`.

**Card 2 -- PCB (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
`Through-Hole and Pattern Plating`. After acid copper: Rinse -> Tin/tin-lead (etch resist) -> Strip photoresist -> Etch -> Strip tin -> Solder mask. Copper fills barrels and builds traces.
Application: `PCBs, flex circuits, RF shielding, semiconductor`.

**Card 3 -- Standalone (accent `#E8A020`):**
`Anti-Tarnish Required`. After acid copper: Rinse -> Anti-tarnish dip (15--60 sec) -> Rinse (gentle) -> Dry. Without anti-tarnish: tarnish in hours, oxide in days.
Application: `Electroforming, EMI shielding, heatsinks, salvage`.

## Phase 5 -- Anti-Tarnish + Dry

Y: 15.0" to 21.5". Section: `ANTI-TARNISH & DRY PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Anti-Tarnish:** Chromate-based or organic (benzotriazole type) | Ambient | 15--60 sec | pH 2--4 (chromate) | Gentle rinse after -- do not scrub off film | Shelf life: chromate weeks-months, organic days-weeks | Skip if proceeding to nickel.

**Right -- Dry:** Forced air, oven, or centrifugal (barrel) | Ambient to 150 F -- do not overheat copper | Dry quickly -- Cu oxidizes faster when hot and wet | Check for tarnish, water spots, staining.

## Phase 7 -- Problems + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Problems:**

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Tarnish within hours | No anti-tarnish | Apply immediately after rinse |
| Hazy anti-tarnish | Over-immersion or contaminated bath | Reduce time; replace bath |
| Water spots | Hard water in final rinse | DI water; dry faster |
| Peeling at Cu-Ni interface | Surface tarnish before nickel | Minimize transfer; keep wet |
| Green/brown corrosion | Anti-tarnish worn off | Reapply; improve packaging |

**Right -- Safety:** Anti-tarnish (chromate) = acidic | Organic inhibitors = mild irritant | CuSO4 on skin: wash immediately | Oven drying = burn hazard | Cu dust (barrel parts) = respiratory | Cu in wastewater = regulated.
