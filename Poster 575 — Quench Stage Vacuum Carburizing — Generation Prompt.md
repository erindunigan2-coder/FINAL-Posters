## Phase 5 -- How HPGQ Works

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `HOW HIGH-PRESSURE GAS QUENCH WORKS`.

**Left -- Mechanics (Teal accent):** 8 steps: carburize complete -> interlocks verify -> gas floods to 10--20 bar in seconds -> 200+ HP blower circulates -> heat exchanger cools recirculating gas -> forced convection extracts heat -> parts cool to 150--300 F -> chamber depressurized, parts unloaded.

**Right -- Why Gas Wins on Distortion (Emerald accent):** No film boiling (oil has 3-stage cooling with non-uniform vapor blanket). Gas: ONE mechanism (forced convection) = uniform. No liquid/vapor interface. Controllable: adjust pressure + blower speed. Typical: 30--50% less distortion.

## Phase 6 -- Oil Quench Option + Decision Matrix

**Oil Quench (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `WHEN YOU STILL NEED OIL` in Amber.

*When Oil Required (Amber):* Thick sections >1.5" in lean alloys (8620, 1020); H-factor >0.40 required; legacy specs mandate oil. Integrated oil quench chamber; parts transfer under vacuum/inert; 100--160 F agitated.

*What You Lose (Coral):* Distortion advantage GONE. Clean parts GONE (need wash). Fire risk BACK. THE BRIGHT SIDE: still get zero IGO, cleaner surface than gas, higher temp still applies, LPC + oil > gas + oil on most metrics.

**Decision Matrix (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `HPGQ OR OIL? -- QUICK DECISION GUIDE`. Four cards, Teal accent:

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| THIN SECTION + HIGH ALLOY | HPGQ (N2 or He). Full hardness achievable. Maximum distortion benefit. |
| THIN SECTION + LEAN ALLOY | HPGQ with He at 20 bar. Verify H-factor sufficient. |
| THICK SECTION + HIGH ALLOY | HPGQ may work. Run trial. Switch to oil if core doesn't harden. |
| THICK SECTION + LEAN ALLOY | Oil quench. HPGQ insufficient. Accept distortion trade-off. |
