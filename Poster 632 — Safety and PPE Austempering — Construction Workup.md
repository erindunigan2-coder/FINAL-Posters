# Poster #632 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Austempering

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Safety poster for austempering operations. Molten salt is the dominant hazard -- nitrate/nitrite salts at 400-700 F cause severe burns, and moisture contact triggers explosive steam eruptions. Transfer of 1500+ F parts through open air adds burn and splash risk. If the austenitizing furnace uses endothermic atmosphere, CO and H2 explosion hazards apply. This poster is coral-heavy by design -- safety demands attention.

### ZONE 4 -- Hazard Matrix

**Section label:** `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION -- AUSTEMPERING OPERATIONS` -- Y: 15.2".

**BLOCK D -- 6-Row Hazard Table**

Y: 15.8" to 21.8". Column widths (23.0" total):
- Hazard (4.0") | Source (6.0") | Severity (3.0") | Mitigation (10.0")

Header row: Rectangle fill `#3A4055`, H: 0.5". Barlow SemiBold, 14 pt, `#F0EDE8`.

| Hazard | Source | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Molten salt burns | Salt bath 400--700 F; splash on part immersion | SEVERE | Full PPE; controlled immersion speed; never rush |
| Moisture explosion | Water or moisture contacts molten salt -- instant steam expansion | CRITICAL | Pre-heat ALL parts and fixtures to 250+ F; strict moisture control; inspect for condensation |
| Nitrate/nitrite fire | Salt is an oxidizer; organic contamination (oil, grease) triggers violent reaction | CRITICAL | Zero oil in salt bath; no organic materials near bath; dedicated degreased fixturing |
| Transfer burns | Parts at 1500+ F moved through open air to salt bath | SEVERE | Enclosed transfer when possible; clear work zone; no bystanders |
| CO / H2 explosion | Endothermic atmosphere in austenitizing furnace (20% CO, 40% H2) | CRITICAL | CO monitors; burn-off pilots at doors; nitrogen purge before endo gas; 5 volume changes |
| Radiant heat | Furnace door opens at 1500+ F; prolonged exposure | MODERATE | Face shield; aluminized clothing; minimize door-open time |

Severity values color-coded: CRITICAL = `#E05C5C`, SEVERE = `#E8A020`, MODERATE = `#2EC4B6`.

Data: Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`. Hazard names: Barlow SemiBold 13 pt.

Rules (numbered list):
- Barlow SemiBold, 18 pt, `#F0EDE8`

```
1. PRE-HEAT everything that enters molten salt -- parts, fixtures, tools. No exceptions.
2. NEVER introduce moisture into molten salt. One drop of water = explosive steam eruption.
3. KEEP all organic materials away from nitrate/nitrite salt -- oil + oxidizer = fire.
4. WEAR full PPE for every salt bath operation -- face shield, gauntlets, apron, boots.
5. VERIFY atmosphere purge before introducing endothermic gas to austenitizing furnace.
```

## Design Notes

This is the safety poster -- coral is the dominant accent. The moisture explosion hazard cannot be overstated: a single drop of water in molten salt at 500+ F generates steam at 1,700x its liquid volume instantly. The PPE diagram must be large enough to read at 8 feet -- this poster lives next to the salt bath. The "no synthetics" warning is life-safety critical and gets its own banner.
