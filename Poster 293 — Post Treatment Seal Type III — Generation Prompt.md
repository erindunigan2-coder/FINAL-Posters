## Phase 4 -- Seal Method Comparison Hero

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section: `FOUR SEAL OPTIONS -- CHOOSE BY APPLICATION`.

Four tall cards side by side:

**Card 1 -- Hot DI Water** (Teal accent): `200--212 F` / `DI water, pH 5.5--6.5` / `2--3 min/um; min 30 min`. Best for: general corrosion protection. Salt spray: `336--750 hr`.

**Card 2 -- Nickel Acetate** (Emerald accent): `180--200 F` / `5--8 g/L Ni(OAc)2, pH 5.5--6.0` / `15--30 min`. Best for: aerospace; maximum corrosion. Salt spray: `750--1500+ hr`.

**Card 3 -- PTFE Impregnation** (Amber accent): `Per vendor TDS` / `PTFE dispersion`. Best for: sliding surfaces; low friction. CoF: `0.08--0.15 (vs. 0.4--0.6 unsealed)` -- prominent `#E8A020`.

**Card 4 -- No Seal** (Silver accent): `N/A -- as-anodized`. Best for: wear applications where pores retain oil/lubricant. Note: `Poor corrosion resistance; acceptable only for lubricated assemblies` `#E05C5C`.

## Phase 5 -- PTFE Detail + Dyeing Limitations

Y: 15.5" to 22.0".

**Left -- PTFE Impregnation Detail:**
PTFE dispersion drawn into open pores by immersion or vacuum. Heat-cured at 400--500 F. Result: CoF 0.08--0.15, self-lubricating, excellent for hydraulic cylinders/pistons/valve bodies. NOT a corrosion seal. NOT reversible. `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Dyeing Limitations:**
Hardcoat's dense pore structure limits dye to dark colors only (black, dark blue, dark green). Light colors NOT achievable. Natural hardcoat color: dark bronze to near-black. `If the customer requires bright colors, they need Type II -- not Type III.` `#E8A020`

## Phase 6 -- Application Decision Tree

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHICH SEAL FOR WHICH APPLICATION?`.

Decision flow:
1. `Is the part a sliding/bearing surface?` YES --> PTFE | NO --> next
2. `Does the part need corrosion resistance?` YES --> next | NO --> NO SEAL (open pores retain lubricant)
3. `Aerospace or military spec?` YES --> NICKEL ACETATE | NO --> HOT DI WATER

## Phase 7 -- Effect on Properties Table

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `HOW SEALING CHANGES COATING PROPERTIES`.

| Property | Unsealed | Hot Water | Ni Acetate | PTFE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hardness | Maximum (500--700 HV) | Slight reduction | Slight reduction | Slight reduction |
| Friction (CoF) | 0.4--0.6 | 0.4--0.6 | 0.4--0.6 | 0.08--0.15 |
| Corrosion (B117) | Poor (<168 hr) | Good (336--750 hr) | Excellent (750--1500+ hr) | Poor (not corrosion seal) |
| Lubricant retention | Excellent (open pores) | None (pores closed) | None (pores closed) | Self-lubricating |
| Abrasion | Maximum | Good | Good | Good + low friction |

Best-in-class values highlighted in `#27AE60`.
