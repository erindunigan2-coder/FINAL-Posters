## Phase 4 -- Nucleation Comparison Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `INITIAL NUCLEATION -- GOOD vs. POOR`.

Two large side-by-side panels:

**Left -- GOOD NUCLEATION (`#27AE60`):** `GOOD: UNIFORM INITIAL COVERAGE`
- Mandrel surface with uniform fine metal nuclei layer
- Strike at 50-75% CD: fine-grained, complete coverage, smooth interior, no pitting, deposit adheres uniformly
- RESULT: Interior replicates mandrel finish exactly. Ra matches mandrel Ra.

**Right -- POOR NUCLEATION (`#E05C5C`):** `POOR: UNEVEN INITIAL COVERAGE`
- Mandrel surface with patchy, irregular clusters and gaps
- Started at full CD: coarse grains, incomplete coverage in recesses, rough interior, H2 pitting, adhesion problems, lamination risk
- RESULT: Rough, pitted interior. SCRAP.

**Bottom insight:** `The strike is the foundation of the electroform -- like surface prep for plating. You cannot fix a bad start by plating over it. The interior surface quality is locked in during the first 10-30 minutes.`

## Phase 5 -- Strike Protocol + Why It Matters

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Two-column layout.

**Left -- STRIKE PROTOCOL (`#E8A020`):**

```
STEP 1: IMMERSE MANDREL (LIVE)
  "Hot entry" -- rectifier on, then immerse.
  Prevents chemical etching or passive layer.

STEP 2: STRIKE AT REDUCED CD
  Ni sulfamate: 1.5-2.5 A/dm2 (50-75% of 3-5 A/dm2)
  Duration: 10-30 min

STEP 3: RAMP TO FULL CD
  Increase gradually over 5-10 min to 3-5 A/dm2

STEP 4: VERIFY COVERAGE
  After 30-60 min, visual inspect if possible.
  Full matte coverage = good. Bright/bare spots = problem.
```

**Right -- WHY IT MATTERS (`#2EC4B6`):** Three key-point cards.

1. `INTERIOR SURFACE QUALITY` -- Coarse grains/pits at start = permanent features. For waveguides and reflectors, this surface IS the product.
2. `ADHESION TO RELEASE LAYER` -- Too much current breaks through release layer, locking deposit to mandrel. Too little leaves gaps.
3. `STRESS DISTRIBUTION` -- High CD at start = high tensile stress in base layer = curling and premature separation.

## Phase 6 -- Bath Check + Hot Entry + Failures

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Pre-Strike Bath Check Table (Y: 22.0" to 28.5"):**

| Parameter | Target | Check Method |
|---|---|---|
| Ni concentration | 300-450 g/L | Titration or SG |
| NiCl2 | 5-30 g/L | Titration |
| H3BO3 | 30-45 g/L | Titration |
| pH | 3.8-4.2 | pH meter (calibrate daily) |
| Temperature | 50-54 C | Thermometer/controller |
| Saccharin | 0.5-3 g/L | Hull cell test |
| Wetting agent | Per supplier | Visual (foaming) |
| Filtration | Running, 1-5 um | Visual flow check |
| Contamination | Hull cell clean | Hull cell 2 A, 10 min |

**Hot Entry Technique (`#E8A020`):**
1. Rectifier ON at strike CD. 2. Lower mandrel smoothly. 3. Fully submerge without pausing (waterline mark). 4. Tilt slightly to avoid trapping air bubbles (bubbles = pits).

**Strike Failures (Y: 28.5" to 32.5"):** Four cards.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| ROUGH INTERIOR | Started at full CD | Strike at 50-75% for 10-30 min |
| PITTING AT INTERFACE | Air bubbles; H2 at high CD | Tilt during immersion; wetting agent; reduce CD |
| ADHESION TO MANDREL | Strike CD too high; broke release layer | Reduce CD; fresh release agent |
| SKIP PLATING (BARE SPOTS) | Release too thick; mandrel not conductive | Thinner release; verify conductivity |
