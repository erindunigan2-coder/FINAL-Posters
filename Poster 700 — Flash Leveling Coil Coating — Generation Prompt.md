## Phase 5 -- Leveling Mechanics + First Seconds

### Three Factor Cards (Y: 14.5"-20.5")

Section label: `THREE FACTORS THAT DETERMINE FILM SMOOTHNESS`. Three cards (W: 7.33", H: 4.5").

**Card 1 -- Roll Speed Ratio (accent `#E8A020`):** Big stat `1.05-1.25:1`. Higher ratio = more shearing = smoother. Too high = chatter. Single most important leveling control.

**Card 2 -- Nip Pressure (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Big stat `20-100 pli`. Controls thickness AND uniformity. Uneven nip = DFT variation. Roll crown compensates.

**Card 3 -- Coating Viscosity (accent `#27AE60`):** Big stat `30-80 sec` Zahn #2. Lower = better flow. Higher = thicker film. Temp-sensitive, monitor at 77 F. Solvent evaporation increases over time.

### First Seconds in Oven (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `WHAT HAPPENS IN THE FIRST 10 SECONDS OF THE OVEN`. Four time-phase columns:

**0-3 sec:** Strip enters, temp rising, solvent starts evaporating, viscosity dropping.
**3-8 sec:** Viscosity minimum = LEVELING WINDOW. Coating flows. Orange peel determined here.
**8-15 sec:** Cross-linking begins. Viscosity rises. Film locks. No further leveling.
**15-60 sec:** Full cross-linking. PMT reached (400-480 F). Fully cured. Exit to quench.

Bottom: `The entire "flash/leveling" window is 3-8 seconds inside the oven. Compare to 5-15 minutes ambient flash in liquid spray.`

## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| ORANGE PEEL | Insufficient leveling / viscosity too high | Reduce oven zone 1 temp; lower viscosity |
| CHATTER / RIBBING | Roll speed ratio too high / roll wear | Reduce ratio; re-grind roll |
| SOLVENT POP | Solvent trapped when surface skins | Adjust solvent blend; check oven zone 1 |
| UNEVEN GLOSS | Viscosity variation / uneven roll contact | Agitate reservoir; verify alignment |

Key insight: `"Leveling" is not a separate step -- it is a property of the roll coater and the first seconds of the oven.`
