## Phase 5 -- Why No Hot Water

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Heat Damage Mechanism:** Coral accent. Heat accelerates: Cr6+ reduction (depleting reservoir), gel dehydration (cracking), crystallization of Cr2O3 (loss of flexibility). Result: coating looks same but lost self-healing. `The damage is invisible and irreversible.`

**Right -- Cold vs. Hot Rinse:**

| Property | Cold (77 F) | Hot (150+ F) |
|---|---|---|
| Cr6+ content | Preserved (10--30%) | Depleted (< 5%) |
| Self-healing | ACTIVE | DESTROYED |
| SST (bare) | 168--336+ hr | < 48 hr |
| Gel structure | Flexible | Brittle, cracked |
| Appearance | Normal gold/brown | May appear similar (deceptive) |

`The hot-rinsed part LOOKS fine. It FAILS in salt spray.`

## Phase 6 -- Handling Rules + 24-Hr Cure

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- 24-Hour No-Touch Cure:** Amber accent. During cure: no handle/stack/rub/wipe/moisture/oven above 150 F. After 24 hr: full hardness, normal handling, paint within 72 hr.

**Right -- Damage Sources:** Coral accent. Hot water rinse, physical contact, water droplets, stacking parts, oven > 150 F.

---

## Phase 7 -- Monitoring

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Temperature < 77 F continuous | Immersion 15--30 sec | Conductivity < 200 uS/cm daily | Visual every load. Three problem cards: failed SST (hot rinse), water spots, coating rubbed off.
