### Acid Drag-In Consequence Callout (Y: 11.5" to 14.0")

Full-width coral panel. Title: `WHAT HAPPENS WHEN ACID REACHES THE ALKALINE COPPER BATH`.

Three columns:
- **pH Drop:** Acid lowers pH below range | At pH < 8: complexant breaks down | Free copper ions appear
- **Immersion Copper:** Free Cu2+ plates immersion copper on steel/zinc | Immersion deposit = non-adherent | Entire load fails
- **Bath Damage:** Complexant decomposition NOT reversible | Bath needs partial dump and rebuild | Expensive; production stops

## Phase 5 -- Rinse Parameters + Speed Callout

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameters:** DI preferred | Ambient | 2 cascade minimum | 15--30 sec | < 500 uS/cm | pH 6--8.

**Right -- Speed Matters (accent `#E8A020`):**
After activation and rinsing, the surface re-oxidizes immediately on air contact. For zinc DC:
- Rinse: 15--30 sec
- Drain: 10 sec
- Into copper strike: within 30 sec of exiting rinse
- Total activation-to-strike: under 90 seconds.

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acid drag-in to Cu bath | pH drops; immersion Cu on parts | Insufficient rinsing | Add stage; increase flow |
| Re-oxidation on zinc DC | White haze; poor adhesion | Slow transfer | Enter strike within 30 sec |
| Spotting/minerals | White spots under Cu | Hard water | Switch to DI |
| Contaminated rinse | Cu residue before strike | Tank not dumped; drag-back | Dump/refresh; check flow direction |
