## Phase 4 -- Hazard Grid (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 16.0". Section label: `FIVE HAZARDS IN VACUUM CARBURIZING` in `#E05C5C`.

**Row 1 (Y: 3.8", three cards W: 7.33", H: 5.5"):**

| Card | Hazard | Stat | Key Details | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ACETYLENE (C2H2) | `EXPLOSIVE: LEL 2.5%` 24pt | Flammable range 2.5--100%; stored with acetone; NEVER exceed 15 psig; detectors at floor level | Dedicated storage. Leak detection. Flash arrestors. Never use copper fittings (acetylide). |
| 2 | HIGH-PRESSURE GAS QUENCH | `10--20 BAR RELEASE` 24pt | 150--300 psi; blower >90 dB; vessel is pressure vessel during quench | Verify all interlocks. Hearing protection mandatory. Pressure relief valves tested per schedule. |
| 3 | BURNS & HOT PARTS | `150--300 F EXIT TEMP` 24pt | Parts hot after HPGQ; fixtures and walls hot; CFC deceptively dark | Heat-resistant gloves. Allow cooling time. Verify temp before bare-hand contact. |

**Row 2 (Y: 9.7", two cards):**

| Card | Hazard | Stat | Key Details | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 (W: 11.0") | NITROGEN ASPHYXIATION | `SILENT KILLER -- NO WARNING` 20pt | N2 displaces O2 silently; no odor/color; <19.5% = deficient; <16% = unconscious; <10% = death in minutes; chamber maintenance = confined space | Continuous O2 monitoring. Confined space permit for ALL maintenance. Never enter vessel without O2 > 20.9%. Buddy system. |
| 5 (W: 11.5") | VACUUM VESSEL INTEGRITY | `IMPLOSION / PRESSURE RISK` 18pt | Thick-walled for both vacuum and HPGQ pressure; viewports and seals = failure points; thermal cycling fatigue; ASME code inspection | Inspect viewports and seals every maintenance. Hydrostatic testing per code. Replace O-rings on schedule. Log thermal cycles. |

## Phase 5 -- Safety Comparison + PPE

**Safety Comparison (Y: 16.0"--18.5"):** Full-width callout, Teal accent. `WHAT LPC ELIMINATES vs. WHAT LPC ADDS`.

*ELIMINATED (Emerald):* CO exposure, H2 explosion from endo, atmosphere explosion on purge failure, quench oil fire (HPGQ path), continuous CO monitoring.

*ADDED (Amber):* Acetylene handling/storage, HPGQ high-pressure operation, N2 asphyxiation risk, vacuum vessel inspection, hearing protection during HPGQ.

**PPE (Y: 18.5"--24.0"):** Section label: `REQUIRED PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT`. 3x2 cards, Teal accent:

| PPE | Details |
|---|---|
| HEAT-RESISTANT GLOVES | Loading/unloading; parts at 150--300 F |
| HEARING PROTECTION | Mandatory during HPGQ; blower >90 dB |
| SAFETY GLASSES | Standard for all furnace area ops |
| STEEL-TOE BOOTS | Dropped fixtures, heavy CFC trays |
| O2 PERSONAL MONITOR | Alarm at 19.5%; mandatory in furnace area |
| SCBA (MAINTENANCE) | Self-contained breathing apparatus for confined space entry |
