## Phase 4 -- Standard vs. Moisture-Tolerant Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.5". Section label: `TWO PATHS -- STANDARD DRY vs. MOISTURE-TOLERANT PRIMER`.

**Left -- `STANDARD DRY PATH` (border `#E8A020`):**
4-step vertical flow: Rinse (DI, < 50 uS/cm) -> Air blow-off -> Force dry (remove ALL moisture) -> Apply primer to completely dry surface.
Rules: Standard epoxy, alkyd, 2K urethane primers: bone dry. ALL topcoats: completely dry.
Warning `#E05C5C`: `Moisture trapped under standard primer = blistering, adhesion loss, premature corrosion`

**Right -- `MOISTURE-TOLERANT PRIMER PATH` (border `#27AE60`):**
4-step flow: Rinse -> Blow off excess -> Apply wash primer or etch primer to damp surface -> Flash dry, then topcoat.
Compatible primers: Wash primer (vinyl butyral etch): DOD-P-15328, PVB resin + H3PO4, DFT 0.3--0.5 mil. Self-etching primers: H3PO4-functional, DFT 0.5--1.0 mil.
Note: Must be topcoated -- excellent for field touch-up.

## Phase 5 -- Rinse Quality + Blow-Off

Y: 15.8" to 20.8". Section label: `RINSE WATER QUALITY AND BLOW-OFF`.

**Left -- Rinse Quality Table:**

| Rinse Position | Source | Max Conductivity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| After alkaline clean | City water | 500 uS/cm | Remove cleaner residuals |
| After conversion coating | DI or RO | 50 uS/cm | Salt deposits cause blistering |
| Final rinse | DI water | 50 uS/cm | Critical for long-term adhesion |

**Right -- `BLOW-OFF AND DRYING` (accent `#E8A020`):**
Compressed air, air dry 15--30 min, force dry 140--180 F, oven dry 200--250 F.
Note `#E05C5C`: `Oil-free compressed air ONLY. Compressor oil contamination causes fish-eye defects.`

## Phase 6 -- Tack Cloth + Intercoat Prep

Y: 21.8" to 26.3". Section label: `BETWEEN-COAT SURFACE PREP`.

**Left -- `TACK CLOTH PROTOCOL`:** 5-step protocol. Low-tack, silicone-free. Spray within 30 min.

**Right -- `WHEN TO SAND BETWEEN COATS`:** Within recoat window? No sanding. Exceeded? Scuff sand 320--400 grit. Typical windows: Alkyd 24--72 hr / 2K Epoxy 4--24 hr (CRITICAL) / 2K Urethane 1--24 hr.

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". Section label: `WHEN RINSE OR DRY FAILS -- 6 COATING DEFECTS`. 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| BLISTERING | `#E05C5C` | Moisture trapped under standard primer | Full drying before primer |
| SALT DEPOSITS | `#E8A020` | High-TDS rinse water | DI rinse; < 50 uS/cm |
| FISH-EYE (COMPRESSED AIR) | `#E05C5C` | Oil-contaminated air | Oil/water separator |
| INTERCOAT ADHESION LOSS | `#E8A020` | Recoat window exceeded | Scuff 320--400 grit |
| DUST NIBS | `#E05C5C` | Sanding dust not removed | Tack wipe after every sand |
| TACK CLOTH FISH-EYE | `#2EC4B6` | Silicone-containing tack cloth | Silicone-free, low-tack only |
