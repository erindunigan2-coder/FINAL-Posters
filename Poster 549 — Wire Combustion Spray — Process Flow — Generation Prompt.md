## Phase 6 -- Wire Combustion vs. Arc Spray + Troubleshooting

### Comparison (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `WIRE COMBUSTION SPRAY vs. ARC SPRAY`.

**Left -- Wire Combustion Spray** (accent `#27AE60`, subtitle `The Portable Classic`):

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Heat source | Oxy-fuel flame (~3100 degC) |
| Particle velocity | 80-200 m/s |
| Porosity | 5-15% |
| Bond strength | 7-25 MPa |
| Deposition rate | 2-8 kg/hr |
| Capital cost | $ (lowest) |
| Portability | Excellent -- truck-portable |

Bottom highlight: `Fits in a pickup truck. No electricity required for the gun. The most field-portable thermal spray process.`

**Right -- Arc Spray** (accent `#E8A020`, subtitle `Higher Volume Alternative`):

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Heat source | Electric arc (~6000 degC) |
| Particle velocity | 50-200 m/s |
| Bond strength | 10-30 MPa |
| Deposition rate | 5-30 kg/hr |
| Capital cost | $$ (moderate) |
| Portability | Good -- needs power supply |

Bottom highlight: `Higher deposition rate and denser coatings, but requires electrical power and more capital.`

### Troubleshooting Strip (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| POOR ADHESION | Inadequate blast profile or contamination | Re-blast to SSPC-SP 5; verify Ra 4-12 um |
| EXCESSIVE POROSITY | Standoff too far; air pressure too low | Reduce standoff to 150-200 mm; increase air to 60-80 PSI |
| UNEVEN COATING | Inconsistent traverse speed or angle | Steady hand speed; crossing passes at 60-90 deg |
| ZINC FUME SICKNESS | Inadequate respiratory protection | P100 RPE or supplied air ALWAYS for zinc |
