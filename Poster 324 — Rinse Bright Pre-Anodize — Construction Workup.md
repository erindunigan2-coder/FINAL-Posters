# Poster #324 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse (Pre-Anodize) -- Bright Anodizing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. This rinse removes all phosphoric acid, nitric acid, and desmut residue before entering the anodize tank. DI water is strongly preferred for bright work because chloride from city water causes pitting on the polished surface. Phosphate drag-in to the anodize tank can cloud the bright finish by altering oxide growth.

## Design Notes

The key message: DI water is mandatory for bright work, not optional. The right-half explanation panel in Zone 3 makes the case clearly -- every contaminant that hides on matte finishes is visible on bright. The phosphate drag-out from bright dip is uniquely viscous (hot H3PO4 is thick) and requires longer drain times than standard processes.
