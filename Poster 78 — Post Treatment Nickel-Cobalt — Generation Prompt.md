## Phase 5 -- Heat Treatment Detail

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `HEAT TREATMENT -- HARDNESS UPGRADE`.

| Parameter | Range | Optimal | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | 250--350 C (482--662 F) | 300 C (572 F) | Higher temp = higher hardness up to limit |
| Time | 1--4 hr at temperature | 2--3 hr | Longer = more complete transformation |
| Ramp rate | 2--5 C/min | 3 C/min | Too fast causes cracking |
| Atmosphere | Inert (N2, Ar) or vacuum | Vacuum preferred | Prevents surface oxidation |
| Cooling | Furnace cool or slow air cool | Furnace cool | Do not quench -- thermal shock cracks deposit |
| As-plated hardness | 400--500 HV | -- | Baseline |
| Post-heat hardness | 600--700 HV | -- | Verify by Vickers |
| Co% effect | Higher Co% = higher peak hardness | 20--30% optimal | Below 15% Co: minimal hardening response |

Below table, two callouts:

*Left (emerald-tinted):* `THE MECHANISM: Heat treatment causes ordering of the Ni-Co solid solution and precipitation of a hard intermetallic phase. Co content must be >15% for meaningful hardening.`

*Right (coral-tinted):* `WARNING: Excessive cobalt (>35%) or too-rapid heating can cause cracking. Always verify deposit Co% before committing to heat treat. Once cracked, the part is scrap.`

## Phase 6 -- HE Bake + Chromium Topcoat

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column layout.

**Left -- H-Embrittlement Bake (accent `#E05C5C`):**
- 375 F (190 C) | 4 hr minimum (8--24 hr critical parts) | Within 4 hr of plating | All steel >=40 HRC or >=145 ksi UTS | Per ASTM B849, AMS 2759/9
- `The 4-hour window is non-negotiable. This bake is NOT the same as the hardness heat treatment.` `#E8A020`

**Right -- Chromium Topcoat (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
- Hard chrome (hexavalent) | 0.5--5 mil typical | 800--1000 HV | Applied over NiCo (after H-bake if required) | Surface hardness + corrosion barrier | AMS 2406 or AMS 2460 over AMS 2424 NiCo
- `NiCo provides ductile, high-strength foundation. Chrome provides extreme surface hardness. Together they outperform either alone.`
