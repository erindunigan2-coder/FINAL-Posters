Five cards across top converging to decision diamond.

**Card 1 -- DFT (`#E8A020`):** PA 2 / D7091. Magnetic/eddy current. Tooke gauge for individual layers. Per spec (+/- 20%). 5 spots per 100 sq ft.

**Card 2 -- Holidays (`#E05C5C`):** D5162 wet sponge @ 67.5 V (< 20 mil). D4787 spark @ 100 V/mil (> 20 mil). Zero holidays. MANDATORY for tank/immersion/pipeline.

**Card 3 -- Adhesion (`#2EC4B6`):** D4541 pull-off / D3359 crosshatch. Hydraulic dolly. > 200 psi (industrial), > 300 psi (immersion). Gold standard for thick coatings.

## Phase 5 -- Holiday Detection + Safety

### Holiday Detection (Y: 14.5"-21.0")

Section label: `HOLIDAY DETECTION -- FINDING THE INVISIBLE`. Two columns.

Left -- Low-Voltage Wet Sponge (D5162): 67.5 V DC. < 20 mils. Wet cellulose sponge. Audible alarm at pinhole. Wetting agent. 6-12 in/sec sweep. `Sponge must stay wet.`

Right -- High-Voltage Spark (NACE SP0188 / D4787): 100 V per mil. > 20 mils. Wire brush electrode. Arc/spark at holiday. Example: 20 mil = 2,000 V. HIGH VOLTAGE -- trained operator, grounding. `Calculate from MEASURED DFT, not target.`

Bottom: `Holiday detection mandatory for immersion/chemical/pipeline. A single pinhole concentrates corrosion and can perforate the substrate.`

### Safety (Y: 21.0"-27.0")

Section label: `SAFETY -- THE CHEMISTRY THAT PROTECTS CAN ALSO HARM`. Safety table.

| Hazard | Details | Protection |
|---|---|---|
| Isocyanate (urethane) | TLV 0.005 ppm; respiratory sensitizer; occupational asthma | SAR for spray; full clothing; chem gloves |
| Epoxy resin (BPA) | Skin sensitizer; once sensitized, cannot work with epoxy | Nitrile gloves; long sleeves; barrier cream |
| Amine hardeners | Skin/eye corrosive; vapor irritant | Splash goggles; chem gloves; ventilation |
| Solvents (MEK, xylene) | CNS effects; flammable | OV cartridge or SAR; LEL monitoring |
| Confined space (tanks) | OSHA 1910.146; O2-deficient/explosive/toxic | Continuous monitoring; supplied air; attendant; permit |

Big stat: `0.005 ppm` 36pt `#E05C5C` -- `TLV-TWA for HDI monomer -- one of the lowest in industrial hygiene.`

EU note: `REACH (Aug 2023): isocyanate training mandatory for ALL workers handling diisocyanates in the EU.`
