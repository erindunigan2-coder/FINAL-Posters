## Phase 4 -- PPE Equipment Diagram (HERO)

Y: 5.0" to 14.0".

| PPE Item | Position | Accent | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full Face Shield over Splash Goggles | Upper right | `#E05C5C` | NON-NEGOTIABLE for HF. Face shield alone is insufficient -- goggles provide sealed splash protection. Both required simultaneously at all times near HF-containing baths. |
| Butyl Rubber Gloves (Double-Glove) | Upper left | `#E05C5C` | NOT nitrile, NOT latex -- HF penetrates thin nitrile in seconds. Butyl rubber or thick neoprene only. Minimum 14" gauntlet. Double-glove recommended. Inspect before every use. |
| Chemical Splash Suit | Right center | `#E8A020` | Full chemical splash suit (PVC or butyl rubber) for tank maintenance, additions, and any work where splash is possible. Heavy PVC apron minimum for routine monitoring. |
| Chemical-Resistant Boots | Lower right | `#E8A020` | With splash guards. HF on exposed ankle skin has caused fatalities. No gaps between boot top and suit leg. |
| Full-Face Respirator | Lower left | `#E05C5C` | Full-face respirator with acid gas / P100 combination cartridge minimum. Supplied-air respirator for enclosed areas, tank entry, or during additions. ACGIH TLV for HF: 0.5 ppm TWA. |
| No Exposed Skin | Lower center | `#E05C5C` | ZERO exposed skin near HF baths. HF vapor absorbs through skin. Long sleeves under suit. Tape suit-to-glove and suit-to-boot junctions if seal is uncertain. |

## Phase 5 -- HF Hazard Panel

Y: 15.3" to 20.3".

```
HF penetrates skin RAPIDLY.
Unlike HCl or H2SO4, HF does not
cause immediate pain on contact.

Burns may not be felt for 1-24 HOURS.

By the time you feel it, the damage
is deep -- HF destroys tissue from
the inside out.
```

```
HF binds calcium and magnesium
in the bloodstream.

This causes HYPOCALCEMIA:
  - Cardiac arrhythmia
  - Cardiac arrest
  - Death

Exposure to as little as 2% body
surface area (one hand) of
concentrated HF can be fatal.
```

```
ACGIH TLV:
  HF: 0.5 ppm TWA, 2 ppm ceiling
  HNO3: 2 ppm TWA, 4 ppm STEL

OSHA PEL:
  HF: 3 ppm TWA
  HNO3: 2 ppm TWA
```

## Phase 6 -- Emergency Procedures

Y: 21.3" to 27.8".

| Card | Position | Title | Steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| HF Skin Contact | R1C1 (X: 0.5", Y: 21.3") | HF SKIN CONTACT -- MEDICAL EMERGENCY | 1. Flush with water for 5 minutes (NOT 15 -- get to calcium gluconate fast). 2. Apply CALCIUM GLUCONATE GEL (2.5%) liberally to exposed area. 3. Massage gel into skin continuously. 4. CALL 911 / Emergency Medical Services. 5. Continue calcium gluconate application until medical help arrives. 6. Even if pain is absent, treat as emergency -- delayed onset is the norm. |
| HF Eye Contact | R1C2 (X: 12.0", Y: 21.3") | HF EYE CONTACT -- MEDICAL EMERGENCY | 1. Flush with water or saline for minimum 15 minutes. 2. Hold eyelids open during flush. 3. Seek IMMEDIATE emergency ophthalmological care. 4. HF causes corneal destruction -- permanent blindness is possible. 5. Do NOT apply calcium gluconate to eyes unless directed by physician. |
| NOx Inhalation | R2C1 (X: 0.5", Y: 24.8") | NOx FUME INHALATION (from HNO3) | 1. Move to fresh air immediately. 2. NOx fumes are brown/orange -- visible indicator. 3. DELAYED PULMONARY EDEMA possible 6-24 hours after exposure. 4. ALL NOx exposures require medical monitoring even if symptoms are absent. 5. Administer oxygen if available. |
| General Acid Burn | R2C2 (X: 12.0", Y: 24.8") | ACID BURN (NON-HF) | 1. Flush with large volumes of water for 15-20 minutes. 2. Remove contaminated clothing while flushing. 3. Seek medical attention. 4. If HNO3 concentrated splash: flush immediately -- exothermic oxidation of skin tissue. |

## Phase 7 -- Calcium Gluconate + Ventilation

Y: 28.2" to 32.3".

```
Every shop using HF MUST stock:
  - Calcium gluconate gel (2.5%)
  - Multiple locations near HF baths
  - Clearly labeled and accessible
  - Check expiration dates monthly
  - Train ALL personnel in its use

Calcium gluconate binds free fluoride
ions, preventing systemic calcium
depletion. It is the ONLY field antidote.
```

```
MANDATORY local exhaust ventilation
150-200 FPM minimum at tank edge
Fume scrubber recommended:
  Packed tower with NaOH solution
  Captures HF and NOx fumes

HF fuming increases with temperature
Keep bath temp as low as practical
Post "DANGER -- HF" signs at all access points
```
