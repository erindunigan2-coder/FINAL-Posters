## Phase 4 -- Coating Formation Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `HOW THE COATING FORMS`.

Tank cross-section: phosphate solution (`#E8A020` tint) with Ti-conditioned steel part. Parameters: pH 2.8--3.5 (spray) / 2.5--3.5 (immersion) | 95--200 F | FA 0.5--2.0 pts | TA 15--40 pts | FA:TA 1:10 to 1:20.

Mechanism inset (4-step):
1. ACID ATTACK: H3PO4 dissolves Fe -> Fe2+
2. pH RISE: Local pH at surface rises to ~5.5+
3. CRYSTAL NUCLEATION: Zn3(PO4)2 crystallizes on Ti sites
4. ACCELERATOR: NO2-/NO3- oxidize Fe2+, depolarize cathode

Crystal types: phosphophyllite Zn2Fe(PO4)2.4H2O (harder, better adhesion) vs. hopeite Zn3(PO4)2.4H2O (softer).

## Phase 5 -- Bath Chemistry + FA/TA Ratio

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY -- MULTI-COMPONENT CONTROL`.

Component table:

| Component | Concentration | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Zinc (Zn2+) | 0.8--2.0 g/L (spray) | Primary coating cation |
| Phosphoric acid | 10--25 g/L | Film-forming anion |
| Nickel (Ni2+) | 0.5--1.5 g/L | Grain refinement; promotes phosphophyllite |
| Manganese (Mn2+) | 0.5--1.5 g/L | Grain refinement; corrosion resistance |
| Nitrite (NO2-) | 0.05--0.15 g/L | Accelerator (immersion) |
| Nitrate (NO3-) | 3--8 g/L | Accelerator (spray) |
| Fluoride (F-) | 0.5--2.0 g/L | Required for Al/galvanized |

FA:TA ratio gauge: < 1:10 = heavy/powdery (red) | 1:10 to 1:20 = OPTIMAL (green) | > 1:20 = light/no coating (red). Marker at 1:15.

## Phase 6 -- Coating Weight + P-Ratio

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `COATING WEIGHT AND CRYSTAL QUALITY`.

**Left -- Coating Weight:** Amber accent. 100--200 mg/ft2 = light | 200--500 = MEDIUM (automotive OEM 150--350) | 500--1000+ = heavy/military. Properties: 2--25 um thick, 2--10 um crystal size (conditioned), medium-dark gray, bare SST 4--48 hr, with e-coat+topcoat 500--1500+ hr.

**Right -- P-Ratio & Spray vs. Immersion:** Teal accent. P-ratio = phosphophyllite / (phosphophyllite + hopeite). P-ratio > 0.5 preferred for automotive OEM. Higher Ni promotes phosphophyllite on steel. Spray: 95--130 F, 1--3 min. Immersion: 130--200 F, 3--10 min.

## Phase 7 -- Defect Diagnosis

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS`. 3x2 grid:

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| COARSE CRYSTALS | Conditioner failure | Check/replace conditioner |
| LIGHT COATING | Low TA, low temp, short time, high FA | Increase TA; reduce FA |
| HEAVY/POWDERY | Low FA, excess zinc, excess time | Increase FA; reduce time |
| INCOMPLETE COVERAGE | Oil contamination; cleaner failure | Improve cleaning |
| EXCESS SLUDGE | High iron carryover | Improve filtration |
| MUD CRACKING | Too heavy; dried too fast | Reduce weight; lower oven temp |
