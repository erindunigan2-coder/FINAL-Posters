## Phase 4 -- Substrate Cleaning Paths (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section: `CLEANING BY SUBSTRATE -- THREE PATHS`.

Three parallel vertical columns (Y: 5.0" to 15.0"):

| Column | X | W | Substrate | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Left | 0.5" | 7.33" | Copper / Brass | `#E8A020` |
| Center | 8.16" | 7.33" | Steel | `#2EC4B6` |
| Right | 15.83" | 7.67" | Electronics / Connectors | `#27AE60` |

Each column: 3-4 vertically stacked step boxes with downward arrows. Fill `#1E2435`, left accent in column color, radius 6. Step name: Barlow SemiBold 16pt accent color. Params: JetBrains Mono 12pt. Notes: Inter 12pt at 70%.

**Copper/Brass Path:**
1. `Alkaline Soak Clean` -- `Mild alkaline, 3--6 oz/gal` / `130--160 F, 3--5 min` / `Non-etch formula -- copper is soft`
2. `Rinse` -- `Ambient, flowing`
3. `Acid Dip` -- `5--10% H2SO4, ambient` / `15--30 sec` / `Removes light tarnish and oxides`
4. `Rinse` -> `TO TIN PLATE`

**Steel Path:**
1. `Alkaline Soak Clean` -- `Standard alkaline, 4--8 oz/gal` / `140--180 F, 3--10 min`
2. `Rinse`
3. `Electroclean` -- `Anodic, 3--6 V, 1--3 min` / `Final soil removal`
4. `Rinse`
5. `Acid Activation` -- `HCl 10--20% or H2SO4 5--15%` / `15--60 sec`
6. `Rinse` -> `TO TIN PLATE`

**Electronics/Connectors Path:**
1. `Mild Aqueous Clean` -- `Semi-aqueous or mild alkaline` / `100--130 F, 1--3 min` / `Residue-free critical`
2. `Rinse (DI water)`
3. `Mild Acid Dip` -- `5% H2SO4, 10--20 sec`
4. `DI Rinse` -> `TO TIN PLATE`

## Phase 6 -- Cleaner Chemistry Callout

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHAT IS IN THE CLEANER -- AND WHAT MATTERS FOR TIN`.

**Left -- Cleaner Components (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`. Title: `ALKALINE CLEANER COMPONENTS`.

| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| NaOH / KOH | Saponifies oils, provides alkalinity |
| Surfactants | Emulsify non-saponifiable oils |
| Chelators | Complex hard water ions |
| Phosphates | Water conditioning, buffering |
| Inhibitors | Prevent copper/brass attack |

**Right -- Critical Rules (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`. Title: `RULES FOR TIN SUBSTRATES`.

- `Non-silicated cleaners only -- silicate films cause skip plating`
- `Low alkalinity for copper/brass -- prevent etch attack`
- `No excessive soak time on copper -- surface roughening`
- `Electroclean is optional for copper -- required for heavy steel soil`
- `Water-break-free test is the ONLY acceptance criterion`

Bottom note (Coral): `If the part is not water-break-free, it is not clean. Period.`

## Phase 7 -- Common Mistakes

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `4 CLEANING MISTAKES THAT RUIN TIN DEPOSITS`.

| # | X | Mistake | Result | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | SILICATED CLEANER | Skip plating, bare spots | Switch to non-silicated formula |
| 2 | 6.33" | OVER-ETCHING COPPER | Rough substrate, grainy tin | Reduce temp, time, or alkalinity |
| 3 | 12.16" | SKIPPING ACID DIP | Oxide film remains, poor adhesion | Always acid dip copper/brass |
| 4 | 18.0" | POOR RINSE AFTER CLEAN | Alkaline drag-in, pH spike in acid bath | Improve rinse flow and time |
