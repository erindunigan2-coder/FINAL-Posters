# Poster #660 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Liquid Spray Painting

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of 8. Cleaning for liquid spray painting ranges from simple solvent wipes to multi-stage spray washers. The two-rag method is the hero technique: wet rag dissolves contamination, dry rag removes it before the solvent evaporates and redeposits the contaminant. Three verification methods confirm cleanliness -- from the simple water-break test to contact angle measurement for aerospace QC.

### ZONE 4 -- Two-Rag Method + Tack Cloth

**Section label:** `THE TWO-RAG METHOD AND TACK CLOTH` -- Y: 15.2".

**Two-column layout (Y: 15.8" to 20.8"):**

**Left -- Two-Rag Method (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Callout box, fill `#1E2435`, border 2 pt `#2EC4B6`.
Title: `THE TWO-RAG METHOD -- DO IT RIGHT` -- Barlow SemiBold, 20 pt, `#2EC4B6`

Steps (Inter Medium 14 pt, numbered):
1. `Saturate first rag with solvent (acetone or MEK)`
2. `Wipe surface with wet rag -- dissolves oils, soils, fingerprints`
3. `IMMEDIATELY follow with dry, clean rag`
4. `Dry rag picks up dissolved contamination BEFORE solvent evaporates`
5. `If solvent evaporates before dry rag pass: contamination redeposits on surface`
6. `Work in small sections (2--3 sq ft at a time)`
7. `Replace rags frequently -- a contaminated rag spreads contaminants`

Warning (Coral): `CRITICAL: The dry rag pass must happen while the surface is still wet. If the solvent dries first, you've moved the contamination, not removed it.`

**Right -- Tack Cloth (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Callout box, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`.
Title: `TACK CLOTH -- BETWEEN COATS` -- Barlow SemiBold, 18 pt, `#E8A020`

Body:
- `After sanding between coats, dust remains on the surface`
- `Tack cloth is a cheesecloth impregnated with a sticky resin`
- `Lightly wipe the surface -- tack lifts dust without leaving residue`
- `USE LOW-TACK (SILICONE-FREE) CLOTHS`
- `Silicone-containing tack cloths cause fish-eye defects`

- Body: `Fluorescent oils and compounds glow under 365 nm UV light. Scan surface in darkened area. Glowing spots = residual contamination.`

## Design Notes

The two-rag method detail panel is the poster's anchor -- this is the single most important shop-floor cleaning technique, and most operators do it wrong (they let the solvent evaporate before the dry pass). The four-method comparison gives supervisors a decision framework. The three verification methods scale from "no equipment" to "goniometer" -- something for every quality level. Silicone contamination from tack cloths is the sneaky failure mode that trips up even experienced painters.
