## Phase 5 -- Protective Materials + Storage

Y: 14.5" to 22.0".

Section label: `PROTECTIVE MATERIALS`

| Material | Type | Protection | Use For | Avoid When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PE film | Wrapping, bags | Moisture barrier, physical | Universal first choice | N/A |
| VCI paper/film | Wrapping, interleaving | Vapor corrosion inhibitor | Long-term storage; steel, SS | Copper/brass (some VCI incompatible) |
| Desiccant packs | Moisture control | Absorbs humidity | Sealed bags; humid climates | Loose near parts (particle risk) |
| Foam sheets | Cushioning | Impact and scratch | Between parts; inside containers | Open-cell (traps moisture) |
| Nitrogen purge | Inert atmosphere | Prevents oxidation | Pharma, semi, long-storage | N/A |
| Custom trays | Rigid support | Prevents movement | High-value complex geometry | Simple flat parts |

**Materials to AVOID (Coral):**
`PVC film (HCl offgassing) | Rubber (sulfur attack) | Newspaper/cardboard (ink transfer) | Bare wood (tannic acid stain) | Kraft paper with sulfur`

**Storage conditions (Teal accent):**
`Temp: 15--30 C | Humidity: < 50% RH | Ship within 72 hr if possible | Long-term: sealed + VCI + desiccant | Avoid temperature cycling (condensation)`

## Phase 6 -- Shipping Damage Prevention + Documentation

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Damage prevention (2x2 grid):**

| Damage Type | Accent | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| SCRATCHING / ABRASION | `#E05C5C` | Foam separators between ALL contacts. No metal-on-metal. Immobilize parts in container. |
| CORROSION IN TRANSIT | `#E8A020` | Sealed moisture barrier with VCI + desiccant. Avoid extreme humidity. Consider salt-air (ocean freight). |
