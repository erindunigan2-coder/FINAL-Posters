## Phase 5 -- Solvent Methods + Oil-Free Air + Contamination

### Solvent Methods + Blotter Test (Y: 13.5"-20.0")

Two columns.

Left -- SSPC-SP1 Methods table: Solvent wipe (field, labor-intensive), Vapor degrease (consistent, VOC issues), Immersion tank (bulk, maintenance), Emulsion clean (heavy oils). Rule: `Clean solvent and clean rags. A dirty rag redistributes oil.`

Right -- ASTM D4285 Blotter Test: 5 steps. White blotter 18" from nozzle, 60 sec, inspect. PASS: clean/dry. FAIL: oil or water = fix compressor.

### Contamination Table (Y: 20.0"-26.5")

Section label: `CONTAMINANTS AND THEIR EFFECTS ON PRIMER`. 6-row table: cutting oil, mill scale, flash rust, soluble salts, weld spatter, compressed air oil -- with effects and detection methods.

## Phase 6 -- Mistakes + Safety

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two columns.

Left -- What NOT to Do: Blast without solvent clean (oil into profile). Dirty rags (redistributes). Skip blotter test (invisible oil). Chemical clean after blast (flash rust).

Right -- Safety: Solvent vapors (flammable/toxic, ventilate). Skin contact (gloves). VOC regulations. Compressed air (never aim at skin).
