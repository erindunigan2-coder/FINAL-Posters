## Phase 6B -- Prep Failures + Key Insight

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 4 cards + full-width insight.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| ADHESION LOSS | Insufficient profile or contamination | Re-blast; verify SSPC grade |
| FLASH RUST | Humidity or delay | Prime within 4 hr; humidity < 85% |
| THIN SPOTS ON PEAKS | Profile too deep | Reduce blast; increase DFT |
| MILL SCALE REMAINING | Incomplete blast | Re-blast to SP6; raking light |

Insight `#E8A020`: `Most common failure is not the blast -- it is the time delay. Flash rust forms in 30 min at > 85% RH. Blast and prime same shift.`
