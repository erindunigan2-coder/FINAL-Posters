## Phase 4 -- Cascade Rinse Hero

## Phase 5 -- Rinse Parameters + Drag-Out Callout

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameters:** DI preferred | Ambient | 2--5 gal/min | 2--3 cascade | 30--60 sec per stage | < 500 uS/cm | Drain 10--15 sec | pH check 6--8.

**Right -- Why This Rinse Matters (accent `#E8A020`):**
Alkaline cleaner at pH 12--14 is dragged in on every rack. If it reaches the activation tank, it neutralizes the acid. If it reaches the copper tank, it decomposes organic brighteners -- causing haze, loss of leveling, and accelerated carbon treatment.

Drag-out rate: ~0.5--2.0 gal per 1000 ft2.

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-ACTIVATION RINSE`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alkaline carry-over | Acid neutralized | Insufficient stages | Add cascade; increase flow |
| Brightener decomposition | Hazy copper | Alkaline reaching Cu tank | Improve rinse; monitor conductivity |
| Spotting/staining | White mineral spots | Hard water (Ca, Mg) | Switch to DI; add softener |
| Foam in rinse | Surfactant drag-out | High-foam cleaner | Low-foam cleaner; defoamer |

## Phase 7 -- Safety + Water Quality

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Water Quality:** DI < 10 uS/cm best | City water 200--600 uS/cm acceptable | Hard water > 300 ppm CaCO3 risk | Monitor daily | Dump > 1000 uS/cm.

**Right -- Safety:** Mildly alkaline rinse | Overflow to waste treatment | Wet floors: slip hazard | pH check periodic (6--8).
