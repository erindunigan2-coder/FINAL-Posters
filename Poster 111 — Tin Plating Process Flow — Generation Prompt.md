**Box contents:**

Box 1 -- Alkaline Clean: `STAGE 1` badge `#2EC4B6`. Params: `130--160 F (54--71 C)` / `3--6 oz/gal` / `3--5 min`. Purpose: `Remove oils, shop soil, drawing compounds`. Check: `Water-break-free after rinse`.

Box 2 -- Rinse: `STAGE 2` badge `#2EC4B6`. Params: `Ambient` / `Flowing or cascade`. Purpose: `Remove alkaline cleaner residue`. Check: `Prevents alkaline drag-in to acid activation`.

Box 3 -- Acid Activation: `STAGE 3` badge `#E8A020`. Params: `5--10% H2SO4` / `Ambient, 15--30 sec`. Purpose: `Remove surface oxides, expose clean metal`. Check: `Substrate-dependent -- copper/brass standard`.

Box 4 -- Rinse: `STAGE 4` badge `#2EC4B6`. Params: `Ambient` / `Flowing or cascade`. Purpose: `Remove activation acid`. Check: `Acid drag-in lowers bath pH and wastes tin`.

Box 5 -- Tin Plate: `STAGE 5` badge `#27AE60`. Params: `Sn2+: 20--30 g/L` / `H2SO4: 130--170 g/L (sulfate)` / `60--85 F (16--29 C)` / `10--30 ASF (rack)`. Purpose: `Electrodeposit tin onto substrate`. Check: `Sn2+ oxidation to Sn4+ is the #1 bath killer`.

Box 6 -- Rinse: `STAGE 6` badge `#2EC4B6`. Params: `Ambient` / `Flowing, multi-stage`. Purpose: `Remove acid drag-out before post-treatment`. Check (Coral): `CRITICAL: Fast rinse prevents white haze (stannic oxide)`.

Box 7 -- Anti-Tarnish: `STAGE 7` badge `#E8A020`. Params: `Proprietary dip` / `Ambient, 15--30 sec`. Purpose: `Protect tin surface from oxidation and discoloration`. Check: `Required for solderability shelf life`.

Box 8 -- Reflow / Dry: `STAGE 8` badge `#E8A020`. Params: `Reflow: 450--500 F (232--260 C)` / `Or: forced air dry` / `Hot air leveling optional`. Purpose: `Fuse tin deposit -- eliminates whisker risk`. Check (Coral): `Reflow is the primary tin whisker mitigation`.

## Phase 6 -- Sulfate vs. MSA Comparison

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `ACID SULFATE VS. MSA -- WHICH TIN BATH?`

**Left -- Acid Sulfate Tin (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`.
Title: `ACID SULFATE TIN` / Subtitle: `The Legacy Standard`.

Properties: Tin source: Stannous sulfate (SnSO4) | Acid: Sulfuric acid (H2SO4) | Sn2+: 15--45 g/L | Temp: 60--85 F | CD: 10--30 ASF | Cath. eff: 85--95% | Throwing power: Moderate | Agitation: Mechanical (no air) | Cost: Lower | Best for: General purpose, barrel tin.

Bottom bar: `Lower cost, proven chemistry -- but lower CD capability limits high-speed applications`.

**Right -- MSA Tin (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#27AE60`.
Title: `MSA (METHANE SULFONIC ACID) TIN` / Subtitle: `The Modern Standard`.

Properties: Tin source: Stannous methane sulfonate | Acid: MSA | Sn2+: 20--55 g/L | Temp: 60--100 F | CD: 10--100 ASF | Cath. eff: 90--99% | Throwing power: Better than sulfate | Agitation: Mechanical (no air) | Cost: Higher | Best for: High-speed reel-to-reel, connectors.

Bottom bar: `Higher CD capability (100+ ASF), better throwing power, superior solderability -- the future of tin`.
