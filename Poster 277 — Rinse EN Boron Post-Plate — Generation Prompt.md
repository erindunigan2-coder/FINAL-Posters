## Phase 4 -- Post-Plate Rinse Sequence Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `FROM EN-B BATH TO RINSE TO DRYING`.

Horizontal 4-step flow:

**Step 1 -- EN-B Bath Exit (accent `#27AE60`):**
- Parts exit with Ni2+, DMAB, borate, complexant, dimethylamine drag-out
- pH 6--8 (DMAB) or 12--14 (NaBH4)
- `Do NOT air-dry before rinse` `#E05C5C`

**Step 2 -- Cold DI Rinse (accent `#2EC4B6`, border 2pt):**
- `COLD WATER PREFERRED` 18pt
- DI counterflow 2--3 stages, 15--25 C, 30--60 sec, <50 uS/cm
- `Dimethylamine volatilizes at rinse -- ventilate` `#E8A020`

**Step 3 -- Handling (accent `#E8A020`):**
- Clean nitrile gloves, no fingerprints, no stacking wet, handle by edges

**Step 4 -- Drying (accent `#E8A020`):**
- Air knife or forced air, low-temp oven 60--80 C, remove all moisture before HT

Arrows 3pt `#3A4055` between steps.

## Phase 6 -- Handling + Dimethylamine Safety

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `HANDLING AND SAFETY -- WHAT IS DIFFERENT ABOUT EN-B`.

**Left -- Surface Handling Rules (accent `#E8A020`):**
1. NEVER touch with bare hands -- fingerprint oils etch into deposit `#E05C5C`
2. Do not stack parts wet -- galvanic attack
3. Do not air-dry before rinsing -- watermarks
4. Transfer rinse to drying immediately
5. Inspect before drying
6. Parts must be completely dry before heat treatment

**Right -- Dimethylamine Byproduct (accent `#E05C5C`):**
- DMAB produces dimethylamine ((CH3)2NH)
- Volatile: boils at 7 C
- Flammable: flash point -17 C (pure)
- Strong fishy/ammonia odor
- Low hazard in dilute rinse but ventilation required
- OSHA PEL: 10 ppm TWA `#E8A020`
