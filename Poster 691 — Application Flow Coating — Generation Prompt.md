## Phase 5 -- Application Methods Table

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `FLOW COATING METHODS`.

| Method | Description | DFT | Best For | Uniformity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Curtain Flow | Coating from weir/slot die; part through on conveyor | 0.5-2.0 mils | Flat panels, sheet | +/- 20-30% |
| Flood/Flow | Nozzles flood from above; gravity pulls down | 0.5-3.0 mils | Large 3D parts: tanks, enclosures | +/- 30-50% |
| Dip-Drain (Hybrid) | Brief immersion, withdraw, drain | 1.0-3.0 mils | Small-medium parts | +/- 20-40% |

Footnote `#E8A020`: `Flood/flow is most common for industrial steel fabrication.`

## Phase 6 -- Viscosity and Recirculation Control

Y: 21.3" to 26.3". Section label: `VISCOSITY CONTROL -- THE #1 DAY-TO-DAY CHALLENGE`.

**Left -- `VISCOSITY DRIFT` (`#E8A020`):** Solvent evaporates from open tank. Rate 1-3 sec/hr Zahn #2 drift. Measure every hour. Correct with solvent per TDS. `Never add reducer by "feel."`

**Right -- `RECIRCULATION SYSTEM CARE` (`#2EC4B6`):** Filter 60-100 mesh continuously. Strain for cured skin every shift. Cover tank when idle (reduces solvent loss 50%+). Agitate during downtime. 2K: pot life applies to ENTIRE reservoir.

## Phase 6B -- Failures + Insight

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 4 cards + insight.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| THIN FILM / POOR COVERAGE | Viscosity too low or flow insufficient | Check Zahn; increase viscosity/flow |
| EXCESSIVE FILM / RUNS | Viscosity high or drain too short | Reduce viscosity; extend drain |
| HOLIDAYS (MISSED AREAS) | Shielded surfaces | Reposition; add nozzles; touch-up spray |
| CONTAMINATION IN FILM | Debris from unfiltered system | Clean filter; strain reservoir |

Insight `#E8A020`: `Viscosity is to flow coating what current density is to electroplating -- it is the single variable that controls film build, coverage, and appearance. Check it hourly. Adjust it carefully. Log it always.`
