## Phase 6 -- Advantage Cards + Troubleshooting

### Arc Spray Advantage (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `WHY ARC SPRAY -- THE STRUCTURAL PROTECTION WORKHORSE`. Three cards:

**Card 1 -- HIGHEST DEPOSITION RATE (`#27AE60`):** 5--30+ kg/hr -- fastest of all thermal spray. Covers large structural areas rapidly. Compared to flame spray 2--8, plasma 2--10 kg/hr.

**Card 2 -- LOWEST COST PER AREA (`#E8A020`):** Simple equipment: DC power + air + wire. No combustion gases. Wire feedstock cheaper than powder. Portable for field use.

**Card 3 -- PROVEN SERVICE LIFE (`#2EC4B6`):** Zinc on structural steel (sealed, per AWS C2.18): Rural >40 yr, Urban/industrial 20--40 yr, Marine/coastal 15--25 yr.

### Troubleshooting Quick Hits (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| POOR ADHESION | Insufficient blast profile or oil contamination | Re-blast to Ra 4--12 um; verify oil-free air |
| EXCESSIVE POROSITY | Moisture in air or standoff too far | Install air dryer; reduce standoff to 100--200 mm |
| UNEVEN SPRAY PATTERN | Wire feed speed imbalance | Synchronize wire feed; check contact tips |
| HIGH FUME GENERATION | Normal at high dep rates; inadequate ventilation | Increase LEV airflow; mandatory PAPR for zinc |
