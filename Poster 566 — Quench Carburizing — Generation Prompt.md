## Phase 4 -- Quench Media Comparison (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `QUENCH MEDIA -- SEVERITY, SPEED, AND SELECTION`.

**Left -- H-Factor Table (W: 14.0", Coral accent):** `GROSSMANN H-FACTOR REFERENCE`.

| Medium | H-Factor | Cooling Rate | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Still oil | 0.25--0.30 | Slow | Low distortion, good hardenability steels |
| Agitated oil (moderate) | 0.35--0.50 | Standard | General production carburizing |
| Agitated oil (vigorous) | 0.50--0.80 | Fast | Higher hardenability demand |
| Marquench (hot oil) 250--400 F | 0.20--0.35 | Controlled | Reduced distortion; held above Ms |
| Polymer (10--20% PAG) | 0.30--0.80 | Adjustable | Cleaner than oil; variable severity |
| Agitated water | 1.0--1.5 | Severe | Rarely for carburized parts -- cracking risk |
| HPGQ N2 (10 bar) | 0.10--0.20 | Slow-moderate | Vacuum furnace quenching |
| HPGQ He (20 bar) | 0.20--0.35 | Moderate-fast | Aerospace vacuum carburizing |

**Right -- Selection Guide (W: 8.5", Amber accent):** The tradeoff: harder quench = more martensite = more distortion. Most carburized gears: H = 0.35--0.70 (8620, 4320, 9310). Precision gears: press quench or marquench. Plain carbon: need higher H. Alloy steels: milder quench OK.

## Phase 5 -- Oil Quench Parameters

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `OIL QUENCH -- PARAMETERS AND HAZARDS`.

Three columns:

**Operating Parameters (Amber):** Standard 100--160 F, hot 250--400 F. Agitation 50--200 ft/min. Transfer time: seconds. Duration: until core below 300 F.

**Oil Maintenance (Emerald):** Monitor viscosity, flash point, water content (<0.05%), cooling curve (ASTM D6200), oxidation number. Replace when flash point drops or viscosity increases >20%.

**Water-in-Oil Hazard (Coral):** THE MOST DANGEROUS CONDITION. 0.1% water = VIOLENT BOIL-OVER. 1500 F parts flash water to steam (1,700x expansion). Sources: roof leaks, condensation, wet parts, cooler leaks. Test oil for water weekly.

## Phase 6 -- Distortion Control + Critical Rules

**Distortion (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `DISTORTION CONTROL STRATEGIES`. 2x2 grid:
1. Press Quench (Emerald) -- die constrains during oil quench; gold standard for gears
2. Plug Quench (Teal) -- expandable plug in bore; controls roundness and taper
3. Marquench / Hot Oil (Amber) -- oil at 250--400 F, above Ms; equalize temp then air cool
4. Part Orientation (Amber) -- thin sections first; bore vertical for ring gears; uniform oil flow

**Critical Rules (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `FOUR RULES FOR EVERY QUENCH`. Four cards, Coral accent:
1. SPEED MATTERS -- seconds of delay = pearlite at surface = unfixable soft spots
2. UNIFORMITY -- non-uniform quench = non-uniform hardness = non-uniform distortion
3. OIL HEALTH -- degraded oil = inconsistent parts; test cooling curves regularly
4. FIRE AWARENESS -- 1500 F + oil + water = quench fire; know where suppression is
