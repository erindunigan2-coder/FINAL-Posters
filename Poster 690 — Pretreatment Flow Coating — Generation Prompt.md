## Phase 4 -- Iron vs. Zinc Phosphate Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `IRON PHOSPHATE vs. ZINC PHOSPHATE -- CHOOSING YOUR PRETREATMENT`.

**Left -- Iron Phosphate (`#2EC4B6`, badge STANDARD):** 25-75 mg/ft2. Amorphous. Blue-to-gold. 100-140 F. 60-120 sec. 3-5 stages. 500-750 hr B117. Minimal sludge. 1x cost. `Best for general industrial flow coating.`

**Right -- Zinc Phosphate (`#E8A020`, badge HIGH PERFORMANCE):** 150-500 mg/ft2. Crystalline (hopeite/phosphophyllite). Gray. 95-115 F. 120-180 sec. 8-10 stages. 750-1,500+ hr B117. Significant sludge. 3-5x cost. `Specify when B117 > 750 hr or severe exposure.`

## Phase 5 -- Selection Guide

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `SELECTION GUIDE -- WHICH PRETREATMENT?`.

| Application | Environment | Pretreatment | Coating Weight | B117 Hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| General industrial | Indoor / mild outdoor | Iron phosphate | 25-75 mg/ft2 | 500-750 |
| Structural steel | Moderate outdoor | Iron phosphate (heavy) | 50-75 mg/ft2 | 600-750 |
| Agricultural / heavy equip | Severe outdoor | Zinc phosphate | 150-300 mg/ft2 | 750-1,200 |
| Extended warranty / OEM | Per spec | Zinc phosphate | 200-500 mg/ft2 | 1,000-1,500+ |
| Multi-metal (steel + Al) | Any | Zirconium nanoceramic | 5-30 mg/ft2 | 500-750 |

Footnote `#E8A020`: `Zirconium coatings are chrome-free, low-sludge, multi-metal capable. Growing adoption.`

## Phase 6B -- Failures + Insight

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 4 cards + insight.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| LOW COATING WEIGHT | Chemistry out of range or low temp | Check total acid; replenish |
| UNEVEN PHOSPHATE | Oil/cleaner residue | Improve cleaning; water break |
| FLASH RUST BEFORE COATING | Time delay | Coat within 4 hr; seal rinse |
| EXCESSIVE SLUDGE (ZnPO4) | Normal byproduct unmanaged | Continuous filtration; settling tank |

Insight `#E8A020`: `Iron phosphate is not inferior -- it is appropriate. For general industrial flow coating, 50+ mg/ft2 delivers 500-750 hr B117. Zinc phosphate is only justified when the spec demands it.`
