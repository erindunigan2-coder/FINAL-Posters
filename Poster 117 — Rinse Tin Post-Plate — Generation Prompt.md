## Phase 6 -- White Haze Prevention

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHITE HAZE -- THE POST-PLATE RINSE DEFECT`.

**Left -- What Is It (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):** Accent `#E05C5C`. Title: `WHITE HAZE = STANNIC OXIDE`.
`Freshly plated tin + air + residual acid = Sn4+ oxide film` / `White, milky haze` / `Destroys solderability` / `Cannot be removed without stripping` / `Forms in 30--60 sec with acid residue`.

**Right -- Prevention (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):** Accent `#27AE60`. Title: `PREVENTION`.
`Transfer in < 30 sec` / `Immerse completely` / `Use flowing rinse` / `Move to anti-tarnish immediately` / `Never leave tinned parts in air between stages` / `DI water reduces mineral spotting`.

Bottom note: `White haze is a speed problem, not a chemistry problem. Move faster.`
