# Plating Posters Inc — Project Home

## What This Is

**Plating Posters Inc** is a startup venture producing high-quality, educational posters focused on metal finishing and plating processes. These are physical, print-ready products — not internal documents. Plating shops hang them on plant walls for quick employee reference, or display them in boardrooms to demonstrate technical credibility to customers.

Every poster must clear two bars simultaneously:
1. **Technically accurate** — content grounded in real process chemistry, industry best practice, and validated field knowledge
2. **Aesthetically excellent** — polished and boardroom-quality, with a functional reference layer that makes them genuinely useful to a process engineer. Think: something you'd be proud to hang in an executive conference room that a lab tech would also actually reach for.

Every poster is a **sellable product** in the Plating Posters Inc product line — dual format: physical print and digital download. Design decisions must serve both.

## Design Philosophy (Drew's Direction — 2026-03-20)

Drew has delegated all design and typography decisions to Alaina. His guidance:

- **Alaina owns design decisions.** Drew is not a design expert and does not intend to second-guess creative choices. He trusts Alaina's judgment.
- **Research current marketing trends.** Alaina should actively look at what is visually compelling in modern technical marketing, educational design, and premium print publications — and draw inspiration from those sources. The posters must look like they belong alongside the best contemporary work in this space, not like clip-art science posters.
- **Informative first, beautiful second — but both bars must be cleared.** Technical accuracy is non-negotiable. Aesthetics are not optional. A poster that is accurate but dull will not sell. A poster that is gorgeous but wrong will not be trusted.
- **Expect iteration.** Drew anticipates that fonts, layouts, and visual choices will evolve across early posters before the series locks in. This is normal and expected — do not treat early choices as permanent until they have been validated across multiple designs.

## Design Standards (Series-Wide)

Full standards reference: [[Plating Posters Inc — Series Design Standards]]

Summary of locked decisions as of 2026-03-16:

- **Primary design tool**: Claude Chat (visual artifact generation) — confirmed 2026-04-20; replaces prior tools
- **Print resolution**: 300 DPI minimum
- **Standard poster sizes**: 18×24" and 24×36"
- **Readability target**: All text legible from 3–8 feet at finished print size
- **Format**: Dual — physical print and digital download. Every poster must be designed for both.
- **Visual direction (LOCKED)**: Polished and boardroom-quality, with a genuine functional reference layer. Not decorative-only; not purely utilitarian.
- **Product framing (LOCKED)**: Every poster is a sellable product, not an internal document.
- **Editions (LOCKED)**: Every poster in both a **Dark edition** (flagship) and a **Light edition** (accessible print). Six export files per poster.
- **Process scope (LOCKED)**: **One plating process per poster.** No exceptions.
- **Illustration approach**: Alaina produces construction workups → Elara engineers generation prompts → Drew generates poster in Claude Chat (SVG/HTML artifact). (Original Affinity Designer spec superseded 2026-03-18; Canva workflow superseded 2026-04-20.)
- **Hull cell panel illustration**: Built as layered geometric shapes — confirmed 2026-03-20.
- **Color palette (LOCKED 2026-03-16)**: Gunmetal Dark `#1A1F2E` background / Warm White `#F0EDE8` text / Amber `#E8A020` HCD accent / Teal `#2EC4B6` LCD accent / Emerald `#27AE60` positive / Coral `#E05C5C` problem — see [[Plating Posters Inc — Series Design Standards]] for full hex table and Light edition remap.
- **Typography (LOCKED 2026-03-16)**: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold (headlines) / Barlow SemiBold (subheadings) / Inter Regular/Medium (body) / JetBrains Mono Regular (data tables) — see [[Plating Posters Inc — Series Design Standards]] for full type scale.
- **Logo placement**: TBD — pending brand identity confirmation
