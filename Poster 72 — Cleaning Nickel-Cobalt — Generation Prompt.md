## Phase 5 -- Cleaning Methods Comparison

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THREE CLEANING METHODS -- KNOW YOUR OPTIONS`.

Three side-by-side callout boxes:

**SOAK CLEAN** (accent `#2EC4B6`): 4--8 oz/gal | 140--180 F, 3--10 min | Non-silicated alkaline | Primary step for most substrates | Limitation: may not remove heavy lapping compound alone.

**ELECTROCLEANER** (accent `#E8A020`): 4--8 oz/gal | 120--160 F, 1--3 min | Anodic preferred or cathodic | Gas scrubbing dislodges embedded soil | Cathodic RISK: H-embrittlement on high-strength steel `#E05C5C`.

**SOLVENT DEGREASE** (accent `#C8D0D8`): Vapor or immersion | Per solvent spec | Precision degreasing for heavy machining oils | Being phased out in favor of aqueous.

## Phase 6 -- Substrate Decision Table

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `SUBSTRATE-SPECIFIC CLEANING -- WHAT CHANGES?`.

| Substrate | Soak Clean | Electrocleaner | Special Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon/alloy steel | Standard 4--8 oz/gal | Anodic preferred | None |
| Hardened steel (>40 HRC) | Standard | Anodic ONLY | H-embrittlement risk -- bake 375 F / 4 hr if cathodic |
| Inconel / Waspaloy | Standard + extended time | Anodic | Wood's strike required after activation |
| Titanium | Standard | Optional | HF/HNO3 etch after clean; Wood's strike mandatory |
| Copper alloys | Standard | Anodic | Avoid excessive alkalinity -- can tarnish Cu |

Special requirement warnings in `#E05C5C`.

## Phase 7 -- Common Cleaning Failures + Footer

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Four problem cards:

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | WATER BREAK | Insufficient cleaning or exhausted cleaner | Increase time/temp; replenish concentration |
| 2 | SKIP PLATING | Silicate residue from silicated cleaner | Switch to non-silicated; acid dip to remove film |
| 3 | ADHESION FAILURE | Oil not removed; wrong electrocleaner polarity | Extend soak; verify anodic vs. cathodic |
| 4 | PITTING ON DEPOSIT | Organic contamination into plating bath | Carbon treat cleaning bath; improve rinsing |
