# Poster #101 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Copper (Acid) -- Post-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 6 of the acid copper process. This rinse removes copper sulfate and sulfuric acid drag-out from the plated parts before anti-tarnish or the next plating step (typically nickel). The critical issue: copper is a severe contaminant in nickel baths (causes dark deposits at LCD) and in zinc baths (causes black spots and dark LCD). If parts move downstream after acid copper, the post-plate rinse quality directly determines whether you contaminate the next bath.

Hero visual: cascade rinse with cross-contamination pathway diagram.

### ZONE 5 -- Cross-Contamination Warning + Problems

**BLOCK E -- Cross-Contamination Warning (Y: 21.2" to 24.0")**

Full-width callout, fill `#1E2435`, border 2 pt `#E05C5C`, accent `#E05C5C`.

Title: `COPPER IS A CONTAMINANT DOWNSTREAM` -- Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 24 pt `#E05C5C`

Two-column interior:

**Left -- Copper in Nickel Baths:**
- `Threshold: > 5 ppm Cu` JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#E05C5C`
- `Effect: dark deposit at LCD, haze, reduced ductility`
- `Fix: dummy plate at 2--5 ASF, then Hull cell check`
- `Prevention: thorough post-copper rinsing`

**Right -- Copper in Zinc Baths:**
- `Threshold: > 5 ppm Cu` JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#E05C5C`
- `Effect: black spots, dark LCD, immersion copper on zinc anode`
- `Fix: dummy plate, then zinc purification`
- `Prevention: segregate copper and zinc lines; rinse aggressively`

Bottom rule: `Any time copper-plated parts share a line with nickel or zinc, post-copper rinsing is the single most important contamination control.` Inter Medium 14 pt `#E05C5C`

**BLOCK F -- Problem Table (Y: 24.3" to 26.8")**

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue-green rinse water | Copper contamination building | Insufficient flow; no drag-out tank | Add cascade; install drag-out recovery |
| Dark LCD in nickel | Cu contamination in Ni bath | Copper drag-in from poor rinsing | Improve rinse; dummy plate Ni bath |
| Tarnish on copper surface | Copper oxidation before next step | Parts drying between rinse and treatment | Minimize transfer time; keep parts wet |
| Staining after dry | Water spots on bright copper | Hard water minerals | Use DI in final rinse stage |

## Design Notes

The unique angle for the post-plate rinse is cross-contamination. In the zinc cluster, the post-plate rinse story was about protecting the passivate. In acid copper, the story is about protecting everything downstream -- nickel, zinc, or any other bath that sees copper as a poison. The contamination threshold callout in coral makes this the central teaching point.

The drag-out recovery tank is featured prominently because it is both an economic and environmental win. Many shops skip it. This poster should make them think twice.
