## Phase 4 -- Fixture Materials (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `FIXTURE MATERIALS -- THE FIXTURES GET TREATED TOO`.

**Two large cards:**

**Low-Carbon Steel Fixtures (Teal):** 1018/1020 wire, rod, hooks. Inexpensive, readily fabricated. Fixtures get nitrocarburized too -- compound zone builds up over cycles, making them brittle. Periodic replacement required.

**Fixture Configurations (Amber):** Hanging (wire hooks, rod racks -- best drainage), Basket (wire mesh for small parts -- avoid nesting), Custom (specific geometry -- ensure drainage on removal).

**Full-width note:** `FIXTURE REPLACEMENT SCHEDULE` -- After 50--100+ cycles, nitride layer makes fixtures brittle. Inspect every load. Replace cracked/brittle/distorted fixtures. A broken fixture dropping parts into molten salt is a safety incident.

## Phase 5 -- Drainage + Bath Transfer

### Salt Drainage (Y: 14.5"--22.0")

Section label: `DRAINAGE ORIENTATION -- THE CRITICAL FIXTURING RULE`.

**Left -- Why Drainage Matters (Emerald):** Trapped salt sequence: removed from bath -> cupped feature traps salt -> contaminated rinse -> uneven polish -> staining under Q2 -> salt residue on finished part -> failed corrosion. FIX: Orient every part for free drainage.

**Right -- Orientation Guidelines (Teal):** Open end DOWN for cups. Bore axis at angle. Drain holes at lowest point. Flat parts vertical or angled. Specific: cylinders open end down, gears hang from bore, shafts vertical. WHEN IN DOUBT: dip fixtured load in water first -- watch where water pools -- those are salt traps.

### Bath-to-Bath Transfer (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `TRANSFER BETWEEN BATHS -- Q-P-Q LOGISTICS`.

Five-step horizontal flow: Preheat Oven (600--700 F) -> FNC Salt Bath (1050--1075 F) -> Oxidizing Quench Q1 (700--800 F) -> Hot Water Rinse -> Polish/Q2/Final Rinse.

Key notes: Minimize transfer time FNC to oxidizing bath. Always lift fixtures SLOWLY from salt baths for drainage and splash reduction.

## Phase 6 -- Loading Rules

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `LOADING RULES FOR SALT BATH FNC`.

Four quick-reference cards, left accent `#2EC4B6`:

| Rule | Content |
|---|---|
| SPACING | Less critical than gas -- liquid salt provides uniform heat. Avoid part-to-part contact (uncoated spots). |
| DRAINAGE | Orient ALL parts for free drainage. No cupping. No trapped salt. #1 fixturing priority. |
| FIXTURE CONDITION | Inspect before every load. Replace cracked/brittle/distorted. Fixture failure in molten salt = safety emergency. |
| SLOW IMMERSION | Lower into bath slowly and steadily. Rapid immersion causes splash. Two-person for heavy loads. |
