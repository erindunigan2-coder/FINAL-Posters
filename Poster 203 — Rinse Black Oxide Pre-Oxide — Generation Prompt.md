## Phase 5 -- Safety Warning -- Hot Caustic Spattering

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `SAFETY -- WATER INTO HOT CAUSTIC`.

Large safety panel, border 2 pt `#E05C5C`. Title: `DANGER: VIOLENT SPATTERING HAZARD`.

**Left -- THE PHYSICS:** Bath operates at 280--295 F -- above water's boiling point. Water on parts INSTANTLY boils and expands ~1,700x (liquid to steam). Result: explosive spattering of concentrated hot caustic (600--900 g/L NaOH at 285 F).

**Right -- PREVENTION:** Rinse thoroughly (multiple dips if needed), DRAIN until dripping stops completely, tilt and rotate baskets, shake parts free of trapped water, pre-heat in hot water rinse (optional). PPE: full face shield, chemical apron, long gloves, heat-resistant over-gloves.

## Phase 6 -- Acid Carryover Consequences

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT ACID CARRYOVER DOES TO YOUR OXIDE BATH`.

Four consequence cards:
1. NEUTRALIZES NaOH -- acid consumes caustic; concentration drops (`#E05C5C`)
2. RED OXIDE -- diluted caustic = lower operating temp = red/brown instead of black (`#E05C5C`)
3. SPATTERING -- acid-water mix entering hot caustic = violent reaction (`#E05C5C`)
4. SLUDGE BUILDUP -- dissolved iron from pickle acid precipitates as sludge (`#E8A020`)

## Phase 7 -- Drain Time + Best Practices

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `DRAIN TIME AND BEST PRACTICES`.

**Left -- Drain Time Guide:**

| Part Type | Minimum Drain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Flat parts / plates | 15--30 sec | Tilt to shed water |
| Baskets of small parts | 30--60 sec | Shake basket; rotate |
| Complex geometries | 60--120 sec | Multiple angles; blind holes trap water |
| Threaded parts | 60--120 sec | Spin or blow out threads |
| Tubes / hollow parts | 60--120 sec | Orient vertically |

**Right -- Six Best Practices:**
1. Two-stage rinse: immersion + spray
2. Check pH on part surface -- must be neutral (6--8)
3. Drain until NO dripping -- count to 10 after last drop
4. Watch for trapped water in cups, channels, threads
5. Hot water pre-heat dip before oxide bath if in doubt
6. Never rush this step -- 30 extra seconds prevents a safety incident
