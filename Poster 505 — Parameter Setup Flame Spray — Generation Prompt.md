## Phase 5 -- Standoff/Angle + Test Coupon

### Standoff & Angle (Y: 15.5"-22.0")

Section label: `STANDOFF DISTANCE & SPRAY ANGLE`.

**Left -- STANDOFF DISTANCE (W: 11.0", accent `#2EC4B6`):** Three bars:
- Too close (<150 mm): `#E05C5C` -- Substrate overheating; coating stress
- Optimal (150-250/300 mm): `#27AE60` -- Best density and adhesion
- Too far (>300 mm): `#E05C5C` -- High porosity; poor adhesion; cold particles

**Right -- SPRAY ANGLE (W: 11.5", accent `#E8A020`):** Three bars:
- 90 deg (perpendicular): `#27AE60` -- Maximum density and bond strength
- 60-75 deg: `#E8A020` -- Acceptable; slight porosity increase
- <45 deg: `#E05C5C` -- Shadowing; severe porosity -- avoid

Below: `Flame spray is more sensitive to standoff and angle than HVOF because particle velocity is lower.`

### Test Coupon Checklist (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `PRE-PRODUCTION TEST COUPON SEQUENCE`. 6 steps, 2 columns.

| Step | Action | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select coupon -- same substrate material and thickness | Match alloy and heat-treat condition |
| 2 | Grit blast to same profile spec | Ra 4-12 um; SSPC-SP 5 or SP 10 |

| 4 | Measure thickness per pass | Record microns per pass |
| 5 | Build to target thickness on second coupon | Verify total thickness and visual quality |
| 6 | Bend test or adhesion spot-check if required | Document results before production |

## Phase 6 -- Common Parameter Errors

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `COMMON PARAMETER ERRORS`. Four cards, accent `#E05C5C`.

| Error | Result | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| WIRE FEED TOO FAST | Spitting, unmelted particles | Reduce feed rate until smooth pattern |
| STANDOFF TOO FAR | Porous, poorly bonded; powdery | Move closer: 150-250 mm (wire), 150-300 mm (powder) |
| OXIDIZING FLAME | Excessive oxide; brittle deposit | Adjust to neutral -- equal inner cones |
| AIR PRESSURE TOO LOW (WIRE) | Coarse droplets; rough, porous | Increase atomizing air to 40-80 PSI |
