## Phase 5 -- Water Break Test

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THE WATER BREAK TEST -- YOUR GO / NO-GO CHECK`.

**Left -- PASS -- WATER SHEETS** (accent `#27AE60`): Water forms continuous, unbroken film across entire surface. No beading, no dewetting, no dry spots. All oils/soils removed. Surface is chemically clean. Action: PROCEED TO ACID PICKLE (Stage 3).

**Right -- FAIL -- WATER BEADS** (accent `#E05C5C`): Water pulls away from areas of residual contamination -- beads, breaks, or dewets. Oil, soil, or silicate residue still present. Action: RETURN TO CLEANING (Stage 1).

## Phase 7 -- Rinse Best Practices

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `RINSE BEST PRACTICES FOR BLACK OXIDE`.

Six rules:
1. Overflow continuously -- stagnant rinse water accumulates cleaner
2. Water-break test every load -- visual confirmation takes 5 seconds
3. Change rinse water when parts start emerging with beading
4. Monitor conductivity -- rising values = cleaner buildup
5. Spray rinse after immersion for complex geometries
6. Black oxide is less forgiving than most processes -- rinse like it matters
