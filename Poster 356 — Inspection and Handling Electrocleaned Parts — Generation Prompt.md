## Phase 5 -- Electrocleaning-Specific Visual Criteria

Y: 14.5" to 20.0". Section label: `VISUAL CRITERIA -- DEFECTS UNIQUE TO ELECTROCLEANING`.

Four coral-accented glass cards in a row:

| Defect | Appearance | Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| CATHODIC SMUT | Dark gray/black residue | Dissolved metals plating during cathodic mode | Switch anodic; carbon treat; partial dump |
| GAS PITTING | Tiny pinholes/dimples | Excessive CD; gas impinging too aggressively | Reduce CD; improve agitation |
| ETCHING | Dull, rough, matte surface | Chloride >10 g/L; low alkalinity; excess voltage | Check chlorides; maintain alk; reduce voltage |
| STAINING | Discolored spots/streaks after rinse | Metal contamination in bath; inadequate rinsing | Carbon treat; improve rinse; check dissolved metals |

## Phase 6 -- The 5-Minute Rule

Y: 20.0" to 25.0". Section label: `THE 5-MINUTE RULE -- TIME IS YOUR ENEMY`.

Full-width coral-tinted glass panel, three columns:

**The Rule:** `5 MINUTES` (Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 36 pt `#E05C5C`) / Transfer to acid activation within 5 minutes. Oxide formation begins immediately on clean metal.

**What Happens at 5+ Min:** Steel: light oxide / Copper: tarnish / Zinc die cast: white oxide / Aluminum: native oxide reforms. Any require re-cleaning or more aggressive acid activation.

**How to Comply:** Stage electrocleaner near acid tank / Do not allow air-dry / If delay unavoidable, keep submerged in clean rinse / Never allow parts to sit on bench.

## Phase 7 -- Handling Rules

Y: 25.0" to 32.5". Section label: `HANDLING AFTER ELECTROCLEANING -- THE RULES`.

2x3 grid of glass cards:

| Rule | Detail | Accent |
|---|---|---|
| CLEAN GLOVES ONLY | Any fingerprint = instant water break failure. Change if contaminated. | `#2EC4B6` |
| NO AIR-DRYING | Air-dried surfaces oxidize and require re-cleaning. Keep wet. | `#E05C5C` |
| NO BARE HANDS | Skin oils create invisible organic films detected by water break. | `#E05C5C` |
| RACK TIPS CLEAN | Corroded tips cause poor contact (uneven gas) and transfer contamination. | `#2EC4B6` |
| PROCESS IMMEDIATELY | The 5-minute rule is not a suggestion. Stage line for direct transfer. | `#E05C5C` |
| DOCUMENT FAILURES | Log every water break failure with date, rack, mode. Patterns reveal root causes. | `#2EC4B6` |
