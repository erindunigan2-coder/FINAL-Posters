## Phase 4 -- Electrodeposition Mechanism Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `HOW CATHODIC ELECTRODEPOSITION WORKS`.

Tank cross-section schematic: Body (cathode -) center, anodes (+) on walls in anode boxes (316 SS), DC 200--400 V. Bath: 18--22% solids, pH 5.8--6.2, 85--95 F, 1,000--1,800 uS/cm. Micelle migration arrows from bath to cathode.

Cathode surface callout: 1. Water electrolysis: 2H2O + 2e- -> H2 + 2OH- / 2. Local pH rises / 3. Cationic resin neutralizes, becomes insoluble / 4. Dense film deposits / 5. Film insulates -- current stops -- deposition moves to uncoated areas.

## Phase 6 -- Anode Management + Self-Limiting

Y: 21.2" to 26.3". Two-column.

**Left -- `ANODE MANAGEMENT` (`#E8A020`):**
Enclosed in anode boxes with dialysis membranes. Anolyte bled off for pH/conductivity control. Cathodic: 316 SS. Anodic: Carbon/graphite. `Without anode boxes, organic acids crash bath pH.` (`#E05C5C`)

**Right -- `THE SELF-LIMITING MECHANISM` (`#27AE60`):**
7-step sequence: Voltage drives resin -> Film deposits -> Film insulates -> Current stops -> Deposition redirects to bare metal -> Uniform thickness. `Self-limiting = uniform thickness = unmatched throwing power.`

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| THIN FILM IN CAVITIES | `#E05C5C` | Voltage too low or conductivity off | Increase voltage; verify 1,000--1,800 uS/cm |
| CRATERS / PINHOLES | `#E05C5C` | Phosphate porosity, contamination | Improve seal rinse; filter bath |
| ORANGE PEEL / ROUGHNESS | `#E8A020` | Solids too high or P/B drift | Check 18--22%; P/B 0.15--0.25 |
| HIGH FILM BUILD (WASTE) | `#E8A020` | Voltage too high or time excessive | Reduce voltage; optimize dwell |
| pH DRIFT | `#2EC4B6` | Anode box membrane failure | Inspect membranes; adjust bleed |
| BATH FOAMING | `#E05C5C` | Oil/surfactant carry-over | Identify contamination; defoamer |
