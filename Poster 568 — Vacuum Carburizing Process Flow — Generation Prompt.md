## Phase 6 -- LPC vs. Gas Carburizing + Troubleshooting

**LPC vs. Gas (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `VACUUM VS. GAS CARBURIZING -- WHY CHOOSE LPC?`

*LPC Advantages (Emerald):* Zero IGO; clean bright surface; higher temp (1800--1900 F) = 30--50% shorter cycles; HPGQ reduces distortion 30--50%; no CO exposure; no generator; excellent for blind holes; computer-controlled recipes; handles high-Cr steels.

*LPC Limitations (Amber):* Higher capital cost; HPGQ may not quench thick sections in lean alloys; no real-time Cp measurement; soot management; smaller loads (single-chamber); CFC fixtures expensive; acetylene handling (explosive, 15 psig max); requires simulation expertise.

Callout bar: Teal tint. `LPC is to gas carburizing what CNC is to manual machining -- higher precision, higher investment, higher capability.`

**Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON LPC FAILURES`. Four cards, Coral accent:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| SOOT DEPOSITS | Excessive boost pressure/time; propane instead of C2H2 | Reduce boost pressure; switch to C2H2; verify mass flow cal |
| NON-UNIFORM CASE | Gas flow pattern; load too dense | Optimize loading; increase diffuse time |
| FREE CARBIDES | Too many boosts without sufficient diffuse | Extend diffuse phases; re-run simulation |
| GRAIN GROWTH | Temp above 1900 F without microalloy control | Reduce temp; use vacuum-grade fine-grain steel |
