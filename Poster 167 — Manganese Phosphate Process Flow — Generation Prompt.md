**Box content:**
1. `150--190 F | 4--10 oz/gal | 5--15 min` -- Two-stage clean common for heavy soils
2. `120--160 F | Overflow 2--5 gal/min` -- Hot water rinse helps remove stubborn residues
3. `Acid pickle if scale: HCl 10--25% or H2SO4 10--20%` / `Ambient--150 F, 2--15 min` -- Hardened steel (>40 HRC) resists attack `#E05C5C`
4. `Fresh water, ambient | Multiple stages if needed` -- Acid carryover = excess sludge `#E05C5C`
5. `190--210 F | pH 2.0--3.0 | 10--30 min | IMMERSION ONLY` -- ALWAYS hot. Below 185 F coating stops.
6. `100--140 F | 30 sec--2 min` -- Do not over-rinse -- porous coating absorbs water
7. `MIL-PRF-3150 or equiv. | 150--180 F | 2--5 min immersion` -- MANDATORY. Bare MnPhos has almost no corrosion protection.
8. `Centrifuge excess oil | No oven dry` -- Parts go from rinse to oil -- no dry-off oven

Stage legend strip: Teal = Cleaning & Rinse, Amber = Conditioning & Post-Treatment, Emerald = Coating (Main Tank), Coral = Caution.

## Phase 6 -- Purpose & Properties

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHY MANGANESE PHOSPHATE? -- PURPOSE & PROPERTIES`.

**Left -- What It IS:** Emerald accent. Hureaulite Mn5H2(PO4)4.4H2O | 500--2500 mg/ft2 | 5--25 um | Dark gray to black | HIGH oil absorption | Bare SST 2--8 hr (useless alone) | With oil 48--200+ hr | Firearms, gears, bearings, fasteners. Bottom: `The "Parkerized" finish -- standard for military weapons since WWII`

**Right -- What It Is NOT:** Coral accent. NOT paint pretreatment | NOT standalone corrosion protection | NEVER spray application | NEVER room temperature | NOT decorative | NOT substitute for hard chrome. Bottom: `Without oil, manganese phosphate is just a porous black coating with almost zero corrosion protection`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| LIGHT/THIN COATING | Low temp (<185 F), short time, high FA | Increase to 195--205 F; extend time |
| RED/BROWN POWDER | Bath overheated (boiling), excess iron | Cool to 195--205 F; decant; replenish |
| NON-UNIFORM / BLOTCHY | Poor cleaning, mixed metallurgy | Improve cleaning; separate hard/soft parts |
| POOR OIL RETENTION | Coating too thin, crystals too fine | Increase coating weight; reduce rinse time |
