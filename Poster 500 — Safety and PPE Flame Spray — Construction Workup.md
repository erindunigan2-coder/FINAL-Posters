# Poster #500 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Flame Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Safety poster for the flame spray cluster. Flame spray has lower noise and UV than plasma or HVOF, but open-flame hazards and zinc fume fever are critical. This poster is designed to hang at the entrance to a flame spray booth or field station.

---

## Part 1 -- Workflow Orientation

### Limitations to Flag
1. **Hazard matrix (Block B -- HERO):** 7-row hazard table with severity indicators.
2. **PPE diagram (Block D):** Full-body silhouette with labeled PPE callouts -- built from rectangles and text labels (no raster images).
3. **Exposure limit reference (Block E):** Compact table of TLV/PEL values.
4. **Emergency strip (Block F):** 4 cards for emergency procedures.

### ZONE 3 -- Hazard Matrix (HERO)

**Section label:** `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION -- 7 KEY RISKS` -- Y: 4.4".

**BLOCK B -- 7-Row Hazard Table**

Y: 5.0" to 14.5". Column widths (23.0" total):
- Hazard (4.0") | Severity (1.5") | Details (8.5") | Controls (9.0")

Header row: `#3A4055`. Data rows: alternating `#1E2435` / `#252B3D`, H: 1.2".

| Hazard | Severity | Details | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noise | MODERATE | 85--105 dB (lower than plasma/HVOF) | Hearing protection above 85 dB; plugs sufficient for most work |
| Flame / Thermal | HIGH | Open oxy-acetylene flame; hot spatter travels 1--3 m | Face shield, leather gloves, leather apron, safety glasses w/ side shields |
| Zinc Fume Fever | HIGH | ZnO fumes from zinc wire; flu-like symptoms 4--12 hr post-exposure | Mandatory P100 RPE; local exhaust ventilation; TLV = 2 mg/m3 |
| Metal Fumes | MODERATE | All metals produce fumes; stainless releases Cr and Ni fumes | Fume extraction; RPE rated for specific metals |
| Compressed Gas | HIGH | Acetylene unstable >15 PSI; propane flammable | Flash-back arrestors; proper storage; leak checks with soapy water |
| Fire | HIGH | Hot spatter and overspray ignite combustibles | Fire-resistant surroundings; extinguisher on hand; fire watch |
| UV Radiation | LOW | Moderate UV (less than plasma or HVOF) | Safety glasses with shade 3--5 lens |

Severity badges: Rounded rect 1.2" x 0.3".
- HIGH: fill `#E05C5C` at 40%, text `#E05C5C`
- MODERATE: fill `#E8A020` at 30%, text `#E8A020`
- LOW: fill `#2EC4B6` at 20%, text `#2EC4B6`

Hazard names: Barlow SemiBold, 16 pt, `#F0EDE8`.
Details/Controls: Inter Regular, 13 pt, `#F0EDE8`.

### ZONE 5 -- Exposure Limits

**Section label:** `EXPOSURE LIMITS -- TLV / PEL REFERENCE` -- Y: 22.2".

**Two-column layout:**

**Left -- TLV/PEL Table (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Substance | OSHA PEL (TWA) | ACGIH TLV (TWA) |
|---|---|---|
| Zinc oxide fume | 5 mg/m3 | 2 mg/m3 (respirable) |
| Nickel (metal dust) | 1.0 mg/m3 | 1.5 mg/m3 |
| Chromium (as Cr) | 1.0 mg/m3 | 0.5 mg/m3 |
| Iron oxide fume | 10 mg/m3 | 5 mg/m3 (respirable) |
| Copper fume | 0.1 mg/m3 | 0.2 mg/m3 |
| Nuisance dust (total) | 15 mg/m3 | 10 mg/m3 |

Table: JetBrains Mono 12 pt. Headers: Barlow SemiBold 14 pt.

**Right -- Zinc Fume Fever Callout (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

- Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C` 0.06"
- Title: `ZINC FUME FEVER` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E05C5C`
- Body (Inter Regular 14 pt):
```
Symptoms: fever, chills, muscle ache,
nausea -- onset 4--12 hours after exposure.
Resolves in 24--48 hours but repeat
exposure does NOT build immunity.

Prevention: P100 RPE + local exhaust
ventilation. If spraying zinc for extended
periods, use supplied-air respirator (PAPR).
```
- Bottom note: `If symptoms occur: remove from exposure, seek medical attention, report to supervisor` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E8A020`
