## Phase 5 -- Masking Methods + Surface Condition

### Masking (Y: 12.0"--19.0")

Section label: `MASKING -- TWO METHODS, ONE GOAL: BLOCK THE CASE`.

**Left -- COPPER ELECTROPLATE STOP-OFF (`#27AE60`):** 0.001--0.002 in; cyanide strike + acid plate; effective C/N barrier; standard per AMS 2759/7; requires plating setup; strip after HT. Highlight: `The gold standard -- most reliable stop-off for production`.

**Right -- COMMERCIAL STOP-OFF PAINT (`#E8A020`):** Brush/spray/dip; multiple coats; faster application; less reliable on deep cases; must be fully cured. Highlight: `Convenient for short runs -- verify coverage on first article`.

### Surface Condition Checklist (Y: 19.0"--26.0")

Section label: `SURFACE CONDITION -- ACCEPT / REJECT`.

**ACCEPT (`#27AE60`):** Chemically clean (water-break-free); no rust/scale/oxide; machined finish per drawing; masking intact; material cert verified; parts dry at ambient; load map reviewed.

**REJECT (`#E05C5C`):** Oil/grease/fingerprints; rust or scale; masking cracked/peeling/incomplete; wrong grade (no cert); moisture on surface; parts nested/touching; stop-off not cured.

## Phase 6 -- Common Prep Failures

Y: 26.0"--32.5". Section label: `WHEN PREP FAILS -- WHAT YOU'LL SEE AFTER HEAT TREAT`. 4 cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| SOFT SPOTS | Oil residue blocked C/N diffusion | Improve cleaning; water-break test every batch |
| UNMASKED HARDENING | Stop-off missed area; paint too thin | Double-check per drawing; first article verify |
| SCALE-RELATED DEFECTS | Oxide scale not removed | Add descaling; verify acid pickle |
| WRONG CASE RESPONSE | Wrong steel grade loaded | Verify material cert at receiving AND before loading |
