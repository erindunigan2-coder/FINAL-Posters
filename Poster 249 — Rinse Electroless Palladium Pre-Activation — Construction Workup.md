# Poster #249 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Electroless Palladium -- Pre-Activation

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of 8. This rinse removes all alkaline cleaning chemistry before the activation step. Alkaline drag-in to an acid activation bath causes pH spikes and poor activation. For ENEPIG lines, this rinse is shared with the broader process sequence.

Hero visual: counterflow rinse tank with conductivity monitoring and drag-out indicators.

## Design Notes

Rinse posters are intentionally simpler than plating or activation posters -- the process is simpler. The hero should emphasize the counterflow concept with clear flow direction arrows. The conductivity target (<50 uS/cm) is the single most important number on this poster. The drag-out science section elevates what most shops treat as a trivial step into something they actively monitor.
