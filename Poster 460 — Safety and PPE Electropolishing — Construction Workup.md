# Poster #460 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Electropolishing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Safety poster for the electropolishing cluster. Electropolishing has a unique hazard profile: hot concentrated acid (65--80 C phosphoric/sulfuric), high DC amperage in a wet environment, acid fume generation, hydrogen and oxygen gas evolution at the electrodes, and the potential formation of hexavalent chromium during processing of stainless steel. This poster hangs next to the EP tank.

### ZONE 3 -- Hazard Matrix

**Section label:** `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION` -- Y: 15.7".

**BLOCK D -- 8-Row Hazard Table**

Y: 16.3" to 21.8". Columns: Hazard (4.5") | Source (5.5") | Severity (3.0") | Control (10.0")

| Hazard | Source | Severity | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot acid splash | H3PO4 + H2SO4 at 65--80 C | SEVERE | Face shield, gauntlet gloves, apron, slow immersion |
| Electrical | DC rectifier 6--30 V at 100--1000+ A | HIGH | Lockout/tagout; GFCI; dry hands before controls |
| Acid fumes | Hot electrolyte generates acid mist | HIGH | Local exhaust ventilation; acid mist respirator backup |
| Hydrogen gas | Evolved at cathode -- flammable (LEL 4%) | HIGH | Ventilate above tank; no ignition sources |
| Oxygen gas | Evolved at anode surface | MODERATE | Ventilation; contributes to combustion |
| Cr(VI) formation | EP of stainless steel liberates hexavalent chromium | HIGH | Monitor air; wastewater treatment; PEL 5 ug/m3 |
| Hot surfaces | Tank walls, fixtures after processing | MODERATE | Heat-resistant gloves for unloading; warning signs |
| Drag-out acid | Concentrated acid on parts leaving tank | MODERATE | Drip trays; recovery rinse tank |

Severity column color-coding: SEVERE = `#E05C5C`, HIGH = `#E8A020`, MODERATE = `#2EC4B6`.

### ZONE 4 -- Emergency Response

**Section label:** `EMERGENCY RESPONSE` -- Y: 22.2".

**BLOCK E -- Two-Panel Emergency Layout**

**Left -- Acid Splash First Aid (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 5.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`
- Title: `ACID SPLASH -- FIRST AID` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E05C5C`
- Content:
```
1. EYES: Flush immediately with water for 15+ min
   Hold eyelids open. Remove contact lenses.
2. SKIN: Remove contaminated clothing immediately
   Flush with water for 15+ min. Do NOT apply creams.
3. INGESTION: Do NOT induce vomiting
   Rinse mouth. Seek medical attention immediately.
4. INHALATION: Move to fresh air
   If breathing difficult, give oxygen. Call 911.
```

**Right -- Spill Response (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 5.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `ACID SPILL RESPONSE` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E8A020`
- Content:
```
1. Alert personnel -- clear the area
2. Don full PPE before approaching spill
3. Contain with acid-resistant dike or absorbent
4. Neutralize with soda ash (Na2CO3) or lime
   CAUTION: Reaction generates heat and CO2
5. Collect neutralized material for disposal
6. Flush area with water
7. Report per facility spill procedure
```

## Design Notes

This poster must read as urgent without being alarmist. The hot acid hazard (65--80 C phosphoric/sulfuric) is the headline risk -- this is not a room-temperature process. The Cr(VI) formation during EP of stainless steel is a less obvious but critical hazard that many shops underestimate. No perchloric acid references per Watson safety flag.
