# Poster #129 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Silver -- Pre-Activation

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of 8. The pre-activation rinse removes alkaline cleaner drag-out before the acid activation step. If alkaline residue reaches the acid bath, it neutralizes the acid and reduces activation effectiveness. For silver plating this rinse is standard but the stakes are high -- any contamination that makes it to the strike bath can cause immersion deposition and peeling.

### ZONE 4 -- Drag-Out / Drag-In

**Section label:** `DRAG-OUT AND DRAG-IN -- THE INVISIBLE ENEMY` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Three-Panel Strip (Y: 15.3" to 20.3")**

Three equal callout boxes side by side:

| Panel | Title | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Left (X: 0.5", W: 7.33") | `WHAT IS DRAG-OUT?` `#E8A020` | The thin film of bath chemistry that clings to parts when they leave any tank. For the cleaner tank, this is alkaline solution. Volume depends on part geometry, withdrawal speed, and rack design. |
| Center (X: 8.16", W: 7.33") | `THE RINSE REMOVES IT` `#2EC4B6` | Immersion in flowing water dilutes and removes the drag-out film. Adequate rinse time and flow rate are critical. Stagnant rinse water concentrates contaminants. |
| Right (X: 15.83", W: 7.67") | `WHAT IS DRAG-IN?` `#E05C5C` | Whatever the rinse does NOT remove gets dragged into the next tank. Alkaline drag-in neutralizes acid activation. Acid drag-in contaminates the plating bath. Both cause defects. |

Each box: fill `#1E2435`, left accent in title color, 0.06".

### ZONE 6 -- What Happens When Rinse Fails

**Section label:** `WHEN THE RINSE FAILS` -- Y: 26.7".

**BLOCK F -- Four Cards (Y: 27.3" to 32.3")**

| Card | Problem | Cause | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ALKALINE CARRY-OVER | Insufficient rinse time or stagnant water | Neutralizes acid activation; poor oxide removal |
| 2 | pH CREEP IN ACID | Repeated alkaline drag-in raises acid bath pH | Acid bath becomes ineffective; must be replaced |
| 3 | CONTAMINATION TO STRIKE | Alkaline or particulate reaches strike bath | Immersion deposit forms; silver peels |
| 4 | WATER SPOTS | Hard water minerals dry on parts | Adhesion defects under silver deposit |

Cards: standard troubleshooting style, left accent `#E05C5C`.

## Design Notes

Rinse posters carry less technical density than main tank or cleaning posters but the drag-out/drag-in concept is fundamental and often poorly understood on the shop floor. The cascade rinse comparison is the key takeaway -- water conservation and cleanliness in one upgrade.
