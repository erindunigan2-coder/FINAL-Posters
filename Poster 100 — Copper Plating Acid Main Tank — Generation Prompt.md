## Phase 5 -- Bath Chemistry (Four Essentials) + Cl- Gauge

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY -- THE FOUR ESSENTIALS`.

### Four-Component Breakdown (4 side-by-side cards)

**Copper Sulfate** (accent `#27AE60`): 180--250 g/L | Cu metal 45--65 g/L | Low = burning at HCD | High = crystallization risk.

**Sulfuric Acid** (accent `#2EC4B6`): 45--100 g/L | Conductivity + solubility | High acid = better TP (PCBs) | Acid:Cu ratio is key balance.

**Chloride** (accent `#E8A020`): 30--80 ppm (optimal 40--60) | Source: HCl addition | Interacts with carrier for leveling | Low = hazy | High (>100) = dull, streaky.

**Brightener System** (accent `#E8A020`): Per supplier TDS | Carrier (PEG-type) + Brightener (SPS-type) + Leveler (N-containing) | All three + Cl- must balance.

### Cl- Control Gauge

Horizontal bar: Red left (<30: hazy) | Green center (30--80: OPTIMAL) | Yellow (80--100) | Red right (>100: dull). Optimal marker at 40--60 ppm.

## Phase 6 -- Defect Diagnosis Grid

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS`.

3x2 grid:

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAZY / MILKY | `#2EC4B6` | Low Cl-, organic contamination | Check Cl- by AgNO3; carbon treat |
| DULL DEPOSIT | `#E8A020` | Excess Cl- (>100 ppm), byproducts | Dilute; H2O2 treatment |
| ROUGHNESS | `#E05C5C` | Anode sludge, no bags, particulate | PP anode bags; increase filtration |
| PITTING | `#E05C5C` | Oil contamination, low carrier | Carbon treat; add carrier; air agitation |
| BURNING (HCD) | `#E8A020` | CD too high, low Cu metal | Reduce CD; add CuSO4 |
| SKIP PLATING | `#E05C5C` | No strike on steel, silicate film | Apply strike; check cleaner |

## Phase 7 -- Hull Cell + Anodes

Y: 26.5" to 32.5".

**Left -- Hull Cell (W: 11.0"):**
Test: `267 mL | 2 A | 10 min | 75 F`. Strip showing 5 zones from HCD (burned) through optimal (mirror-bright) to LCD (dull/streaky).
Note: `Good acid copper panel: mirror-bright across 70--80% of width.` -- `#27AE60`

**Right -- Anodes (W: 11.5"):**
Title: `ANODES -- PHOSPHORIZED COPPER`.
Type: 0.04--0.06% P | Balls/nuggets in Ti baskets | PP bags 1--2 um | A:C 1:1 to 2:1.

`WHY PHOSPHORIZED?` -- Phosphorus forms black film (Cu phosphide) preventing Cu+ formation. Pure Cu anodes produce Cu+ -> metallic Cu particles -> roughness, clogging, contamination.

`NEVER use pure copper anodes in acid copper sulfate. Phosphorized only.` -- `#E05C5C`
