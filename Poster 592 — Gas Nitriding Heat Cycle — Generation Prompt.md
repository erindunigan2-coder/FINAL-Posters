## Phase 4 -- Cycle Profiles (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 14.0". Section label: `CYCLE PROFILES -- SINGLE-STAGE VS. TWO-STAGE`.

**Top -- SINGLE-STAGE (`#E8A020`):** Ramp 100--200 F/hr (2--6 hr) | Hold 925--975 F, 15--30% diss (24--90 hr) | Furnace cool under NH3 to 300 F (4--8 hr) | Air cool (1--2 hr). Total: 30--106 hr (1.3--4.4 days).

### Ramp & Cool (Y: 20.5"--27.0")

**Left -- HEATING RATE (`#E8A020`):** 100--200 F/hr; slow ramp deliberate (thermal gradients = distortion); light load 2--3 hr, heavy 4--6 hr; N2 purge before NH3.

**Right -- COOLING (`#2EC4B6`):** Furnace cool under NH3 to 300 F; then air cool. Why under NH3: prevents oxidation, parts stay bright. Why NOT quench: no phase transformation; hardness from precipitates; quenching = thermal shock with zero benefit.

## Phase 6 -- Nitride Precipitation Mechanism

Y: 27.0"--32.5". Section label: `WHAT HAPPENS DURING THE HOLD -- NITRIDE PRECIPITATION`.

**Left -- THE MECHANISM:** (1) NH3 dissociates on surface; (2) nascent N diffuses into ferrite; (3) N reacts with Al/Cr/Mo/V to form nitrides; (4) fine coherent precipitates = extreme hardness; (5) compound zone (iron nitride) at surface.

**Right -- WHY IT WORKS WITHOUT A QUENCH:** In carburizing, C dissolves in austenite -- only hard when quenched to martensite. In nitriding, N forms PRECIPITATES inside ferrite. Precipitates ARE the hardening mechanism. No transformation needed. No quench needed. This is PRECIPITATION hardening, not TRANSFORMATION hardening.
