## Phase 4 -- Three-Column Comparison (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section label: `PRIMER APPLICATION -- THREE SYSTEMS COMPARED`. Three tall panels.

**Col 1 -- IOZ (accent `#27AE60`):** Ethyl silicate. 75-85% zinc. D520 Type II. 2.5-4.0 mils. Airless 2,500-3,500 psi. Tip 0.017-0.023". Pot life 4-8 hr. CONTINUOUS agitation. `Maximum galvanic -- if applied correctly.`

**Col 2 -- OZ (accent `#E8A020`):** Epoxy/PU binder. 65-80% zinc. 2.0-3.5 mils. Airless/air/brush/roll. 2,000-3,000 psi. Tip 0.017-0.021". Pot life 15 min-8 hr (2K) or unlimited (1K). Continuous agitation. `Easier -- brush/roll for field repair.`

**Col 3 -- Epoxy (accent `#2EC4B6`):** BPA epoxy + amine. No zinc. 1.0-3.0 mils. Airless/HVLP/brush/roll. 2,000-3,000 psi. Pot life 30 min-8 hr. No zinc settling. `Broad range -- lower skill barrier.`

## Phase 6 -- Agitation + Mistakes

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Two columns.

Left -- The #1 Rule: Big stat `AGITATE` 60pt `#E05C5C`. Zinc density 7.14 g/cm3. Settles in minutes. Un-agitated = clear binder = zero protection. Mechanical agitation must run continuously. `If the agitator stops, stop spraying.`

Right -- Mistakes: No agitation (zero zinc in film). Over-application > 5 mils (mud cracking). Wrong mix ratio (undercure/brittleness). High wind (DFT variation). Skip stripe coat (thin edges = first failure).
