## Phase 6 -- Alternative Methods

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `ALTERNATIVE CLEANING METHODS`.

**Left -- Vapor Degrease (Legacy):**
Accent `#3A4055`. Title: `VAPOR DEGREASE (LEGACY)` -- `#C8D0D8`.
Trichloroethylene or perchloroethylene | Vapor condensation on cold parts | EPA NESHAP regulated | Being phased out -- some aerospace shops still under permit.

**Right -- Electrocleaning (Optional):**
Accent `#2EC4B6`. Title: `ELECTROCLEAN (OPTIONAL)`.
Anodic electrocleaning | 4--8 oz/gal | 30--50 ASF | 140--180 F | 1--3 min | O2 scrubbing dislodges embedded soils.
Caution: `ANODIC only for steel going to hard chrome. Cathodic cleaning risks hydrogen absorption.` -- `#E05C5C`

## Phase 7 -- Failure Modes

2x2 grid:

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHROME PEELING | `#E05C5C` | Oil/soil residue under deposit | Extend soak; increase temp; verify water-break |
| PITTING IN CHROME | `#E05C5C` | Embedded contaminant outgassing | Pre-wipe; extend cleaning; ultrasonic |
| ROUGH DEPOSIT | `#E8A020` | Particulate from inadequate cleaning | Increase agitation; filter cleaner tank |
| WATER-BREAK FAILURE | `#E8A020` | Cleaner exhausted or temp too low | Replenish cleaner; check heater |
