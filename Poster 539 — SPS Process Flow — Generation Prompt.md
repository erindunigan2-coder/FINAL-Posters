## Phase 6 -- SPS vs. APS Comparison + Troubleshooting

### SPS vs. APS (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `WHY SPS? -- SPS VS. CONVENTIONAL APS`.

**Left -- SPS (W: 11.0", accent `#27AE60`):**

Feedstock: submicron suspension 0.05-5 um | Carrier: ethanol or water | Microstructure: COLUMNAR (mimics EB-PVD) | Porosity: 10-25% | Thermal conductivity: 0.7-1.2 W/mK | Strain tolerance: HIGH | Bond strength: > 15 MPa | Standoff: 40-80 mm | Cost: medium | Status: emerging.

Highlight: `EB-PVD performance at 1/10 the cost -- no vacuum chamber required`.

**Right -- Conventional APS (W: 11.5", accent `#E8A020`):**

Feedstock: dry powder 10-90 um | Carrier: Ar gas | Microstructure: LAMELLAR | Porosity: 10-20% | Thermal conductivity: 0.8-1.2 W/mK | Strain tolerance: LOW | Bond strength: > 10 MPa | Standoff: 75-150 mm | Cost: low | Status: mature.

Highlight: `Proven workhorse for 50+ years -- but lamellar structure limits thermal cycling life`.

### Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four coral-accented cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| LAMELLAR INSTEAD OF COLUMNAR | Standoff too far; particles too large | Reduce standoff 40-60 mm; verify particle < 5 um |
| DELAMINATION FROM BOND COAT | Contaminated interface; inadequate bond coat | Verify APS bond coat; minimize gap between coats |
| INJECTOR CLOGGING | Suspension sedimentation; high solids | Agitate; reduce loading; check shelf life |
| SUBSTRATE OVERHEATING | Standoff too close; insufficient cooling | Increase cooling air; monitor temp < 400 degC |

Problem: `#E05C5C`. Fix: `#27AE60`.
