## Phase 5 -- Rinse Type Comparison

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THREE RINSE CONFIGURATIONS`.

Three side-by-side panels:

| Config | X | W | Accent | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single Overflow | 0.5" | 7.33" | `#3A4055` | `Minimum standard` |
| Counterflow Cascade | 8.0" | 7.33" | `#2EC4B6` | `RECOMMENDED for tin lines` |
| Spray Rinse | 15.5" | 8.0" | `#27AE60` | `Good supplement to immersion` |

Single: `One tank, continuous fill and drain` / `Simple, low cost` / `Higher water usage` / `Adequate for light carry-over`.
Cascade: `Two or three tanks in series` / `Fresh water enters last tank, overflows backward` / `Lowest water consumption` / `Best rinse quality`.
Spray: `High-pressure nozzles above tank` / `Excellent for rack lines` / `Fast cycle time` / `May miss recessed areas`.

## Phase 6 -- Drag-In Consequences

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT HAPPENS WHEN ALKALINE REACHES THE ACID`.

Full-width panel, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`. Three-column layout:

| Column | Header | Header Color | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left | `THE DRAG-IN` | `#E8A020` | Alkaline cleaner carried on parts and rack. Typical: 0.1--0.5 mL per sq ft. |
| Center | `THE REACTION` | `#E05C5C` | NaOH + H2SO4 -> Na2SO4 + H2O. Neutralizes acid. Raises pH. Reduces activation effectiveness. |
| Right | `THE RESULT` | `#E05C5C` | Oxide film remains. Tin adhesion fails. Blistering, peeling, skip plating. |

Bottom rule (Emerald): `One good rinse prevents one bad plating lot. The math is simple.`
