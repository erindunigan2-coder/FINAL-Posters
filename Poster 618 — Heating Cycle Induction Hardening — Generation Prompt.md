## Phase 4 -- Power Density & Heating (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `POWER DENSITY -- THE PRIMARY CONTROL VARIABLE`.

**Left -- Visual Power-Depth Relationship:**

Four horizontal bars showing inverse relationship:

| Power Density | Case Depth | Time | Fill Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 kW/in2 (very high) | 0.015--0.030 in (shallow) | 0.5--2 sec | Amber 80% |
| 20 kW/in2 (high) | 0.030--0.060 in (medium) | 2--5 sec | Amber 60% |
| 10 kW/in2 (moderate) | 0.060--0.120 in (deep) | 5--15 sec | Amber 40% |
| 1--5 kW/in2 (low) | 0.120--0.300 in (very deep) | 15--30 sec | Amber 20% |

**Right -- Key Principles:** THE POWER-DEPTH TRADEOFF: High power + short time = shallow. Low power + longer time = deeper. At any frequency, control depth by power-to-time ratio. SURFACE TEMPERATURE TARGET: 1500--1700 F (816--927 C). Must exceed Ac3.

**Below -- Single-Shot vs. Progressive Scan:**

**SINGLE-SHOT (Amber):** Entire area simultaneously. 1--10 sec. No movement during heating. Best for gear teeth, bearing seats, cam lobes. Highest production rate.

**PROGRESSIVE SCAN (Teal):** Scan rate 0.1--2.0 in/sec. Trailing spray quench follows coil. Part may rotate. Best for long shafts, rods, rolls, bars. Lower instantaneous power.

## Phase 6 -- Temperature Monitoring + Overheating

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Temperature Monitoring (Amber):**

Methods: IR pyrometer (non-contact, emissivity 0.85--0.95, response < 10 ms), Dual-color ratio pyrometer (preferred -- less emissivity-sensitive), Thermocouple (not practical during heating -- EMF interference -- setup only). Why it matters: +/- 50 F variation directly affects case depth and hardness. Real-time pyrometry enables closed-loop power control.

**Right -- Overheating Consequences (Coral):**

`OVERHEATING -- CONSEQUENCES`.

GRAIN GROWTH: Excessive temp/time = coarse austenite = brittle martensite = crack-prone.

INCIPIENT MELTING: Above 2500 F = localized melting at grain boundaries, especially sharp corners and thin edges.

THROUGH-HARDENING: Heat soaks too deep = entire section austenitizes = no tough core = brittle part shatters under load.

DISTORTION: Excessive thermal expansion + severe contraction during quench = OD growth, bowing, cracking.

Bottom: `Monitor surface temperature with pyrometry on EVERY production run. A 100 F overshoot = difference between good part and scrap.`
