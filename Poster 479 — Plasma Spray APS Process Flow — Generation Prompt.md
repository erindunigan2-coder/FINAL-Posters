## Phase 6 -- APS vs. HVOF + Troubleshooting

### APS vs. HVOF Comparison (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `WHEN TO CHOOSE APS -- APS VS. HVOF`.

**Left -- APS:** accent `#2EC4B6`. Plasma 10,000-15,000 degC, 200-600 m/s, powder 10-90 um, 40-80 kW, porosity 2-15%, bond >10 MPa ceramic / >30 MPa metal. Signature: TBC, ceramic wear. Highlight: `APS is the only process that can spray ceramics like YSZ for thermal barrier coatings`.

**Right -- HVOF:** accent `#E8A020`. Gas jet 2,500-3,200 degC, 600-900 m/s supersonic, powder 5-45 um, fuel kerosene/H2/propylene+O2, porosity <1%, bond >70 MPa. Signature: WC-Co carbide. Highlight: `HVOF WC-Co is replacing hard chrome in aerospace -- but APS owns the ceramic space`.

### Troubleshooting Quick Hits (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four coral-accented cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| DELAMINATION | Poor surface prep or contamination | Verify SP-5 cleanliness; check profile Ra |
| UNMELTED PARTICLES | Power too low or standoff too far | Increase power; reduce standoff distance |
| SUBSTRATE OVERHEATING | Traverse too slow, insufficient cooling | Add air jets; increase traverse speed |
| EXCESSIVE POROSITY | Standoff too far or powder too coarse | Optimize standoff; verify powder size |
