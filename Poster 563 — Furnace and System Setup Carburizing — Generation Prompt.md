## Phase 6 -- Atmosphere Generation + Checklist

**Atmosphere (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `ATMOSPHERE GENERATION -- THE CHEMISTRY BEHIND THE GAS`.

*Left -- Endothermic Generator (accent `#E8A020`):* CH4 + air -> catalytic reaction at 1900--2050 F. Air:gas ratio: methane 2.77:1, propane 7.16:1. Output: CO ~20%, H2 ~40%, N2 ~40%, CH4 <0.5%. Residual CH4 <0.5% = good cracking. Dew point at outlet: -40 to 0 F.

*Right -- N2-Methanol (accent `#2EC4B6`):* ~40% N2 + ~60% methanol; cracks in situ. No generator needed. Lower capital, higher operating cost. Best for small batch, backup systems.

Callout bar: Amber tint. `The endothermic generator is to carburizing what the rectifier is to electroplating -- the invisible engine that makes everything possible.`

**Checklist (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `PRE-CYCLE SYSTEM READINESS CHECKLIST`. Eight items, checkbox border `#E8A020`.

Left: Furnace sealed, generator at temp, burn-off pilots lit, N2 purge complete.
Right: Endo gas introduced, O2 probe calibrated, quench oil at temp, load TCs placed.
