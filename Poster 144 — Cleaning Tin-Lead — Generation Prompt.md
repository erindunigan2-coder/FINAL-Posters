## Phase 4 -- Substrate Cleaning Paths (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section: `CLEANING BY SUBSTRATE -- THREE PATHS`.

Three vertical columns, each a different substrate path with 3--4 stacked step boxes and downward arrows.

| Column | X | W | Substrate | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Left | 0.5" | 7.33" | Copper / Brass Components | `#E8A020` |
| Center | 8.16" | 7.33" | PCB Panels | `#2EC4B6` |
| Right | 15.83" | 7.67" | Nickel-Plated Parts | `#27AE60` |

**Copper/Brass Path:**
1. `Alkaline Soak Clean` -- `Mild alkaline, 3--6 oz/gal` / `120--150 F, 3--5 min` / `Non-etch formula for copper`
2. `Rinse` -- `Ambient, flowing, DI preferred`
3. `Electroclean (optional)` -- `Anodic, 3--6 V, 1--2 min` / `For heavy soil or stamping compounds`
4. `Rinse` --> `TO ACTIVATION`

**PCB Panel Path:**
1. `Spray Clean (conveyorized)` -- `Mild alkaline or semi-aqueous` / `100--130 F, 30--90 sec`
2. `Spray Rinse` -- `DI water, high-pressure nozzles`
3. `Acid Microetch (optional)` -- `Sodium persulfate or peroxide/sulfuric` / `Creates copper surface texture for adhesion`
4. `DI Rinse` --> `TO ACTIVATION`

**Nickel-Plated Parts Path:**
1. `Mild Alkaline Soak` -- `Mild alkaline, 2--4 oz/gal` / `120--140 F, 2--3 min` / `Light touch -- protect Ni layer`
2. `Rinse` -- `Ambient, flowing`
3. `Mild Acid Dip` -- `5% MSA or H2SO4, 10--15 sec` / `Remove light tarnish only`
4. `Rinse` --> `TO ACTIVATION`

Each step box: fill `#1E2435`, left accent in column color, radius 6.

## Phase 6 -- Cleaner Chemistry Callout

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHAT IS IN THE CLEANER -- AND WHAT MATTERS FOR SOLDER PLATE`.

**Left -- Alkaline Cleaner Components (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`
- Title: `ALKALINE CLEANER COMPONENTS` `#2EC4B6`

| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| NaOH / KOH | Saponifies oils, provides alkalinity |
| Surfactants | Emulsify non-saponifiable oils |
| Chelators | Complex hard water ions |
| Phosphates | Water conditioning, buffering |
| Inhibitors | Prevent copper/brass etch attack |

**Right -- Critical Rules for Tin-Lead (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `RULES FOR SOLDER PLATE SUBSTRATES` `#E8A020`

Rules list:
- `Non-silicated cleaners only -- silicate films cause skip plating`
- `Low alkalinity for copper/brass -- prevent etch and roughening`
- `Residue-free is mandatory for electronics -- ionic residue causes field failures`
- `DI water rinse after cleaning for electronics-grade substrates`
- `Water-break-free test is the ONLY acceptance criterion`
- `Lead safety: wear PPE even at the cleaning stage -- parts may have lead residue from previous processing`

Bottom: `If the part is not water-break-free, it is not clean. Period.` `#E05C5C`

## Phase 7 -- Common Mistakes

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `4 CLEANING MISTAKES THAT RUIN SOLDER DEPOSITS`.

Four cards, W: 5.5", H: 2.7", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Card | X | Mistake | Result | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | SILICATED CLEANER | Skip plating, bare spots on copper | Switch to non-silicated formula |
| 2 | 6.33" | OVER-ETCHING COPPER | Rough substrate, grainy solder deposit | Reduce temp, time, or alkalinity |
| 3 | 12.16" | IONIC RESIDUE LEFT | Electrochemical migration, field failure | Use DI water rinse, verify conductivity |
| 4 | 18.0" | SKIPPING ELECTROCLEAN | Organic film trapped under solder, blistering | Electroclean heavy-soil copper parts |
