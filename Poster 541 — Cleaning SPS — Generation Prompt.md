## Phase 4 -- Cleaning Sequence (HERO)

Y: 3.8" to 15.0". Section label: `PRE-SPRAY CLEANING SEQUENCE`.

4-step vertical sequence. Each card W: 23.0", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6. Arrow connectors between steps with time-window callouts in `#E8A020`.

| Step | Accent | Title | Parameters | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#2EC4B6` | SOLVENT DEGREASE | Vapor degrease (legacy) or aqueous alkaline (preferred). Remove oils, greases, machining fluids. | No visible residue |
| 2 | `#2EC4B6` | ALKALINE WASH | Immersion/spray, 50-70 degC, pH 10-12, 5-15 min. Rinse thoroughly. | No alkaline residue |
| 3 | `#27AE60` | WATER-BREAK-FREE TEST | ASTM F22. Surface must sheet water uniformly -- any break = contamination. | PASS = uniform water sheet |
| 4 | `#E8A020` | DRY | Forced air or oven. No moisture at blast time. Moisture = flash rust on steel. | Bone dry before blast |

Time-window callouts between steps:
- After Step 2: `Rinse immediately -- do not allow cleaner to dry on surface`
- After Step 4: `Minimize time to grit blast -- ideally same shift`

## Phase 5 -- Cleaning Methods + Water-Break-Free

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `CLEANING METHODS -- TWO APPROACHES`.

**Left -- Solvent vs. Alkaline (W: 11.0"):**

| Property | Solvent Degrease | Alkaline Wash |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Vapor or wipe | Immersion or spray |
| Temperature | Per solvent | 50-70 degC |
| Effectiveness | Oils and greases | Oils, particulate, shop soils |
| Environmental | VOC concerns; restricted | Preferred; water-based |
| Best for | Light contamination | General purpose |

**Right -- Water-Break-Free Test (W: 11.5"):**

Two sub-panels:
- `PASS` (`#27AE60`): Water sheets uniformly. No beading. Surface is clean.
- `FAIL` (`#E05C5C`): Water beads or breaks. Contamination present. Re-clean.

Gate note: `If the surface fails water-break-free, do NOT proceed to grit blast.` `#E05C5C`.

## Phase 6 -- Timing + Contamination + SPS Notes

### Time Windows (Left, Y: 22.0"-28.5")

| Window | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Clean to blast | Same shift; minimize delay | Recontamination from handling |
| Blast to spray | < 4 hrs (some < 2 hr) | Oxide regrowth; moisture |
| After blast | Clean lint-free gloves | Fingerprints = contamination |

### Common Contaminants (Right)

| Contaminant | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Machining oil | Prior operations | Delamination; poor bond |
| Fingerprints | Bare-hand contact | Localized adhesion failure |
| Moisture | Humidity, incomplete dry | Flash rust; interface porosity |
| Shop dust | Airborne particulate | Inclusions at interface |
| Old coatings | Incomplete strip | Non-uniform bonding |

### SPS-Specific Notes (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `SPS-SPECIFIC CLEANING NOTES`. Four cards:

1. SPS goes over APS bond coat -- cleaning is for initial substrate, not bond coat surface
2. No additional cleaning between bond coat and SPS topcoat -- spray promptly
3. Protocol identical to standard APS -- no SPS-specific deviations at this stage
4. Aerospace: final rinse with DI water to avoid mineral deposits
