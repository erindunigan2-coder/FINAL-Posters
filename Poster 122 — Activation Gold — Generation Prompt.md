## Phase 5 -- The Chloride Rule

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THE #1 RULE IN GOLD ACTIVATION`.

**Full-width Coral panel** (border 3pt `#E05C5C`, fill `#E05C5C` at 12%). Title: `NEVER USE HYDROCHLORIC ACID (HCl) FOR GOLD ACTIVATION` (28pt).

Three columns:

| Column | Header | Content |
|---|---|---|
| THE RULE | `#E05C5C` | H2SO4 only. No exceptions. Chloride ions are poison to gold baths. |
| THE CHEMISTRY | `#E05C5C` | Cl- complexes with Au+ depleting gold. Even 1 ppm detectable. |
| THE COST | `#E05C5C` | One contamination event = partial or complete bath replacement. $1,000+ loss. |

Bottom warning: `Post this rule at the activation tank. No HCl. Ever.`

## Phase 6 -- Activation Parameters

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `ACTIVATION PARAMETERS BY SUBSTRATE`.

| Substrate | Acid | Concentration | Temp | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nickel underplate | H2SO4 | 5--10% v/v | Ambient | 15--30 sec | Most common |
| Kovar / alloy 42 | Proprietary or H2SO4 | Per supplier | Ambient | 15--30 sec | Specialty |
| Copper (rare) | H2SO4 | 5--10% v/v | Ambient | 15--30 sec | Ni underplate preferred |

Note: `Activation for gold is simple. The rule is absolute. Get the acid right and the rest follows.`
