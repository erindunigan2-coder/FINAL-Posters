## Phase 5 -- Contaminants + Cleaning Methods

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `CONTAMINANTS & CLEANING METHODS`.

**Left -- What You Are Removing (W: 11.0"):** accent `#E05C5C`.

| Contaminant | Source | Removal Method |
|---|---|---|
| Mill scale | Hot rolling | Grit blast only |
| Rust | Atmospheric corrosion | Grit blast to SP 5 |
| Oil / grease | Machinery, handling | Solvent or aqueous degrease |
| Old paint systems | Prior protection | Grit blast (multiple passes) |
| Weld spatter | Fabrication | Grinding + blast |
| Chalk / marker | Layout markings | Solvent wipe |
| Salt deposits | Marine/coastal | Fresh water wash first |

**Right -- Cleaning Methods (W: 11.5"):** accent `#2EC4B6`.

| Method | When to Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Solvent wipe | Light oil, grease | MEK, acetone, or citrus |
| Aqueous alkaline wash | Heavy contamination (shop) | 50-70 degC, pH 10-12 |
| Steam cleaning | Heavy oil/grease (field) | Effective on large structures |
| High-pressure water | Salt, loose debris (field) | Not a substitute for blast |
| Power tool (SP 3) | Loose rust/paint pre-blast | Needle gun, wire wheel |
| Hand tool (SP 2) | Accessible surfaces only | Wire brush, scraper |

Note: `None of these methods replace grit blasting. They prepare the surface FOR blasting.`

## Phase 6 -- Time Windows + Field Conditions + Checkpoints

### Time Windows (Y: 22.0"-28.5", left W: 11.0")

Section label: `TIMING RULES & FIELD CHALLENGES`.

| Window | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Degrease to blast | Same day; minimize delay | Recontamination |
| Blast to spray | < 4 hr (general); < 2 hr (high humidity) | Flash rust |
| After blast | Do NOT touch with bare hands | Fingerprint = contamination |
| Humidity limit | No blast/spray if RH > 85% or surface < 3 degC above dew point | Condensation |

### Field Challenges (right W: 11.5")

Title: `FIELD-SPECIFIC CHALLENGES` accent `#E8A020`.
- Rain can flash-rust a blasted surface in minutes
- Wind carries grit and fumes
- Morning dew condensation before crew arrives
- Marine salt: fresh water wash before degrease
- Old lead paint: lead-safe work practices (EPA RRP)
- Multiple coating layers may need staged blasting

### Go/No-Go Checkpoints (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `GO / NO-GO CHECKPOINTS`. Four cards.

| Checkpoint | Pass (`#27AE60`) | Fail Action (`#E05C5C`) |
|---|---|---|
| DEGREASE COMPLETE | No visible oil; solvent wipe test clean | Re-degrease; identify source |
| BLAST PROFILE | Ra 4-12 um; SSPC-SP 5 verified | Re-blast; check media and pressure |
| TIME WINDOW | < 4 hr since blast; no flash rust | Re-blast if flash rust present |
| ENVIRONMENTAL | RH < 85%; surface > 3 degC above dew point | Wait for conditions; cover surface |
