## Phase 5 -- Cobalt Exposure + PPE Checklist

### Cobalt Deep-Dive (Y: 15.5"-22.0")

Section label: `COBALT -- THE SILENT HAZARD IN EVERY WC-Co SPRAY` centered, 28 pt.

**Left -- Exposure Limits (W: 11.5", accent `#E05C5C`):**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| OSHA PEL (TWA) | 0.1 mg/m3 |
| ACGIH TLV (TWA) | 0.02 mg/m3 |
| Biological Exposure Index | Urine cobalt < 15 ug/L |
| Health effects | Hard metal lung disease; occupational asthma |
| Onset | Months to years; often irreversible |
| Carcinogenicity | IARC Group 2B (possibly carcinogenic -- cobalt with WC) |

Values in `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- Required Controls (W: 11.0", accent `#27AE60`):**

Title: `MANDATORY CONTROLS FOR WC-Co SPRAYING`. Six numbered items: enclosed booth with LEV + HEPA; P100 half-face minimum / PAPR for extended; medical surveillance (annual PFTs + urine cobalt); air monitoring per OSHA method; wet wipe or HEPA vacuum -- NEVER dry sweep; shower and change facilities.

Bottom note: `The 0.02 mg/m3 TLV is EXTREMELY LOW. For context, that is 50x stricter than the nickel TLV. Achieving compliance requires engineering controls, not just RPE.` in `#E8A020`.
