## Phase 4 -- PPE Requirements (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5".

Section label: `REQUIRED PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT`

Six PPE callout boxes with left-border accents, arranged in two columns:

**Left column (Coral accents):**

| PPE | Requirement |
|---|---|
| EYES & FACE | Full face shield (ANSI Z87.1) + chemical splash goggles underneath. Hot acid splash at 65--80 C = immediate severe burns. Safety glasses alone NOT sufficient. |
| HANDS | Rubber gauntlet gloves -- PVC or neoprene. Extend past wrist to mid-forearm. Inspect for pinholes before each use. |
| BODY | Acid-resistant apron (PVC or rubber). Full coverage chest to below knee. Chemical-resistant suit for high-splash ops. |

**Right column (Amber/Coral accents):**

| PPE | Requirement |
|---|---|
| RESPIRATORY | Local exhaust ventilation = primary control. Acid mist respirator (P100 + acid gas cartridge) when ventilation inadequate. |
| FEET | Chemical-resistant rubber boots. Steel-toe preferred. No leather -- acid destroys leather on contact. |
| EMERGENCY EQUIPMENT | Eyewash + safety shower within 10 sec travel (ANSI Z358.1). Spill kit with acid neutralizer. Class B/C extinguisher. |

Summary strip (Coral): `MINIMUM PPE: Face shield + splash goggles + gauntlet gloves + acid apron + rubber boots + local exhaust ventilation`

## Phase 5 -- Hazard Matrix

Y: 15.5" to 22.0".

Section label: `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION`

| Hazard | Source | Severity | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot acid splash | H3PO4 + H2SO4 at 65--80 C | SEVERE | Face shield, gauntlets, apron, slow immersion |
| Electrical | DC rectifier 6--30 V at 100--1000+ A | HIGH | Lockout/tagout; GFCI; dry hands |
| Acid fumes | Hot electrolyte generates acid mist | HIGH | Local exhaust; respirator backup |
| Hydrogen gas | Evolved at cathode -- LEL 4% | HIGH | Ventilate above tank; no ignition sources |
| Oxygen gas | Evolved at anode surface | MODERATE | Ventilation; combustion contributor |
| Cr(VI) formation | EP of stainless steel | HIGH | Monitor air; wastewater treatment; PEL 5 ug/m3 |
| Hot surfaces | Tank walls, fixtures | MODERATE | Heat-resistant gloves; warning signs |
| Drag-out acid | Concentrated acid on parts | MODERATE | Drip trays; recovery rinse |

Severity color-coding: SEVERE = `#E05C5C`, HIGH = `#E8A020`, MODERATE = `#2EC4B6`.

## Phase 6 -- Emergency Response + Gas Evolution

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left panel -- ACID SPLASH FIRST AID (Coral accent):**

```
1. EYES: Flush 15+ min, hold lids open, remove contacts
2. SKIN: Remove clothing immediately, flush 15+ min, no creams
3. INGESTION: Do NOT induce vomiting, rinse mouth, seek medical
4. INHALATION: Move to fresh air, oxygen if needed, call 911
```

**Right panel -- ACID SPILL RESPONSE (Amber accent):**

```
1. Alert personnel -- clear area
2. Don full PPE before approaching
3. Contain with acid-resistant dike/absorbent
4. Neutralize with soda ash (Na2CO3) or lime
   CAUTION: Generates heat and CO2
5. Collect neutralized material for disposal
6. Flush area with water
7. Report per facility procedure
```

**Gas evolution cards:**

| Gas | Source | Warning |
|---|---|---|
| HYDROGEN (H2) -- CATHODE | Flammable, LEL 4% | Ventilate above tank; no flames/sparks/hot work within 3 ft |
| OXYGEN (O2) -- ANODE | Enriches atmosphere | Accelerates combustion; ventilation prevents accumulation |
