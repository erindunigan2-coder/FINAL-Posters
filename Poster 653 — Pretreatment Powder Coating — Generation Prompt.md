## Phase 4 -- Three-Column Comparison Hero

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `THREE TECHNOLOGIES -- ONE GOAL: ADHESION + CORROSION PROTECTION`. Three columns:

**Iron Phosphate** (Teal, "The Workhorse"): pH 3.5--5.5, 25--75 mg/ft2, amorphous, 100--140 F, 60--120 sec. Salt spray: 500--750 hr. Lowest cost.

**Zinc Phosphate** (Amber, "The Premium"): Zn/Mn/Ni modified, 150--500 mg/ft2, crystalline, 95--130 F, 120--180 sec. Salt spray: 750--1,500+ hr. 3--5x cost.

**Zirconium Nanoceramic** (Emerald, "The Rising Star"): H2ZrF6, 5--30 mg/ft2 nanoscale, ambient to 110 F, 60--120 sec. Salt spray: 500--750 hr. Chrome-free, multi-metal, growing adoption.

## Phase 5 -- Performance Table + Chromate Callout

**Performance Comparison Table**: technology, coating weight, crystal type, B117 hours, complexity, cost index.

**Chromate Conversion** (Coral accent, RoHS flag): Hex chromate (MIL-DTL-5541 Type I) still required by some aerospace specs. Trivalent (Type II) gaining approval. Non-chrome alternatives growing. `Hex chrome being phased out under RoHS/REACH.`

**Decision Matrix**: general industrial = iron phos | automotive/heavy equipment = zinc phos | multi-metal line = zirconium nanoceramic | aerospace aluminum = chromate per spec | extended warranty (10+ yr) = zinc phos.
