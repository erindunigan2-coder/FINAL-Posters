**Box contents:**

*Box 1 -- Alkaline Clean:*
- Badge: `STAGE 1` fill `#2EC4B6`
- Params: `130--160 F (55--70 C)` / `30--60 g/L (4--8 oz/gal)` / `2--10 min soak`
- Purpose: `Remove oils, compounds, fingerprints`
- Check: `CHECK: Water-break-free after rinse`

*Box 2 -- Rinse (Pre-Etch):*
- Badge: `STAGE 2` fill `#2EC4B6`
- Params: `Ambient temp` / `Cascade preferred` / `<500 uS/cm target`
- Purpose: `Remove alkaline cleaner before etch`
- Check: `Dwell 10--15 sec over cleaner to drain`

*Box 3 -- Caustic Etch:*
- Badge: `STAGE 3` fill `#E8A020`
- Params: `NaOH 40--80 g/L` / `130--150 F (55--65 C)` / `1--5 min (alloy-dependent)`
- Purpose: `Uniform matte surface, remove mill finish`
- Check: `CAUTION: H2 gas -- ventilation required` (`#E05C5C`)

*Box 4 -- Desmut:*
- Badge: `STAGE 4` fill `#E8A020`
- Params: `HNO3 25--50% v/v` / `Ambient, 30--120 sec` / `HF for Cu alloys (2024, 7075)`
- Purpose: `Remove etch smut (Cu, Si, Fe residues)`
- Check: `HF: calcium gluconate required at station` (`#E05C5C`)

*Box 5 -- Rinse (Pre-Anodize):*
- Badge: `STAGE 5` fill `#2EC4B6`
- Params: `DI water preferred` / `<100 uS/cm target` / `60--120 sec, double cascade`
- Purpose: `Prevent electrolyte contamination`
- Check: `Cl- and F- dragover destroy coating quality` (`#E05C5C`)

*Box 6 -- Anodize (Main Tank):*
- Badge: `STAGE 6` fill `#27AE60`
- Params: `H2SO4 150--200 g/L` / `64--72 F (18--22 C)` / `12--18 ASF (1.2--1.8 A/dm2)` / `20--60 min`
- Purpose: `Grow anodic oxide film (5--25 um)`
- Check: `Temperature is the #1 parameter` (`#27AE60`)

*Box 7 -- Dye (Optional):*
- Badge: `STAGE 7` fill `#E8A020`
- Params: `Organic dye 1--3 g/L` / `120--150 F (50--65 C)` / `pH 5.0--6.5, 5--30 min`
- Purpose: `Color the porous oxide before sealing`
- Check: `Min 8 um coating for good color uptake`

*Box 8 -- Seal:*
- Badge: `STAGE 8` fill `#E8A020`
- Params: `Hot DI: 205--212 F (96--100 C)` / `or Ni acetate: 158--185 F` / `15--30 min`
- Purpose: `Close pores, lock dye, corrosion protection`
- Check: `Dye spot test (ASTM B680) to verify seal`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

Four problem cards:

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BURNING | Excessive CD, high temp, or low acid | Reduce CD; check temperature |
| 2 | POWDERING | Bath temp >77 F (>25 C) or too long | Cool bath; reduce cycle time |
| 3 | PITTING | Chloride >25 ppm in anodize bath | Analyze bath; improve pre-anodize rinse |
| 4 | POOR DYE UPTAKE | Coating too thin (<8 um) or sealed too early | Increase anodize time; verify seal timing |
