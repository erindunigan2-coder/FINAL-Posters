## Phase 5 -- Parameters + Why Double Clean

Y: 15.0" to 22.0". Section: `CLEANING PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameter Tables (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

**Soak Clean:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Cleaner type | Non-silicated alkaline soak |
| Concentration | 4--8 oz/gal (30--60 g/L) |
| Temperature | 140--180 F (60--82 C) |
| Time | 3--10 min |
| pH | 12--14 |
| Agitation | Mechanical or air |

**Electroclean:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Concentration | 4--8 oz/gal |
| Temperature | 140--180 F |
| Current density | 20--75 ASF |
| Time | 1--3 min |
| Polarity | Cathodic then anodic (anodic last) |

**Right -- Why Double Clean? (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):**
Title: `WHY DOUBLE CLEAN FOR Zn-Ni?` `#E8A020`.
- Alloy composition (12--16% Ni) controlled by CD, temperature, and surface condition
- Organic contamination changes cathode film chemistry -- Ni% shifts unpredictably
- Low Ni% (< 12%): corrosion drops to plain zinc levels
- High Ni% (> 16%): cracking and adhesion failures
- Passivate adhesion more sensitive to contamination than plain zinc
- Electroclean removes embedded contaminants soak cannot reach

Key stat: `A 1% shift in Ni% can mean a 200-hour shift in salt spray performance.` `#E05C5C`

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE CLEANING STAGE`.

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ni% out of spec | Alloy composition inconsistent | Organic residue shifting cathode chemistry | Verify double-clean; check cleaner strength |
| Passivate flaking | Chromate peels on tape test | Organic film between Zn-Ni and passivate | Add anodic electroclean; extend time |
| Skip plating | Bare areas on Zn-Ni deposit | Silicate residue from cleaner | Switch to non-silicated cleaner |
| Poor adhesion | Zn-Ni peels from substrate | Under-cleaning, residual oxide | Anodic electroclean; verify water-break-free |
| Pitting | Small pits in Zn-Ni deposit | Oil micro-droplets carried to plate bath | Carbon treat Zn-Ni bath; improve cleaning |

## Phase 7 -- Safety

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `SAFETY -- CLEANING CHEMISTRY`.

**Left -- Chemical Hazards (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- NaOH/KOH: severe burns -- pH > 12
- Hot solution (140--180 F): thermal burn risk
- Mist/vapor: respiratory irritant -- ventilation required
- Add cleaner to water, never reverse
- Electrocleaner generates gas -- avoid ignition near cathodic tanks

**Right -- PPE (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Chemical splash goggles or face shield
- Chemical-resistant gloves (nitrile or neoprene)
- Chemical-resistant apron
- Eyewash station within 10 seconds
- SDS posted and accessible

**Bottom note (Y: 31.5"):**
`Nickel salts in the Zn-Ni plating bath downstream are IARC Group 1 carcinogens (inhalable). Maintain clean PPE to prevent cross-contamination.`
