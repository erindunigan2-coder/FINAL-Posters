## Phase 4 -- Gas Evolution Mechanism (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `HOW ELECTROCLEANING ACTUALLY WORKS -- THE ELECTROCHEMISTRY`.

Two-column hero.

**Left -- Cathodic Reaction** (coral-accented glass card, H ~9.5"):
- Title: `CATHODIC (Work = Cathode -)`
- Subtitle: `Hydrogen Evolution at the Workpiece`
- Reaction: `2H2O + 2e-` --> `H2 (gas) + 2OH-`
- Key points:
  - More gas volume than anodic (2x per mole of electrons)
  - Aggressive mechanical scrubbing
  - RISK: Hydrogen embrittlement on high-strength steel
  - RISK: Cathodic smut -- dissolved metals plate onto work
  - NEVER use as final step before nickel plating

**Right -- Anodic Reaction** (emerald-accented glass card, H ~9.5"):
- Title: `ANODIC (Work = Anode +)`
- Subtitle: `Oxygen Evolution at the Workpiece`
- Reaction: `2OH-` --> `H2O + 1/2 O2 (gas) + 2e-`
- Key points:
  - Less gas volume but larger O2 bubbles
  - Slight metal dissolution -- exposes fresh surface
  - NO hydrogen embrittlement risk
  - NO cathodic smut
  - REQUIRED as final step before nickel plating

**Bottom callout** (amber glass pill):
`Periodic Reverse (PR) alternates cathodic and anodic -- gets the aggressive cathodic scrub followed by the anodic finish. Typical cycle: 10 sec cathodic / 10 sec anodic.`

## Phase 5 -- Mode Selection Decision Table

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `WHICH MODE? -- MATCH TO YOUR APPLICATION`.

Glass table. Seven application rows:

| Application | Mode | CD (ASF / A/dm2) | Voltage | Time | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steel before Ni/Cr plate | ANODIC (`#27AE60`) | 30-75 / 3.2-8.1 | 6-12 V | 60-120 sec | REQUIRED anodic before Ni |
| Steel -- heavy soil | CATHODIC then ANODIC (`#E05C5C`/`#27AE60`) | 20-50 / 2.2-5.4 | 6-9 V | 30-60 sec each | Final phase MUST be anodic |
| High-strength steel (>40 HRC) | ANODIC ONLY (`#27AE60`) | 20-50 / 2.2-5.4 | 6-9 V | 30-60 sec | NO cathodic -- H embrittlement |
| Copper / brass | ANODIC (`#27AE60`) | 20-40 / 2.2-4.3 | 6-9 V | 30-90 sec | Light touch -- avoid dissolution |
| Zinc die cast | ANODIC (`#27AE60`) | 10-30 / 1.1-3.2 | 3-6 V | 15-30 sec | VERY short -- alkaline attack |
| Highly polished surfaces | CATHODIC (`#E05C5C`) | 20-50 / 2.2-5.4 | 6-9 V | 30-60 sec | When anodic etch unacceptable |
| General / difficult soils | PERIODIC REVERSE (`#2EC4B6`) | 20-50 / 2.2-5.4 | 6-12 V | 60-120 sec total | Best overall; final phase anodic |

## Phase 6 -- Immersion Time by Substrate

Y: 22.0" to 27.5". Section label: `TIME LIMITS -- SUBSTRATE SENSITIVITY`.

Four glass cards:

**Steel / Iron** (`#2EC4B6`): 30-120 sec standard; up to 3 min heavy soil. Most forgiving. Limit cathodic on >40 HRC.

**Copper / Brass** (`#27AE60`): 30-90 sec. Anodic can dissolve thin flash copper. Use light current.

**Zinc Die Cast** (`#E05C5C`): 15-30 sec MAX. Alkaline dissolution risk. Low CD, short time.

**Stainless Steel** (`#E8A020`): 30-90 sec. May require higher CD (50-75 ASF) for passive film removal.

## Phase 7 -- Common Failures

Y: 27.5" to 32.5". Section label: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 5 COMMON FAILURES`.

Glass table rows:

| Failure | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Adhesion failure | Cathodic smut; cathodic as final step before Ni | Switch to anodic final step; carbon treat; check metals |
| Hydrogen embrittlement | Cathodic on >40 HRC; excessive time | Anodic ONLY for >40 HRC; bake 375 +/- 25 F per ASTM B850 |
| Etching / pitting | Chloride >10 g/L; low alkalinity; excess voltage | Titrate chlorides; maintain alk; reduce voltage; partial dump |
| No gas (dead tank) | Open circuit; bad bus bar; blown fuse; corroded tips | Check connections; clean tips; verify rectifier with ammeter |
| Excessive foam | Surfactant overdose; oil drag-in | Reduce surfactant; improve soak cleaner upstream |
