## Phase 4 -- Steel Selection Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.0". Section label: `SUITABLE STEEL GRADES FOR AUSTEMPERING`. 5-row grade table: spring steels (1075/1095/5160/6150, 0.200"/0.5" max), medium-carbon alloy (4140/4150/4340, 1.0--1.5"), ductile cast iron (ADI per spec), thin plain carbon (1060/1075/1095, ~0.200" max), NOT SUITABLE (1018/1020).

Rule of thumb: `Best candidates have 0.40--1.00% carbon and sufficient hardenability to avoid the pearlite nose.`

---

## Phase 5 -- Section Thickness + Cleaning

**Section Thickness Decision** (Y: 14.0" to 20.5"): Flowchart -- plain carbon --> max ~0.200" | alloy steel --> max 0.5--1.5" | ductile iron --> per ASTM A897. Key insight: pearlite nose at 1000--1100 F; thicker sections cool slower at core.

**Cleaning Checklist**: solvent degrease, remove scale/rust, verify dry, stress relieve if cold-worked, inspect for defects. Contaminant risks: oil = fire in salt, moisture = explosion, scale = non-uniform microstructure.
