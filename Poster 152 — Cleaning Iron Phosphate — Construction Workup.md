# Poster #152 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Iron Phosphate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of 6. The cleaning stage for iron phosphate is deceptively important -- if the surface is not oil-free and silicate-free, the phosphate coating skips. This poster covers spray vs. immersion parameters, cleaner compatibility requirements, and the silicate warning that trips up more shops than anything else.

### ZONE 5 -- Cleaner Compatibility + Defects

**Two-column layout (Y: 20.7" to 26.3"):**

**Left -- Cleaner Compatibility (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Section label: `WHAT TO AVOID` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#E05C5C`.

Three warning items (each a small card with coral left accent):

| Warning | Detail |
|---|---|
| SILICATE CLEANERS | Silicate residues interfere with phosphate crystal nucleation. If you must use silicates, control below 0.5% and rinse aggressively. |
| EXCESS CAUSTIC (NaOH > 5%) | Overly caustic cleaners passivate the steel surface and inhibit phosphate deposition. Keep pH below 11.5. |
| ETCH INHIBITORS | Some cleaners contain etch inhibitors that leave the surface too passive for iron phosphate. Match cleaner to process. |

Warning title: Barlow SemiBold 14 pt `#E05C5C`. Detail: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`.

**Right -- Cleaning Defects (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Section label: `CLEANING FAILURES` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Skip areas on phosphate | Residual oil, fingerprints | Improve cleaner conc/temp/time |
| Phosphate too heavy/powdery | Over-etched surface | Reduce cleaner alkalinity |
| Staining after phosphate | Silicate residue | Switch to silicate-free cleaner |
| Uneven coating | Mixed soils, variable racking | Pre-clean heavily soiled parts |

Defect: Barlow SemiBold 13 pt `#E05C5C`. Cause: Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`. Fix: Inter Medium 12 pt `#27AE60`.

## Design Notes

The silicate warning is the single most important message on this poster. More iron phosphate skip problems trace back to silicate-containing cleaners than any other single cause. Make that warning visually prominent -- coral accent, bold text, prominent position.
