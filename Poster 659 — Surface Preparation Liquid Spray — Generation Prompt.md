## Phase 4 -- Three-Substrate Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.5". Section label: `PREP PATH BY SUBSTRATE`.

Three columns, each rounded rect fill `#1E2435`.

**Steel (W: 7.33", accent `#C8D0D8`):**
1. `SSPC-SP1: Solvent clean (remove oils)` 2. `SSPC-SP2/SP3: Hand or power tool` 3. `SSPC-SP6 to SP-10: Blast to spec` 4. `Profile: 1.5--3.0 mils (38--76 um)` 5. `Phosphate conversion coating` 6. `Prime within 4 hours (humid environments)`
Key metric `#E8A020`: `SP-6 Commercial Blast = standard for liquid paint`

**Aluminum (W: 7.33", accent `#2EC4B6`):**
1. `Alkaline etch or non-etch clean` 2. `Chromate conversion (MIL-DTL-5541) or non-chrome` 3. `Scuff sand: 180--320 grit Scotch-Brite` 4. `Anodize (sulfuric or chromic) for aerospace` 5. `Apply primer within spec window`
Key metric: `Chromate conversion = aerospace standard (Type I or II)`
RoHS note `#E05C5C`: `Hex chrome phasing out -- trivalent or non-chrome alternatives gaining spec approval`

**Plastic (W: 7.67", accent `#E8A020`):**
1. `Solvent wipe (IPA) -- remove mold release` 2. `CPO primer for PP/PE` 3. `Flame treatment (3--5 sec exposure)` 4. `Plasma treatment (industrial)` 5. `Static dissipation for electrostatic spray` 6. `Apply primer per adhesion promoter window`
Key metric: `Adhesion promotion is MANDATORY for polyolefins (PP, PE). Without it: zero adhesion.`

## Phase 5 -- SSPC Grades + Profile

Y: 15.8" to 20.8". Section label: `BLAST GRADES AND PROFILE TARGETS`.

6-row table. Columns: Grade | Name | Removal Level | Profile (mils) | Use Case.

| SP-1 | Solvent Clean | Visible oil/grease | N/A | Minimum first step |
| SP-2 | Hand Tool | Loose rust, scale, paint | Variable | Field touch-up |
| SP-3 | Power Tool | Loose rust, scale, paint | Variable | Better than SP-2 |
| SP-6 | Commercial Blast | 66% removal | 1.5--3.0 | Standard for liquid paint |
| SP-10 | Near-White Blast | 95% removal | 1.5--3.0 | High-performance coatings |
| SP-5 | White Metal Blast | 100% removal | 2.0--3.5 | Immersion service, tank lining |

Bottom note `#E8A020`: `Profile measured per ASTM D4417. Liquid primers generally tolerate slightly coarser profiles than powder coatings.`

## Phase 6 -- Aluminum + Plastic Detail

Y: 21.2" to 26.3". Two-column layout.

**Left -- `ALUMINUM PREP -- THREE OPTIONS` (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
Chemical conversion: Chromate (MIL-DTL-5541): 40--150 mg/ft2 / Non-chrome (Ti/Zr nanoceramic): growing adoption. Mechanical: Scuff sand 180--320 grit. Anodize: Sulfuric or chromic; required by many aerospace paint specs.

**Right -- `PLASTIC PREP -- ADHESION IS EVERYTHING` (accent `#E8A020`):**
CPO primer for PP/PE / Flame treatment: 3--5 sec, raises surface energy ~30 to ~50+ dyne/cm / Plasma treatment / Static dissipation. Test note `#27AE60`: `Dyne pen test to verify surface energy > 38 dyne/cm before painting.`

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". Section label: `WHEN PREP FAILS -- 6 COATING DEFECTS`. 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADHESION LOSS (STEEL) | `#E05C5C` | Mill scale, oil, insufficient profile | Blast to SP-6; solvent clean first |
| ADHESION LOSS (ALUMINUM) | `#E05C5C` | Missing conversion coating | Apply chromate or non-chrome conversion |
| ADHESION LOSS (PLASTIC) | `#E8A020` | No adhesion promoter on polyolefin | CPO primer or flame/plasma treatment |
| FLASH RUST | `#E05C5C` | Blast-to-prime time exceeded | Prime within 4 hr; control humidity |
| FISH-EYE | `#E8A020` | Silicone contamination or mold release | IPA wipe; identify contamination source |
| PROFILE TOO COARSE | `#2EC4B6` | Aggressive media or excessive blast | Verify 1.5--3.0 mils; change media |
