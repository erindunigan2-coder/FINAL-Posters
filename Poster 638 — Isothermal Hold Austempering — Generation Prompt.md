## Phase 5 -- Hold Time Guidelines

Y: 14.0" to 21.0". Section label: `RECOMMENDED HOLD TIMES BY MATERIAL AND SECTION`. 7-row table: 1075/1095 thin strip (30--45 min), 5160 spring (45--60), 4150/4340 structural (60--90), 52100 bearing (45--60), ADI Grade 1 (60--90), ADI Grade 3 (90--120), ADI Grade 5 (90--120).

Rule of thumb: `Hold for at least 1.5x the estimated transformation time. Under-holding is far worse than over-holding.`

## Phase 6 -- Early Pull Consequences + Salt Bath Management

**Three-panel "early pull" layout** (Y: 21.0" to 27.5"):
- COMPLETE HOLD (Emerald): 100% bainite, optimal, no temper.
- SLIGHTLY EARLY (Amber): 80--95% bainite + retained austenite --> martensite on cooling, marginal.
- WAY TOO EARLY (Coral): < 60% bainite + untempered martensite, failure/scrap.

Warning: `RETAINED AUSTENITE that transforms to UNTEMPERED MARTENSITE on cooling is the #1 failure mode.`

**Salt Bath Management** (Y: 27.5" to 32.5"): Monitor: bath temp (continuous), recovery after loading, agitation, salt level, timer from recovery not immersion. Do NOT: add second load mid-cycle, turn off agitation, start timer before recovery, estimate hold time.
