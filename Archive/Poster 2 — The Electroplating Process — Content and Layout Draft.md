## Section 1 — Watson Flag Status and Design Decisions

**Status: TWO FLAGS — both non-blocking.**

**Flag 1 (Drew):** Confirm ion migration conventions — cations toward cathode (negative), anions toward anode (positive). This is fundamental electrochemistry and is correct, but Watson flagged it for a sanity check since it will be permanently printed.

**Flag 2 (Drew):** Confirm the "anode dissolves / cathode builds" simplification covers both soluble and insoluble systems. The poster presents both systems clearly — soluble anodes that dissolve and replenish, insoluble anodes that conduct only.

**Design decisions:**

- **Layout: hero tank diagram + supporting panels.** The centerpiece of this poster is a large, labeled electroplating cell cross-section — the single most important diagram in plating education. Supporting elements (four components, process flow, defect strip) orbit around it. This poster is more diagram-driven than data-driven.

- **Hero visual: the electroplating cell.** A large, clear, labeled cross-section of a plating tank showing rectifier, anode, cathode (part), electrolyte, bus bars, and current/ion flow arrows. This must be the biggest, most beautiful technical illustration in the series so far.

- **Audience: dual.** New hires learn the mechanism. Customers in a boardroom see a credible, polished explanation of what their plater does. The tone is explanatory, not dense.

- **Color coding for the cell diagram:** Emerald `#27AE60` for the cathode/deposit (where metal builds up — positive connotation). Amber `#E8A020` for the anode (where metal dissolves — neutral/active connotation). Teal `#2EC4B6` for ion migration arrows (the solution-phase connection). Coral `#E05C5C` for electron flow in the external circuit.

### BLOCK C — The Electroplating Cell (HERO Illustration — Zone 2)

**Section label (Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 30 pt, `#F0EDE8`, centered):**

> THE ELECTROPLATING CELL

This is the poster's centerpiece — a large, labeled cross-section of a plating tank. It must be clear enough to read from 6 feet and beautiful enough for a boardroom.

**Illustration elements:**

1. **Plating tank** — large rounded rectangle, `#3A4055` Mid Slate outline (3 pt stroke), `#1E2435` Dark Callout fill, approximately 20" wide x 9" tall, centered in zone
2. **Electrolyte** — the interior of the tank. A subtle gradient or horizontal wavy line treatment at 30% opacity in `#2EC4B6` Teal to suggest solution fill
3. **Rectifier** — rounded rectangle above the tank (~4" wide x 1.5" tall), `#252B3D` fill, `#3A4055` border, centered above tank. Labeled `DC RECTIFIER` in Barlow SemiBold 18 pt. Shows `(-)` on left and `(+)` on right in JetBrains Mono 16 pt, `#F0EDE8`
4. **Bus bars** — two horizontal lines from rectifier to tank edges, 2 pt, `#3A4055`, connecting (-) terminal to cathode side and (+) terminal to anode side
5. **Cathode (part)** — vertical rectangle on LEFT side of tank interior, `#27AE60` Emerald fill, approximately 1.5" wide x 5" tall. This is the workpiece.
   - Label above: `CATHODE (Part)` — Barlow SemiBold, 16 pt, `#27AE60`
   - Sub-label: `Connected to (-) terminal` — JetBrains Mono, 12 pt, `#F0EDE8` at 70%
   - Deposit buildup on surface: thin rectangle (0.2" wide) on the cathode face closest to the anode, `#C8D0D8` Bright Silver — this represents the growing deposit
6. **Anode** — vertical rectangle on RIGHT side of tank interior, `#E8A020` Amber fill, approximately 1.5" wide x 5" tall
   - Label above: `ANODE (Metal)` — Barlow SemiBold, 16 pt, `#E8A020`
   - Sub-label: `Connected to (+) terminal` — JetBrains Mono, 12 pt, `#F0EDE8` at 70%
   - Dissolution arrows: 3-4 small arrows pointing away from the anode surface INTO the solution, `#E8A020`, labeled `M²⁺`
7. **Ion flow arrows (in solution):**
   - Cation arrows (M²⁺): 3-4 horizontal arrows from anode side toward cathode side, `#2EC4B6` Teal, 2 pt with arrowheads
   - Label: `Cations (M²⁺) → toward cathode` — Inter Regular, 13 pt, `#2EC4B6`
   - Anion arrows: 2-3 lighter arrows going from cathode side toward anode side, `#2EC4B6` at 50% opacity, 1.5 pt
   - Label: `Anions (SO₄²⁻, Cl⁻) → toward anode` — Inter Regular, 12 pt, `#2EC4B6` at 60%
8. **Electron flow arrows (external circuit):**
   - Arrows along the bus bars from (-) terminal DOWN to cathode, `#E05C5C` Coral, 1.5 pt
   - Arrows along the bus bars from anode UP to (+) terminal, `#E05C5C` Coral, 1.5 pt
   - Label near rectifier: `Electron flow (e⁻)` — JetBrains Mono, 12 pt, `#E05C5C`
9. **Cathode reaction equation (callout near cathode):**
   - Small text box adjacent to cathode: `M²⁺ + 2e⁻ → M⁰` — JetBrains Mono, 14 pt, `#27AE60`
   - Below: `Metal deposits here` — Inter Regular, 12 pt, `#27AE60`
10. **Anode reaction equation (callout near anode):**
    - Small text box adjacent to anode: `M⁰ → M²⁺ + 2e⁻` — JetBrains Mono, 14 pt, `#E8A020`
    - Below: `Metal dissolves here` — Inter Regular, 12 pt, `#E8A020`
11. **Key simplification banner (below the tank):**
    - Full-width text box, centered, `#F0EDE8`
    - Inter Medium, 18 pt
    - Text: `Metal ions travel through the solution toward the part. When they arrive, they pick up electrons and become solid metal.`

### BLOCK E — Soluble vs. Insoluble Anode Comparison (Zone 4)

**Section label (Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 26 pt, `#F0EDE8`, centered):**

> TWO ANODE SYSTEMS — ONE PRINCIPLE

**Layout:** Two columns with 0.3" gutter.

**Left column — "Soluble Anodes" (Amber accent)**

Callout box: fill `#1E2435`, left border 4 pt `#E8A020` Amber, corner radius 6 pt

Title (Barlow SemiBold, 20 pt, `#E8A020`):
> SOLUBLE ANODES

Equation (JetBrains Mono, 16 pt, `#F0EDE8`):
> M⁰ (anode) → M²⁺ (solution) → M⁰ (deposit)

Body (Inter Regular, 16 pt, `#F0EDE8`):
> The anode dissolves, releasing metal ions into solution. The cathode consumes those ions as deposit. The bath is self-replenishing — metal concentration stays stable.

Process examples (JetBrains Mono, 14 pt, `#F0EDE8` at 80%):
> Acid copper (Cu-P) | Watts nickel (R-Rounds)
> Silver (Ag) | Tin (Sn) | Acid zinc (Zn)

Principle line (Inter Medium, 16 pt, `#E8A020`):
> What the anode gives up, the cathode builds.

**Right column — "Insoluble Anodes" (Teal accent)**

Callout box: fill `#1E2435`, left border 4 pt `#2EC4B6` Teal, corner radius 6 pt

Title (Barlow SemiBold, 20 pt, `#2EC4B6`):
> INSOLUBLE ANODES

Equation (JetBrains Mono, 16 pt, `#F0EDE8`):
> Anode conducts current only → no dissolution

Body (Inter Regular, 16 pt, `#F0EDE8`):
> The anode does not dissolve. Metal ions are consumed at the cathode, depleting the bath. The operator must analyze and add metal salts to maintain concentration.

Process examples (JetBrains Mono, 14 pt, `#F0EDE8` at 80%):
> Hard chrome (Pb-Sn alloy) | Alk. zinc (steel)
> Trivalent chrome (carbon)

Principle line (Inter Medium, 16 pt, `#2EC4B6`):
> Operator adds chemistry. The anode just conducts.

### BLOCK F — Key Process Variables Table (Zone 5 — Left)

**Section label (Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 24 pt, `#F0EDE8`):**

> WHAT THE OPERATOR CONTROLS

**Table structure — 3 columns:**
1. **Variable** (~25% width) — Inter Medium, 16 pt
2. **What It Affects** (~40% width) — Inter Regular, 15 pt
3. **How It's Controlled** (~35% width) — Inter Regular, 15 pt

Column header row: `#3A4055` Mid Slate fill, Warm White text, Barlow SemiBold 18 pt.

Data rows (alternating `#1A1F2E` / `#252B3D`):

| Variable | What It Affects | How It's Controlled |
|----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Current density (ASF) | Deposition rate, deposit quality | Rectifier amperage + surface area |
| Temperature | Bath conductivity, brightener activity | Heaters, chillers, thermostats |
| Time | Deposit thickness | Timer or operator judgment |
| Agitation | Uniformity, limiting CD | Air sparging, mechanical movement |
| Bath chemistry | Deposit properties, throwing power | Analysis + chemical additions |
| pH | Stress, brightness, efficiency | Acid or alkali additions |
| Filtration | Smoothness, particle freedom | Filter pump, carbon treatment |

### BLOCK G — Common Defects Quick Reference (Zone 5 — Right)

**Section label (Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 24 pt, `#F0EDE8`):**

> WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Callout box: fill `#1E2435`, border `#E05C5C` Coral 1.5 pt, corner radius 8 pt

**Defect list (compact table format inside the callout):**

Each defect row: left-border accent in Coral (0.06"), defect name in Inter Medium 15 pt `#E05C5C`, cause in Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`.

| Defect | Likely Cause |
|--------|-------------|
| **Burning** (dark, rough at edges) | Current density too high (HCD) |
| **Skip plating** (bare spots) | CD too low; poor cleaning (LCD) |
| **Pitting** (random pinholes) | H₂ gas adhesion; organics |
| **Peeling** (adhesion failure) | Surface prep failure |
| **Dullness** (matte finish) | Brightener depletion; low temp |
| **Roughness** (gritty surface) | Particles; metallic contamination |
| **Streaking** (vertical lines) | Inadequate rinsing; drag-in |
