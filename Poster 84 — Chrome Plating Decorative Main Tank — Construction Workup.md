# Poster #84 -- Construction Workup
## Chrome Plating (Decorative) -- Main Tank

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. This is the heart of the decorative chrome process -- the tank where the brilliant chrome layer is deposited. The poster covers both trivalent and hexavalent chemistries side-by-side, with trivalent positioned as the modern standard. This is the densest poster in the EP-07 cluster. It covers bath composition, operating parameters, the critical CrO3:SO4 ratio (hex) and pH control (tri), anode configuration, temperature-CD bright range, and defect diagnosis.

Hero visual: a plating tank cross-section showing anodes, cathode (workpiece), current flow, and the extremely thin chrome deposit being formed over nickel.

## Design Notes

This is the densest poster in EP-07 -- comparable to Poster #23 (Watts Nickel) and Poster #36 (Zinc Alkaline Main Tank). The dual chemistry panels are essential; a shop needs to know both systems even if they only run one. The bright range chart is the visual that a process engineer will reference most -- it explains more chrome plating problems than any single parameter. The safety panel must be impossible to miss. Hex chrome is the most hazardous chemistry in the entire poster series. The multi-layer workpiece cross-section in the hero (showing Cu + Ni + Cr layers at relative scale) tells the story: the chrome is almost nothing, but it is everything you see.
