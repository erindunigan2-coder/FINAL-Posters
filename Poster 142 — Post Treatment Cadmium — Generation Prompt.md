## Phase 6 -- AMS 2759/9 + Bake Rules

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `BAKE SPECIFICATIONS -- AMS 2759/9 AND ASTM B850`.

**Left -- AMS 2759/9 (accent `#E05C5C`):** Applies to >200 ksi steel / 375 +/- 25 F / 12--24 hours / Start within 1 hour / Verification: ASTM F519 notch tensile.

**Right -- ASTM B850 (accent `#E8A020`):** General HE relief / 375 +/- 25 F / 3 hours minimum / Start within 4 hours / Less stringent -- non-aerospace.

Bottom note (Emerald): `When in doubt, use the MORE stringent specification. Over-baking is not harmful. Under-baking can be fatal.`
