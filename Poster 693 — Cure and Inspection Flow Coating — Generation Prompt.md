## Phase 5 -- Coating Chemistry / Cure Table

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `COATING TYPE vs. CURE METHOD`.

| Coating | Cure Method | Temp | Time | Key Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alkyd enamel | Air/force dry | Ambient or 140-180 F | 1-7 days / 30-60 min | Tack-free to hard dry |
| Modified alkyd | Force dry | 140-180 F | 30-60 min | Through-dry |
| Baking enamel | Bake | 250-350 F | 15-30 min at PMT | Pencil hardness |
| 2K epoxy | Chemical cure | 50-100 F | 7-14 days | MEK rub 50+; recoat 4-24 hr |
| 2K urethane | Chemical cure | 50-100 F | 5-7 days | Recoat per TDS |
| Latex/waterborne | Air dry | 50-85 F, 40-70% RH | 1-7 days | Film formation > 50 F |

Footnote `#E8A020`: `PMT = Peak Metal Temperature. Confirm by metal temp, not oven air temp.`

## Phase 6 -- Inspection Checklist

Y: 21.3" to 26.3". Section label: `FLOW COATING INSPECTION CHECKLIST`.

**Left -- `FILM PROPERTY TESTS` (`#2EC4B6`):**

| Test | Standard | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| DFT | D7091 | Min per spec; top/mid/bottom |
| Adhesion | D3359 Method B | 4B-5B |
| Hardness | D3363 | Per spec (F-2H typical) |
| Flexibility | D522 | Per spec (1/8"-1/2" mandrel) |
| Cure verification | MEK rub D4752 | 50+ (thermoset only) |

**Right -- `VISUAL AND DEFECT CHECKS` (`#E8A020`):** Runs/sags at bottom edges. Holidays in inner corners, upward faces. Orange peel under raking light. Drip marks at lowest points. Color uniformity. Blistering in recesses.

Bottom `#E8A020`: `Flow coating requires MORE measurement points than spray because of +/- 30-50% variation. Min: 3 zones/part, 3 readings/zone.`

## Phase 6B -- Failures + Insight

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 4 cards + insight.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| UNDERCURE (SOFT FILM) | Oven cool, insufficient PMT, low ambient | Profile oven; extend cure; increase temp |
| DFT BELOW MIN (TOP) | Natural drainage -- top always thinnest | Increase viscosity; reduce drain; specify min DFT |
| SOLVENT POP / PINHOLES | Insufficient flash before bake | Extend flash; slower ramp |
| ADHESION FAILURE | Pretreatment, contamination, or undercure | Verify phosphate; check cleanliness; MEK rub |

Insight `#E8A020`: `Most common failure is DFT below minimum at the top. Fix is almost never "coat heavier" -- adjust orientation, viscosity, and drain time to redistribute.`
