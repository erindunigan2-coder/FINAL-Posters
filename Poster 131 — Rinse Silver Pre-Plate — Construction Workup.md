# Poster #131 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Silver -- Pre-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 4 of 8. The pre-plate rinse removes activation acid before parts enter the cyanide silver strike. This is a SAFETY-CRITICAL rinse -- acid drag-in to a cyanide bath generates lethal hydrogen cyanide gas. The rinse must be thorough. One tank, single immersion, then immediately to the silver strike.

## Design Notes

This is the most safety-critical rinse poster in any cluster. The acid/cyanide reaction hazard is real and lethal. The Zone 4 safety panel must be visually dominant -- larger and more prominent than a standard information panel. The chemical equations are included because operators should understand WHY this rinse matters, not just follow the rule blindly.
