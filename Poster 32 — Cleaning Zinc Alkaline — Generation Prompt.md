- Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, `28` pt, `#F0EDE8`, Center. Y: **22.2"**.

### Step 12 -- Problem table (5 rows)

Y: **22.9"** to **28.3"**. Full width, row H: `1.0"`, alternating fills, left accent `#E05C5C`.

Columns: Problem (3.5") | Symptom (5.0") | Cause (7.0") | Fix (7.5%).

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water break | Fish-eyes/skip plating | Residual oil -- weak/cold cleaner | Increase conc or temp; extend time |
| Poor adhesion | Zinc peels on bending | Oxide layer (under-cleaning) | Add electroclean; check cleaner age |
| Pitting in zinc | Scattered pits | Oil micro-droplets carried forward | Carbon treat zinc bath; improve agitation |
| Staining | Discolored before activation | Cleaner residue -- bad rinse | Add rinse stage; check flow |
| H-embrittlement | Delayed brittle fracture | Excess cathodic electrocleaning | Limit cathodic time; anodic-only >150 ksi |
