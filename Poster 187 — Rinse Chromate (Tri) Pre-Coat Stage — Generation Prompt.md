## Phase 5 -- Contamination Removal + Reoxidation

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Two-column.

**Left -- What This Rinse Removes:**

| Contaminant | Source | Why It Must Go |
|---|---|---|
| Acid residue | Deox drag-out | Accelerates bath consumption |
| Dissolved copper | 2xxx/7xxx alloys | Dark spots; bath contamination |
| Dissolved iron | Steel fixtures | Discoloration |
| Dissolved silicon | Cast alloys | Interferes with film |
| Fluoride | HF deoxidizers | Excess causes over-attack |

**Right -- The Reoxidation Problem:** Amber accent. Fresh oxide forms in seconds. Thin fresh oxide = acceptable. Thick aged oxide (> 5 min) = thin or absent coating. Rule: `< 5 minutes from last rinse to chromate. No exceptions.` If line stalls: `re-deoxidize rather than hope.`
