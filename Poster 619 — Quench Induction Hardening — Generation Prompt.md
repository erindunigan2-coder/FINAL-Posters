## Phase 4 -- Quench Configurations (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `THREE QUENCH CONFIGURATIONS`.

Three panels (W 7.33", H 6.5") with diagram placeholders:

| Panel | Config | Accent | Description | When Used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | INTEGRAL SPRAY | `#2EC4B6` | Orifices built into coil body. Quenchant sprays from coil after power-off. Delay 0.1--0.5 sec. | Single-shot; most common; shafts, bearing seats, cam lobes |
| 2 | FOLLOWING SPRAY (SCAN) | `#E8A020` | Separate spray ring below coil. Follows coil during scan, quenching each section after heating. Delay ~0 (continuous). | Progressive scan; long shafts, rolls, bars |
| 3 | IMMERSION QUENCH | `#27AE60` | Part dropped/lowered into tank after heating. Less precise. Delay 1--3 sec (transfer). | Large parts; full-body quench; some spin hardening |

Full-width callout below: `NO OIL QUENCH TANK` (Emerald). Induction uses water-based polymer (PAG) spray. Eliminates: oil fires, smoke/fumes, oil disposal, oil maintenance. Safety and environmental advantages are significant.

## Phase 5 -- Quenchant Parameters

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `QUENCHANT CONCENTRATION & SYSTEM PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- PAG Concentration vs. Severity:**

| PAG % | Severity | Equivalent | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0% (water) | Maximum (severe) | Water quench | Rarely used -- cracking risk |
| 5% | High | Fast oil | High-carbon; deep case |
| 10% | Moderate-high | Standard | Most common production |
| 15% | Moderate | Mild oil | Crack-sensitive parts |
| 20% | Mild | Slow oil | Thin sections; complex geometry |

Note: `Increasing PAG slows quench. Higher concentration reduces cracking on complex geometries.` Teal.

**Right -- System Parameters (Teal):**

Spray pressure: 20--60 psi. Flow rate: cover heated zone within 1 sec. Reservoir temp: 70--110 F. Concentration: refractometer weekly min. pH: 8.5--9.5 with biocide. Filtration: required (debris clogs orifices). Biological control: biocide per supplier.

## Phase 6 -- Quench Delay + Defects

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Quench Delay (Coral):**

`QUENCH DELAY -- THE CRITICAL FRACTION OF A SECOND`. Time between power-off and first quenchant contact. During delay, heat conducts from surface inward. Even 1--2 sec can: deepen austenitized zone, heat core, cause through-hardening on thin sections. Ranges: integral spray 0.1--0.5 sec, following spray ~0 sec, immersion 1--3 sec. RULE: minimize to shortest achievable. If > 2 sec, redesign delivery.

**Right -- Quench Defects (Coral):**

Four stacked cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| QUENCH CRACKING | Too severe (low PAG); sharp corners; residual stress | Increase PAG; radius transitions; stress relieve before |
| SOFT SPOTS (QUENCH) | Non-uniform spray; clogged orifices; no rotation | Clean orifices; verify pattern; ensure rotation |
| THROUGH-HARDENED | Delay too long; heat soaked to core | Reduce delay; verify spray timing |
| DISTORTION | Asymmetric quench; one side quenched first | Balance spray around circumference; verify orifice flow |
