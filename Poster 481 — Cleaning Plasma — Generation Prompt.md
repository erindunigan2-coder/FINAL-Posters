## Phase 4 -- 4-Step Cleaning Sequence (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `THE 4-STEP CLEANING SEQUENCE`.

Four vertical step cards with downward arrows. Each: W: 22.0", H: 2.2", fill `#1E2435`, left accent.

| Step | Accent | Name | Method | Pass/Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#2EC4B6` | SOLVENT DEGREASE | Vapor degrease (legacy) or aqueous alkaline (preferred). Remove oils, greases, machining fluids, fingerprints. | Visible contamination = fail |
| 2 | `#2EC4B6` | ALKALINE WASH | Immersion or spray. 50-70 degC, pH 10-12, 5-15 min. Rinse thoroughly. | Residual alkaline = fail |
| 3 | `#27AE60` | WATER-BREAK-FREE TEST | ASTM F22 equivalent. Surface must sheet water uniformly -- any beading = contamination. | Any water break = FAIL -- repeat Steps 1-2 |
| 4 | `#E8A020` | DRY | Forced air or oven dry. No moisture at grit blast. Moisture causes flash rust on steel. | Surface must be bone dry |

## Phase 5 -- Contamination Table

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `WHAT CONTAMINANTS DO TO YOUR COATING`.

| Contaminant | Source | Consequence | Detection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oil / grease | Machining fluids, fingerprints | Delamination, poor bond strength | Water-break-free test |
| Rust / mill scale | Storage, ambient exposure | Weak interface, porosity at bond line | Visual; SSPC-SP 5 verification |
| Moisture | Humidity, incomplete drying | Flash rust; hydrogen porosity | Dew point check; forced air dry |
| Cutting fluid residue | Prior machining operations | Outgassing during spray; porosity | Solvent wipe + UV inspection |
| Oxide films | Prior heat treatment, welding | Reduced mechanical interlocking | Grit blast removes (Stage 2) |
| Masking adhesive | Premature masking over dirty surface | Contamination trapped under mask | NEVER mask before cleaning |

Consequence column in `#E05C5C`.
