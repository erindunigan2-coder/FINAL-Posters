## Phase 4 -- Seal Coat Importance + Types (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section label: `WHY SEAL COATING IS ESSENTIAL`.

**With vs. Without Seal (Y: 3.8"-9.5"):** Two large side-by-side panels.

*Left -- WITHOUT Seal Coat* (accent `#E05C5C`):
- Interconnected porosity (5-15%) remains open
- Moisture, salt, chemicals reach steel through pores
- Cathodic protection works but zinc consumed faster
- White corrosion products form rapidly
- Service life reduced by 50-70%
- Appearance degrades quickly

Bottom: `Unsealed wire spray: functional but short-lived.`

*Right -- WITH Seal Coat* (accent `#27AE60`):
- Sealer fills interconnected porosity
- Moisture path to steel blocked
- Cathodic protection preserved at holidays/damage
- Extended service life (20-40+ years)
- Smooth surface for topcoat adhesion
- Professional appearance

Bottom: `Sealed wire spray: a complete corrosion protection system.`

**Seal Coat Selection Guide (Y: 10.0"-15.3"):**

| Sealer Type | Temp Range | Chem Resistance | Best For | Per AWS C2.18 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vinyl sealer | -30 to 60 degC | Moderate | Atmospheric; general purpose | Yes |
| Epoxy sealer | -30 to 120 degC | Good | Industrial; chemical; immersion | Yes |
| Silicone sealer | -50 to 250 degC | Good | High-temperature service | Yes |
| Polyurethane | -30 to 80 degC | Good | UV resistance; aesthetics | Compatible |
| Acrylic | -20 to 60 degC | Moderate | Fast-dry field application | Compatible |

Note: `Epoxy is the most commonly specified. Vinyl for cost-sensitive atmospheric. Silicone where temps exceed 120 degC.`

## Phase 5 -- Application Procedure + Paint System

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `SEAL COAT & PAINT SYSTEM APPLICATION`.

**Left -- Seal Coat Application Steps (W: 11.0"):** accent `#27AE60`.

| Step | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apply while coating still warm | Better sealer penetration |
| 2 | Brush, roll, or spray | Brush preferred for pore penetration |
| 3 | First coat: thin, worked in | Force sealer into porosity |
| 4 | Allow to tack | 15-60 min per sealer spec |
| 5 | Second coat if specified | Some specs require two coats |
| 6 | Cure per manufacturer | Full cure before topcoat or service |

Note: `Apply sealer as soon as practical after spray -- while still warm. Maximizes penetration.`

## Phase 6 -- Service Life + Maintenance + Problems

### Service Life Table (Y: 22.0"-28.5", left W: 11.0")

Section label: `EXPECTED SERVICE LIFE`.

| System | Mild (yr) | Moderate (yr) | Severe (yr) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zinc spray -- unsealed | 10-15 | 5-10 | 3-5 |
| Zinc spray + seal | 25-40 | 15-25 | 10-20 |
| Zinc spray + seal + paint | 30-50+ | 25-40 | 15-30 |
| Aluminum spray + seal | 30-40+ | 20-30 | 15-25 |
| Aluminum spray + seal + paint | 40-50+ | 30-40 | 20-35 |

Note: `Per AWS C2.18 and SSPC guidelines. Actual life depends on thickness, sealer quality, environment, and maintenance.`

### Maintenance Guidance (right W: 11.5")

Title: `MAINTENANCE & RE-COATING` accent `#2EC4B6`.
- Inspect annually
- Touch up topcoat/seal at first signs of degradation
- White corrosion product on zinc = protection working (not failure)
- Re-spray possible over existing if bond is sound
- Full re-coating: blast, re-spray, re-seal

Callout: `Life-cycle cost: thermal spray + seal is typically 2-3x cheaper than repeated paint maintenance over 30 years.`

### Common Problems (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `POST-TREATMENT PROBLEMS -- 4 COMMON ISSUES`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| SEALER NOT PENETRATING | Coating cooled too much; sealer too viscous | Apply while warm; thin per manufacturer |
| SEALER PEELING | Contamination; incompatible sealer | Clean surface; verify compatibility |
| TOPCOAT ADHESION FAILURE | Seal coat not cured; incompatible topcoat | Allow full cure; use compatible system |
| PREMATURE CORROSION | Insufficient seal coverage; holidays | Inspect seal for holidays; apply second coat |
