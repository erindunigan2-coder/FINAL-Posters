### Step 5 -- Stage detail panel (Y: 3.8" to 9.5")

Full-width rounded rect (23.0" x 5.5"), fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`.

**Left half:**
- Badge: `STAGE 1 -- CLEAN` fill `#2EC4B6`
- Chemistry: `Non-Etch Alkaline Cleaner` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt
- Characteristics: Type: Mildly alkaline, surfactant-based / pH: 9--11 / Function: Dissolve oils, soils, shop contaminants / NOT a caustic etch -- must not attack aluminum / Silicate-inhibited cleaners preferred

**Right half -- two callout cards:**

1. `WHY NON-ETCH?` (accent `#27AE60`): `Strongly caustic cleaners (NaOH > 5%) etch aluminum aggressively, producing a heavy smut layer that is harder to remove in the deox stage. For Ti/Zr lines, clean WITHOUT etching -- the deoxidizer handles surface activation.`

2. `MULTI-METAL LINES` (accent `#E8A020`): `Automotive body-in-white: one cleaner must handle steel + galvanized + aluminum panels in the same line. Low-energy, non-etch formulations are mandatory.`

### Step 6 -- Spray vs. Immersion comparison (Y: 10.0" to 15.0")

Two side-by-side panels:

**Left -- Spray Cleaning** (accent `#2EC4B6`):
- Params: `2--4 oz/gal (15--30 g/L)` / `100--140 F (38--60 C)` / `1--3 min` / `10--25 psi`
- Advantages: Faster processing, mechanical action, lower energy, standard for automotive

**Right -- Immersion Cleaning** (accent `#E8A020`):
- Params: `4--8 oz/gal (30--60 g/L)` / `120--160 F (49--71 C)` / `3--10 min` / `Air or mechanical agitation`
- Advantages: Better soil penetration, uniform contact in recesses, standard for aerospace

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `MULTI-METAL SUBSTRATE COMPATIBILITY`.

5-row table. Columns: Substrate (4.0") | Cleaner Type (5.0") | Caution (6.5") | Notes (7.5").

| Substrate | Cleaner Type | Caution | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrought Al (6xxx) | Non-etch alkaline, pH 9--11 | Caustic etch dissolves Al; silicate inhibitors OK | Most common automotive alloy family |
| Cast Al (356, A356) | Same; may need stronger surfactancy | High Si produces tenacious smut | Deox step critical for cast alloys |

| Cold-Rolled Steel | Multi-metal alkaline, pH 9--11 | Must not passivate the steel surface | Steel + Al in same automotive line |
| Galvanized Steel | Same multi-metal cleaner | Zinc sensitive to strongly alkaline pH | Avoid pH > 12 on galvanized |

## Phase 6 -- Cleaning Failures

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `CLEANING FAILURES -- DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCES`.

Four vertical failure chain cards (W: 5.5", H: 5.0", fill `#1E2435`, top accent `#E05C5C`):

| # | X | Failure | Downstream Effect | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | RESIDUAL OIL | Zr does not deposit -- bare spots, skip areas; paint peels | Water-break test after clean; no beading = clean |
| 2 | 6.33" | SILICATE RESIDUE | Silicate film blocks Zr deposition; invisible barrier | Use silicate-free or silicate-controlled cleaner |
| 3 | 12.16" | EXCESSIVE ETCH | Heavy smut on aluminum; requires aggressive deox | Use non-etch cleaner; avoid NaOH > 5% |
| 4 | 18.0" | ALKALINE DRAG-OVER | Raises deox pH; reduces acid effectiveness | Adequate rinse between clean and deox (Stage 2) |

Failure: Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#E05C5C`. Effect: Inter 13 pt `#F0EDE8`. Prevention: Inter Medium 13 pt `#27AE60`.

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | WATER BREAK (BEADING) | Oil not fully removed; cleaner too dilute or cold | Increase conc/temp/time; check surfactant level |
| 2 | 6.33" | WHITE SMUT ON AL | Caustic etch of aluminum; cleaner pH too high | Switch to non-etch cleaner; verify pH < 11 |
| 3 | 12.16" | FOAM OVERFLOW | Excess surfactant; drag-in of cutting oils | Reduce cleaner concentration; improve pre-rinse |
| 4 | 18.0" | FLASH RUST ON STEEL | Insufficient inhibitor in multi-metal cleaner | Add rust inhibitor to cleaner; speed up line |
