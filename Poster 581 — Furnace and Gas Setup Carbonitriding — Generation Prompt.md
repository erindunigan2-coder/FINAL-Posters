## Phase 5 -- Gas Composition + Control

### Gas Table (Y: 14.0"--19.5")

Section label: `ATMOSPHERE COMPOSITION`. 6-row table:

| Component | Source | Concentration | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| N2 | Endo gas | ~40% | Carrier; inert diluent |
| CO | Endo gas | ~20% | Primary carbon source |
| H2 | Endo + NH3 | ~40%+ | Reducing agent |
| CH4 | Enrichment | Added to raise Cp | Secondary carbon source |
| NH3 | Anhydrous cylinder | 2--10% | Nitrogen source |
| CO2 | Reaction product | < 0.5% | Cp indicator (inverse) |

### Cp + NH3 Control (Y: 19.5"--26.0")

**Left -- CARBON POTENTIAL (`#E8A020`):** Target Cp 0.5--0.8% C; lower than carburizing because N supplements hardening. Methods: zirconia O2 probe (primary), IR CO2 analyzer, shim stock (gold standard). Too high + NH3 = excessive RA; too low = inadequate hardness.

**Right -- AMMONIA CONTROL (`#2EC4B6`):** Addition 2--10% by volume; typical production 3--5%; residual 0.5--5% undissociated; at 1500 F 95--98% dissociates. Monitoring: Orsat or IR analyzer. Too much = RA + porosity; too little = no nitrogen benefit.
