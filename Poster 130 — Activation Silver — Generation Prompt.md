## Phase 4 -- Activation Tank Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE ACID ACTIVATION TANK`.

Tank cross-section (fill `#E8A020` at 8%, border 3pt `#C8D0D8`). Chemical equation: `Metal oxide + H2SO4 --> Metal sulfate + H2O`. Params: `H2SO4 5--10%` / `Ambient` / `15--30 sec` / `Gentle rack movement`.

Bottom callout (Coral): `CRITICAL: Parts must proceed DIRECTLY to silver strike after activation and rinse. Surface re-oxidizes within minutes.`

## Phase 5 -- Substrate-Specific Activation

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `ACTIVATION BY SUBSTRATE`.

| Substrate | Acid | Concentration | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Copper / Brass | H2SO4 | 5--10% v/v | 15--30 sec | Most common |
| Nickel-plated | H2SO4 | 5% v/v | 10--15 sec | Lighter dip |
| Steel (after Cu strike) | HCl | 10--20% v/v | 15--30 sec | Steel path uses HCl |
| Kovar / Alloy 42 | H2SO4 | 5--10% v/v | 15--30 sec | Electronics |

Note: `H2SO4 for copper-family. HCl for steel. Never mix acid types in same tank without thorough rinse.`

## Phase 6 -- Oxide Removal Concept

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHY ACTIVATION MATTERS`.

Three panels: **BEFORE** (`#E05C5C`) -- oxide film 10--50 angstroms, invisible, fatal to adhesion | **ACID DISSOLVES OXIDE** (`#E8A020`) -- acid attacks oxide preferentially, does NOT attack base metal | **AFTER** (`#27AE60`) -- bare metal exposed, highly reactive, clock starts on re-oxidation.

---

## Phase 7 -- Common Errors

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `TIMING AND COMMON ERRORS`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OVER-ACTIVATION | Time too long / acid too strong | Pitting on copper; follow time limits |
| 2 | UNDER-ACTIVATION | Time too short / spent acid | Oxide remains; silver peels |
| 3 | DELAY TO STRIKE | Parts sit in air | Re-oxidation; process within 2 min |
| 4 | WRONG ACID | H2SO4 on steel or HCl on copper | Smut or damage; match per table |
