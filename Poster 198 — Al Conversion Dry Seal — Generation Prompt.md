## Phase 4 -- Three Drying Pathways Hero

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `DRY / SEAL -- THREE PATHWAYS`.

### Step 5 -- Three pathway panels (Y: 3.8" to 10.5")

Three side-by-side panels (H: 6.5", fill `#1E2435`, top accent 4 pt):

**Air Dry** (X: 0.5", W: 7.33", accent `#2EC4B6`):
- `Temperature: Ambient` / `Method: Room temp or forced air (filtered)` / `Time: 15--30 min`
- When: Pure Zr (inorganic) formulations
- Caution: Slow; dust contamination risk

**Low-Temp Oven** (X: 8.08", W: 7.33", accent `#E8A020`):
- `Temperature: 180--250 F (82--121 C)` / `Method: Convection oven` / `Time: 5--15 min`
- When: Production lines needing throughput
- Caution: Do NOT exceed 250 F for pure Zr -- over-cure degrades film

**Thermal Cure (Hybrids)** (X: 15.67", W: 7.83", accent `#27AE60`):
- `Temperature: 200--350 F (93--177 C) -- PER SUPPLIER TDS` / `Method: Convection oven with calibrated thermocouples` / `Time: 5--15 min at metal temp`
- CRITICAL: Under-cure = uncrosslinked organic; Over-cure = degraded film

### Step 6 -- Thermal cure decision tree (Y: 11.0" to 15.0")

Wide panel (23.0" x 3.8", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`):
`DO YOU NEED A THERMAL CURE?`

- YES (hybrid silane + inorganic) -> Thermal cure REQUIRED. Check TDS. Measure METAL temperature. Typical 200--350 F for 5--15 min.
- NO (pure inorganic Zr) -> Air dry or low-temp oven. 180--250 F, 5--15 min. Do not exceed 250 F.
- NOT SURE? -> Check supplier TDS. If it specifies "cure temperature," it is a hybrid.

| Automotive OEM specs | GM, Ford, Toyota -- proprietary approval for specific Zr products |
| No dedicated MIL-SPEC | As of 2026, no MIL-DTL or MIL-PRF for Zr conversion coatings |

**Right -- THE STANDARDS GAP** (X: 14.75", W: 8.75", accent `#E8A020`):
Zr coatings commercially mature but specification-immature. No MIL-SPEC. Each customer must individually qualify. Aerospace actively developing specifications. Until then: qualify to customer requirements and supplier TDS.

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | POOR PAINT ADHESION | Hybrid under-cured; organic not crosslinked | Verify oven temp at PART surface; extend cure time |
| 2 | 6.33" | COATING DEGRADATION | Over-cure; oven too hot; dwell too long | Reduce oven temp; calibrate thermocouples; follow TDS |
| 3 | 12.16" | BLISTERING UNDER PAINT | Moisture trapped; incomplete drying | Extend dry time; verify parts fully dry before paint |
| 4 | 18.0" | PREMATURE CORROSION | No sealer on bare Zr; thin coating | Add supplementary sealer; verify XRF coating weight |
