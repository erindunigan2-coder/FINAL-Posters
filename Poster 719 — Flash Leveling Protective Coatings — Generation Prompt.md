## Phase 5 -- Amine Blush + Stripe Coating

### Amine Blush (Y: 14.5"-20.5")

Section label: `AMINE BLUSH -- THE INVISIBLE ADHESION KILLER`. Three columns.

Col 1 -- What: Amine + CO2 + moisture = amine carbamate (waxy). May be invisible. = intercoat adhesion failure.

Col 2 -- When: < 50 F, > 80% RH, cool/damp nights, dew formation, surface near dew point. `Can form even when application conditions were fine -- overnight cooling triggers it.`

Col 3 -- Remove: 1) Water wash + scrub. 2) Solvent wipe (MEK/acetone). 3) Dry. 4) Recoat within window. 5) If window exceeded: sand first. Note: some modern "blush-free" hardeners exist.

### Stripe Coating (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `STRIPE COATING -- PROTECTING THE WEAK POINTS`. Two columns.

Left -- What/Why: Brush-apply before each full spray coat to edges, welds, bolts, crevices. DFT thinnest at edges. Stripe ensures minimum at weakest points.

Right -- Sequence: 1) Mix per TDS. 2) Stripe edges/welds/bolts by brush. 3) Flash 30-60 min. 4) Spray full coat. 5) Measure DFT at striped locations. `Stripe coating is required by most marine/offshore/infrastructure specs. Not optional.`

## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| INTERCOAT ADHESION FAILURE | Amine blush / window exceeded | Wash/sand; remove blush; verify window |
| SOLVENT ENTRAPMENT | Topcoat before primer flashed | Extend flash; verify solvent rub; reduce DFT |
| LIFTING / WRINKLING | Strong solvent attacking undercured primer | Verify cure (MEK 50+) before topcoating |
| THIN DFT AT EDGES | Spray atomization thins film | Stripe coat ALL edges by brush |

Key insight: `The recoat window is the most commonly violated rule. Exceeding it means sanding or blasting. Check TDS. Time it. No exceptions.`
