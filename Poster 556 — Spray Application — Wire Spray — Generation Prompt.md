## Phase 4 -- Application Technique + Thickness Table (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section label: `SPRAY APPLICATION TECHNIQUE`.

**6-Step Technique (Y: 3.8"-10.5"):** Six cards in two rows of three. Each card: W: 7.33", H: 3.0", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6.

| Step | Title | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PREHEAT | Flame gun (no wire) to 80-120 degC. Drives off moisture; improves adhesion. Verify with temp crayon or IR gun. |
| 2 | FIRST PASS | 150-250 mm standoff, 75-90 deg angle. Consistent traverse speed. Cover entire surface one direction. |
| 3 | CROSSING PASS | Second pass at 60-90 deg to first. Builds uniform thickness and fills gaps. |
| 4 | BUILD THICKNESS | Continue crossing passes to target. Check DFT between pass sets. |
| 5 | EDGE & CORNER WORK | Extra attention to edges, corners, welds, bolts. Reduce standoff slightly. First areas to fail. |
| 6 | FINAL DFT CHECK | Verify thickness at multiple points per SSPC-PA 2. Record readings. Flag thin areas. |

Steps 1-2: accent `#E8A020`. Steps 3-4: accent `#27AE60`. Steps 5-6: accent `#2EC4B6`.

## Phase 5 -- Coating Properties + Cathodic Protection

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `WIRE SPRAY COATING PROPERTIES`.

**Left -- Zinc Coating Properties (W: 11.0"):**

| Property | Typical Range |
|---|---|
| Porosity | 5-15% |
| Oxide content | 5-15% |
| Bond strength (ASTM C633) | 7-25 MPa |
| Surface roughness (Ra) | 10-25 um |
| Hardness | 40-60 HV |
| Density | 85-95% of wire density |
| Protection mechanism | Cathodic (sacrificial) |

Note: `Porosity is sealed by the seal coat. Interconnected porosity allows sealer to penetrate and lock the system together.`

**Right -- How Cathodic Protection Works (W: 11.5"):** accent `#27AE60`.

Zinc and aluminum are MORE ANODIC than steel in the galvanic series. When moisture bridges the coating and steel, zinc/aluminum corrodes INSTEAD of the steel. Sacrificial protection works at holidays, scratches, and cut edges. White rust (zinc) or white powder (aluminum) = protection working. Expected service life: 20-40+ years depending on thickness and environment.

## Phase 6 -- Wire Material Comparison + Common Defects

### Zinc vs. Aluminum vs. Zn-Al 85/15 (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `ZINC vs. ALUMINUM vs. ZINC-ALUMINUM`.

Three-column comparison. Zn-Al 85/15 column has `RECOMMENDED ALL-AROUND` badge in `#27AE60`.

| Property | Zinc (`#2EC4B6`) | Aluminum (`#E8A020`) | Zn-Al 85/15 (`#27AE60`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Melting point | 420 degC | 660 degC | ~450 degC |
| Protection type | Cathodic (sacrificial) | Cathodic + barrier | Cathodic + barrier |
| Best for | Atmospheric; mild to moderate | Marine; high-temp; immersion | Best all-around |
| Max service temp | ~250 degC | ~500 degC | ~300 degC |
| Sealer adhesion | Good | Good | Excellent |
| Relative cost | Lowest | Moderate | Moderate |

Bottom: `Zn-Al 85/15 is increasingly specified as the default choice -- combines cathodic protection of zinc with barrier protection and sealer adhesion of aluminum.`

### Application Defects (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `APPLICATION DEFECTS -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| THIN SPOTS | Inconsistent traverse speed | DFT check between passes; mark and touch up |
| POOR ADHESION | Contaminated surface; exceeded time window | Re-blast and re-spray; respect time windows |
| BLISTERING | Moisture on substrate; insufficient preheat | Preheat to 80-120 degC; verify dew point |
| EDGE/CORNER FAILURES | Insufficient coverage at edges | Extra passes; reduce standoff; round sharp edges |
