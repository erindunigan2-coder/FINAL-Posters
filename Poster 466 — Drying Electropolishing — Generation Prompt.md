## Phase 4 -- Drying Methods (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5".

Section label: `DRYING METHODS`

**Three drying method panels side by side:**

| Method | Accent | Subtitle | Parameters | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HOT AIR BLOW-OFF | `#2EC4B6` | Most Common Industrial | Filtered compressed air, 60--100 C, 5 um filter (0.3 um pharma), oil-free MANDATORY, 30--60 psi | Fast, inexpensive, widely available | Recontamination risk from dirty air; difficult for recesses |
| NITROGEN (N2) BLOW-OFF | `#27AE60` | Pharma / Semiconductor Standard | Clean dry N2, ambient--60 C, 99.99% purity, < 10 ppm dew point, 0.1--0.3 um filter | Zero contamination, no oxidation, spec-required | Higher cost; N2 asphyxiation hazard |
| OVEN / VACUUM DRY | `#E8A020` | Complex Geometries & Recesses | Oven 80--120 C, 15--60 min, clean air or N2, vacuum 50--100 mbar at 40--80 C | Reaches blind holes, uniform drying, vacuum reduces temp needed | Slower, requires chamber, hot part handling |

**DI Water Prerequisite callout (Emerald, prominent):**
`PREREQUISITE: DI WATER FINAL RINSE BEFORE DRYING`
`City water contains dissolved minerals (Ca, Mg, silica) that precipitate as white spots when water evaporates. The ONLY way to prevent mineral water spots is DI water (> 1 MOhm-cm) as final rinse. No DI rinse = guaranteed water spots on a mirror surface.`

## Phase 5 -- Handling + Contamination Control

Y: 14.5" to 22.0".

Section label: `CLEAN HANDLING PRACTICES`

**DO / DO NOT two-column panel (Amber accent):**

| DO (Emerald) | DO NOT (Coral) |
|---|---|
| Wear clean lint-free gloves (nitrile/nylon) | Touch with bare hands (fingerprints = acid etch) |
| Handle by edges or non-critical surfaces | Set on dirty, rough, or metallic surfaces |
| Use clean padded fixtures for transport | Stack parts without separators |
| Package immediately after drying | Expose to shop air longer than necessary |
| Store clean, dry, low-humidity | Use shop rags or contaminated cloths |
| Use PE film or VCI paper | Allow condensation (cold to warm) |

**Contamination sources table:**

| Source | Contaminant | Effect | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bare hands | Skin oils, NaCl | Fingerprints; salt pitting | Lint-free gloves |
| Compressed air | Oil mist, moisture, particles | Hazy film; particles | Oil-free compressor; filters |
| Shop environment | Dust, grinding swarf | Embedded particles; scratches | Package immediately |
| Packaging material | Sulfur (rubber), PVC offgas | Tarnishing, discoloration | PE film or VCI paper; avoid PVC |

## Phase 6 -- Drying Defects + Packaging

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Drying defects (2x2 grid):**

| Defect | Accent | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| WATER SPOTS | `#E05C5C` | Mineral deposits from non-DI water | DI final rinse; never skip |
| FLOW MARKS | `#E8A020` | Uneven air flow; slow drainage | Tilt parts; uniform air flow |
| HAZE / FILM | `#E8A020` | Oil from compressed air; residual chemicals | Oil-free air; verify rinse quality |
| FINGERPRINTS | `#E05C5C` | Bare-hand contact | Gloves mandatory; re-process if contaminated |

**Packaging cards:**

| RECOMMENDED (Teal) | AVOID (Coral) |
|---|---|
| PE film or bags | PVC film (HCl offgassing) |
| VCI paper | Rubber (sulfur attack) |
| Foam separators | Newspaper/printed cardboard (ink) |
| Sealed plastic containers | Bare wood (tannic acid stain) |
| Custom crates with padded inserts | Metal-on-metal contact |
