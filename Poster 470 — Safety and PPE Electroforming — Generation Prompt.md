## Phase 5 -- Hazard Matrix + Cyanide Warning

Y: 15.5" to 28.5".

**Hazard Table (Y: 15.5" to 22.0"):** Section label: `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION`.

| Hazard | Source | Severity | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nickel compounds (inhalation) | Ni sulfamate/sulfate mist; IARC Group 1 | SEVERE | LEV, respiratory; PEL 0.1 mg/m3 |
| Nickel sensitization (skin) | Nickel salts -- allergic contact dermatitis | HIGH | Barrier cream + nitrile + gauntlet |
| Cyanide (Ag, Au baths) | NaCN/KCN; HCN gas if acidified | LETHAL | Supplied air; antidote kit; NEVER mix with acid |
| Acid mist | H2SO4, HCl from bath additions | HIGH | LEV; acid gas respirator |
| NaOH (mandrel dissolution) | 10-20% NaOH at 60-80 C | HIGH | Full PPE; face shield; ventilate |
| Electrical | DC rectifier: 3-12 V at 100-10,000+ A | HIGH | LOTO; GFCI; insulated bus bars |
| Hydrogen gas | Evolved at cathode | HIGH | Ventilate; LEL monitor; no ignition sources |

Severity color: LETHAL/SEVERE = `#E05C5C`, HIGH = `#E8A020`.

**Cyanide Warning Panel (Y: 22.0" to 25.5"):** Full-width Coral-bordered panel.

```
NEVER mix cyanide baths with ANY acid -- HCN gas. LETHAL at 300 ppm.
NEVER allow pH < 10 -- HCN liberation begins.
ALWAYS have antidote kit: amyl nitrite, sodium nitrite, sodium thiosulfate.
OSHA PEL: 5 mg/m3 as CN.
If HCN suspected: EVACUATE. SCBA to re-enter.
```

**Emergency Procedures (Y: 26.0" to 28.3"):** Two side-by-side cards.

Left -- `CHEMICAL FIRST AID` (`#E05C5C`): Eyes flush 15+ min; skin flush 15+ min; ingestion no vomiting; NaOH flush with water; cyanide: amyl nitrite + 911.

Right -- `SPILL RESPONSE` (`#E8A020`): Acid: soda ash/lime. NaOH: citric acid. Cyanide: SCBA + NaOCl at pH > 10. Nickel solution: dike, absorb, hazmat disposal.
