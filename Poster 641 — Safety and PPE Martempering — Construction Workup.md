# Poster #641 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Martempering

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Martempering shares most of austempering's salt bath hazards -- molten nitrate/nitrite salt, moisture explosion risk, organic contamination fire hazard. But martempering adds a wrinkle: some operations use hot oil (marquench oil at 250-400 F) instead of salt, which introduces elevated-temperature oil fire risk. This poster covers both media and their distinct hazard profiles.

### ZONE 4 -- Hazard Matrix

**Section label:** `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION -- MARTEMPERING OPERATIONS` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- 7-Row Hazard Table**

Y: 15.3" to 21.3". Column widths (23.0" total):
- Hazard (4.0") | Source (5.5") | Severity (3.0") | Mitigation (10.5")

Header row: fill `#3A4055`, H: 0.5". Barlow SemiBold, 14 pt, `#F0EDE8`.

| Hazard | Source | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Molten salt burns | Salt bath 350--600 F; splash on immersion | SEVERE | Full PPE; controlled immersion; pre-heat parts |
| Moisture explosion | Water contacts molten salt | CRITICAL | Pre-heat ALL parts and fixtures to 250+ F; strict moisture protocol |
| Nitrate/nitrite fire | Organic contamination in salt (oil, grease) | CRITICAL | Zero organics in salt bath; dedicated degreased fixturing |
| Hot oil fire | Marquench oil at 250--400 F; close to flash point | SEVERE | Monitor flash point; fire suppression on tank; ventilation hood |
| Hot oil water eruption | Water contamination in marquench oil | CRITICAL | Test oil for water (< 0.05%); pre-dry all parts |
| Transfer burns | Parts at 1500+ F moved through open air | SEVERE | Enclosed transfer when possible; clear work zone |
| CO / H2 (if endo atm.) | Endothermic gas in austenitizing furnace | CRITICAL | CO monitors; burn-off pilots; N2 purge protocol |

Severity color-coded: CRITICAL = `#E05C5C`, SEVERE = `#E8A020`.

Data: Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`. Hazard names: Barlow SemiBold 13 pt.
