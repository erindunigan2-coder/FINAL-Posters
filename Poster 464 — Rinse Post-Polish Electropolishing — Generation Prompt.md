## Phase 4 -- Post-Polish Rinse System (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5".

Section label: `POST-POLISH RINSE SYSTEM`

**Three-element rinse path diagram:**
- EP Tank (left, Emerald border): `H3PO4/H2SO4 at 65--80 C`
- Arrow: `DRIP 10--15 sec`
- Drag-Out Recovery (center, Amber border): `Still water | Captures concentrated acid | Return to EP tank when SG rises | Reduces chemical cost 20--40%`
- Arrow: `IMMERSE + AGITATE`
- Clean Rinse (right, Teal border): `Flowing DI or city water | Cascade overflow to drain | Conductivity target: < 50 uS/cm`

**Timing callout (Coral, prominent):**
`TOTAL TIME FROM EP TANK TO CLEAN RINSE: < 30 SECONDS. No air-drying. No delay. No exceptions.`

**Why immediate rinse is non-negotiable (Coral accent panel):**

```
1. ACID CONCENTRATION: Drag-out film is full-strength. As water evaporates, concentration INCREASES.
2. CONTINUED DISSOLUTION: Uncontrolled etching outside polishing plateau -- orange peel and matte spots.
3. STAINING: Dissolved metal salts (Fe, Cr, Ni phosphates) precipitate as acid dries -- permanent stains.
4. STREAKING: Gravity causes acid to run and pool -- visible flow lines on polished surface.
```

Note: `Even 60 seconds of air exposure at 65 C can cause visible staining on 316L stainless steel.`

Y: 14.5" to 22.0".

Section label: `RINSE PARAMETERS + WATER QUALITY`

| Parameter | Drag-Out Recovery | Clean Rinse |
|---|---|---|
| Water type | DI or city water | DI (pharma); city (industrial) |
| Temperature | Ambient | Ambient |
| Flow | Static (no overflow) | Flowing cascade or overflow |
| Time | 10--30 sec immersion | 30--60 sec immersion |
| Agitation | Part agitation (dip/withdraw) | Part agitation recommended |
| Conductivity target | N/A (will be high) | < 50 uS/cm |
| pH target | N/A | 6--8 (neutral) |
| Purpose | Recover concentrated acid | Remove all residual acid |

**Water quality cards:**

| Application | Requirement |
|---|---|
| INDUSTRIAL | City water OK, conductivity < 200 uS/cm, visual check |
| PHARMA / BIOTECH / SEMI | DI water > 1 MOhm-cm, ASME BPE, 2--3 stage cascade, SEMI F19 |

## Phase 6 -- Defect Gallery + Economics

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Defect gallery (2x2 grid):**

| Defect | Accent | Description | Root Cause | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACID STAINING | `#E05C5C` | Brown/yellow discoloration at bottom of part | Acid dried on surface; metal phosphates precipitated | Immediate rinse; no air-drying |
| FLOW STREAKS | `#E05C5C` | Visible vertical lines | Gravity drain of concentrated acid before rinse | Immerse quickly; do not let acid run |
| WATER SPOTS | `#E8A020` | Circular marks from water droplets | Non-DI water for final rinse; slow drying | Use DI water; immediate blow-dry |
| UNEVEN FINISH | `#E8A020` | Matte patches amid bright areas | Contaminated rinse water; insufficient rinsing | Monitor conductivity; cascade system |

**Economics cards:**

| Topic | Key Points |
|---|---|
| ACID RECOVERY | Drag-out recovery captures 60--80% of carried-out acid. Return at SG ~1.4. Reduces cost 20--40% annually. |
| WASTEWATER REDUCTION | Less acid in rinse = lower treatment cost. H3PO4 discharge requires phosphorus treatment. Drag-out recovery is the single most effective waste-reduction step. |
