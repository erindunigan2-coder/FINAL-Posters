# Poster #470 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Electroforming

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Electroforming safety is electroplating safety amplified by time and scale. The same nickel sulfamate bath used for a 10-minute nickel plate runs for days or weeks in electroforming -- chronic exposure risk is the defining concern. Nickel compounds are IARC Group 1 carcinogens via inhalation and potent contact sensitizers. Silver and gold electroforming baths may use cyanide. Mandrel dissolution with NaOH adds strong alkali handling. This poster hangs at the EF tank entrance.

### ZONE 3 -- Hazard Matrix

**Section label:** `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION` -- Y: 15.7".

**BLOCK D -- 7-Row Hazard Table**

Y: 16.3" to 21.8". Columns: Hazard (4.0") | Source (5.5") | Severity (3.0") | Control (10.5")

| Hazard | Source | Severity | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nickel compounds (inhalation) | Ni sulfamate/sulfate mist; IARC Group 1 carcinogen | SEVERE | LEV, respiratory protection; PEL 0.1 mg/m3 (soluble Ni) |
| Nickel sensitization (skin) | Nickel salts in solution -- allergic contact dermatitis | HIGH | Barrier cream + nitrile + gauntlet; hygiene practices |
| Cyanide (Ag, Au baths) | NaCN or KCN in solution; HCN gas if acidified | LETHAL | Supplied air; antidote kit; NEVER mix with acid |
| Acid mist | H2SO4, HCl from bath additions and electrolyte | HIGH | LEV at tank lip; acid gas respirator backup |
| NaOH (mandrel dissolution) | 10--20% NaOH at 60--80 C for Al mandrel dissolve | HIGH | Full PPE; face shield; slow addition; ventilate |
| Electrical | DC rectifier: 3--12 V at 100--10,000+ A | HIGH | LOTO; GFCI; insulated bus bars; dry hands |
| Hydrogen gas | Evolved at cathode during deposition | HIGH | Ventilate above tank; LEL monitor; no ignition sources |

Severity color-coding: LETHAL/SEVERE = `#E05C5C`, HIGH = `#E8A020`, MODERATE = `#2EC4B6`.

### ZONE 4 -- Cyanide Warning + Emergency Response

**Section label:** `CYANIDE BATH WARNING + EMERGENCY RESPONSE` -- Y: 22.2".

**BLOCK E -- Cyanide Warning Panel (Y: 22.9" to 25.5")**

Rounded rect, X: 0.5", W: 23.0", H: 2.4", fill `#E05C5C` at 15%, border 2 pt `#E05C5C`.

Title: `CYANIDE BATH OPERATIONS -- SILVER AND GOLD ELECTROFORMING` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E05C5C`

Content (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 155%):

```
Silver and some gold electroforming baths contain sodium or potassium cyanide (NaCN / KCN).

NEVER mix cyanide baths with ANY acid -- generates hydrogen cyanide (HCN) gas. LETHAL at 300 ppm.
NEVER allow cyanide solution pH to drop below 10 -- HCN liberation begins at pH < 10.
ALWAYS have cyanide antidote kit immediately accessible: amyl nitrite inhalants, sodium nitrite injection, sodium thiosulfate injection.
OSHA PEL for cyanide: 5 mg/m3 as CN.
If HCN gas is suspected (bitter almond odor -- but 40% of people cannot detect it): EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY. Do not re-enter without SCBA.
```

**BLOCK F -- Emergency Procedures (Y: 26.0" to 28.3")**

Two side-by-side cards:

**Left -- Chemical Exposure (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 2.1", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`
- Title: `CHEMICAL FIRST AID` Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#E05C5C`
- Body (Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`):
```
EYES: Flush 15+ min with water. Remove contacts.
SKIN: Remove clothing. Flush 15+ min. Barrier cream is prevention only -- not treatment.
INGESTION: Do NOT induce vomiting. Rinse mouth. Emergency medical.
NaOH BURN: Flush with water (NOT neutralizing acid). Seek medical attention.
CYANIDE: Administer amyl nitrite inhalant. Call 911 IMMEDIATELY.
```

**Right -- Spill Response (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Rounded rect, H: 2.1", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `SPILL RESPONSE` Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#E8A020`
- Body (Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`):
```
ACID SPILL: Dike, neutralize with soda ash or lime, collect, dispose.
NaOH SPILL: Dike, neutralize with citric acid or CO2, collect, dispose.
CYANIDE SPILL: Evacuate area. Full SCBA. Oxidize with sodium hypochlorite
  (NaOCl) at pH > 10 BEFORE neutralizing. Never acidify cyanide waste.
NICKEL SOLUTION: Dike, absorb, collect for proper disposal as hazardous waste.
```

## Design Notes

This poster is heavier on the SEVERE/LETHAL end of the hazard spectrum than most plating safety posters. The nickel carcinogen risk is the chronic background threat, but the cyanide warning (Block E) is the acute emergency anchor. The cyanide antidote kit requirement must be impossible to miss. The nickel exposure monitoring section (Zone 5) targets the plant EHS manager -- this is their audit reference.
