## Phase 4 -- Three Pillars (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `THE THREE PILLARS OF PART PREPARATION`.

Three side-by-side boxes, each H: 8.8", fill `#1E2435`, left accent 0.06".

**Cleaning (X: 0.5", W: 7.33", accent `#2EC4B6`):** Remove Everything That Blocks Carbon. Required: oil-free, grease-free, scale-free, rust-free. Methods: alkaline wash (140--160 F, 5--10 min), solvent degreasing, wire brush for heavy scale. Why: oil/grease = carbon barrier = SOFT SPOTS. Water-break test: water should sheet uniformly; beading = contamination.

**Masking & Stop-Off (X: 8.17", W: 7.33", accent `#E8A020`):** Protect Areas That Must NOT Harden. Cu electroplate: 0.001--0.002" min thickness, cyanide strike + acid plate, per AMS 2759/7. Stop-off paint: less reliable for deep cases, suitable for <0.030" ECD. Areas typically masked: threads, bearing seats, weld preps, areas requiring subsequent machining.

**Surface Condition (X: 15.83", W: 7.67", accent `#27AE60`):** The Starting Point Determines the Finish. Acceptable: machined, forged (scale removed), stamped (clean). Not acceptable: heavy mill scale, rust, decarburized layer, EDM recast layer. Finer finish = more uniform carburizing.

## Phase 5 -- Copper Stop-Off Detail

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `COPPER ELECTROPLATE STOP-OFF -- THE HEAT TREATER'S BRIDGE TO PLATING`.

**Left -- Process Steps (W: 11.0", accent `#E8A020`):** 7 numbered steps: Clean -> Activate -> Cu Strike (0.0002--0.0005" flash) -> Cu Plate (0.001--0.002" min) -> Rinse & Dry -> Inspect (XRF or micrometer) -> Mask Verification.

**Right -- Why Copper Works (W: 11.5", accent `#27AE60`):** Carbon solubility in copper: ~0%. Cu acts as absolute barrier. 0.001" = standard; 0.002" = deep case. After carburizing: strip with ammonium persulfate, dilute nitric, or cyanide strip.

**Callout bar:** Amber tint. `Copper stop-off is where electroplating meets heat treatment. A plater's precision protects a heat treater's case.`

## Phase 6 -- Good vs. Bad + Checklist

**Good vs. Bad (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `PREP RIGHT VS. PREP WRONG`.

*Good (Emerald accent):* Uniform carbon profile, clean masked areas, no soft spots, predictable distortion, first-pass acceptance.

*Bad (Coral accent):* Soft spots (oil residue), carburized threads (missing Cu), non-uniform case (scale/rust), cracking, rework or scrap.

**Pre-Load Checklist (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `PRE-LOAD INSPECTION CHECKLIST`. Eight checkbox items in two columns. Checkboxes bordered `#2EC4B6`.

Left: Parts clean, water-break test passed, masked areas verified, no heavy scale.
Right: Surface condition per drawing, stop-off paint intact, parts identified/lot tracked, paperwork complete.
