## Phase 4 -- Hazard Matrix (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `HAZARD IDENTIFICATION -- 7 KEY RISKS`.

7-row table. Columns: Hazard (4.0") | Severity (1.5") | Details (8.5") | Controls (9.0").

| Hazard | Severity | Details | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noise | MODERATE | 85-105 dB (lower than plasma/HVOF) | Hearing protection above 85 dB; plugs sufficient |
| Flame / Thermal | HIGH | Open oxy-acetylene flame; hot spatter 1-3 m | Face shield, leather gloves, leather apron, safety glasses w/ side shields |
| Zinc Fume Fever | HIGH | ZnO fumes from zinc wire; flu-like 4-12 hr post-exposure | Mandatory P100 RPE; local exhaust ventilation; TLV = 2 mg/m3 |
| Metal Fumes | MODERATE | All metals produce fumes; stainless releases Cr and Ni | Fume extraction; RPE rated for specific metals |
| Compressed Gas | HIGH | Acetylene unstable >15 PSI; propane flammable | Flash-back arrestors; proper storage; leak checks soapy water |
| Fire | HIGH | Hot spatter and overspray ignite combustibles | Fire-resistant surroundings; extinguisher on hand; fire watch |
| UV Radiation | LOW | Moderate UV (less than plasma or HVOF) | Safety glasses with shade 3-5 lens |

Severity badges: HIGH = `#E05C5C` at 40%; MODERATE = `#E8A020` at 30%; LOW = `#2EC4B6` at 20%. Header fill `#3A4055`.

Center: simplified body silhouette (rectangle stack). Callout labels:

| Body Zone | PPE Item | Detail | Color |

**Right -- ZINC FUME FEVER callout (W: 11.5", accent `#E05C5C`):**

## Phase 6 -- Emergency Procedures

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE`. Four cards, accent `#E05C5C`.

| Emergency | Action |
|---|---|
| FIRE | Shut off gas; dry chemical or CO2 extinguisher; evacuate if uncontrolled |
| GAS LEAK | Shut off at cylinder; ventilate area; no ignition sources; evacuate |
| BURN INJURY | Cool with running water 10+ min; do not remove adhered clothing; seek medical |
| FUME EXPOSURE | Move to fresh air; remove contaminated clothing; seek medical if symptomatic |
