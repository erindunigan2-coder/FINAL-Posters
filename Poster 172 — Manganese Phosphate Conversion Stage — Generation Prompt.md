## Phase 5 -- Bath Chemistry + FA Ratio

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY -- THE THREE ESSENTIALS`.

Three component boxes: Manganese (8--15 g/L, coating cation, optional Ni 0--2 g/L) | Phosphate (20--50 g/L, film-forming anion, FA/TA ratio is KEY) | Accelerator (NO3- 5--20 g/L, oxidizes Fe2+).

## Phase 6 -- Coating Weight + Sludge

Y: 20.5" to 26.0". Two-column.

**Left -- Coating Weight:**

| Weight (mg/ft2) | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| < 500 | Too light |
| 500--1000 | Light -- acceptable for some |
| 1000--2000 | OPTIMAL (military spec) |
| 2000--2500 | Heavy -- approaching max |
| > 2500 | Too heavy -- powdery |

Thickness: 5--25 um | Test: ASTM B767.

**Right -- Sludge Management:** Amber accent. Sludge is normal (Fe3(PO4)2 and Mn3(PO4)2). Excessive if overheated, high iron, excess acid. Continuous filtration standard. Settle-and-decant weekly. Warning: `Sludge on parts = rough, non-uniform coating. Filter continuously.`

---

## Phase 7 -- Defects + Rules

Y: 26.0" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- 5 Defects (3+2 grid):** Light/thin, red/brown powder, non-uniform, excessive sludge, poor oil retention.

**Right -- 7 Rules:**
1. 190--210 F. ALWAYS.
2. Below 185 F, coating stops.
3. Do not boil -- excessive sludge.
4. Immersion only -- never spray.
5. 10--30 min -- longer than zinc phosphate.
6. FA/TA ratio 1:7 to 1:12.
7. Filter continuously.
