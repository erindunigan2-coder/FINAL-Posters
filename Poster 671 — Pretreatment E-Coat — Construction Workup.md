# Poster #671 -- Construction Workup
## Pretreatment -- E-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 9. Zinc phosphate is the adhesion foundation under every automotive e-coat primer. The crystalline phosphate matrix -- hopeite and phosphophyllite -- grips the e-coat film and provides the under-film corrosion barrier that makes 10-year perforation warranties possible. Surface conditioning with colloidal TiPO4 refines crystal size. This is a 10-stage chemical gauntlet and every stage matters.

**Right -- Surface Conditioning (X: 12.0", W: 11.5", H: 5.3"):**

- Title: `SURFACE CONDITIONING -- THE SECRET STAGE` Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E8A020`

Content:
```
What: Colloidal titanium phosphate (TiPO4)
      seed crystals applied before zinc
      phosphate stage.

Why:  Provides nucleation sites for zinc
      phosphate crystals. Without it, crystals
      grow large, coarse, and porous.

With conditioning:
  Fine, dense crystals -- maximum adhesion

Without conditioning:
  Coarse, porous crystals -- poor adhesion,
  reduced corrosion resistance

Concentration: 0.5--2.0 g/L
Temperature: Ambient to 100 F
Time: 30--60 sec immersion
```

Bottom highlight:
- `This single stage transforms phosphate quality. Skip it and the entire coating system suffers.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E8A020`

## Design Notes

This is the most chemistry-dense poster in the E-Coating cluster. The 10-stage line diagram is the hero -- it communicates the sheer complexity of automotive pretreatment. Surface conditioning is the hidden stage that most people outside automotive don't know about, and it deserves prominent callout treatment. The crystal composition section bridges chemistry and practical performance in a way that makes the "why" of zinc phosphate tangible. Sludge management is the unglamorous reality that separates zinc phosphate from simpler pretreatments.
