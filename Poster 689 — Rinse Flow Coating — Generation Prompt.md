## Phase 5 -- Rinse Quality Table

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `RINSE QUALITY PARAMETERS`.

| Rinse Stage | Water Source | Conductivity | Temp | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Post-Clean | City water | < 500 uS/cm | Ambient | Remove bulk cleaner |
| Post-Clean (if pretreatment follows) | DI preferred | < 200 uS/cm | Ambient | Prevent cleaner drag-in |
| Final Rinse | DI water | < 50 uS/cm | Ambient | Eliminate salt deposits |
| Counterflow Design | Fresh water enters last | Per stage | Ambient | Conserve water |

Footnote `#E8A020`: `Conductivity over target? Dump and recharge -- do not dilute.`

---

## Phase 6 -- Drying Methods Comparison

Y: 21.3" to 26.3". Section label: `DRYING METHODS -- GET IT BONE DRY`. Three columns.

**Forced Air (`#2EC4B6`):** Compressed air nozzles. Ambient. Until no visible water. Oil-free air (D4285 blotter test).

**Oven Dry (`#E8A020`):** Convection or IR. 200-250 F. 10-15 min at metal temp. Part must cool before coating.

**Air Knife (`#27AE60`):** High-velocity laminar curtain. Ambient or 120-160 F. Continuous. Combine with forced air for complex parts.

## Phase 6B -- Failures + Insight

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 4 cards + insight.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| BLISTERING IN RECESSES | Trapped moisture vaporizing | Forced air + oven; tilt parts |
| SALT DEPOSITS / WHITE SPOTS | High-TDS rinse water | DI final < 50 uS/cm |
| OSMOTIC BLISTERING (FIELD) | Soluble salts under coating | Monitor conductivity; counterflow |
| CLEANER RESIDUE | Insufficient rinse | Add rinse stage; increase spray pressure |

Insight `#E8A020`: `Most dangerous moisture is invisible. Absorbed moisture in porous substrates or blind holes only reveals itself as blisters after curing. When in doubt, oven dry.`
