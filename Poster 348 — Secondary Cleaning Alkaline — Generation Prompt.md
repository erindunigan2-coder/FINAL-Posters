## Phase 5 -- Two-Stage Cleaning

Y: 13.5" to 19.5". Section label: `TWO-STAGE CLEANING -- HEAVY SOIL PROTOCOL`.

Two glass cards side by side.

**Left -- Stage 1: Heavy-Duty** (amber-accented):
- Title: `STAGE 1: HEAVY-DUTY SOAK` / Subtitle: `The Workhorse`
- Params: `Higher concentration` / `170-195 F (75-90 C)` / `5-10 min`
- `Handles heavy buffing compound, drawing compounds, carbonized oils`
- `This bath gets dirty fast -- dump more frequently`
- `May include mechanical agitation or ultrasonics`

**Right -- Stage 2: Maintenance** (teal-accented):
- Title: `STAGE 2: MAINTENANCE SOAK` / Subtitle: `The Finisher`
- Params: `Lower concentration` / `150-170 F (65-75 C)` / `3-5 min`
- `Bath stays clean much longer`
- `Only sees residual soil the heavy-duty missed`
- `Dump cycle 3-5x longer than Stage 1`
- `This is the bath that matters for water break pass`

## Phase 6 -- Aluminum Desmut + Spray Pre-Clean

Y: 19.5" to 25.0". Two glass cards.

**Left -- Aluminum Desmut** (coral-tinted glass):
- Title: `ALUMINUM DESMUT -- NOT OPTIONAL`
- After alkaline cleaning of aluminum, dilute acid dip REQUIRED to remove:
  - Silicate residue from the cleaner
  - Light etch smut (aluminum oxide + alloying elements)
- Typical: 1-5% HNO3 (nitric acid) or proprietary desmut
- The cleaner leaves silicate behind intentionally (protecting aluminum from excessive etch)
- Highlight: `Every aluminum plating line should have a desmut tank after the alkaline clean` (`#E8A020`)

**Right -- Spray Pre-Clean** (teal-accented):
- Title: `SPRAY PRE-CLEAN` / Subtitle: `Automatic Lines Only`
- Used BEFORE soak clean to remove gross contamination
- Requirements: Low-foam surfactant (HLB 8-12) / 15-30 psi / 130-170 F (55-75 C)
- Reduces soil load on soak cleaner, extends bath life significantly
- Warning: `High-foam surfactant in a spray system = overflow disaster` (`#E05C5C`)

## Phase 7 -- The Electroclean Handoff

Y: 25.0" to 32.5". Section label: `THE SOAK-TO-ELECTROCLEAN HANDOFF`.

Full-width glass card with teal top accent.

**Left column -- Why the Combination?**
- Soak cleaning removes BULK soil: oils, greases, fingerprints, compounds
- Electrocleaning removes TRACE contamination: monolayer films, smut, embedded particles
- The combination is industry standard for critical plating: decorative Ni/Cr, gold, silver, any deposit where adhesion is paramount

**Right column -- The Golden Rule** (amber-tinted glass inset):
- `Soak clean FIRST, then electroclean.`
- `Never use electrocleaning as the primary cleaning step.`
- `Electrocleaning is a finishing step, not a workhorse. Overloading it with gross soil contaminates the bath and defeats its purpose.`
