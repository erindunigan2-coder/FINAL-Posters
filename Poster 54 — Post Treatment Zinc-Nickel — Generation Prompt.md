### Step 6 -- Trivalent Passivation Parameters (Y: 9.0" to 13.3")

Sublabel: `TRIVALENT PASSIVATION -- THE STANDARD FOR Zn-Ni` `#E8A020`.

| Passivation Type | White Rust SST | Red Rust SST | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trivalent clear | 120--200 hr | 500--1,000 hr | Automotive standard; transparent to iridescent |
| Trivalent iridescent | 200--400 hr | 720--1,500 hr | Higher protection; slight yellow-green |
| Trivalent black | 96--200 hr | 500--1,000 hr | Appearance applications; lower corrosion |
| Trivalent + topcoat sealer | 400--1,000 hr | 1,000--2,000+ hr | Premium automotive / aerospace |

Common parameters (accent `#E8A020`):
- pH: 1.5--3.0 (trivalent Cr3+ solution)
- Temperature: 70--110 F (21--43 C)
- Immersion time: 30--90 sec
- Agitation: Mild -- do not damage film during dip
- Rinse after: Gentle -- film is soft until cured

Note: `Hexavalent chromates are almost universally PROHIBITED for Zn-Ni in automotive and aerospace. This poster covers trivalent only.` `#E05C5C`

## Phase 5 -- Sealer + Cure

Y: 13.5" to 20.0". Section: `TOPCOAT SEALER AND CURE`.

**Left -- Sealer (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Accent `#2EC4B6`. Title: `TOPCOAT SEALER`.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Proprietary organic or inorganic (per TDS) |
| Application | Dip, spray, or flood |
| Temperature | 150--180 F (66--82 C) for dip |
| Immersion time | 30--60 sec |
| Purpose | Seals micro-porosity; adds organic barrier layer |
| Performance boost | Doubles or triples SST hours vs. passivation alone |
| Note | Sealer almost always REQUIRED per OEM specs |

**Right -- Cure/Dry (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

**Top -- Hot Air Dry (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
- Temp: 150--170 F (66--77 C). Time: 15--20 min.
- Purpose: Cure passivation + sealer; drive off water.
- CRITICAL: Do not exceed 250 F -- passivation degrades.

**Bottom -- Oven Cure (accent `#E8A020`):**
- Some sealers require dedicated oven cure.
- Temp and time per TDS (typically 150--200 F, 10--30 min).
- Verify: finger-touch dry =/= fully cured.

## Phase 6 -- HE Bake + Common Failures

Y: 20.0" to 27.0".

**Left -- HE Bake Requirements (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**
Section: `HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT BAKE` `#E05C5C`.
Full panel, border 2pt `#E05C5C`.
Title: `BAKE BEFORE PASSIVATION` `#E05C5C`.
- Zn-Ni predominantly applied to high-strength steel
- HE bake is the DEFAULT assumption
- Bake BEFORE passivation -- heat degrades conversion coatings
- REQUIREMENTS:
  - Temperature: 375 +/- 25 F (190 +/- 14 C)
  - Aerospace: within 1 HOUR of plating (AMS 2759/9)
  - Automotive / general: within 4 HOURS (ASTM B850)
  - Hold: 8--24 hr (aerospace typically 23 hr min at >= 39 HRC)
  - After bake: proceed to passivation within a few hours
- Specs: `ASTM B850 | AMS 2759/9 | ASTM B841` `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Common Failures (X: 15.5", W: 8.0"):**
Title: `WHAT GOES WRONG`.

| Failure | Cause |
|---|---|
| Passivate flaking | NaOH drag-in; pH out of range; overexposure |
| Poor salt spray | Alloy out of spec; passivate exhausted; no sealer |
| Sealer haze | Over-applied; wrong cure temp |
| HE failure in service | Bake skipped or delayed; insufficient time |
| Passivate discoloration | Contaminated bath; Ni or Fe buildup |

**Right -- Safety (X: 15.5", W: 8.0"):**
Fill `#E05C5C` at 10%, border 1pt `#E05C5C`. Title: `SAFETY`.
- Trivalent passivation: Cr(III) low toxicity but acidic (pH 1.5--3.0). Acid burn hazard.
- Sealer: generally low toxicity; check SDS.
- HE bake ovens: burn hazard at 375 F. Lockout/tagout for maintenance.
- Waste treatment: Cr(III) precipitates as hydroxide at pH 8--9.
- PPE: acid-resistant gloves, goggles, apron for passivation.
