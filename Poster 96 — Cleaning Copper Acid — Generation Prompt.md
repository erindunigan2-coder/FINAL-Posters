## Phase 6 -- Substrate Pathways + Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `SUBSTRATE-DEPENDENT CLEANING SEQUENCES`.

Three pathway cards:

| Card | Accent | Substrate | Sequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#27AE60` | COPPER / BRASS / NICKEL | Soak -> EC -> Rinse -> H2SO4 dip -> Rinse -> Acid copper (DIRECT) |
| 2 | `#E8A020` | STEEL (requires strike) | Soak -> EC -> Rinse -> HCl dip -> Rinse -> CN/alk Cu STRIKE -> Rinse -> Acid copper |
| 3 | `#2EC4B6` | PCB (PRINTED CIRCUIT) | No alkaline clean -> Microetch (Na persulfate) -> Rinse -> Acid copper (DIRECT) |

Key: `Steel in acid copper without a strike = immersion deposit = peel. No exceptions.` -- `#E05C5C`

Problem table: Water break -> pitting | Silicate film -> skip plating | Poor adhesion -> oxide | Roughness -> particulate.
