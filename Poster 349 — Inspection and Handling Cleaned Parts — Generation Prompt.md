## Phase 5 -- ASTM F22 Procedure + Wipe Test

Y: 14.5" to 20.0". Two glass cards.

**Left -- ASTM F22 Water Break Test** (emerald-accented):
1. Rinse part with clean water
2. Hold vertically and observe water film
3. Look for breaks, beading, or dry spots
4. Must be performed within 30 seconds of rinse (air-dry allows recontamination)
5. PASS: uniform water sheet, no breaks
6. FAIL: any break = residual contamination

Note: `Per ASTM F22 -- Standard Test Method for Hydrophobic Surface Films by the Water-Break Test` (JetBrains Mono 11 pt at 50%)

**Right -- Wipe Test (Supplemental)** (amber-accented):
- When to use: Buffed parts / Dark substrates / Confirmation of water break
- Method: Wipe with clean white cloth / Examine for discoloration / Any color = residual soil
- Limitation: Less sensitive than water break / Does not detect monolayer contamination

## Phase 6 -- Handling Rules

Y: 20.0" to 25.0". Section label: `HANDLING AFTER CLEANING -- THE RULES`.

2x3 grid of glass cards:

| Rule | Detail | Accent |
|---|---|---|
| NO BARE HANDS | NEVER touch cleaned surfaces with bare hands. Fingerprint oils = instant re-contamination. Use clean cotton gloves or handle by edges/racks only. | `#E05C5C` |
| PROCESS QUICKLY | Move to next step within 15-30 minutes. Steel flash-rusts in air. Every minute of delay is a risk. | `#2EC4B6` |
| CLEAN GLOVES ONLY | Cotton gloves must be clean and oil-free. Dirty gloves are worse than bare hands. | `#E05C5C` |
| KEEP PARTS WET | If delay is unavoidable, keep parts submerged in clean rinse water. Air exposure is the enemy. | `#2EC4B6` |
| NO STACKING | Do not stack cleaned parts. Surface-to-surface contact traps moisture and creates corrosion. | `#E05C5C` |
| RACK INSPECTION | Racks must be clean. Contaminated rack tips transfer soil to parts at every contact point. | `#2EC4B6` |

## Phase 7 -- Flash Rust Warning

Y: 25.0" to 32.5". Section label: `THE CLOCK IS TICKING -- FLASH RUST AND OXIDATION`.

Full-width coral-tinted glass panel with three-column interior:

**Steel / Iron** (`#E05C5C`):
- Flash rust begins within minutes of air exposure
- In humid environments (>60% RH), visible rust in 5-15 minutes
- RULE: Process to acid activate or electroclean within 15-30 min
- `15-30 MIN MAX` (JetBrains Mono 16 pt)

**Zinc Die Cast** (`#E8A020`):
- Zinc oxidizes rapidly in air
- White oxide film forms quickly on freshly cleaned surfaces
- Particularly sensitive -- process immediately if possible
- `PROCESS IMMEDIATELY` (JetBrains Mono 16 pt)

**Aluminum** (`#2EC4B6`):
- Aluminum reforms native oxide film almost instantly in air
- For plating: oxide must be removed by acid activation (zincate or other) regardless of delay
- Less time-critical than steel but still process promptly
- `WITHIN 30 MIN` (JetBrains Mono 16 pt)
