## Phase 6 -- Wire vs. Powder + Troubleshooting

### Wire vs. Powder (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `WIRE VS. POWDER -- TWO FEEDSTOCK PATHS`.

**Left -- WIRE FLAME SPRAY (W: 11.0", accent `#27AE60`):**

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Feedstock | Wire 1.6-4.8 mm |
| Materials | Zn, Al, stainless, carbon steel, bronze, Monel, Mo |
| Velocity | 80-200 m/s |
| Dep rate | 2-8 kg/hr |
| Dep efficiency | 50-70% |
| Typical use | Corrosion protection (Zn/Al on steel) |

Highlight: `Wire is the workhorse -- higher feed rates, lower cost, and simpler operation`.

**Right -- POWDER FLAME SPRAY (W: 11.5", accent `#E8A020`):**

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Feedstock | Powder 40-120 microns |
| Materials | NiCrBSi, stellite, WC blends, ceramics, bronze |
| Velocity | 40-100 m/s |
| Dep rate | 1-4 kg/hr |
| Dep efficiency | 30-50% |
| Unique | NiCrBSi can be fused to near-zero porosity |

Highlight: `Powder unlocks self-fluxing alloys -- fuse after spray for metallurgical bond and <1% porosity`.

### Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| POOR ADHESION | Inadequate blast profile or contamination | Re-blast to Ra 4-12 um; verify clean surface |
| HIGH POROSITY | Standoff too far or velocity too low | Reduce standoff; check gas pressures |
| SPITTING (WIRE) | Wire feed mismatch or moisture in air | Adjust feed rate; install air dryer |
| COATING DELAMINATION | Substrate overheated or oxide at interface | Monitor temp <150 degC; spray within 4 hr of blast |
