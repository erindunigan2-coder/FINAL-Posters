Tank cross-section showing: H2SO4 electrolyte, aluminum workpiece as ANODE (+), lead-antimony cathodes (-), DC rectifier above (<5% ripple), cooling coil at bottom (`maintain 18--22 C`), oil-free air agitation.

Bath parameters:

## Phase 5 -- Temperature Effects + Thickness by Application

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `TEMPERATURE -- THE #1 VARIABLE`.

**Left -- Temperature Effects Table:**

| Bath Temperature | Coating Result | Color |
|---|---|---|
| <59 F (<15 C) | Hard, dense, brittle; poor dye -- Type III territory | `#2EC4B6` |
| 64--72 F (18--22 C) | OPTIMUM -- balanced growth/dissolution; excellent dye | `#27AE60` |
| 73--77 F (23--25 C) | Softer; increased pore size; acceptable for some applications | `#E8A020` |
| >77 F (>25 C) | POWDERING RISK -- rapid dissolution; unacceptable | `#E05C5C` |

Note: `+/- 1 C control is recommended. A few degrees changes everything.`

**Right -- Thickness Targets:**

| Application | Typical Thickness |
|---|---|
| Architectural (exterior) | 18--25 um (0.7--1.0 mil) per AA-M45 |
| Decorative (interior) | 8--15 um (0.3--0.6 mil) |
| MIL-A-8625F Class 1 min | 10 um (0.4 mil) |
| Electronics / consumer | 10--15 um (0.4--0.6 mil) |

Note: `Dimensional change: ~50% inward, ~50% outward.`

## Phase 6 -- Alloy Effects + Contamination Thresholds

Y: 20.5" to 26.5".

**Left -- Alloy Effects:**

| Alloy | Oxide Appearance | Dye Response | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6061 / 6063 | Clear, uniform | Excellent | Gold standard for Type II |
| 5052 | Slightly grayish | Good | Mg content causes tint |
| 1100 | Clear, bright | Excellent | Soft oxide |
| 2024 | Tan/brown (Cu) | Poor for bright colors | Lower efficiency |
| 7075 | Yellow-bronze | Moderate | Zinc dissolves preferentially |
| Cast (high Si) | Salt and pepper | Mottled | Si does not convert to oxide |

**Right -- Contamination Thresholds:**

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Dissolved Al | >15 g/L | Gray coatings; reduced efficiency |
| Chloride (Cl-) | >25 ppm | PITTING -- most damaging |
| Copper (Cu) | >10 ppm | Mottling, discoloration |
| Iron (Fe) | >100 ppm | Brownish discoloration |
| Rectifier ripple | >5% | Soft, powdery coatings |

## Phase 7 -- Defect Diagnosis Grid

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS`.

3x2 grid:

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| BURNING | Excessive CD, high temp, low acid | Reduce CD; check temp; analyze bath |
| POWDERING | Temp >77 F or time too long | Cool bath; reduce time |
| PITTING | Chloride >25 ppm or substrate defects | Analyze for Cl-; check rinse water |
| UNEVEN THICKNESS | Poor racking, temp gradients | Improve agitation; check rack contacts |
| GRAY COATING | High dissolved Al, high temp | Partial dump; cool bath |
| POOR DYE COLOR | Coating too thin or pores too small | Increase time; verify temp in range |
