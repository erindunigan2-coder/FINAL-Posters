## Phase 4 -- Batch vs. Continuous Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `TWO CLEANING WORLDS -- BATCH AND CONTINUOUS`.

**Left -- `BATCH SOAK CLEANING` (`#E8A020`):** Alkaline immersion, pH 10--12, 2--5%, 120--160 F, 5--15 min. 7-step process. Controls: Free alk titration / Oil loading < 5 g/L / Water-break test.

**Right -- `CONTINUOUS WIRE LINE CLEANING` (`#2EC4B6`):** In-line spray + ultrasonic, 1--3%, 130--150 F, 100--1,000+ ft/min, 1--5 sec contact. Controls: Nozzle alignment / Ultrasonic 25--40 kHz / Drawing lubricant removal.

## Phase 5 -- Cleanliness Verification

Y: 15.3" to 19.3". Section label: `HOW TO VERIFY CLEANLINESS`. Three cards:

**Water-Break (ASTM F22, `#27AE60`):** Unbroken sheet 30 sec = PASS. Beading = FAIL.
**UV Inspection (365 nm, `#E8A020`):** Fluorescent oils glow. Supplement only.
**Visual (`#2EC4B6`):** Good lighting. Not sensitive enough for trace oils.

## Phase 6 -- Chemistry + Controls

Y: 20.3" to 25.3". Two-column.

**Left -- `ALKALINE CLEANER CHEMISTRY`:** NaOH, sodium metasilicate, TSP. Surfactants. Chelating agents. pH 10--12, 2--5%.

**Right -- `CRITICAL CONTROL POINTS`:** Free Alk / Total Alk / Oil Loading < 5 g/L / Temperature / Water-Break. Note: Cleanliness less critical for 8--40 mil coats than 1--3 mil spray, but adhesion still needs clean substrate.
