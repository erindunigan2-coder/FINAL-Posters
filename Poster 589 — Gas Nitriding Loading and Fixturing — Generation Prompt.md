## Phase 6 -- Common Loading Errors

Y: 27.0"--32.5". Section label: `COMMON LOADING ERRORS`. 4 cards:

| Error | Consequence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| PARTS TOUCHING | Soft spots after 40+ hr | Min 0.5 in; hang vertically |
| OVERLOADED TRAYS | Creep; parts sag; new contacts | Reduce weight; use creep-resistant alloys |
| POOR GAS CIRCULATION | Uneven case depth | Space uniformly; verify fan |
| TC IN WRONG LOCATION | Temperature not representative | Coldest/heaviest point; multiple TCs |
