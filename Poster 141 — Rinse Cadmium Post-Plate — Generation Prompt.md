## Phase 5 -- Dual Waste Streams

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `DUAL WASTE STREAMS -- CYANIDE AND CADMIUM`.

**Left -- Cyanide Destruction (accent `#E05C5C`):** Two-stage alkaline chlorination: `CN- --> CNO-` (pH >10, ORP +350) then `CNO- --> CO2 + N2` (pH 8--9, ORP +600). CN must be destroyed BEFORE Cd precipitation. Residual CN <0.1 mg/L.

**Right -- Cadmium Precipitation (accent `#E8A020`):** After CN destruction: precipitate as Cd(OH)2 at pH 9--11 (most common) or CdS (lower solubility). Settle, filter, dewater. Sludge is RCRA hazardous waste (D006) -- manifest for disposal. EPA discharge: 0.1 mg/L monthly avg.

## Phase 6 -- Cadmium Recovery + Discharge

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `CADMIUM RECOVERY AND DISCHARGE COMPLIANCE`.

**Left -- Recovery Methods (accent `#27AE60`):** Ion exchange captures Cd from dilute rinse / Electrolytic recovers Cd metal / Evaporation concentrates drag-out / Recovery reduces waste volume and cost.

**Right -- Discharge Limits (accent `#E05C5C`):**

| Parameter | Limit | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Cadmium | 0.1 mg/L (monthly avg) | EPA Clean Water Act |
| Cadmium | 0.69 mg/L (daily max) | EPA 40 CFR 433 |
| Cyanide (total) | 1.2 mg/L (monthly avg) | EPA 40 CFR 433 |
| Cyanide (A) | 0.86 mg/L (daily max) | EPA 40 CFR 433 |
| pH | 6.0--9.0 | POTW permit |

`Federal minimums. Your local POTW may set stricter limits.`
