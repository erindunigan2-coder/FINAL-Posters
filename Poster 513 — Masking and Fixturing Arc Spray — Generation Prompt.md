## Phase 4 -- Masking Materials (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `MASKING MATERIALS FOR ARC SPRAY`.

**Materials Table (Y: 5.0"--9.0"):** 5-row table, full width. Columns: Material | Temp Rating | Adhesion | Best For | Reusable?

| Material | Temp Rating | Adhesion | Best For | Reusable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminum foil tape | 300 degC (570 degF) | Good | Irregular surfaces, quick field masking | No |
| High-temp masking tape | 200--260 degC | Good | Straight edges, small areas | No |
| Sheet metal shields | Unlimited | Mechanical | Large flat areas, repeated production | Yes |
| Magnetic masks | Unlimited | Magnetic hold | Ferrous substrates; fast on/off; field-friendly | Yes |
| Silicone plugs and caps | 260 degC (500 degF) | Friction fit | Bolt holes, threaded holes, tube ends | Yes |

Reusable: `#27AE60` for Yes, `#E05C5C` for No. Header fill `#3A4055`.

**Field Masking Techniques (Y: 9.5"--14.0"):** Left accent `#E8A020`. Title: `FIELD MASKING -- BRIDGES & STRUCTURES`.
- Al foil tape + duct tape is the field workhorse
- Sheet metal shields clamped or magnetically attached
- Silicone plugs for bolt holes, pipe ends, threaded inserts
- Mask faying surfaces for proper bolt tension
- Overspray on structural steel generally acceptable
- Remove masking promptly after spray and before seal
- Inspect: no overspray in threads, holes, or bearing surfaces

| Approach | Details |
|---|---|
| HAND-HELD (NO FIXTURE) | Operator walks the workpiece. Standard for field. No fixture cost; relies on operator skill. |
| TURNTABLE | Simple rotary table for small cylindrical parts. Manual or motorized rotation. |
| LATHE-TYPE ROTATION | Chuck and tailstock for long cylinders. Consistent rotation. Gun on traverse or hand-held. |
| ROBOT + TURNTABLE | 6-axis robot for high-volume shop production. Max uniformity. Higher capital cost. |

## Phase 6 -- Must-Mask Areas + Quick Reference

### Must-Mask Areas (Y: 21.0"--27.5")

Section label: `AREAS THAT MUST BE MASKED`. 3x2 grid, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Area | Why |
|---|---|
| BOLT HOLES | Coating prevents proper bolt fit and torque. Use silicone plugs. |
| THREADED HOLES | Coating prevents fastener engagement. Plugs or tape. |
| BEARING SURFACES | Changes dimensional tolerance. Mask precisely to drawing. |
| FAYING SURFACES | Contact faces need bare metal for friction and clamping force. |
| MATING / SEAL FACES | Arc spray roughness compromises gasket/O-ring seals. |
| ELECTRICAL CONTACTS | Zinc increases contact resistance. Mask grounding points. |

### Quick Reference (Y: 27.5"--32.5")

Section label: `MASKING QUICK REFERENCE`. Four cards, left accent `#27AE60`:

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| MASK BEFORE BLAST IF POSSIBLE | Protect threads and precision bores from profile damage |
| VERIFY MASKING AFTER BLAST | Blast can dislodge tape and shift plugs -- inspect all masking |
| REMOVE MASKING BEFORE SEALING | Seal coat covers coating edge, not the masked area |
| DOCUMENT MASKED AREAS | QA records: what was masked, materials used, verified post-spray |
