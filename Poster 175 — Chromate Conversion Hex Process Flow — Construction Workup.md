# Poster #175 -- Construction Workup
## Chromate Conversion (Hex) -- Process Flow

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This is the cluster overview poster for CC-04: Hexavalent Chromate Conversion Coating on Aluminum. The hero story is the self-healing mechanism -- hexavalent chromium trapped in the gel layer migrates to damaged areas and redeposits a protective Cr3+ oxide. No other conversion coating does this. The process is simple (7 stages), but the critical rules are strict: NO HEAT DRYING above 150 F, 24-hour no-touch cure, and always the prominent health and regulatory warning -- Cr(VI) is a known human carcinogen subject to OSHA PEL and REACH/RoHS restrictions.

EVERY poster in this cluster must carry the Cr(VI) carcinogen/regulatory warning. Factual. Not alarmist. But unmissable.

Visual: Two-state diagram (left half = intact coating, right half = damaged + repaired):

Text explanation:

- `When scratched, Cr6+ ions migrate to exposed aluminum`
- `Cr6+ reduces to Cr3+ oxide -- resealing the damage`

- `Trivalent chrome (Type II) CANNOT self-heal`

**BLOCK F -- Cr(VI) Safety & Regulatory Warning (X: 12.0", Y: 21.2", W: 11.5", H: 5.0")**

- Rounded rect, fill `#E05C5C` at 10%, border 2 pt `#E05C5C`, left accent `#E05C5C` 0.06"

**Health hazard:**
- `Cr(VI) is a KNOWN HUMAN CARCINOGEN (IARC Group 1)`
- `Primary exposure routes: inhalation, skin contact`
- `OSHA PEL: 5 ug/m3 (8-hour TWA)`
- `Engineering controls: local exhaust ventilation at all Cr(VI) tanks`
- `PPE: respiratory protection, chemical-resistant gloves, face shield`

**Regulatory status:**
- `EU REACH: Cr(VI) restricted -- Authorization required`
- `RoHS: Cr(VI) prohibited in electronics (exemptions exist)`
- `MIL-DTL-5541 Type II (trivalent) is the designated replacement`
- `Type I remains specified for legacy aerospace programs`
- `Many DoD programs still require Type I for field repair`

**Bottom note:**
- `This poster presents hexavalent chromate as a technical process. It remains in use under regulatory exemption for aerospace and defense applications where no qualified replacement exists.` Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8` at 60%

|---|---|---|
