## Phase 5 -- Chemistry & Parameters

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `Ti COLLOID BATH -- CHEMISTRY AND CONTROL`.

**Left -- Bath Chemistry:** Amber accent. Colloidal titanium phosphate Ti(HPO4)2 | 0.1--0.5% (1--5 g/L) | pH 7.5--9.5 | Ambient to 100 F | 30 sec--2 min | Immersion or spray. Note: Colloid is a DISPERSION, not dissolved. Particle viability is everything.

**Right -- Non-Negotiable Rules:** Coral accent. Five numbered rules:
1. NEVER HEAT this bath
2. pH 7.5--9.5 ONLY
3. NO ACID carryover from upstream
4. NO ALKALINE carryover
5. Dump and remake when contaminated (> 1500--2000 uS/cm)

## Phase 6 -- No Rinse Rule + Conditioner Health

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- DO NOT RINSE AFTER CONDITIONING:** Coral accent with coral-tinted background. `THE TITANIUM COLLOID MUST REMAIN ON THE SURFACE`. Rinsing washes away nucleation sites. Result: coarse crystals, failed adhesion. Most common setup error in zinc phosphate lines. Parts go DIRECTLY to phosphate bath.

**Right -- Is Your Conditioner Alive?** Emerald accent. Health assessment table:

| Check | Healthy | Dead/Dying |
|---|---|---|
| pH | 7.5--9.5 | Below 7 or above 10 |
| Conductivity | < 1500 uS/cm | > 2000 uS/cm |
| Appearance | Slightly milky/opalescent | Clear (settled) or chunky |
| Settling test | Stays suspended 30+ min | Settles rapidly |
| Downstream crystals | Fine (2--10 um) | Coarse (50+ um) |

Bottom: `When in doubt, dump and remake. Fresh conditioner costs less than one rejected load.`

## Phase 7 -- Failure Diagnosis

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `CONDITIONER PROBLEMS -- DIAGNOSIS & FIX`. Four cards (2x2):

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| COARSE CRYSTALS | pH wrong; dead; contaminated; overheated; dilute | Check pH; replace; verify concentration |
| CONDITIONER DEAD | Excess alkaline/acid carryover; bacterial growth; aged | Dump and remake; improve upstream rinsing |
| pH RISING | Alkaline cleaner drag-over | Improve pre-condition rinse; increase overflow |
| pH DROPPING | Acid drag-over or contamination | Check for splash-back; dump and remake |
