## Phase 4 -- Application Technique (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `SPRAY APPLICATION PROCEDURE`.

**6-Step Technique (Y: 5.0"--10.5"):** 3x2 grid of cards, top accent `#27AE60`:

| Step | Title | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PREHEAT TO DEW POINT MARGIN | Substrate min 10 degC above dew point. Cold substrate = condensation = adhesion failure. |
| 2 | FIRST PASS -- BOND LAYER | Close standoff (100--150 mm) for max impact and density. This layer anchors everything. Steady traverse -- no stopping. |
| 3 | BUILD PASSES | Subsequent passes at 150--250 mm. Overlap 25--50%. Maintain consistent distance and angle. |
| 4 | MONITOR THICKNESS | DFT with magnetic gauge per SSPC-PA 2. Multiple locations. Compare to AWS C2.18 target. |
| 5 | CHECK UNIFORMITY | Visual: uniform coverage, consistent texture, no bare spots. Blend edge buildup with light final pass. |
| 6 | PROCEED TO SEAL | Seal within 4 hours. Do not let porous coating absorb moisture. Go directly to post-treatment. |

**AWS C2.18 Thickness Table (Y: 11.0"--15.0"):** Section sublabel: `COATING THICKNESS BY MATERIAL & EXPOSURE -- AWS C2.18`.

| Material | Mild Exposure | Moderate Exposure | Severe / Marine |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zinc (99.9%) | 100--150 um (4--6 mils) | 150--250 um (6--10 mils) | 200--350 um (8--14 mils) |
| Aluminum (99.0%) | 100--150 um (4--6 mils) | 150--250 um (6--10 mils) | 150--350 um (6--14 mils) |
| Zinc-Aluminum 85/15 | 100--150 um (4--6 mils) | 150--250 um (6--10 mils) | 150--300 um (6--12 mils) |

Thickness values in `#E8A020`. Header fill `#3A4055`. Callout: `These are MINIMUM thickness requirements. Many specs call for upper end for additional service life margin.`

## Phase 5 -- Coating Properties

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `AS-SPRAYED COATING PROPERTIES`.

6-row table. Columns: Property | Zinc | Aluminum | Stainless 316L.

| Property | Zinc | Aluminum | Stainless 316L |
|---|---|---|---|
| Porosity | 5--15% | 5--15% | 5--15% |
| Oxide content | 5--15% | 5--15% | 5--15% |
| Bond strength (ASTM C633) | 10--30 MPa | 10--30 MPa | 15--35 MPa |
| Surface roughness (Ra) | 10--25 um | 10--25 um | 10--25 um |
| Hardness | 40--60 HV | 30--50 HV | 250--350 HV |
| Corrosion mechanism | Sacrificial (cathodic) | Barrier + sacrificial (marine) | Barrier only |

**Left callout (`#2EC4B6`):** `Zinc provides CATHODIC protection -- even scratched, zinc corrodes preferentially to protect steel.`

**Right callout (`#E8A020`):** `Aluminum provides BARRIER protection plus sacrificial in marine. Preferred for high-temp (no white corrosion products).`

## Phase 6 -- Seal Window + Defects

### 4-Hour Seal Window (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `THE 4-HOUR SEAL WINDOW`.

**Warning Banner:** Fill `#E05C5C` at 12%, border `#E05C5C`. `SEAL WITHIN 4 HOURS. Porous coatings (5--15%) absorb moisture rapidly. Trapped moisture under seal accelerates corrosion at the interface you are trying to protect.`

**3 Seal Coat Option Cards (top accent `#27AE60`):**

| Seal Type | Application | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| Vinyl wash primer | Brush/spray; 12--25 um DFT | Low-cost; fills surface porosity; first coat of paint system |
| Epoxy sealer | Brush/spray; 25--75 um DFT | Most common; excellent corrosion barrier; topcoat compatible |
| Full paint system | Primer + intermediate + topcoat; 150--300 um | Maximum service life; many specs require complete system |

Exception note: `Some cathodic protection specs leave zinc UNSEALED for sacrificial corrosion. Verify before omitting seal.`

### Defect Watch (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `DEFECT WATCH -- WHAT TO LOOK FOR DURING SPRAY`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Defect | Visual Cue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| BARE SPOTS | Visible substrate through coating | Additional pass; check traverse overlap |
| EXCESSIVE SPATTER | Large solidified droplets; rough/bumpy | Check wire balance; verify air pressure; check tips |
| DELAMINATION | Coating lifting or peeling during spray | Stop; strip and re-blast; investigate contamination/dew point |
| ARC INSTABILITY | Flickering arc; popping sounds | Check wire sync; verify voltage; replace worn tips |
