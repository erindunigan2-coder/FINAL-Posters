Four step boxes connected by vertical arrows. Each: W: 13.5", H: 2.2", fill `#1E2435`, radius 8, left accent 4pt.

| Step | Accent | Title | Details | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#2EC4B6` | Solvent Degrease | Aqueous alkaline preferred. Remove all oils, greases, machining fluids, fingerprints. | No visible residue; no odor |

| 4 | `#27AE60` | Forced Air Dry | Forced air or oven dry. No moisture at time of grit blast. | Completely dry before next step |

**Right -- "Why It Matters More" Callout (W: 8.5"):**

Y: 3.8" to 8.0". Amber-accented glass card.
Title: `WHY IT MATTERS MORE` Barlow Condensed 800, 22pt, `#E8A020`.

Body: `In plasma or HVOF, molten droplets can burn through thin contamination layers and still bond. In cold spray, particles are SOLID. They cannot burn through anything. Any contamination film = a bond-prevention layer. Cleanliness is not "best practice" in cold spray -- it is the ONLY path to adhesion.`

|---|---|---|---|

| R1C2 | `#E8A020` | COPPER ELECTRICAL | Avoid residual cleaning agents that increase contact resistance. Acetone wipe + mild alkaline + DI rinse. |
| R2C1 | `#C8D0D8` | TITANIUM | Standard sequence. Ti oxidizes rapidly -- minimize exposure after cleaning. Grit blast within 2 hr. |
| R2C2 | `#27AE60` | POLYMERS / COMPOSITES | Cold spray = ONLY thermal spray option. Light solvent wipe only. Mechanical abrasion for activation. |

**Time Windows Strip (Y: 22.0"--25.3"):** Coral top accent 4pt.

Title: `CRITICAL TIME WINDOWS`.

| Window | Time Limit | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Clean to grit blast | Same shift (< 4 hr) | Recontamination from airborne oils, handling |
| Grit blast to spray | < 2 hours preferred | Oxide regrowth; moisture pickup |
| Handling after blast | NEVER bare hands | Skin oils = adhesion failure |

Time limits in `#E05C5C`. Note: `Wear clean lint-free gloves at all times after grit blast.` in `#27AE60`.

## Phase 5 -- Verification Methods

Y: 25.5" to 32.5". Section label: `VERIFICATION METHODS`.

**Left -- Water-Break-Free Test (W: 11.0", accent `#27AE60`):**

Title: `WATER-BREAK-FREE TEST` (ASTM F22 equivalent).
Procedure: 1. Rinse with clean water. 2. Observe film behavior. 3. PASS: uniform sheet. 4. FAIL: beading or breaks.
Visual: PASS badge `#27AE60` | FAIL badge `#E05C5C`. `Any break = reclean entire part` in `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- Alternative Methods (W: 11.5", accent `#E8A020`):**

| Method | Application |
|---|---|
| UV fluorescence | Detects residual oils under UV light |
| Contact angle measurement | Quantitative cleanliness metric |
| Solvent wipe + visual | Quick field verification |
| FTIR surface analysis | Laboratory confirmation |

Note: `Water-break-free is the universal field standard. Use alternatives when spec requires quantitative data.`
