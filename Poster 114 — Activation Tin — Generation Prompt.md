## Phase 4 -- Activation Mechanism Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `HOW ACID ACTIVATION WORKS`.

Three-panel before/during/after sequence (each W: 7.0", H: 8.0", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6, connected by arrows 3pt `#3A4055`):

**Panel 1 -- BEFORE:** Substrate layer (fill `#C8D0D8`, `BASE METAL`) + oxide layer (fill `#E05C5C` at 40%, `OXIDE FILM`). Annotation: `Surface oxides block adhesion`.

**Panel 2 -- DURING:** Substrate + partially dissolved oxide with gaps. Acid arrows `H2SO4`. Equation: `Metal oxide + H2SO4 -> Metal sulfate + H2O`. Annotation: `Acid dissolves oxide layer`.

**Panel 3 -- AFTER:** Clean substrate, no oxide, `#27AE60` glow. Annotation: `Clean, active metal surface` / `Ready for tin deposition`.

Key params below (Y: 13.0"): `Typical: 5--10% H2SO4 | Ambient | 15--30 sec`

## Phase 5 -- Substrate Activation Table

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `ACTIVATION BY SUBSTRATE`.

| Substrate | Acid | Concentration | Temp | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Copper / Brass | H2SO4 | 5--10% v/v | Ambient | 15--30 sec | Most common for tin |
| Steel | HCl or H2SO4 | HCl 10--20% or H2SO4 5--15% | Ambient | 15--60 sec | HCl preferred for heavy oxide |
| Nickel-plated | H2SO4 | 5% v/v | Ambient | 10--15 sec | Light touch -- protect Ni layer |
| Pre-tinned (rework) | H2SO4 | 5--10% | Ambient | 10--20 sec | Remove tarnish, not the tin |

Note below: `H2SO4 is the default activation acid for tin plating. HCl is acceptable for steel but introduces chloride -- keep it out of the tin bath.`

## Phase 6 -- Over vs. Under Activation

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `GET IT RIGHT -- OVER VS. UNDER`.

**Left -- Under-Activated (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):** Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`. Title: `UNDER-ACTIVATED`.
Symptoms: Oxide remains / Tin plates over oxide -- poor adhesion / Blistering on thermal cycling / Solderability fails after aging / Peel test failure.
Visual cue: `Looks OK wet -- fails in test`.

**Right -- Over-Activated (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):** Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`. Title: `OVER-ACTIVATED`.
Symptoms: Base metal attacked -- roughened / Copper turns pink or matte / Grainy, rough tin deposit / Dimensional loss / Increased drag-in of dissolved metal.
Visual cue: `Visible etch on copper = too long or too strong`.

Center verdict: `The window is narrow: 15--30 seconds in 5--10% sulfuric. Set a timer. Every time.`

## Phase 7 -- Safety

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `ACID SAFETY -- NON-NEGOTIABLE`.

Two safety cards:

**Left -- Sulfuric Acid (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):** Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`. Title: `SULFURIC ACID (H2SO4)`.
`Corrosive -- severe burns` / `Add acid to water, NEVER water to acid` / `Exothermic on dilution` / `PPE: face shield, gloves, apron` / `Spill: soda ash, flush with water`.

**Right -- Hydrochloric Acid (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):** Fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`. Title: `HYDROCHLORIC ACID (HCl)`.
`Corrosive -- severe burns, strong fumes` / `Fuming at room temp -- ventilation mandatory` / `PPE: face shield, gloves, apron, respirator` / `Chloride contamination risk to tin bath` / `Rinse thoroughly after HCl activation`.
