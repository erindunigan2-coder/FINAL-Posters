# Poster #513 -- Construction Workup
## Masking & Fixturing -- Arc Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Masking and fixturing for arc spray. Arc spray is a "big area" coating process -- it covers entire bridge beams, ship hulls, and structural members. Masking is simple compared to precision thermal spray: aluminum foil tape, sheet metal shields, and magnetic masks. Fixturing is often nonexistent -- the operator holds the gun and walks the structure. This poster covers field masking for infrastructure work alongside shop fixturing for cylindrical parts.

Content (Inter Regular 14 pt, line height 160%):
- `Aluminum foil tape + duct tape combination is the field workhorse`
- `Sheet metal shields clamped or magnetically attached for large exclusion zones`

- `For bolted connections: mask the faying surface (contact face) to ensure proper bolt tension`
- `Overspray on structural steel is generally acceptable -- arc spray is often full-coverage`
- `Remove masking promptly after spray and before seal coat application`
- `Inspect masked areas: no overspray in threads, holes, or bearing surfaces`

### ZONE 4 -- Shop Fixturing

**Section label:** `SHOP FIXTURING OPTIONS` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Four Fixturing Approaches**

Y: 15.3" to 20.5". Four cards in a row.

Each card: W: 5.5", H: 4.8", fill `#1E2435`, top accent `#2EC4B6`.

| Card | Approach | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HAND-HELD (NO FIXTURE) | Operator holds gun and walks the workpiece. Standard for field work on bridges and large structures. No fixture cost; relies on operator skill for uniformity. |
| 2 | TURNTABLE | Simple rotary table for small cylindrical parts (shafts, rollers). Manual or motorized rotation. Gun position fixed or hand-held. |
| 3 | LATHE-TYPE ROTATION | Chuck and tailstock fixture for long cylinders. Consistent rotation speed for uniform thickness. Gun mounted on linear traverse or hand-held. |
| 4 | ROBOT + TURNTABLE | 6-axis robot for high-volume shop production. Maximum uniformity and repeatability. Higher capital cost; justified for production runs. |

Card number: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 28 pt `#2EC4B6`. Approach: Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#F0EDE8`. Details: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8` at 80%.

### ZONE 5 -- Must-Mask Areas

**Section label:** `AREAS THAT MUST BE MASKED` -- Y: 21.2".

**BLOCK E -- Critical Masking Areas**

Y: 21.8" to 27.0". Six cards in a 3x2 grid. Each: W: 7.3", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Card | Area | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | BOLT HOLES | Arc spray in bolt holes prevents proper bolt fit and torque. Silicone plugs are the fast solution. |
| 2 | THREADED HOLES | Coating in threads prevents fastener engagement. Use silicone plugs or tape over threaded inserts. |
| 3 | BEARING SURFACES | Any coating on bearing seats changes dimensional tolerance. Mask precisely to drawing dimensions. |
| 4 | FAYING SURFACES | Contact faces of bolted joints must be bare metal for proper friction and clamping force. |
| 5 | MATING / SEAL FACES | Gasket or O-ring surfaces require specific finish. Arc spray roughness will compromise the seal. |
| 6 | ELECTRICAL CONTACT POINTS | Arc spray coatings (especially zinc) increase contact resistance. Mask grounding and electrical connections. |

Area: Barlow SemiBold 14 pt `#E05C5C`. Why: Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`.

### ZONE 6 -- Quick Reference

**Section label:** `MASKING QUICK REFERENCE` -- Y: 27.7".

Four cards, W: 5.5", H: 3.0", left accent `#27AE60`.

| Card | X | Rule | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | MASK BEFORE BLAST IF POSSIBLE | Some areas (threads, precision bores) should be masked before grit blasting to prevent profile damage |
| 2 | 6.33" | VERIFY MASKING AFTER BLAST | Grit blast can dislodge tape and shift plugs. Inspect all masking before starting the arc. |
| 3 | 12.16" | REMOVE MASKING BEFORE SEALING | Seal coat should cover the coating edge but not the masked area. Remove masking cleanly. |
| 4 | 18.0" | DOCUMENT MASKED AREAS | QA records should note which areas were masked, what materials were used, and that masking was verified post-spray. |

Rule: Barlow SemiBold 14 pt `#27AE60`. Detail: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`.
