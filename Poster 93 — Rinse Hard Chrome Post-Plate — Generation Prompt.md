## Phase 5 -- Drag-Out Reduction + Rinse Params

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `REDUCING DRAG-OUT AND RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Drag-Out Reduction Methods (accent `#E8A020`):**
- Fume suppressants: reduce surface tension + misting AND drag-out (PFAS-free)
- Drain time: 10--30 sec over chrome bath before transfer
- Slow withdrawal: reduce film thickness on parts
- Air knife/blow-off: blow excess back into tank (enclosed area)
- Recovery tank design: heated (140 F) evaporates water, concentrates returns

**Rinse Parameter Table:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Configuration | Drag-out recovery + double/triple counterflow |
| Water temp | Ambient; DI for final |
| Final conductivity | < 50 uS/cm (precision parts) |
| Immersion per tank | 30--60 sec |
| All rinse water | Cr(VI) reduction + hydroxide precipitation |

## Phase 6 -- Common Failures + Regulatory

Y: 20.5" to 27.0".

**Left -- Common Failures (W: 14.0"):**

| Failure | Root Cause | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Cr(VI) exceeds discharge | Inadequate reduction; bypass | EPA violation; fines; criminal liability |
| Chrome staining | Insufficient rinsing | Yellow-orange stain visible at inspection |
| Recovery tank contaminated | Tramp metal fell in | Contaminated solution returned to bath |
| No drag-out recovery | Tank skipped | 2--5x higher chrome consumption |
| Flash rust before bake | Slow transfer to oven | Oxidation before HE bake |

**Right -- Regulatory (W: 8.0", coral panel):**

| Regulation | Requirement |
|---|---|
| OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1026 | PEL 5 ug/m3; worker protection |
| EPA 40 CFR 63 Subpart N | NESHAP for hard chrome |
| EPA 40 CFR 433 | Metal finishing effluent limits |
| RCRA (D007) | Cr waste = hazardous waste |
| State/local | May be stricter than federal |

## Phase 7 -- Monitoring + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Rinse Water Monitoring (W: 14.0", accent `#2EC4B6`):**
- Conductivity meters in tanks 2 and 3
- DPC spot test for Cr(VI) in final rinse
- Total Cr in effluent: AA or colorimetric, test daily
- Record keeping: log all treatments and discharge analyses

**Right -- Safety (W: 8.0", coral panel):**
- ALL rinse water contains Cr(VI) until proven otherwise
- Splashes: flush immediately -- Cr(VI) penetrates skin rapidly
- Chrome ulcers from skin contact
- PPE: acid-resistant gloves, splash goggles, face shield, rubber apron
- Rinse area: local exhaust ventilation
- Spills: contain and treat as hazardous waste
