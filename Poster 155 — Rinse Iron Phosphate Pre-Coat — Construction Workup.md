# Poster #155 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Iron Phosphate -- Pre-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This rinse exists only in full 5-stage iron phosphate lines -- between surface conditioning (or the mild acid rinse) and the phosphate coating stage. In 3-stage systems it does not exist. Purpose is identical to Poster #153: prevent contamination of the phosphate bath. This poster focuses on water quality and the impact of hard water on phosphate film formation.

### ZONE 4 -- Water Quality Deep-Dive

**Section label:** `WATER QUALITY MATTERS MORE THAN YOU THINK` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Three Water Quality Cards (Y: 15.3" to 20.3")**

| Card | X | W | Title | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | 7.33" | CITY WATER | Acceptable for most applications. Watch for hardness > 150 ppm, chlorine, and seasonal variation. Conductivity varies 200--800 uS/cm. |
| 2 | 8.16" | 7.33" | SOFTENED WATER | Preferred in hard-water areas. Removes Ca/Mg that interfere with phosphate film. Does not remove dissolved solids. |
| 3 | 15.83" | 7.67" | DI / RO WATER | Best quality. < 50 uS/cm. Recommended for OEM-spec lines. Higher cost justified by coating consistency. |

Each card: Rounded rect, H: 4.8", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6.

### ZONE 5 -- Contamination Transfer

**Section label:** `WHAT GETS DRAGGED INTO THE PHOSPHATE BATH` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK E -- Contaminant Impact Table (Y: 21.3" to 26.3")**

Full-width rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`.

| Contaminant | Source | Impact on Phosphate |
|---|---|---|
| Alkaline residue | Poor upstream rinsing | Raises phosphate bath pH; reduces coating weight |
| Calcium / Magnesium | Hard rinse water | Interferes with Fe phosphate deposition; spotty coating |
| Chlorides | City water or acid carry-over | Accelerates flash rust between stages |
| Silicates | Cleaner drag-through | Inhibits phosphate nucleation; bare spots |
| Oil / organics | Incomplete cleaning | Skip areas; poor adhesion |

Contaminant: Inter Medium 14 pt `#E05C5C`. Source: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8` at 70%. Impact: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`.

Bottom note: `In 3-stage systems, this rinse does not exist. The phosphate bath must tolerate all upstream contamination -- which is why 5-stage lines produce better coatings.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E8A020`.

### ZONE 6 -- Parameters & Monitoring

**Section label:** `RINSE PARAMETERS` -- Y: 26.7".

**BLOCK F -- Parameter Table (Y: 27.3" to 30.5")**

| Parameter | Target | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | Ambient to 80 F (27 C) | Thermometer |
| Conductivity | < 500 uS/cm (< 200 ideal) | Conductivity meter |
| pH | 6.0--8.0 (neutral) | pH meter |
| Overflow rate | 1--3 gal/min | Flow meter or visual |
| Hardness | < 150 ppm (as CaCO3) | Test kit or water report |

**BLOCK F2 -- Key Rules (Y: 31.0" to 32.3")**

Three rule callouts in a row:

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| KEEP IT FLOWING | Stagnant rinse water concentrates contaminants. Continuous overflow is non-negotiable. |
| DUMP WHEN DIRTY | When conductivity exceeds 1000 uS/cm despite overflow, dump and refill. |
| MATCH TO SPEC | OEM paint specs often specify DI water. Check before defaulting to city water. |

Rule: Barlow SemiBold 14 pt `#E8A020`. Detail: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8`.
