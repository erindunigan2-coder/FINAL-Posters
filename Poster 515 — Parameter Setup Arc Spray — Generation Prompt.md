## Phase 4 -- Parameter Table (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `ARC SPRAY OPERATING PARAMETERS`.

**Full Parameter Table (Y: 5.0"--12.0"):** 11-row table. Columns: Parameter (4.0") | Typical Range (5.0") | Effect (7.0") | Notes (7.0").

| Parameter | Typical Range | Effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arc voltage | 24--35 V | Higher = wider pattern, coarser droplets | Primary arc control |
| Arc current | 100--300 A | Determined by wire speed at given voltage | Not directly set |
| Wire feed speed | 2--15 m/min (each) | Higher = higher dep rate, higher current | Both wires synchronized |
| Atomizing air pressure | 40--80 PSI (275--550 kPa) | Higher = finer atomization, denser coating | Most critical for quality |
| Air volume | 40--80 CFM | Must maintain flow at working pressure | Undersized compressor = pressure drop |
| Standoff distance | 100--250 mm (4--10 in) | Closer = denser; farther = more porosity | Optimal 150--200 mm |
| Spray angle | 60--90 degrees | <45 deg = severe porosity, poor adhesion | 90 deg ideal |
| Traverse speed | Manual or robot 200--800 mm/s | Faster = thinner; slower = thicker but overheat risk | Consistency = uniformity |
| Deposition rate | 5--30+ kg/hr | Highest of all thermal spray | Material dependent |
| Deposition efficiency | 60--80% | Higher than flame spray (50--70%) | Remainder is overspray |
| Particle velocity | 50--200 m/s | Air pressure is primary driver | Velocity at nozzle exit |

Header fill `#3A4055`. Ranges in `#E8A020`.

**Parameter Interaction (Y: 12.5"--15.0"):** Three panels:

**VOLTAGE 24--35 V (`#E8A020`):** Low (24--28 V) = narrow, focused, finer. High (30--35 V) = wider, diffuse, coarser. Start low, increase to match application.

**WIRE SPEED 2--15 m/min (`#2EC4B6`):** Controls dep rate directly. Higher = more material = higher current. Both wires must match -- asymmetry = arc instability.

**AIR PRESSURE 40--80 PSI (`#27AE60`):** Single most important parameter for density. Higher = finer atomization = denser. Lower = coarser = more porosity. Too high = overspray waste.

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `STANDOFF DISTANCE & SPRAY ANGLE`.

| Range | Effect | Color |

| Optimal (100--250 mm) | Best density, adhesion, surface finish | `#27AE60` |

## Phase 6 -- Test Coupon + Common Errors

### Test Coupon Checklist (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `PRE-PRODUCTION TEST COUPON SEQUENCE`. 6 steps in 2x3 grid:

| Step | Action | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select coupon -- same substrate as production | Match material, thickness, condition |
| 2 | Grit blast coupon to same profile spec | Ra 4--12 um; SSPC-SP 5 |
| 3 | Spray one pass at production parameters | Uniform fan; stable arc |
| 4 | Measure thickness per pass | Record um/pass for planning |
| 5 | Build to target thickness on second coupon | Verify total thickness + visual |
| 6 | Bend test or adhesion check per spec | Document before production |

Steps in `#2EC4B6`. Verify in `#27AE60`.

### Common Errors (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `COMMON PARAMETER ERRORS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Error | Result | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| WIRE FEED IMBALANCE | Asymmetric arc; uneven pattern | Synchronize speeds; check conduit drag |
| AIR PRESSURE TOO LOW | Coarse droplets; excessive porosity | Increase to 60--80 PSI; verify compressor |
| VOLTAGE TOO HIGH | Wide pattern; high overspray; waste | Reduce in 2 V increments |
| WORN CONTACT TIPS | Arc wanders; inconsistent; spitting | Replace every shift or per OEM schedule |
