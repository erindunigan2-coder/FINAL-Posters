## Phase 5 -- DFT + Adhesion + Zinc Loading

### DFT Measurement (Y: 14.5"-21.0")

Two columns.

Left -- ASTM D7091: Magnetic gauge (steel). IOZ: calibrate for rough surfaces. Min 5 spots per area. DFT targets: IOZ 2.5-4.0 (max 5.0), OZ 2.0-3.5, Epoxy 1.0-3.0.

Right -- SSPC-PA 2: Spot = avg 3-5 readings in 1.5" circle. Area = avg of spots. No spot < 80% of spec min. No area avg below spec min. `DFT is a statistical distribution.`

### Adhesion + Zinc Loading (Y: 21.0"-27.0")

Two columns.

Left -- Adhesion: D4541 pull-off > 200 psi. D3359 X-cut tape. IOZ cohesive failure at > 200 psi = acceptable (steel bond is stronger). Adhesive failure at interface at ANY psi = prep problem.

Right -- Zinc Loading: Manufacturer COA (75-85% IOZ, 65-80% OZ). SSPC-Paint 20, PS 12.01. XRF (forensic, not routine). ASTM D520/D521.
