# Poster #365 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Acid Pickling (Stainless Steel)

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Poster 2 of 7 in the CT-04 cluster. This is the most critical safety poster in the entire Chemical Treatment series. Hydrofluoric acid (HF) is in a different hazard class than any other chemical covered so far. HF penetrates skin rapidly, causes deep tissue destruction, depletes calcium from the bloodstream (hypocalcemia), and can be FATAL from exposure to areas as small as 2% of body surface. The delayed onset of pain means workers may not realize they have been exposed until hours later. This poster must be unambiguous, aggressive, and readable from 6 feet by someone who is panicking.

The hero visual is an HF-specific emergency procedure panel with the calcium gluconate protocol front and center. The PPE requirements are stricter than any other poster in the series.

## Design Notes

This is the most important safety poster in the entire poster library. HF kills people in metal finishing shops. The design must be viscerally clear -- no subtlety, no understatement. The Coral accent dominates every zone except ventilation. The calcium gluconate panel is given its own dedicated space because this is the single most actionable piece of information on the poster: if someone gets HF on their skin, the gel is the difference between a recoverable injury and a fatality. The delayed onset warning must be prominent -- workers who dismiss a "minor splash" because it does not hurt immediately are the ones who end up in the emergency room 6 hours later.
