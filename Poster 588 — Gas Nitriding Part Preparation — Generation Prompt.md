## Phase 4 -- Pre-Heat-Treat Requirement (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 13.0". Section label: `CRITICAL: PRE-HEAT-TREATMENT IS MANDATORY` in `#E05C5C`.

Main panel (left accent `#E05C5C`): `QUENCH & TEMPER BEFORE NITRIDING -- NON-NEGOTIABLE`. Gas nitriding operates below Ac1 -- no austenite, no quench. Core retains whatever hardness it had going in. If annealed, core will be SOFT.

Temperature Rule Box: `THE 50 F RULE` in `#E8A020`. Left: temper must exceed nitriding temp by 50 F; violation = core softens during 15--90 hr cycle. Right: examples -- nitriding at 975 F -> min temper 1025 F; nitriding at 1050 F -> min temper 1100 F. Bottom: stress relieve after rough machining recommended.

## Phase 5 -- Contaminants + Surface Checklist

### Contaminant Table (Y: 13.0"--18.5")

Section label: `SURFACE CONTAMINANTS -- WHAT GOES WRONG`. 6-row table:

| Contaminant | Effect | How to Remove |
|---|---|---|
| Cutting fluid (sulfur) | Poisons reaction; soft spots | Solvent then alkaline clean |
| Fingerprint oils | Blocks N; visible soft spot pattern | Wear gloves; vapor degrease |
| Cr2O3 passive film | Blocks N on high-Cr steels | Light abrasive blast or chemical activation |
| Rust / iron oxide | Uneven absorption; blotchy case | Pickle or abrasive clean |
| Paint / ink / markers | Complete N blockage | Solvent strip; verify removal |
| Residual plating chemicals | Contaminate atmosphere | Thorough rinse and dry |

### Surface Checklist (Y: 18.5"--25.0")

Section label: `SURFACE PREPARATION CHECKLIST`. 4 items: Verify Q&T (`#27AE60`); Degrease/Clean (`#27AE60`); Activate high-Cr surfaces (`#27AE60`); Handle with gloves (`#E05C5C` -- single fingerprint = visible soft spot after 40+ hr).

## Phase 6 -- Masking Comparison

Y: 25.0"--32.5". Section label: `MASKING FOR SELECTIVE NITRIDING`. 3 cards side by side:

**TIN PLATE (`#27AE60`):** 0.0003--0.0005 in; blocks N effectively; most widely used; readily available. Verdict: `RECOMMENDED`.

**NICKEL PLATE (`#2EC4B6`):** 0.001 in; effective alternative; thicker deposit needed; higher cost. Verdict: `ACCEPTABLE`.

**COPPER PLATE (`#E05C5C`):** Copper does NOT block nitrogen (unlike carburizing where Cu is standard). Nitrogen passes through copper at nitriding temps. Verdict: `NOT EFFECTIVE -- DO NOT USE`.
