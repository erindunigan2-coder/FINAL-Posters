7-row primary table. Columns: Parameter (5.5") | Typical Range (8.5") | Notes (9.0").

| Parameter | Typical Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Media | White or brown alumina (Al2O3) | White preferred for aerospace |
| Grit size | 24-36 mesh (coarse); 60 mesh thin substrates | Finer for delicate parts |
| Blast pressure | 40-80 PSI (275-550 kPa) | Adjust for substrate hardness |

| Blast angle | 60-90 deg to surface | 90 deg = maximum depth |

## Phase 5 -- Media Selection + Why Alumina

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `MEDIA SELECTION`.

**Left -- Media Comparison (W: 11.0"):**

| Media | Grit Size | Hardness | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| White alumina | 24-60 mesh | Mohs 9 | Aerospace preferred |
| Brown alumina | 24-60 mesh | Mohs 9 | General; lower cost |
| Silicon carbide | 24-60 mesh | Mohs 9.5 | Hard substrates; Ti |
| Garnet | 36-80 mesh | Mohs 7-8 | Non-ferrous; less aggressive |

**Right -- Why Alumina for Aerospace (W: 11.5", accent `#2EC4B6`):**

- No ferrous contamination risk on Ni or Ti substrates
- Fractures to expose fresh cutting edges (self-sharpening)
- Steel grit can embed and cause galvanic corrosion
- 99%+ purity required for critical aerospace apps

## Phase 6 -- Profile Verification + Cleanliness + SPS Notes

### Three Verification Methods (Left, Y: 22.0"-28.5")

| Method | Description | Precision |
|---|---|---|
| Replica tape (Testex) | Press-o-film + micrometer | +/- 0.5 um |
| Profilometer | Ra, Rz measurement | +/- 0.1 um |
| Visual comparison | SSPC-VIS 1 comparators | Qualitative |

### Cleanliness Standards (Right)

| Standard | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| SSPC-SP 5 | White Metal Blast | 100% bare metal |
| SSPC-SP 10 | Near-White Blast | 95% bare metal |
| ISO 8501 SA 3 | Visually Clean Steel | Equivalent to SP 5 |

### SPS-Specific Notes (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `SPS-SPECIFIC SURFACE PREP NOTES`. Four cards:

1. Grit blast is for substrate -- APS bond coat is sprayed onto this profile
2. No additional blasting between APS bond coat and SPS topcoat
3. Bond coat's natural as-sprayed roughness provides adhesion for SPS
4. Profile verification done BEFORE bond coat, not before SPS topcoat
