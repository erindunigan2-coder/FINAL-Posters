## Phase 4 -- Application Technique (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 14.0". Section label: `APPLICATION TECHNIQUE`.

### Application Steps (Left, W: 14.5")

Six vertically stacked step cards:

| Step | Accent | Title | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#E8A020` | VERIFY SUBSTRATE PREP | Confirm blast clean/dry/within time window; masking secure |
| 2 | `#2EC4B6` | CONFIRM PARAMETERS | O2/C2H2 ratio; frequency; powder charge; standoff programmed |
| 3 | `#27AE60` | INITIATE DETONATION CYCLE | Start remotely outside booth; first spots establish bond |
| 4 | `#E8A020` | BUILD BY SPOT OVERLAP | Robot traverses; 25 mm spot at 5--20 um thick; overlapping builds continuous coating |
| 5 | `#2EC4B6` | MONITOR SUBSTRATE TEMP | IR pyrometer; pause and cool if approaching limit; pulsed heat allows recovery |
| 6 | `#27AE60` | COMPLETE TO TARGET | Total 75--500 um; verify via reference coupons or in-process measurement |

### Spot Overlap Concept (Right, W: 8.0")

Amber-accented glass card. Title: `SPOT OVERLAP DEPOSITION` 22pt `#E8A020`.

```
Single spot:  ~25 mm diameter
Spot thickness: 5--20 um
Frequency:    1--15 spots/second
Overlap:      50--75% of previous spot
```

Two unique advantages: (1) Intermittent heat -- substrate cools between cycles. (2) Each spot independently dense -- no continuous splat cascade porosity.

Stat: `5--20 um/spot` 20pt `#E8A020` vs. `25--75 um/pass (HVOF continuous)` at 60%.

### Pulsed Heat Advantage (below, emerald accent)

D-Gun delivers heat in discrete pulses. Between pulses, substrate conducts heat away. Result: lower peak temp, less distortion, better adhesion on heat-sensitive substrates, air cooling sufficient.

## Phase 5 -- Coating Properties Benchmark

Y: 14.0" to 22.0". Section label: `COATING PROPERTIES -- WC-12Co BENCHMARK`.

8-row comparison table. Columns: Property | D-Gun WC-12Co | HVOF WC-12Co | Advantage.

| Property | D-Gun | HVOF | Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Porosity | < 0.5% (often <0.2%) | < 1% (typ <0.5%) | D-Gun: denser |
| Oxide content | < 0.3% | < 0.5% | D-Gun: less oxidation |
| Bond strength (C633) | > 80 MPa (exceeds epoxy) | > 70 MPa | D-Gun: stronger |
| Hardness (HV300) | 1200--1500 | 1100--1400 | D-Gun: harder |
| Surface roughness (Ra) | 2--5 um as-sprayed | 3--6 um | D-Gun: smoother |
| Wear rate (G65) | 0.5--3 x10^-7 mm3/Nm | 1--5 x10^-7 | D-Gun: more resistant |
| Deposition rate | 1--5 kg/hr | 2--10 kg/hr | HVOF: faster |
| Thickness range | 75--500 um | 75--750 um | HVOF: broader |

D-Gun values `#E8A020`. HVOF values `#2EC4B6`.

Summary callout: `D-Gun wins on coating quality. HVOF wins on throughput and versatility. Both are hard-chrome replacements.`
