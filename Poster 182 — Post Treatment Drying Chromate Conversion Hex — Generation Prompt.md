## Phase 6 -- Regulatory & Safety

Y: 19.5" to 26.5". Section: `HEXAVALENT CHROMIUM -- HEALTH & REGULATORY STATUS` coral 28pt.

**Left -- Health Hazard:** Coral-tinted panel. IARC Group 1 carcinogen | OSHA PEL 5 ug/m3 | Inhalation + skin contact | Local exhaust ventilation | Respiratory protection | Chemical-resistant gloves | Medical surveillance.

**Right -- Regulatory Status:** Amber accent. EU REACH Authorization List | RoHS prohibited (exemptions) | OSHA PEL + engineering controls | EPA hazardous waste.
