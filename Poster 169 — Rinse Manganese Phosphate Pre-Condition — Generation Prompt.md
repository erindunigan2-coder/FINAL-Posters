## Phase 6 -- Single vs. Multiple + Consequences

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Single vs. Multiple:** Single adequate for light soils, low cleaner conc, conductivity < 300. Multiple needed for heavy machining oil, high cleaner conc, conductivity > 500, precision parts.

**Right -- Carryover Consequences:** Alkaline into acid pickle (neutralizes), alkaline into phosphate bath (kills coating), surfactant residue (foam/skip areas), silicate residue (passivates steel).

---

## Phase 7 -- Key Rules + Economics

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Rules:** Run HOT 120--160 F | Continuous overflow | Monitor conductivity daily | If pH stays above 9.0, rinse is overloaded | Multiple stages for heavy soils.

**Right -- Rinse Economics:** Counter-flow uses 40--60% less water | Dragout reduction: withdraw slowly, 10--15 sec drain.
