# Poster #578 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Carbonitriding

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Safety poster for the carbonitriding cluster. This poster carries all the gas carburizing hazards (CO, H2, quench oil fire, burns, atmosphere explosion) PLUS the ammonia hazards unique to carbonitriding. Ammonia is a severe respiratory and eye irritant with a TLV-TWA of only 25 ppm. This is the poster that hangs next to the furnace door -- high-visibility, high-contrast, unmissable.

## Design Notes

This poster must be HIGH CONTRAST and immediately scannable. The ammonia hazard card (R1C3) is the unique differentiator from a standard carburizing safety poster -- ammonia's 25 ppm TLV means even small leaks are dangerous. The dual emergency response panels cover the two scenarios most likely to cause injury: ammonia exposure and quench oil fire.
