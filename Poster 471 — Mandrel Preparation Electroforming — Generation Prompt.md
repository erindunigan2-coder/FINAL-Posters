## Phase 4 -- Mandrel Types Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `MANDREL TYPES`.

Three tall panels side by side:

**Panel 1 -- PERMANENT (`#27AE60`):**
- Materials: stainless steel, nickel, chrome-plated steel, glass
- Separation: release agent each cycle (chromate passivation, proprietary); mechanical (pry, flex) or thermal differential
- Best for: production runs, highest repeatability, lowest per-part cost at volume

**Panel 2 -- EXPENDABLE (`#E8A020`):**
- Materials: aluminum (NaOH), zinc (HCl), wax (melt), ABS (acetone), 3D-printed polymer
- Separation: mandrel destroyed -- no release agent needed
- Al: 10-20% NaOH at 60-80 C; Zn: 10-20% HCl ambient
- Best for: complex internal geometry, undercuts, prototypes

**Panel 3 -- SEMI-EXPENDABLE (`#2EC4B6`):**
- Materials: Cerrolow (47 C), Cerrobend (70 C), Wood's metal (70 C)
- Separation: melt out, collect, re-melt, re-cast
- NOTE: Some contain lead, cadmium -- check RoHS

**Key Principle Callout (Y: 12.8"):** `The mandrel is a negative of the final part. The mandrel's EXTERIOR surface becomes the electroformed part's INTERIOR surface -- which is the precision surface. Polish the mandrel to the spec the part must meet.`

## Phase 5 -- Release Agents + Conductive Coatings

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `RELEASE AGENTS & CONDUCTIVE COATINGS`.

**Release Agent Table:**

| Agent | Application | Mandrel Type | Pros | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chromate passivation (K2Cr2O7 2-5%) | Dip 30-60 sec, 20-50 C | Permanent (SS, Ni) | Proven, reliable | Cr(VI) -- RoHS/REACH |
| Trivalent Cr passivation | Alternative to hex Cr | Permanent | RoHS compliant | Less proven |
| Proprietary parting compounds | Brush/spray | Permanent | No Cr(VI) | Cost; supplier-dependent |
| Electrolytic oxidation | Anodic in NaOH | Permanent (Ni) | No chemicals to dispose | Setup required |
| None required | -- | Expendable/semi-expendable | Simplest | N/A |

**Conductive Coating Table:**

| Method | Thickness | Application | Pros | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electroless nickel | 0.5-2 um | Universal (wax, plastic, glass) | Excellent adhesion; uniform | Requires activation |
| Silver paint | 5-25 um | Art, jewelry, prototypes | Quick, easy | Less uniform |
| Graphite spray | 1-5 um | Quick prototyping | Fast, cheap | Porosity; rough |
| Vacuum metallization | 50-200 nm | Precision: LIGA, optical | Excellent detail | Vacuum equipment |
| Conductive lacquer | 5-50 um | Commercial | Available | Thickness variation |

## Phase 6 -- Surface Finish + Troubleshooting

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Surface Finish Table (Y: 22.0" to 28.5"):** Section label: `MANDREL SURFACE FINISH REQUIREMENTS`.

| Application | Ra (um) | Ra (uin) | Finish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Optical reflectors | < 0.01 | < 0.4 | Optical polish |
| Precision waveguides | < 0.05 | < 2 | Mirror polish |
| CD/DVD stampers | < 0.02 | < 0.8 | Optical polish |
| Mold inserts | 0.05-0.4 | 2-16 | Fine grind to polish |
| General industrial | < 0.2 | < 8 | Fine grind |
| Prototyping | 0.4-1.6 | 16-63 | Machine finish |

**Draft Angle Guidance:** Include 1-3 deg draft on permanent mandrels. Without draft = mechanical lock. Expendable mandrels: no draft needed.

**Mandrel Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5" to 32.5"):** Four problem cards.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CANNOT SEPARATE | No release agent; undercuts; no draft | Fresh release; add draft; switch to expendable |
| POOR INTERIOR FINISH | Mandrel too rough; scratches | Re-polish; handle with gloves |
| NON-UNIFORM DEPOSITION | Patchy conductive layer | Verify full coverage; inspect before plating |
| MANDREL DAMAGE | Deformed during separation/reuse | Inspect after each cycle; replace when worn |
