# Poster #163 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Zinc Phosphate -- Pre-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This poster occupies the "pre-coat rinse" slot in the 8-poster sequence, but its entire message is: THIS RINSE DOES NOT EXIST. Do NOT rinse between the conditioner and the zinc phosphate bath. The Ti colloid must remain on the surface. This is the most counterintuitive rule in zinc phosphate processing and the most common setup error.

Like Poster #154 (Surface Conditioning -- Iron Phosphate, which explained why no conditioner is needed), this is a "what you must NOT do" poster. It exists to prevent the single most damaging process mistake in zinc phosphate lines. The poster also clarifies the actual rinse placement: the pre-coat rinse is placed BEFORE the conditioner, not after it, in properly designed lines.

## Design Notes

This is a "rule" poster, not a "process" poster. Its value is entirely in communicating a single critical rule with enough visual force that it sticks. The oversized NO RINSE text in the hero zone (180 pt + 120 pt) is intentionally dramatic -- this must be readable from across the shop floor. The correct vs. incorrect sequence diagram in Zone 4 provides the mental model, and the before/after nucleation comparison in Zone 5 provides the scientific backing. Together they answer both "what is the rule?" and "why does the rule exist?"

The rinse placement clarification in Zone 6 addresses a legitimate source of confusion in process documentation -- some reference materials show a "pre-coat rinse" in the zinc phosphate sequence without specifying that it goes before the conditioner, not after it.
