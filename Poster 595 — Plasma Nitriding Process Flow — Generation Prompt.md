## Phase 4 -- Process Flow (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section label: `THE COMPLETE PROCESS -- STAGE BY STAGE`.

Seven-stage U-flow. Top row (Stages 1--4), bottom row R-to-L (Stages 5--7). Each box 5.0" x 4.5".

**Top row:**

| Stage | Name | Accent | Params | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Part Preparation | `#2EC4B6` | Degrease/clean; verify Q&T; temper > nitride + 50 F | Surface must be chemically clean |
| 2 | Loading | `#2EC4B6` | Min 10 mm spacing; non-magnetic fixtures; electrical contact to cathode | Avoid hollow cathode -- holes < 3x depth cause arcing |
| 3 | Vacuum Pumpdown | `#E8A020` | Pump to < 0.1 mbar; mech pump + Roots blower; leak rate check | Moisture causes oxidation -- base vacuum must be verified |
| 4 | Plasma Ignition & Heat-Up | `#E8A020` | 400--1000 V DC pulsed; N2/H2 @ 1--5 mbar; sputter clean | Visible purple/violet glow confirms plasma -- uniform = good |

**Bottom row:**

| Stage | Name | Accent | Params | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Nitriding Cycle | `#27AE60` | 930--1000 F; 25% N2 / 75% H2; 4--40 hr; 1--5 mbar | Gas ratio controls compound layer: more H2 = thinner/no WL |
| 6 | Cooling | `#2EC4B6` | Cool under N2 or N2/H2; no quench; slow cool in vacuum | NO QUENCH -- hardness from precipitation |
| 7 | Inspection & QA | `#E8A020` | Microhardness traverse; WL metallography; dimensional check | Use Vickers/Knoop -- HRC penetrates through thin cases |

Legend: Teal = Prep & Cooling | Amber = System Setup & Quality | Emerald = Nitriding Cycle | Coral = Caution.

## Phase 6 -- Comparison + Troubleshooting

### Plasma vs. Gas Nitriding (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

**Left -- PLASMA (`#27AE60`):** N2+H2 (no NH3); 650--1050 F (wider); fully controllable CL (incl. NONE); stainless YES (sputters passive film); mechanical masking; 20--30% shorter; edge effect/hollow cathode risk; higher capital. Highlight: `Total compound layer control -- the only method that can produce ZERO white layer`.

**Right -- GAS (`#E8A020`):** NH3; 925--1050 F; limited CL control (two-stage reduces); stainless difficult; electroplate mask; 10--90+ hr; blind hole/circulation risk; lower capital; NH3 handling. Highlight: `Lower cost and simpler equipment -- the workhorse`.

### Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"--32.5"). 4 cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| ARCING / HOLLOW CATHODE | Small holes, sharp edges, parts too close | Min hole 3x depth; 10 mm spacing; ASPN |
| NON-UNIFORM CASE | Complex geometry; temp variation | Pulsed DC; supplemental heaters; multi-TC |
| EDGE EFFECT | Higher plasma density at corners | Generally acceptable; active screen minimizes |
| NO CL (WHEN SPECIFIED) | Gas ratio too H2-rich; temp too high | Increase N2 %; reduce temperature |
