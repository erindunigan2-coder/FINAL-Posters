## Phase 5 -- Bath Composition + Ratio

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY -- THE THREE ESSENTIALS`.

### Step 7 -- Three component boxes

| Component | X | Accent | Conc |
|---|---|---|---|
| ZINC METAL | 0.5" | `#27AE60` | 10--14 g/L |
| CAUSTIC (NaOH) | 8.0" | `#2EC4B6` | 100--140 g/L |
| BRIGHTENER | 15.5" | `#E8A020` | Per TDS |

Each: W: ~7.33", H: 3.0", fill `#1E2435`, accent left.

Zinc: Source ZnO in NaOH. Low = dull. High = rough/burn.
Caustic: Dissolves ZnO, conductivity. Low = poor throw. High = pitting.
Brightener: Proprietary. Low = dull. High = stress.

## Phase 6 -- Defect Grid

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS`.

3x2 grid, each card W: 7.33" H: 2.3".

| Pos | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | DULL DEPOSIT | `#2EC4B6` | Low Zn or brightener | Add ZnO; Hull cell |
| R1C2 | BURNING (HCD) | `#E8A020` | High Zn, high CD, low NaOH | Reduce CD; adjust ratio |
| R1C3 | PITTING | `#E05C5C` | Organic contamination | Carbon treat |
| R2C1 | DARK LCD | `#E05C5C` | Metal contam (Cu, Pb) | Dummy plate 2--5 ASF |
| R2C2 | ROUGH/GRAINY | `#E8A020` | Particulate, torn anode bags | Inspect bags; filtration |
| R2C3 | POOR THROWING | `#2EC4B6` | Low NaOH or high ratio | Increase caustic |

## Phase 7 -- Hull Cell + Contamination

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Hull Cell (W: 11.0"):**
Conditions: `267 mL | 2 A | 5 min | 75 F`.
5-segment panel strip: HCD (`#E8A020` burned) -> optimal (`#27AE60` bright) -> LCD (`#3A4055` dull).
Note: `Good ANC zinc: bright 60--70% of width. LCD slightly dull is normal.`

**Right -- Contamination (W: 11.5"):**

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | > 5 ppm | Dark LCD, black spots |
| Lead | > 2 ppm | Dark LCD, brittle |
| Iron | > 50 ppm | Rough, dull |
| Chromium | > 1 ppm | Dull, poor coverage |
| Organic | visible | Pitting, poor adhesion |
| Carbonate | > 30 g/L | Reduced conductivity |

Carbonate note: `Freeze-out at < 35 F (2 C) to remove excess` `#2EC4B6`.
