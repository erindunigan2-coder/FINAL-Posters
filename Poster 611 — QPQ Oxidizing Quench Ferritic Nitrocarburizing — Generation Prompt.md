## Phase 4 -- Q-P-Q Three-Step Hero

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `THE Q-P-Q SEQUENCE -- THREE STEPS TO EXCEPTIONAL CORROSION RESISTANCE`.

Three large step cards with arrows between:

**Q1 -- First Oxidizing Quench (Amber):** NaNO3/NaNO2 salt, 700--800 F, 15--30 min. Part from FNC bath (1075 F) into oxidizing salt. Oxidizing salt reacts with porous compound zone. Iron oxidizes to magnetite (Fe3O4) -- hard, adherent, black. Fills pores, creating sealed surface. Appearance after Q1: black, slightly rough.

**P -- Polish (Teal):** Buffing, lapping, or centerless grinding. Target Ra 8--16 micro-inch. Removes outermost rough oxide. EXPOSES fresh compound zone for Q2. Polish quality directly affects final corrosion. CRITICAL: Do NOT grind through the compound zone.

**Q2 -- Second Oxidizing Quench (Amber):** SAME bath, 700--800 F, 15--30 min. Fresh compound zone receives new magnetite layer. Better adhesion on smooth surface. Combined Q1+P+Q2 = multi-layer corrosion barrier. Final appearance: uniform matte black.

## Phase 6 -- Polish Specs + Magnetite Mechanism

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Polish Specification (Teal):** Target Ra 8--16 micro-inch. Methods: mechanical buffing, centerless grinding, lapping, vibratory finishing. Rules: do NOT remove compound zone; polish quality = corrosion quality; verify Ra with profilometer; clean after polish. Common mistakes: over-polishing (through zone), under-polishing (rough = poor Q2), contamination into Q2 bath.

**Right -- Magnetite Mechanism (Amber):** Compound zone is porous -- corrosive media penetrates. Oxidizing quench: NaNO3/NaNO2 at 700--800 F oxidizes pore walls to magnetite (Fe3O4). Magnetite is hard (580--630 HV), adherent, black, corrosion-resistant, space-filling. Result: sealed compound zone. Why two quenches? Q1 fills initial pores, polish exposes fresh surface, Q2 seals polished surface. Why beats hard chrome: chrome micro-cracks under stress; Q-P-Q has no cracks -- oxide-sealed zone is continuous.
