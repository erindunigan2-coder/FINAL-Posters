Mechanism inset (3 steps):
1. ALUMINUM DISSOLVES: Al -> Al3+ + 3e-
2. CHROMATE REDUCES: Cr2O7(2-) + 14H+ + 6e- -> 2Cr3+ + 7H2O
3. GEL FORMS: Cr3+ + Al3+ + oxide + RESIDUAL Cr6+ -> mixed oxide gel

`The gel contains BOTH Cr3+ (barrier) and Cr6+ (self-healing reservoir)` amber.

## Phase 5 -- Self-Healing + Bath Chemistry

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Self-Healing:** Emerald accent. Two-state cross-section: intact gel with Cr6+ dots vs. damaged with Cr6+ migrating to scratch. `10--30% of total Cr is hexavalent` | `Cr6+ migrates to exposed Al` | `Reduces to Cr3+ oxide -- resealing damage` | `NO OTHER CONVERSION COATING HAS THIS PROPERTY` | `Trivalent CANNOT self-heal`.

**Right -- Bath Chemistry:** Amber accent.

| Component | Concentration | Function |
|---|---|---|
| CrO3 or Na2Cr2O7 | 3--6 g/L (as Cr6+) | Oxidizing agent, film-forming |
| K3Fe(CN)6 | 0.5--1.5 g/L | Accelerator |
| NaF or HF | 0.5--1.5 g/L as F- | Activator |
| H3PO4 (optional) | 1--3 g/L | Phosphate-bearing films |
| pH | 1.2--2.0 | MASTER VARIABLE |

`pH is the single most important control. Below 1.2: over-attack. Above 2.0: no coating.`

## Phase 6 -- Film Properties + Color Guide

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Film Properties:** Emerald accent. Clear to gold to golden-brown | 0.25--1.0 um | 10--40 mg/ft2 | Cr6+ 10--30% | Low electrical resistance | SST bare 168--336 hr | SST painted 1000--3000+ hr | Degrades above 140 F.

**Right -- Color Interpretation:** Amber accent. Clear/iridescent = Class 3 (low resistance) | Pale gold = standard | Gold = Class 1A | Golden-brown = heavy | Brown/dark = check if too thick | Rainbow = normal on mixed alloys. `Color is a GUIDE, not a spec.
