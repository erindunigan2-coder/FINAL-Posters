## Phase 4 -- Two-Rule Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `TWO RULES -- NO EXCEPTIONS`.

Two large side-by-side panels, each H: 8.5":

**Left -- Rule 1: No Hot Water (accent `#E05C5C`):** Title: `NO HOT WATER` 36pt `#E05C5C`. `Ambient to 80 F (27 C) ONLY`. Film is thin (0.25--1.0 um) and amorphous; hot water causes thermal shock = micro-cracking, delamination, poor seal adhesion. `NEVER rinse freshly phosphated parts with water above 80 F.` `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Rule 2: No Delay (accent `#E8A020`):** Title: `NO DELAY` 36pt `#E8A020`. `Phosphate to rinse: IMMEDIATELY`. Wet film oxidizes in air = flash rust (orange-brown staining). Automated lines: conveyor must not allow dwell > 30 seconds. `Parts should move from phosphate to rinse without pause.` `#E8A020`

---

## Phase 5 -- Temperature Impact

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHY TEMPERATURE MATTERS`.

Full-width panel, accent `#E05C5C`. Horizontal temperature bar:

| Range | Fill | Label | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ambient--80 F | `#27AE60` | SAFE | Film preserved; no thermal stress |
| 80--120 F | `#E8A020` | CAUTION | Risk of micro-cracking on thinnest films |
| > 120 F | `#E05C5C` | DAMAGE | Thermal shock; delamination; poor seal adhesion |

`The post-coat rinse is the ONLY rinse in this process with a strict upper temperature limit.`

Rinse quality params: `pH: 6.5--8.0` / `Overflow: 1--2 gal/min` / `Rinse must be clean -- contaminated rinse = staining`.

---

## Phase 6 -- Flash Rust Prevention

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `FLASH RUST -- THE SILENT KILLER`.

**Left -- What Is Flash Rust (accent `#E05C5C`):** Orange-brown staining on wet phosphate film within minutes. Causes: long dwell, high humidity, slow conveyor, poor air circulation.

**Right -- Prevention (accent `#27AE60`):** Speed up line, improve ventilation, ensure continuous overflow, consider air knife. `Once flash rust forms, the part must be re-cleaned and re-phosphated. There is no touch-up.` `#E05C5C`

---

## Phase 7 -- Parameters & Monitoring

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `POST-COAT RINSE PARAMETERS`.

| Parameter | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | Ambient to 80 F | STRICT upper limit |
| pH | 6.5--8.0 | Neutral |
| Conductivity | < 500 uS/cm | Contaminated rinse = staining |
| Overflow | 1--2 gal/min | Clean water essential |
| Transfer time | < 30 sec from phosphate | Prevent flash rust |

Downstream note: `A good post-coat rinse protects BOTH the phosphate coating AND the seal rinse chemistry.` `#E8A020`
