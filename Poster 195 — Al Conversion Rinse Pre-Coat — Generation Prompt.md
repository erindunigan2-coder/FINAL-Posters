### Step 6 -- Mineral competition diagram (Y: 9.0" to 15.0")

Two panels:

**Left -- WHY TAP WATER FAILS** (accent `#E05C5C`):
The Ti/Zr reaction deposits a 20--100 nm film by pH-driven hydrolysis. Dissolved minerals compete: Ca2+/Mg2+ deposit CaCO3/MgO; Fe2+/Fe3+ create iron oxide inclusions; SO4 2-/Cl- interfere with film uniformity. Invisible but show up as poor adhesion, inconsistent XRF, premature corrosion.

**Right -- DI / RO WATER -- THE STANDARD** (accent `#27AE60`):
- DI: < 10 uS/cm (typ. < 5); negligible ions; all applications, aerospace mandatory
- RO: 10--50 uS/cm; very low ions; cost-effective alternative
- Either acceptable for most Ti/Zr lines

Bottom callout bar (amber): `A DI system costs pennies per gallon. A coating failure costs thousands in rework. The math is simple.`

## Phase 5 -- Transit Time Window

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `THE RE-OXIDATION CLOCK`.

Four time-step cards (W: 5.5", H: 5.0", fill `#1E2435`, top accent 4 pt):

| # | X | Time | Status | Accent | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | < 1 MINUTE | IDEAL | `#27AE60` | Fresh, active aluminum. Oxide just beginning to reform. Best coating adhesion. |
| 2 | 6.33" | 1--5 MINUTES | ACCEPTABLE | `#E8A020` | Thin oxide reforming. Zr deposition still effective. Aerospace target: < 5 min. |
| 3 | 12.16" | 5--15 MINUTES | MARGINAL | `#E05C5C` | Significant oxide regrowth. Coating thinner, less uniform. Adhesion compromised. |
| 4 | 18.0" | > 15 MINUTES | RE-DEOX NEEDED | `#E05C5C` | Oxide fully reformed. Must return to deoxidize. Coating will not adhere. |

Below cards: `Aluminum re-oxidizes in SECONDS in air or water. The deoxidize-to-coat transit is the tightest window in the entire line. Design your rack flow to minimize this gap.` (Inter Medium 14 pt `#E8A020`)
