## Phase 4 -- Cleaning Sequence (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section label: `PRE-SPRAY CLEANING SEQUENCE`.

4-step vertical flowchart. Each box: W: 23.0", H: 2.0", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`. Arrows: 3 pt `#3A4055`, downward.

| Step | Title | Shop Method | Field Method | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SOLVENT DEGREASE | Vapor degrease or aqueous alkaline clean | Solvent wipe (acetone or MEK) | No visible oil residue |
| 2 | ALKALINE WASH | Immersion/spray, 50-70 degC, pH 10-12, 5-15 min | Alkaline wipe or scrub | Rinse thoroughly |
| 3 | RINSE & DRY | Clean water rinse; forced air or oven dry | Solvent wipe to remove residue; air dry | No moisture at blast time |
| 4 | INSPECT | Water-break-free test (ASTM F22 equivalent) | Visual -- surface dry and contamination-free | Proceed to grit blast immediately |

Shop: `#F0EDE8`. Field: `#E8A020`. Verification: `#27AE60` JetBrains Mono.

## Phase 5 -- Contaminant ID + Field vs. Shop

### Contaminant ID (Y: 15.0"-21.0")

Section label: `WHAT KILLS ADHESION -- 6 CONTAMINANTS`. 3x2 grid, cards W: 7.33", accent `#E05C5C`.

| Contaminant | Source | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| OIL / GREASE | Machining fluids, fingerprints, lubricants | Coating delamination; porosity at interface |
| MOISTURE | Humidity, condensation, incomplete drying | Steam blisters during spray; porosity |
| MILL SCALE | Hot-rolled steel surface oxide | Flakes off -- catastrophic adhesion loss |
| RUST | Ambient corrosion on stored steel | Weak oxide layer -- coating bonds to rust, not metal |
| OLD COATINGS | Previous paint, thermal spray, or plating | Must be completely removed |
| SHOP DUST / GRIT | Airborne particles on cleaned surface | Inclusions at coating interface; porosity |

### Field vs. Shop (Y: 21.0"-27.5")

Section label: `SHOP CLEANING VS. FIELD CLEANING`.

**Left -- SHOP STANDARD (accent `#27AE60`):** Full immersion degrease, alkaline wash tank at temp, rinse tank, oven dry, water-break-free test, controlled environment. `The gold standard -- use whenever the part can come to the shop.`

**Right -- FIELD EXPEDIENT (accent `#E8A020`):** Solvent wipe, mechanical abrasion + solvent, air dry (verify no condensation), visual inspection, weather dependent. `Field work demands improvisation -- but never skip cleaning entirely.`

## Phase 6 -- Time Windows

Y: 27.5" to 32.5". Section label: `TIME-CRITICAL WINDOWS`. Four cards.

| Window | Time | Note |
|---|---|---|
| CLEAN TO BLAST | Same shift (ideally) | Minimize recontamination |
| BLAST TO SPRAY | < 4 hours (spec-dependent) | Some specs require < 2 hours |
| HUMIDITY CHECK | Substrate > 3 degC above dew point | Moisture condenses on cold surfaces |
| WARNING | Do NOT touch blasted surfaces | Wear clean lint-free gloves |
