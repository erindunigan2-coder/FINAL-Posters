## Phase 4 -- Four Methods Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.5". Section label: `FOUR CLEANING METHODS -- CHOOSE BY APPLICATION`.

Four columns, each rounded rect fill `#1E2435`.

**Solvent Wipe (accent `#2EC4B6`):** The Shop Floor Standard. Solvents: Acetone, MEK, IPA. Method: Two-rag technique. Pros: Simple, fast, portable. Cons: VOC, fire hazard. Best for: Touch-up, small batches.

**Alkaline Spray Wash (accent `#E8A020`):** Production Volume. Params: pH 10--12, 2--5%, 120--150 F, 60--120 sec. Pros: Consistent, high volume. Cons: Capital cost, waste management. Best for: Production lines.

**Vapor Degreasing (accent `#E05C5C`):** Declining Use. Solvents: TCE or nPB. Pros: Excellent cleaning. Cons: EPA regulated, hazardous. Best for: Precision cleaning (aerospace legacy).

**Aqueous Washer (accent `#27AE60`):** Environmentally Preferred. Params: Heated alkaline, 120--160 F. Pros: No VOC, effective on heavy oils. Cons: Slower, requires rinsing. Best for: Batch ops, environmental compliance.

## Phase 5 -- Two-Rag Method + Tack Cloth

Y: 15.8" to 20.8". Section label: `THE TWO-RAG METHOD AND TACK CLOTH`.

**Left -- `THE TWO-RAG METHOD -- DO IT RIGHT` (border `#2EC4B6`):**
7 numbered steps. Warning `#E05C5C`: `CRITICAL: The dry rag pass must happen while the surface is still wet. If the solvent dries first, you've moved the contamination, not removed it.`

**Right -- `TACK CLOTH -- BETWEEN COATS` (accent `#E8A020`):**
After sanding, dust remains. Tack cloth lifts dust. `USE LOW-TACK (SILICONE-FREE) CLOTHS` -- silicone causes fish-eye.

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". Section label: `WHEN CLEANING FAILS -- 6 COATING DEFECTS`. 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| FISH-EYE / CRATERING | `#E05C5C` | Silicone from tack cloth or gloves | Silicone-free tack cloth; nitrile gloves |
| OIL BLISTERING | `#E8A020` | Residual oils under primer | Two-rag method; verify with water-break |
| ADHESION LOSS | `#E05C5C` | Surface contamination under paint | Re-clean; UV inspection |
| DUST NIBS | `#E8A020` | Sanding dust not removed | Tack cloth after every sand |
| SOLVENT REDEPOSIT | `#E05C5C` | Dry rag pass too slow | Smaller sections; faster dry pass |
| MOLD RELEASE (PLASTIC) | `#2EC4B6` | Mold release not removed | IPA wipe + adhesion promoter |
