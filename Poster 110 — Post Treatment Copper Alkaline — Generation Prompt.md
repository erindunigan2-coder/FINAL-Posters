## Phase 4 -- Three-Path Decision Tree Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `WHERE DOES THE PART GO NEXT?`.

### Decision Tree Root (Y: 5.0")

Centered box: `COPPER STRIKE COMPLETE -- CHOOSE PATH`. Three arrows descend.

### Path 1 -- To Acid Copper (X: 0.5", W: 7.0", accent `#27AE60`)

Title: `PATH 1: TO ACID COPPER` -- `Most common route`.
Steps: Rinse (done) -> Acid dip (5--10% H2SO4, 15--30 sec) -> Rinse -> Enter acid copper.
Notes: Do NOT use HCl on copper surface | Transfer within 2 min | Typical: 0.5--2.0 mil bright acid Cu over 0.1--0.3 mil strike.

### Path 2 -- To Nickel (X: 8.0", W: 7.0", accent `#2EC4B6`)

Title: `PATH 2: TO NICKEL` -- `Common for decorative Cu-Ni-Cr`.
Steps: Rinse (done) -> Mild acid activation (3--5% H2SO4, 10--15 sec) -> Rinse -> Enter nickel bath.
Notes: Cu surface must be clean and active | Some shops skip dip if transfer is fast | Typical stack: Cu strike -> Ni -> Cr.

### Path 3 -- Standalone (X: 15.5", W: 8.0", accent `#E8A020`)

Title: `PATH 3: STANDALONE COPPER` -- `Rare -- copper is usually an undercoat`.
Steps: Rinse (done) -> Anti-tarnish dip (BTA 0.1--0.5%, 30--60 sec) -> Rinse (DI, gentle) -> Dry (120--140 F).
Notes: Cu tarnishes rapidly | BTA inhibits oxidation | For longer term: lacquer or clear coat | Applications: EMI shielding, thermal, solderability | Inspect per ASTM B734.

## Phase 5 -- Inspection + Final Rinse/Dry

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `INSPECTION AND FINAL RINSE`.

**Left -- Adhesion Testing (accent `#E05C5C`):**
Title: `ADHESION TESTING -- CATCH FAILURES HERE`.
- Bend test (ASTM B571): 180 degrees, no peeling
- Tape test: adhesive tape on scored surface, no Cu removal
- Thermal cycle: 300 F for 30 min, quench, no blistering
- When: every new setup, every substrate change, every process adjustment
- Zinc DC: test EVERY batch until stable

Bottom: `If adhesion fails, look backward: activation time (Poster #106), rinse quality (Poster #107), live entry (Poster #108).` -- `#E8A020`

**Right -- Final Rinse/Dry:**
Ambient DI preferred | Forced air or warm air (120--140 F) | Optional hot water rinse aids drying | Minimize time -- Cu tarnishes fast.

Note: `If copper must wait, apply anti-tarnish (BTA) or store in sealed bag with desiccant.` -- `#E8A020`

## Phase 6 -- Specifications + Common Failures

Y: 20.5" to 27.0".

**Left -- Common Failures:**

| Failure | Root Cause | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Cu peeling in acid Cu | Over-etching at Stage 3 | Entire stack fails |
| Tarnish before next step | Slow transfer; no anti-tarnish | Adhesion failure at Cu/Ni interface |
| Nickel peeling over Cu | Tarnished Cu; skipped activation | Decorative stack rejects |
| Blistering in thermal cycle | Immersion Cu under strike (no live entry) | Latent defect -- may not appear until use |
| Incomplete rinse before acid dip | Alkaline residue neutralizes dip | Acid dip ineffective |

**Right -- Applicable Specifications (accent `#E8A020`):**

| Spec | Coverage |
|---|---|
| ASTM B734 | Electrodeposited Cu for engineering |
| AMS 2418 | Copper plating |
| MIL-C-14550 | Copper plating (federal) |
| ASTM B571 | Adhesion testing |
| ASTM B487 | Thickness by cross-section |
| ASTM B567 | Thickness by beta backscatter |

Note: `No unique ASTM for alkaline non-cyanide copper specifically.`

## Phase 7 -- Transition Checklist + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Pre-Transition Checklist (accent `#27AE60`):**
- [ ] Copper deposit uniform color (pink to salmon)
- [ ] Adhesion tested per ASTM B571 -- PASS
- [ ] Thickness verified (X-ray or magnetic)
- [ ] Surface clean, rinsed, residue-free
- [ ] Next step identified (acid Cu / Ni / standalone)
- [ ] Acid dip prepared if needed
- [ ] Transfer time minimized -- copper does not wait

**Right -- Safety (accent `#2EC4B6`):**
Acid dip (H2SO4): corrosive, PPE | Anti-tarnish (BTA): low toxicity | Cu dust (handling): respiratory irritant | Wastewater: contains Cu, route to treatment | Standard plating shop PPE.
