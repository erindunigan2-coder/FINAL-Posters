## Phase 5 -- Rinse Parameters + Consequences

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameter Table (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Water type | DI preferred; city water acceptable |
| Temperature | Ambient |
| Stages | 1 minimum; 2-stage counterflow preferred |
| Flow rate | 2--5 gal/min |
| Immersion time | 30--60 sec |
| Agitation | Air or part movement |
| Conductivity target | < 200 uS/cm (tighter than pre-activation) |
| Drain time | 10--15 sec |
| Rinse criterion | Neutral pH, no acid residue |

**Right -- What Acid Does to the Bath (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):**
Title: `WHAT ACID DRAG-IN DOES` `#E05C5C`. Accent `#E05C5C`.
- 1. HCl drag-in lowers bath pH
- 2. Below pH 4.8: boric acid solubility drops
- 3. Boric acid precipitates as white crystals
- 4. Precipitated boric acid causes ROUGH deposits
- 5. Brightener performance degrades at low pH
- 6. Dark deposits appear at LCD areas

Recovery: `Raise pH with KOH or NaOH (0.5 units at a time). Filter boric acid precipitate. Hull cell after adjustment.` `#27AE60`

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-PLATE RINSE`.

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acid carry-over | pH drop in zinc bath; rough deposit | Rinse too short or single stage | Add counterflow stage; extend time |
| Boric acid crash | White crystals in zinc bath; roughness | Acid drag-in dropped pH below 4.8 | Raise pH; filter; Hull cell |
| Parts dry between rinse and plate | Flash oxide; poor zinc adhesion | Transfer time too long | Reduce transfer time; keep parts wet |
| High conductivity | Accumulating acid in rinse | Water flow too low or exhausted | Increase flow; dump and refill |

## Phase 7 -- Monitoring + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Monitoring (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Title: `MONITORING` `#2EC4B6`.
- Conductivity: check every 2 hours minimum
- pH paper: spot-check rinse water -- should be 5--7
- Visual: clear water, no cloudiness
- If zinc bath pH drifting down, this rinse is the first suspect
- Track rinse dump frequency -- increasing = increasing drag-out

**Right -- Safety (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
Title: `SAFETY NOTES` `#E8A020`.
- Rinse water is mildly acidic from acid drag-out
- HCl residue in rinse: corrosive to skin and eyes
- All overflow to waste treatment
- Wet floors: slip hazard
- Standard PPE: goggles, gloves, apron
