## Phase 5 -- Rinse Parameters + Chloride Callout

Y: 14.5" to 21.0". Section: `RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left -- Parameters:** DI preferred | Ambient | 2--5 gal/min | 1--2 stages | 30--60 sec | < 200 uS/cm.

**Right -- The Chloride Window (accent `#E8A020`):**
Acid copper Cl- band: 30--80 ppm. Below 30: brightener fails (hazy). Above 100: dull and streaky. HCl from activation is the #1 source of uncontrolled Cl- addition. One poorly rinsed rack can spike Cl- by 5--10 ppm.

Monitor: `Copper bath Cl- weekly by AgNO3 titration or ISE`.

## Phase 6 -- Common Problems

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-PLATE RINSE`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chloride spike | Dull, streaky at LCD | HCl drag-in | Improve rinse; switch to H2SO4 |
| Iron contamination | Roughness, dark spots | Fe from HCl on steel | Fresh rinse water; drag-out rinse |
| Insufficient rinsing | General haze | Low flow or no cascade | Add cascade; increase flow |
| Dry spots | Skip plating | Parts left too long | Minimize transfer; keep wet |
