## Phase 4 -- Drainage Orientation Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `PART ORIENTATION FOR SALT BATH IMMERSION`. Two-column layout.

**Left -- CORRECT** (Emerald accent): bore vertical, open side DOWN, tilt flat parts 10--15 deg, min 0.25" spacing. Note: `Proper drainage reduces salt carryover by 50--70%`.

**Right -- COMMON MISTAKES** (Coral accent): cupped parts facing up (trapped air = soft spot), parts nested/touching, flat parts horizontal, no drainage clearance.

Warning banner: `TRAPPED AIR = SOFT SPOTS. If salt cannot reach the surface, bainite cannot form there.`

## Phase 5 -- Fixture Materials + Transfer Speed

**Fixture Materials Table** (Y: 14.5" to 21.0"): RA 330 (2100 F, salt OK), Inconel 601 (2200 F, best), low-alloy hooks (1200 F, austempering only), carbon steel wire (1000 F, limited/disposable).

**15-Second Transfer Rule** (Y: 21.0" to 27.5"): From furnace door to last part submerged = 15 sec max. Transfer methods: robot 3--6 sec, conveyor 5--8 sec, crane 8--12 sec. Outcome table: < 15 sec = full bainite (Emerald) | 15--30 sec = mixed (Amber) | > 30 sec = pearlite/scrap (Coral).
