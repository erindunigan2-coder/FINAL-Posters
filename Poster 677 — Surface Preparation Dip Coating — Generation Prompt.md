## Phase 4 -- Three-Substrate Prep Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `SURFACE PREP BY SUBSTRATE TYPE`. Three columns.

**Steel Parts (`#E8A020`):** Alkaline clean, blast 80--120 grit SP-6 min, 1.5--3.0 mil profile, phosphate optional, primer per coating family. `Blast-to-prime within 4 hours.`

**Wire & Cable (`#2EC4B6`):** In-line alkaline spray + ultrasonic, DI rinse, hot air dry. No blasting. Line speed 100--1,000+ ft/min. `Drawing lubricant residue is the #1 adhesion killer.`

**Tool Handles/Consumer (`#27AE60`):** Degrease, blast/Scotch-Brite, adhesion promoter matched to coating. PVC: phenolic/polyester. PE: CPO or flame. Nylon: epoxy. `Flame treatment activates PE/PP surfaces.`

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| COATING PEELING | `#E05C5C` | Wrong primer | Match per matrix |
| BLISTERING AT INTERFACE | `#E05C5C` | Trapped moisture | Complete drying; pre-bake |
| RUST UNDER COATING | `#E8A020` | Insufficient blast | Blast to SP-6; add phosphate |
| FLASH RUST BEFORE PRIME | `#E8A020` | Blast-to-prime delay | Prime within 4 hours |
| THIN SPOTS / POOR WETTING | `#2EC4B6` | Oil contamination | Re-clean; water-break test |
| PRIMER ADHESION FAILURE | `#E05C5C` | Primer over contaminated surface | Clean, dry, re-prime |
