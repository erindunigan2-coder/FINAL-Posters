## Phase 5 -- Spacing Rules + Fixture Materials

### Spacing (Y: 13.5"--20.0")

Section label: `SPACING -- THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN UNIFORM AND REJECTED`.

**Left -- CORRECT (`#27AE60`):** 0.25 in min clearance; larger parts need more; orient for uniform gas flow; thin sections enter quench first; load map documented.

**Right -- INCORRECT (`#E05C5C`):** Parts touching = soft spots; nesting = uneven case; overloaded basket = non-uniform quench; no load map = no traceability; random orientation = distortion.

|---|---|---|---|

## Phase 6 -- Loading Mistakes

Y: 26.0"--32.5". Section label: `FOUR LOADING MISTAKES THAT COST LOADS`. 4 cards:

| Mistake | Consequence | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| PARTS TOUCHING | Soft spots at contact areas | 0.25 in gap; inspect before closing |
| OVERLOADED BASKET | Non-uniform quench; center cools slowly | Respect weight limits; test worst-case |
| WRONG QUENCH ORIENTATION | Thin sections warp; keyways crack | Thin section enters oil first; fixture to control |
| NO LOAD MAP | Cannot trace defect to position | Document every load; photo recommended |
