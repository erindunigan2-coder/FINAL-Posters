## Phase 5 -- Three Seal Options

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THREE SEAL OPTIONS -- CHOOSE FOR YOUR APPLICATION`.

**OIL (STANDARD)** (accent `#27AE60`): Water-displacing or light machine oil, 150--180 F, 2--5 min. Wet, oily, lustrous black. Best corrosion (24--100+ hr SST). First choice for most production.

**WAX** (accent `#E8A020`): Microcrystalline wax or polymer sealer, 150--200 F, 2--5 min. Dry, semi-lustrous. Good protection (slightly less than oil). Best for fasteners needing dry lubricity.

**LACQUER** (accent `#2EC4B6`): Clear lacquer or polymer, spray or dip after air drying. Dry, non-oily, uniform matte or gloss. Moderate protection (less pore penetration). For parts requiring dry, clean finish.

## Phase 7 -- MIL Spec Classes + Best Practices

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `MIL-DTL-13924 + BEST PRACTICES`.

**Left -- MIL Spec Classes:**

| Class | Process | Seal | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hot alkaline | Supplementary (per customer) | General purpose |
| 3 | Hot alkaline | Wax or oil (mandatory) | Defense / mil-spec |
| 4 | Hot alkaline | Phosphate sealant | Heavy-duty corrosion |

Note: `Class 2 (room temp) is NOT recommended.` `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Eight Best Practices:**
1. Oil immediately after hot rinse -- no delay
2. Oil temp: 150--180 F for best penetration
3. Drain excess oil (2--3 min drain)
4. Centrifuge small parts
5. Store in VCI paper or clean dry environment
6. Re-oil parts stored > 90 days
7. Dry finish needed? Wax or lacquer, not "no seal"
8. NEVER ship bare (unsealed) black oxide parts
