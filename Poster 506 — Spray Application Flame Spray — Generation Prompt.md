## Phase 4 -- Application Technique + Coating Properties (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section label: `SPRAY APPLICATION PROCEDURE`.

**6-Step Technique (Y: 5.0"-11.5"):** 2x3 grid. Each card: W: 7.3", H: 3.0", fill `#1E2435`, top accent `#27AE60`.

| Step | Title | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PREHEAT SUBSTRATE | Flame gun (no feedstock) to 80-120 degC. Reduces thermal shock. Verify with pyrometer or IR gun. |
| 2 | FIRST PASS -- BOND LAYER | Close standoff for max particle impact. Anchors everything above. Steady traverse -- no stopping on surface. |
| 3 | BUILD PASSES | 25-75 um per pass. Overlap 25-50% of spray footprint. Consistent standoff and traverse speed. |
| 4 | MONITOR TEMPERATURE | Check between passes. Keep below 150 degC (steel), 120 degC (aluminum). Compressed air cooling if needed. |
| 5 | IN-PROCESS THICKNESS CHECK | Mag gauge or eddy current on sacrificial tabs. Total range: 100-2000 um depending on application. |
| 6 | FINAL VISUAL CHECK | Uniform coverage, no bare spots, no blistering, no excessive orange peel. Consistent texture and color. |

## Phase 5 -- Wire vs. Powder Application + Thickness Guide

### Wire vs. Powder (Y: 15.5"-22.0")

Section label: `WIRE VS. POWDER -- APPLICATION DIFFERENCES`.

**Left -- WIRE APPLICATION (accent `#27AE60`):**
- Wire feed rate controls deposition: 1-8 m/min
- Watch for "spitting" -- unmelted wire fragments
- Fix: reduce wire speed or increase flame heat
- Compressed air atomizes molten wire tip
- Higher velocity (80-200 m/s) than powder
- Best for: Zn/Al corrosion coatings, dimensional repair

**Right -- POWDER APPLICATION (accent `#E8A020`):**
- Powder feed rate: 20-60 g/min via gravity or carrier gas
- Lower velocity (40-100 m/s) -- more porous deposits
- Adjust carrier gas for consistent powder plume
- Self-fluxing alloys (NiCrBSi): spray first, fuse later
- Fusing remelts at 1000-1100 degC -- eliminates porosity
- Best for: wear coatings, self-fluxing alloys, exotic materials

### Thickness Guide (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `COATING THICKNESS GUIDE`.

| Application | Per-Pass | Total | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zinc corrosion protection | 25-50 um | 100-350 um | Seal required; per AWS C2.18 |
| Aluminum corrosion protection | 25-50 um | 150-350 um | High-temp or marine service |
| NiCrBSi wear (as-sprayed) | 50-75 um | 250-1000 um | Will be fused in post-treatment |
| Dimensional repair (steel, bronze) | 50-75 um | 500-2000 um | Machine to final dimension |
| Babbitt bearing overlay | 50-75 um | 500-2000 um | Conventional machining to finish |

Highlight: `RULE OF THUMB: If you cannot measure thickness after 3 passes, something is wrong. Check standoff, feed rate, and gas pressures.` `#27AE60`.

## Phase 6 -- Defect Watch

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `DEFECT WATCH -- WHAT TO LOOK FOR DURING SPRAY`. Four cards, accent `#E05C5C`.

| Defect | Visual Cue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| SPITTING (WIRE) | Sparks or chunks in pattern; bumpy deposit | Reduce wire feed rate; verify neutral flame |
| SUBSTRATE OVERHEATING | Part discoloration; coating lifting; warping | Stop; cool with compressed air; increase traverse speed |
| UNEVEN THICKNESS | Ridges or valleys; inconsistent color | Check traverse speed; verify 25-50% overlap |
| DELAMINATION DURING SPRAY | Coating flaking off as new passes applied | Stop immediately; strip and re-blast; check contamination |
