## Phase 5 -- HE Risk During Activation

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT RISK AT THIS STAGE`.

**Left -- The Mechanism (accent `#E05C5C`):** Title: `WHAT HAPPENS IN 30 SECONDS`. 1. HCl attacks iron oxide (desired) / 2. HCl attacks bare metal (undesired) / 3. Liberates atomic H / 4. H diffuses into lattice / 5. Migrates to grain boundaries, recombines / 6. H2 trapped / 7. Pressure builds / Longer = more H = higher risk.

**Right -- Why HCl (accent `#E8A020`):** Title: `WHY HCl FOR STEEL?` Faster oxide dissolution / FeCl2 soluble -- no smut / H2SO4 forms insoluble FeSO4 smut / Faster = less H absorbed / Trade-off: more fume, needs ventilation.

## Phase 6 -- Operating Window

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `OPERATING WINDOW -- HARD LIMITS`.

| Parameter | Minimum | Maximum | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| HCl concentration | 10% v/v | 20% v/v | Higher = faster = less time needed |
| Temperature | Ambient | Ambient | NEVER heat acid for Cd activation |
| Time | 15 sec | 30 sec | HARD MAXIMUM -- timer mandatory |
| Agitation | None | Gentle rack | Do not over-agitate |

Below: `If not fully activated in 30 seconds: Remove. Rinse. Re-clean. Try again with fresh acid. DO NOT extend acid time past 30 seconds.`

## Phase 7 -- Common Errors

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `ACTIVATION ERRORS ON CADMIUM SUBSTRATES`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OVER-ACTIVATION (>30 sec) | No timer; distracted | Timer with alarm at tank |
| 2 | SPENT ACID | Too weak to activate in time | Replace on schedule; titrate |
| 3 | WRONG ACID | H2SO4 instead of HCl | Smut on steel; verify acid type |
| 4 | DELAY TO PLATING | Parts sit after activation | Plate within 5 min |
