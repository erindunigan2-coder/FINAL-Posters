## Phase 4 -- Dual-Bath Comparison Hero

Y: 4.2" to 15.5". Section: `TWO EN-B CHEMISTRIES -- DMAB vs. BOROHYDRIDE`.

**Left -- DMAB Bath (X: 0.5", W: 11.0", H: 9.5"):** Accent `#27AE60`. Title: `DMAB-BASED EN-B`. Subtitle: `Most Common Commercial`.

Reaction: `Ni2+ + (CH3)2NHBH3 + H2O -> Ni0 + (CH3)2NH + H3BO3 + ...`

| Component | Concentration | Role |
|---|---|---|
| NiCl2.6H2O or NiSO4 | 20--30 g/L Ni2+ | Metal source |
| DMAB ((CH3)2NHBH3) | 2--5 g/L | Reducing agent |
| Ethylenediamine (EDA) | 30--60 g/L | Complexant |
| NaOH | As needed | pH adjust |
| Thallium/lead acetate | 0.5--2 ppm | Stabilizer |

Operating: pH 6.0--8.0, 60--75 C (140--167 F), 10--20 um/hr, 0.5--3 wt% B, 3--5 MTO.
Deposit: 700--800 HV as-plated, 1000--1200 HV HT, CoF 0.08--0.12, 200--500 hrs NSS.

**Right -- Borohydride Bath (X: 12.0", W: 11.5", H: 9.5"):** Accent `#E8A020`. Title: `BOROHYDRIDE-BASED EN-B`. Subtitle: `Higher Boron -- Higher Hardness -- Harder to Control`.

Reaction: `Ni2+ + NaBH4 + ... -> Ni0 + B0 + ...`

| Component | Concentration | Role |
|---|---|---|
| NiCl2.6H2O | 20--30 g/L Ni2+ | Metal source |
| NaBH4 | 0.5--1.5 g/L | Reducing agent |
| Ethylenediamine | 40--80 g/L | Complexant |
| NaOH | 40--90 g/L | pH control |
| Thallium nitrate | 1--5 ppm | Stabilizer |

Operating: pH 12.0--14.0, 90--95 C (194--203 F), 20--30 um/hr, 3--8 wt% B, 2--4 MTO.
Deposit: 750--850 HV as-plated, 1100--1300 HV HT, CoF 0.05--0.10, 300--600 hrs NSS.

Warning: `NaBH4 is EXTREMELY flammable. Explosive H2 evolution on contact with acid. pH 12--14. Full PPE required.` `#E05C5C`.

## Phase 6 -- Bath Stability Management

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `BATH STABILITY -- THE EN-B CHALLENGE`.

**Left -- Decomposition Risk (accent `#E05C5C`):**
- DMAB decomposes slowly at temp even without catalytic surface
- Never idle at temp without load
- More susceptible to Fe/Cu than EN-P
- Under-loaded (<0.1 dm2/L) = highest risk
- Decomposition is EXOTHERMIC -- can be violent
- Warning: `NEVER leave EN-B bath at operating temperature without load`

**Right -- Stability Rules (accent `#27AE60`):**
1. Cool to <40 C when not in use
2. Maintain stabilizer per protocol
3. Filter continuously (1--5 um)
4. Loading: 0.2--0.5 dm2/L optimal
5. Check stabilizer every shift
6. Inspect heater elements -- hot spots nucleate decomposition
7. NiCl2 preferred over NiSO4 in many formulations
8. Borate accumulates -- monitor viscosity
