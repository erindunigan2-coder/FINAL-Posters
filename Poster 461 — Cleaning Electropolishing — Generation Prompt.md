## Phase 4 -- Pre-Finish Decision Guide + Cleaning Sequence (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5".

Section label: `SURFACE PREPARATION + CLEANING SEQUENCE`

**Pre-Finish Decision Guide (Amber accent):**
Title: `BEFORE YOU CLEAN -- IS THE SURFACE READY FOR EP?`

```
[ ] Machining marks: 120--240 grit or finer. Heavy marks will NOT be removed by EP.
[ ] Welds: Ground smooth and blended BEFORE EP. EP highlights weld defects.
[ ] Heat tint: Remove mechanically or by pickling BEFORE EP.
[ ] Scratches: Deep scratches (> 25 um) survive EP. Mechanical polish first.
[ ] Alloy grade: Avoid 303 SS -- MnS inclusions cause pitting during EP.
[ ] Casting porosity: EP opens pores. Consider mechanical polish only.
```

Rule of thumb: `EP improves Ra by 50--75%. Starting at Ra 0.8 um -> expect 0.2--0.4 um. Starting at Ra 3.2 um -> EP alone will NOT reach 0.5 um.`

**Four-Step Cleaning Sequence cards:**

| Step | Accent | Title | Parameters | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#2EC4B6` | ALK SOAK CLEAN | 50--70 C, 5--15 min, soak/ultrasonic | Remove oils, grease, shop soils |
| 2 | `#2EC4B6` | RINSE | Ambient, flowing, 30--60 sec | Remove cleaner residue |
| 3 | `#E8A020` | ACID DIP (OPTIONAL) | HCl 10--20% or HNO3, ambient, 1--5 min | Remove scale, oxide, heat tint |
| 4 | `#2EC4B6` | RINSE | Ambient, flowing, 30--60 sec | Remove acid before EP tank |

## Phase 5 -- What EP Reveals

Y: 14.5" to 22.0".

Section label: `WHAT ELECTROPOLISHING REVEALS`

**Left panel -- CLEANING REMOVES THESE (Emerald accent):**
- Oils, greases, fingerprints
- Shop soils and cutting fluids
- Light surface oxide (via acid dip)
- Water stains and mineral deposits
- Residual cleaning compounds

**Right panel -- EP WILL EXPOSE THESE (Coral accent):**
- Deep machining marks (> 120 grit)
- Weld spatter, undercut, discoloration
- Heat tint (welding or heat treatment)
- MnS inclusions (303 SS -- causes pitting)
- Embedded particles (grinding debris)
- Grain boundary etching (sensitized material)

Key principle highlight (Emerald): `Electropolishing does not create a finish -- it reveals and improves the metallurgical surface. The starting condition IS the limiting factor.`

## Phase 6 -- Substrate Cautions + Fixturing

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Substrate Caution Cards (2x2 grid):**

| Substrate | Accent | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| 303 SS (FREE-MACHINING) | `#E05C5C` | MnS inclusions dissolve during EP, leaving pits. AVOID. Specify 304 or 316. |
| WELDED ASSEMBLIES | `#E8A020` | Grind welds smooth before EP. Matching filler metal. HAZ etches differently. |
| 400-SERIES SS | `#E8A020` | May need glycerol in electrolyte. More sensitive to etching. Consult supplier. |
| CAST PARTS | `#E05C5C` | Porosity and inclusions common. EP opens pores. Mechanical polish may be better. |

**Fixturing cards:**

| Topic | Key Points |
|---|---|
| ELECTRICAL CONTACT | Contact point will NOT be polished -- witness mark remains. Cu or Ti fixtures. Minimize contact area. Place in non-critical areas. |
| CATHODE & SPACING | Cu, Pb, or 316 SS cathode. Cathode:anode ratio 1:2 to 1:1. Part spacing 50--150 mm. Avoid shielding. |
