## Phase 4 -- Prior Microstructure (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `PRIOR MICROSTRUCTURE -- THE HIDDEN VARIABLE`.

Four side-by-side panels (W 5.5", H 7.0"):

| Panel | Structure | Accent | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FINE Q&T (Tempered Martensite) | `#27AE60` | IDEAL | Fine, uniform. Austenitizes rapidly under induction. Most consistent case. |
| 2 | FINE PEARLITE (Normalized) | `#27AE60` | GOOD | Fine lamellar. Slightly slower than Q&T. Acceptable for most applications. |
| 3 | COARSE PEARLITE (Annealed) | `#E8A020` | CAUTION | Coarse lamellar. Needs higher temp or longer time. Risk of incomplete transformation. |
| 4 | BANDED / SEGREGATED | `#E05C5C` | PROBLEM | Chemical segregation from hot working. Non-uniform carbon. Hard/soft bands in case. |

Each has metallographic image placeholder.

Full-width callout below: `Induction heating is FAST (100--1000 F/sec). No time for slow diffusion to homogenize. What you start with is what you get -- magnified. A furnace has minutes to hours. Induction has seconds.`

## Phase 6 -- Surface Checklist + Decarb Warning

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Surface Condition GO/NO-GO:**

| Item | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Scale-free | GO | Scale interferes with EM coupling -- soft spots |
| Rust-free | GO | Same as scale |
| Oil/grease-free | GO | Burns off but creates smoke and uneven heating |
| No copper stop-off needed | NOTE | Induction heats only where coil is -- coil IS the mask |
| Proper prior micro | GO | Fine pearlite or Q&T; verify first-article |
| Dimensional check | GO | Must be in tolerance BEFORE hardening (distortion moves dimensions) |
| Deburr sharp edges | GO | Sharp edges overheat (current concentration) -- can crack |

GO = `#27AE60`, NOTE = `#E8A020`.

**Right -- Decarburization Warning (Coral):**

`DECARBURIZATION -- THE SILENT KILLER`. Decarb surface CANNOT harden to spec. Carbon loss during prior heat treat or hot working. If surface carbon < ~0.35%, no amount of power produces 58+ HRC. How to check: microhardness traverse, nital etch (lighter layer), carbon analysis. Sources: normalizing without atmosphere, hot forging, prolonged storage of hot-rolled bar. Fix: machine off decarb layer (if tolerance allows) or reject lot. `Decarb is invisible. Always verify surface carbon on first articles from new lots.`
