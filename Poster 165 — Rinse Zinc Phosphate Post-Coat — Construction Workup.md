# Poster #165 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Zinc Phosphate -- Post-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. This rinse removes soluble zinc phosphate salts and acid residues from the freshly coated surface before sealing. The critical rule: prompt transfer. Extended dwell time between the phosphate bath and this rinse causes white salt efflorescence on the crystalline coating -- visible, damaging, and a common cause of seal and paint adhesion failures.

Two-stage counter-flow rinsing is standard for automotive lines, the same principle as the pre-condition rinse (Poster #161) but now protecting the seal chemistry downstream.

## Design Notes

The efflorescence warning is the unique value of this poster. Most rinse posters are "add clean water" -- this one has a time-critical element that makes it operationally significant. The before/after efflorescence panels in Zone 4 are the visual hook. The prompt transfer callout in the hero zone must be in coral with enough visual weight to catch attention from across the room. The counter-flow reinforcement is repetition from Poster #161, but deliberate -- operators who skip Poster #161 still get the concept here.
