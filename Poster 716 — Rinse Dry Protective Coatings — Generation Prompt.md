## Phase 4 -- Dual-Track Drying (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `DRYING TIMELINES -- STEEL VS. CONCRETE`. Two stacked timelines.

**Top -- Steel (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Timeline: 0-4 hr `#27AE60` PRIME NOW | 4-8 hr `#E8A020` CAUTION | 8+ hr `#E05C5C` RE-BLAST. After salt wash: dry fully BEFORE blast. After blast: NO RINSE, prime before flash rust.

**Bottom -- Concrete (accent `#E8A020`):** Timeline: 0-24 hr `#E05C5C` TOO WET | 1-7 days `#E8A020` TEST MOISTURE | 7+ days (new: 28+) `#27AE60` LIKELY READY -- VERIFY. After wash/etch: min 24 hr. New concrete: 28+ days. ALWAYS test F2170 < 75% RH or F1869 < 3 lb.

Contrast callout: `Steel is hours. Concrete is days. Never apply the steel timeline to concrete.`

## Phase 5 -- Dew Point + Concrete Drying

### Dew Point (Y: 14.5"-21.0")

Section label: `DEW POINT -- THE 5 DEG F RULE`. Big stat: `5 deg F` 72pt `#E8A020`. Surface temp > dew point + 5 F. On steel: flash rust/adhesion failure. On concrete: adds moisture. On uncured epoxy: amine blush.

Methods: Sling psychrometer ASTM E337, digital dew point meter, IR surface thermometer. `Check every shift and whenever conditions change. Stop if conditions deteriorate.`

### Concrete Drying (Y: 21.0"-27.0")

Section label: `CONCRETE DRYING -- WHY IT TAKES SO LONG`. Two columns.

Left -- Variables: Slab thickness (1 month per inch rule), ambient RH, vapor barrier below, mix water-cement ratio, temperature, air circulation.

Right -- Moisture Migration: Even "dry" concrete transmits vapor from ground. Without barrier: continuous. Coating must block (risk blistering) or breathe (limited resistance). `If MVER > 3 lb: DO NOT coat. Address source first.`

## Phase 6 -- Steel Window + Mistakes

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Two columns.

Left -- Blast-to-Coat Window: < 50% RH = 8 hr. 50-80% = 4 hr. > 80% = 1-2 hr. Rain/dew = zero. Cross-ref Poster #707.

Right -- Drying Mistakes: Coat concrete without moisture test (blistering). Blasted steel overnight (flash rust). Ignore dew point (invisible condensation). Assume 14-day concrete is dry (likely > 75% RH). Coat over standing water (immediate failure).
