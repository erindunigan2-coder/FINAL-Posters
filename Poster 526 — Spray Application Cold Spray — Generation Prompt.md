## Phase 3 -- Application Technique (HERO)

Y: 2.9" to 14.0". Section label: `APPLICATION TECHNIQUE`.

**Left -- 6 Application Steps (W: 14.5"):**

Six step cards, vertically stacked with arrows. Each: W: 14.0", H: 1.4", fill `#1E2435`, left accent 4pt.

| Step | Accent | Title | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#27AE60` | NO SUBSTRATE PREHEAT | Proceed directly -- no warming passes (unlike plasma, HVOF, flame) |
| 2 | `#2EC4B6` | VERIFY PARAMETERS | Confirm gas pressure, temp, powder feed, standoff, traverse at setpoint |
| 3 | `#E8A020` | FIRST PASS -- INTERFACE LAYER | Critical -- establishes bonding. Adiabatic shear creates metallurgical bond |
| 4 | `#27AE60` | BUILD THICKNESS | 50--500 um/pass. Virtually unlimited total -- multi-mm additive buildup |
| 5 | `#2EC4B6` | MONITOR DEPOSITION | Track thickness on sacrificial tabs or reference coupons |
| 6 | `#E8A020` | COMPLETE SPRAY | Verify total thickness meets spec. Allow natural cooling |

**Right -- "No Preheat" Callout (W: 8.0"):**

Y: 3.8" to 8.5". Emerald-tinted glass card.
Stat: `0 C` Barlow Condensed 800, 60pt, `#27AE60`. Label: `Substrate preheat required`.

Comparison (JetBrains Mono 14pt):
```
Plasma Spray:    80--120 C preheat
HVOF:            60--100 C preheat
Flame Spray:     80--120 C preheat
Cold Spray:      NONE
```
"NONE" in `#27AE60`, others at 60%.

Note: `Substrate stays cool because particles are solid -- no latent heat of fusion at impact.`

**Thickness Callout (Y: 9.0"--13.5"):** Amber-accented card.
Title: `UNLIMITED THICKNESS`.
Body: `Cold spray deposits build compressive stress, not tensile. Coatings do not delaminate as thickness increases -- enabling multi-mm buildups. Other thermal spray limited to ~2 mm before tensile stress failure.`
Stat: `50--500 um/pass` JetBrains Mono 18pt `#E8A020`.

**Material limitation (below table):** Coral-bordered banner.
`LIMITATION: Cold spray can only deposit DUCTILE metals. Ceramics (Al2O3, Cr2O3, YSZ) CANNOT be cold sprayed -- they shatter on impact instead of plastically deforming.` in `#E05C5C`.

## Phase 5 -- Applications + Defects + Footer

**Left -- Key Applications (W: 11.5", 2x2 grid):**

Section label: `KEY APPLICATIONS`.

| Application | Accent | Details |
|---|---|---|
| DIMENSIONAL RESTORATION | `#2EC4B6` | Rebuild corroded aerospace components. Deposit into machined damage, machine flush. |
| CONDUCTIVITY COATINGS | `#E8A020` | Cu cold spray for electrical/thermal. 80--95% IACS as-sprayed. |
| ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING | `#27AE60` | Near-net-shape preforms. Multi-mm deposits. Compressive stress enables unlimited buildup. |
| CORROSION PROTECTION | `#C8D0D8` | Zn or Al on steel. Dense, low-porosity alternative to arc/flame spray. |

**Right -- Common Defects (W: 11.0", 5 stacked cards):**

Section label: `COMMON DEFECTS`.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| LOW DE (bouncing) | `#E05C5C` | Velocity below critical | Increase pressure/temp; switch to He |
| POROSITY | `#E8A020` | Insufficient velocity; wrong powder | Increase pressure; gas-atomized spherical |
| POOR ADHESION | `#E05C5C` | Contamination; oxide film | Reclean; reduce clean-to-spray time |
| NOZZLE CLOGGING | `#E8A020` | Powder buildup on throat | Clean; reduce gas temp; WC-Co nozzle |
| SURFACE ROUGHNESS | `#2EC4B6` | Large particles or low velocity | Reduce powder size; increase velocity |
