## Phase 6 -- What Gets Made + Troubleshooting

### What Gets Made (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `WHAT GETS MADE FROM PRE-PAINTED COIL`. 2x3 grid of cards (W: 7.33", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`).

| Card | Accent | Title | Body |
|---|---|---|---|
| BUILDING PANELS | `#E8A020` | Roofing, siding, wall panels. PVDF for 30-40 yr color retention. SMP for 25-30 yr economy. |
| APPLIANCES | `#2EC4B6` | Refrigerator panels, washer/dryer housings. Polyester or PU for formability. |

### Troubleshooting (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four cards (W: 5.5", H: 2.7", left accent `#E05C5C`).

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| COATING CRACKING AFTER FORMING | Insufficient flexibility; overcure | Verify T-bend; check PMT; switch chemistry |
| DFT VARIATION ACROSS WIDTH | Uneven nip pressure or roll deflection | Check roll alignment; adjust nip pressure profile |
| POOR ADHESION | Conversion coating failure or cleaner residue | Check coating weight; verify oil removal < 5 mg/m2 |
| COLOR SHIFT BETWEEN COILS | Batch variation or PMT drift | Verify Delta E < 1.0; monitor PMT continuously |
