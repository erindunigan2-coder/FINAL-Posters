# Poster #166 -- Construction Workup
## Seal / Post Treatment -- Zinc Phosphate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

The final stages of the zinc phosphate line: seal rinse, DI final rinse, and dry-off oven. This poster is heavier than its iron phosphate counterpart (Poster #158) because the seal chemistry is more consequential -- zinc phosphate specifications (MIL-DTL-16232, automotive OEM) often mandate specific seal types. The chrome vs. non-chrome transition is the same industry story, but the stakes are higher: mil-spec Class 2 explicitly requires chromic acid seal, which puts military zinc phosphate shops in a regulatory bind.

The poster also covers the full MIL-DTL-16232 classification system (Type M/Z/ZM, Class 1-4) and the three major automotive OEM specs.

## Design Notes

The MIL-DTL-16232 classification matrix is the unique value proposition of this poster. Every military zinc phosphate shop needs to know the Type/Class system, and having it on the wall eliminates the most common spec interpretation question. The chrome seal dilemma (military mandate vs. regulatory restriction) is a live industry tension that gives this poster timely relevance beyond the iron phosphate version.

The DI rinse stage is often overlooked -- some sealers skip it, some require it. The "check supplier TDS" callout is deliberately placed to prevent operators from making assumptions.
