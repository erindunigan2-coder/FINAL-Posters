## Phase 5 -- Holiday Detection Guide

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `HOLIDAY DETECTION -- VERIFYING SEAL COAT INTEGRITY`.

**Left -- 6-Step Procedure (W: 14.0"):**

| Step | Accent | Action | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#2EC4B6` | VERIFY SEAL FULLY CURED | Uncured seal gives false positives |
| 2 | `#E8A020` | WET SPONGE WITH TAP WATER | Saturate; squeeze excess; uniformly damp |
| 3 | `#2EC4B6` | SET DETECTOR VOLTAGE | 9--90 V DC per NACE SP0188 or ASTM D5162 |
| 4 | `#E8A020` | GROUND CLIP TO BARE METAL | Clean metal contact to substrate |
| 5 | `#27AE60` | TRAVERSE SEALED SURFACE | 150--300 mm/s; full contact; overlapping passes |
| 6 | `#E05C5C` | MARK ALL HOLIDAYS | Audible alarm = pinhole; chalk mark; repair and re-test |

**Right -- WHY HOLIDAYS MATTER (`#E05C5C`):** A holiday is a pinhole where moisture reaches porous TSC. Even one holiday allows water through interconnected porosity to steel. Zinc still provides cathodic protection, but seal system has failed and service life is reduced. Stat: `ZERO HOLIDAYS` -- acceptance criterion for all sealed systems.

## Phase 6 -- Documentation + Reject Criteria

### Documentation Checklist (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `QA DOCUMENTATION -- AWS C2.18 REQUIREMENTS`. 10-item checklist in 2 columns:

| Item | Record |
|---|---|
| Surface preparation | Blast media, grit size, Ra, cleanliness standard, ambient conditions |
| Time between blast and spray | Humidity and temperature at time of spray |
| Wire feedstock | Material, diameter, lot number, certificate of conformance |
| Spray parameters | Voltage, amperage, wire speed, air pressure, standoff |
| Coating thickness | DFT per SSPC-PA 2; average and minimum reported |
| Seal coat application | Type, manufacturer, method, time spray-to-seal, cure time |
| Holiday detection | Method, voltage, results (pass/fail), repairs |
| Bond strength (if tested) | Method, number of tests, individual and average results |
| Visual inspection | Inspector, date, accept/reject, observations |
| Weather conditions (field) | Temp, RH, dew point, wind speed per AWS C2.18 |

Callout: `AWS C2.18 requires full documentation of every step. If the inspector cannot verify it from records, it was not done.` `#2EC4B6`.

### Reject Criteria (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `REJECT CONDITIONS -- STRIP AND RE-SPRAY`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| THICKNESS BELOW MINIMUM | Add spray to deficient areas and re-measure. If bond compromised, strip and re-spray. |
| BOND STRENGTH BELOW 7 MPa | Strip completely; investigate root cause (contamination, stale blast, moisture); re-blast and re-spray. |
| HOLIDAYS IN SEALED SYSTEM | Re-apply seal; re-test. If persistent, strip seal, investigate porosity, re-seal. |
| BARE SPOTS OR DELAMINATION | Strip affected area; re-blast to profile; re-spray. Investigate contamination or time window. |
