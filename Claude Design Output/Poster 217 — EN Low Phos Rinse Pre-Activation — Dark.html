A 3-stage cascade uses 90% less water than a single flowing rinse. Target: <50 µS/cm conductivity at final stage exit before activation.
Rinse Parameters
ParameterValue
Water sourceDI/RO preferred; city <200 ppm TDS acceptable
TemperatureAmbient 18–30°C
Flow rate2–5 gal/min per stage
Number of stages2–3 counterflow minimum
Immersion time30–60 sec per stage
AgitationAir or part movement
Conductivity target<50 µS/cm final stage
Drain time between tanks10–15 sec
Dump frequencyWhen >500 µS/cm or pH >10
Why This Rinse Matters
Key Risks if Rinse Fails
1 Alkaline drag-in neutralizes HCl or H₂SO₄ activator — activation fails silently
2 Aluminum zincate surface — alkaline re-dissolves fresh zinc layer before activation
3 Drag-out rate ~0.5–2.0 gal/1000 ft² — multiply by part area to size cascade tanks
EFFICIENCY NOTE
Counter-current cascade: tank 3 overflows into tank 2, tank 2 overflows into tank 1. Drain tank 1 for waste treatment. Most economical design for water use and chemical recovery.

Safety
● Rinse water is mildly alkaline — pH 9–11
● Overflow to waste treatment — do not discharge untreated
● Wet floors create slip hazard — non-slip matting required
● pH check rinse periodically — alkaline carryover can raise pH
● Nitrile gloves and splash goggles during tank maintenance
