6-Cell Defect Diagnosis Grid
Skip Plating
  • Inadequate activation
  • Contamination from rinse
  • pH too low (<8.0)
  • Bath too cold (<80°C)
Pitting
  • Cl⁻ drag-in from HCl rinse
  • H₂ not released — agitate
  • Organic contamination
  • High load density
Low Rate
  • Ni²⁺ below 4.5 g/L
  • NaH₂PO₂ depleted
  • pH outside 8.5–9.5
  • Temp below 80°C
Rough Deposit
  • Particulates in bath
  • Insufficient filtration
  • Stabilizer depleted
  • Bath approaching 8 MTO
Dark Deposit
  • P content too high
  • pH too high (>9.5)
  • Cu or Pb contamination
  • Bath analysis required
Bath Decomposition
  • Stabilizer depleted
  • Temp spike >95°C
  • Heavy metal contamination
  • Exothermic — hazard!

⚠ Bath Stability Warning
Exothermic hazard: EN bath decomposition is violent and exothermic — can boil over and spray caustic nickel solution.
Never leave hot EN bath idle without continuous agitation and filtration. Stabilizer maintains control.
Filter continuously — 1–2 tank volumes/hour minimum. Filter removes nucleation sites that trigger decomposition.
Stabilizer depletion signs: rapid cloudiness, unusual bubbling at bath surface, rate spike — stop immediately.
Emergency: dilute bath with cold DI water to reduce temperature. Do not add chemicals to a decomposing bath.
