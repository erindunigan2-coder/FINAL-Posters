⚠
HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT WARNING
High-strength steel (≥1000 MPa UTS / ≥40 HRC) absorbs hydrogen during acid contact. This can cause delayed brittle fracture hours or days after plating.
During Activation:
● Limit acid activation to 15–30 seconds maximum
● Anodic electroclean only — no cathodic
● Use inhibited acid where available
After Plating:
● Bake at 190–210°C within 4 hours of plating
● Hold minimum 4 hours (ASTM B849)
● Up to 23 hours for aerospace (ASTM B850)
Reference: ASTM B849 / ASTM B850 / AMS 2759/9
Acid Handling Safety
● HCl — fuming; use local exhaust ventilation at tank
● H₂SO₄ — always add acid to water, never reverse
● HNO₃ — produces NOx fumes; bright yellow-brown gas
● HF (Ti/Mg) — extremely dangerous; bone penetration risk
● CrO₃ — Cr6+ carcinogen; hazardous waste regulations apply
PPE Required
● Chemical splash goggles — mandatory at all times
● Heavy nitrile or PVC gloves — acid-resistant
● Chemical-resistant apron or suit
● Eyewash and emergency shower within 10 seconds
● Ventilation: local exhaust at acid tanks
