HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT RELIEF — THE 4-HOUR WINDOW
PLATE DONE
0–4 hr: BAKE WINDOW
>4 hr: RISK ZONE
High-strength steel (>1000 MPa UTS or >40 HRC):
● Bake at 190–210°C within 4 hours of plating completion
● Minimum hold: 4 hours (ASTM B849)
● Up to 23 hours for critical aerospace applications (ASTM B850)
● FAILURE TO BAKE = RISK OF CATASTROPHIC DELAYED BRITTLE FRACTURE
Refs: ASTM B849 / B850 / AMS 2759/9

Furnace Safety
● Furnace at 190–400°C — severe burn hazard on contact
● Heat-resistant gloves required for loading/unloading
● Ventilation required — H₂ released from EN deposit during bake
● Inert atmosphere furnace (N₂, Ar) — asphyxiation risk — confined space, verify O₂ before entry
● Allow parts to cool before handling
● Furnace calibration: verify ±5°C across load; thermocouples calibrated annually
● Log every bake cycle: part ID, temp, time, furnace ID
