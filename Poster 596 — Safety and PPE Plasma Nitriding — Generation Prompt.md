### PPE (Y: 15.5"--22.0")

Section label: `REQUIRED PPE`. 6-item list:

| PPE | When Required |
|---|---|
| Safety glasses / face shield | Always; full shield during load/unload |
| Heat-resistant gloves (500 F+) | Loading, unloading, hot fixturing |
| Electrical-rated gloves (Class 00+) | Any maintenance on power supply |
| Steel-toed boots | Always in furnace area |
| Hearing protection (> 85 dB) | During plasma if magnetostrictive noise |

### Safety Advantages (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

**NO AMMONIA** -- no TLV exposure risk; no cylinder storage; no corrosive NH4OH.
**NO CARBON MONOXIDE** -- no endo gas; no CO at 1200 ppm IDLH.
**NO QUENCH OIL** -- no fire risk; no flash point monitoring; no fire suppression needed.
