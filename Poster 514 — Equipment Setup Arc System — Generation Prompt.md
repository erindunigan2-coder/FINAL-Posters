## Phase 5 -- Air Supply

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `COMPRESSED AIR -- THE CRITICAL UTILITY`.

**Left -- Requirements Table:**

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Pressure | 80--120 PSI (550--830 kPa) |
| Volume | 40--80 CFM (1,130--2,265 L/min) |
| Oil content | Zero -- oil-free compressor or coalescing filter |
| Moisture | Dew point at least 10 degC below ambient |
| Particulate | <5 microns -- inline filter required |

Values in `#E8A020`.

**Right -- AIR QUALITY = COATING QUALITY (`#E05C5C`):**
- MOISTURE --> porosity (water flash-evaporates on molten metal contact)
- OIL --> adhesion failure (oil film prevents bonding)
- Air supply is as important as power supply and wire quality
- Install: refrigerated dryer + coalescing filter + desiccant dryer
- Test daily: white cloth blowdown (ISO 8573 Class 2 recommended)

## Phase 6 -- Startup Checklist + Safety

### Startup Checklist (Y: 22.0"--28.5")

Section label: `PRE-SPRAY STARTUP CHECKLIST`. 8 steps in 2x4 grid:

| Step | Action | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect gun -- worn contact tips, damaged wire guides | Replace worn tips -- uneven arc |
| 2 | Load wire spools; thread wire through conduit | Correct material and diameter (1.6--3.2 mm) |
| 3 | Connect power cables; verify polarity | Each wire = one electrode; polarity per OEM |
| 4 | Connect air; verify pressure and volume | 80--120 PSI; blowdown test for oil/moisture |
| 5 | Set voltage and wire feed speed | Per parameter spec for material |
| 6 | White cloth blowdown test | Zero oil, zero moisture on cloth |
| 7 | Strike arc on scrap coupon | Uniform fan; no spitting; arc stable |
| 8 | Check wire feed synchronization | Both wires feeding evenly |

Steps in `#2EC4B6`. Verify in `#27AE60`.

### Safety Warnings (Y: 28.5"--32.5")

Section label: `ELECTRICAL SAFETY WARNINGS`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Warning | Detail |
|---|---|
| LOCKOUT/TAGOUT BEFORE SERVICE | Never service gun/tips/feeder with power energized. LOTO every time. |
| INSULATED GLOVES | Gun is live during operation. Welding-grade insulated gloves required. |
| CABLE INSPECTION | Inspect all power cables before each shift. Replace frayed/damaged cables. |
| GFCI PROTECTION | Use GFCI on all circuits. Field installations especially vulnerable. |
