## Phase 5 -- Contaminant Impact + Water-Break Test

Y: 14.5" to 20.5".

**Left -- Contaminant Impact Table:**

| Contaminant | Source | Effect on Type I (0.5--2.5 um) |
|---|---|---|
| Organic residue (oil) | Machining, handling | Skip anodizing -- bare spot visible |
| Silicate deposit | Wrong cleaner | No oxide growth at deposit |
| Chloride residue | Cleaner or water | Pitting in CrO3 bath |
| Fingerprint | Bare-hand handling | Visible shadow through thin coating |

**Right -- Water-Break Test:**
PASS (`#27AE60`): Continuous water film. FAIL (`#E05C5C`): Water beads or breaks.

## Phase 7 -- Type I-Specific Cleaning Rules

Y: 26.5" to 32.5".

- Chloride-free cleaner is MANDATORY for Type I
- No silicates -- same as all anodizing
- Extended soak for tight geometries
- Ultrasonic cleaning for fatigue-critical aerospace parts
- Water-break test is the quality gate
- Cr(VI) safety reminder: `Downstream Cr(VI) bath requires full PPE and engineering controls. Clean parts entering the CrO3 bath must not introduce organic contamination that disrupts the electrolyte.` `#E05C5C`
