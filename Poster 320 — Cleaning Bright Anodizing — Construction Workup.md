# Poster #320 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Bright Anodizing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of 8. Cleaning for bright anodize uses the same non-silicated alkaline soak cleaner as all anodizing. The stakes are higher because any residual contamination becomes a visible defect on the mirror-like bright-dipped surface. Additionally, many bright anodize substrates arrive with buffing compound that requires a two-step cleaning approach: solvent pre-clean followed by alkaline soak.

## Design Notes

The unique element for bright anodize cleaning is the two-step cleaning sequence for polished substrates. Many parts arrive with buffing compound that standard alkaline cleaning cannot remove alone. The poster emphasizes this two-step approach and the mechanical pre-polish context. On a mirror finish, every cleaning defect is amplified -- this is the poster's throughline.
