## Phase 4 -- Cure Chemistry Hero

Y: 5.0" to 13.5". Section label: `BLOCKED ISOCYANATE CROSS-LINKING -- THE CURE REACTION`.

Three-stage visual L-to-R:

**Deposited Film (`#2EC4B6`):** Epoxy-amine resin + blocked isocyanate (caprolactam-blocked MDI/IPDI). Film: wet, gel-like, no solvent resistance.

**Unblocking (`#E8A020`, ~300 F):** Blocking agent volatilizes. Blocked-NCO -> Free -NCO + Caprolactam (gas). Caprolactam captured by afterburner. Primary VOC emission source.

**Cross-Linking (`#27AE60`, 340--360 F, 20--30 min):** -NCO + -OH -> Urethane linkage. Dense 3D thermoset network. 500--1,000+ hr B117. MEK rub 100+. Cannot be remelted.

Y: 14.8" to 20.3". Section label: `OVEN PROFILE -- METAL TEMP IS WHAT MATTERS`.

## Phase 6 -- Undercure vs. Overcure

Y: 21.3" to 26.3". Section label: `UNDERCURE VS. OVERCURE -- BOTH ARE FAILURES`.

**Left -- `UNDERCURE` (`#E05C5C`):** MEK rub < 100. Poor solvent resistance. Topcoat delaminates. May look normal -- HIDDEN failure. Detection: `MEK double rub test every oven zone change.`

**Right -- `OVERCURE` (`#E8A020`):** Yellowing. Brittleness. Intercoat adhesion loss. Energy waste. Prevention: `Data loggers. Metal temp within 340--360 F. Do not exceed 380 F.`

## Phase 6B -- Troubleshooting Strip

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 4 cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| MEK RUB FAILURE | Metal temp below 340 F or time under 20 min | Profile oven with data loggers |
| YELLOWING | Oven too hot or dwell too long | Reduce peak metal temp |
| POOR TOPCOAT ADHESION | Undercure or overcure | Verify MEK 100+; scuff if overcured |
| AFTERBURNER ALARM | Blocking agent exceeds LEL | Check afterburner; verify exhaust volume |
