## Phase 4 -- Post-Coat Rinse Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE POST-COAT RINSE`.

Rinse tank with coated workpiece. Thin Cr3+/Zr film overlay on aluminum. Parameters: Ambient to 100 F | 15--60 sec | DI preferred | Gentle -- no aggressive spray. Film is ultra-thin (0.02--0.10 um) but more robust than fresh hex.

Handling caution banner: `HANDLE WITH CARE: The coating is thin but durable once cured (24 hr). First few hours are most vulnerable.`

Bottom: `Tri coatings tolerate warm water rinse better than hex. Hex must be cold-water-only to protect Cr6+ content.`

## Phase 5 -- Handling Comparison + Water Quality

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Tri vs. Hex Post-Coat Handling:**

| Factor | Trivalent | Hexavalent |
|---|---|---|
| Rinse temp | Ambient to 100 F OK | Cold only (< 77 F) |
| Duration | 15--60 sec | 15--30 sec |
| Agitation | Mild OK | Minimal |
| Touch/handle after | Careful 2--4 hr | NO touch 24 hr min |
| Full cure | 24 hr | 24--72 hr |
| DI water? | Preferred (aerospace required) | Preferred |

**Right -- Water Quality:** Teal accent. Film is ULTRA-THIN (0.02--0.10 um) -- any mineral deposit visible through it. DI < 50 uS/cm eliminates spots. Chloride < 10 ppm | TDS < 25 ppm | pH 6.0--8.0.
