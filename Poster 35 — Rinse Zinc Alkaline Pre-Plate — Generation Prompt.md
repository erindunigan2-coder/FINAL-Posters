## Phase 6 -- Problems

Y: 21.5" to 27.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-PLATE RINSE`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acid carry-over | Low caustic; poor throw | Rinse too brief | Extend; add cascade |
| Rough deposit | Roughness in LCD | Dissolved iron carried in | Improve rinse; refresh acid |
| Chloride contamination | Pitting | HCl residue | Cascade to <200 uS/cm; use H2SO4 |
| Flash rust | Orange tint at entry | Delay/drying | Immediate transfer |
