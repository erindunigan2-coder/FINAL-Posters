## Phase 4 -- Masking Materials (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section label: `MASKING MATERIALS FOR FLAME SPRAY`.

**Materials Table (Y: 5.0"-12.0"):** 6-row table, full width.

| Material | Max Temp | Reusable | Best For | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hi-temp masking tape (silicone adhesive) | 260 degC (500 degF) | No | Edges, transitions, irregular shapes | Adhesive residue if overheated |
| Aluminum foil tape | 300 degC (572 degF) | No | Flat surfaces, field work | Tears easily on rough profiles |
| Metal masks (mild steel, stainless) | >500 degC | Yes | Production runs; reusable shields | Must be fabricated to part geometry |
| Silicone plugs and caps | 260 degC (500 degF) | Yes | Holes, bores, threaded features | Limited size range |
| Sheet metal shields | >500 degC | Yes | Large flat areas, bolt patterns | Heavy; fixturing needed |
| Ceramic fiber tape | >1000 degC | No | Near-fusing zones (NiCrBSi post-fuse) | Only needed for fusing operations |

Header fill `#3A4055`. Alternating rows.

## Phase 5 -- Fixturing + Do's/Don'ts

### Fixturing (Y: 15.0"-22.0")

Section label: `FIXTURING APPROACHES`. Four cards, top accent 4 pt.

| Approach | Accent | Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAND-HELD GUN | `#E8A020` | Operator holds gun manually; workpiece clamped on bench | Field repair; one-off jobs; large structures |
| TURNTABLE | `#2EC4B6` | Simple rotation fixture; 10-60 RPM | Cylindrical parts; shafts; rollers |
| LATHE-TYPE | `#2EC4B6` | Part between centers; gun on manual traverse | Precision cylindrical; uniform thickness |
| ROBOT + GUN | `#27AE60` | 6-axis robot; part on turntable | Production volume; consistency-critical |

Bottom: `Most flame spray is manual -- automation improves consistency but is not required for most applications.` `#E8A020`.

### Do's and Don'ts (Y: 22.0"-28.5")

Section label: `MASKING BEST PRACTICES`.

**Left -- DO (accent `#27AE60`):**
- Mask AFTER grit blasting -- tape on blasted profile adheres better
- Use metal masks for production -- reusable and precise
- Protect all threads, bearing surfaces, and mating faces
- Verify mask integrity between spray passes
- Keep 3-5 mm gap between mask edge and coating edge for blending

**Right -- DON'T (accent `#E05C5C`):**
- Never mask BEFORE cleaning -- traps contamination
- Never use plastic tape not rated for temperature
- Never leave gaps in masking -- overspray reaches further than you think
- Never reuse single-use tape -- adhesive degrades with heat
- Never block cooling air paths with masking material
