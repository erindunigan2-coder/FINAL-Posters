## Phase 5 -- Heat Treatment Parameters

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `HEAT TREATMENT SPECIFICATIONS`.

| Treatment | Temperature | Time | Atmosphere | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HE Relief | 190--210 C (375--410 F) | 2--23 hours | Air acceptable | ~700--800 HV | MANDATORY within 4 hrs for >1000 MPa UTS. ASTM B849/B850 |
| Intermediate | 280--320 C (535--610 F) | 1--2 hours | Air or N2 | 900--1000 HV | Retains ductility |
| Maximum Hardness | 350--400 C (660--750 F) | 1 hour | N2 or vacuum preferred | 1000--1200 HV (DMAB) / 1100--1300 HV (NaBH4) | Ni3B precipitation hardening |

HE Relief Rule (fill `#E05C5C` at 10%): `HIGH-STRENGTH STEEL (>1000 MPa / >40 HRC): HE bake at 190--210 C for minimum 4 hours, within 4 hours of plating. Failure = risk of catastrophic delayed brittle fracture. ASTM B849 / B850 / AMS 2759/9.`

## Phase 6 -- Ni3B Hardening Mechanism

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHY HEAT TREATMENT WORKS -- THE Ni3B MECHANISM`.

Full-width panel, accent `#27AE60`:

**Left:** `AS-PLATED: AMORPHOUS Ni-B` -- Amorphous metallic glass at >3% B; boron randomly distributed; no grain structure; 700--850 HV.

**Center arrow:** `Heat at 350--400 C` with stylized visual.

**Right:** `HEAT-TREATED: NANOCRYSTALLINE Ni + Ni3B` -- Matrix crystallizes; boron segregates into Ni3B precipitates; Ni3B pins dislocations; 1000--1300 HV.

Bottom highlight: `Ni3B is to EN-B what Ni3P is to EN-P. Same mechanism -- only the intermetallic changes. And Ni3B is harder.`
