## Phase 4 -- Three-Phase Breakdown (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `THREE PHASES OF THE INTERRUPTED QUENCH`. Three panels:

| Phase | Accent | Title | What Happens | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#E05C5C` | RAPID COOL (THE QUENCH) | Plunge from austenitize to salt; must pass pearlite nose | Seconds |
| 2 | `#27AE60` | EQUALIZATION (THE HOLD) | Surface + core equalize; no transformation; austenite stable above Ms | 5--15 min |
| 3 | `#2EC4B6` | AIR COOL (THE TRANSFORMATION) | Remove from bath; still air; martensite forms uniformly | 15--60 min |

**Martempering vs. Austempering Comparison**: Martempering = equalization only (5--15 min), transformation AFTER removal, final = martensite (temper required). Austempering = full transformation (30--120 min), IN salt bath, final = bainite (no temper).

Critical: `If you hold martempering parts too long in salt, bainite starts to form. That's austempering -- the wrong microstructure.`

## Phase 6 -- Timing Rules + Footer

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Three full-width timing callouts:

1. TRANSFER: < 15 SECONDS (Coral) -- automated preferred; pearlite nucleates if exceeded.
2. EQUALIZATION HOLD: 5--15 MINUTES (Emerald) -- longer risks bainite; thin sections 5 min, heavy 15 min.
3. AIR COOL: STILL AIR ONLY (Teal) -- do NOT fan cool or water quench; forced cooling defeats the purpose.
