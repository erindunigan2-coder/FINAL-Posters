## Phase 4 -- Rinse Stage Detail Hero

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `RINSE -- POST-CLEAN`.

### Step 5 -- Stage detail panel (Y: 3.8" to 8.5")

Full-width panel, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`. Badge: `STAGE 2 -- RINSE`.

**Left -- Parameters:** Water: DI/RO for aerospace/medical / Temperature: Ambient to warm / Conductivity: < 500 uS/cm (< 50 for DI) / Chloride: < 25 ppm general, < 5 ppm aerospace/medical / Method: Overflow immersion or spray / Time: 1--2 min.

**Right -- Two callouts:**
1. `PURPOSE` (`#27AE60`): Remove ALL cleaner residues. Prevent chemical carryover into passivation bath.
2. `CHLORIDE IN WATER` (`#E05C5C`): Municipal water contains 10--250+ ppm chloride. Enough to pit stainless during passivation. Test your water. Use DI/RO for critical parts.

### Step 6 -- Water quality requirements (Y: 9.0" to 15.0")

Two panels:

**Left -- Water Quality Specs** (`#27AE60`):

| Parameter | General Industrial | Aerospace / Medical |
|---|---|---|
| Chloride (Cl-) | < 25 ppm | < 5 ppm |
| Conductivity | < 500 uS/cm | < 50 uS/cm |
| Sulfate (SO4 2-) | < 100 ppm | < 25 ppm |
| pH | 5.5--8.0 | 6.0--7.5 |
| Water type | Low-Cl tap or softened | DI or RO |

**Right -- Testing for Chloride** (`#E8A020`):
- Silver Nitrate Spot Test: AgNO3 drops, white precipitate = chloride present
- Chloride Test Strips: Dip, compare color, 0--500 ppm range
- Ion Chromatography: Lab method, quantitative to < 1 ppm, for aerospace qualification

## Phase 5 -- Double Rinse Protocol

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `DOUBLE RINSE -- WHEN AND WHY`.

Single wide panel:
- **When:** Aerospace/medical (AMS 2700), after descaling pickle, high-value components, single rinse conductivity exceeds target
- **How:** Stage 1 captures bulk drag-out (periodically dump). Stage 2 is final quality rinse (DI/RO, low conductivity). Counter-flow: fresh DI enters Stage 2, overflows to Stage 1.

---

## Phase 6 -- Pickle Rinse Addendum

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `SPECIAL RINSE AFTER DESCALING PICKLE`.

**Left -- THE HF RISK** (`#E05C5C`): HF trapped in crevices continues etching, causes pitting, contaminates passivation bath, creates safety hazards. Double rinse MANDATORY after HF pickling.

**Right -- RECOMMENDED PROTOCOL** (`#27AE60`): 1. First rinse: flowing water 1--2 min. 2. Second rinse: DI/RO 1--2 min. 3. Verify: pH strip neutral (6.0--7.5). 4. Check crevices: rotate parts to drain trapped acid.

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | PITTING AFTER PASSIVATION | Chloride in rinse water (> 25 ppm) | Test water; switch to DI/RO |
| 2 | 6.33" | STAINING AFTER DRY | Alkaline residue carried into passivation | Double rinse; verify conductivity |
| 3 | 12.16" | PASSIVATION BATH pH RISING | Excessive alkaline drag-over | Improve rinse; reduce cleaner conc |
| 4 | 18.0" | ETCHING FROM RESIDUAL HF | Pickle acid trapped in crevices | Double rinse; rotate parts; pH verify |
