# Poster #86 -- Construction Workup
## Post Treatment -- Chrome (Decorative)

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stages 7 and 8 of 8 (combined). Post-treatment for decorative chrome is the simplest finish step in the series. Chrome does not need a passivate (it IS the corrosion-resistant surface). The post-treatment involves an optional anti-tarnish dip, hot DI water rinse, forced-air drying, and final inspection. The inspection stage is where the full Cu+Ni+Cr system gets verified -- thickness, adhesion, corrosion resistance, and visual quality.

Hero visual: a quality inspection checklist layout showing the acceptance criteria for decorative chrome, with a cross-section diagram of the multi-layer system and measurement points.

### ZONE 5 -- Acceptance Criteria Table

**Section label:** `ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA -- DECORATIVE CHROME` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK E -- Spec Table (Y: 21.3" to 26.3")**

| Test | Method | Typical Spec | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chrome thickness | XRF (ASTM B568) | 0.15--0.75 um | Per customer drawing |
| Nickel thickness | XRF (ASTM B568) | 5--25 um | Varies by service condition |
| Total Cu+Ni+Cr | XRF | Per customer spec | System approach |
| Adhesion | Bend/tape (ASTM B571) | No peeling or flaking | Destructive -- sample basis |
| Corrosion (CASS) | CASS test (ASTM B368) | 8--16 hr to rating 9 | Multi-layer dependent |
| Corrosion (NSS) | Salt spray (ASTM B117) | 24--96 hr (per spec) | Depends on Ni thickness |
| Visual | Visual, 100% | Bright, uniform, defect-free | Compare to limit samples |

Header: `#3A4055`. Alt rows: `#1E2435` / `#252B3D`.
Data: JetBrains Mono 12 pt. Headers: Barlow SemiBold 14 pt.

Bottom note: `Corrosion performance is primarily a function of the NICKEL layer, not the chrome. Thin chrome over thick, well-structured nickel outperforms thick chrome over thin nickel every time.` -- Inter Medium, 13 pt, `#E8A020`

### ZONE 6 -- Common Defects at Inspection

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 INSPECTION FAILURES` -- Y: 26.7".

**BLOCK F -- 2x2 Grid (Y: 27.3" to 32.3")**

| Position | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | WATER SPOTS | `#E8A020` | Hard water in final rinse; slow drying | Use DI for final rinse; improve drying speed |
| R1C2 | HAZE / TARNISH | `#E8A020` | Incomplete anti-tarnish or no hot rinse | Add anti-tarnish dip; use hot DI rinse |

## Design Notes

This poster pivots from "how to plate" to "how to verify." The multi-layer cross-section with measurement points is the hero -- it visually answers the question "what am I measuring and where?" The scale callout (chrome is 100-500x thinner than a human hair) is the kind of fact that makes people stop and think. The acceptance criteria table is the reference a quality engineer will actually use, pulling ASTM method numbers and typical specs. The bottom note about nickel being the real corrosion performer is the single most important insight on this poster -- it reframes the whole decorative chrome conversation.
