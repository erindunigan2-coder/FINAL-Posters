CLEAN GLOVES ONLY
No Bare Hands on Plated Surfaces — Ever
From this point forward, all handling of plated busbars must be with clean, lint-free gloves. Fingerprints deposit oils and salts that cause permanent tarnish marks on silver. A single bare-hand touch can create a visible defect that fails customer inspection. Use fresh gloves — do not reuse gloves contaminated with plating chemicals.

Handling & Packaging Flow
Why Dedicated Air — Not Shop Air
Recirculated shop air in a plating facility contains sulfur compounds (from acid baths, heating systems, and atmospheric H₂S), chloride aerosols (from HCl-based processes), and general particulate. Any of these will attack freshly plated silver. Sulfur in particular reacts with silver to form silver sulfide (Ag₂S) — the dark tarnish film. Use a dedicated warm-air dryer with filtered, clean air from outside the plating area. If your facility uses hot air recirculation, verify the air source does not draw from the plating line.

Packaging Requirements

Immediately after drying, package plated busbars in sulfur-free polyethylene (PE) bags. Seal bags to prevent atmospheric exposure. Do NOT use: cardboard (contains sulfur), kraft paper, rubber bands, or foam with sulfur-based blowing agents. For long-term storage, include volatile corrosion inhibitor (VCI) strips rated for silver. Store in a clean, dry environment away from the plating floor.

PE bags + VCI strips for silver. No cardboard, no rubber, no kraft paper.

Inspection Before Packaging

Perform final visual inspection under 500+ lux lighting at 10× magnification before packaging. Check for: uniform bright or semi-bright silver appearance, no burns or dark spots, no skip plating, no water stains, no fingerprints. Measure thickness with XRF if not already verified at Step 16. Document inspection results per your quality system.

Visual + XRF before package. 100% inspection on silver surface.

Troubleshooting Final step issues

Water Spots After Drying

DI water quality poor (conductivity too high) or air not warm enough for complete drying. Improve DI water quality. Increase air temperature (stay within 40–55°C). Ensure complete water removal before packaging.

Fingerprint Marks on Silver

Someone handled parts with bare hands. These marks cannot be removed without re-plating. Enforce clean-glove protocol. Parts with visible fingerprints are non-conforming and must be dispositioned.

Rapid Tarnishing in Storage

Sulfur-bearing packaging materials (cardboard, rubber), inadequate sealing, or atmospheric sulfur exposure. Switch to sulfur-free PE bags with VCI. Verify storage environment. May need to re-plate and re-package.

Dropped or Damaged Parts

Silver-plated busbars that are dropped, dented, or scratched are likely scrap. The damage exposes copper or aluminum beneath the silver, creating galvanic corrosion sites. Handle with care. Use padded fixtures for transport.

⚠ Safety
Hot air burn risk from the drying station (40–55°C / 104–131°F). Use care when positioning parts in the dryer. Handle plated parts carefully — dropped busbars are scrap and can cause foot/hand injuries due to weight. Continue wearing safety shoes and standard PPE. If parts have been through anti-tarnish (Step 18), residual chemicals may be on surfaces until the final rinse is complete.
