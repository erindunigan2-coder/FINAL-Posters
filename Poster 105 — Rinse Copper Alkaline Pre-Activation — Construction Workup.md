# Poster #105 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Copper (Alkaline) -- Pre-Activation

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Creative Lead*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of the alkaline non-cyanide copper process. Same fundamental purpose as all pre-activation rinses: remove alkaline cleaner residue before the acid activation step. The additional concern for this line is zinc die castings -- the rinsing must be thorough but gentle, as prolonged water contact on freshly cleaned zinc can cause light oxidation.

Hero visual: cascade rinse tank diagram.

### ZONE 4 -- Rinse Parameters + Drag-Out

**Section label:** `RINSE PARAMETERS` -- Y: 14.7".

**Left -- Rinse Parameters (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Water type | DI preferred; city water acceptable |
| Temperature | Ambient |
| Flow rate | 2--5 gal/min per stage |
| Stages | 2--3 cascade recommended |
| Immersion time | 30--60 sec (steel/brass); 20--30 sec (zinc DC) |
| Agitation | Air or part movement |
| Conductivity target | < 500 uS/cm at final stage |
| Drain time | 10--15 sec |
| Rinse criterion | Neutral pH (paper check: 6--8) |

**Right -- Drag-Out Callout (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):**

Title: `WHY THIS RINSE MATTERS` -- `#E8A020`

Body:
> Alkaline cleaner drag-in to the acid activation tank neutralizes the acid and reduces its effectiveness. On zinc die castings, any residual alkaline film creates uneven etching during activation -- leading to adhesion failure in the copper strike.
>
> For steel substrates, insufficient rinsing means the acid activation wastes acid on neutralization instead of oxide removal.

Key metric: `For zinc DC: rinse quickly, move to activation fast. Time is the enemy of clean zinc.`

### ZONE 5 -- Common Problems & Fixes

**Section label:** `WHAT GOES WRONG AT THE PRE-ACTIVATION RINSE` -- Y: 21.2".

| Problem | Symptom Downstream | Root Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alkaline carry-over | Acid activation neutralized; poor oxide removal | Insufficient rinse stages | Add cascade stage; increase flow |
| Zinc oxidation in rinse | White haze on zinc DC surface | Prolonged water contact on reactive zinc | Reduce rinse time for zinc DC (20--30 sec) |
| Spotting/staining | Mineral deposits on surface | Hard water | Switch to DI water |
| Foam in rinse | Surfactant carry-over from cleaner | High-foam cleaner | Use low-foam cleaner; add defoamer |

Problem: `#E05C5C`. Symptom: `#E8A020`. Cause: `#F0EDE8`. Fix: `#27AE60`.

## Design Notes

The zinc die casting time sensitivity is the unique angle -- rinse too long and the zinc starts to re-oxidize, creating the same adhesion problem you just cleaned away. This is a subtlety that experienced zinc DC platers know instinctively but newer operators miss. The poster makes it explicit.
