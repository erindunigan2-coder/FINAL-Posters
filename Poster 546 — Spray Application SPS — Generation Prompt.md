## Phase 5 -- Microstructure

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section label: `COLUMNAR MICROSTRUCTURE -- THE SPS ADVANTAGE`.

**Left -- How Columns Form (W: 11.0", accent `#27AE60`):**

1. Submicron particles deposit as very fine splats
2. Surface roughness creates shadowing effects
3. Peaks receive more material than valleys
4. Preferential growth builds vertical columns
5. Inter-columnar gaps form naturally
6. Columns 50-200 um wide, 5-15% porosity

Callout: `Column gaps provide strain tolerance -- columns flex independently during thermal cycling.` `#27AE60`.

**Right -- SPS vs. APS vs. EB-PVD (W: 11.5"):**

| Property | SPS | APS | EB-PVD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microstructure | Columnar | Lamellar | Columnar |
| Porosity | 10-25% | 10-20% | 15-25% |
| Thermal conductivity | 0.7-1.2 W/mK | 0.8-1.2 W/mK | 1.5-2.0 W/mK |
| Strain tolerance | HIGH | LOW | HIGH |
| Bond strength | > 15 MPa | > 10 MPa | > 20 MPa |
| Cost | Medium | Low | Very High |
| Vacuum required | No | No | Yes |

SPS column highlighted `#27AE60` at 10%. Bottom: `SPS delivers EB-PVD-class performance at APS-class cost`.

## Phase 6 -- Coating Properties + Defects

### YSZ SPS Topcoat Properties (Left, Y: 22.0"-28.5")

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Porosity | 10-25% (intentional) |
| Thermal conductivity | 0.7-1.2 W/mK |
| Microhardness | 600-900 HV |
| Bond strength | > 15 MPa |
| Column width | 50-200 um |
| Cycling life | > 1000 cycles (1100 degC) |
| Phase | Tetragonal prime ZrO2 (XRD verified) |

### Application Notes (Right, accent `#E8A020`)

- Apply SPS directly over APS bond coat -- no delay
- Constant standoff 40-80 mm -- critical for columns
- Cooling air jets on substrate backside -- mandatory
- Monitor substrate temp -- max 400 degC
- Robot traverse must be consistent -- manual SPS not practical
- Columnar structure sensitive to standoff variation

### Common Defects (Y: 28.5"-32.5")

Section label: `APPLICATION DEFECTS -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`. Four cards:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| LAMELLAR MICROSTRUCTURE | Standoff too far; particles too large; power low | Reduce standoff; verify suspension; increase power |
| DELAMINATION FROM BOND COAT | Contaminated bond coat; excessive delay | Spray SPS promptly; minimize handling |
| SUBSTRATE OVERHEATING | Close standoff + slow traverse; poor cooling | Increase traverse; increase cooling; monitor temp |
| UNEVEN THICKNESS | Inconsistent traverse/standoff; robot error | Verify robot path; calibrate standoff; test coupons |

Problem: `#E05C5C`. Fix: `#27AE60`.
