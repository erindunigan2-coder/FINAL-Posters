## Phase 4 -- Reaction Mechanism Hero

Y: 4.2" to 13.5". Section: `HOW THE TRIVALENT COATING FORMS`.

Three-layer diagram: Al substrate (Al -> Al3+ + 3e-) -> Reaction interface (pH rises, ZrF62- hydrolyzes to ZrO2, Cr3+ co-deposits) -> Bulk solution (Cr3+ 0.5--2.0 g/L | Zr4+ 0.5--3.0 g/L | Free F- 0.5--2.0 g/L | pH 3.5--4.2 CRITICAL | 65--95 F | 2--5 min).

Result: `ZrO2/Cr2O3/Al2O3 mixed oxide gel | 0.02--0.10 um | NO Cr6+ in the film`.

Key insight: `Unlike hex, there is NO Cr6+ reduction step. Cr is already Cr3+. Film forms by co-deposition as local pH rises at the surface.`

## Phase 5 -- Bath Chemistry + pH Window

Y: 13.5" to 19.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Bath Chemistry (wider):** Three components: Cr3+ (0.5--2.0 g/L, barrier film) | Zr4+/Ti4+ fluorocomplexes (0.5--3.0 g/L, primary barrier-forming) | Fluoride (0.5--2.0 g/L, activator). Operating params: Immersion 65--95 F, 2--5 min | Spray 70--90 F, 1--3 min | pH 3.5--4.2.

**Right -- Critical pH Window:** Coral accent. Tri gauge: < 3.0 = white haze (red) | 3.0--3.5 = thin but usable (amber) | 3.5--4.2 = OPTIMAL (green) | 4.2--4.5 = coating thinning (amber) | > 4.5 = no coating (red). For comparison: Hex 1.3--1.8 (wider, more forgiving). `Tri window: 0.7 units. Hex is more forgiving of drift. MONITOR pH CONTINUOUSLY.`

## Phase 6 -- Film Characteristics

Y: 19.5" to 25.5". Section: `FILM CHARACTERISTICS -- TRI VS. HEX AT A GLANCE`.

| Property | Trivalent (Type II) | Hexavalent (Type I) |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | ZrO2/Cr2O3/Al2O3 | Cr2O3/CrO4/Al2O3 gel |
| Cr6+ in film | ZERO | 10--30% |
| Self-healing | NO | YES |
| Appearance | Clear to pale blue | Gold to golden-brown |
| Thickness | 0.02--0.10 um | 0.25--1.0 um |
| SST bare | 168 hr min | 168--336 hr |
| Electrical resistance | Very low | Low |
| Thermal stability | More stable | Degrades > 140 F |
| Regulatory | RoHS+REACH compliant | Restricted |

Specs: MIL-DTL-5541F Type II | AMS 2487 | ASTM B921 | SAE ARP 6584 | NADCAP AC7108.

|---|---|---|
