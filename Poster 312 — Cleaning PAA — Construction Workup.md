# Poster #312 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- PAA

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of the PAA sequence. Cleaning for PAA is essentially the same as for other anodizing processes, but the stakes are higher because organic residue trapped under the intentionally thin PAA oxide directly reduces adhesive bond strength. The poster emphasizes absolute cleanliness, the silicate prohibition, and the water-break test.

Hero visual: a cleaning tank cross-section showing soak immersion with labeled components and the water-break test illustration.

> Silicated cleaners deposit an invisible silicate film on aluminum. This film:
>
> -- Blocks anodic oxide growth (skip anodizing)
> -- Creates adhesion failures in the bond joint
> -- Cannot be removed by subsequent etching or desmut
> -- Is undetectable by visual inspection
>
> For PAA, where the oxide is only 0.5--1.5 um thick, even trace silicate causes catastrophic bond failure.

### ZONE 6 -- Why PAA Cleaning Matters

**Section label:** `WHY IT MATTERS -- THE BOND STRENGTH CONNECTION` -- Y: 26.7".

**BLOCK G -- Full-width callout (Y: 27.3" to 32.3"):**
- Rounded rect, X: 0.5", W: 23.0", H: 4.8", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020` 0.06"

Title: `CLEAN SURFACE = STRONG BOND` Barlow SemiBold 22 pt `#E8A020`

Body (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 160%):

> PAA creates a whisker-like oxide structure only 0.5--1.5 um thick. This micro-roughness provides mechanical interlocking for structural adhesives, achieving bond strengths exceeding 40 MPa (6,000 psi) in aluminum-to-aluminum joints.
>
> But the whiskers are only as good as the surface beneath them. Organic contamination trapped under the oxide:
> -- Reduces adhesive wetting of the oxide surface
> -- Creates weak boundary layers that fail under load
> -- Causes disbonding in service (potentially catastrophic in aerospace)
>
> Good surface prep is to adhesive bonding what a great foundation is to a skyscraper. Skip it, and everything above fails.

Key stat callout (bottom of box):
- `TARGET: Bond strength > 40 MPa (6,000 psi) -- achievable only with properly cleaned substrate` JetBrains Mono 14 pt `#27AE60`

## Design Notes

This poster establishes the core message of the entire PAA cluster: everything in this process serves the bond. Cleaning is not about appearance -- it is about bond strength. The silicate prohibition and water-break test are the two most actionable items an operator can take away. The failure modes table connects cleaning failures to downstream bond failures, reinforcing the chain-of-consequences thinking that PAA demands.
