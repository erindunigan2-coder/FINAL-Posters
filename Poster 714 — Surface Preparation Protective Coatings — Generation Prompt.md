## Phase 4 -- Three-Substrate Hero

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section label: `THREE SUBSTRATES -- THREE PREP PATHS`. Three panels.

**Panel 1 -- Steel (accent `#2EC4B6`):** SP10 (general) / SP5 (immersion). Profile 2.0-4.0 mils. D4417 replica tape. Soluble salts: Cl < 3, SO4 < 10 ug/cm2. `Blast profile provides mechanical adhesion.`

**Panel 2 -- Concrete (accent `#E8A020`):** ICRI CSP 2-5 (shot blast, grind). Moisture: F2170 < 75% RH, F1869 < 3 lb. pH 7-10. No curing compounds/sealers. `Moisture is #1 cause of coating failure on floors.`

**Panel 3 -- Maintenance (accent `#E05C5C`):** Test adhesion D3359 (>= 3B: overcoat; < 3B: full removal). SP11 spot repair. Feather edges. Full removal: blast SP10/SP5. Check compatibility. `Poor adhesion of existing coating = guaranteed failure.`

## Phase 5 -- Concrete Moisture + Salts/Profile

### Concrete Moisture (Y: 15.0"-21.0")

Section label: `CONCRETE MOISTURE -- THE HIDDEN ENEMY`. Two columns.

Left -- F1869 Calcium Chloride: Seal CaCl2 dish under dome, 60-72 hr, weigh. PASS: < 3 lb/1,000 ft2/24 hr. New concrete: 28+ days drying.

Right -- F2170 RH Probe: Drill to 40% slab depth, insert probe, 24-72 hr equilibration. PASS: < 75% RH. Preferred by most manufacturers.

### Salts + Profile (Y: 21.0"-27.0")

Two columns.

Left -- Soluble Salts: Cl < 3, SO4 < 10 ug/cm2. Bresle patch ISO 8502-6. `Immersion service: even stricter.` Salt removal: pressurized water before blast, test after, re-wash if exceeded.

Right -- Profile by Service: General 2.0-3.0 mils SP10. Marine 2.5-4.0 SP10. Tank lining 3.0-4.0 SP5. Pipeline 2.5-3.5 SP10. Bridge 2.5-4.0 SP10.

## Phase 6 -- Maintenance Prep + Failures

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Two columns.

Left -- Maintenance Decision: 1) Test adhesion D3359. >= 3B proceed, < 3B full removal. 2) Check compatibility. 3) Feather edges 80-grit. 4) Spot-blast SP11. 5) Primer on bare, then full system.

Right -- Prep Failures: Coating over curing compound (grind off). Insufficient profile (check nozzle, angular media). Coating over moisture (wait 28+ days, re-test). Overcoat over failed coating (test first, remove if < 3B).
