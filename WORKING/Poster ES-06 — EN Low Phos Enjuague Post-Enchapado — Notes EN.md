# English Translation Notes
## Poster ES-06 — EN Low Phos: Final Rinse Post-Plating (Stage 6 of 7)

---

### HEADER

**Eyebrow tag (amber monospace):**
Electroless Nickel — Stage 6 of 7

**Main title:**
FINAL RINSE

**Subtitle:**
Stops the reaction and protects the fresh nickel deposit

**Body text:**
Three rules with no exceptions: no air-drying, no bare-hand contact, no stacking while wet.

---

### KEY NUMBER ZONE

**Label:** THE KEY NUMBER

**Number:** < 10 seconds

**Note:**
Maximum time between when the part leaves the EN bath and enters the first rinse tank. EN solution drag-out dries as a permanent stain.

---

### WHAT YOU MUST DO

1. Transfer the part from the EN bath to the first rinse tank in under 10 seconds.
2. Use cold water — cold water stops the autocatalytic reaction faster.
3. Submerge 30–60 seconds in each tank; minimum double counterflow rinse.
4. Keep parts wet at all times until final treatment or oven.
5. Handle parts with nitrile gloves — fingerprints are permanently etched into the fresh deposit.

---

### COMMON PROBLEMS

| Problem | What to Look For | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Water spots / staining | Visible stains after part dries | Transfer immediately; use DI water in final stage; never air-dry |
| Engraved fingerprints | Fingerprints visible in final deposit | Use nitrile gloves at all times after the EN bath |
| Galvanic contact pitting | Craters at contact points between parts | Hang parts individually; never stack them wet |

---

### SAFETY

- Rinse water contains dissolved nickel — a regulated heavy metal; goes to the treatment system, never to the direct drain.
- Nickel is a sensitizer — avoid repeated skin contact; use nitrile gloves.
- Wet floors are a slip hazard — use anti-slip mats.
- Report any nickel solution spill to the supervisor immediately.

---

### FOOTER

Consult the supervisor before changing the rinse setup or if you see unusual staining in the deposit.
