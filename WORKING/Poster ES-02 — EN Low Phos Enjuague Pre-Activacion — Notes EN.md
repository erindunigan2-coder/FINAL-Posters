# English Translation Notes
## Poster ES-02 — EN Low Phos: Rinse Pre-Activation (Stage 2 of 7)

---

### HEADER

**Eyebrow tag (amber monospace):**
Electroless Nickel — Stage 2 of 7

**Main title:**
RINSE

**Subtitle:**
Cascade rinse — removes alkaline drag-out before activation

**Body text:**
The rinse is the bridge between the caustic cleaner and the acid activator. If you drag alkali into the activator, activation fails without giving a visible sign.

---

### KEY NUMBER ZONE

**Label:** THE KEY NUMBER

**Number:** < 50 µS/cm

**Note:**
Maximum conductivity in the final tank. If it reads more than 500 µS/cm — dump and replenish.

*Translator note: µS/cm = microsiemens per centimeter — a measure of water purity. Lower = cleaner water.*

---

### WHAT YOU MUST DO

1. Submerge parts in the first tank and let them drain 10–15 seconds before moving to the next.
2. Move parts from tank to tank in order: from dirtiest to cleanest.
3. Keep each immersion between 30 and 60 seconds per tank.
4. Measure the conductivity of the final tank at the start of the shift — it must be below 50 µS/cm.
5. If conductivity exceeds 500 µS/cm or pH rises above 10, dump the tank and replenish.

---

### COMMON PROBLEMS

| Problem | What to Look For | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Alkaline drag-out | Incomplete plating after activation | Increase rinse stages; slow down transfer speed |
| High conductivity | Meter out of range (> 500 µS/cm) | Dump the tank; check water flow |
| Part staining | Visible stains after plating | Use deionized (DI) water; never let parts air-dry |

---

### SAFETY

- Rinse water is slightly alkaline (pH 9–11) — use gloves and goggles during tank maintenance.
- Wet floors are a slip hazard — use anti-slip mats.
- Drain water goes to the treatment system — never to the direct drain.

---

### FOOTER

Consult the supervisor before changing the water source or modifying the number of rinse stages.
