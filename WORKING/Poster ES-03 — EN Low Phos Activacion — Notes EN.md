# English Translation Notes
## Poster ES-03 — EN Low Phos: Activation (Stage 3 of 7)

---

### HEADER

**Eyebrow tag (amber monospace):**
Electroless Nickel — Stage 3 of 7

**Main title:**
ACTIVATION

**Subtitle:**
Prepares the surface so the nickel deposit can initiate

**Body text:**
The part material determines the process. Steel is the simplest. Aluminum needs a double zincate. If activation fails, there is no deposit — and it's not always obvious until too late.

---

### KEY NUMBER ZONE

**Label:** THE KEY NUMBER

**Number:** 30–120 sec

**Note:**
Immersion time for steel (HCl 10–20%). For high-strength steel (> 1000 MPa): maximum 30 seconds.

*Translator note: HCl = hydrochloric acid. MPa = megapascals, a measure of tensile strength.*

---

### WHAT YOU MUST DO

1. Confirm the part material before choosing the activation process.
2. For steel: immerse in 10–20% HCl at room temperature for 30 to 120 seconds.
3. For aluminum: follow the complete sequence — desmut with HNO₃, first zincate, strip, second zincate. Do not skip the double zincate.
4. Transfer aluminum parts to rinse and EN bath in under 30 seconds — the zinc layer oxidizes quickly.
5. For high-strength steel (> 1000 MPa / > 40 HRC): limit acid exposure to 15–30 seconds maximum, and notify the supervisor for hydrogen embrittlement baking.

*Translator note: "Desmut" (desmutado) is the acid treatment that removes the smut layer from aluminum before zincating. "Zinconato" = zincate.*

---

### COMMON PROBLEMS

| Problem | What to Look For | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Unplated areas | Bare patches in the nickel | Re-activate; check acid concentration and time |
| Poor adhesion on aluminum | Deposit lifts or blisters | Review double zincate protocol; shorten transfer time |
| Dark deposit on stainless | Dark or stained surface | Verify nickel strike (Wood's Nickel Strike): NiCl₂ 240 g/L, HCl 125 mL/L |

---

### SAFETY

- Use splash goggles, heavy PVC or nitrile gloves, and acid-resistant apron.
- HCl produces fumes — use local ventilation; never work without air extraction.
- For HNO₃: produces yellow-brown NOx gases — if you see that smoke, move away and alert someone.
- The emergency shower must be within 10 seconds.

---

### FOOTER

For high-strength steel (> 1000 MPa), hydrogen embrittlement baking is mandatory — consult the supervisor before proceeding.
