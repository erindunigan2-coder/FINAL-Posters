# English Translation Notes
## Poster ES-00 — EN Low Phos: Process Flow

---

### HEADER

**Eyebrow tag (amber monospace):**
Electroless Nickel — Low Phosphorus (2–4% P) — Overview

**Main title:**
PROCESS FLOW

**Subtitle:**
7 stages from dirty part to finished deposit

**Body text:**
No current, no anode — the bath plates itself. Every stage matters because a silent failure doesn't show until the part comes out wrong.

---

### KEY NUMBER ZONE

**Label:** THE KEY NUMBER

**Number:** 7 PROCESS STAGES

**Flow strip (left to right):**
CLEANING → RINSE → ACTIVATION → RINSE → EN BATH → RINSE → FINAL TREAT.

**Note below flow strip:**
Do not skip any stage. Each step prepares the surface for the next one.

---

### WHAT YOU MUST DO

1. Follow the 7-stage sequence without skipping any step.
2. Verify that each tank is at the correct temperature and concentration before starting the shift.
3. Transfer parts from one tank to the next without letting them dry in air.
4. Log any problem in the shift logbook immediately.
5. Consult the supervisor before changing the order or skipping a step.

---

### COMMON PROBLEMS

| Problem | What to Look For | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete plating | Areas with no nickel on the part | Check cleaning and activation; run water break test |
| Pores or pits | Small craters in the deposit | Check the pre-bath rinse; filter the bath |
| **Bath decomposition** | Sudden turbidity, abnormal bubbling | **STOP the process; call supervisor immediately** |

---

### SAFETY

- Use splash goggles, chemical-resistant gloves, and an apron at all times on the line.
- This line handles acids, caustics, and hot solutions — one mistake can cause severe burns.
- The emergency shower and eyewash station must be clear and operational at all times.
- Chemical contact on eyes or skin: flush with water for 15 minutes and notify supervisor.
