# English Translation Notes
## Poster ES-01 — EN Low Phos: Cleaning (Stage 1 of 7)

---

### HEADER

**Eyebrow tag (amber monospace):**
Electroless Nickel — Stage 1 of 7

**Main title:**
CLEANING

**Subtitle:**
Alkaline tank cleaning — removes oil, grease, and contaminants

**Body text:**
Electroless plating only deposits on a completely clean surface. Contamination doesn't reduce quality — it stops it entirely.

---

### KEY NUMBER ZONE

**Label:** THE KEY NUMBER

**Number:** 60–80 °C

**Note:**
Cleaner bath temperature. Below 60 °C the cleaner does not work well.

---

### WHAT YOU MUST DO

1. Verify that the bath is between 60 and 80 °C before immersing parts.
2. Submerge parts completely — trapped air pockets hold grease and cause unplated areas.
3. Keep parts in the tank 3 to 10 minutes with agitation.
4. Perform the water break test: water must run in a continuous film without beading up. If beads form, clean again.
5. Transfer parts to the rinse immediately — do not let them air-dry.

---

### COMMON PROBLEMS

| Problem | What to Look For | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Failed water break test | Water beads up when rinsing | Increase time or temperature; replenish cleaner |
| Unplated areas | Bare spots in the final deposit | Re-clean; verify water break test before continuing |
| Rough deposit | Irregular nickel surface | Filter the bath; replace it if heavily contaminated |

*Translator note: "Ruptura de agua" is the Spanish name for the water break test. In the problem table it refers to a failed water break result.*

---

### SAFETY

- Use splash goggles, nitrile or neoprene gloves, and chemical-resistant apron.
- NaOH (caustic soda) causes serious burns. At 60–80 °C the risk is higher.
- Never pour water onto concentrated NaOH — always add the caustic to the water.
- The eyewash station must be within 10 seconds of the tank.

---

### FOOTER

Consult the supervisor before adjusting cleaner concentration or tank temperature.
