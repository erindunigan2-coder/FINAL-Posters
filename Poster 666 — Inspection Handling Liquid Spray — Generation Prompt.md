## Phase 4 -- Six-Test Battery Hero

Y: 5.0" to 15.0". Section label: `THE SIX-TEST BATTERY -- EVERY QUALITY INSPECTOR'S CHECKLIST`. 3x2 grid.

**1. DFT MEASUREMENT (`#27AE60`):** D7091 magnetic/eddy current. 3--5 readings/part, min 5 parts/batch. +/- 0.5 mil. Also: Tooke gauge (D4138) for multi-coat.

**2. ADHESION (`#2EC4B6`):** D3359 Method B cross-cut tape pull. 0B--5B scale. Pass: 4B or 5B. Method A X-cut for quick check.

**3. GLOSS (`#E8A020`):** D523, 60-degree glossmeter. High > 80 GU / Semi 30--80 / Satin 15--30 / Flat < 15 GU.

**4. COLOR (DELTA E) (`#E8A020`):** D2244 spectrophotometer. < 1.0 imperceptible / < 3.0 most specs / > 3.0 reject.

**5. HARDNESS (`#2EC4B6`):** D3363 Pencil Hardness. 6B (softest) to 9H (hardest). Report hardest pencil that does NOT cut. Typical: F to 3H.

**6. FLEXIBILITY (`#27AE60`):** D522 Mandrel Bend (smallest mandrel, no cracking). D2794 Impact (80--160 in-lb typical).

## Phase 5 -- Tooke Gauge + Multi-Coat

Y: 16.3" to 21.3". Section label: `THE TOOKE GAUGE -- MEASURING INDIVIDUAL COATS`.

**Left -- `TOOKE GAUGE (ASTM D4138)` (border `#27AE60`):**
Destructive -- cuts V-groove. Measures individual coat thicknesses. 50x microscope. When total DFT gauge cannot distinguish primer from topcoat.

**Right -- `TYPICAL MULTI-COAT DFT TARGETS` (accent `#E8A020`):**

| Coat | DFT (mils) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Primer | 0.8--2.0 | Adhesion + corrosion |
| Basecoat | 0.5--1.5 | Color, hiding |
| Clearcoat | 1.5--2.5 | Gloss, UV, scratch |
| Total system | 2.8--6.0 | Full protection |

## Phase 6 -- Gloss Scale + Color Delta E

Y: 22.3" to 26.3". Section label: `APPEARANCE QUALITY -- GLOSS AND COLOR`.

**Left -- `GLOSS SCALE (D523, 60-DEGREE)`:** Four horizontal bars: High > 80 GU (`#27AE60`) / Semi 30--80 (`#2EC4B6`) / Satin 15--30 (`#E8A020`) / Flat < 15 (`#3A4055`).

**Right -- `COLOR TOLERANCE (D2244)`:**

| Delta E | Perception | Typical Spec |
|---|---|---|
| < 0.5 | Undetectable | Master standard |
| 0.5--1.0 | Barely perceptible | Premium automotive |
| 1.0--2.0 | Slight | High-quality industrial |
| 2.0--3.0 | Noticeable | General industrial pass |
| > 3.0 | Obvious mismatch | REJECT |

Note `#E05C5C`: `Always measure against an approved physical color standard.`

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| DFT OUT OF SPEC | `#E05C5C` | Gun distance, fluid flow error | Adjust; verify with wet film gauge |
| ADHESION FAILURE (< 4B) | `#E05C5C` | Poor prep, contamination, exceeded window | Verify pretreatment; sand if window exceeded |
| GLOSS OUT OF SPEC | `#E8A020` | Orange peel, contamination | Review flash/leveling; verify batch |
| COLOR MISMATCH (dE > 3.0) | `#E8A020` | Wrong tint, metamerism, bleed-through | Verify batch; standard illuminant; hiding coat |
| LOW HARDNESS | `#2EC4B6` | Undercure or wrong mix ratio | Check oven; verify A:B; MEK rub |
| HANDLING DAMAGE | `#E05C5C` | Handled before full cure | Wait for full cure; clean gloves; interleave |
