## Phase 7 -- Contamination Thresholds + XRF

Y: 26.5" to 32.5".

**Left -- Contamination Thresholds (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | > 5 ppm | Immersion deposit, dark spots, alloy shift |
| Iron | > 20 ppm | Dark deposit, catalyzes Sn2+ oxidation |
| Chloride | > 10 ppm | Pitting, anode attack |
| Organic (oil) | Visible | Pitting, dull deposit |
| Sn4+ (stannic) | > 10% of total Sn | Sludge, rough deposit |

Thresholds in `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- XRF Alloy Verification (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
- Left accent `#27AE60`. Title: `ALLOY VERIFICATION BY XRF`
- Non-destructive, no sample prep, measures Sn + Pb directly, accuracy +/- 1--2%, frequency: every lot/rack/reel
- Specs: `ASTM B579` / `SAE AS5272 (aerospace)` / `MIL-P-81728 (superseded)`

Bottom: `If you are not running XRF on every lot, you are shipping unverified alloy. Stop guessing.` `#E05C5C`
