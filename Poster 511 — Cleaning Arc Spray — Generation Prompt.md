## Phase 4 -- Cleaning Sequence (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section label: `STRUCTURAL STEEL CLEANING SEQUENCE`.

**5-Step Vertical Flowchart (Y: 5.0"--10.5"):** Full-width cards connected by arrows.

| Step | Title | Action | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | REMOVE LOOSE MATERIAL | HP water blast or mechanical scraping -- loose rust, scale, debris | `#2EC4B6` |
| 2 | DEGREASE | Solvent wipe (acetone, MEK) or alkaline wash -- oil, grease, machining fluids | `#2EC4B6` |
| 3 | REMOVE OLD COATINGS | Grit blast, chemical strip, or mechanical removal of paint/galvanize/old TSC | `#E8A020` |
| 4 | FINAL INSPECTION | Verify SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal) or SP 10 (Near-White) per spec | `#27AE60` |
| 5 | DRY AND PROTECT | Forced air dry; protect from moisture until grit blast | `#2EC4B6` |

**Contaminant Identification (Y: 11.0"--14.5"):** Section sublabel: `WHAT KILLS ADHESION`. 3x2 grid, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Contaminant | Why It Must Go |
|---|---|
| Oil and grease | Prevents mechanical bond; causes porosity and delamination |
| Rust (iron oxide) | Weak oxide crumbles under coating; adhesion failure guaranteed |
| Mill scale | Hard oxide from hot rolling; appears intact but delaminates |
| Old paint / coatings | Cannot bond to paint -- must expose bare metal |
| Moisture / condensation | Causes flash rust; creates porosity; weakens bond |
| Lead paint (HAZMAT) | Must be handled per EPA RRP rule; containment required |

## Phase 5 -- Field vs. Shop

Y: 15.0" to 21.0". Section label: `FIELD CLEANING VS. SHOP CLEANING`.

**Left -- FIELD (Amber accent):** HP water blast; solvent wipe; portable grit blast; containment if lead paint; weather-dependent; surface temp >3 degC above dew point.

**Right -- SHOP (Teal accent):** Alkaline wash 50--70 degC, pH 10--12, 5--15 min; solvent degrease; full blast room; controlled humidity/temp; easier time windows; ideal conditions.

## Phase 6 -- Time Windows + Critical Rules

### Humidity & Time Windows (Y: 21.0"--27.5")

Section label: `TIME WINDOWS -- CLEAN TO BLAST TO SPRAY`.

4-row table per AWS C2.18:

| RH | Max Time: Blast to Spray | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| <40% | Up to 8 hours | Low moisture risk |
| 40--60% | 4 hours max | AWS C2.18 baseline |
| 60--80% | 2 hours max | Flash rust risk increasing |
| >80% | 1 hour or less | High risk -- spray ASAP |

>80% row: highlighted `#E05C5C` at 12%. RH values in `#E8A020`, time in `#2EC4B6`.

Callout: `These are general guidelines per AWS C2.18. If flash rust appears, re-blast.`

### Critical Rules (Y: 27.5"--32.5")

Section label: `NON-NEGOTIABLE CLEANING RULES`. Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| CLEAN BEFORE BLAST | Blasting contaminated surfaces embeds oil into the profile |
| BLAST BEFORE SPRAY | Arc spray bonds by mechanical interlocking. No profile = no bond. |
| RESPECT THE TIME WINDOW | Flash rust begins within minutes in humid conditions |
| IDENTIFY LEAD PAINT FIRST | Lead removal requires containment, monitoring, disposal per EPA |
