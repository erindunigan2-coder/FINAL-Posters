# Poster #335 -- Construction Workup
## Two-Step Color Anodizing -- Process Flow

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This is the cluster overview poster for Two-Step Electrolytic Color Anodizing. The process has TWO electrochemical steps: (1) standard sulfuric acid anodize creates the pores, then (2) AC electrolysis deposits metal pigment (tin, cobalt, nickel) into the pore bases. Color from champagne to black, controlled by time. UV-stable. The industry standard for architectural color anodizing worldwide (AAMA 611).

Design philosophy: the U-flow diagram must clearly show BOTH electrochemical tanks and the transition between them. This is the most complex flow in the anodizing series.

## Design Notes

This is the most complex process flow in the anodizing series -- it has TWO electrochemical steps within a single stage. The flow box for Stage 7 needs to clearly show the two-tank transition: first the sulfuric acid anodize tank, then the metal salt coloring tank. Watson's note #6 emphasizes this. The comparison zone is intentionally reversed from Poster #327 -- here two-step is on the left (featured) and integral is on the right (comparison).
