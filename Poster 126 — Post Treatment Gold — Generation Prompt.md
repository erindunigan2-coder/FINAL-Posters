## Phase 4 -- Post-Treatment Sequence Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE FINAL STAGES -- RINSE, DRY, INSPECT`.

Three large panels (L-to-R):

**Stage 7 -- Hot DI Rinse (X: 0.5", W: 7.33"):** Badge `STAGE 7` (`#E8A020`). `140--160 F` / `DI only` / `30--60 sec` / `Gentle overflow`. Removes residual chemistry, prevents water spots. Note: `Hot DI is the difference between water-spotted and flawless.`

**Stage 8 -- Dry (X: 8.16", W: 7.33"):** Badge `STAGE 8` (`#2EC4B6`). Methods: Forced warm air / Vacuum dry / Centrifugal. `120--160 F air temp`. Note: `Do not overheat -- discoloration on decorative gold.`

**Inspect (X: 15.83", W: 7.67"):** Badge `INSPECT` (`#27AE60`). Visual: uniform color, no staining / Thickness: XRF or beta backscatter / Adhesion: tape or bend test / Hardness: 130--200 HK / Contact resistance per spec / Porosity: nitric acid vapor test. Note (Coral): `Deposits under 0.5 um are porous. Porosity testing required for corrosion-critical applications.`

## Phase 5 -- Thickness Measurement

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `GOLD THICKNESS MEASUREMENT -- THREE METHODS`.

**XRF (`#27AE60`):** Non-destructive / Fast 10--30 sec / Measures Au + Ni + substrate / Most common in production / +/- 5--10% for thin films.

**Beta Backscatter (`#E8A020`):** Non-destructive / Uses radioactive source / Good for multi-layer / Better accuracy on thin films / Requires radiation safety.

**Gravimetric (`#2EC4B6`):** Destructive / Weigh -> strip -> reweigh / Most accurate absolute / Used for calibration / ASTM B488 reference.

## Phase 6 -- Quality Specifications

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `KEY SPECIFICATIONS FOR GOLD PLATING`.

| Specification | Scope | Key Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| ASTM B488 | General electrodeposited gold | Thickness types I--VII, purity grades A--D, hardness |
| MIL-G-45204 | Military gold plating | Types I--III (purity), Classes 00--5 (thickness) |
| IPC-4552 | ENIG for PCBs | Au 0.05--0.23 um over Ni 3--6 um |
| IPC-4556 | ENEPIG for PCBs | Au/Pd/Ni stack for wire bonding |
| AMS 2422 | Aerospace gold plating | Hard gold for electrical contacts |

Note: `Know your customer specification before plating.`

## Phase 7 -- Application Matrix

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `POST-TREATMENT BY APPLICATION`.

| Application | Hot DI Rinse | Dry Method | Thickness Test | Hardness | Porosity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Connectors | REQUIRED | Forced air | XRF per lot | 130--200 HK | Per spec |
| PCB edge fingers | REQUIRED | Forced air | XRF | Per IPC | Per spec |
| Semiconductor (wire bond) | REQUIRED | Vacuum | XRF + gravimetric | Soft 60--80 HK | Required |
| Aerospace contacts | REQUIRED | Forced air | XRF + beta | Per AMS 2422 | Required |
| Jewelry / decorative | REQUIRED | Gentle air | XRF | Not critical | Optional |

Color code: REQUIRED = `#E05C5C`, specific = `#E8A020`, optional = `#2EC4B6`.

Bottom note: `Soft gold (wire bonding) and hard gold (connectors) have opposite priorities. Know which one you are plating.`
