## Phase 6 -- Defects

Y: 27.5" to 32.5". Four cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| DFT VARIATION ACROSS WIDTH | Uneven nip pressure / roll deflection | Check alignment; adjust nip profile |
| CHATTER MARKS (RIBBING) | Roll speed ratio too high / surface defect | Reduce ratio; re-grind roll |
| THIN EDGES | Edge effect from pressure profile | Adjust edge nip; check roll crown |
| VISCOSITY DRIFT | Temp change or solvent evaporation | Monitor continuously; add solvent to target |

Key insight: `Uniformity depends on nip pressure, roll speed ratio, and viscosity. Change one without the others and the film goes out of spec.`
