## Phase 4 -- Ten-Stage U-Flow Diagram

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section label: `THE COMPLETE PROCESS -- STAGE BY STAGE`.

Ten flow boxes in U-flow (top row L-to-R stages 1-5, bottom row R-to-L stages 6-10). Each box: rounded rect `#1E2435`, 4 pt top accent by stage type.

**Top Row (Stages 1-5):**

| Stage | Name | Accent | Key Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mandrel Fabrication | `#E8A020` | Machine, cast, or 3D-print; shape = final part shape; draft angles 1-3 deg |
| 2 | Mandrel Surface Prep | `#E8A020` | Polish to required finish; mandrel Ra = part exterior Ra; optical: Ra < 0.01 um |
| 3 | Release Agent / Make Conductive | `#E8A020` | Permanent: Cr passivation dip; expendable: no release needed; non-conductive: electroless Ni or Ag paint |
| 4 | Rack & Connect | `#2EC4B6` | Mount mandrel as CATHODE (-); position anodes (Ni or Cu); good electrical contact |
| 5 | Low-Current Strike | `#27AE60` | 50-75% of full CD; 10-30 min; ensures uniform nucleation |

**Bottom Row (Stages 6-10):**

| Stage | Name | Accent | Key Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Electroform (CORE) | `#27AE60` | Ni sulfamate: 3-5 A/dm2; 40-55 C, pH 3.5-4.5; hours to weeks; CRITICAL: monitor stress, thickness, chemistry (`#E05C5C`) |
| 7 | Remove & Rinse | `#2EC4B6` | DI water rinse; remove all electrolyte; trapped electrolyte causes staining |
| 8 | Mandrel Separation | `#E8A020` | Mechanical (pry/flex), thermal differential, chemical dissolution, melt-out |
| 9 | Post-Processing | `#C8D0D8` | Trim flash; machine to final dimensions; anneal 400-600 C optional |
| 10 | Inspect & Document | `#C8D0D8` | Thickness uniformity, interior Ra, hardness, ductility, dimensional (CMM); ASTM B832 |

**Stage Legend Strip (Y: 14.3"):** Amber = Mandrel Prep | Teal = Setup & Rinse | Emerald = Electroforming (Core) | Silver = Post-Process & QA | Coral = Caution/Critical

## Phase 6 -- Electroforming vs. Electroplating + Applications

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Two-panel comparison (Y: 22.0" to 28.5"):** Section label: `ELECTROFORMING VS. ELECTROPLATING`.

Left panel (`#27AE60` accent): `ELECTROFORMING` -- deposit separated from mandrel; thickness 25 um to 25 mm; hours to weeks; interior replicates mandrel; waveguides, molds, screens, printing plates.

Right panel (`#2EC4B6` accent): `ELECTROPLATING` -- deposit permanently bonds; thickness 1-250 um typical; minutes to hours; exterior is visible surface; corrosion, wear, decorative.

Bottom callout: `Same chemistry. Same physics. Different intent. In electroforming, the plating bath is not a surface treatment -- it is a manufacturing process.`

**Six Application Cards (Y: 28.5" to 32.5"):** Section label: `KEY APPLICATIONS`.

| Application | Metal | Thickness |
|---|---|---|
| Waveguides & Reflectors | Ni, Cu | 0.5-5 mm |
| Printing Plates & Holograms | Ni | 100-500 um |
| Precision Screens & Meshes | Ni, Cu | 25-250 um |
| Mold Inserts & Tooling | Ni, Ni-Co | 2-10 mm |
| CD/DVD Stampers | Ni | 100-300 um |
| LIGA Microstructures | Ni | 10-1000 um |
