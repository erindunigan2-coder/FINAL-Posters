## Phase 4 -- Rinse Tank Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE POST-COAT RINSE -- BRIEF AND WARM`.

Tank with parts entering from hot coating bath (190--210 F), exiting to oil seal. Temperature: `100--140 F` (amber). Time: `30 sec to 2 min MAXIMUM`. Purpose: remove acid and salts.

Warning callout (coral): `DO NOT OVER-RINSE. The porous coating absorbs water. Trapped water under oil = poor corrosion protection.`

Bottom: `Warm water prevents thermal shock on hot parts and dries faster than cold water. But keep it BRIEF.`

## Phase 5 -- The Over-Rinse Danger

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THE OVER-RINSE PROBLEM`.

**Left -- Correct (30 sec -- 2 min):** Emerald accent. Crystal cross-section with pores EMPTY. Surface salts washed away | Pore structure intact | Oil will fully penetrate | Corrosion protection MAXIMUM.

**Right -- Over-Rinsed (>3 min):** Coral accent. Crystal cross-section with pores FILLED with water. Water trapped in pores | Oil cannot displace water | Moisture causes under-film corrosion | Corrosion protection COMPROMISED.

---

## Phase 6 -- Timing + Temperature

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Why Warm Water?** Three temp zones: Cold (< 80 F) = thermal shock, slow drying = AVOID. Warm (100--140 F) = gradual cool-down, faster evaporation = CORRECT. Hot (> 150 F) = continued etching = AVOID.

**Right -- Timing Window:** Timeline bar: 0--30 sec = acid still on surface | 30 sec--2 min = OPTIMAL | 2--5 min = water absorption begins | > 5 min = OVER-RINSED. Bottom: `Rinse just long enough to remove surface acid. Then get the parts into oil.`

---

## Phase 7 -- Transition to Oil + Rules

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- No Dry-Off Oven:** Amber accent. Parts go directly from rinse to oil. No air dry, no oven, no forced air. The oil displaces residual water. This is unique to MnPhos. The oil IS the drying step.

**Right -- 6 Rules:** Warm rinse 100--140 F | 30 sec--2 min MAX | No aggressive agitation | Move to oil immediately | No air dry, no oven, no delay | Monitor rinse pH (neutral).
