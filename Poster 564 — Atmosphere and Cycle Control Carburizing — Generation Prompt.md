## Phase 5 -- Atmosphere Composition + Cp Interpretation

**Left -- Composition Table (W: 11.0", accent `#E8A020`):**

| Component | Target Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CO | 18.8--20.5% | Primary carbon carrier |
| H2 | 38--42% | Reducing agent; flammable |

| CO2 | 0.10--0.50% | Varies with Cp setpoint |
| CH4 | <0.5% | Generator efficiency indicator |
| Dew Point | -5 to +10 F | At 0.80% Cp |

**Right -- Cp Interpretation (W: 11.5", accent `#27AE60`):** Cp = % carbon atmosphere would produce at equilibrium. Critical thresholds: <0.70% = insufficient; 0.75--0.85% = DIFFUSE; 0.90--1.10% = BOOST; >1.10% = CARBIDE NETWORK RISK. O2 probe mV increases as Cp increases.

## Phase 6 -- Boost/Diffuse Cycle + Enrichment

**Boost/Diffuse (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `THE BOOST/DIFFUSE CYCLE -- TWO PHASES, ONE CASE`.

*Left -- Boost (Emerald):* Cp 0.90--1.10%. Rapid carbon absorption. High Cp gradient drives carbon in. Duration: ~2/3 of total. RISK: Cp >1.10% = carbide network.

*Right -- Diffuse (Teal):* Cp 0.75--0.85%. Carbon redistribution. Surface C drops to target. Dissolves any surface carbides. Duration: ~1/3. Without diffuse: excessive surface C, carbide networks, parts that LOOK carburized but fail.

Callout bar: Emerald tint. `Boost puts the carbon in. Diffuse puts the carbon where it belongs. Skip either and the metallurgy will punish you.`

**Enrichment & Soot (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `ENRICHMENT GAS & SOOT PREVENTION`. Four cards, Amber accent:
1. NATURAL GAS (CH4) -- most common enrichment; controlled by O2 probe feedback
2. PROPANE (C3H8) -- higher carbon per molecule; more soot-prone
3. SOOT = TROUBLE -- insulates surface; causes non-uniform case
4. OVER-ENRICHMENT -- probe reads atmosphere, not what PART sees through soot layer
