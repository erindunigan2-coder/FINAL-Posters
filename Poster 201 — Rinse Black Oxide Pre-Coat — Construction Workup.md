# Poster #201 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Black Oxide -- Pre-Coat

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 2 of 8. The post-clean rinse removes alkaline cleaner and surfactant residues before the acid pickle stage. In black oxide processing, this rinse also serves as the final quality gate before activation -- the water-break test happens here. If the water beads, the part goes back to cleaning.

### ZONE 6 -- Rinse Best Practices

**Section label:** `RINSE BEST PRACTICES FOR BLACK OXIDE` -- Y: 26.7".

**Single callout panel (Y: 27.3" to 32.3"):**

Rounded rect, full width (23.0"), H: 4.8", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`.

Six best practices, Inter Medium 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 180%:

```
1. Overflow continuously -- stagnant rinse water accumulates cleaner
2. Water-break test every load -- visual confirmation takes 5 seconds
3. Change rinse water when parts start emerging with beading
4. Monitor conductivity -- rising values = cleaner buildup
5. Spray rinse after immersion for complex geometries (blind holes, threads)
6. Black oxide is less forgiving than most processes -- rinse like it matters
```

## Design Notes

The water-break test visual is the most important element on this poster. Pass/fail should be immediately obvious at 6 feet. The dragover impact diagram connects this seemingly simple rinse to real downstream consequences -- it justifies why a rinse poster exists at all. Black oxide's extreme sensitivity to surface contamination makes this rinse stage more consequential than in most other processes.
