## Phase 6 -- Recovery Methods

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `GOLD RECOVERY METHODS`.

Three panels:

**Return to Bath (`#27AE60`):** Stagnant recovery -> test -> add back / Simplest / Risk: introducing contamination / Best for clean lines.

**Electrolytic Cell (`#E8A020`):** Gold deposits on cathode from dilute rinse / 95%+ recovery / Produces high-purity gold / Best for high-volume.

**Ion Exchange (`#2EC4B6`):** Gold-selective resin captures Au / 99%+ recovery / Resin stripped periodically / Best for precision recovery.
