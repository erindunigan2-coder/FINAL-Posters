# Poster #236 -- Construction Workup
## Electroless Nickel (High Phos) -- Main Tank

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. This is the heart of the process -- where chemistry does all the work without a single amp of external current. No rectifier. No anodes. Just a carefully balanced autocatalytic reaction that deposits uniform Ni-P alloy on every surface it can reach. This poster is the densest in the EN High-P cluster, comparable to Poster #36 (Zinc Alkaline Main Tank). It covers bath composition, operating parameters, the autocatalytic reaction, deposit properties, MTO tracking, and bath stability management.

Hero visual: a plating tank cross-section -- but unlike electrolytic plating posters, there are no anodes and no current flow lines. The hero shows parts immersed in heated solution with uniform deposition occurring on all surfaces simultaneously.

## Design Notes

This is the densest poster in the EN High-P cluster. The visual absence of anodes and rectifier in the tank diagram is the single most important design element -- it communicates "autocatalytic" more powerfully than any text. The pH gauge is the equivalent of the NaOH:Zn ratio gauge in Poster #36 -- it is the master control parameter. The MTO tracking panel teaches bath life management, which is unique to electroless processes (electrolytic baths can run indefinitely with proper maintenance; EN baths have a finite, measurable life). The decomposition warning is deadly serious -- spontaneous EN decomposition is exothermic and has caused real-world fires.
