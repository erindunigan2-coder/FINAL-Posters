# Poster #552 -- Construction Workup
## Grit Blasting -- Wire Combustion Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Grit blasting for wire combustion spray is more aggressive than aerospace-grade thermal spray preparation. The profile is coarser (Ra 4--12 um vs. 3--8 um for APS) because wire combustion produces larger, slower droplets that need a deeper anchor pattern for mechanical interlocking. Steel grit is common for infrastructure work (cheaper, recyclable in shop blasters); alumina is used when ferrous contamination is a concern. SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal Blast) is the standard per AWS C2.18.

Column widths: Parameter (5.0") | Specification (6.0") | Notes (12.0")

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Media | Angular alumina (Al2O3) or angular steel grit | Alumina: no ferrous contamination; steel grit: lower cost, recyclable |
| Grit size | 16--36 mesh | Coarser than APS/HVOF (24--60 mesh); deeper profile needed |
| Blast pressure | 60--100 PSI (415--690 kPa) | Higher than aerospace (40--80 PSI); structural steel is less sensitive |

| Surface cleanliness | SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal Blast) | Per AWS C2.18; equivalent to ISO 8501-1 Sa 3 |

### ZONE 4 -- Field vs. Shop Blasting

**Section label:** `FIELD BLASTING vs. SHOP BLASTING` -- Y: 22.2".

**Left -- BLOCK F: Field Blasting (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020` 0.06".

Title: `FIELD BLASTING` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`.

- `Portable blast pots (100--600 lb capacity)`
- `Expendable media (alumina or garnet -- not recyclable in field)`
- `Dust control: vacuum shroud, wet abrasive blasting, or tarps`
- `Environmental permits may be required (dust, lead paint)`
- `Humidity and dew point monitoring critical in open air`
- `Wind affects blast pattern and fume/dust direction`
- `Old lead paint requires lead-safe work practices`
- `Containment may be required on bridges (blast curtains, vacuum)`

**Right -- BLOCK G: Shop Blasting (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#27AE60` 0.06".

Title: `SHOP BLASTING` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#27AE60`.

- `Blast room or cabinet with recyclable media (steel grit preferred)`
- `Dust collection integrated into booth`
- `Controlled humidity and temperature`
- `Consistent blast parameters (pressure, distance, angle)`
- `Media recycling via magnetic separator and classifier`
- `Shorter blast-to-spray time window (same facility)`
- `Better quality control overall`
- `Not always practical -- many structures cannot be brought to shop`
