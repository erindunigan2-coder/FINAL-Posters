## Phase 5 -- Parameters + Drag-Out

Y: 14.5" to 21.5". Section: `POST-PLATE RINSE PARAMETERS`.

**Left table (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Water | DI strongly recommended |
| Temperature | Ambient |
| Stages | 3-stage cascade MINIMUM |
| Flow | 3--5 gal/min/stage |
| Time | 30--60 sec/stage |
| Agitation | Air + part movement |
| Conductivity | < 100 uS/cm (tightest) |
| pH | 6--8 final stage |
| Drain | 15--20 sec (longer) |

**Right callout (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):** `DRAG-OUT ECONOMICS` `#E8A020`.
- Each rack: 50--150 mL of bath = 7--21 g NaOH
- 50 racks/shift = 350--1050 g NaOH into rinse
- If inadequate, this enters and destroys the passivate
- Tip: spray rinse nozzles at zinc exit reduce drag-out

## Phase 6 -- Problems

Y: 21.5" to 27.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT POST-PLATE RINSE`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passivate failure | No color; salt spray fail | NaOH carry-over | Add stages; spray rinse |
| White haze | Cloudy before passivate | Zn(OH)2 in alkaline rinse | Neutral rinse water |
| Passivate pH rising | Constant acid additions | Drag-out overwhelming | Improve efficiency |
| Staining/streaks | Uneven passivate | Localized alkaline residue | Air agitation; spray |
| Water spots | Marks after dry | Hard water | DI final stage |

## Phase 7 -- Monitoring + Safety

**Left -- Monitoring:** Conductivity EVERY HOUR; pH paper; if passivate drifting up, rinse is failing; dump at >300 uS/cm.
**Right -- Safety:** Alkaline rinse water (pH 9--11); burns from drag-out; overflow to waste treatment; standard PPE.
