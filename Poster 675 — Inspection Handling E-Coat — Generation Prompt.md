## Phase 4 -- Six-Test Grid Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.0". Section label: `THE SIX ESSENTIAL E-COAT TESTS`. 3x2 grid.

**DFT (`#27AE60`):** D7091. 0.6--1.2 mils. 3--5 readings/part, min 5 parts/rack.
**Adhesion (`#27AE60`):** D3359 Method B. 5B (no removal).
**MEK Double Rub (`#E8A020`):** D4752. 100+ double rubs. Every oven zone change.
**Wet Adhesion (`#E8A020`):** Soak 104 F DI for 240 hr. 4B--5B after soak.
**Throwing Power (`#2EC4B6`):** DFT inside standardized test box (2x4x12"). Interior/exterior ratio.
**Rupture Voltage (`#2EC4B6`):** Ramp voltage on wet film until breakdown. Weekly.

## Phase 5 -- Bath Monitoring Schedule

Y: 15.3" to 20.3". Section label: `BATH MONITORING -- DAILY AND WEEKLY DISCIPLINE`.

**Left -- Daily:** pH (5.8--6.2) | Conductivity (1,000--1,800) | Temp (85--95 F) | Solids (18--22%) | P/B (0.15--0.25).

**Right -- Weekly:** MEQ (30--45) | Solvent content | Rupture voltage | UF permeate flow (>70% baseline) | UF conductivity | UF pressure (< 40 psi).

Bottom: `Bath monitoring is not optional. E-coat drifts slowly -- by the time you see defects, the bath has been off-spec for days.` (`#E05C5C`)

## Phase 6 -- Throwing Power + Salt Spray

Y: 20.7" to 25.8". Two-column.

**Left -- `THROWING POWER MEASUREMENT` (`#2EC4B6`):**
Test box 2x4x12". Measure DFT at outside, 2", 6", 12" depth. Throwing power index = DFT at 12" / DFT at outside. Cathodic: 8--12" penetration. Higher voltage = deeper (but rupture risk at edges).

**Right -- `CORROSION PERFORMANCE` (`#27AE60`):**

| System | B117 Hours |
|---|---|
| E-coat alone on CRS + ZnPO4 | 500--1,000+ |
| Full automotive (e-coat + surfacer + BC/CC) | 1,500--4,000+ |
| E-coat alone on galvanized | 1,000--2,000+ |

Cyclic corrosion (SAE J2334, GMW14872) now primary OEM method.

## Phase 6B -- Troubleshooting Strip

Y: 26.8" to 32.3". 4 cards, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| LOW DFT IN CAVITIES | Voltage low, conductivity off | Increase voltage; profile test box |
| MEK RUB FAILURE | Undercure | Oven profile with data loggers |
| ADHESION FAILURE (WET) | Phosphate compromised or seal depleted | Check phosphate weight; replenish seal |
| DECLINING THROWING POWER | Bath chemistry drift | Full bath analysis; check anode boxes |
