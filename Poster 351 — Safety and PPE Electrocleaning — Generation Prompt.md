## Phase 6 -- Emergency Procedures

Y: 21.0" to 28.5". Section label: `EMERGENCY RESPONSE -- THREE SCENARIOS`.

2x2 grid of glass cards (coral top accent):

**Caustic Skin Contact:** 1. Flush immediately, large volumes. 2. Continue 15 min. 3. Do NOT neutralize with acid. 4. Remove clothing while flushing. 5. Seek medical attention.

**Eye Contact:** 1. Flush eyewash 15 min. 2. Hold eyelids open. 3. Alkaline burns cause LIQUEFACTIVE NECROSIS -- damage worsens. 4. Seek IMMEDIATE attention.

**Electrical Shock / Arc Flash:** 1. De-energize rectifier (emergency stop). 2. Do NOT touch victim if still in contact. 3. Call emergency services. 4. First aid/CPR if trained. 5. Report per OSHA 29 CFR 1904.

**Hydrogen Gas Alarm:** 1. Evacuate immediate area. 2. Eliminate ignition sources (no switches, no sparks). 3. Increase ventilation. 4. Do NOT de-energize rectifier if switch could spark -- use remote disconnect. 5. Monitor with combustible gas detector before re-entry.
