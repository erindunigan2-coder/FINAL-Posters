Y: 20.0" to 25.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Salt Spray Performance:** Two comparison bars: Bare MnPhos (no oil) = 2--8 hours (coral). MnPhos + Oil = 48--200+ hours (emerald). Bottom: `That is a 25x to 100x improvement. The coating without oil is almost pointless.`

## Phase 7 -- Defects + Rules

Y: 25.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- 4 Defects:** Rust under oil (water trapped before oiling), dripping excess oil (insufficient drain), dry spots (air traps), oil staining (wrong oil type).

**Right -- 7 Rules:**
1. OIL IS MANDATORY -- bare MnPhos is useless
2. Hot oil (150--180 F) for military spec
3. Immerse 2--5 min for full pore penetration
4. Go directly from rinse to oil -- no drying step
5. Drain and centrifuge excess oil
6. No oven dry before OR after oil
7. Verify oil meets specification
