# Poster #139 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Cadmium -- Pre-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 4 of 8. Same acid-to-cyanide safety concern as silver (Poster #131) -- HCl drag-in to the cyanide cadmium bath generates lethal HCN gas. This rinse is SAFETY CRITICAL. Additionally, cadmium adds a second hazard layer: the cadmium bath itself is a carcinogenic and cyanide-bearing solution.

**Section label:** `THE PRE-PLATE RINSE -- SAFETY CRITICAL` -- Y: 4.4".

**BLOCK B -- Tank Cross-Section (Y: 5.0" to 14.0")**

Standard rinse tank. Same layout and safety callout format as Poster #131.

**Operating parameters:**
- `Temperature: Ambient`
- `Flow: Continuous overflow`
- `Time: 30--60 sec`
- `Type: Single rinse, then to cadmium plate`

**Safety callout (large, centered):**
- `THIS RINSE REMOVES HCl BEFORE PARTS ENTER CYANIDE CADMIUM. HCl + NaCN = HCN (LETHAL GAS). RINSE MUST BE THOROUGH.` Barlow SemiBold 16 pt `#E05C5C`

- `CADMIUM BATH = CYANIDE + CADMIUM. Both are lethal hazards. This rinse protects against adding a third (HCN gas).` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E05C5C`

## Design Notes

Structurally similar to Poster #131 (Silver Pre-Plate Rinse) because the acid/cyanide hazard is identical. The cadmium-specific addition is the "double hazard" note -- the cadmium bath is already a dual-hazard environment (cyanide + cadmium carcinogen), and acid drag-in would create a triple threat.
