## Phase 4 -- Substrate Stack + Cleaning Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section: `THE GOLD PLATING SUBSTRATE STACK`.

**Left half -- Substrate Stack Cross-Section:** Layered horizontal rects (bottom to top): Base metal (Cu, Kovar) fill `#C8D0D8` | Nickel barrier (0.5--2 um) fill `#2EC4B6` at 30% | Gold deposit (target) fill `#E8A020` at 50%. Arrow: `THIS IS THE SURFACE YOU ARE CLEANING`. Note: `Nickel underplate is REQUIRED under gold. Prevents copper migration.`

**Right half -- Cleaning Steps:** Vertical flow of 4 steps (fill `#1E2435`, accent `#2EC4B6`):
1. `Mild Alkaline Soak Clean` -- `3--5 oz/gal, 130--150 F, 3--5 min, non-silicated`
2. `Rinse` -- `Ambient, flowing`
3. `Acid Dip` -- `5--10% H2SO4, 15--30 sec` / `NEVER HCl -- chloride kills gold baths`
4. `DI Water Rinse -> Gold Bath` -- `DI water only for final rinse`

Optional branch: `Gold Strike` (dashed border, `#E8A020`) -- `1--4 g/L Au, pH 3.5--5.0, 90--120 F, 15--60 sec` / `Flash coat for difficult substrates`.

## Phase 6 -- Contamination Sensitivity

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHY GOLD BATHS DEMAND PERFECT CLEANING`.

**Left -- Gold Bath Sensitivity (accent `#E05C5C`):**

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | > 5 ppm | Dark deposit, porosity |
| Iron | > 10 ppm | Dark deposit, roughness |
| Zinc | > 1 ppm | Pitting, discoloration |
| Chloride | > 1 ppm | Catastrophic -- attacks gold complex |
| Organic | Any visible | Dark, stressed deposit |

Note: `These thresholds are 5--10x lower than zinc or nickel baths.`

**Right -- The Standard (accent `#27AE60`):** Title: `THE STANDARD`.
Water-break-free (absolute) / No visible residue under 10x / pH neutral after rinse / DI water final rinse / Parts processed immediately / Gloves at all times.

Bottom note: `In gold plating, cleaning is not a step. It is a religion.`

## Phase 7 -- Common Mistakes

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `4 CLEANING MISTAKES THAT CONTAMINATE GOLD BATHS`.

| # | X | Mistake | Result | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | HCl ACTIVATION | Chloride drag-in -- gold bath contamination | H2SO4 only, always |
| 2 | 6.33" | TAP WATER RINSE | Mineral drag-in (Ca, Mg, Fe) | DI water for pre-plate rinse |
| 3 | 12.16" | BARE-HAND CONTACT | Fingerprint oils contaminate bath | Gloves mandatory |
| 4 | 18.0" | DELAYED TRANSFER | Nickel oxidizes in minutes | Process immediately after cleaning |
