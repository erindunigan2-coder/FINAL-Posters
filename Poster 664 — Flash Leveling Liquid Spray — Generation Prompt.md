## Phase 4 -- Three Flash Environments Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.5". Section label: `THREE FLASH ENVIRONMENTS -- MATCHED TO YOUR COATING`. Three columns.

**Ambient Flash (`#27AE60`):** 65--85 F / 5--15 min between coats / < 85% RH. Solvent evaporates naturally. Best for: Standard alkyds, lacquers.

**Force Flash (`#E8A020`):** 140--180 F / 5--10 min. Drives off solvent before bake. Best for: Before bake cure. Warning `#E05C5C`: `Skipping force flash before bake = solvent pop`

**Waterborne Flash (`#2EC4B6`):** 70--80 F / 50--70% RH (CRITICAL) / 3--10 min. Water evap slower than solvent. Best for: Waterborne basecoats. Note: Requires environmental control.

## Phase 5 -- Intercoat Recoat Window

Y: 15.8" to 20.8". Section label: `THE RECOAT WINDOW -- MISS IT AND YOU ARE SANDING`.

**Left -- Recoat Window Table (W: 13.0"):**

| Coating Type | Recoat Window | If Exceeded | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alkyd enamel | 24--72 hr | Surface too hard | Scuff 320--400 grit |
| 2K Epoxy | 4--24 hr (CRITICAL) | No topcoat adhesion | Scuff 320--400 grit |
| 2K Urethane | 1--24 hr | Cures beyond compatibility | Scuff 320--400 grit |
| Lacquer | Unlimited | Solvent re-dissolves | No sanding needed |
| Waterborne latex | 24--48 hr | Film coalesces fully | Scuff sand |

**Right -- `THE EPOXY TRAP` (border `#E05C5C`):**
`2K epoxy is the most common recoat window failure. The 4--24 hour window is short and temperature-dependent. No exceptions.`

## Phase 6 -- Leveling Mechanics + Tail Solvents

Y: 21.8" to 26.3". Section label: `HOW SOLVENT BLEND CONTROLS LEVELING`.

**Left -- `LEVELING -- WHAT CAUSES ORANGE PEEL` (accent `#E8A020`):**
4 causes: too-fast evap, low atomization, gun too far, spray too dry. Fix: slower blend, higher pressure, closer, heavier wet film.

**Right -- `TAIL SOLVENTS -- THE LEVELING SECRET` (accent `#27AE60`):**
Slow-evaporating solvents (butyl cellosolve, PM acetate, butyl carbitol) keep film fluid for flow. Too much = sag. Too little = orange peel.

## Phase 6B -- Defect Grid

Y: 27.3" to 32.3". 3x2 grid.

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORANGE PEEL | `#E8A020` | Solvent too fast | Slower thinner; more tail solvent |
| SOLVENT POP | `#E05C5C` | Trapped solvent boiling in bake | Force flash before bake |
| SAGS / RUNS | `#E05C5C` | Flash too slow or too heavy | Reduce thickness; improve airflow |
| INTERCOAT DELAMINATION | `#E8A020` | Recoat window exceeded | Scuff 320--400 grit |
| SKIN-OVER (WATERBORNE) | `#2EC4B6` | Too dry or aggressive airflow | Increase humidity; reduce airflow |
| LIFTING / WRINKLING | `#E05C5C` | Strong solvent attacking uncured primer | Full primer flash; verify compatibility |
