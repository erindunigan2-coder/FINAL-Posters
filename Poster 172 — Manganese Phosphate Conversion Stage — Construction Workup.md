# Poster #172 -- Construction Workup
## Manganese Phosphate -- Conversion Stage

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 5 of 8. This is the heart of the process -- the hot immersion tank where hureaulite crystals form on the steel surface. This poster is the most content-dense in the CC-03 cluster. It covers bath chemistry, the critical temperature requirement (190--210 F, ALWAYS), the free acid / total acid ratio, coating weight interpretation, and sludge management. The hero story: hureaulite crystal structure -- Mn5H2(PO4)4.4H2O -- a porous crystalline matrix purpose-built to absorb and hold oil.

Hero visual: coating tank cross-section showing immersion at operating temperature, with a magnified inset of the hureaulite crystal structure on the substrate surface.

### ZONE 5 -- Coating Weight + Sludge Management

**Left -- Coating Weight Interpretation (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Section label: `COATING WEIGHT INTERPRETATION` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

| Weight (mg/ft2) | Interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| < 500 | Too light -- insufficient crystal coverage | Increase time/temp; check F/A ratio |
| 500--1000 | Light -- acceptable for some applications | Verify against spec requirement |
| 1000--2000 | OPTIMAL (military spec target) | Ideal for oil retention and wear |
| 2000--2500 | Heavy -- acceptable but approaching max | Monitor for powdering |
| > 2500 | Too heavy -- powdery, poor adhesion | Reduce time; check bath chemistry |

Weight column: JetBrains Mono 13 pt. 1000--2000 row highlighted with emerald accent.

Additional data:
- `Thickness: 5--25 um (0.2--1.0 mil)` JetBrains Mono 12 pt `#F0EDE8`
- `Test method: ASTM B767 (gravimetric)` JetBrains Mono 12 pt `#2EC4B6`

**Right -- Sludge Management (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Section label: `SLUDGE -- THE INEVITABLE BYPRODUCT` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#E8A020`.

- Rounded rect, H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`

Content (Inter Regular 14 pt `#F0EDE8`, line height 155%):
- `Sludge is normal -- it is the spent reaction byproduct`
- `Fe3(PO4)2 and Mn3(PO4)2 precipitate as the bath works`
- `Excessive sludge: bath overheated, high iron, excessive acid`
- `Continuous filtration is standard -- bag or cartridge filters`
- `Settle-and-decant weekly for heavy production`
- `Sludge volume: 2--5% of bath volume per week (typical)`

Warning callout:
- `Sludge that re-deposits on parts causes rough, non-uniform coating. Filter continuously.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E05C5C`

### ZONE 6 -- Defect Grid + Key Rules

**Left -- Defect Grid (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**

Section label: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 5 COMMON DEFECTS` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

5 defect cards in a grid (3 top + 2 bottom):

| Position | Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | LIGHT/THIN COATING | Low temp (<185 F); short time; high free acid | Increase to 195--205 F; extend time; reduce F/A |
| R1C2 | RED/BROWN POWDER | Bath boiling; excess iron; depleted bath | Cool to 195--205 F; decant; add replenisher |
| R1C3 | NON-UNIFORM | Poor cleaning; mixed metallurgy; passive surface | Improve clean; separate hard/soft parts |
| R2C1 | EXCESSIVE SLUDGE | Overheated; excess acid; high iron | Control temp; filter continuously; maintain F/A |
| R2C2 | POOR OIL RETENTION | Too thin; crystals too fine; over-rinsed | Increase coating weight; reduce rinse |

Each card: Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, radius 4, left accent `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- Key Rules (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):**

Section label: `THE RULES` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

7 rule rows, each with emerald left border:
1. `190--210 F. ALWAYS.`
2. `Below 185 F, coating stops`
3. `Do not boil -- excessive sludge`
4. `Immersion only -- never spray`
5. `10--30 min -- longer than zinc phosphate`
6. `F/A ratio 1:7 to 1:12`
7. `Filter continuously`

## Design Notes

This is the densest poster in CC-03, comparable to Poster #36 (Zinc Main Tank) in information density. The temperature callout must be impossible to miss -- "190--210 F, ALWAYS" should read from 10 feet. The crystal structure inset is the unique visual element that no other poster in the series has -- it explains WHY the coating works (porous crystal matrix holds oil). The F/A ratio gauge mirrors the NaOH:Zn ratio gauge from Poster #36, giving the series visual consistency.
