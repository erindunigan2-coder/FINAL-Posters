## Phase 5 -- Drag-Out and Alkaline Carry-Over

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `DRAG-OUT AND ALKALINE CARRY-OVER`.

Three panels: DRAG-OUT (`#E8A020`) -- thin film on parts | RINSE REMOVES IT (`#2EC4B6`) -- immersion dilutes | DRAG-IN (`#E05C5C`) -- whatever is NOT removed enters acid. Cadmium-specific: `Alkaline drag-in to acid is doubly wasteful -- acid must be effective because parts need MINIMUM time in it. Weak acid = longer immersion = more hydrogen absorbed.`

## Phase 6 -- Rinse Efficiency

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `SINGLE VS. CASCADE RINSE`.

Single (accent `#E8A020`): adequate but higher risk. Cascade (accent `#27AE60`): recommended for cadmium lines.

Banner: `Cascade rinse recommended to ensure thorough alkaline removal before the time-critical acid activation step.`

## Phase 7 -- Contamination Consequences

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHEN THE RINSE FAILS`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ALKALINE IN ACID BATH | Insufficient rinse | Acid neutralized; activation fails |
| 2 | EXTENDED ACID TIME | Weak acid requires longer dip | More hydrogen absorbed into steel |
| 3 | PARTICULATE CARRY-OVER | Dirty rinse water | Rough cadmium deposit |
| 4 | INCONSISTENT ACTIVATION | Variable rinse quality | Variable adhesion lot to lot |
