## Phase 5 -- Operating Parameters

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `PARAMETERS AT A GLANCE`.

| Parameter | External Acid Dip | In-Tank Activation |
|---|---|---|
| Chemistry | 1--5% H2SO4 or 1--3% HCl | Chrome bath solution |
| Temperature | Ambient | Bath temp (80--120 F tri; 95--120 F hex) |
| Time | 10--30 sec | 5--15 sec (before plating current) |
| Current | None (chemical activation) | None (immersion) or brief anodic (hex) |
| Rinse after | Single rinse before chrome | Not applicable (already in chrome) |
| Advantage | Guaranteed activation even after delay | Eliminates a tank; fastest path |
| Disadvantage | Adds transfer time; another tank | Requires fast Ni-to-Cr transfer |

## Phase 6 -- Adhesion Science

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHY ACTIVATION MATTERS -- THE NICKEL PASSIVE FILM`.

Full-width panel. Three-stage horizontal illustration:

| Stage | Visual Tint | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emerald | `FRESH NICKEL` | Active metal surface. Chrome bonds directly to the crystal lattice. |
| 2 | Coral | `PASSIVATED NICKEL` | Nickel oxide film (NiO/Ni(OH)2). Only nanometers thick but blocks adhesion. |
| 3 | Emerald | `ACTIVATED NICKEL` | Acid dissolves the oxide. Clean metal exposed. Chrome can bond. |

Each: rounded rect W: 7.0", H: 2.5", fill `#252B3D`.

Bottom: `The passive film is invisible. You cannot see it, smell it, or feel it. But chrome knows it is there -- and it will peel.` -- `#E05C5C`

## Phase 7 -- Failure Modes

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 ACTIVATION FAILURES`.

2x2 grid:

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHROME PEELING | `#E05C5C` | Activation skipped or insufficient | Add or extend activation step |
| SPOTTY ADHESION | `#E8A020` | Uneven acid contact (air pockets, poor racking) | Improve rack design; agitate during dip |
| ETCHED NICKEL | `#E05C5C` | Acid too strong or dwell too long | Reduce concentration; tighten time control |
| HAZE ON CHROME | `#E8A020` | Acid drag-in to chrome bath (no rinse) | Rinse after external acid dip |
