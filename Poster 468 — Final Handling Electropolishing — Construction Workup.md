# Poster #468 -- Construction Workup
## Final Handling -- Electropolishing

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

The last poster in the Electropolishing cluster. A perfect EP finish can be ruined in the last 30 feet between the inspection bench and the shipping dock. This poster covers protective packaging, storage conditions, contamination prevention during transport, and customer-facing documentation. It is the bridge between the EP process and the customer receiving a flawless part.

### ZONE 4 -- Protective Materials

**Section label:** `PROTECTIVE MATERIALS` -- Y: 14.7".

---

**BLOCK D -- Materials Matrix (Y: 15.3" to 19.5")**

Table -- columns: Material (4.5") | Type (4.0") | Protection (5.0") | Use For (5.0") | Avoid When (4.5")

| Material | Type | Protection | Use For | Avoid When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Polyethylene (PE) film | Wrapping, bags | Moisture barrier, physical protection | Universal -- first choice | N/A -- always acceptable |
| VCI paper/film | Wrapping, interleaving | Vapor corrosion inhibitor + physical | Long-term storage; steel, SS | Copper/brass (some VCI incompatible) |
| Desiccant packs | In-package moisture control | Absorbs humidity inside sealed package | Sealed bags/containers; humid climates | Loose desiccant near parts (particle risk) |
| Foam sheets/inserts | Cushioning, separation | Impact and scratch protection | Between stacked parts; inside containers | Closed-cell only; open-cell traps moisture |
| Nitrogen purge | Inert atmosphere inside bag | Prevents oxidation and tarnishing | Pharma, semi, long-storage | N/A -- always beneficial if available |
| Custom trays/fixtures | Rigid support | Prevents movement during transit | High-value, complex geometry parts | Overkill for simple flat parts |

Header: `#3A4055`. Data: Inter Regular 12 pt `#F0EDE8`.

**Materials to AVOID (below table, Y: 19.8"):**
- Rounded rect, X: 0.5", W: 23.0", H: 0.8", fill `#E05C5C` at 15%, border 1 pt `#E05C5C`
- Text: `NEVER USE: PVC film (HCl offgassing), rubber (sulfur attack), newspaper/printed cardboard (ink transfer), bare wood (tannic acid stain), kraft paper with sulfur` Barlow SemiBold 13 pt `#E05C5C`

**BLOCK F -- Four Damage Prevention Cards (Y: 22.9" to 28.0")**

2x2 grid:

| Position | Damage Type | Accent | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | SCRATCHING / ABRASION | `#E05C5C` | Foam separators between ALL contact surfaces. Never allow metal-to-metal contact. Immobilize parts inside container so they cannot shift during transit. |
| R1C2 | CORROSION IN TRANSIT | `#E8A020` | Sealed moisture barrier with VCI and desiccant. Avoid shipping during extreme humidity. If international shipment, consider salt-air exposure (coastal and ocean freight). |
| R2C1 | IMPACT DAMAGE | `#E05C5C` | Rigid outer container (wood crate or heavy corrugated). Minimum 2 inches of cushioning on all sides. FRAGILE marking. Pallet-mount for large parts. |
| R2C2 | CONTAMINATION | `#E8A020` | Sealed inner packaging. Double-bagged for pharma/semi. Clean packaging area (not next to grinding or welding operations). N2 purge for highest spec. |

Each card: Rounded rect, W: 11.0", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`, left accent 0.06".

## Design Notes

This is the bookend to the cluster -- it closes the loop. The packaging-by-application cards (Block B) give immediate, industry-specific guidance. The materials-to-avoid callout is a direct loss-prevention tool -- PVC film and rubber are common packaging materials that will damage an EP surface. The documentation checklist (Zone 6) is the quality engineer's pre-shipment checklist. A shop that follows this poster from top to bottom ships a perfect part with complete traceability.
