## Phase 5 -- Parameters + Corona vs. Tribo

**Application Parameters** (7-row table): gun voltage 60--100 kV, current 10--80 uA, flow 100--400 g/min, distance 6--12 in, particle D50 30--45 um, booth air 60--100 fpm, transfer 60--70%/95--98%.

**Corona vs. Tribo**: Corona (Amber) -- HV electrode ionizes air, fast build, all powder types, back-ionization risk, poor Faraday penetration, general production. Tribo (Teal) -- friction charge in PTFE barrel, no back-ionization, better Faraday penetration, slower build, tribo-specific powder needed, complex geometries.

## Phase 6 -- DFT Targets + NFPA 654 + Reclaim + Defect Grid

**DFT Table**: decorative 1.5--3.0 mils, general industrial 2.0--4.0, architectural 2.5--4.0, automotive 3.0--5.0, functional 7--14, fluidized bed 8--25+.

**Fluidized Bed**: parts preheated 400--500 F, dipped in bed, 8--25+ mils, primarily thermoplastic.

**NFPA 654 Safety** (Coral): combustible dust hazard, maintain booth air velocity, ground everything, housekeeping < 1/32" accumulation, NO ignition sources.

**Reclaim**: cyclone separator (90--95%, long runs) | cartridge filter (95--98%, fast color change).

**Defect Grid** (3x2): back-ionization, orange peel, thin in recesses, uneven DFT, poor wrap, contamination/seeds.
