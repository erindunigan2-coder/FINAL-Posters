# Poster #200 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Black Oxide

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 1 of 8. Cleaning for black oxide is more demanding than most processes. Every fingerprint, every trace of oil, every speck of shop soil will appear as a red or brown defect on the finished black part. The motto: "If you can see it before blackening, everyone will see it after." This poster hammers that point with cleaning parameter data, a two-method comparison (soak clean vs. electro-clean), and a contamination consequence grid.
