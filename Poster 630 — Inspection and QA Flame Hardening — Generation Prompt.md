## Phase 4 -- Five-Step Inspection Sequence (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `FIVE-STEP INSPECTION SEQUENCE`. Five cards in a row, top accent `#27AE60`.

| Step | Title | Method | Standard | What to Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HARDNESS | Rockwell C | ASTM E18 | Surface hardness at specified locations |
| 2 | PATTERN | Acid etch (nital/HCl) | Visual/print | Hardened zone boundary vs. template |
| 3 | CRACK CHECK | MPI or dye penetrant | ASTM E1444/E165 | Surface cracks; focus on section transitions |
| 4 | CASE DEPTH | File test or microhardness | ASTM E384/SAE J423 | Effective case to 50 HRC |
| 5 | OVERLAP ZONES | Visual + hardness traverse | Per process spec | Soft/hard spots at progressive overlap |

Overlap zone diagram: horizontal bar with Pass 1, Overlap, Pass 2, Overlap, Pass 3 zones. Risk: `Overlap heated twice -- may temper or re-austenitize.` Check: `Run hardness traverse across overlap.`

## Phase 5 -- Defect Reference Table

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `COMMON DEFECTS -- CAUSE AND REMEDY`. 7-row table.

| Defect | Cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Overheating / melting | Flame too close; dwell too long | Increase distance; increase speed |
| Soft spots | Insufficient temp; overlap zone temper-back | Verify temp; adjust overlap |
| Cracking | Quench too severe; pre-existing stress | Reduce quench; preheat; stress relieve |
| Non-uniform case | Manual technique variation | Automate traverse; CNC control |
| Distortion | Asymmetric heating | Balance heat pattern; reduce case depth |
| Excessive oxidation | Oxidizing flame; too many passes | Neutral flame; minimize exposure |
| Decarburization | Oxidizing flame; excessive temp | Neutral/reducing flame; minimize heat time |

## Phase 6 -- Acceptance Criteria + Footer

**Left -- PASS** (Emerald accent): hardness within spec (55--62 HRC typical), case depth within tolerance, pattern matches, no cracks (MPI/PT), overlap within 3 HRC, no melting/oxidation/decarb, distortion within tolerance.

**Right -- REJECT/REWORK** (Coral accent): hardness below min, case depth out, cracks (zero tolerance), melting (scrap), decarb > 0.005 in, pattern deviation, distortion beyond limits.
