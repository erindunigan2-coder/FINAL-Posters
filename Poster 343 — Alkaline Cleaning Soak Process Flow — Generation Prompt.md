## Phase 5 -- Soil Classification Table

Y: 15.0" to 21.5". Section label: `KNOW YOUR ENEMY -- SOIL CLASSIFICATION (ASTM B322)`.

Three-row glass table. Columns: Soil Type | Examples | Removal Mechanism | Cleaner Requirement.

| Soil Type | Examples | Mechanism | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saponifiable (Polar) `#27AE60` | Lard oil, tallow, palm oil, stearic acid | Saponification -- alkali reacts with fatty acid to form water-soluble soap + glycerol | Elevated temp (>60 C); sufficient NaOH |
| Non-Saponifiable (Non-Polar) `#2EC4B6` | Mineral oil, petroleum grease, silicone oil, synthetic coolants | Emulsification -- surfactants encapsulate oil droplets in micelles | Surfactant above CMC (0.1-1 g/L nonionic); temp below cloud point |
| Semi-Solid / Particulate `#E8A020` | Buffing compound (grease + abrasive), metal fines, carbon | Combination of emulsification + mechanical displacement | May require pre-soak, spray, or two-stage clean |

Bottom callout (emerald-tinted glass pill): `Rule of thumb: if you can identify the soil, you can select the cleaner. Saponifiable soils are easy. Non-saponifiable need surfactant. Buffing compound needs everything.`

## Phase 6 -- Substrate Routing

Y: 21.5" to 28.5". Section label: `SUBSTRATE ROUTING -- ONE CLEANER DOES NOT FIT ALL`.

Four side-by-side glass cards:

**Steel / Iron** (accent `#2EC4B6`):
- `NaOH: 45-90 g/L (6-12 oz/gal)` / `150-195 F (65-90 C)` / `3-10 min`
- `Standard high-caustic cleaner. Most forgiving substrate.`
- Check: `Water-break-free after rinse`

**Aluminum** (accent `#E8A020`):
- `NaOH: 10-30 g/L (1.3-4 oz/gal)` / `Silicate: 30-60 g/L inhibitor` / `120-150 F (50-65 C)` / `1-3 min`
- `Low caustic + silicate inhibitor required. NaOH etches aluminum above ~30 g/L.`
- Check: `CAUTION: Silicate residue may require desmut step` (`#E05C5C`)

**Zinc Die Cast** (accent `#E05C5C`):
- `NaOH: 30-60 g/L (4-8 oz/gal)` / `130-160 F (55-70 C)` / `2-5 min MAX`
- `Zinc dissolves in strong caustic -- limit exposure.`
- Check: `CAUTION: Exceeding 5 min causes substrate attack` (`#E05C5C`)

**Copper / Brass** (accent `#27AE60`):
- `NaOH: Low-caustic formula` / `Non-silicated preferred` / `130-160 F (55-70 C)` / `2-5 min`
- `High silicate can leave residual film on copper alloys.`
- Check: `Non-etch formulations preferred`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting Quick Hits

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON CLEANING FAILURES`.

Four coral-accented glass cards in a row:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| WATER BREAK | Insufficient time/temp; bath exhausted; surfactant depleted | Increase time/temp; rebuild bath; add surfactant |
| SILICATE RESIDUE | Silicate too high on aluminum; rinse inadequate | Reduce silicate; improve rinse; add acid desmut |
| SUBSTRATE ETCH | NaOH too high for substrate; temp too high | Reduce caustic; add silicate inhibitor; lower temp |
| FOAM OVERFLOW | Surfactant overdose; incompatible drag-in chemistry | Check surfactant level; use low-foam grade for spray |

Problem: Barlow SemiBold `#E05C5C`. Cause: Inter `#F0EDE8`. Fix: Inter Medium `#27AE60`.
