## Phase 5 -- Cleaner Selection + Aluminum Rules

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Cleaner Selection:** Three stacked cards: Non-Etch Alkaline (emerald, standard default) | Inhibited Alkaline/silicate (teal, heavy soils) | Fluoride-Bearing (amber, cast alloys, controlled).

**Right -- "Aluminum Is Different":** Coral accent. Strongly caustic NaOH > 5% aggressively etches aluminum. Steel-line cleaners NOT compatible. Silicate preferred but residues must be rinsed. Fluoride effective but risky on 2xxx/7xxx.

## Phase 6 -- Operating Parameters

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `OPERATING PARAMETERS`.

| Parameter | Immersion | Spray | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concentration | 4--8 oz/gal | 2--4 oz/gal | Titration (free alkalinity) |
| Temperature | 120--160 F | 100--140 F | Thermocouple |
| Time | 3--10 min | 1--3 min | Timer |
| pH | 9--11 | 9--11 | pH meter |
| Water break | Must pass | Must pass | Visual |

## Phase 7 -- Defect Diagnosis

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 CLEANING FAILURES`.

| Problem | Cause | Downstream Effect |
|---|---|---|
| OIL RESIDUE | Insufficient conc/time | Skip areas in chromate |
| ALUMINUM ETCH | Cleaner too caustic or hot | Heavy smut; patchy coating |
| SILICATE RESIDUE | Over-concentration; poor rinse | Inhibits chromate deposition |
| WATER BREAK FAIL | Contaminated part or rinse | Entire process compromised |
