## Phase 4 -- Three-Family Drainage Hero

Y: 5.0" to 14.5". Section label: `THREE FAMILIES -- THREE DRAINAGE BEHAVIORS`. Three columns.

**Plastisol Drain (`#E8A020`):** No solvent. Drain 10--60 sec. Thixotropic. Rotate/invert. Higher viscosity = more uniform. `Drainage stops when viscosity prevents further flow. Thick bottoms and thin tops are the default.`

**Hot-Dip Cool-Down (`#2EC4B6`):** No flash. Solidification IS the "leveling." Coating freezes as part cools below melt point. Uniform preheat is critical. `No flow after solidification.`

**Solution Dip Flash (`#27AE60`):** Solvent evaporation + drainage. 5--15 min ambient or 5--10 min heated tunnel. Hang at angle. `Only dip family with true solvent flash.`

## Phase 5 -- Rotation and Part Orientation

Y: 15.8" to 20.8". Section label: `ROTATION AND ORIENTATION -- YOUR UNIFORMITY TOOLS`.

**Left -- `ROTATION DURING DRAIN` (border `#E8A020`):** Slow rotation prevents thick bottom buildup. 5--15 RPM. Applicable: plastisol (most), solution (moderate), hot-dip (minimal).

**Right -- `FIXTURE ORIENTATION RULES` (`#27AE60`):** 5 rules: Angle for drainage away from critical surfaces. Drain holes at lowest point. Blind holes face DOWN. Coat or mask hooks. Document fixture orientation.

## Phase 6 -- Viscosity-Uniformity Trade-Off

Y: 21.8" to 26.3". Section label: `THE FUNDAMENTAL TRADE-OFF`.

Full-width callout (border `#2EC4B6`): `VISCOSITY vs. UNIFORMITY -- THE DIP COATER'S DILEMMA`.

High Viscosity: Thicker, less drainage (uniform), slower recess coverage, bridging risk, fewer dips. Low Viscosity: Thinner, more drainage (uneven), better recess penetration, less bridging, more dips.

Bottom `#E8A020`: `No perfect viscosity -- only the right compromise. Run test panels at 2-3 viscosity levels.`
