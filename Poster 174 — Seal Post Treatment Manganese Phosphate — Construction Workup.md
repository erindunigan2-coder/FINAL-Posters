# Poster #174 -- Construction Workup
## Seal / Post Treatment -- Manganese Phosphate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stages 7--8 of 8. This poster covers the oil seal (the most critical post-treatment -- mandatory for all MnPhos coatings) and the drain/final handling. Bare manganese phosphate provides almost zero corrosion protection. The entire value proposition -- oil retention, lubrication, anti-galling -- depends on filling those porous hureaulite crystals with preservative oil. This poster also covers wax dip (fastener applications) and the legacy chromic acid seal option (MIL-DTL-16232 Class 2). The hero story: without oil, MnPhos is just a porous black coating sitting on steel, waiting to rust.

Hero visual: oil immersion tank with parts submerged, showing oil penetrating into the porous coating structure.

### ZONE 6 -- Defects + Key Rules

**Left -- Defects (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Section label: `POST-TREATMENT DEFECTS` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

4 defect cards stacked:

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| RUST UNDER OIL | Water trapped in pores before oiling; over-rinsed | Reduce rinse time; use hotter oil |
| DRIPPING / EXCESS OIL | Insufficient drain time; oil viscosity too low | Extend drain; centrifuge; consider heavier oil |
| DRY SPOTS (no oil) | Part geometry trapping air; insufficient immersion | Reposition on rack; extend immersion; agitate |
| OIL STAINING | Wrong oil type; oil contaminated with water | Check oil specification; test for water content |

Each card: Rounded rect, W: 11.0", H: 1.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- Key Rules (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Section label: `NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES` Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 22 pt `#F0EDE8`.

7 rule rows, each with amber left border:
1. `OIL IS MANDATORY -- bare MnPhos is useless`
2. `Hot oil (150--180 F) for military spec work`
3. `Immerse 2--5 minutes for full pore penetration`
4. `Go directly from rinse to oil -- no drying step`
5. `Drain and centrifuge excess oil after immersion`
6. `No oven dry before OR after oil`
7. `Verify oil meets specification (MIL-PRF-3150 or per customer)`
