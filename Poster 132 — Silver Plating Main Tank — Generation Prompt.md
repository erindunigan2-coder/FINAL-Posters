## Phase 5 -- Bath Composition + CN:Ag Ratio

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `BATH CHEMISTRY -- STRIKE VS. PLATE`.

**Left -- Strike (accent `#E8A020`):** Low Metal, High Cyanide. Ag: 1--6 g/L (target 3--5) / Free KCN: 75--120 g/L (target 90--100) / Temp: 70--80 F / CD: 10--20 ASF / Time: 15--30 sec. CN:Ag ratio 20:1--30:1 by weight.

**Right -- Plate (accent `#27AE60`):** High Metal, Controlled Cyanide. Ag: 30--50 g/L (target 35--45) / Free KCN: 75--150 g/L (target 100--120) / K2CO3: < 75 g/L / Temp: 75--85 F / CD: 8--15 ASF rack / Cathode eff: ~100%.

**Free Cyanide Gauge (full width):** Red = LOW (anode passivation, rough) | Green = OPTIMAL (bright, smooth) | Yellow = HIGH (reduced eff, dull). Targets: Strike 20:1--30:1 | Plate 2:1--4:1. Note: `Monitor free cyanide DAILY. Single most important analytical control.`

## Phase 6 -- Defect Grid

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 6 COMMON DEFECTS`.

| Pos | Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | TARNISHING | `#E8A020` | Sulfide, no anti-tarnish | Anti-tarnish (Stage 8) |
| R1C2 | DARK DEPOSIT | `#E05C5C` | Organic, carbonate >75 g/L, sulfide, low CN | Carbon treat; freeze-out |
| R1C3 | PEELING FROM COPPER | `#E05C5C` | No strike or inadequate | Verify strike; extend time |
| R2C1 | ROUGH / NODULAR | `#E8A020` | Anode sludge, particulate | Check bags; filter |
| R2C2 | BURNING AT HCD | `#2EC4B6` | CD too high, low Ag | Reduce CD; add AgCN |
| R2C3 | DULL DEPOSIT | `#2EC4B6` | Low brightener, organics | Hull cell; carbon treat |

## Phase 7 -- Anodes + Contamination

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Anodes & Brightener:** Anodes: Pure silver 99.9%+, bars or shot in Ti baskets, A:C 1:1--2:1, bags required. Brightener: Antimony-based grain refiner, selenium co-brightener. `Bright silver has lower conductivity than matte -- for RF, specify matte + mechanical polish.`

**Right -- Contamination Thresholds:**

| Contaminant | Threshold | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Sulfide | TRACE | Dark, stained (most sulfide-sensitive metal) |
| Iron | > 5 ppm | Dark spots, roughness |
| Carbonate | > 75 g/L | Reduced throwing power, dark |
| Organic | visible | Dark, streaky |
| Copper | > 10 ppm | Discoloration |

Carbonate note: `Remove by freezing or barium treatment.`
