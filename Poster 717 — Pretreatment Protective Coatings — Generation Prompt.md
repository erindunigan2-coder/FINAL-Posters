## Phase 4 -- Dual Decision Tree (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `STEEL vs. CONCRETE -- TWO DIFFERENT APPROACHES`. Two panels.

**Left -- Steel:** Decision tree: 1) Blast to spec (SP10/SP5). 2) Verify profile 2.0-4.0 mils D4417. 3) Zinc-rich primer? YES: no conversion coating (zinc must contact steel). NO: optional iron phosphate/wash primer. 4) Apply within blast-to-coat window. Big callout: `DO NOT ADD CONVERSION COATING UNDER ZINC-RICH PRIMER` `#E05C5C`.

**Right -- Concrete:** 1) Mechanical profile ICRI CSP 2-5. 2) Verify moisture F2170 < 75% RH, F1869 < 3 lb. 3) Check pH 7-10. 4) No sealers/curing compounds (water droplet test). 5) Apply epoxy primer. No zinc-rich on concrete. Big callout: `NO CURING COMPOUNDS -- GRIND OFF IF PRESENT` `#E05C5C`.

## Phase 5 -- Steel by Tier + Concrete Detail

### Steel by Service Tier (Y: 14.5"-20.5")

Section label: `STEEL -- PRETREATMENT BY SERVICE TIER`. 4-row table.

| Tier | Blast | Profile | Primer | Salt Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| General industrial | SP6/SP10 | 1.5-3.0 mils | Epoxy or OZ | Cl < 7 ug/cm2 |
| Marine / offshore | SP10/SP5 | 2.0-4.0 mils | IOZ mandatory | Cl < 3, SO4 < 10 |
| Immersion (tank) | SP5 | 2.0-4.0 mils | Solventless epoxy | Cl < 3, SO4 < 10 |
| Maintenance | SP11 | Feather edges | Epoxy or OZ | Test at repair area |

### Concrete Detail (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `CONCRETE -- THE SUBSTRATE THAT FIGHTS BACK`. Two columns.

Left -- Surface Requirements table: ICRI CSP 2-5, F2170 < 75% RH, F1869 < 3 lb, pH 7-10, no curing compounds, no laitance, no oil.

Right -- Challenges: Porous (uneven absorption). Contains moisture. Alkaline (pH 12-13 fresh). Weak laitance layer. Curing compounds prevent adhesion. No galvanic possible. `Epoxy penetrating sealer is the standard concrete primer.`

## Phase 6 -- Pretreatment Failures

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section label: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 PRETREATMENT FAILURES`. Four cards.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| ZINC-RICH FAILS ON STEEL | Conversion coating insulates zinc | Blast to bare; apply zinc directly |
| BLISTERING ON CONCRETE | Moisture exceeds F1869 limit | Test MVER; install vapor barrier; wait |
| COATING PEELING OFF CONCRETE | Curing compound on surface | Grind off; re-profile; water droplet test |
| OSMOTIC BLISTERING ON STEEL | Soluble salts trapped | Bresle patch; wash; re-blast; verify |

Key insight: `Adding anything between the metal and the zinc defeats the galvanic protection that is the entire point of the system.`
