## Phase 4 -- IOZ vs. Liquid Paint (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `WHY IOZ PRIMER LOOKS DIFFERENT -- AND THAT IS CORRECT`. Two panels.

**Left -- Liquid Paint (accent `#2EC4B6`):** Cross-section: smooth paint film. Solvent flash 10-30 min. Surface tension pulls smooth. Uniform gloss. `Leveling = good (expected).`

**Right -- IOZ Primer (accent `#27AE60`):** Cross-section: rough, irregular top with zinc particles packed. Solvent flash 30-60 min. 75-85% zinc. Film cannot flow. `Rough, matte, porous = CORRECT.` Porosity enables moisture cure.

Bottom callout: `Do not judge IOZ by liquid paint standards. Rough and matte IS correct.`

## Phase 5 -- Flash Time Table + Mud Cracking

### Flash/Recoat Table (Y: 14.5"-21.0")

Section label: `FLASH TIME AND RECOAT WINDOWS`. 6-row table.

| Primer | Flash Time | Min Recoat | Max Recoat | Exceeding Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IOZ (ethyl silicate) | 30-60 min at 50% RH | 30 min | 4-8 hr | Must fully cure (24 hr) |
| IOZ (alkali silicate) | 30-60 min | 30 min | 4-8 hr | Same |
| OZ (2K epoxy-zinc) | Per binder pot life | 4-24 hr | Per TDS | Scuff sand |
| OZ (1K moisture-cure) | Overnight | 24 hr | Per TDS | Scuff sand |
| Epoxy (2K) | 30 min-4 hr | 4-24 hr | 3-7 days | Scuff sand 80-120 grit |
| Chromated epoxy (aero) | 30-60 min | Per TDS | Per TDS | Per spec |

### Mud Cracking (Y: 21.0"-27.0")

Section label: `MUD CRACKING -- THE SIGNATURE IOZ DEFECT`. Two panels.

Left -- Mechanism: Ethyl silicate shrinks during cure. Normal DFT = manageable. > 5 mils = stress exceeds cohesion = cracking. `> 5 mils = MUD CRACKING ZONE` 24pt `#E05C5C`.

Right -- Prevention/Repair: Apply 2.5-4.0 mils, never exceed 5. WFT check D4414. Two thin coats. Repair: must strip, re-blast, reapply. No touch-up fix.

## Phase 6 -- Amine Blush + Moisture Cure

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Two columns.

Left -- Amine Blush (Epoxy): Waxy carbamate film. Forms < 50 F + > 80% RH + cool nights. Causes adhesion failure if overcoated. Remove: water wash + scrub, or solvent wipe. MUST remove before recoat.

Right -- IOZ Moisture Cure: Needs atmospheric moisture > 40% RH. Dry air (< 30%) inhibits cure. Acceleration: mist coat with clean water. Humidity tenting in arid climates. RH table: < 30% very slow, 30-50% slow, 50-80% optimal, > 80% fast.
