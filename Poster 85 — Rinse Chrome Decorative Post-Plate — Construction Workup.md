# Poster #85 -- Construction Workup
## Rinse -- Chrome (Decorative) -- Post-Plate

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 6 of 8. This rinse removes chrome bath drag-out from parts after plating. For hexavalent lines, the drag-out contains Cr(VI) -- a carcinogenic hazardous waste that must be recovered or reduced to Cr(III) before discharge. For trivalent lines, the drag-out is less hazardous but still needs removal to prevent staining and ensure a clean finish.

Hero visual: multi-stage cascade rinse system with drag-out recovery tank, showing the flow from chrome bath through recovery rinse to final rinse.

### ZONE 5 -- Environmental Compliance

**Section label:** `ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE -- CHROME WASTEWATER` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK E -- Compliance Panel (Y: 21.3" to 26.3")**

Rounded rect, X: 0.5", W: 23.0", H: 4.8", fill `#E05C5C` at 5%, border 1 pt `#E05C5C`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

**Hexavalent chrome wastewater rules:**

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cr(VI) reduction | MUST reduce Cr(VI) to Cr(III) before discharge -- sodium metabisulfite or ferrous sulfate |
| pH adjustment | After reduction: precipitate Cr(OH)3 at pH 7.5--8.5 |
| Discharge limits | Total Cr: varies by jurisdiction (typically 0.5--2.0 mg/L) |
| Record keeping | Monitor and log all chrome discharges |
| Segregation | Chrome wastewater must be segregated from cyanide wastewater |

**Trivalent chrome wastewater:**
- `Cr(III) wastewater is simpler -- no reduction step needed. Precipitate directly at pH 7.5--8.5.` -- Inter Medium, 14 pt, `#27AE60`
- `But still a regulated metal -- discharge limits apply.` -- Inter Regular, 13 pt, `#F0EDE8` at 70%

## Design Notes

This is where the environmental rubber meets the road. The cascade rinse diagram with counter-current flow arrows is the centerpiece -- it shows both the quality function (clean parts) and the environmental function (minimize chrome discharge). The drag-out recovery economics panel makes the business case that a shop supervisor can point to when requesting a recovery rinse tank. The compliance panel in Zone 5 is not optional decoration -- it is the reason many shops invest in better rinsing. For trivalent lines, the compliance burden is lighter, and the poster should make that contrast clear.
