## Phase 4 -- Process Flow Hero (U-Flow)

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `THE COMPLETE PROCESS -- STAGE BY STAGE`.

Seven-stage U-flow: Top row 4 stages, bottom row 3 + empty spacer.

**Top Row:** 1. Alkaline Clean (Teal) | 2. Rinse Pre-Deox (Teal) | 3. Deoxidize/Desmut (Amber) | 4. Rinse Pre-Coat (Teal).

**Bottom Row:** 5. Chromate Coat (Emerald) | 6. Rinse Post-Coat (Teal) | 7. Air Dry/Cure (Amber) | (empty -- place coral safety badge: `Cr(VI) CARCINOGEN -- SEE SAFETY PANEL`).

**Box content:**
1. `Non-etch / mild alkaline pH 9--11` | `120--160 F | 3--10 min` -- Avoid strongly caustic cleaners on Al
2. `Ambient | Double rinse standard (aerospace)` -- DI/RO preferred -- chlorides cause pitting on Al
3. `HNO3 30--50%` / `HNO3+HF for high-Si/Cu alloys` | `Ambient, 1--5 min` -- ALLOY-SPECIFIC formula `#E8A020`
4. `Fresh water, ambient | Double rinse` -- TIME-CRITICAL: < 5 min to chromate `#E05C5C`
5. `Cr6+: 3--6 g/L, F-: 0.5--1.5 g/L | pH 1.3--1.8 | 60--100 F, 1--5 min` -- Visible in 15--30 sec
6. `COLD WATER ONLY -- ambient to 77 F | 15--30 sec BRIEF` -- NEVER hot water `#E05C5C`
7. `Ambient air or filtered forced air | NO oven above 150 F | 24 hr cure` -- NO HEAT DRY `#E05C5C`

Stage legend strip + Cr(VI) warning badge (coral-tinted).

## Phase 6 -- Self-Healing + Safety Warning

Y: 21.0" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Self-Healing Mechanism:** Emerald accent. Cross-section: intact gel with Cr6+ reservoir dots. Damaged + repaired: Cr6+ migrating to scratch, redepositing Cr3+. `10--30% of total Cr is hexavalent` | `NO OTHER CONVERSION COATING HAS THIS PROPERTY` | `Trivalent (Type II) CANNOT self-heal`.

**Right -- Cr(VI) Safety & Regulatory:** Coral accent with coral border. `Cr(VI) is a KNOWN HUMAN CARCINOGEN (IARC Group 1)` | `OSHA PEL: 5 ug/m3` | PPE + local exhaust required | EU REACH restricted | RoHS prohibited (exemptions) | MIL-DTL-5541 Type II is designated replacement | Type I remains for legacy aerospace.
