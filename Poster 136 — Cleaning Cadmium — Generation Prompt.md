## Phase 6 -- HE Primer

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT -- WHY IT STARTS AT CLEANING`.

Three panels:

**WHAT IS HE? (`#E05C5C`):** H atoms diffuse into steel lattice / Recombine to H2 at grain boundaries / Trapped -- cannot diffuse out / Internal pressure builds / Steel cracks without warning / Failure is delayed -- hours, days, weeks.

**H2 SOURCES IN CLEANING (`#E8A020`):** 1. Acid activation (HCl releases H2) / 2. Cathodic electroclean (H2 at cathode = part) / 3. Plating itself (co-deposition) / Total H2 = sum of ALL steps.

**CONTROLLING HE (`#27AE60`):** 1. Vapor degrease when possible / 2. Anodic electroclean only / 3. Minimize acid time (15--30 sec MAX) / 4. BAKE after plating (Stage 8). `The bake fixes it. Minimizing absorption makes the bake more effective.`

## Phase 7 -- Common Errors

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `CLEANING ERRORS ON CADMIUM SUBSTRATES`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CATHODIC ELECTROCLEAN | Reversed polarity | Verify anodic before every load |
| 2 | EXCESSIVE ACID TIME | No timer; operator distracted | Timer with alarm at acid tank |
| 3 | SKIP ELECTROCLEAN | Shortcut -- soak only | Electroclean required for adhesion |
| 4 | CONTAMINATED CLEANER | Oil buildup | Monitor concentration; change on schedule |
