## Phase 5 -- Acid Carryover Danger

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `WHAT ACID CARRYOVER DOES TO YOUR PHOSPHATE BATH`.

Four-row consequence table:

| Carryover | What Happens | Bath Impact |
|---|---|---|
| HCl residue | Lowers pH, increases free acid | Thin/powdery coating |
| H2SO4 residue | Sulfate contamination | Sludge, poor crystal structure |
| Dissolved iron | Excess iron beyond normal | Accelerates sludge, brown/red coating |
| Dissolved copper/chrome | Metallic contamination | Immersion deposits, skip areas |

Bottom banner: `The MnPhos bath operates at 190--210 F. Hot baths amplify every contamination problem.`

## Phase 6 -- Multi-Stage Rinse + Water Quality

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Two-column.

**Left -- When to Add Stages:** Single adequate for light pickle. Two/three needed for heavy pickle (15+ min), high-alloy parts, high-production lines, or drifting free acid.

**Right -- Water Quality:** Conductivity < 500 uS/cm | pH 6.0--8.0 | Hardness < 200 ppm | Chloride < 100 ppm | Ambient.

## Phase 7 -- Key Rules + Dragout

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Two-column.

**Left -- Rules:** ZERO acid into phosphate bath | Multiple stages after heavy pickle | Monitor rinse pH | Don't let parts dry | Move to phosphate promptly.

**Right -- Dragout Reduction:** Withdraw slowly (10--15 sec drain) | Rack for drainage | Air knife above pickle | Every ounce saved extends bath life.
