## Phase 4 -- Temper Parameters + Case Depth Measurement (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 15.0". Section label: `TEMPERING AND CASE DEPTH VERIFICATION`.

**Left -- Temper Parameters (W: 11.0", Amber accent):** 300--375 F (149--191 C), 2 hr min per AMS 2759/7. Air or N2 atmosphere. Double temper for aerospace (esp. 9310). Sub-zero treatment: -100 to -120 F between tempers, transforms retained austenite, required when RA < 10%. WHY: as-quenched martensite is hard but brittle; tempering trades a few HRC for significant toughness.

**Right -- Case Depth Methods (W: 11.5", Emerald accent):** `CASE DEPTH MEASUREMENT (SAE J423)`. Four methods stacked:
1. MICROHARDNESS TRAVERSE -- primary method; Knoop/Vickers at incremental depths; ECD = depth to 50 HRC; per ASTM E384
2. CHEMICAL METHOD -- step-machine layers; combustion analysis for actual carbon profile
3. FILE TEST -- qualitative shop-floor check; go/no-go only; not acceptable for spec compliance
4. FRACTURE TEST -- break test piece; case appears fine-grained and lighter; visual ECD estimate

## Phase 6 -- Metallographic Criteria + Defect Table

**Metallographic (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `METALLOGRAPHIC EXAMINATION -- WHAT TO LOOK FOR`. 2x2 grid:

| Card | Criterion | Accent | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retained Austenite (RA) | `#E8A020` | Must not exceed 20% in outer 10% of case per AMS 2759/7. Point count or XRD. Remedy: sub-zero at -100 to -120 F. |
| 2 | Intergranular Oxidation (IGO) | `#E05C5C` | Dark network along grain boundaries in outer 0.0005--0.001". Must be ground off (0.002--0.003" min). Eliminated by vacuum carburizing. |
| 3 | Carbide Network | `#E05C5C` | Continuous Fe3C at grain boundaries. Surface carbon >1.0%. Makes case brittle. Remedy: reduce Cp, extend diffuse. |
| 4 | NMTP | `#E8A020` | Bainite or pearlite in case = insufficient quench severity. Remedy: increase agitation, faster oil, higher hardenability steel. |

**Defect Table (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `COMMON DEFECTS -- CAUSE AND REMEDY`.

| Defect | Cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Excess retained austenite | High surface C, insufficient quench | Reduce Cp; sub-zero treat |
| IGO | CO2 and H2O at grain boundaries | Vacuum carburize; or grind 0.002--0.003" |
| Decarburization | Air leaks, low Cp, improper purge | Fix seals; verify atmosphere |
| Soft spots | Part contact, gas starvation, oil residue | Improve fixturing; clean oil |
| Carbide network | Surface C >1.0%, insufficient diffuse | Lower Cp; extend diffuse |
| Grain growth | Temp too high, time too long | Reduce temp; use fine-grain steels |
