## Phase 6 -- Cleanliness Verification + Defects

### Verification (Y: 20.5"-26.5")

Section label: `PROVING THE SURFACE IS CLEAN`. Two columns.

Left -- Test Methods: Water break test (per coil), Surface carbon < 2 mg/m2, Gravimetric oil, Rinse conductivity continuous.

Right -- The 5 mg/m2 Threshold: Primer is only 0.15-0.30 mil. Trace oil prevents adhesion. Industry consensus max. Big stat: `< 5 mg/m2` 48pt `#E8A020`.

### Defects (Y: 26.5"-32.5")

4 cards, left accent `#E05C5C`:

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| OIL SKIP (ADHESION FAILURE) | Insufficient brush contact | Replace brushes; verify nip pressure |
| CLEANER RESIDUE | Inadequate rinse | Verify conductivity; check nozzles |
| UNEVEN CLEANING (STREAKS) | Clogged nozzles or misaligned brushes | Clean nozzles; realign pressure |
| FOAM IN CLEANER | Wrong cleaner type | Switch to low-foam formulation |

Key insight: `The coil coating cleaner is the shortest-contact-time cleaning step in all of industrial painting.`
