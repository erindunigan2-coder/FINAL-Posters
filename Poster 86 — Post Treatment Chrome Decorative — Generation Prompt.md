## Phase 5 -- Anti-Tarnish + Drying

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `STAGE 7: ANTI-TARNISH + STAGE 8: DRY & INSPECT`.

**Left -- Anti-Tarnish (optional):**
Accent `#E8A020`. Title: `ANTI-TARNISH DIP (OPTIONAL)`.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Chemistry | Proprietary anti-tarnish (per supplier) |
| Temperature | Ambient to 120 F |
| Time | 15--60 sec |
| Purpose | Enhances tarnish resistance |
| When used | Automotive, high-cosmetic applications |

Note: `Not all lines run anti-tarnish. Check customer requirements.`

Hot DI rinse sub-section: `140--160 F, 30--60 sec. Promotes fast drying, reduces water spots.`

**Right -- Drying & Inspection:**
Accent `#27AE60`. Title: `DRY & FINAL INSPECTION`.

Drying: Forced air or oven | 120--150 F | 5--10 min | Air knives preferred for high-volume.

Inspection checklist: Visual (uniform bright finish) | Thickness (XRF per spec) | Adhesion (bend/tape per ASTM B571) | Color match to standard | No water spots or handling marks.

## Phase 6 -- Acceptance Criteria Table

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA -- DECORATIVE CHROME`.

| Test | Method | Typical Spec | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chrome thickness | XRF (ASTM B568) | 0.15--0.75 um | Per customer drawing |
| Nickel thickness | XRF (ASTM B568) | 5--25 um | Varies by service condition |
| Total Cu+Ni+Cr | XRF | Per customer spec | System approach |
| Adhesion | Bend/tape (ASTM B571) | No peeling or flaking | Destructive -- sample basis |
| Corrosion (CASS) | CASS test (ASTM B368) | 8--16 hr to rating 9 | Multi-layer dependent |
| Corrosion (NSS) | Salt spray (ASTM B117) | 24--96 hr | Depends on Ni thickness |
| Visual | Visual, 100% | Bright, uniform, defect-free | Compare to limit samples |

Bottom: `Corrosion performance is primarily a function of the NICKEL layer, not the chrome. Thin chrome over thick, well-structured nickel outperforms thick chrome over thin nickel every time.` -- `#E8A020`

## Phase 7 -- Common Defects at Inspection

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG -- 4 INSPECTION FAILURES`.

2x2 grid:

| Defect | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| WATER SPOTS | `#E8A020` | Hard water in final rinse; slow drying | Use DI for final rinse; improve drying speed |
| HAZE / TARNISH | `#E8A020` | Incomplete anti-tarnish or no hot rinse | Add anti-tarnish dip; use hot DI rinse |
| THIN CHROME | `#E05C5C` | Plating time too short, CD too low | Increase plating time; check main tank parameters |
| CHROME PEELING | `#E05C5C` | Adhesion failure from passivated nickel | Root cause is upstream -- fix activation stage |
