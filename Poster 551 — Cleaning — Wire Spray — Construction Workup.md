# Poster #551 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Wire Combustion Spray

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Wire combustion spray is primarily applied to structural steel that has been in service or is fresh from fabrication with mill scale. The cleaning challenge is different from aerospace thermal spray -- you are dealing with rust, mill scale, old paint systems, and field contamination rather than machining oils on precision parts. The blast step does most of the heavy lifting, but degreasing before blasting is still essential to avoid embedding contaminants into the profile. Hero visual: 4-step cleaning sequence tailored to structural steel.

### ZONE 2 -- Cleaning Sequence Hero

**Section label:** `PRE-SPRAY CLEANING SEQUENCE -- STRUCTURAL STEEL` -- Y: 3.1".

**BLOCK B -- 4-Step Vertical Sequence**

Y: 3.8" to 15.0". Four large step cards stacked vertically with arrow connectors and time-window callouts.

Each step card: Rounded rect, W: 23.0", H: 2.3", fill `#1E2435`, radius 6.

| Step | Top Accent | Title | Parameters | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#2EC4B6` | DEGREASE | Solvent wipe or aqueous degreaser. Remove oil, grease, lubricants, hydraulic fluid. For field work: citrus-based degreasers are practical. | No visible oil sheen |
| 2 | `#E8A020` | REMOVE LOOSE MATERIAL | Power tool cleaning (SSPC-SP 3) or hand tool (SP 2) to remove loose rust, flaking paint, and mill scale BEFORE grit blasting. | Loose material knocked off |
| 3 | `#E8A020` | GRIT BLAST TO WHITE METAL | Angular alumina or steel grit, 60--100 PSI, SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal Blast). Removes remaining rust, mill scale, and old coatings. Establishes anchor profile Ra 4--12 um. | SSPC-SP 5 verified |
| 4 | `#27AE60` | VERIFY & PROTECT | Visual verification per SSPC-VIS 1. Protect blasted surface from moisture, contamination, and handling. Coat within time window. | Surface ready for spray |

Time-window callouts between steps (Inter Medium 14 pt `#E8A020`):
- Between Step 1 and 2: `Degrease BEFORE mechanical cleaning -- solvent residue embedded in profile is worse than surface oil`
- Between Step 3 and 4: `Blast-to-spray: < 4 hours (humidity-dependent; some specs < 2 hr)`

### ZONE 3 -- Contaminant Types + Removal

**Section label:** `CONTAMINANTS & CLEANING METHODS` -- Y: 15.7".

**Left -- BLOCK C: Common Contaminants (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C` 0.06".

Title: `WHAT YOU ARE REMOVING` -- Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#E05C5C`.

| Contaminant | Source | Removal Method |
|---|---|---|
| Mill scale | Steel fabrication (hot rolling) | Grit blast -- only reliable method |
| Rust | Atmospheric corrosion | Grit blast to SP 5 |
| Oil / grease | Machinery, handling, hydraulic leaks | Solvent or aqueous degrease |
| Old paint systems | Prior corrosion protection | Grit blast (may need multiple passes) |
| Weld spatter | Fabrication joints | Grinding followed by blast |
| Chalk / marker | Layout markings | Solvent wipe |
| Salt deposits | Marine/coastal environments | Fresh water wash before degrease |

Data: Inter Regular 12 pt.

**Right -- BLOCK D: Cleaning Methods (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6` 0.06".

Title: `CLEANING METHODS` -- Barlow SemiBold 20 pt `#2EC4B6`.

| Method | When to Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Solvent wipe | Light oil, grease | MEK, acetone, or citrus degreaser |
| Aqueous alkaline wash | Heavy contamination (shop) | 50--70 degC, pH 10--12 |
| Steam cleaning | Heavy oil/grease (field) | Effective on large structures |
| High-pressure water | Salt, loose debris (field) | Not a substitute for blast |
| Power tool (SP 3) | Loose rust/paint pre-blast | Needle gun, wire wheel |
| Hand tool (SP 2) | Accessible surfaces only | Wire brush, scraper |

Note: `None of these methods replace grit blasting. They prepare the surface FOR blasting.` Inter Medium 13 pt `#E8A020`.

### ZONE 4 -- Time Windows + Field Conditions

**Section label:** `TIMING RULES & FIELD CHALLENGES` -- Y: 22.2".

**Left -- BLOCK E: Time Windows (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Window | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Degrease to blast | Same day; minimize delay | Recontamination from environment |
| Blast to spray | < 4 hours (general); < 2 hours (high humidity) | Flash rust; moisture absorption |
| After blast | Do NOT touch with bare hands | Fingerprint = contamination |
| Humidity limit | Do not blast or spray if RH > 85% or surface temp < 3 degC above dew point | Condensation causes flash rust |

Data: JetBrains Mono 12 pt.

**Right -- BLOCK F: Field Challenges (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**

Rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020` 0.06".

Title: `FIELD-SPECIFIC CHALLENGES` -- Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`.

- `Rain can flash-rust a blasted surface in minutes -- stop work and cover`
- `Wind carries grit and fumes -- orient work area accordingly`
- `Morning dew condensation can form on blasted steel before spray crew arrives`
- `Marine salt must be removed (fresh water wash) before any other cleaning`
- `Old lead paint on bridges requires lead-safe work practices (EPA RRP)`
- `Multiple coating layers may require blasting in stages`
