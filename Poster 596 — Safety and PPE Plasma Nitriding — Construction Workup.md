# Poster #596 -- Construction Workup
## Safety & PPE -- Plasma Nitriding

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Safety poster for the Plasma Nitriding cluster. This poster hangs at the furnace entrance. Every hazard is coral-tinted; every mitigation is emerald. The rule card big-number callout: 400--1000 V DC -- the voltage range that makes electrical safety the primary concern.

| Pos | Hazard | Icon Placeholder | Details | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1C1 | ELECTRICAL SHOCK | Lightning bolt | 400--1000 V DC at significant current; electrocution during maintenance | Lockout/tagout; interlocked panels; NEVER open vessel under power |
| R1C2 | HYDROGEN GAS | Flame | Process gas is typically 75% H2; explosive at 4--75% in air | Leak detection; burn-off or ventilation of exhaust; no ignition sources |
| R1C3 | ARC DAMAGE | Spark | Arcing on sharp edges, small holes, closely spaced parts; local overheating | Min hole 3x depth; 10 mm spacing; pulsed DC; active screen |

### ZONE 3 -- PPE Requirements

**Section label:** `REQUIRED PPE` -- Y: 15.7". Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 28 pt.

**BLOCK D -- PPE Checklist (Y: 16.3" to 21.8")**

Vertical list, 6 items. Each row: rounded rect full width, H: 0.8", alternating `#1E2435` / `#252B3D`.

| Item | PPE | When Required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Safety glasses / face shield | Always near furnace; full face shield during loading/unloading |
| 2 | Heat-resistant gloves (min 500 F rated) | Loading, unloading, any contact with hot fixturing |
| 3 | Electrical-rated gloves (Class 00 min) | Any maintenance on power supply or vessel electrical connections |
| 4 | Steel-toed boots | Always in furnace area |
| 5 | Hearing protection (if > 85 dB) | During plasma operation if magnetostrictive noise exceeds threshold |
| 6 | No loose clothing / jewelry / ferrous items | Remove watches, rings, belt buckles near energized vessel |

Item number: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, 18 pt, `#E8A020`. PPE name: Inter Medium 14 pt `#F0EDE8`. When required: Inter Regular 13 pt `#F0EDE8` at 70%.
