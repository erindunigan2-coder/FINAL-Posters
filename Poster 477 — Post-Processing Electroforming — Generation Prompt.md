## Phase 4 -- Post-Processing Flow Hero

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `POST-PROCESSING SEQUENCE`.

Five horizontal flow panels with arrows:

| Step | Title | Accent | Key Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TRIM FLASH | `#E05C5C` | Remove excess at edges/contacts. Grinding, rotary tool, laser, wire EDM. Do not let heat/vibration damage interior. |
| 2 | MACHINE | `#E8A020` | CNC turning, milling, grinding, EDM. Fixture on interior ONLY with soft jaws/custom fixtures. |
| 3 | ANNEAL | `#27AE60` | 400-600 C, 1-2 hr, inert atmosphere. Reduces stress, increases ductility, REDUCES hardness (300 HV -> 150-180 HV). Skip if hardness critical. |
| 4 | PLATE EXTERIOR | `#2EC4B6` | Optional: chrome, gold, tin, nickel. Mask precision interior before plating. |
| 5 | FINAL CLEAN | `#F0EDE8` | Ultrasonic alkaline, DI rinse, IPA wipe. Wrap interior in lint-free material. Package and label. |

**Bottom insight:** `Not every electroform needs all five steps. A thin screen mesh may only need flash trimming. A precision waveguide needs all five. Match the post-processing to the application.`

## Phase 5 -- Flash Trim + Machining Details

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Two-column layout.

**Left -- FLASH TRIMMING (`#E05C5C`):**

```
WHAT IS FLASH? Excess metal beyond part boundary.
Caused by current wrapping, deposit overgrowth, contact points.

METHODS:
| Method         | Precision  | Speed    |
| Hand grinding   | Low        | Fast     |
| Band saw        | Low        | Fast     |
| Wire EDM        | Very high  | Slow     |
| Laser cutting   | High       | Moderate |
| CNC milling     | High       | Moderate |

RULES: Soft fixtures only. Do not compress/deform.
Deburr after trimming -- sharp edges are cut hazards and stress concentrators.
```

**Right -- MACHINING (`#E8A020`):**

```
Exterior Ra as-deposited: 1-10 um. Machine to final dimensions.

FIXTURING: Interior is precision -- no hard clamps.
Solutions: expanding mandrel, soft jaw chucks, wax fill, vacuum fixtures.

ALLOWANCE: 0.1-0.5 mm per side above final dimension.
Tight tolerance (+/- 0.01 mm): measure frequently.

CUTTING: Carbide/ceramic inserts. 50-100 m/min. Coolant recommended.
Machines like wrought Ni.
```

## Phase 6 -- Annealing + Exterior Plating + Problems

Y: 22.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- STRESS-RELIEF ANNEAL (`#27AE60`):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Temperature | 400-600 C (typical 500 C) |
| Time | 1-2 hr at temperature |
| Atmosphere | Inert (Ar, N2) or vacuum |
| Ramp rate | 3-5 C/min |
| Cooling | Furnace cool, 2-5 C/min |

| Property | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Stress | 20-50 MPa | < 10 MPa |
| Hardness | 250-350 HV | 150-180 HV |
| Ductility | 5-15% | 20-30% |
| Stability | May creep | Stable |

`SULFUR WARNING:` Ni sulfamate deposits contain 0.01-0.1% S. Above 600 C, S migrates to grain boundaries = embrittlement. NEVER exceed 600 C. For brazing (> 800 C), S must be < 0.03%. (`#E05C5C`)

**Right -- EXTERIOR PLATING (`#2EC4B6`):**

When needed: corrosion, decorative, solderability, wear, conductivity. Mask interior completely. Common: decorative chrome (0.25-0.5 um), hard chrome (25-250 um), gold (0.5-5 um), tin (5-25 um). Activate exterior with mild acid dip before plating.

**Post-Processing Problems (Y: 28.5" to 32.5"):** Four cards.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| INTERIOR DAMAGED | Hard fixturing; careless handling | Soft fixtures; training; protective wrapping |
| DISTORTION AFTER ANNEAL | Stress release causes dimensional change | Constrained anneal on fixture; measure before/after |
| EMBRITTLEMENT AT HIGH TEMP | Sulfur at grain boundaries > 600 C | Never exceed 600 C for sulfamate; verify S content |
| EXTERIOR PLATING PEELED | Poor activation of machined surface | Acid activate; verify cleanliness |
