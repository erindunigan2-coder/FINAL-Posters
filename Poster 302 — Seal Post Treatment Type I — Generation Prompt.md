## Phase 5 -- Self-Healing Mechanism

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THE SELF-HEALING PROPERTY -- UNIQUE TO TYPE I`.

Visual: simplified cross-section showing a scratch in the oxide. Cr(VI) leaches from surrounding sealed oxide into the scratch site, passivating the exposed aluminum.

- `Residual Cr(VI) in the sealed coating acts as a corrosion inhibitor reservoir`
- `When the oxide is damaged, Cr(VI) migrates to the scratch and passivates the bare aluminum`
- `This "self-healing" property is the single hardest feature to replicate in Cr(VI)-free alternatives`
- `It is the primary reason aerospace OEMs have been slow to transition from Type I to BSAA`
- `BSAA does NOT self-heal -- corrosion primers (e.g., chromated epoxy) compensate` `#E8A020`

## Phase 6 -- Seal Quality Testing + Defects

Y: 20.5" to 26.5".

**Left -- Seal Quality Testing:**
Dye spot test (ASTM B680) | Admittance test (ISO 2931) | Acid dissolution (ASTM B680).
Note: `Unsealed parts: dye spot test is expected to SHOW dye absorption -- this is normal and intended.`

**Right -- Failure Modes:**

| Failure | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Seal bloom | Tap water minerals; pH wrong | Use DI water; check pH 5.5--7.5 |
| Crazing | Thermal shock from cold part to hot seal | Pre-warm parts |
| Poor paint adhesion | Over-sealed (pores too closed) | Reduce seal time or leave unsealed |
| Cr(VI) leaching from sealed coating | Normal -- this is the self-healing mechanism | Expected behavior; document for regulatory |

## Phase 7 -- Cr(VI) Waste + Regulatory Context

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `Cr(VI) WASTE TREATMENT + REGULATORY CONTEXT`.

- `Rinse water from the post-anodize rinse (Stage 7) contains Cr(VI) -- treat as hazardous waste`
- `Seal tank water will contain trace Cr(VI) from parts -- segregate and treat`
- `Reduce Cr(VI) to Cr(III) with sodium metabisulfite or ferrous sulfate before discharge`
- `Cr(VI) regulatory phase-out timeline: REACH SVHC since 2013; RoHS restricted`
- `Plan for conversion to BSAA (Type IC) or other Cr(VI)-free process` `#E8A020`
