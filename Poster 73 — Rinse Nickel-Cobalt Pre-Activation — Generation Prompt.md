## Phase 5 -- Rinse Types Comparison

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `THREE RINSE CONFIGURATIONS`.

Three callout boxes:

**SINGLE OVERFLOW** (accent `#2EC4B6`): 1 tank, continuous flow | Simplest setup | Highest water use | Best for low-volume rack lines.

**COUNTERFLOW CASCADE** (accent `#27AE60`): 2--3 tanks in series | Fresh water enters last tank, overflows backward | 90% less water for same rinse ratio | Industry standard for water efficiency.

**SPRAY RINSE** (accent `#E8A020`): Nozzle array | Lowest water consumption | Poor coverage on complex geometry | Often combined with immersion as pre-spray.

## Phase 6 -- Conductivity + Drag-Out Economics

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `MONITORING + DRAG-OUT ECONOMICS`. Two-column layout.

**Left -- Conductivity Monitoring (accent `#27AE60`):**
- Fresh DI: < 5 microS/cm | Good rinse: < 50 | Marginal: 50--200 | Dump: > 200
- `A handheld conductivity meter costs under $100. It tells you more about your rinse quality than any visual inspection.`

**Right -- Drag-Out Economics (accent `#E8A020`):**
- Typical drag-out: 1--4 gal per 1000 ft2 | Rack orientation matters | Dwell time 5--10 sec minimum
- `Every drop of process solution on the part that ends up in the rinse tank is chemistry you paid for. Drain time is free. Use it.`

## Phase 7 -- Common Rinse Failures

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG`. Four cards:

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ALKALINE CARRY-OVER | Insufficient rinse time or stagnant water | Increase flow; extend to 30+ sec |
| 2 | ACTIVATION NEUTRALIZED | High pH rinse water reaching acid | Monitor conductivity; dump and refill |
| 3 | WATER SPOTTING | Minerals in city water drying on parts | Switch to DI or add final spray rinse |
| 4 | CROSS-CONTAMINATION | Metals/organics from upstream | Dedicated rinse per stage; never share tanks |
