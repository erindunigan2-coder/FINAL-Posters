## Phase 5 -- Loading Rules

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `LOADING RULES -- SPACING, ORIENTATION, AND LIMITS`.

Three callout boxes side by side:

**Spacing (Teal):** Min 0.25" (6 mm) between parts. Gas must circulate all surfaces. Touching = blocked gas = soft spots. Large parts: 1" per foot rule.

**Orientation (Amber):** For gas flow: orient for uniform circulation; avoid stacking flat parts; use spacers. For quench: thin sections enter oil first; parts must not shift; holes/cavities must drain.

**Weight Limits (Amber):** 500--2,000 lb typical. Creep is the enemy at 1700 F. Track fixture cycles. Load thermocouple in densest part of load.

## Phase 6 -- Nesting + Quench Orientation

**Nesting (Y: 22.0"--28.5"):** Section label: `NESTING -- THE #1 CAUSE OF SOFT SPOTS`.

*Good (Emerald):* `CORRECT: SPACED LOADING` -- Gas circulates freely, all surfaces exposed, uniform ECD.
*Bad (Coral):* `WRONG: NESTED / TOUCHING` -- Contact areas get ZERO carbon, soft spots at every contact point.

**Quench Orientation (Y: 28.5"--32.5"):** Section label: `FIXTURING FOR THE QUENCH`. Four cards, Teal accent:
1. THIN SECTIONS FIRST
2. DRAIN HOLES (trapped oil = fire risk)
3. NO SHIFTING during transfer
4. UNIFORM COOLING (space for oil flow)
