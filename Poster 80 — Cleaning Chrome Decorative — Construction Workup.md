# Poster #80 -- Construction Workup
## Cleaning -- Chrome (Decorative)

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

This poster covers a cleaning stage unlike any other in the series. The substrate is not raw steel or aluminum -- it is freshly nickel-plated. There is no soak tank, no electrocleaner, no water-break test. The "cleaning" is about timing: keeping nickel active and oxide-free until it enters the chrome bath. Many shops do not even have a dedicated cleaning station for this step -- the parts go straight from nickel rinse to chrome.

Hero visual: a timeline-style diagram showing the critical path from nickel plating to chrome immersion, with a countdown clock emphasizing the <30 second window before nickel passivates.

### ZONE 5 -- Nickel Passivation Science

**Section label:** `WHY SPEED MATTERS -- NICKEL PASSIVATION` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK E -- Passivation Panel (Y: 21.3" to 26.3")**

Rounded rect, X: 0.5", W: 23.0", H: 4.8", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

Content:

**What happens when nickel sits in air:**
- `0--10 sec: minimal oxide, chrome will adhere` -- `#27AE60`
- `10--30 sec: thin oxide forming, chrome adhesion still acceptable` -- `#E8A020`
- `30--60 sec: significant passive film, adhesion risk increases` -- `#E05C5C`
- `> 60 sec: heavy passivation, chrome WILL peel` -- `#E05C5C` bold

**Contributing factors:**
- `Warm parts passivate faster than cool parts`
- `High humidity accelerates oxide formation`
- `Parts that dry are almost guaranteed to fail`
- `Rack density matters -- move full racks quickly`

**The fix if nickel passivates:**
- Inter Medium, 14 pt, `#E8A020`
- `Mild acid dip (1--5% H2SO4 or 1--3% HCl, ambient, 10--30 sec) reactivates the surface`
- `Or: reverse current activation in the chrome bath itself (first seconds as anode)`

## Design Notes

This is the most unusual cleaning poster in the entire series. There is no tank to illustrate, no chemical concentrations to specify. The entire message is: speed and timing. The countdown overlay in the hero zone is the most important visual on the poster -- it should be readable from 10 feet away. The passivation science panel gives the "why" behind the urgency. Every other cleaning poster in the series is about chemistry -- this one is about physics and timing.
