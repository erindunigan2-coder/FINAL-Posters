## Phase 5 -- Grinding / Honing

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `GRINDING AND HONING TO FINAL DIMENSION`.

**Left -- Grinding (W: 11.0", accent `#2EC4B6`):**
- Wheel: Diamond or CBN. Conventional abrasives cannot cut HV 800--1000.
- Speed: 5,000--6,500 SFPM.
- Infeed: 0.0002"--0.001" per pass. Light passes prevent micro-cracking.
- Coolant: Flood coolant required.
- Stock removal: Plate 0.002"--0.010" over, then grind back.

**Right -- Honing (W: 11.5", accent `#E8A020`):**
- For bores (hydraulic cylinders, gun barrels).
- Stone: Diamond or CBN honing stones.
- Speed: 100--200 SFPM. Pressure: Light.
- Finish: 4--16 microinch Ra for hydraulic cylinders.
- Crosshatch: 15--30 degree angle for oil retention.

### Surface Finish Targets

| Application | Target Ra | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Hydraulic cylinders | 4--16 microinch | Hone |
| Piston rings | 8--20 microinch | Grind + lap |
| Mold surfaces | 2--8 microinch | Grind + polish |
| Industrial tooling | 16--32 microinch | Grind |
| Dimensional restoration | Match original spec | Grind or hone |

## Phase 7 -- Common Failures + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5".

**Left -- Failures (W: 14.0"):**

| Failure | Root Cause | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Delayed HE bake | Parts sat too long | Irreversible embrittlement; scrap |
| Insufficient bake time | Wrong schedule for hardness | Residual hydrogen; delayed fracture |
| Grinding burn | Excessive infeed, poor coolant | Micro-cracking, adhesion loss |
| Over-grinding | Removed too much chrome | Undersize; strip and re-plate |
| Chrome flaking during grind | Poor adhesion from etch | Strip, re-etch, re-plate |

**Right -- Safety (W: 8.0", coral panel):**
- HE bake ovens: burn hazard (375--430 F)
- Grinding chrome: fine dust is Cr(VI) concern
- Wet grinding with flood coolant -- dry grinding NEVER permitted
- Chrome swarf/coolant: hazardous waste
- Coolant mist: local exhaust over grinders
- PPE: safety glasses, face shield, hearing protection
