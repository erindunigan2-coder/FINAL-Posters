## Phase 4 -- Cleaning Stage Detail Hero

Y: 2.9" to 15.5". Section: `ALKALINE CLEANING -- THE FOUNDATION`.

### Step 5 -- Stage detail panel (Y: 3.8" to 9.0")

Full-width rounded rect, fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#2EC4B6`. Badge: `STAGE 1 -- CLEAN`.

**Left -- Parameters:** Type: Alkaline cleaner pH 10--13 / Concentration: 4--8 oz/gal (30--60 g/L) / Temperature: 130--180 F (54--82 C) / Time: 5--15 min. Soil targets: machining oils, cutting fluids, grinding compounds, polishing compounds, fingerprints, organic soils.

**Right -- CHLORIDE PROHIBITION** (coral-tinted glass, border 2 pt `#E05C5C`): `ABSOLUTE RULE: NO CHLORIDE` -- Chloride-containing cleaners MUST NEVER be used on stainless. Chloride initiates pitting corrosion -- the exact failure passivation prevents. Check cleaner SDS. Silver nitrate spot test if in doubt.

### Step 6 -- Chloride contamination sources (Y: 9.5" to 15.0")

Six source cards (3x2 grid, left accent `#E05C5C`):
1. CHLORINATED CLEANERS -- Some industrial cleaners contain HCl or chlorinated solvents. Read the SDS.
2. TAP WATER -- Municipal water 10--250+ ppm chloride. Use DI for critical rinsing.
3. SHOP AIR -- Shops near salt water or using HCl contaminate the air.
4. HANDLING / GLOVES -- PVC gloves release chloride. Sweat contains NaCl. Use nitrile or cotton.
5. MARKING MATERIALS -- Some markers, tapes, labels contain chloride. Use chloride-free marking.
6. CARBON STEEL CONTACT -- Steel fixtures transfer iron AND may carry chloride residues.

## Phase 5 -- Cleaning Method Comparison

Y: 15.5" to 22.0". Section: `CLEANING METHODS -- THREE OPTIONS`.

Three panels:

**Soak Clean** (accent `#2EC4B6`): 4--8 oz/gal, 130--180 F, 5--15 min. Standard method. Best for general machined parts.

**Electrocleaner** (accent `#E8A020`): Cathodic preferred, 30--60 ASF, 130--160 F, 1--3 min. For precision and medical parts. Gas evolution scrubs microscopic soils.

**Descaling Pickle** (accent `#E05C5C`): 10--15% HNO3 + 1--3% HF, 120--140 F, 5--15 min. For heavy heat tint from welding/heat treat. WARNING: This is NOT passivation -- passivation follows separately.

## Phase 6 -- Heat Tint Decision

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `DO YOUR PARTS NEED DESCALING BEFORE PASSIVATION?`.

**Left -- DESCALE FIRST** (accent `#E05C5C`): Heavy heat tint, thick thermal oxide, weld discoloration, visible scale, cast with inclusions. Sequence: Clean -> Rinse -> Pickle -> Rinse -> Passivate -> Rinse -> Dry.

**Right -- PASSIVATE DIRECTLY** (accent `#27AE60`): Machined parts with light contamination, embedded free iron from tooling, stamped/formed (no heat tint), polished/ground, electropolished. Sequence: Clean -> Rinse -> Passivate -> Rinse -> Dry.

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

| # | X | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5" | PITTING AFTER PASSIVATION | Chloride in cleaner or rinse water | Eliminate ALL chloride sources |
| 2 | 6.33" | RESIDUAL OIL (WATER BREAK) | Cleaner too dilute; temp too low | Increase concentration/temp/time |
| 3 | 12.16" | HEAT TINT WON'T REMOVE | Thermal oxide too heavy for passivation acid | Descale with HNO3/HF pickle first |
| 4 | 18.0" | ETCHING DURING PICKLE | HF too concentrated; temp too high | Reduce HF to 1--2%; lower temp |
