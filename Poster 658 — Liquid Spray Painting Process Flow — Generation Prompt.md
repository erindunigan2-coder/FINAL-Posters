## Phase 6 -- Film-Forming Mechanisms + Spray Methods

Y: 22.2" to 27.3". Two-column layout.

**Left -- `SEVEN FILM-FORMING MECHANISMS` (W: 11.0"):**

| Mechanism | How It Works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Lacquer | Solvent evaporation only; no reaction | Nitrocellulose, acrylic lacquer |
| Oxidative dry | Solvent evap + O2 cross-linking | Alkyd enamels |
| Moisture cure | Isocyanate + atmospheric moisture | Moisture-cure PU |
| 2K chemical cure | Part A + Part B react when mixed | 2K urethane, 2K epoxy |
| Waterborne coalescence | Water evap; particles deform + fuse | Acrylic latex |
| High-solids | Solvent evap + cross-linking (less solvent) | High-solids epoxy |
| UV cure | Photoinitiator + UV light = polymerization | UV acrylate |

**Right -- `SPRAY METHOD COMPARISON` (W: 11.5"):**

| Method | Transfer Eff. | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Conventional Air Spray | 25--45% | High-quality topcoats, small parts |
| HVLP | 65%+ | General industrial, EPA compliance |
| Airless | 50--70% | Large surfaces, high production |
| Air-Assisted Airless | 60--75% | Heavy industrial, structural steel |
| Electrostatic Rotary Bell | 85--95% | Automotive OEM, highest efficiency |

Bottom note in `#27AE60`: `HVLP is the regulatory minimum in most jurisdictions. Electrostatic rotary bell is the gold standard for automotive OEM.`

## Phase 6B -- Troubleshooting Strip

Y: 28.3" to 32.3". Section label: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

4 cards, W: 5.5", H: 1.4", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`.

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| RUNS / SAGS | Too heavy a coat or too slow gun speed | Reduce fluid flow; increase gun speed; check viscosity |
| ORANGE PEEL | Too-fast solvent evap, low atomization, gun too far | Slow thinner; increase atomizing pressure; reduce distance |
| FISH-EYE / CRATERING | Silicone or oil contamination | Identify source; use fish-eye eliminator additive |
| POOR ADHESION | Contamination or exceeded recoat window | Clean surface; scuff sand if recoat window exceeded |
