## Phase 5 -- Operating Parameters

Y: 14.5" to 20.5". Section: `OPERATING PARAMETERS`.

Full-width panel, fill `#1E2435`:

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Water source | City water or DI | DI preferred for quality-critical work |
| Temperature | Ambient | No heating required |
| Rinse type | Single overflow | Not cascade -- speed over efficiency |
| Dwell time | 15--30 sec | Shorter is better |
| Flow rate | Moderate continuous | Enough to dilute drag-out, not more |
| Agitation | Part movement (dip/withdraw) | 2--3 dips sufficient |
| Conductivity target | < 500 uS/cm | Monitor but do not chase perfection here |

Bottom callout: `This is NOT a rinse where you chase conductivity readings. Adequate is enough. Speed is the priority.` -- `#E8A020`

## Phase 6 -- Drag-Out vs. Passivation Trade-Off

Y: 20.5" to 26.5". Section: `THE TRADE-OFF -- CLEAN VS. FAST`.

Two-column:

**Left -- Rinse Longer (Cleaner):**
Accent `#2EC4B6`. Title: `RINSE LONGER`.
- Removes more nickel drag-out
- Reduces contamination of activation bath
- Cleaner surface entering chrome
- BUT: nickel passivation risk increases with every second
- RISK: chrome adhesion failure (`#E05C5C`)

**Right -- Rinse Shorter (Faster):**
Accent `#27AE60`. Title: `RINSE SHORTER`.
- Nickel stays active longer
- Better chrome adhesion
- Some nickel drag-out enters activation
- Activation bath life slightly reduced
- PREFERRED: adhesion trumps rinse quality (`#27AE60`)

Center verdict banner (pill shape): `When in doubt, rinse shorter. You can top off the activation bath. You cannot un-passivate nickel.` -- `#E8A020`

## Phase 7 -- Common Issues

Y: 26.5" to 32.5". Section: `COMMON RINSE PROBLEMS`.

2x2 grid:

| Problem | Color | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHROME PEELING | `#E05C5C` | Parts sat in rinse too long -- nickel passivated | Tighten dwell time; train operators on urgency |
| SPOTTY CHROME | `#E8A020` | Uneven rinsing -- drag-out concentrated in recesses | Agitate parts; 2--3 dips minimum |
| STAINING | `#E8A020` | Stagnant rinse water, high TDS | Increase overflow rate; monitor water quality |
| PARTS DRYING | `#E05C5C` | Rack pulled from rinse and left waiting | Never let parts dry; move immediately to next stage |
