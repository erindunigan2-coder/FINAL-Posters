## Phase 4 -- Blast Parameters (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.0". Section label: `BLAST SPECIFICATION FOR ARC SPRAY`.

**Blast Parameter Table (Y: 5.0"--9.5"):** 6-row table, full width.

| Parameter | Typical Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Media | Angular steel grit (G25, G40) or aluminum oxide (24--36 mesh) | Steel grit preferred for structural -- recyclable |
| Blast pressure | 60--100 PSI (415--690 kPa) | More aggressive than APS/HVOF |
| Nozzle distance | 150--300 mm (6--12 in) | Closer for deeper profile |
| Blast angle | 60--90 degrees | 90 deg optimal; 45 deg minimum |
| Anchor profile (Ra) | 4--12 um (175--500 uin) | Rougher profile needed for mechanical interlock |
| Surface cleanliness | SSPC-SP 5 (White Metal) per AWS C2.18 | SP 10 acceptable for some specs |

Values in `#E8A020`. Header fill `#3A4055`.

**Field Blasting Considerations (Y: 10.0"--13.5"):** Left accent `#E8A020`. Title: `FIELD BLASTING -- ENVIRONMENTAL & REGULATORY`.
- Containment required for lead paint removal
- Spent media must be tested for lead and hex chrome before disposal
- Steel grit recyclable; alumina is not
- Dust suppression: wet blasting or vacuum recovery
- Worker exposure monitoring per OSHA PEL
- Waste disposal per RCRA if hazardous

## Phase 5 -- Media Selection

Y: 14.0" to 20.5". Section label: `MEDIA SELECTION -- STEEL GRIT VS. ALUMINA`.

**Left -- STEEL GRIT (G25--G40) (`#E8A020`):** 7--8 Mohs; recyclable 100+ cycles; aggressive on carbon steel; lower per-use cost; NOT for stainless/aluminum/titanium (ferrous contamination); standard for bridge work.

**Right -- ALUMINUM OXIDE (24--36 mesh) (`#2EC4B6`):** 9 Mohs; single-use; self-sharpening; no ferrous contamination; required for stainless and non-ferrous; higher per-use cost; preferred for shop/precision.

Callout: `For structural steel (primary arc spray market), steel grit is the standard -- cheaper, recyclable, excellent profile on carbon steel.`

## Phase 6 -- Profile Verification + Ambient Conditions

### Profile Verification (Y: 20.5"--27.0")

Section label: `PROFILE VERIFICATION METHODS`. Three cards:

**Card 1 -- TESTEX REPLICA TAPE (`#27AE60`):** Field-portable; press tape, measure with spring micrometer; subtract 2 mil backing; accuracy +/- 0.5 mil; coarse grade for Ra 4--12 um.

**Card 2 -- SURFACE PROFILOMETER (`#2EC4B6`):** Electronic Ra/Rz; higher accuracy; requires calibration; preferred for spec disputes; records data for QA.

**Card 3 -- VISUAL COMPARATOR SSPC-VIS 1 (`#E8A020`):** Reference plates; quick field check; subjective -- not for final acceptance; useful for training; always confirm with tape or profilometer.

### Ambient Conditions (Y: 27.0"--32.5")

Section label: `AMBIENT CONDITIONS -- WHEN TO HOLD`. Four cards:

| Rule | Detail | Accent |
|---|---|---|
| DEW POINT RULE | Surface temp within 3 degC of dew point = stop. Condensation = instant flash rust. | `#E05C5C` |
| RAIN / SNOW HOLD | Stop in precipitation. Resume only after surface confirmed dry. | `#E05C5C` |
| WIND HOLD | >25 mph scatters media, reduces control. Containment may be required. | `#E8A020` |
| TEMPERATURE MIN | Surface >5 degC (40 degF). Cold steel harder to clean, flash rusts faster. | `#E8A020` |
