## Phases 5-7 -- Content Zones

Step 1 -- Anodize: H2SO4 150--200 g/L, 64--72 F, 12--18 ASF, 20--45 min. Standard Type II anodize. Film: 15--25 um for architectural color.

Step 2 -- Electrolytic Color: Metal salt bath (SnSO4, CoSO4, or NiSO4). AC power (alternating current). Voltage: 10--18 V AC. Time controls shade: 30 sec (champagne) to 15 min (black). Temp: ambient to 85 F.

Color mechanism: AC current deposits metal at pore bases. Longer time = more metal = darker shade.

Color range: champagne, light bronze, medium bronze, dark bronze, black. NO red, blue, green, yellow -- those require organic dyes (Type II process).

Color uniformity depends on: uniform pore structure (from anodize) + uniform AC voltage distribution + consistent time.

Comparison: Two-step vs organic dye vs integral color.

4 defect cards: uneven color, too light/dark, streaking, color after seal.
