### Step 8 -- Surfactant carry-over risk (Y: 18.5" to 20.8")

Full-width callout, fill `#E05C5C` at 10%, border 1pt `#E05C5C`.
- Title: `SURFACTANT CARRY-OVER -- THE HIDDEN ORGANIC SOURCE` `#E05C5C`
- Alkaline cleaners contain surfactants. If rinsing is inadequate, surfactants travel into the sulfamate bath as organic contaminants. In Watts: pitting. In sulfamate: stress spike.
- Conductivity meters catch ionic residues but NOT surfactants. Supplement with periodic TOC testing or simply rinse more aggressively.

## Phase 6 -- Rinse Types + Failure Modes

Y: 21.0" to 27.0". Two-column layout.

**Left -- Rinse Configurations (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Type | Efficiency | Sulfamate Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Single overflow | Low | Minimum acceptable |
| Double overflow | Moderate | Good for moderate volume |
| Counterflow (2-stage) | High | RECOMMENDED for sulfamate lines |
| Spray rinse | Very high | Excellent if available |

**Right -- 4 Failure Modes (X: 12.5", W: 11.0"):**

| Problem | Cause | Downstream Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Alkaline drag-in to acid | Insufficient rinse | Acid bath weakened; staining |
| Surfactant carry-through | Cleaner not fully rinsed | Organic contamination --> stress in sulfamate |
| Contamination buildup | No overflow; stagnant rinse | Progressive organic loading |
| Cross-contamination | Shared rinse between processes | Chemical reactions on surface |

## Phase 7 -- Practical Tips + Safety

Y: 27.0" to 32.5". Two-column layout.

**Left -- Tips (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**
Accent `#27AE60`.
- For sulfamate lines, double the rinse time you would use for Watts. Cost of over-rinsing is water. Cost of under-rinsing is a stressed deposit.
- Drain 5--10 sec over process tank. Dip racks 3--4 times minimum.
- If you can smell cleaner in the rinse tank, you are not overflowing fast enough.
- Consider a dedicated rinse system -- do not share with a Watts line if possible.

**Right -- Safety (X: 15.5", W: 8.0"):**
Coral-tinted glass. Mildly caustic rinse water | Route overflow to treatment | Wet floors: slip hazard.
