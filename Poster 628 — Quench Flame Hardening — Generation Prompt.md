## Phase 4 -- Three Quench Methods (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 14.5". Section label: `THREE QUENCH METHODS`. Three side-by-side panels.

| Method | Accent | Parameters | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPRAY FOLLOW (PROGRESSIVE) | `#2EC4B6` | Water spray 0.5--2.0 in behind flame; 20--60 psi; 2--10 GPM/nozzle | Long surfaces: ways, rails, shafts |
| IMMERSION | `#E8A020` | Part immersed after heating; water 60--80 F; agitation recommended | Small-medium parts; spot and spin |
| SELF-QUENCH (MASS QUENCH) | `#27AE60` | No external quenchant; cold core acts as heat sink | Very large parts; field repairs; case < 10% section |

Critical: `If spray-follow distance is too large, surface air-cools below Ac3. Too close, steam blanket forms.`

## Phase 5 -- Quench Severity Comparison

Y: 14.5" to 22.0". Section label: `QUENCH SEVERITY -- FLAME HARDENING IN CONTEXT`. 7-row H-factor table.

| Method | Medium | H-Factor | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flame -- spray follow | Water spray | 0.50--1.0 | Depends on spray pressure/distance |
| Flame -- immersion | Still water | 1.0 | Standard immersion |
| Flame -- immersion | Agitated water | 1.0--1.5 | Agitation increases severity |
| Flame -- self-quench | Mass (no medium) | 0.02--0.10 | Part mass vs. case volume |
| Induction -- spray | Polymer (5--15% PAG) | 0.30--0.50 | Lower severity than water |
| Conventional | Oil (agitated) | 0.35--0.80 | Standard furnace quench |
| Conventional | Water (agitated) | 1.0--1.5 | Most severe |

Note: `Flame hardening uses water (H = 0.5--1.5) -- more severe than induction's polymer. Acceptable because flame parts are usually large.`

## Phase 6 -- Quench Defects + Footer

**Quench Defects** (Y: 22.0" to 28.5"). Section label: `QUENCH-RELATED DEFECTS`. Four cards.

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CRACKING | Quench too severe; no preheat on complex geometry | Reduce spray pressure; preheat 300--400 F |
| SOFT SPOTS | Spray-follow distance too large; steam blanket | Reduce gap to 0.5--1.0 in; increase water flow |
| DISTORTION | Asymmetric quench; one-sided spray | Balance spray from multiple nozzles |
| INCOMPLETE HARDENING | Self-quench on part too small | Switch to spray or immersion quench |
