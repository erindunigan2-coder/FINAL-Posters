## Phase 4 -- Tempering Parameters (HERO)

Y: 4.2" to 12.5". Section label: `TEMPERING -- STRESS RELIEF WITHOUT HARDNESS LOSS`.

Four parameter cards (2x2):

| Card | Title | Accent | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (TL) | STANDARD TEMPER | `#E8A020` | 300--375 F, 2 hr min, air or N2, per AMS 2759/7. Low-temp temper: relieves stress, improves toughness. Same as gas carburizing -- process-agnostic. |
| 2 (TR) | DOUBLE TEMPER (AEROSPACE) | `#2EC4B6` | Required for 9310 and some aerospace specs. Temper -> cool to RT -> temper again. Ensures all fresh martensite (from RA conversion) is tempered. Not optional -- spec-driven. |
| 3 (BL) | SUB-ZERO TREATMENT | `#2EC4B6` | -100 to -120 F, between temper cycles. Transforms RA to martensite (RA < 10% spec). Method: dry ice/alcohol (-109 F) or mechanical refrigeration. |
| 4 (BR) | FULL SEQUENCE (AEROSPACE) | `#27AE60` | 1. Quench 2. Temper 1 3. Sub-zero (if req) 4. Temper 2 5. Inspect. Total post-quench: 8--12 hr. Non-aerospace: steps 3-4 may be omitted per spec. |

## Phase 5 -- Inspection Advantage + Hardness + Defects

**Inspection Advantage (Y: 12.5"--15.0"):** Full-width callout, Emerald accent. `WHAT LPC ELIMINATES FROM INSPECTION`.

*ELIMINATED (Emerald):* IGO measurement (NO IGO EXISTS -- step eliminated entirely). Surface oxidation evaluation (no atmosphere = no oxidation).

*STILL REQUIRED (Amber):* Hardness traverse, surface hardness, core hardness, RA measurement, microstructure evaluation, carbide morphology, soot deposit inspection (LPC-specific).

**Hardness Targets (Y: 15.0"--19.0"):** Section label: `HARDNESS TARGETS`.

| Location | Range |
|---|---|
| Surface (case) | 58--63 HRC |
| Core (8620) | 30--40 HRC |
| Core (9310) | 35--44 HRC |
| Core (M50NiL) | 38--45 HRC |

ECD to 50 HRC per SAE J423. TCD = 1.5--2.0x ECD.

**Common Defects (Y: 19.5"--28.5"):** Section label: `COMMON DEFECTS -- LPC-SPECIFIC FAILURES` in Coral.

| Defect | Cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Soot / carbon black deposits | Excessive boost pressure/time; propane vs. acetylene | Reduce boost pressure; switch to C2H2; verify mass flow cal |
| Non-uniform case | Gas flow pattern; load density too high | Optimize loading; increase diffuse time |
| Excessive retained austenite | High surface C; high alloy; insufficient quench | Sub-zero treatment; verify surface C via simulation |
| Free carbides at surface | Too many boosts without sufficient diffuse | Extend diffuse phases; re-run simulation |
| Grain growth | Temp >1900 F without microalloy control | Reduce temp; use vacuum-grade fine-grain steel |

Defect column in Coral.

## Phase 6 -- Inspection Methods

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section label: `INSPECTION METHODS`. Four cards, Emerald accent:

| Card | Method | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | MICROHARDNESS TRAVERSE | Knoop/Vickers at incremental depths. ECD = depth to 50 HRC. Per ASTM E384 / SAE J423. |
| 2 | METALLOGRAPHY | Mount, polish, etch (2% Nital). Martensite quality, carbide morphology, soot deposits. |
| 3 | RETAINED AUSTENITE | Point count or XRD. Must not exceed 20% in outer 10% of case per AMS 2759/7. |
| 4 | SURFACE CARBON | Verified by simulation (not real-time). Validate on coupons by combustion analysis or GDOES. |
