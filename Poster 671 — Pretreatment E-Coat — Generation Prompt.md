## Phase 5 -- Crystal Quality + Surface Conditioning

Y: 15.8" to 21.3". Section label: `ZINC PHOSPHATE CRYSTAL QUALITY -- THE FOUNDATION`.

**Left -- `CRYSTAL COMPOSITION` (`#27AE60`):**
Target 150--400 mg/ft2. Hopeite Zn3(PO4)2.4H2O / Phosphophyllite Zn2Fe(PO4)2.4H2O. Higher phosphophyllite = better adhesion. Fine uniform microcrystalline. Salt spray: 500--1,000+ hr B117 (primer alone).

**Right -- `SURFACE CONDITIONING -- THE SECRET STAGE` (`#E8A020`):**
Colloidal TiPO4 seed crystals. Nucleation sites for zinc phosphate. With conditioning: fine, dense crystals. Without: coarse, porous, poor adhesion. 0.5--2.0 g/L, ambient--100 F, 30--60 sec. `This single stage transforms phosphate quality.`
