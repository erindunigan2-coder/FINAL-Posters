# Poster #138 -- Construction Workup
## Activation -- Cadmium

*Alaina -- Plating Posters Inc Poster Designer*
*v1.0 -- 2026-04-26*

Stage 3 of 8. Acid activation for cadmium plating is the highest hydrogen-embrittlement-risk step in the pre-treatment sequence. Hydrochloric acid (HCl) dissolves surface oxides but simultaneously generates atomic hydrogen on the steel surface. The acid concentration, temperature, and time must all be tightly controlled. MAXIMUM 30 seconds -- this is not a suggestion, it is a specification limit per AMS 2759/9 best practices.

### ZONE 4 -- HE Risk During Activation

**Section label:** `HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT RISK AT THIS STAGE` -- Y: 14.7".

**BLOCK D -- Two-Panel (Y: 15.3" to 20.3")**

**Left -- The Mechanism:**
- Rounded rect, W: 11.0", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E05C5C`
- Title: `WHAT HAPPENS IN 30 SECONDS` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E05C5C`
- `1. HCl attacks iron oxide on steel surface (desired)`
- `2. HCl also attacks bare steel metal (undesired)`
- `3. Reaction liberates atomic hydrogen (H)`
- `4. Atomic H diffuses into steel lattice`
- `5. H migrates to grain boundaries, recombines to H2`
- `6. H2 molecule is trapped -- cannot diffuse out`
- `7. Internal pressure builds at grain boundary`
- `Longer acid time = more H absorbed = higher HE risk`

**Right -- Why HCl (Not H2SO4):**
- Rounded rect, W: 11.5", H: 4.5", fill `#1E2435`, left accent `#E8A020`
- Title: `WHY HCl FOR STEEL?` Barlow SemiBold 18 pt `#E8A020`
- `HCl is preferred for steel activation because:`
- `1. Faster oxide dissolution -- less time needed`
- `2. FeCl2 is soluble -- no smut residue on surface`
- `3. H2SO4 on steel can form insoluble FeSO4 smut`
- `4. Faster = less hydrogen absorbed`
- `The trade-off: HCl generates more fume (require ventilation)`

### ZONE 5 -- Operating Window

**Section label:** `OPERATING WINDOW -- HARD LIMITS` -- Y: 20.7".

**BLOCK E -- Parameter Table (Y: 21.3" to 26.3")**

| Parameter | Minimum | Maximum | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| HCl concentration | 10% v/v | 20% v/v | Higher = faster activation = less time needed |
| Temperature | Ambient | Ambient | NEVER heat acid for Cd activation |
| Time | 15 sec | 30 sec | HARD MAXIMUM -- timer mandatory |
| Agitation | None | Gentle rack | Do not over-agitate |

Below table:
- `If parts are not fully activated in 30 seconds:` Inter Medium 14 pt `#E05C5C`
- `1. Remove parts. 2. Rinse. 3. Re-clean. 4. Try again with fresh acid.`
- `DO NOT extend acid time past 30 seconds to "finish" activation.` Barlow SemiBold 14 pt `#E05C5C`

**Section label:** `ACTIVATION ERRORS ON CADMIUM SUBSTRATES` -- Y: 26.7".

**BLOCK F -- Four Cards**

| Card | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OVER-ACTIVATION (>30 sec) | No timer; operator distracted | Install timer with alarm at acid tank |
| 2 | SPENT ACID | Acid too weak to activate in time limit | Replace acid on schedule; titrate |
| 3 | WRONG ACID | H2SO4 used instead of HCl | Smut on steel; verify acid type |
| 4 | DELAY TO PLATING | Parts sit after activation | Plate within 5 min; surface re-oxidizes |
