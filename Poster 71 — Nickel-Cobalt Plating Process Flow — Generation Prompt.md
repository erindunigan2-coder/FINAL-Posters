**Box contents:**

**Box 1:** Badge `STAGE 1` `#2EC4B6`. `Alkaline Soak Clean`. 140--180 F | 4--8 oz/gal | 3--10 min. Remove oils, soils, machining compounds. Check: `Water-break-free after rinse`.

**Box 2:** Badge `STAGE 2` `#2EC4B6`. `Rinse` / `Pre-Activation`. Ambient | Flowing or cascade. Remove alkaline residue. Check: `Prevents neutralizing the acid activation`.

**Box 3:** Badge `STAGE 3` `#E8A020`. `Activation`. HCl 20--50% v/v (steel) | Wood's strike (Inconel/Ti) | Ambient, 15--60 sec. Remove surface oxides. Check: `AEROSPACE: Wood's strike mandatory for superalloys` `#E05C5C`.

**Box 4:** Badge `STAGE 4` `#2EC4B6`. `Rinse` / `Pre-Plate`. Ambient | Flowing or cascade. Remove activation acid. Check: `Acid drag-in contaminates the NiCo bath`.

**Box 5:** Badge `STAGE 5` `#27AE60`. `Nickel-Cobalt Plate` / `Main Tank`. Ni sulfamate 300--400 g/L | CoSO4 10--60 g/L | pH 3.5--4.5 | 120--140 F | 20--60 ASF. Electrodeposit Ni-Co alloy. Check: `Co% controlled by bath ratio + CD`.

**Box 6:** Badge `STAGE 6` `#2EC4B6`. `Rinse` / `Post-Plate`. Ambient | Flowing, multi-stage. Remove plating solution drag-out. Check: `CRITICAL: Thorough rinse before any post-treatment` `#E05C5C`.

**Box 7:** Badge `STAGE 7` `#E8A020`. `Post-Treatment`. Cr topcoat (wear) | Heat treat 300 C / 1--4 hr | Final deposit (magnetic). Maximize hardness or apply topcoat. Check: `Heat treat can push hardness to 600--700 HV`.

**Box 8:** Badge `STAGE 8` `#E8A020`. `Dry / Final Inspect`. Forced air or oven | XRF for Co% | Hardness test (Vickers). Verify deposit composition and properties. Check: `AMS 2424 specifies Co% and hardness requirements`.

## Phase 6 -- Why Nickel-Cobalt? Comparison

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `WHY NICKEL-COBALT? -- NiCo ALLOY VS. STANDARD NICKEL`.

**Left -- Nickel-Cobalt Alloy (X: 0.5", W: 11.0"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#27AE60`. Title: `NICKEL-COBALT ALLOY` `#27AE60`. Subtitle: `The Hardness Advantage`.
- Deposit: 65--85% Ni / 15--35% Co | 400--500 HV as-plated | 600--700 HV heat treated | Superior wear resistance | High magnetic permeability (tunable) | Higher stress than sulfamate Ni | Aerospace turbine, molds, tooling | AMS 2424
- Bottom bar `#27AE60`: `Higher cobalt = higher hardness. But formulations are OEM-specific -- always verify against the governing spec.`

**Right -- Standard Nickel (X: 12.0", W: 11.5"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E8A020`. Title: `STANDARD NICKEL` `#E8A020`. Subtitle: `The General-Purpose Baseline`.
- Deposit: 99%+ Ni | 150--250 HV (Watts) / 180--300 HV (sulfamate) | Good but lower wear resistance | Moderate magnetic permeability | Low stress (especially sulfamate) | Decorative, corrosion, engineering | AMS 2403, ASTM B689
- Bottom bar `#E8A020`: `Standard nickel covers 90% of applications. NiCo is for when pure nickel is not hard enough.`

## Phase 7 -- Troubleshooting Quick Hits

Y: 28.5" to 32.5". Section: `QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING -- 4 COMMON PROBLEMS`.

Four cards, gap 0.33". Each: W: 5.5", H: 2.7", fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E05C5C`.

| # | Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CO% OUT OF SPEC | Bath ratio imbalance, temp drift, CD mismatch | Analyze bath metals; adjust CoSO4; check temperature |
| 2 | CRACKING (POST HEAT TREAT) | Too rapid heating, excess cobalt, hydrogen | Slow ramp rate; verify Co%; H2 bake before heat treat |
| 3 | ROUGH / NODULAR | Particulate contamination, anode sludge | Inspect anode bags; increase filtration; filter bath |
| 4 | LOW HARDNESS | Cobalt too low, organic contamination | Add CoSO4; carbon treat; verify by XRF |
