## Phase 6 -- Technology Comparison

Y: 22.0" to 28.5". Section: `FOUR TECHNOLOGIES -- ONE GOAL: ZERO CHROMIUM`.

Four vertical callout boxes (H: 5.0", fill `#1E2435`, top accent 4 pt):

| Technology | X | W | Accent | Status Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zr-Only | 0.5" | 5.5" | `#27AE60` | `COMMERCIAL STANDARD` |
| Ti/Zr Hybrid | 6.25" | 5.5" | `#2EC4B6` | `ESTABLISHED` |
| Cerium (Rare Earth) | 12.0" | 5.5" | `#E8A020` | `EMERGING` |
| Sol-Gel Hybrid | 17.75" | 5.75" | `#C8D0D8` | `SPECIALIZED` |

Each box contains: active component/concentration, film thickness, multi-metal compatibility, bare salt spray range, key advantage, key limitation.

- Zr-Only: H2ZrF6; 50--200 ppm Zr; 20--100 nm; nearly invisible; minimal sludge; multi-metal. Dominant automotive.
- Ti/Zr Hybrid: Combined Ti/Zr fluorocomplexes; broader operating window; some aerospace approvals.
- Cerium: CeO2/Ce(OH)3 on cathodic sites; promising for 2xxx/7xxx Cu-alloys; still primarily R&D.
- Sol-Gel: Organic-inorganic hybrid; silane + metal oxide; may require thermal cure; excellent paint base.
