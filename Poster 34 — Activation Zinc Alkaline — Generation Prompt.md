### Step 10 -- Right: HCl vs H2SO4 (X: 12.0", W: 11.5")

**HCl box (Y: 14.8", H: 3.2"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#E8A020`. Title: `HYDROCHLORIC ACID (HCl)`.
- Most common for steel
- Faster oxide removal
- Lower H-embrittlement risk
- Fumes -- ventilation required
- Attacks Cu/brass

**H2SO4 box (Y: 18.3", H: 3.2"):**
Fill `#1E2435`, accent `#2EC4B6`. Title: `SULFURIC ACID (H2SO4)`.
- Lower fuming
- Slower removal -- longer immersion
- Higher H-absorption risk on steel
- Safe for copper substrates
- More economical high-volume

## Phase 6 -- Problems

Y: 22.0" to 28.0". Section: `WHAT GOES WRONG AT ACTIVATION`.

| Problem | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under-activation | Zinc peels | Time too short / acid weak | Extend time; check conc |
| Over-activation | Rough zinc, pitting | Time too long / acid strong | Reduce time; dilute |
| H-embrittlement | Delayed fracture (HTS) | H absorbed during acid | 15--30 sec max; bake 375 F |
| Smut | Dark residue | Carbon smut from high-C steel | De-smutter; mechanical |
| Flash rust | Orange tint | Parts dried before rinse | Immediate transfer |

## Phase 7 -- H-Embrittlement + Safety

Y: 28.0" to 32.5". Section: `CRITICAL SAFETY -- HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT` `#E05C5C`.

**Left -- H-Embrittlement (X: 0.5", W: 14.0"):**
Rounded rect fill `#1E2435`, FULL border 2pt `#E05C5C`.
Title: `HYDROGEN EMBRITTLEMENT WARNING` `#E05C5C`.
- High-strength steel >= 145 ksi / >= 31 HRC absorbs H during acid
- Limit activation to 15--30 sec
- Bake 375 F within 4 hr, hold 23 hr min (ASTM B850)
- Never skip the bake
- Ref: `ASTM B850, AMS 2759` JetBrains Mono 12pt `#E05C5C`

**Right -- Acid Safety (X: 15.0", W: 8.5"):**
Accent `#E8A020`. Title: `ACID HANDLING SAFETY`.
- HCl fumes: hood ventilation
- H2SO4: add acid to water, NEVER reverse
- Splash goggles + face shield
- Acid-resistant gloves/apron
- Eyewash + shower within 10 sec
- Neutralize spills: soda ash (Na2CO3)
